# Mixed canonical-pivot search for `HC(4)`

## Status

This note records a bounded exact search in the first source--dual mechanism
left open by the pure-source obstruction `SDX2`.  It is a computation, not a
theorem about all mixed canonical transformations or all coisotropic
embeddings.

The search contains 312 exact polynomial symplectic charts generated by
quadratic or cubic Hamiltonians.  Every generator mixes source and dual
variables; no pure source transformation is included.  Every chart has an
affine scalar pivot, and 258 charts/pivot choices have a jointly affine
two-coordinate block.  The staged outcome is:

\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r}
\text{gate}&\text{trials}&\text{survivors}\\ \hline
\text{affine scalar pivot}&312\text{ charts}&312\text{ charts}\\
D(\mu+\lambda A,w)&4320&240\\
\operatorname{rank}M\le 2&258&0\\
\text{complete descended determinant}&41796&0.
\end{array}
\]

The 240 scalar survivors are exact, not merely modular: they are 40
quadratic charts with the original \(z\)-pivot, for all six tested
\((\lambda,\mu)\), and have \(D\equiv0\) by the doubled row count followed
by constant symplectic congruence.  They reproduce one-step `HC(5)`
descents in mixed linear coordinates, but none has the simultaneous corank
needed for `HC(4)`.

Every corank rejection and every complete-determinant rejection has an exact
witness modulo \(1000003\).  Unequal values, or rank greater than two, after
reduction modulo a good prime prove failure over characteristic zero.  Thus
the zero survivor counts are exact for the declared finite coefficient
boxes.

## 1. Collision-centred doubling

Use canonical coordinates

\[
 q=(x,y,z),\qquad p=(u,v,w),\qquad
 \omega=\sum_{i=1}^3dq_i\wedge dp_i.
\]

For the normalized foundational Keller map, put

\[
\begin{aligned}
t&=1+xy,\\
Q&=t^2z+y^2(1+3t),\\
P&=tQ,\\
B&=y+3xQ,\\
C&=x(5-3t)-x^3z.
\end{aligned}
\]

The points

\[
 (1,-3/2,13/2),\qquad(-1,3/2,13/2)
\]

have common image \((-1/4,0,0)\).  The integral collision-centred doubled
potential is

\[
 \Phi_0=u(4P+1)+4vB+4wC.
\]

It has

\[
 \det\operatorname{Hess}\Phi_0=-4\cdot4^6=-16384,
\]

and the two lifted points with \(p=0\) are critical.  Centering is useful
because polynomial changes of independent variables preserve these two
critical points, even though nonlinear changes do not generally preserve
the constant-Hessian equation.

## 2. Exact Hamiltonian charts

If \(L_1,\ldots,L_s\) are Poisson-commuting linear forms and
\(H=h(L_1,\ldots,L_s)\), their values are constant along the Hamiltonian
flow.  Its time-one map is therefore the exact polynomial symplectomorphism

\[
 T_H(q,p)=\left(q+\frac{\partial H}{\partial p},
 p-\frac{\partial H}{\partial q}\right),
\]

with inverse \(T_{-H}\).  No truncated Lie series is used.

The coefficient box is

\[
 \tau\in\{-2,-1,1,2\},\qquad \epsilon\in\{-1,1\}.
\]

### 2.1 One mixed source--dual line

For every \(0\le i,j\le2\) and \(d\in\{2,3\}\), use

\[
 H=\tau(q_i+\epsilon p_j)^d.
\]

This gives 144 charts.

### 2.2 Two source--dual pairs

For \(0\le i<j\le2\), the forms

\[
 L_1=q_i+\epsilon p_j,\qquad
 L_2=q_j+\epsilon p_i
\]

Poisson-commute.  Use

\[
 H=\tau L_1^aL_2^b,\qquad
 (a,b)\in\{(1,1),(1,2),(2,1)\}.
\]

Both forms occur in every generator, giving 72 genuinely two-line charts.

### 2.3 One nonlinear coisotropic graph constraint

Fix \(k\), choose \(i,j\ne k\), and set

\[
 L=q_i+\epsilon p_j,\qquad H=\tau q_kL^2.
\]

Because \(\{q_k,L\}=0\), this again has an exact polynomial time-one flow.
The \(p_k\)-coordinate changes by \(-\tau L^2\), so the image of
\(p_k=0\) is a coisotropic hypersurface given by one nonlinear graph
constraint.  These choices give 96 charts.

The family census is:

\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|r}
\text{family}&\text{charts}&\text{affine scalars}&
\text{jointly affine pairs}\\ \hline
\text{one mixed line}&144&384&144\\
\text{two mixed lines}&72&112&18\\
\text{coisotropic graph}&96&224&96.
\end{array}
\]

## 3. Staged tests

For each pullback \(\Phi=\Phi_0\circ T_H\), the checker performs the
following tests in order.

### 3.1 Affine pivots

A coordinate \(t\) is retained only when

\[
 \Phi=tA(w)+B(w).
\]

A pair \((t_1,t_2)\) is retained only when

\[
 \Phi=t_1A_1(w)+t_2A_2(w)+B(w),
\]

with no \(t_1t_2\) term.  This is checked from the complete monomial
support, not from evaluations.

### 3.2 Specialized scalar remainder

For every scalar pivot, test

\[
 D(\mu+\lambda A,w),\qquad
 D(s,w)=\det\operatorname{Hess}_w(B+sA),
\]

in the box

\[
 \lambda\in\{-1,1\},\qquad
 \mu\in\{-1,0,1\}.
\]

Of 4320 trials, 4080 have two unequal values modulo \(1000003\).  The other
240 are exactly zero.  They occur precisely when a quadratic generator is
supported on \((x,y,u,v)\) and the affine pivot is \(z\).  Before the
linear symplectic change, \(B+sA\) is a doubling with two source and three
dual variables, so its reduced Hessian is singular by row count.  The
constant symplectic congruence preserves zero determinant.

### 3.3 Simultaneous corank budget

For every affine pair, form

\[
 M(s,w)=\operatorname{Hess}_w
 \bigl(B+s_1A_1+s_2A_2\bigr).
\]

Here \(m=4,r=2\), so the sufficient simultaneous gate is

\[
 \operatorname{rank}M\le m-r=2.
\]

All 258 candidates have an exact modular specialization of rank at least
three.  The simultaneous corank mechanism therefore never fires in this
box.

### 3.4 Complete descended determinant

Failure of the sufficient corank bound does not exclude the exact bordered
graph gate.  The checker therefore proceeds to every symmetric repair

\[
 \Lambda=
 \begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
a,b,c\in\{-1,0,1\},\qquad ac-b^2\ne0,
\]

and every \(\mu\in\{-1,0,1\}^2\).  There are 18 matrices and nine shifts.
For

\[
 \psi=B+\mu^TA+\frac12A^T\Lambda A
\]

the complete four-variable Hessian determinant is evaluated at 15
deterministic points modulo \(1000003\).  Every one of the

\[
 258\cdot18\cdot9=41796
\]

trials has two unequal values.  Hence none is constant over characteristic
zero.

Collision transfer is not used as a pruning condition.  Since the
determinant gate has no survivor, the postponed linear-algebra step is
unnecessary.

## 4. Parent-Hessian side audit

This audit is performed after the four search gates and does not prune
candidates.  The 96 quadratic generators give linear symplectic maps, so
their parent Hessian determinant remains \(-16384\) by constant
congruence.  Every one of the 216 cubic charts has unequal parent-Hessian
values modulo \(1000003\).  This confirms a useful warning:

\[
\boxed{\text{nonlinear canonical changes of independent variables do not
automatically preserve the constant-Hessian equation.}}
\]

Accordingly, the specialized scalar remainder is the exact Schur gate only
for the 96 quadratic parent charts.  The search nevertheless evaluates the
complete four-variable determinant in every affine pair, including all
cubic and coisotropic charts.

## 5. Interpretation and next box

The search reaches the first mechanism not globally excluded by `SDX2`, but
the smallest exact Hamiltonian-shear box does not descend to `HC(4)`.
Its positive information is structural:

1. mixed linear canonical coordinates readily preserve the known scalar
   \(D\equiv0\) route to `HC(5)`;
2. adding a second affine coordinate immediately exceeds the corank budget;
3. the relaxed complete graph determinant does not rescue any small
   constant repair;
4. the cubic coisotropic graphs fail already without a scalar-remainder
   survivor.

The next meaningful enlargement is not a larger integer box for the same
one-monomial generators.  It is one of:

- a symbolic two-parameter linear combination of commuting quadratic and
  cubic generators;
- a length-two composition of mixed Hamiltonian shears, since a sum of
  generators is not the same canonical word;
- a coisotropic constraint whose conjugate characteristic coordinate is
  also nonlinear;
- a coefficient-dependent repair matrix \(\Lambda(w)\), with polynomial
  critical solution checked before determinant expansion.

These remain computations to design, not consequences of the present
search.
