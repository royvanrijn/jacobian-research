## 1. Confirmed upstream contradiction

The exact q6 child is a globally minimal elliptic K3 with

```text

deg B = 12,
deg Delta = 24,
root lattice = E8+E6,
MW rank = 3,

[[8/3,1/3,-1],

```

`derive_h92_q6_child_q8_marking.sage` constructs an exact rational section
`S` on that child and assigns it MW coordinates

```text

```

whose height in the certified Gram matrix is `24`.

The same exact rational functions have

```text
x(S) denominator = constant * h^2,
y(S) denominator = constant * h^3,
deg h = 46,
gcd(h,Delta)=1.
```

Moreover the standard zero-section parameter `z=-x/y` has a simple zero at
every root of `h`.  Hence

```text
S.O = 46
```

and all 46 intersections occur on smooth fibres.

For an elliptic K3, Shioda's self-height formula gives

```text
<S,S> = 4 + 2(S.O) - sum contr_v(S).
```

The only reducible fibres are E8 and E6.  Bounding the two possible local
corrections by the largest diagonal entries of the inverse Cartan matrices
gives the deliberately loose upper bound

```text
sum contr_v(S) <= 30+6 = 36.
```

Therefore the actual rational section satisfies

```text
<S,S> >= 4 + 2*46 - 36 = 60,
```

contradicting the claimed height `24` by at least `36`.
