## Bug 2: q-regular normalization omitted `Dx`

For the corrected standard-Jacobian section write

```text

y(S)=Ny/Dy,
m=(y+y(S))/(x-x(S)),
p=-y(S)/x(S),
q=(m-p)/h.
```

The old q-regular normalization used

```text
R*h*Dy == Ny mod Nx.
```

But

```text
-p/h = Ny*Dx/(h*Dy*Nx),
```

```text
R*h*Dy == Ny*Dx mod Nx.
```
