## Why this is an unconditional obstruction

Suppose a nonzero section `P` exists over `QQ(u)`.

First, `P` is nontorsion. Over `QQ(u,sqrt(d))` the untwisted base change has
`48 I1` fibres, arithmetic genus four, and no reducible fibres. Its
nonzero-section height is `8+2(P.O)>0`, which excludes torsion. This is the
torsion argument already proved in the
[Tate-cohomology reduction](R17_PRODUCT_TATE_COHOMOLOGY_REDUCTION_2026-09-04.md).

Second, its positive Shioda height `h(P)` is preserved at 131 and 137.
The certified good reductions preserve the four distinct `I0*` fibres and
the 24 distinct `I1` fibres. In odd characteristic greater than three,
their simultaneous resolutions give good smooth surface reduction after
an unramified extension splitting the fibre components. Specialization of
Néron-Severi groups preserves the intersection form and the geometric
trivial lattice generated by `O`, `F`, and the four `D4` configurations.
Orthogonal projection away from this lattice therefore commutes with
specialization. The Shioda height, the negative of the projected square,
is unchanged. If the closure of `P` acquires vertical components, they are
removed by the same projection. Its restriction to the generic special
fibre is the reduction of `P`, defined over `F_p(u)`. Positive height
prevents this reduction from being torsion. This argument does not assume
that affine polynomial coefficients of a section are integral at `p`.

Third, a nonzero reduction would force

\[
1\leq\operatorname{rank}E_p(\mathbf F_p(u))
\leq\operatorname{ord}_{T=1/p}L_p(T)=1.
\]

For a Jacobian over a finite function field, equality of algebraic and
analytic ranks **implies** finiteness of `Sha` and the refined BSD formula.
This is a theorem, not an additional conjecture being assumed here; see
[Ulmer, *Curves and Jacobians over function fields*, Theorems 6.2.6 and
6.3.1](https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/BSDseminar/refs/Ulmer.pdf).
The same source, Proposition 6.3.3, gives square order for `Sha` in this
case, since the elliptic curve has its rational origin and hence all global
and local indices are one.

With heights divided by `log p`, refined BSD reads

\[
C_p=\frac{\operatorname{Reg}(E_p)\,\#\Sha(E_p)}
          {\#E_p(\mathbf F_p(u))_{\rm tors}^{\,2}}.
\]

The differential contributes `p^(1-chi)=p^-3`: it is minimal at the finite
places and has the infinity change of variables of a `chi=4` model.
This accounts for the power of `p` in the definition of `C_p`.

If `G_p` generates the rank-one free group, then `P_p=n_p G_p` modulo
torsion for a nonzero integer `n_p`. Thus

\[
h(P)=h(P_p)=n_p^2\operatorname{Reg}(E_p)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
h(P)/C_p\in\mathbf Q^{\times2}.
\]

The same height cannot have squareclasses 282 and 154. This contradiction
proves rank zero. Notice the order of the argument: a hypothetical section
forces the rank equality that makes the leading-term formula a theorem.
No Tate/BSD conjecture for a rank-unknown `chi=4` surface is assumed.
The standard two-reduction height-squareclass method is also illustrated
in [Magma handbook Example H134E5](https://magma.maths.usyd.edu.au/magma/handbook/text/1583).

Already the `p=131` row excludes heights eight and ten separately, since
`v_3(h)=0` for either height but `v_3(C_131)=1`. The two-prime argument
excludes every positive height, so there is no reason to launch the
height-ten solver after the height-eight exclusion.

## Kummer calculation retained from the descent attempt

Before the regulator obstruction, branch-local square characters were
computed on the 17 certified invariant representatives `x(P_i)-theta`.
At the two quadratic characteristic-zero branch places the original cubic
is irreducible, so an `I0*` twist has no rational nonidentity component.
Its identity component is additive and its formal group is uniquely
2-divisible. Consequently the whole local twist group is 2-divisible,
and every twist Kummer class is locally zero there.

The certificate retains a `17 x 17` matrix of actual unit square characters
at residue places over `131,137,151,157,167,173`, together with its inverse
over `F_2`. Its rank is 17. Any invariant Kummer combination locally square
at both characteristic-zero branch places must therefore be zero.

Comparison after adjoining `sqrt(d)` introduces no extra kernel among
square-norm cubic classes: the restriction kernel is generated by `d`,
whose cubic norm is `d^3`, a nonsquare in `QQ(u)`. Thus the calculation
already proved zero intersection of the invariant and twist Kummer spans.
It is a separation certificate, not a complete Selmer calculation; the
rank-zero theorem subsequently makes the entire point-Kummer image zero.
