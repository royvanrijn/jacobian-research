# A bounded Malle-style census in the fixed quintic Keller family

## Status

This is a bounded exact computation, not an asymptotic theorem.  It counts
target presentations and field isomorphism classes separately.  The
height-`10` census is complete for all connected fibers in the declared
target box.  The height-`21` census is complete only for the four exceptional
transitive groups `A_5`, `C_5`, `D_5`, and `F_{20}`; it scans the entire box
but uses necessary exact screens before invoking PARI/GP.

The computation does not estimate a Malle exponent.  A fixed target-height
cutoff makes the discriminant count saturate, and target height and field
discriminant are strongly correlated.  The data instead establish a
reproducible baseline for presentation multiplicity, field discriminants,
signatures, and exceptional-group frequency.

## 1. Counting convention

Write a rational target as a primitive projective quadruple

\[
 [W:P:B:C],\qquad
 (\Pi,B,C)=(P/W,B/W,C/W),
\]

with `W>0` and

\[
 \gcd(W,P,B,C)=1,\qquad
 \max(|W|,|P|,|B|,|C|)\le H.
\]

The exact isomorphism

\[
 (B,C)\longmapsto(-B,-C),\qquad S\longmapsto-S,
\]

is quotiented by retaining `B>0`, or `B=0,C>=0`.  The primitive integral
inverse polynomial is obtained from

\[
 E_{\Pi,B,C}(S)
 =\Pi^5S^5-5\Pi S^3-2BS^2+4S-2C.
\]

If its primitive leading coefficient is `a`, the root is rescaled to
`\alpha=aS`.  This gives a monic integral polynomial for `\alpha`.  PARI/GP
then computes:

1. irreducibility with `polisirreducible`;
2. the transitive group with `polgalois`;
3. the maximal-order field discriminant with `nfinit`;
4. the real-root count with `polsturm`;
5. a reduced defining polynomial with `polredabs`; and
6. the index of the displayed integral generator from the exact square
   quotient of polynomial and field discriminants.

Potential duplicate fields are bucketed by Galois group, field
discriminant, and signature.  Equal reduced polynomials are identified
immediately; distinct reduced polynomials in the same bucket are compared
with `nfisisom`.  No such cross-key collision bucket occurred in either
census, and no repeated field presentation was found.

## 2. Reproduction

The complete height-`10` census is generated by

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/census_fixed_quintic_malle.py --bound 10
```

It used Python `3.14.6` and PARI/GP `2.17.4`, with isolated PARI processes,
an explicit `256M` stack, and adaptive bisection of any slow batch.  Its
output is
[`fixed_quintic_malle_census_h10.json`](artifacts/generated-results/fixed_quintic_malle_census_h10.json),
with SHA-256

```text
c5dde982c1c548bd85777b44721a4ef81f931911acdffb291125f1a6a48aafd0
```

The exceptional-only height-`21` census is generated by

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/census_fixed_quintic_malle.py \
  --bound 21 --exceptional-only
```

Its output is
[`fixed_quintic_malle_exceptional_census_h21.json`](artifacts/generated-results/fixed_quintic_malle_exceptional_census_h21.json),
with SHA-256

```text
e43aba12ccf5be37db4f4327d1538763caebb8722f07f202bba4088565fb1a9f
```

The exceptional screen retains every positive square discriminant and every
nonsquare discriminant passing the declared forty-prime `F_20` cycle screen.
The square condition is necessary for `A_5`, `C_5`, and `D_5`.  The
finite-prime screen is necessary, but not sufficient, for `F_20`; all
survivors are subsequently classified exactly by PARI.  Thus false
positives cost time but do not affect completeness for the four exceptional
groups.

## 3. Complete height-10 result

The full box contains `40,606` primitive targets:

| outcome | presentations | field classes |
|---|---:|---:|
| zero discriminant | 2 | — |
| reducible and squarefree | 2,102 | — |
| `S_5` | 38,500 | 38,500 |
| `A_5` | 1 | 1 |
| `C_5` | 1 | 1 |
| `D_5` | 0 | 0 |
| `F_{20}` | 0 | 0 |

Thus `38,502` targets give connected fibers, of which
`99.994805%` are `S_5`.  No two connected targets in the box present
isomorphic fields.

The `S_5` signature counts are

| real roots | fields |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 13,702 |
| 3 | 18,384 |
| 5 | 6,414 |

The cumulative `S_5` field counts by target height are

| `H` | fields |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 4 |
| 2 | 67 |
| 3 | 347 |
| 4 | 1,027 |
| 5 | 2,586 |
| 6 | 5,055 |
| 7 | 9,675 |

A log-log least-squares fit over `3<=H<=10` gives target-height exponent
`3.929`.  This is close to the `H^4` growth of primitive points in the
four-coordinate projective box.  It is a target-presentation exponent, not
Malle's discriminant exponent.

The absolute `S_5` field discriminants range from `6,793` to
`3,074,454,029,475,076,639,008,352`; their median is
`3,286,466,793,398,025`.  This enormous range at one target-height cutoff
is a concrete reason not to infer a discriminant asymptotic from the raw
height counts.

## 4. Exceptional height-21 result

The exceptional pass scans all `759,358` primitive targets.  Only `27`
survive the necessary screens, and only seven are connected exceptional
fibers:

| group | target `[W:P:B:C]` | field discriminant | real roots |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `A_5` | `[5:5:0:2]` | `1,314,062,500` | 5 |
| `A_5` | `[4:8:16:-1]` | `11,464,996` | 1 |
| `A_5` | `[3:3:4:-18]` | `544,644` | 1 |
| `A_5` | `[5:2:21:-5]` | `100,251,390,625` | 5 |
| `C_5` | `[10:10:0:7]` | `390,625` | 5 |
| `F_{20}` | `[10:5:15:4]` | `11,250,000` | 5 |
| `D_5` | `[10:4:21:-20]` | `63,504,961,000,000` | 5 |

These seven presentations give seven distinct fields.  The first target
heights are `5,10,15,21` for `A_5,C_5,F_{20},D_5`, agreeing with the
independent minimal-height witness search.

## 5. What the pilot establishes

1. Exceptional groups are extremely sparse in projective target height:
   seven connected exceptional presentations occur among `759,358` targets
   through height `21`.
2. Presentation multiplicity is not yet visible: every connected target in
   the complete height-`10` box, and every exceptional target through height
   `21`, gives a new field isomorphism class.
3. Polynomial discriminant is a poor substitute for field discriminant.
   For example, the height-`5` `A_5` presentation has integral-generator
   index `62,500`, while the height-`10` `C_5` presentation has index
   `671,875`.
4. Both `A_5` real signatures already occur in the target box, but the
   sample of four fields is far too small to test its predicted
   `X^{1/2}\log X` behavior.
5. The `F_{20}` and `D_5` samples contain one field each.  They certify
   occurrence and supply exact discriminants, not an exponent estimate.

The next statistically meaningful calculation should count directly on the
known rational `F_{20}` surface and `D_5` curve, with separate parameter
height and field-discriminant cutoffs.  That avoids spending almost all
runtime on generic `S_5` targets and permits the predicted thin-family
scales to be tested over several orders of magnitude.
