## The gains are beyond all supplied images

The original input is a cloud of fourteen divisor/covariant images, from
which eleven independent points were selected. A separate audit proposes
relations using numerical heights with rational coefficient denominators at
most 64. It then checks three relations per fibre by **exact rational group
arithmetic**, and replays those identities without numerical heights.
Together with the independent eleven-point lower bounds, these identities
prove that the subgroup generated by all supplied images has **rank exactly
11** on every one of the twelve fibres. This is a subgroup statement, not a
whole-curve or generic-rank upper bound, and does not assert saturation.

The [relation certificate](../../artifacts/generated-results/elliptic-curves/mestre_parent_calibration_input_span_v1.json)

fourteen images. Separate finite quotients modulo 3 and 5 also return 11

## A structural question before scaling these searches

An [exact geometry audit](../../artifacts/generated-results/elliptic-curves/mestre_parent_fibre_geometry_v1.json)
checks that all six Weierstrass equations have even coefficient polynomials
and descend under `s=T^2`. Their quotient equations have degrees `(4,6)` and
singular-fibre configuration `8 I1 + 2 I2`. They are geometrically rational elliptic
surfaces, so Shioda–Tate gives geometric Mordell–Weil rank `10-2-2=6` over
`Qbar(s)`. This does not bound the K3's rank after the quadratic base change.

On each K3 the two finite I2 fibres lie at rational base values, and the
I4 fibre at infinity is split. Their five nonidentity component classes are
rational. Together with the fibre, zero section, and eleven independent
rational sections, this proves **arithmetic Néron–Severi rank at least 18**.
The exact Néron–Severi lattice, its full rank, and its transcendental
complement are not computed here.

This is a reason to investigate whether these already constructed parents
have more useful fibrations before enlarging their current rank-11 parameter
search. Removing all five component contributions in another fibration could
make more section rank available, but neither such a fibration nor rank 16
is certified. No rootless frame search, coefficient search or different-NS
foundry admission is made; the arithmetic-first foundry gates remain in force.
