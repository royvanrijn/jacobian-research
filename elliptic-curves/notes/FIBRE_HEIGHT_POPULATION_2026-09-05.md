# Changing the fibre population by arithmetic height

This is a bounded population experiment on the fixed anonymous MW16
`a1-fibration-01` / `a1-presentation-01`, and separately on the fixed MW18
cover `07ca9-orbit-08c1e`. Mathematical status remains governed by
[`MATH_STATUS.json`](../../MATH_STATUS.json). The earlier 104-fibre
[sensitivity null](MW16_SENSITIVITY_RECOVERY_2026-09-05.md) is preserved.

**Completed bounded result:** the coordinate changes produce substantially
smaller fibres, but both matched selection arms return zero finite points and
zero new directions. All 468 distinct charts finish. Arithmetic size remains
an unresolved explanation for the earlier null; this pilot does not meet a
criterion of excluding a useful detection advantage.

## Search result

Every arm contains eight curves. There are 96 chart allocations per MW16 arm
and 144 per MW18 arm. All 31 distinct selected curves have certified
independent specialized subgroups of rank 16 or 18, respectively. There are
no subgroup-gate failures, incomplete charts or unresolved discovered points.

| Family | Mean largest minimal coefficient, bits: Nagao → height/cost | Completed worker seconds: Nagao → height/cost | New directions in either arm |

| MW16 | 2,521.4 → 940.0 | 570.45 → 524.04 | 0 |
| MW18 | 3,876.6 → 2,782.5 | 820.94 → 801.30 | 0 |

The observed reductions in summed completed-worker time are 8.1% and 2.4%.
These are descriptive timings of the retained jobs, not randomized estimates
of a causal speed effect or total preparation cost. Both measured direction
rates are zero. The batch executor takes 403.45 wall seconds; this excludes
preparation, earlier retained serial charts and subsequent certificate replay.
Eight curves per arm and an uncalibrated MW18 centre menu cannot support an
equivalence claim. No larger Nagao census follows from this result.

## Protocol

The [frozen protocol](../data/fibre_height_population_v1.json) precedes
prospective detection. For each family, take the union of primitive rational
boxes `|a| <= 6`, `1 <= b <= 6` in the original coordinate and an improved
coordinate. The two projective-infinity entries generated by the shared box
iterator are recorded as outside this implemented finite-coordinate scope.
Singular or undefined section specializations remain explicit failures.

For MW16, if `A(lambda)` has degree eight, first translate by
`c = -A[7]/(8 A[8])`. Write the degree-six and degree-five coefficients of
`A(c+z)/A[8]` as `q2,q3`. Form the rational number with numerator
`gcd(num(q2)^3,num(q3)^2)` and denominator
`lcm(den(q2)^3,den(q3)^2)`. Extract verified sixth-power divisors of its
numerator and denominator using primes below 1000 and exact root tests.
Their quotient is the nonzero scale `s` in `lambda=c+s*z`.
This is a deterministic coordinate recipe, not an assertion of optimality.

For MW18, write its certified conic as `u^2=q(t)`. Clear denominators and
remove verified square content, obtaining `u=k*v` with integral coefficients
in `v^2=q(t)/k^2`. The new coordinate is the secant slope in this normalized
ordinate. If the existing anchored coordinate has slope `m0+L*r`, use
`r=(k*z-m0)/L`. The exact existing `t(r),u(r)` maps and eighteenth section
are evaluated after this substitution. No new conic solubility search is
needed. The anchor is part of the fixed cover definition and is excluded
from prospective outcomes.

Both recipes change the fibres reached by a small box. An existing fibre's
reduced rational j-invariant is unchanged by any Weierstrass isomorphism.

## Selection and search

Before selection, exclude 290 exact j-classes from the pinned calibration
and prior prospective sources. This conservative quarantine also excludes
their rational twists. Among the remaining population, equal j only starts
a duplicate check: collapse a pair only after exact `is_isomorphic` over
`QQ`. Retain the isomorphism and all sampled aliases. No calibration rank or
exceptional point appears in the protocol or scoring inputs.

Both arms select eight classes from the same deduplicated union:

- **Nagao:** the existing prime blocks, three-stage capped Pareto policy,
  and final score ordering, separately on each coordinate box before merging.
  The caps are `16,8,4`; height buckets have width one for this small box.
- **Height and cost:** equal-weight sums of empirical ranks for logarithmic
  j-height, normalized minimal Weierstrass size, and measured chart cost.
  Smaller values rank first; exact metric ties share a rank. Final ties use
  j-height and the candidate identifier. The height-only shortlist is retained
  as a descriptive diagnostic and receives no additional search allocation.

Here `h_j=log2(max(|num(j)|,den(j)))`. On the integral global minimal model,
normalized size is
`log2(max(1,|a1|^12,|a2|^6,|a3|^4,|a4|^3,|a6|^2))/12`.
Exact powers and j-values are retained alongside these floating summaries.
Known coordinate and denominator factors are split by GCD before removing
verified fourth/sixth powers; PARI then computes the global minimal model.
All sections are transported to its integral short model with exact round
trips. Minimization has a three-second cap; failure never means low rank.

Cost is the median of three repetitions of a fixed sample at section indices
zero and one: `metric:16`, height 1000, two seconds per chart. Measurements
include chart construction and worker wall time, so process overhead is part
of this small pilot. Samples use explicit centres and an empty subgroup to
avoid charging unrelated independence calculations to chart cost. Sample
points are quarantined and never admitted as prospective discoveries.
Candidate timing order is fixed by a hash. Measurements are frozen before
selection; timing noise is not regenerated during exact replay.

The detector is the shared
[`PointedQuarticSearch`](POINTED_QUARTIC_SEARCH.md), always `metric:16`,
height 100000 and twenty seconds per chart. MW16 uses all twelve retained
generic maximum-depth centres of its fixed Gram. MW18 uses all eighteen
singleton section centres. This is an MW18 pilot without a transferred
retrospective sensitivity claim. Both arms within each family have identical
centres, normalized-model policy and chart budgets. An overlapping class is
searched once and contributes the same result and attributed worker cost to
both policy summaries; it is not an independent replicate.

This pilot uses the initial generic-centre wave only. The historical 55/55
result also includes adaptive waves and its original model presentations;
it is not a separately replayed sensitivity guarantee for every normalized
presentation in this new experiment.

Every selected subgroup must pass exact finite-reduction independence and
a no-rational-torsion witness. Discovered points are checked on the exact
curve. A new direction requires an independent extension certificate;
ambiguous finite images remain `UNKNOWN`. All charts and candidates are
checkpointed. Timeouts are incomplete, not misses. No descent or enlarged
Nagao census is part of this experiment.

## Evidence and replay

The runner is [fibre_height_population.sage](../cas/fibre_height_population.sage).
The sanitizer is
[freeze_fibre_height_protocol.sage](../cas/freeze_fibre_height_protocol.sage).
Raw preflight and resumable data remain under the ignored local-artifact
directory; these are coordinate/implementation checks, not prospective arms.

```sh
sage -python elliptic-curves/cas/freeze_fibre_height_protocol.sage --check
sage -python -m unittest discover -s elliptic-curves/tests -p test_fibre_height_population.py
sage -python elliptic-curves/cas/fibre_height_population.sage prepare
sage -python elliptic-curves/cas/run_fibre_height_parallel.sage --workers 8
sage -python elliptic-curves/cas/fibre_height_population.sage verify
```

The [bounded executor](../cas/run_fibre_height_parallel.sage) batches exactly
the frozen union of selected chart jobs. It writes a separate execution
manifest with the population hash, executor hash, eight-worker limit and
complete job list, then invokes the unchanged certificate runner on the
validated checkpoints. The runner's `search` command also supports serial
execution at identical per-chart limits. Early serial charts remain retained;
completed charts are reused rather than charged another search allocation.

Preparation and search bind source hashes and reject changed checkpoints.
Exact replay re-evaluates the family and section maps, checks minimality,
deduplication, exclusions, all local scores, frozen arm selection, chart
maps/hits and independence. It uses retained worker transcripts; it does not
independently enumerate the whole search box again. Fresh timing measurements
can change a new experiment's selection and require a new version.

An all-zero result can establish the observed absence of a selection advantage
at these exact budgets. It cannot establish equivalence, exclude a practically
useful advantage, or decide whether arithmetic height explains the older
104-fibre null. This small experiment can therefore finish with the hypothesis
still unresolved.
