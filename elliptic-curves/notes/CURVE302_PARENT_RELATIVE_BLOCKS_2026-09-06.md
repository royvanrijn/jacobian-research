# Curve 302: shared-cover tests after reducing the parent quotient

The stored additive rank-17 candidate has an exact residual quotient
`D/M = Z^14`, where `D` is the subgroup generated by the 31 certified public
points. Its reduced residual classes do **not** share a smaller halving or
pair-governing extension. All 91 pair-cochain central directions are
independent, including after adjoining the parent's halving field. The
candidate also surjects onto the full bad-component product.

This is an exact retrospective computation for a plausible **lattice
candidate**, not a recovered generic parent. The full `E_302(Q)/M`, an
equation carrying M as generic sections, a low-genus carrier, and a shared
solubility mechanism remain **UNKNOWN**. In particular, `D/M = Z^14` embeds
in `E_302(Q)/M`; equality is not certified.

## Candidate and quotient

The available explicit curve-302 candidate is the saturated additive core
in the [height-lattice audit](RECORD_CURVES_28_29_273_302_HEIGHT_LATTICES.md).
Its integrality enrichment motivates testing it, while its failed provenance
calibration prevents identifying it with R17. Other displayed dimension-scan
counts are not parent matrices. No newer curve-302 candidate matrices were
available in the inspected artifacts, and none were manufactured from those
counts. The driver accepts additional primitive matrices in the public basis.

Write C for this core in its stored LLL basis and H for the stored 100-digit
canonical-height matrix. We complete C integrally, obtaining `[C,Q]` of
determinant plus or minus one. On the quotient we reduce the Schur metric

\[
S=Q^tHQ-Q^tHC(C^tHC)^{-1}C^tHQ.
\]

If V is its LLL transform, set

\[
W=QV-C\operatorname{round}\big((C^tHC)^{-1}C^tHQV\big).
\]

This is a numerical choice of short representatives, not exact closest-vector
reduction. The resulting integral matrix B=`[C,W]` has determinant **-1**.
The artifact retains B's inverse and the last fourteen rows pi, with exact
identities `pi C=0`, `pi W=I14`. Thus pi identifies `D/M` with `Z^14`.
The projected basis lengths range approximately from 16.48 to 31.59; these
are projected numerical heights, not the heights of the rational lifts.

The first reduced lift is `Q1=P1-P2+P5`; the full fourteen lifts use between
3 and 17 public basis directions. They are neither `P18,...,P31` nor a
subset selected at the first-17 boundary. Their coordinates, points and
cochains are frozen in the
[input certificate](../../artifacts/generated-results/elliptic-curves/curve302_parent_blocks_inputs_v1.json)
and [result certificate](../../artifacts/generated-results/elliptic-curves/curve302_parent_blocks_v1.json).

The complete good-reduction mod-2 code is recomputed on the public points
through 1000 and has rank 31. The reduced point identities are also replayed

Restriction to C has rank 17 in both layers, and the quotient contributes
14 dimensions. These are exact statements about displayed points; no full

## Why zero CT cannot choose the parent

Let `U=delta(M)` and `A=delta(D)` in `Sel_2(E_302)`. The exact sequence

\[
0\longrightarrow E(\mathbf Q)/2E(\mathbf Q)
\longrightarrow\operatorname{Sel}_2(E)
\longrightarrow\Sha(E)[2]\longrightarrow0
\]

shows that rational Kummer images have zero image in Sha. The induced CT
pairing therefore kills A against **every** Selmer class. It descends through
U at this anchor, and its restriction to `A/U` is zero for any choice of M
inside D. We record this deduction as automatic vanishing, not as an
independent 2-descent or a measured low obstruction.

In a prospective deformation the appropriate object is a specified Selmer
space modulo the transported generic image U. One must first establish
that U remains rational, hence in the CT radical; otherwise CT need not
descend to that quotient. Zero internal pairing of a proposed block is
weaker than annihilation against the full Selmer space, and full radical
membership still allows higher-divisible Sha. Full Selmer dimension is
UNKNOWN here. A quotient of Mordell--Weil groups is not itself a list of
unresolved Selmer covers.

Within the displayed 31-dimensional class space, the parent-relative
accounting is `binomial(17,2)=136` parent entries, `17*14=238` cross entries,
and `binomial(14,2)=91` residual entries. All 465 vanish at the anchor.
In a comparison they cannot simply be discarded: keeping the first 374
zero is necessary for the parent to remain in the radical of this displayed
space, but does not prove that the parent remains rational. Pairings against
additional, currently unknown Selmer classes would still be needed. This is
why a scalar-twist governing calculation cannot replace a deformation that
actually preserves M's sections.

## The field result is stronger than the finite table

On the frozen integral short model, the two-division cubic is irreducible
modulo 31 and its exact rational discriminant is nonsquare. Thus its Galois
group is S3. The recomputed mod-2 independence supplies the other hypothesis
of the [halving-field theorem](../rank-jump/HALVING_FIELDS_AND_BLOCKS.md).
With L=`Q(E[2])` and F_T the joint halving field of a class subspace T,

\[
[F_A:F_U]=4^{14},\qquad F_U\cap F_R=L,
\]

where R is the complementary class space furnished by the frozen W. Its
choice depends on the lifts, whereas the relative degree does not.

For a pair of reduced points `Qi=(xi,yi)`, `Qj=(xj,yj)`, put `c=xj-xi`.
The [rational-pair construction](../rank-jump/EXPLICIT_PAIR_GOVERNING_OCTIC.md)
gives the actual octic

\[
h_{ij}(T)=T^8-4(y_i+y_j)T^6+6c^2(x_i+x_j)T^4
          -4c^3(y_j-y_i)T^2+c^6.
\]

All 91 octics are retained exactly, each with a squarefree reduction and
the expected inert cycle type. Their individual splitting fields have degree
192. Let N be the compositum of F_A and these 91 splitting fields. Then

\[
\boxed{[N:F_A]=2^{91}
=2475880078570760549798248448.}
\]

This follows from the independent-cup/cochain argument of
[Morgan, Propositions 4.5 and 4.13](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.02374v2#S4.SS3).
Here it also has a direct proof. On the translation subgroup `V^31`, with
`V=E[2]`, the corrected pair cochain has coboundary
`e_i cup e_j`. For each pair i<j choose two commuting translations, one
supported only at i and the other only at j, whose Weil pairing is one.
The commutator of their central lifts is one in slot ij and zero in every
other slot. Consequently no nonzero sum of the 91 cup classes is a
coboundary on `V^31 semidirect S3`. A dependence among their central
characters over F_A would make just such a sum a coboundary, a contradiction.
Adding the seventeen parent translation factors cannot change this argument.

This rules out shared **central extensions over F_A**, not every possible
intersection of number fields over Q. The fields share two-torsion data,
and pairs with a common class share its first-level data. Those expected
overlaps do not couple or remove the 91 central slots. The proof applies to
every primitive candidate `M <= D` of rank m with these mod-2 independence
hypotheses: replace 14 by `31-m` and 91 by `binomial(31-m,2)`.

## What the norm and prime probes actually add

The scalars `c_ij` are a finite dictionary associated with the displayed
rational-pair norm construction. For all 4095 pairs of these 91 nonzero
rational scalars, exact integer square-root tests show that `c_ij/c_kl` is
not a rational square. This misses repeated scalar squareclasses after the
frozen reduction; translating by M, changing signs or changing a cochain
gauge can change the dictionary. It is not a global nonexistence result for
coupled norm equations.

For each inert good prime at most 1999, choose square roots of `xi-theta`
in `F_(p^3)` with prescribed norm yi. The governing value is the quadratic
character of `Norm(sqrt(xi-theta)+sqrt(xj-theta))`. There are 105 inert good
primes in range; three are excluded by lift denominators and 25 by pair
x-coordinate collisions. The common 77-prime table contains **7007** values.

Its `77 x 91` binary matrix has rank **77**; all 91 columns are distinct.
The fourteen-dimensional sampled kernel is forced by having only 77 rows
and supplies no global relation. No prime-bound extension was needed to
prove central independence: that is already the preceding exact theorem.
These values are not CT entries on the original fibre, whose rational block
has CT zero. Nor do they measure a frequency of rank jumps. No sampled
prime is asserted to meet the extra local-square and ramification conditions
of a CT twist-comparison theorem.

For independently supplied strict classes, the
[unpointed norm construction](../rank-jump/UNPOINTED_NORM_COCHAINS_AND_THE_DYADIC_BLOCK_SWITCH.md)
is still the right implementation route. Its norm-solvability gate is
automatic under the strictness hypotheses; the discriminating computation
is the local evaluation of the cochain relative to a correct CT baseline.
Using the supplied rational points here avoids an expensive norm solver but
does not turn its success into new evidence of solubility.

## The remaining carrier and parent gate

If a future deformation has full generic rational span M, and one rational
point on a smooth auxiliary curve C is to supply all fourteen directions
through prescribed generic maps to E, the
[elliptic-multiplicity bound](../rank-jump/ONE_AUXILIARY_POINT_HAS_A_MULTIPLICITY_BOUND.md)
requires `rank Hom(J_C,E) >= 14`, hence `genus(C) >= 14`. Thus a single
genus-one or genus-two point cannot explain the whole block under these
hypotheses. A smaller subblock remains an appropriate target.

This is conditional on M being the **full** generic span and on the maps
being prescribed over the generic field. Multiple auxiliary points, covers
of the parameter line, and maps appearing only after specialization are
different cases. No genus bound for all possible curve-302 parents follows.
In particular, this audit supplies no equation on which a meaningful search
for low-genus branch cancellation can yet run.

The next concrete object needed is a family, parameter and section map
realizing one candidate M. With that object, construct residual classes
without reading the exceptional points, calculate their cochains and local
CT matrix including cross-pairings, then test a proposed shared carrier for
solubility and exact independence modulo the specialized M. This is a
parent-relative implementation gate, not another first-17 quotient or raw
cover-count score.
