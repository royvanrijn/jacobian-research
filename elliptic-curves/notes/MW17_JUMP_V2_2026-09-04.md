# MW17-jump-v2: immutable-population quotient-rank evaluation

## Decision

Do not repeat the 121,589,944-parameter `H=10000` scan.  MW17-jump-v2 reads
only existing frozen populations and measures each selected fibre with the
calibrated exact generic-depth-to-adaptive half-lattice detector.  Local and
Nagao values select and order inputs; they do not enter the result ranking.

The frozen campaign has 2,239 candidate addresses:

- all 256 prospective ordinary rows in each of `07ca9`, `08234`, `08f72`,
  `11952`, `074d9`, and `0e80b`, with `07ca9` and `08234` first, the two
  alternate-Q80 classes next, and the remaining published-R17 classes next;
- the first 191 entries of the immutable stored `H=10000` finalist ranking;
- 256 rows from each CRT anchor population, balanced `52/51/51/51/51` over
  full, `C`, `D`, `E`, and `F` lanes and ordered within each lane by weakest
  Nagao block, total score, then sample id.

The older `H=10000` artifact contains 1,000 ranked finalists, not a separate
191-row object.  The campaign therefore binds “the old 191” transparently to
the first 191 stored entries.  It makes no claim that the other 809 entries
were tested or excluded.

The `0e80b` representative is evaluated on its exactly PGL2-equivalent
compiled `103b2` chart using the atlas-certified base map.  This avoids
creating a parallel family population or changing any source parameter.

The later zero-gain rescue arm does not change these 2,239 addresses, their
order, or the exact-gain leaderboard.  It assigns one deterministic hash
bucket out of eight before looking at rescue outcomes and adds exposure only
for assigned fibres whose original 43 charts completed cleanly with exact gain
zero.  It does not repeat any parameter census.

## Stopped result: negative strongest-family signal

The evaluation was stopped on the explicit negative-signal rule after 66
completed fibres, all in the leading `07ca9` block (campaign indices among
0--69; four in-flight indices were not checkpointed).  All 66 workers passed
exact specialization, MW17 independence, and recovered-subgroup
classification.  Their 2,838 initial charts all completed: there were no chart
timeouts and no backend failures.  Every initial gain, and therefore every
measured actual certified quotient-rank gain, was `+0`; every reported rank
lower bound remained 17.  No fibre entered the adaptive phase.

This is a negative signal for the highest-priority slice under the frozen
height-100,000, 43-chart detector.  In particular, repeated large jumps in the
atlas refresh did not transfer to these 66 selected parameters at this budget.
It is not a rank upper bound, a saturation result, or evidence that the
remaining 2,173 frozen candidates have gain zero.  Those candidates remain
immutable and unevaluated.  The four shards were stopped and the reproducible
partial ledger was merged; restarting them now requires a new deliberate
research decision rather than being the default continuation of this run.

## Exact response

For every fibre the runner:

1. exactly specializes and verifies the 17 generic sections;
2. certifies their independence by finite reductions;
3. enumerates all `2^17` generic half-classes and searches the first 43 in
   exact generic-norm order, using specialized canonical height only to choose
   the shortest representative in each class;
4. after any exactly certified initial escape, constructs the deterministic
   mod-2 quotient complement and searches the calibrated 301-chart adaptive
   pool; and, in the separately frozen rescue arm, gives assigned clean-zero
   fibres the next 301 generic classes in seven 43-chart batches, switching
   unused slots to this same quotient-adaptive policy after the first certified
   escape;
5. exactly classifies every returned point before increasing the score.

The sole leaderboard value is

`actual_certified_quotient_rank_gain`,

with associated lower bound

`certified_rank_lower_bound = 17 + actual_certified_quotient_rank_gain`.

The initial 43-chart gain is retained as a diagnostic and is never a candidate
or leaderboard filter.  An initial gain of three or four proceeds to the same
adaptive phase as any other positive gain.  Initial zero is a bounded detector
result, not rejection or a rank upper bound; the curve-544 calibration remains
the explicit warning against that inference.  The rescue arm removes the
structural requirement that a quotient direction already be known before a
treated zero-gain fibre can receive the full 344-chart exposure.  A certified
gain at least 15 writes the global stop sentinel immediately.

## Frozen zero-gain rescue arm

The rescue protocol was frozen after the stopped 66-fibre checkpoint was
known, but its treatment assignment does not use those outcomes.  For every
one of the 2,239 existing sample ids it computes

```text
SHA256("mw17-jump-v2-zero-gain-rescue-v1" || NUL || sample_id) mod 8
```

and assigns bucket zero.  This places 264 immutable candidates in the
one-in-eight arm, including 12 of the 66 currently measured clean zeros.
Eligibility still requires a successful exact base record with gain zero,
43 attempted charts, and no timeout or backend failure; unmeasured and
censored rows wait rather than becoming zeros.

For an eligible fibre, the rescue pool is generic-class ranks 44 through 344
in the already-computed exact `MW17/2MW17` order, disjoint from the original
top 43.  The 301 classes are reranked within that fixed set by specialized
canonical depth and searched in seven batches of 43.  After each batch, every
returned point is checked on the curve and the discovered group is classified
exactly.  The first certified quotient escape ends generic rescue and assigns
all unused slots to the established adaptive quotient policy.  Therefore an
eligible fibre receives exactly 301 additional charts and 344 total whether
or not it bootstraps.

This is a prospective search-budget repair, not a new calibration result: no
rescue chart has been run.  A rescue miss remains bounded.  A nonzero score is
an unconditional rank lower bound for the certified subgroup and does not
depend on completing a Selmer computation.

## Frozen limits and checkpoints

Each chart uses rational `x`-height 100,000, a 15-second wall timeout, and a
1GB PARI stack.  Exact relation classification uses 64-point chunks and a
180-second chunk timeout.  Each fibre runs in a separate worker with a
7,200-second wall envelope and 24GB address-space ceiling.  Live resource
probes established that 8GB fails global minimalization and 16GB censors even
the direct-reduction wrapper.  Those preprocessing routes are no longer part
of the detector; the ceiling remains a conservative fail-closed worker bound.
Resident use remains bounded by the separate 1GB GP stack.  The supervisor
rewrites its chunk after every completed fibre.  Backend failures and timeouts
are censored explicitly; they never become zero gains.

Every fibre is certified and exactly classified on the
p=2-minimal-to-short model.  The established `074d9` path also searches on
that model.  Other compiled atlas charts search on the exactly isomorphic
direct short integral model, and every returned point is transported back and
checked before classification.  A frozen
preflight that selected the apparently smaller p=2 model by coefficient bits
was rejected before measurement: all four first `07ca9` workers exhausted GP
memory during hyperelliptic minimization.  Those diagnostic checkpoint files
are retained only in the ignored local preflight directory and are not inputs
to this campaign.

A second canary isolated global minimalization on the first `07ca9` quartic.
Skipping it and applying `hyperellred` directly made that chart complete in
about 58 milliseconds.  A harder adjacent fibre then showed that reduction
itself could exceed 60 seconds on a 4,835-bit pointed quartic, while the exact
height-100,000 `hyperellratpoints` search on that same raw quartic completed in
20 milliseconds.  MW17-jump-v2 therefore performs no quartic preprocessing:
it searches the exact denominator-cleared pointed model itself.  Every returned
point is checked on the raw quartic, mapped to the elliptic curve, transported
to the certificate model, and subjected to the same exact independence tests.
All preprocessing-bound canaries are quarantined as diagnostics and are not
campaign measurements.

## Claim boundary

Every non-null gain is an exact lower-bound certificate for the subgroup
actually recovered.  A bounded search does not prove saturation, absence of
points, a Selmer bound, or exact Mordell-Weil rank.  The campaign changes
candidate ranking, not the theorem status, until an exact promoted certificate
is separately recorded.  A complete residual Selmer computation can exclude a
candidate only when its certified upper bound lies below 32; incomplete
descent is scheduling information and is not a prerequisite for this bounded
production rescue.
