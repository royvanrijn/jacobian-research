# Four-division ramification cannot supply the missing jump classes

Follow-up: [fourth division of every generic point](GENERIC_FOURTH_DIVISION_HAS_NO_NEW_SELMER_CLASSES.md)
also adds no Selmer class on all sixteen fibres. Its translation group is
maximal; the new standard layers lie in doubles and do not survive in H1.

On every fibre in the frozen sixteen-case panel, the four-division field
contains **exactly one** cubic Kummer direction, and that direction is
**outside the 2-Selmer group**. Thus it supplies zero strict classes and
zero rational Mordell–Weil directions. This includes the fresh retained
rank-27 fibres, their same-family low-gain controls, and historic +12/+14
controls. Exceptional points are not arithmetic inputs.

The conclusion does not assume a full mod-4 Galois image. It follows from
a universal calculation for an S3 cubic and exact local exclusions.
This is an incidence-capacity exclusion for a specified field source,
not a new class-group computation or an explanation of the large jumps.

There is a stronger compositum consequence: adjoining the four-division
field to the entire generic governing compositum still adds **no Selmer
class**. The derivative class below accounts for its sole additional
Kummer direction, and that direction fails local admissibility.

## The only possible four-division Kummer class

Write E: y²=f(x)=x³+Ax+B, K=Q(θ), Δ=disc(f), M=Q(E[2]), and assume
Gal(M/Q)=S3. Let V=E[2]. Define

\[
 \beta=-\Delta f'(\theta),\qquad N_{K/\mathbb Q}(\beta)=\Delta^4.
\]

Let F4=Q(E[4]). The precise statement is

\[
 \boxed{\ker\bigl(H^1(\mathbb Q,V)\longrightarrow H^1(F4,V)\bigr)
             =\langle[\beta]\rangle.}
\tag{1}
\]

The right side can have dimension zero on a general curve. On this panel
the local witnesses prove [β] nonzero, so its dimension is exactly one.

To prove (1), label the roots e1,e2,e3, put dij=ei−ej for i<j, and choose
δ=d12*d13*d23, so δ²=Δ. The root-difference formula gives

    F4 = M(i, sqrt(d12), sqrt(d13), sqrt(d23)).

This is the degree-three case of
[Yelton, Theorem 1(a)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.06190).
Square roots of β's three conjugates are, up to individual signs,

    δ*i*sqrt(d12)*sqrt(d13),
    δ*sqrt(d12)*sqrt(d23),
    δ*i*sqrt(d13)*sqrt(d23).

Their product can be chosen to be Δ². Thus [β] is split by F4 as a
norm-square cubic Kummer class.

Write a sign automorphism over M as (z,a,b,c) in F2⁴, giving its signs
on i, sqrt(d12), sqrt(d13), sqrt(d23). The actual kernel
H=Gal(F4/M) is a subgroup of this space; radical relations can make it
smaller. Its conjugation action is the restriction of the following
root-permutation actions:

    S(z,a,b,c) = (z,z+a,c,b),        S corresponds to (12),
    T(z,a,b,c) = (z,c,z+a,z+b),      T corresponds to a three-cycle.

These satisfy S²=T³=1 and STS=T⁻¹. The restriction of β to H is

\[
 \phi(z,a,b,c)=(z+a+b,\ a+c,\ z+b+c)
       \in\{(u,v,w):u+v+w=0\}\simeq V.
\tag{2}
\]

The three-cycle-fixed subspace C=ker φ has dimension two. Its complementary
subspace D=im(1+T) has dimension two and is the simple standard S3 module;
φ restricts to an isomorphism D→V. Since three is odd, every S3-stable
subgroup H decomposes into its three-cycle-fixed and complementary parts.
The latter is either zero or all of D. Consequently

    Hom_S3(H,V) is zero or the line generated by φ|H.

The normal order-three subgroup of S3 has V-invariants zero and positive
degree cohomology zero. Hence H¹(S3,V)=0. Inflation–restriction injects
H¹(Gal(F4/Q),V) into Hom_S3(H,V). The class β already supplies the only
possible nonzero image. If its restriction vanishes, the same injection
makes β and the whole group zero. This proves (1), including all proper
mod-4 images with the given S3 quotient.

The verifier enumerates all 67 subspaces of the sign space. Exactly six
are stable under S and T; every one has precisely the decomposition
used above. This checks the smaller-image cases as well as the full kernel.

## Adding torsion to all generic governing fields does not help

Let G be the marked generic Kummer subgroup, L_G its joint S4 normal
closure, and N_G the all-generic governing compositum of the
[central governing closure theorem](CENTRAL_GOVERNING_FIELDS_CANNOT_CREATE_THE_JUMP_CLASSES.md).
On a panel fibre, β is outside Selmer while G is inside Selmer. Therefore
β is independent of G. Put

    B = L_(G + <β>),       T_G = N_G * F4.

The kernel Gal(T_G/B) embeds into the product of the central kernel from
N_G/L_G and the kernel ker φ from F4/L_β. It is an elementary abelian
2-group on which every lift of a three-cycle acts trivially. Its maps
to V equivariant under Gal(B/Q) are zero, since a three-cycle has no
nonzero fixed vector on V. The restriction of any cocycle to this kernel
is therefore zero. Inflation gives

\[
 \boxed{\{\text{Kummer classes split by }T_G\}=G+\langle\beta\rangle,
 \qquad \operatorname{Sel}_2(E)\cap H^1(T_G/\mathbb Q,V)=G.}
\tag{3}
\]

Here H¹(T_G/Q,V) is identified with its inflated subgroup of H¹(Q,V).
In particular the strict part remains G∩U. This proof uses the kernel's
trivial three-cycle action, a slightly wider condition than centrality.
The compositum is treated mathematically, not explicitly constructed.

An independent strict block W of dimension r modulo G still requires

\[
             [T_G L_{G+W}:T_G]=4^r.
\tag{4}
\]

Indeed the extension of B obtained from W has kernel V^r; its intersection
with T_G would also be a quotient of a three-cycle-trivial module.
There is no such nonzero quotient of V^r. The new extension is unramified
and split at the strict places over the cubic splitting field; these
properties survive base change.

The already established necessary strict dimensions beyond G are
9,5,6,8,11,4,5 on cases02,04,06,09,13,14,15 respectively. Equation (4)
shows that these same missing dimensions remain missing even after the
torsion field is adjoined to all generic governing fields. These numbers
are consequences of retained rank labels and the independent boundary
bounds, not a point-free construction of those classes. They differ from
the smaller lower bounds *beyond the entire global root-curve pool*.

## What is now excluded, and what remains missing

The tested implication

    new four-division ramification → several new Selmer classes

is false for this panel: the source has at most one Kummer direction,
and that direction is locally inadmissible in every case. Its compositum
with generic governing fields does not repair the problem. The identical
result in the 103b2, compact11952, larger11952 and MW16 high/low comparisons
makes this an exclusion with a capacity proof, not a weak correlation.

The strongest remaining incidence target is still a **new standard S3
summand in the S-split unramified class module of the cubic splitting
field**, outside the retained global root pool. A change in ramification
must be shown to create that summand, rather than merely changing the
torsion representation or a CT obstruction on old classes. Neither the
relevant independent class-group computation nor a sufficient condition
on t is supplied by this turn.

For a subsequent bounded construction experiment, the pass condition
should remain an explicit norm-square α in K_t, its unramified/S-split
certificate, and its independence from the allowed old pool. A mere
increase in division-field degree cannot pass this gate. Three extra
strict independent classes modulo G would force at least one outside
the global pool whose excess capacity is at most two. Only after such
incidence classes exist should a CT calculation or simultaneous-solubility
carrier be used to decide whether they are rational rather than Sha.

Feature labels: both the field-capacity bound and the local-admissibility
test are **incidence** gates for potential Selmer directions. The latter
excludes Selmer membership before the rational-versus-Sha question arises.
Global **solubility** of the missing classes remains unknown. No
**visibility** statistic or prospective rank selector is proposed.
