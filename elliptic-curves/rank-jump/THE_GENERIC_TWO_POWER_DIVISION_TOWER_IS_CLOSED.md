# The entire generic two-power division tower is closed to new Selmer classes

The level-four exclusion extends to **every level** on all sixteen frozen
fibres, without computing their actual higher torsion images. If G is the
marked generic Kummer subgroup and D_G is the field generated by all
2-power torsion and all 2-power preimages of its basis points, then

    ker(H1(Q,E[2]) -> H1(D_G,E[2])) = G + <beta>,
    Sel2(E) intersect ker(res_D_G) = G.

Here beta=-disc(f)*f'(theta) is the retained derivative class, nonzero and
outside Selmer on every row. No exceptional point is an input. This is a
capacity theorem for the specified tower, not a construction of the
missing unramified block.

The key new step is a uniform finite-group bound:

    dim_F2 H1(Gamma,F2^2) <= 1

for every subgroup Gamma of GL2(Z/2^n) surjecting onto GL2(F2)=S3, with
the natural mod-two action. The proof below handles all n and proper
images, rather than extrapolating from computations on full linear groups.

## The standard part of the congruence filtration

Let V=F2^2 and M=M2(F2), with S3 acting on M by conjugation. For a
three-cycle tau, the central idempotents in F2[S3] are

    e0=1+tau+tau²,       e1=tau+tau².

The nontrivial block e1 F2[S3] is M2(F2); thus every module in this block
is a direct sum of the simple standard V. The
[earlier standard-module proof](STRICT_CLASS_CREATION_IS_A_STANDARD_S3_BLOCK.md)
establishes this algebra statement explicitly. For the conjugation module
M, D=e1 M is one copy of V, while e0 M is the two-dimensional centralizer
of tau. Every A in D has trace zero. Cayley–Hamilton in characteristic two
therefore gives A²=det(A)I, whence

    e1(A+A²)=A  for A in D.

Now let K be the kernel of Gamma -> S3 and put

    K_r=Gamma intersect (1+2^r M2(Z/2^n)),   1<=r<=n,
    K_1=K,                                K_n=1.

Mapping 1+2^r A to A mod2 identifies K_r/K_(r+1) with an S3-stable
subspace M_r of M. Its e1 part is either zero or all of D. This assertion
uses conjugation by the actual Gamma; its action on each grade factors
through the full S3 quotient regardless of the higher linear image.

Let r0 be the first depth where e1 M_r is nonzero, if any. Squaring gives

    (1+2A)² = 1+4(A+A²) mod8,
    (1+2^r A)² = 1+2^(r+1) A mod2^(r+2),  r>=2.

For every depth before the final grade, the standard projection of the
squares of lifts of D therefore spans D in the next grade. The first
identity is essential: it is not legitimate to use the r>=2 formula at
r=1. Trace zero makes its standard projection work nonetheless.

Consequently D appears in every subsequent grade after r0, but is supplied
there by squares from the preceding grade.

## Why at most one standard component survives the Frattini quotient

The finite 2-group K has Frattini subgroup Phi(K)=K²[K,K]. Its elementary
abelian quotient Q=K/Phi(K) has an S3 action, since inner conjugation by K
acts trivially on it. Give Q the filtration induced by K_r.

Before r0 the graded pieces have no standard component. At r0 there is
at most one. After r0 every standard component of K_r/K_(r+1) lies in
the image of Phi(K), by the squaring calculation, so none survives in
the corresponding grade of Q. Projection by e1 is exact and its algebra
block is semisimple. Adding dimensions over the finite filtration proves

    dim_F2 e1 Q <= 2,
    dim_F2 Hom_S3(Q,V) <= 1.

This statement does not assert that the whole Frattini quotient has
dimension at most two; its trivial-block part may be larger.

Since H1(S3,V)=0, inflation–restriction injects H1(Gamma,V) into
Hom_S3(K,V)=Hom_S3(Q,V). This proves the uniform bound. If no standard
grade occurs, H1 is zero. The bound applies also when the first standard
grade is deeper than level four; it does not universally identify its
generator with the derivative class.

## Applying the bound to the actual torsion fields

Write F_n=Q(E[2^n]) and Gamma_n=Gal(F_n/Q). Every panel row has full S3
image. Its [retained four-torsion certificate](FOUR_DIVISION_RAMIFICATION_CANNOT_SUPPLY_THE_JUMP.md)
proves that beta is nonzero, split by F_2, and locally outside Selmer.
Inflation from Gamma_2 into Gamma_n is injective for every n>=2.
The uniform upper bound is already attained by beta, so

    ker(H1(Q,V) -> H1(F_n,V)) = <beta>  for every n>=2.

Thus arbitrary changes in higher torsion images cannot add another cubic
Kummer direction on these fibres. Actual mod-eight and higher images
remain uncomputed; they are no longer a missing hypothesis for this
particular exclusion.

## Adjoining every division level of every generic point

Let P_1,...,P_m be the marked generic basis and

    D_(G,n)=Q(E[2^n], [2^n]^-1 P_i for every i).

Because beta is outside Selmer and G is inside it, restriction to F_n
is injective on G. The proof in the
[fourth-division note](GENERIC_FOURTH_DIVISION_HAS_NO_NEW_SELMER_CLASSES.md)
then applies at every n: the translation subgroup T in (Z/2^n)^(2m)
surjects modulo two onto V^m. Hence T+2A=A, for A=(Z/2^n)^(2m), and
iteration in the quotient A/T gives A/T=2^n(A/T)=0. Therefore

    Gal(D_(G,n)/F_n)=(Z/2^n)^(2m),
    [D_(G,n):F_n]=2^(2nm),
    Gal(D_(G,n)/Q)= (Z/2^n)^(2m) semidirect Gamma_n.

For this affine group, cocycles split into a torsion-group cocycle and
an equivariant homomorphism from the translations. Such homomorphisms
factor through reduction modulo two and supply exactly the m generic
coordinate classes. Thus for every n>=2,

    ker(res_D_(G,n))=G+<beta>,       Sel2(E) intersect ker(res_D_(G,n))=G.

The fields are nested. A class in H1(Q,V) has a finite splitting field;
if it is split by their union, that finite field lies in some finite
stage. Taking the union therefore gives the theorem in the opening
paragraph, with no limiting or closure assumption omitted.

Adjoining the all-generic central governing compositum N_G does not alter
the result. Since D_G contains L_G, the kernel after this base change is
a subgroup of Gal(N_G/L_G), central under conjugation over Q. The
[central closure theorem](CENTRAL_GOVERNING_FIELDS_CANNOT_CREATE_THE_JUMP_CLASSES.md)
again adds no H1 direction.

## Checks on proper images and the retained panel

The [protocol](GENERIC_TWO_POWER_TOWER_PROTOCOL.json) freezes five small
proper-image controls. Generate Gamma by integral lifts of S3 and
1+2^r A for a basis A of D. These deliberately include standard components
first appearing after level four:

| Modulus | First standard depth r | Group order | dim H1(Gamma,V) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 | 1 | 384 | 1 |
| 8 | 2 | 24 | 1 |
| 16 | 1 | 6144 | 1 |
| 16 | 2 | 384 | 1 |
| 16 | 3 | 24 | 1 |

For r>=2 the mod-four image is just the lifted S3 and has zero H1.
These controls show why the panel's *nonzero level-four* hypothesis is
needed to identify the surviving direction; the universal dimension
bound alone would not make it the derivative.

The [worker certificate](../../artifacts/generated-results/elliptic-curves/rank_jump_generic_two_power_tower_v1.json)
checks all 67 subspaces of M2(F2), of which six are S3-stable; the standard
square projection; and all 256 lowest-depth lifts A modulo four. It
computes generator cocycles by a breadth-first Cayley graph.
The [independent verifier](../../artifacts/generated-results/elliptic-curves/rank_jump_generic_two_power_tower_verification_v1.json)
uses Sage matrix conjugation and subspace enumeration, then different
depth-first word representatives and Sage GF2 kernels for the proper
groups. Both explicit coboundaries are checked. All results agree.
The arbitrary-depth conclusion rests on the filtration proof, not on
finite sampling. A verifier-only Sage-integer serialization failure was
fixed before the first valid verification artifact; its partial output
is preserved under the rank-jump local work directory.

The sixteen-row application retains MW17 on thirteen rows and MW16 on
three. It gives split Kummer dimensions 18 and 17 respectively, split
Selmer dimensions 17 and 16, and **zero additional Selmer dimension in
the whole tower** on every high and low row. Inputs are the already
certified generic-only and derivative data, bound by hashes. No new point,
class-group, integer-factorization or actual higher-image computation ran.

## What this resolves and what remains open

This closes the entire inherited two-power division route, including
proper higher images and central governing extensions. More computations
at mod-eight, mod-sixteen or later levels are not needed to seek additional
Selmer classes from this source on the frozen panel.

The missing jump block must instead come from independent arithmetic
extensions outside this tower. It must survive the elementary abelian
quotient, be unramified with the required S splitting, and then satisfy
the separate rational-solubility condition. For example, the retained
103b2 high fibre still needs its nine strict directions outside G; none
is supplied by adjoining arbitrarily deep division data for G.

No condition on t producing those external classes has been derived.
The generic ideal/S-unit constructor remains uncompleted, and the chord
dictionary's private ramification remains an obstruction. No visibility
feature or candidate-selection policy follows from this theorem.
