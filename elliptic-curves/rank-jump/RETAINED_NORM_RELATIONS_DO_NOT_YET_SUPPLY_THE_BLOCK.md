# Principal norm relations are not yet additional strict classes

The fixed 512-box relation batch for MW16-05 at t=3/17 supplies **296
principal elements**, but their norm projections have an injective
outside-S valuation-parity map of rank **296**. No nonempty product of
these projections is unramified outside S, even after generic correction.
Thus this entire retained dictionary supplies **zero additional strict
classes** on the [known-soluble +6 reference](CONDITIONALLY_EXACT_SOLUBLE_BLOCK_REFERENCE.md).

A separate early-wave survivor does cancel its outside-S ramification. An
exact square-root identity proves that it is already in the generic Kummer
subgroup. These are two different failures: the first dictionary fails the
unramifiedness gate; the later hit fails independence modulo the generic
subgroup. Neither is evidence for a rational solubility obstruction or Sha.

For each principal element alpha_j put

\[
 \pi_j=N(\alpha_j)\alpha_j,\qquad N(\pi_j)=N(\alpha_j)^4.
\]

If P lies over p with ramification index e_P, its valuation is exactly

\[
 v_P(\pi_j)=v_P(\alpha_j)+e_P
             \sum_{Q\mid p}f_Q v_Q(\alpha_j).
\tag{1}
\]

The sum in (1) includes **every** prime over p, including primes where
alpha_j is a unit. Discarding those zero original coordinates would miss
ramification introduced by the scalar norm factor.

We verify each principal ideal as a product of its retained prime powers,
then form the parity matrix using all primes outside S. The result is

\[
 \mathbf F_2^{296}\longrightarrow
 \bigoplus_{P\notin S_K}\mathbf F_2,
 \qquad \dim\ker=0.
\tag{2}
\]

All generic point classes are unramified outside S because these are odd
good-reduction places. Adding a generic class cannot cancel a nonzero
valuation vector in (2). Consequently the strict image of the group
generated by G and this dictionary is exactly G∩U.

Allowing arbitrary rational scalar corrections does not rescue the same
principal-element dictionary. In a cubic field, if r times a product of
alpha_j has square norm, then in Q*/Q*² its scalar class must equal the
product of N(alpha_j). Its field squareclass is therefore the corresponding
product of pi_j. Equation (2) already excludes it.

This is a **construction-capacity obstruction**, not a bound on the field's
S-class group. The reference is already known to have at least six extra
strict rational directions, independently of the conditional upper bound.
The dictionary fails to reach those existing directions.

## What this changes

The class-group upper-bound programme and the present class-construction
programme need different outputs from norm relations. Independent principal
rows help reduce a formal ideal-class presentation. To construct unramified
Kummer classes, products must instead **cancel all valuation parity**. Even
a surviving parity relation may be a square or an inherited class, as (3)
demonstrates. High relation yield alone measures neither additional Selmer
incidence nor rational solubility.

The next useful retained-data test is therefore:

1. Audit which early relation pools were selected without exceptional
   classes or point-derived rows; preserve any outcome-dependent stopping
   rule as a disclosed retrospective selection condition.
2. Deduplicate principal elements, retain complete outside coordinates,
   and calculate valuation-parity kernels. Do not run a new norm campaign.
3. Quotient surviving global squareclasses by G with exact certificates,
   then impose all remaining S-local conditions. A dimension beyond G is
   the first relevant incidence endpoint.
4. Only for such additional classes compute CT and the remaining rational
   solubility obstruction. A generic survivor must not count as progress
   toward an additional soluble block.

For the original matched103b2 high/low pair, the same construction must be
available on both fields before comparison. This one-fibre calibration
does not establish a discriminator or a condition on t. It identifies why
a seemingly productive class-relation input still fails to supply the
needed block. No visibility feature or scoring change is proposed.
