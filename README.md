# From one collision to marked-root Keller maps

A separate [elliptic-curve rank/conductor research programme](elliptic-curves/README.md)
develops a reproducible Hensel--CRT--rational-lattice search without changing
the mathematical scope of the Keller-map results below. It now independently
replays the 2026 public curve with 30 rationally independent points, proving
the programme's rank-at-least-30 target while leaving its separate
low-conductor rank-at-least-21 target open.

This repository verifies a three-dimensional polynomial Keller map with a
three-point collision, explains it through a tangent-map normal form, and
develops weighted, cancellation, decorated-normalization, Hurwitz, and
rank-two symplectic consequences.  Separately, it records Mondello's external
characteristic-two plane theorem over \(\overline{\mathbb F}_2\), replays
its preserved-fibre proof, and proves a repository corollary over every
characteristic-two field.  These etale noninjective plane maps have separable
generic degree three and do not address the characteristic-zero plane
Jacobian conjecture.  In generic degree `N>=4`, the coarse
decorated normalization already gives an `(N-3)`-dimensional family of stable
classes.  Adding one affine root sheet generically recovers the seed exactly.
The same parameters yield four-real-Gaussian witnesses with an optimal
`(N-3)`-moment algebraic fingerprint; in those moment coordinates, the full
affine nonsurjective partition-lattice geometry and every other functorially
defined seed locus are preserved in their native categories.  In degree six,
the two vertical Ritt loci become explicit sextic moment hypersurfaces, one
coinciding with the all-double nonsurjective component.  The
[Moment--Prony theorem](extended-geometry/MOMENT_PRONY_DETERMINANTAL_GEOMETRY.md)
now derives every omitted partition and every vertical Ritt stratum from
intrinsic log-Hessenberg, saturated Christoffel--Hankel/subresultant, or
Krylov minors in those optimal moments; degree-five, degree-six, and
degree-eight ideals are checked scheme-theoretically.  The required
saturation removes the wrong marked-Fitting collision thickness while
retaining the genuine intersection nilpotents.  The same parameters
also yield inequivalent filtered Weyl endomorphisms.  The nonsurjective seed locus has
dimension `floor(N/2)-1`: quartics are generically exceptional, whereas
generic seeds of every degree `N>=5` are surjective.
The [exact degree-spectrum corollary](verified/GEOMETRIC_DEGREE_SPECTRUM.md)
shows that the geometric degrees of noninvertible Keller maps of
`A^3_C -> A^3_C` are precisely `3,4,5,...`.
The [atomic-spectrum theorem](verified/ETALE_MONOID_ATOMIC_SPECTRUM.md)
shows that every one of those degrees admits absolutely and stably atomic
maps.  It also gives a negative answer to quasi-torus generation: the
explicit ungraded quartic is outside the monoid generated by polynomial
automorphisms and every Keller map having a positive-dimensional
algebraic-torus-equivariant left--right representative.  The degreewise
classification endpoint is now the
[stable Keller-moduli problem](extended-geometry/STABLE_KELLER_MODULI_PROBLEM.md):
construct the moduli stack of geometric-degree-`N` Keller maps modulo stable
polynomial left--right equivalence, then determine its dimension,
irreducible components, and componentwise generic monodromy.  The weighted
`(N-3)`-dimensional family now gives a represented reduced coarse core:
after shrinking it is locally closed in the bounded coefficient scheme,
its unmarked rerooting cover has monodromy `S_(N-2)`, and the intrinsic
affine root sheet makes its stable-class map faithful on reduced families.
This is a test chart inside the hypothetical object, not a component theorem
or a count of all classes.  The next gate is the simultaneous-boundary
deformation stack and normal cone along this core, beginning with the
equinormalization stratum of the biprojective universal graph.  In the
quartic chart, two exact rational sections have reduced length `28`, while
the full graph over `F_32003` has modular multidegrees `(1,12,28,4)` and
`38` minimal generators.  Its reduced source base is a coordinate triangle
with generic ideal orders `(6,4,2)`, stratifying normalization into three
generic-line and three vertex problems.  This supplies the first graph-cycle
calibration.  Exact quadratic transforms now determine the normalized ideals
on all three open sides, with closure-defect lengths `(9,6,1)`.  The vertex
problem reduces to six compact Newton facets and six generically
principalized colors.  The finite vertex-corner atlas is now exact: its
eight maximal Newton cones are base-point free, the only residual color
conductors are one rational and one quadratic cusp contribution of total
delta `3`, and the colored `V_23` overlaps glue smoothly.  The side-center
curves are now compactified and matched as well: their only nontrivial
packets have bidegrees `(4,4)`, `(6,2)`, `(6,2)`, endpoint deltas `(6,3,3)`,
and explicit Kummer/vertex transitions.  The remaining exact task is to
assemble these curves inside the rational exceptional surfaces and compute
one global conductor pushout and bigraded Hilbert polynomial, not another
family construction.
Unrestricted cubic-class uniqueness is false: fiber-invisible cubic gauge
lifts give infinitely many
stable atomic classes with any prescribed complete cubic fiber, but this
alone determines neither the cubic moduli dimension nor its number of
components.  Degree arithmetic
gives a further exact dichotomy: every map is forced atomic in degrees
\(p\), \(2p\), and \(8\) (with \(p\) prime and degree at least three),
while a decomposable nonunit exists exactly in degrees \(ab\) with
\(a,b\ge3\); the first such degree is \(9\).

[coefficient-space corollary](extended-geometry/JELONEK_COEFFICIENT_COMPONENTS.md):

determinant-one coefficient scheme containing `F_N` is generically
noninvertible and has dimension at least eight.  This is Jelonek's theorem
applied to the family, not a new component theorem here.  Exact full-box
linearization now gives tangent dimensions `58,88,123` at `F_4,F_5,F_6`,
and explicit raw quadratic classes show that all three bounded coefficient
schemes fail formal smoothness there.  Nevertheless the unrestricted
infinitesimal left--right quotient is identically zero in every degree:
every tangent is source-trivial over the dual numbers.  The coefficient
component, filtered-contact, and decorated-normalization questions are
therefore organized as one programme, `OP-CCDM`: **local coefficient
components versus decorated stable moduli**.  Ordinary tangent quotients
cannot see the `N-3` stable parameters; the first meaningful comparison must
be filtered, boundary-decorated, or formal-to-algebraic.

## The foundational map in two forms

The same counterexample appears naturally in two polynomial coordinate
systems.  The first is the compact expanded form used in the original
announcement.  Put `u=1+xy` and define

\[
F(x,y,z)=\left(
u^3z+y^2u(4+3xy),
y+3xu^2z+3xy^2(4+3xy),
2x-3x^2y-x^3z
\right).
\]

On `x!=0`, set `t=y+1/x` and `r=2/x`.  For target coordinates `(a,b,c)`,

\[
a=t^2+rt/2-ct^3,\qquad b=r+4t-3ct^2.
\]

The two coordinate Jacobians give

\[
\det\frac{\partial(a,b,c)}{\partial(t,r,c)}
\det\frac{\partial(t,r,c)}{\partial(x,y,z)}
=\frac r2(-2x)=-2,
\]

so `det DF=-2` polynomially everywhere.  Exact substitution gives

\[
F(0,0,-1/4)=F(1,-3/2,13/2)=F(-1,3/2,13/2)=(-1/4,0,0).
\]

The second is the normalized factorization form.  For coordinates `(a,y,z)`,
set

\[
\begin{aligned}
b&=1+ay,\\
c&=1-\frac32ay+a^2z,\\
d&=\frac12y-az+\frac32ay^2-a^2yz,\\
e&=-2z+4y^2-4ayz+3ay^3-2a^2y^2z
\end{aligned}
\]

and define

\[
G(a,y,z)=(ac,\ ae+bd,\ be).
\]

Then `det DG=-1`, and

\[
\begin{aligned}
G(0,0,1/2)
&=G(1,-3/2,-13)\\
&=G(-1,3/2,-13)
=(0,0,-1/4).
\end{aligned}
\]

These are two forms of the same map, not two inequivalent counterexamples.
Indeed, with

\[
A(x,y,z)=(x,y,-z/2),\qquad B(p,q,r)=(r,2q,2p),
\]

one has \(F=B\circ G\circ A\).  The expanded form makes the low-degree
formula and collision immediate; the factorization form exposes the
coprime linear-times-quadratic construction and its resultant.

## Mondello's characteristic-two plane theorem and repository extensions

### External theorem and proof architecture

Romy Mondello proves in [*A Dimension-Two Counterexample to the Separable
Jacobian Conjecture in Characteristic Two*,
arXiv:2608.02634v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02634) that, over
\(k=\overline{\mathbb F}_2\), the plane map

\[
\begin{aligned}
P&=x+x^2y+x^4+x^6y^2,\\
Q&=y+x^5+x^6y+x^7y^2+x^8y^3.
\end{aligned}
\]

has \(\det JF=1\), is etale, and sends the three distinct points
\((0,1),(1,0),(1,1)\) to \((0,1)\).  Mondello proves that \(P,Q\) are
algebraically independent, \([k(x,y):k(P,Q)]=3\), the extension is separable,
and the geometric generic fibre has three points.  Thus HKM2 is an
**external theorem with an internal exact replay**, not a repository theorem.

The reusable proof mechanism is explicit.  Put

\[
r=1+xy,\qquad u=1+x^3r,\qquad w=ru^2.
\]

Then \(w\) satisfies

\[
H(T)=T^3+T^2+(PQ+P^3)T+P^3,
\]

and the source is recovered by

\[
x=\frac{w+1}{Q},\qquad
r=\frac{P^2}{x^2w},\qquad
y=\frac{r+1}{x}.
\]

The decisive step is **actual-target irreducibility**: \(H\) is proved
irreducible over the literal subfield \(k(P,Q)\), after algebraic
independence identifies it with the appropriate rational function field;
irreducibility is not inferred from a generic symbolic cubic.  This is a
useful template for other hidden-root constructions.

Finally, the kernel derivation on the `b=0` fiber is not locally nilpotent,
and in fact has no polynomial slice. Therefore a normalizer's target-`b`
component cannot have a polynomial Poisson mate on its zero fiber. It cannot
be an elementary coordinate in any source variable, excluding all
twenty-six displayed corrections and every elementary `b`-moving repair. A
surviving construction needs a genuinely non-elementary, no-slice coordinate
or a different target polarization. This condition is attainable: the tame
coordinates `F_k=a+c^k*(b+a*d)` have no polynomial Poisson mate for every
`k>=1`, beginning with a cubic example. Conversely every coordinate of degree
at most two has a linear Poisson mate. On their invariant curves the tame
family has pole order and transverse weight `(k,k)`, while the R21 kernel has
the split signature `(2,3)`. The complete polynomial constant rings make
this mismatch intrinsic: the tame row has Danielewski exponent `k`, while
R21 has exponent three; the generic time divisor simultaneously forces
`k=2`. Hence the entire family is excluded. The closest row `k=2` is exactly
conjugate to R21 after localizing the invariant `I`, but the conjugating
polynomial map has Jacobian `-I/5184`. The remaining construction problem is
therefore to correct the affine modification over `I=0` without changing
the generic pole order. This correction is now achieved on the complete
zero hypersurface. The degree-seven Bezout coordinate
`F=A*(Q+G^2/2)-B*P`, with `A=G*C^4/6` and `B=G^2*C^3/3-1`, belongs to an
explicit determinant-one coordinate system and pulls the full R21 `b=0`
two-form back to the restricted standard form. Its first transverse equation
also has a polynomial divergence-free solution: the apparent primitive
`-2/C` is polynomialized by the invariant correction `-I^2*L/144`. The
resulting normal vector is not locally nilpotent, so this is exact fiber
admission plus a compatible first jet, not yet a finite four-dimensional
normalizer. The canonical `F^2` calculation is also exact: every
tangent--tangent residual vanishes, and Bezout removes the three remaining
normal form coefficients. The corresponding volume equation reduces to
`D(lambda)=-div(Z)`. Its weight-forced coefficient recurrence reaches
`p_5=-17/5388768` and then remains nonzero forever, so the canonical minimal
first jet has no polynomial second completion. The invariant freedom is now
closed as well: the obstruction is affine-linear in an arbitrary first-jet

`I^2*U*K[S]`, and the top coefficient for `I^2*U*S^m` is nonzero for every

fiber admission instead of climbing indefinitely through homogeneous jets.

fact, independent translations

form. In
general, the new obstruction is the well-defined class

differ by `lambda*D` and change the divergence by `D(lambda)`.

admission would force this algebra to be the whole coordinate ring. The exact
canonical screen proves that `X_P`, `X_Q`, and `X_e` are not locally nilpotent,

subalgebra `K[P,Q,R]`, and separately inside `K[e,R]`. Classifying Hamiltonian
LNDs outside these subalgebras initially remained open. Factorial closure now
extends the classification to the full stable-independent source ring. If
`f in K[x,y,z]` has locally nilpotent Hamiltonian, then its restriction fixes
both factors of `R=x*S`. Over `K(x)`, a nonzero LND would force the generic

has four simple branch points and genus one. Consequently
`{f in K[x,y,z]: X_f is LND}=K[R]`. Every still-missing Hamiltonian
`G_a`-direction would therefore have to have positive `e`-degree. The
`e`-filtration closes this last gate. For `f=sum(e^j*f_j)`, the leading
graded derivation either induces a forbidden nonzero LND of `K[x,y,z]`
fixing `R`, or forces `f_m=h(R)`. In the latter case, the next graded layer

`R` a slice. The slice theorem would make `(R)` prime, contradicting
`R=x*S`. Thus positive `e`-degree is impossible and the full
Hamiltonian-LND locus is exactly `K[R]`. Hence the Hamiltonian LND-generator
algebra is the proper subalgebra `K[R]` and its kernel intersection is
`K[x,y,z]`. A standard Darboux chart would instead provide four locally
nilpotent coordinate Hamiltonians generating the whole ring. Therefore
`Omega_21` is not polynomially symplectomorphic to the standard form. This
settles global polynomial Darboux admission for `R21`. The first quantization

principal symbols would all lie in `K[R]` and could not generate the source
Poisson algebra. Thus the natural centralizer and every strict filtered PBW
bridge are excluded. An essentially filtration-collapsing, non-PBW
identification remains open, so `(DC_2)` is not settled.
