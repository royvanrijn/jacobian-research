# AI Math Research

Mathematical research by Roy van Rijn, developed with AI research assistants, especially OpenAI Codex. This repository brings together written proofs, exact computations and Lean formalizations around the Jacobian Conjecture, polynomial maps, moment-vanishing problems and additive number theory.

Current PDFs and sources are linked below. The [paper catalogue](results/) records submissions and public deposits.

## Released and submitted papers

| Paper | Main result | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **The Strong Factorial Conjecture for Univariate Binomials** | Proves that two consecutive positive factorial moments of a nonzero univariate binomial cannot both vanish over a characteristic-zero field. | [Paper and verification](results/strong-factorial-binomials/) · Hexagon processing |
| **Cubic sphere moments and the two-pair Image problem** | Classifies pure cubic sphere phases and proves the sharp two-pair cubic Image cutoff and balanced degree-four failure boundary. | [Paper and verification](results/cubic-sphere-image/) · Hexagon under review |
| **The Mathieu Property over Fields of Characteristic Zero: Classification and Ground-Field Obstructions** | Classifies normalized Reynolds kernels for split reductive actions in characteristic zero and extends the classification over real closed fields and on compact homogeneous spaces. | [Paper and verification](results/mathieu-ground-fields/) · Hexagon under review |
| **The Mathieu property for quadratic complements** | Proves the sharp dimension cutoff for the normalized trace, sharp witness bounds and a finite test for all multipliers. | [Paper and verification](results/mathieu-quadratic-complements/) · Hexagon under review |
| **Additive basis thresholds for random prime semigroups** | Determines the least additive basis order and the critical cofiniteness threshold, with probability strictly between zero and one at equality. | [Paper and verification](results/random-prime-semigroups/) · Hexagon under review |
| **Pointwise Correlations of Quadratic Form Values and Splitting Semigroups** | CK/Smilansky pair asymptotics, genus-invariant primitive value counts in one quadratic field and target-uniform splitting-semigroup sums. | [Paper and verification](results/pointwise-binary-splitting-semigroups/) · [Hexagon v1](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00672v1) |
| **Exact Sumsets of Primitive Quadratic Norms** | Exact primitive norm sumsets at discriminants -3, -7, -8 and -11, with finite certificates and an independent classical proof route. | [Paper and verification](results/eisenstein-restricted-prime-sums/) · [Hexagon v1](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00673v1) |
| **The Dimension-Degree Boundary for Gaussian Moment Vanishing** | Classifies pure ternary cubics and establishes the sharp mixed-moment cutoff and the universal dimension–degree boundary. | [Paper and verification](results/gaussian-boundary/) |
| **Prime Knots with Augmentation-Rank Gaps at Unbounded Bridge Number** | Constructs hyperbolic prime knots with an augmentation-rank gap at bridge numbers five and six and every sufficiently large bridge number. | [Paper and verification](results/prime-knot-augmentation-gaps/) |
| **Pointwise representation counts for Eisenstein restricted sums** | Gives two-sided pointwise counts in prescribed summand windows for ordinary and mixed sums. | [Paper and verification](results/eisenstein-pointwise-counts/) |
| **Counterexamples to the Mathieu Property: From Explicit Quotient Constructions to a General Classification** | Classifies the reductive homogeneous spaces with the Mathieu property and constructs fixed-multiplier counterexamples. | [Paper and verification](results/mathieu-reductive-homogeneous-spaces/) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/24310836) · [Hexagon](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00556v1) |
| **The Generalized Vanishing Conjecture: The Two-Variable Theorem and the First Failing Dimension** | Proves GVC in two variables and constructs a homogeneous failure in dimension three. | [Paper and verification](results/generalized-vanishing-two-variables/) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/21803325) · [Hexagon](https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00580v2) |
| **A Filtered Frobenius Proof of the Two-Variable Gaussian Moments Conjecture** | An alternative proof of the known binary classification and sharp cutoff; Lean coverage has two explicit mathematical inputs. | [Paper and verification](results/gaussian-moments-two-variables/) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/21534699) |
| **A Four-Term Counterexample to the Special Image Conjecture in Three Pairs** | An explicit four-term three-pair construction, with the later two-pair boundary proved in the current edition. | [Paper and verification](results/three-pair-image-counterexample/) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/21634058) |
| **Sparse Minimality of Gaussian-Moments Counterexamples in Dimension Three** | Excludes degree-at-most-four counterexamples with at most four monomials in Gaussian Witt coordinates. | [Paper and verification](results/sparse-minimality-gaussian-moments-dimension-three/) · [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/21534917) |
