# Rodrigues: exact local length 17 and the minimal moment prefix

Date: 17 November 2026.

## Result and scope

Let \(q=2zw+t^2\), let \(h\) be the normalized sphere functional on \(q=1\), and let
\[
F_0=w+zt-\tfrac12z^3,\qquad \mu_n(F)=h(F^n).
\]
For the full sixteen-dimensional space of harmonic representatives of degree at most three, take the local moment algebra transverse to the scalar and \(SO_3\) orbit of \(F_0\). Equivalently, take the local algebra at its point of the cyclic \(SO_3\) quotient normalized by \(D=1/8\).

The exact rational calculation supplied in this bundle gives
\[
\boxed{d_{13}=18,\qquad d_{14}=17,\qquad d_\infty=17.}
\]
The completed local ideal of all positive moments is generated by the first fourteen moments, and fourteen is the least consecutive prefix with this property. Its maximal ideal has exact nilpotence index seven.

These are local statements. They do not prove global finiteness of the normalized cyclic quotient \(A_{14}\), or exclude pure components elsewhere. Given the three known normalized Rodrigues points, the correct global exhaustion target is now
\[
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb C}A_{14}=51.}
\]
The local calculation uses rational arithmetic, not `msolve`. The separately retained global lower bound for \(A_{12}\) and removal of the anti-halfturn chart keep their existing dependencies. This bundle is a computational proof certificate for review and integration; it does not claim external independent review or formal verification.

## 1. An explicit transverse slice

Use the harmonic basis
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal H=(&1,z,t,w,z^2,zt,t^2-zw,wt,w^2,z^3,z^2t,\\
&z(t^2-zw/2),t^3-3zwt,w(t^2-zw/2),w^2t,w^3).
\end{aligned}
\]
The exact moment rule is
\[
h(z^i t^j w^k)=0\quad\text{unless }i=k\text{ and }j\text{ is even},
\qquad
h(z^i t^{2r}w^i)=\frac{i!(2r-1)!!}{(2i+2r+1)!!}.
\]
Define
\[
Q_a=w^2-\frac1{10}(t^3-3zwt)+\frac{17}{70}t,
\qquad
Q_b=wt+\frac25z(t^2-zw/2)-\frac15z,
\]
and let the ordered eliminated directions be
\[
\mathcal E=(1,z,t,w,t^2-zw,wt,w^2,w(t^2-zw/2),w^2t,w^3).
\]
Write
\[
F=F_0+aQ_a+bQ_b+\sum_{j=1}^{10}x_j\mathcal E_j.
\]
The Jacobian of \((\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_{10})\) in \(x_1,\ldots,x_{10}\) at the origin has determinant
\[
\frac{33554432}{789441476538345505875}\ne0.
\]
The formal implicit function theorem therefore gives unique \(x_j(a,b)\in(a,b)^2\) solving these ten moments.

Appending \(Q_a,Q_b\) and the four orbit directions
\[
F_0,\quad (z\partial_z-w\partial_w)F_0,\quad
(z\partial_t-t\partial_w)F_0,\quad
(w\partial_t-t\partial_z)F_0
\]
to the ten columns \(\mathcal E\) gives determinant \(-3/50\) in \(\mathcal H\). Thus this is a genuine formal transversal.

Set \(R_n(a,b)=\mu_n(F(a,b))\), \(J_N=(R_{11},\ldots,R_N)\subset\mathbb Q[[a,b]]\), and \(L_N=\mathbb Q[[a,b]]/J_N\). These coordinates are explicit and need not agree with the parameter normalization in earlier local certificates.

## 2. Two additional weighted initial relations

Use weights \(\operatorname{wt}(a,b)=(3,2)\), with initial form meaning the terms of **least** weight. Put
\[
A=\frac{80990528}{22950790547685},\qquad
C=-\frac{5219584}{26059988554145}.
\]
The weighted initial forms of \(R_{11}/A\) and \(R_{12}/C\) are
\[
f=a^3b+\alpha ab^4,\qquad
g=a^4+\beta a^2b^3+\gamma b^6,
\]
where
\[
\alpha=\frac{6488776}{18982155},\quad
\beta=\frac{3109877708}{1048504325},\quad
\gamma=\frac{4973565856}{29245471875}.
\]
Subtracting the appropriate constant multiple of \(bR_{11}\) from \(R_{13}\) leaves weighted initial form
\[
\frac{2538438656}{2305418019694453125}\,ab^5.
\]
For \(R_{14}\), eliminate its \(a^4b\) term using \(bR_{12}\), then eliminate the \(a^2b^4\) term using the combination whose initial form is \(bg-af\). The remaining weighted initial form is
\[
-\frac{809733013864448}{95603262363699979798828125}\,b^7.
\]
Both coefficients are nonzero. The replay derives and checks them from the actual implicit Taylor corrections.

The zero-dimensional weighted homogeneous ideals
\[
(f,g,ab^5),\qquad (f,g,ab^5,b^7)
\]
have respective standard monomial sets
\[
\mathcal B_{13}=\{b^0,\ldots,b^7;\ a,ab,\ldots,ab^4;\ a^2,a^2b,\ldots,a^2b^3;\ a^3\},
\]
\[
\mathcal B_{14}=\{b^0,\ldots,b^6;\ a,ab,\ldots,ab^4;\ a^2,a^2b,\ldots,a^2b^3;\ a^3\}.
\]
Their lengths are eighteen and seventeen. Their largest weights are fourteen and twelve. Since these are ideals generated by actual weighted initial forms, they give upper bounds for the lengths of the corresponding complete local quotients. Equivalently, formal reduction by these leading forms converges in the positive-weight topology. In particular, weight at least fifteen vanishes in \(L_{13}\), and \((a,b)^8\subset J_{13}\).

## 3. Exact local lengths, not just jet lengths

Work in the thirty-six-dimensional rational vector space
\[
V=\mathbb Q[a,b]/(a,b)^8,
\]
ordered by the monomials \(a^i b^j\) with \(i+j\le7\). The replay calculates \(R_{11},\ldots,R_{14}\) through ordinary degree seven, using the actual formal implicit corrections.

The matrix of all surviving monomial multiples of \(R_{11},R_{12}\) has rank sixteen. Adding \(R_{13}\) gives rank eighteen. Adding \(R_{14}\) gives rank nineteen. Consequently
\[
\dim V/\overline J_{12}=20,\quad
\dim V/\overline J_{13}=18,\quad
\dim V/\overline J_{14}=17.
\]
The first number is a truncation length; the previously retained full local length \(d_{12}\) is twenty-two. For indices thirteen and fourteen the matching weighted upper bounds prove that these truncation lengths are the full local lengths.

There is also a direct local containment certificate at index fourteen. The rank-nineteen row space contains every degree-seven monomial and every monomial of weight at least thirteen among the thirty-six columns. Degree-seven containment modulo \((a,b)^8\) implies
\[
\mathfrak m^7L_{14}=\mathfrak m^8L_{14}=\mathfrak m\,\mathfrak m^7L_{14}.
\]
Nakayama's lemma gives \(\mathfrak m^7L_{14}=0\). The weight-thirteen row memberships then give the actual filtration containment \(F^{13}L_{14}=0\). The row for \(b^6\) is not in the ideal span, so \(\mathfrak m^6L_{14}\ne0\): the nilpotence index is exactly seven.

This step does not substitute multiplication in a weighted initial quotient for actual multiplication in the local algebra.

One can compress the containment argument to the six generators of the weight-thirteen monomial ideal
\[
K=(a^5,a^4b,a^3b^2,a^2b^4,ab^5,b^7).
\]
Their exact row memberships give \(K L_{14}\subset\mathfrak m^8L_{14}\). Since \(\mathfrak m^7\subset K\), one has \(\mathfrak m^8L_{14}\subset\mathfrak m K L_{14}\); Nakayama gives \(K L_{14}=0\). This proves both the actual weight-thirteen vanishing and actual ordinary nilpotence without any interpretation of a computational monomial order.

The exported multiplication matrices are multiplication in the actual local algebra, after this identification. Their maximal-ideal power dimensions are
\[
(17,16,14,11,7,4,1,0),
\]
and the ordinary local Hilbert function is
\[
(1,2,3,4,3,3,1).
\]

## 4. A finite bound for every later moment

The inverse of the functional-preserving Rodrigues shear is
\[
\Phi_{-1}:(z,t,w)\longmapsto(z,t+z^2,w-zt-\tfrac12z^3).
\]
It sends \(F_0\) to \(w\). Give \((z,t,w)\) torus weights \((1,0,-1)\). The pulled-back tangent markers \(Q_a,Q_b\) have maximum torus weights four and three.

The actual quadratic implicit correction is
\[
\begin{aligned}
T_2={}&a^2\left(-\frac4{637}tw+\frac14w^3-\frac{57}{6370}z\right)\\
&+ab\left(-\frac4{385}t^2+\frac12tw^2+\frac4{385}wz\right)\\
&+b^2\left(\frac15t^2w-\frac1{10}w^2z+\frac1{75}w\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
Its three pulled-back coefficient markers have maximum torus weights at most nine, eight and seven. Every coefficient marker of ordinary parameter degree at least three still has original spatial degree at most three; after pullback its maximum torus weight is at most nine.

It follows, for every coefficient marker \(Q_{ij}\) of \(a^i b^j\) in the pulled-back phase correction, that
\[
\operatorname{maxwt}_{\rm torus}Q_{ij}\le5i+4j-1.
\]
Indeed the degree-one and degree-two cases were just checked, and for \(i+j\ge3\) the right side is at least eleven.

In an expansion of \(h((w+\delta)^n)\), the coefficient of \(a^i b^j\) has torus weight at most \(5i+4j-n\). Torus invariance of \(h\) therefore implies
\[
[a^ib^j]R_n\ne0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad5i+4j\ge n.
\]
Since \(5i+4j\le2(3i+2j)\), every monomial of \(R_n\) has weight at least \(\lceil n/2\rceil\). Thus
\[
n>24\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R_n=0\text{ in }L_{14},
\]
using the actual containment \(F^{13}L_{14}=0\).

## 5. The remaining finite check

The thirty-two rows generating \(\overline J_{14}\) in \(V\) form a \(32\times36\) rational matrix of rank nineteen. The residuals \(R_{15},\ldots,R_{21}\) and all their surviving monomial multiples add eighteen rows. The resulting \(50\times36\) matrix still has rank nineteen. Residuals \(R_{22},\ldots,R_{28}\) have identically zero jets modulo \((a,b)^8\).

Hence \(R_{15},\ldots,R_{24}\in J_{14}\). The previous section handles every later index, proving
\[
J_\infty=J_{14},\qquad \dim L_\infty=17.
\]
Because \(d_{13}=18>17=d_{14}\), fourteen is the minimal consecutive local generating prefix. Faithful flatness of completion transfers this ideal equality back to the ordinary local ring.

## 6. Relation to the normalized global quotient

On the cyclic locus the \(SO_3\) stabilizer is trivial: an element fixing the phase fixes \(v,Bv,B^2v\), which are a basis. Scaling the phase by \(\lambda\) scales \(D\) by \(\lambda^6\); at \(D=1/8\), its amplitude derivative \(6D\) is nonzero. The transverse slice therefore identifies formally with the \(D=1/8\) cyclic quotient at each Rodrigues point.

The relative sign involution can act on the transverse local algebra, but it is a residual automorphism, not an additional quotient in the definition of \(A_N\). There is no division of the local length by two.

Each of the three normalized Rodrigues points contributes seventeen. Consequently
\[
\dim A_{14}=51<\infty
\]
is equivalent to global support consisting exactly of those three points, under the stated complete cyclic quotient model. The local calculation supplies the target and the local contribution; it does not establish that global equality.
