# Degree-twelve four-weight ideals and the full-module quartic obstruction

15 November 2026. Internal written proof with exact coefficient and Haar
certificates. No novelty, independent specialist review or formal verification
is claimed. The [checker](verify_degree12_four_weights.py),
[frozen relations](degree12_four_weight_relations.json),
[receipt](degree12_four_weight_verification.json), and
[source audit](degree12_four_weight_source_audit.json) delimit the evidence.

The [octahedral lowest-type theorem](OCTAHEDRAL_LOWEST_TYPE_AND_SHARP_PREFIXES.md)
closes all degree<=11 functions on SL_2/2O. The present result addresses
degree12 on both remaining models without assuming that the octavic
invariant-generator method extends to V_12.

There are three conclusions.

* A full sector with four left weights has pure-null set exactly two planes,
  ordinary moment ideal generated through6, and optimal uniform zero-offset
  marked coefficient1/4. This is an all-order sector classification.
* On each entire V_12, a concrete function has ten zero pure moments
  followed by a nonzero eleventh. Any sufficient pure-only prefix for the
  full next space must be at least11. Sufficiency of11 is not proved.
* On V_12, the ordinary moment ideal cannot equal the ideal generated by
  all positive invariants. The missing quartic is exhibited explicitly.
  This statement does not imply different radicals or an MZ failure.

The full octahedral mixed space C+V_8+V_12, the full icosahedral space
C+V_12, and both all-degree Mathieu--Zhao questions remain open.

## 1. Actual invariant degree-twelve modules

Let g=[[x,y],[z,w]], xw-yz=1, and F=z partial_x+w partial_y. Use

    f_O(X,Y)=X^2 Y^2 (X^4-Y^4)^2,
    f_I(X,Y)=X Y (X^10+11X^5Y^5-Y^10).

The checker verifies these forms under exact generators of compact 2O
and2I, respectively, and verifies dim V_12^Gamma=1. In each case put

    p=f_Gamma(x,y), p_j=F^j p/j!  (j=0,...,12).

They span the entire copy of the left module V_12 in the quotient algebra.
Their substitution weights are 12-2j. The bottom function p_12 is
f_Gamma(z,w)=conjugate(p) on SU_2. The derivation and Haar conventions are
those of the [Haar-shear proof](HAAR_SHEARS_AND_GENERAL_NULLCONE_CLASSIFICATION.md).

Study every coefficient in the sector

    P=a p_0+b p_4+c p_8+d p_12.                           (1)

Its weights are 12,4,-4,-12. A left diagonal matrix with parameter zeta_8
negates every member, so all odd pure moments vanish identically.
A left Weyl element exchanges (a,b,c,d) with (d,c,b,a); all relevant
ladder indices are even, so there is no extra sign.

The sector is a full eigenspace inside V_12 for this specified finite
torus action. It is not the full thirteen-dimensional module and does
not include the octahedral V_8 summand.

## 2. The elementary invariant ring controlling every sector moment

Scale the torus weights by4, obtaining 3,1,-1,-3. Define

    u=ad, v=bc, s=ac^3, t=b^3d, z=s+t.

Every balanced monomial is a product of u,v,s,t: strip all possible
factors ad and bc; the remaining nonconstant balanced monomial is a power
of ac^3 or b^3d. The only relation is st=uv^3. Indeed after using it to
remove simultaneous s and t, the images of the remaining monomials in
a,b,c,d are distinct. Thus the torus-invariant ring is

    C[u,v,s,t]/(st-uv^3).

The Weyl involution fixes u,v and interchanges s,t. Its fixed ring is

    C[u,v,z], deg(u)=deg(v)=2, deg(z)=4.                  (2)

One can average a representative under the involution, use symmetric
polynomials in s,t, and replace st by uv^3. There is no relation among
u,v,z: the highest s-power in a purported polynomial relation in z
cannot cancel in the preceding monomial normal form.

Every pure moment h(P^m) belongs to (2) and is homogeneous of degree m.
This controls **all** moment orders without a generator bound for the
full binary duodecimic invariant ring.

## 3. Exact moment normal forms through six

Write H_m=h(P^m). Direct Haar integration verifies all coefficients of
H_2,H_4,H_6 in C[u,v,z]; the complete rational expressions are in the
[frozen certificate](degree12_four_weight_relations.json). In particular,

    H_2=eta (u+495v),
    eta_O=16/3003, eta_I=25/546.                          (3)

Set kappa=-1/495. The next normal forms are

    H_4=gamma (z-lambda u^2)  modulo (v-kappa u),
    H_6=delta u^3  modulo (v-kappa u,z-lambda u^2).        (4)

The values are:

| Model | gamma | lambda | delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2O | 1699200/7436429 | 122/963765 | 47243853824/58274314498849449 |
| 2I | -17015625/676039 | -98/408375 | 80800000/67461255771 |

Every displayed eta,gamma,delta is nonzero. It follows that

    (H_2,H_4,H_6)=(v+u/495,z-lambda u^2,u^3)              (5)

both in C[u,v,z] and after extension to C[a,b,c,d]. The quartic contains
gamma z; its two parameter monomials ac^3 and b^3d have equal coefficient.

For a later even moment H_(2k), reduction by the first two generators
in (5) gives a scalar multiple of u^k, by the weighted homogeneity in
(2). When k>=3 this is in (u^3). Odd moments are identically zero.
Consequently the **full ordinary** pure-moment ideal is exactly

    I_infinity=(v+u/495,z-lambda u^2,u^3) in C[a,b,c,d].   (6)

This all-order conclusion uses the invariant normal form, not a finite
moment-prefix plateau. There is no uncomputed higher-order condition in
this sector.

The same criterion works conditionally for any four-weight module with
weights 3r,r,-r,-3r, positive Weyl exchange on the four basis functions,
and nonzero second/quartic/sextic coefficients giving (3)--(4). In a
type V_(6s), use indices0,2s,4s,6s, so r=2s and replace495 by
binom(6s,2s). When these nonvanishing conditions hold, the proof of (6)
is unchanged. The present checker establishes those conditions for the
two degree-twelve modules only; they are not asserted automatically for
every representation or right vector.

## 4. The two pure planes, sharp prefix six, and all markers

Equations (5)--(6) give u=v=z=0 set-theoretically. Since st=uv^3 and
s+t=z, this also gives s=t=0. In the original parameters it is equivalent
to

    a=b=0  or  c=d=0.                                    (7)

For example ad=bc=ac^3=b^3d=0 forbids every combination of a positive
and negative weight. Each plane in (7) contracts by a single torus
cocharacter and is marked-safe for every regular Q in the whole quotient
algebra. Its weights have absolute value at least4, so

    h(QP^m)=0 whenever 4m>deg_matrix_entries(Q).           (8)

The coefficient1/4 is optimal: P=p_4,Q=p_12 have deg(Q)=12 and

    h(p_12 p_4^3)=gamma/4
       =424800/7436429                  on2O,
       =-17015625/2704156                on2I.            (9)

These values are directly integrated. They disprove any smaller uniform
zero-offset coefficient. They do not assert optimality for affine bounds
with an additional constant offset.

Both the pure-only set prefix and ordinary generating prefix are sharply6.
For explicit set sharpness take a=d=1, choose any root b of

    b^6-lambda b^3+kappa^3=0, c=kappa/b.                  (10)

The roots are nonzero and the sextic is squarefree in both cases.
Then u=1,v=kappa,z=lambda. Hence H_2=H_4=0, the odd moments vanish,
but H_6=delta!=0. The checker verifies this in the exact six-dimensional
algebra, including c=(lambda b^2-b^5)/kappa^2. A specific real witness is
obtained by choosing the unique positive real b: the quadratic equation
for b^3 has one positive and one negative root since kappa^3<0.

The parameter binary form is

    X^12+495b X^8Y^4+495c X^4Y^8+Y^12.

It is stable: neither coordinate point is a root, and every other root
comes from a cubic in X^4/Y^4 and therefore has multiplicity at most3.
This is a first-five-zero witness, not an all-order pure-null element.

Ordinary leastness follows without a primary decomposition. If H_6 were
in (H_2,H_4) in C[a,b,c,d], averaging its coefficients over the torus
and Weyl involution would give such an identity in C[u,v,z]. Reduction
by H_2,H_4 leaves the nonzero polynomial delta u^3, a contradiction.
Adding a constant coefficient e simply adjoins e to (6), by the first
moment and binomial expansion; both prefixes remain6.

## 5. A full-module ordinary-ideal obstruction already at degree four

Now allow all thirteen coefficients A_0,...,A_12 in

    P_A=sum A_j p_j,
    f_A(X,Y)=sum binom(12,j) A_j X^(12-j)Y^j.

Let R=C[A_0,...,A_12]^SL2. Counting weight-zero minus weight-two
multiplicities in Sym^d(V_12) gives

    dim R_1=0, dim R_2=1, dim R_3=1, dim R_4=3.           (11)

The checker obtains these dimensions directly from finite weight counts.
Using the primitive-transvectant convention of the octavic note, put

    I_2=(f_A,f_A)_12,
    A=((f_A,f_A)_10,(f_A,f_A)_10)_4,
    B=((f_A,f_A)_8,(f_A,f_A)_8)_8.

Here A denotes a quartic invariant, distinct from the coefficient tuple
A_j. Exact coefficient rank proves that I_2^2,A,B form a full basis of
R_4. Reynolds projection of the actual identity-evaluation functional
computes the full H_4, not just its four-weight restriction. For this
computation take the weight-zero part of L_Gamma^m, where

    L_O=A_2-2A_6+A_10, L_I=A_1+11A_6-A_11.

Replace8 by12 in the raising/lowering parameter derivations of the
octavic note and apply the same finite projector, now summed through6m.
The zero-weight irreducible-line proof there applies verbatim. The full
quadratic and quartic are constructed this way; their restrictions also
agree coefficientwise with the direct sector integrals. In the ordered
quartic basis their coordinates are:

| Model | coefficient of I_2^2 | coefficient of A | coefficient of B |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2O | 8192/156165009 | -44032/1093155063 | 16992/364385021 |
| 2I | 38125/4368252 | 15125/198755466 | -680625/132503644 |

In both cases H_2 is a nonzero multiple of I_2 and the coefficient of B
is nonzero. Thus A is not in span(I_2^2,H_4).

Let J be the ordinary ideal generated by **all** positive pure moments
in the full parameter polynomial ring. If an invariant quartic belongs
to J, homogeneity only permits H_2,H_3,H_4 to contribute. Reynolds
averaging their coefficient polynomials leaves coefficients in R_2,R_1,R_0.
By (11), the invariant part of J in degree four is exactly

    J intersect R_4=span(I_2^2,H_4),                      (12)

of dimension2. Therefore A is not in J. In particular,

    J is strictly smaller than the ideal generated
    by all positive SL_2 invariants.                     (13)

Higher moments cannot change this degree-four deficit. More generally,
for any single coefficient module V_12 and any fixed right vector,
there are at most two invariant quartics in its moment ideal, because
there is only one moment polynomial in each degree. The nonzero rank2
and the explicit missing A above are certified for our two actual models.

This is an ordinary-ideal obstruction, not a claim about radicals.
The missing A could lie in the radical of J. Thus (13) neither constructs
a stable all-order pure-null function nor disproves marked safety.
It rules out copying the octavic proof that moments generate the whole
invariant ring or its positive invariant ideal. A V_12 nullcone proof
needs a different argument, such as a justified radical or parameter test.

Adding other full representation summands cannot restore this ordinary
generator equality. Restrict their coefficients to zero. The larger
moment ideal restricts exactly to J. The ideal generated by all positive
invariants restricts exactly to the corresponding invariant-generated
ideal on V_12: every V_12 invariant extends by the equivariant projection
onto that summand. Equality upstairs would contradict (13) downstairs.
This excludes the same route on the full octahedral V_8+V_12 family and
on any larger full coefficient space containing this V_12. It still
does not exclude radical equality or a sufficient pure-moment prefix.

## 6. Ten moments are insufficient on the full next spaces

On each full V_12 take

    P_*=p_0+p_11.

Its two weights are12 and-10. A balanced product of positive total order
m requires 12j-10(m-j)=0, so the first possible m is11, with j=5.
Consequently the first ten pure moments vanish. Direct Haar integration
of the single balanced term gives

    h(P_*^11)=binom(11,5) h(p_0^5 p_11^6)
      =-43987763200000/23813095609334142593573    on2O,
      =23788050537109375/807223579977428562494    on2I.    (14)

Both are nonzero. The corresponding parameter binary form is
X^12+12XY^11, with twelve distinct roots. P_* has exact matrix-entry
degree12. This proves the lower bound11 for any sufficient pure-only
prefix on the entire V_12 and on larger families containing it. It gives
no sufficient numerical upper bound and is not an MZ counterexample.

## 7. Scope and next work

The exact sector result closes all coefficients a,b,c,d in (1), with
arbitrary regular markers in the whole quotient. It should not be
reopened by changing coefficients or finite prefix length. Equation (13)
is an all-order barrier on the full thirteen-dimensional type, and (14)
is an exact full-space prefix lower bound. No general duodecimic generator
list, unproved system of parameters, or heuristic stabilization is imported.

The remaining target is all coefficients of C+V_12 on2I and of
C+V_8+V_12 on2O, then the all-degree model questions. A future proof
must classify the actual pure-null radical or produce an infinite fixed
marked tail; the finite nonzero moments here expressly exclude those
displayed functions from the all-order pure-null locus.
