# A seven-condition global Rodrigues certificate

Date: 17 November 2026.

## Scope and main result

Work in the complete normalized cyclic coordinate ring with \(D=1/8\), including the monic Gram relation and all three harmonic trace relations. Let \(I_N\) be the ideal of the first \(N\) moments, and \(A_N\) the corresponding quotient. The constant phase coefficient is removed by the first moment, as in the retained model. Both \(\mu_{13}\) and \(\mu_{14}\) belong to \(I_{14}\).

Use the retained local theorem: the three normalized Rodrigues points have local moment algebras of length seventeen, generated by the first fourteen moments, with maximal-ideal nilpotence index seven. The supplied local certificate is included unchanged in its own subdirectory.

Define
\[
\begin{aligned}
K_1&=4p+5s_0, & K_2&=s_0, & K_3&=s_1,\\
K_4&=U_{000}, & K_5&=U_{002}-6U_{011},\\
K_6&=4U_{022}-13U_{112}, &
K_7&=U_{222}+128s_2+\frac{896}{75}p^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Then the following are equivalent:

1. \(\dim_{\mathbb C} A_{14}=51\).
2. The seven powers
   \[
   K_1^3,\quad s_0^7,\quad s_1^5,\quad U_{000}^3,\quad
   K_5^2,\quad K_6^3,\quad K_7^2
   \]
   belong to \(I_{14}\).
3. The following seven polynomials belong to \(I_{14}\):
   \[
   \boxed{K_1^3,\quad \Psi,\quad s_1^5,\quad U_{000}^3,\quad
   K_5^2,\quad K_6^3,\quad K_7^2,}
   \]
   where
   \[
   \begin{aligned}
   \Psi={}&s_0^3
   +\frac{55782484748580}{23118017560861}s_1^2
   -\frac{1009306881744}{399965701745}s_2K_1\\
   &-\frac{34813910270351}{13198868157585}K_1s_0^2
   -\frac{119786979}{79993140349}K_6.
   \end{aligned}
   \]

The polynomial degrees of the seven tests in item 3 are respectively
\[
(3,3,5,3,2,3,4).
\]
Thus the largest raw polynomial degree is five. No claim is made that this bounds the degrees of their membership witnesses.

**None of these seven global membership identities is proved here. Global finiteness and length fifty-one remain open.** The new results are an exact augmented global ideal identity, an explicitly verified reduction of the target list, local identities for the adapted targets, and a grading that reduces any chosen finite witness ansatz.

## 1. A global support theorem with a scheme refinement

Let
\[
\mathcal Z=(p,s_0,s_1,U_{000},U_{002},U_{022},T),
\qquad T=U_{222}+128s_2,
\]
and let \(P\) be the ideal of the three reduced normalized Rodrigues points in the ambient cyclic ring. The independent support certificate proves
\[
\boxed{I_9+(\mathcal Z)=P.}
\]
Already \(I_6+(\mathcal Z)\) has those three points as its complex support. The ninth moment is used to prove reducedness of this intersection, not to assert any global prefix bound away from the selected locus.

Here is the geometric reduction, with every cubic coefficient retained. Write \(c=s_2\). Imposing \(\mathcal Z=0\) gives
\[
G=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&c\\0&c&0\\c&0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad c^3=1/64,
\]
and the traces force
\[
U_{011}=U_{112}=0,\qquad U_{111}=-2U_{012}.
\]
Scalar symmetry permutes the three values of \(c\), so take \(c=1/4\). The oriented cyclic frame can be transported uniquely to
\[
(e_z,\tfrac12 e_t,\tfrac14 e_w).
\]
For completeness, if \(L\) is any frame in this fibre and \(L_0\) is the reference frame, \(L_0L^{-1}\) is an \(SO_3\) isometry: both frames have the same Gram matrix and determinant \(1/8\). Its entries are regular because that determinant is a unit. This argument applies over coefficient algebras and does not discard nonreduced structure.

Cayley-Hamilton then forces the quadratic operator to be
\[
\begin{pmatrix}0&0&R\\1/2&0&0\\0&1/2&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad R=4r.
\]
The remaining phase is exactly
\[
F=F_0+Rw^2+Atw^2+B_*(t^3-3ztw)+Cz^2t,
\quad F_0=w+zt-\tfrac12z^3,
\]
where
\[
A=\tfrac32U_{001},\quad B_*=-\tfrac14U_{012},\quad
C=\tfrac3{32}U_{122}.
\]
The four-parameter coefficient change has constant determinant \(-9/64\). The parameter \(r\) remains unrestricted.

In this family the primitive second and third moment equations are
\[
4AC+15B_*^2=0,\qquad -4B_*CR-9B_*-6R=0.
\]
The cases \(B_*=0\) or \(C=0\) reduce to the origin using the fifth moment. Otherwise set \(x=B_*C\), \(T_*=B_*^4\). The fourth moment solves for \(T_*\); moments five and six then give coprime polynomials of degrees eight and ten in \(x\). The supplied certificate contains the exact polynomials and verifies rational Bezout identities. All excluded denominator cases are checked separately. Therefore the first six moments have only the origin in this entire four-parameter family.

The linear parts of primitive moments \(3,5,7,9\), in the parameter order \((R,A,B_*,C)\), form
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
-6&0&-9&0\\
0&-78&0&286\\
646&0&6460&0\\
0&5244&0&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det=49\,411\,883\,664.
\]
This proves reducedness at the only point on each branch, establishing the scheme identity \(I_9+(\mathcal Z)=P\). See the included `ODD_SLICE_PROOF.md` for the full elimination and `verify_odd_slice.py` for two independent moment calculations.

## 2. The adapted seven generators define the same augmented ideal

In the ambient cyclic ring,
\[
(K_1,\ldots,K_7)=(\mathcal Z).
\]
Indeed \(K_1,K_2,K_3\) generate \((p,s_0,s_1)\). Modulo this ideal the Gram relation gives \(s_2^3=1/64\), so \(s_2\) is a unit. The trace relations therefore give
\[
U_{011}=-2U_{002},\qquad U_{112}=-2U_{022}.
\]
Consequently
\[
K_5=13U_{002},\qquad K_6=30U_{022},\qquad K_7=T
\]
in this quotient. All the scalar factors are units in characteristic zero. Equality of the two ideals follows, not merely equality of their radicals. In particular,
\[
\boxed{I_9+(K_1,\ldots,K_7)=P.}
\]

## 3. Actual local multiplication supplies the lower powers

The coordinate adapter reconstructs \(v,B,H_3\) from the actual formal phase in the seventeen-dimensional local ring. It builds the cyclic frame, changes all ten symmetric cubic coefficients, and checks the full Gram, trace, adjugate and Cayley-Hamilton identities.

Normalization is performed exactly. With \(\lambda=(1/(8D))^{1/6}\), evaluated by a finite binomial series, the normalized cubic coefficients are
\[
U'_{ijk}=\lambda^{-i-j-k-2}D^{-3}\overline U_{ijk},
\qquad \overline U_{ijk}=D^3U_{ijk}.
\]
For the final target the unit-equivalent expression is
\[
U'_{222}+128s'_2
=\lambda^{-8}D^{-3}(\overline U_{222}+2Ds_2).
\]
This avoids a normalization error in the inhomogeneous-looking target \(T\).

Multiplication in the actual local algebra gives the exact least nilpotence exponents
\[
(e_{K_1},\ldots,e_{K_7})=(3,7,5,3,2,3,2).
\]
Each preceding power is checked to be nonzero. These are calculations in the retained multiplication matrices, not in a weighted initial quotient.

The five local vectors
\[
s_0^3,\quad s_1^2,\quad s_2K_1,\quad K_1s_0^2,\quad K_6
\]
have exact rank four. Their unique dependence with first coefficient one is the polynomial \(\Psi\) displayed above. Its value in the actual normalized local algebra is zero. The entire calculation uses rational arithmetic.

## 4. Why the certificates are globally equivalent

Assume the seven power memberships in item 2. At every complex closed point of \(A_{14}\), all \(K_i\) vanish. The support theorem then puts that point at one of the three Rodrigues points. A finite-type complex algebra with this finite support is Artinian. Its three local lengths are seventeen, so its total dimension is fifty-one.

For item 3, the six power memberships imply pointwise
\[
K_1=s_1=U_{000}=K_5=K_6=K_7=0.
\]
On this locus \(\Psi=s_0^3\), so its membership forces \(s_0=0\) at every closed point. The same support argument applies.

Conversely, dimension fifty-one means that the global quotient consists exactly of the three known Rodrigues local algebras. The verified local powers and \(\Psi\) vanish in each. The symmetry in the next section transports the calculation at one point to the other two. Thus all the required polynomials belong to \(I_{14}\).

## 5. Twelve homogeneous parts over the rational field

The complete normalized cyclic ring has a \(\mathbb Z/12\mathbb Z\) grading:
\[
\deg p=2,\quad\deg r=3,\quad\deg s_j=j+2,\quad
\deg U_{ijk}=-i-j-k-2\pmod {12}.
\]
For \(\lambda^{12}=1\), put \(g=\lambda^6 I\in O_3\), and send the phase to \(\lambda F(gx)\). The determinant remains \(D\), because its scaling factor is \(\lambda^6\det g=\lambda^{24}=1\). The functional is invariant under \(g=\pm I\), so \(\mu_n\) has weight \(n\).

The Gram relation has weight zero; trace relation \(k\) has weight \(-k\). The monic Gram basis \((1,s_2,s_2^2)\) has weights \((0,4,8)\). This grading is defined over \(\mathbb Q\); it requires no roots of unity in the coefficient field.

The seven tests in the degree-five variant have weights
\[
(6,6,3,6,4,6,8)\pmod {12}.
\]
For a target of weight \(w\), a multiplier of a homogeneous ideal generator of weight \(v\) only needs monomials of weight \(w-v\). Projecting any witness identity to its target weight proves that this restriction is exact. It reduces a chosen finite coefficient ansatz, but neither gives a witness-degree bound nor proves that any target belongs to the ideal.
