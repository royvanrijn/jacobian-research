[The Collateral Impact of the Three-Dimensional Jacobian Counterexample:
a Source-Verified Cascade, v0.07](https://zenodo.org/records/23873236),
1 November 2026, five pages. The old search result pointed to v0.06;

The author reports an independent reconstruction of de Bondt's degree-seven
homogeneous map in dimension fifteen, and an explicit homogeneous
Hessian-nilpotent quartic/gradient witness in dimension48. The paper cites
de Bondt's dimensions28 and30 without replaying those constructions.
Its Mathieu consequence reaches SU(N), N>=15, through that map, while
its open-problem list still includes SU(3),...,SU(14) and other nonabelian
compact groups. It calls the two-variable Gaussian theorem a claimed proof.
These are source-specific assertions and assurance statements, not new
local reproductions.

The current knowledge base needs three distinctions:

* The dimension-fifteen Mathieu bound describes this particular transfer
  route. It does not reopen any compact connected group already covered
  by [Long's primary all-group theorem](COMPACT_GROUPS.md) or our
  [finite-component extension](DISCONNECTED_COMPACT_GROUPS.md).
* The two-variable Gaussian result is already closed locally by G2T,
  with its recorded proof assurances and the additional Wilson source.
  A survey's wording supplies no independent external review and does
  not replace the current canonical proof.
* The ordinary-Laplacian Hessian-nilpotent potential question and our
  ternary generalized-vanishing witness for a higher power of Delta have
  different operator and degree hypotheses. This survey does not close
  OP-GVC3-MIN for lower powers, nor justify treating three variables as
  a replacement for its homogeneous-quartic dimension48 statement.
