# Intrinsic complement monodromy and ramified Haar classes

16 November 2026. Internal written proofs, with exact classical connection
inputs pinned below. Independent replay, formal verification and external
review are not claimed. Both COMPLETE exceptional Mathieu--Zhao cases
remain **OPEN**. Review remains deferred.

The semisimple-fibre1 obstruction is intrinsic: its projected Haar class
does not depend on the chosen lift of the ambient generator. It vanishes
exactly when the FULL complement monodromy is semisimple at eigenvalue 1.
Thus in this case an eventually pure phase is all-original-marker safe
iff that complement condition holds, a necessary AND sufficient criterion.
There is no additional physical-cycle selection problem in this case.

Polynomial ramification has an exact rule. A root of multiplicity e
contributes the primitive intrinsic class divided by e on each branch.
Different primitive roots occupy different fibre summands and cannot
cancel. Canonical residue matrices express these local conditions by
finite algebraic tests, when the full connection and its canonical
lattice are supplied. Their general producer is not implemented.

For nonsemisimple fibre1 there is still an intrinsic finite extension
obstruction: if it is nonzero, EVERY lift moves and an eventually pure
phase fails. Its vanishing alone does not control the actual Haar lift.

## 1. Local unipotent extensions and the ambient generator

Let X=SL2/Gamma for ANY finite Gamma, and f nonconstant regular. Retain
the FULL fibre/complement bifurcation set, including finite values caused
by infinity. At a puncture a the retained integral tube sequence becomes
over Q

    0 -> F=H_2(F_s;Q) --Tube--> V=H_3(U_s;Q)
                                    -> Q -> 0.        (1)

The quotient is the fixed ambient generator [SU2/Gamma]. Let A and T be
local monodromy on F and V, respectively. Restrict (1) to generalized
eigenvalue 1, using the compatible rational spectral projectors:

    0 -> F_1 -> V_1 -> Q -> 0.                        (2)

For a specified lift gamma of the generator, put

    (T-I)gamma=Tube(d),       c=pi_1(A)d.              (3)

Its projected motion is (T-I)pi_1(T)gamma=Tube(c). All statements about
the actual Haar lift use its physical continuation, as before.

Assume first that A is semisimple on F_1. Then A|F_1=I. Changing gamma
to gamma+Tube(b) changes c by

    pi_1(A)(A-I)b=0.                                  (4)

Thus c is INDEPENDENT of every choice of lift at the fixed puncture.
It is a canonical rational vector in F_1, once the ambient generator
and local fibre identifications are fixed. Changing local basis or
transport conjugates it; its vanishing is intrinsic. In particular no
convenient fixed lift can hide a nonzero c.

On (2), T-I kills F_1 and has image in F_1. Hence

    (T-I)^2=0,       rank(T-I)<=1,
    (T-I)|V_1=0 iff c=0.                              (5)

The last equivalence follows because V_1 is generated by F_1 and ANY
one lift of the quotient generator. This is elementary extension
linear algebra, not an inference from dimensions of the boundary.

**Intrinsic local theorem.** With semisimple fibre monodromy at 1,

    c=0 iff FULL complement monodromy is semisimple at1. (6)

No purity hypothesis is needed for (4)--(6). If f is EVENTUALLY PURE
and a=0, the retained all-original-marker cycle theorem gives

    f safe for ALL original markers
      iff c=0 iff complement monodromy semisimple at1. (7)

Thus the formerly sufficient complement condition is necessary here too.
For an actual failure, the nontrivial local extension itself forces the
logarithm detected by original markers. A separate Haar selection is
unnecessary in this semisimple case. The actual local system is still
the COMPLEMENT system H3(X minus {f=s}), not merely the generic ordinary
Gauss--Manin connection or the exponential source localized in time.

## 2. A canonical algebraic residue test, retaining resonances

Let C_U be the regular-singular meromorphic connection for H3(U_s),
and C_F its fibre quotient H2(F_s)(-1). The ambient line Q(-2) is a
specified flat subconnection:

    0 -> ambient line -> C_U -> C_F -> 0.              (8)

Use the CANONICAL logarithmic lattice with residue representatives in
[0,1), or equivalently the canonical V-lattice of the meromorphic
connection. Geometric exponents are rational. Let E_U and E_F be the
generalized zero-eigenvalue parts of their finite residue fibres, and
let R_U,R_F be the residue operators there. They are nilpotent.
Canonical strictness makes

    0 -> Q -> E_U -> E_F -> 0                         (9)

exact with the specified ambient line. The monodromy convention for
horizontal sections is exp(-2*pi*i R). This is the canonical-extension
formula in Brosnan--El Zein Theorem1.23 and Remark1.25; Sabbah's
V-strictness and exact graded functors give the same zero block.

Semisimple fibre1 is equivalent to R_F=0. In that case (9) makes R_U
a map from E_F to the ambient line, with R_U^2=0, and

    c=0 iff R_U=0.                                    (10)

This is a FINITE algebraic zero test given a certified full connection,
canonical lattice, zero block and the maps (8). It does not require
evaluating transcendental periods or continuing the physical Haar cycle.
No construction algorithm for those inputs, general complexity bound,
matrix-degree cutoff or implementation is claimed. Over an exact
coefficient field, the stated supplied matrices require only finite
exact linear algebra. An arbitrary complex coefficient oracle is not
silently assumed computable.

The word canonical is essential. An arbitrary logarithmic residue with
integer resonances can be diagonalizable while the true monodromy is
unipotent. For example the horizontal system

    y1'=0,        y2'=y1+y2/z                         (11)

has solutions y1=a, y2=z(b+a log z). Its raw connection residue is
diag(0,-1), diagonalizable. But z2=y2/z gives

    z1'=0,        z2'=z1/z,
    R_can=[[0,0],[-1,0]].                             (12)

The canonical residue is nonzero nilpotent, and its exponential has
nontrivial unipotent monodromy. A raw residue eigenvalue test would
miss the extension. This is a connection countercontrol, not an
exceptional polynomial phase.

## 3. What the intrinsic test gives WITHOUT semisimple fibre1

The canonical sequence (9) exists without R_F=0. For v in ker(R_F),
choose a lift v_tilde in E_U. Then

    delta(v)=R_U(v_tilde) in the ambient line          (13)

is well-defined, since R_U kills that line. This is the connecting map
from ker(R_F) to the ambient line in the kernel/cokernel sequence.

The dual of delta is precisely the extension class obstructing a FIXED
lift of the ambient homology generator in (2). Explicitly, choose any
vector-space splitting of (9), so that

    R_U=[[0,ell],[0,R_F]].                            (14)

Then delta=ell|ker(R_F). A homology lift is fixed iff its functional
on the fibre can be chosen to cancel ell, that is ell lies in the row
space of R_F. This is equivalent to delta=0. It is also the elementary
dual criterion in coker(R_F^t), up to the harmless monodromy scalar.
Therefore

    delta!=0 => EVERY ambient lift has nonzero
                eigenvalue-1 monodromy motion.        (15)

For an EVENTUALLY PURE phase at0, (15) is a genuine full-original-marker
FAILURE certificate, without computing its Haar continuation. It proves
existence of a fixed original marker with an infinite tail by the full
de Rham detection/cycle theorem. The certificate must come from the
ACTUAL full complement connection and maps (8), not an abstract matrix.

Conversely delta=0 proves only existence of some fixed lift. If R_F is
nonzero, the actual Haar lift can still differ by a fibre class that
moves. For a simple abstract control take T=diag(J2(1),1), with the last
coordinate the ambient quotient. The lift e3 is fixed but e3+e2 moves
by e1. Thus zero connecting map, or a split extension, cannot be used
as a general positive MZ theorem. The control is not asserted to be an
actual exceptional phase. When R_F=0, delta=0 is exactly R_U=0 and
the ambiguity disappears, recovering (6)--(10).

## 4. Fibre semisimplicity reflects through EVERY polynomial

Let P=H(f), with H nonconstant. For every root a of H let e_a be its
multiplicity, and retain ALL e_a nearby root branches. Write A_P for
the composite middle-fibre monodromy around0 and A_a for primitive
middle-fibre monodromy around a. The fibre is the disjoint union of
its primitive fibres. On the a-block, A_P cyclically permutes e_a
summands and its e_a-th power acts as A_a on each.

Consequently

    A_P semisimple at1 iff EVERY A_a semisimple at1.    (16)

For a direct proof, isolate an original eigenvalue-1 Jordan block J
of A_a. Its unipotent e_a-th root B=J^(1/e_a) is a finite binomial
polynomial in J-I. Conjugating the cyclic block gives the direct sum
of zeta*B over the e_a-th roots of unity. The block with zeta=1 has
the SAME Jordan sizes as J, since the derivative of z^e_a at1 is
nonzero. Primitive eigenvalues other than1 cannot produce composite
eigenvalue1. This proves both directions and keeps every Jordan block,
not just an eigenvalue count or generic rank.

## 5. Exact intrinsic ramification formula, with no purity reflection

Assume the equivalent conditions in (16). Let c_P and c_(a,j) be the
intrinsic projected classes (3) for P and its induced primitive branch
j over a. Then, in the actual direct sum of primitive fibre homology,

    (c_P)_(a,j) = c_(a,j)/e_a.                        (17)

This holds BEFORE purity. Primitive classes may be compared in any
compatible local flat frames; they are independent of the primitive
ambient lift by (4). No chosen physical continuation can introduce an
additional ambiguity in their vanishing.

To prove (17), take a positive common multiple L of all e_a and put
k_a=L/e_a. The retained exact tube-transfer identity is

    [S_L(A_P)d_P]_(a,j)=S_(k_a)(A_(a,j))d_(a,j),
    S_k(A)=I+A+...+A^(k-1).                           (18)

Project each summand to its primitive generalized eigenvalue-1 part.
Their direct sum S is A_P-stable. Under (16), A_P on S has finite order
dividing L: its e_a-th power is the identity on the a-block. Therefore

    S_L(A_P)|S = L*pi_1(A_P)|S.

The projected right side of (18) is k_a*c_(a,j), because A_(a,j) fixes
its eigenvalue-1 space. The left side is L*(c_P)_(a,j). Dividing proves
(17). It explains the RATIONAL class in a ramified composite even when
the primitive class was integral.

Different roots and branches are distinct summands. Thus

    c_P=0 iff every primitive intrinsic c_a=0.         (19)

Under eventual purity of P, (19) is equivalent to full all-original-marker
safety. The primitive f-a need not be pure or eventually pure. The
genuine SL2 control f=xw, P=f^2 has primitive c_0=-[torus] and composite
c_P=(-1/2,-1/2), exactly (17), with the marked logarithm retained in the
[residue theorem](ALGEBRAIC_HAAR_LOG_COEFFICIENTS_AND_ORIGINAL_RESIDUES.md).

## 6. One primitive finite set controls all recomposition degrees

Suppose primitive fibre monodromy is semisimple at1 at EVERY finite
primitive puncture. At regular values the local class is zero. Define
the finite intrinsic set

    E_f={a : primitive complement R_(U,a),0 !=0}.       (20)

All finite bifurcations caused by infinity must be included when forming
this set. With supplied canonical systems the test in (10) identifies
it without a Haar-cycle continuation. Then for EVERY nonconstant H
for which H(f) is EVENTUALLY PURE,

    H(f) safe for ALL original markers
      iff H^-1(0) intersect E_f is empty.              (21)

This does not decide which H(f) are pure. In particular a root in E_f
alone does not construct a pure counterexample. For example f=xw has
E_f={0,1}, but its interval image already excludes nonzero eventually
pure polynomial composites by the retained thin-image theorem.

If E_f is empty, the previous exact safety-reflection theorem completely
classifies the eventually pure composites: they are H(c)=0 for the
unique safe centre c, or none when no such centre exists. If f already
has a safe centre, E_f is empty under the present fibre hypothesis:
given any a choose H(T)=(T-c)(T-a); its composite is safe by expansion,
so (19) kills the intrinsic class at a. No primitive-purity reflection
or expansion in H degree is used.

For the exceptional canonical primitive factor, use its actual regular
quotient: 2I, 2O in the invariant case, or 2T for the octahedral sign case
with H even. The finite-cover marker trace preserves safety of this
pulled-back composite. Every root and all original markers remain.
