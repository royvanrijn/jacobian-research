## 1. Deleting divisors creates no lowest-weight H2

Let X=SL2/Gamma, ANY finite Gamma. It is smooth affine and H2(X;Q)=0.
Here H2 denotes COHOMOLOGY throughout. If D is ANY closed divisor, with
arbitrary singularities and multiplicities, and V=X minus D, then

    W_2 H2(V;Q)=0.                                      (1)

Choose a smooth proper compactification Y of X with SNC boundary E.
The kernel of H2(Y)->H2(X) is generated by the Chern classes of the
irreducible boundary divisors. This is the degree2 support/Gysin sequence:
in that degree the supports are the codimension1 components; their
intersections have codimension>=2 and contribute only from degree4.
Since H2(X)=0, those boundary classes span ALL H2(Y).

Resolve E union closure(D) by blowups in smooth boundary centres,
obtaining Y' with SNC boundary for V. Each such blowup adds in degree2
only the exceptional divisor classes, one per connected centre of
codimension>=2. This follows from the projective-bundle formula for its
exceptional divisor and the relative cohomology sequence; codimension1
blowups add nothing. All old and new generators are supported in the
deleted boundary. Thus H2(Y')->H2(V) is zero. Deligne's compactification
image characterization W2 H2(V)=image H2(Y') proves (1). No normality or
smoothness of D, and no tameness of a defining function, is used.

## 2. There are no global compact-middle invariant cycles

Fix ANY nonconstant regular f and let S=A1 minus the FULL finite
bifurcation set. All finite values caused by infinity are included.
Set X_S=f^-1(S), F_s=f^-1(s). The map X_S->S is topologically locally
trivial on this regular locus, including disconnected fibres if present.

The algebraic base S has the homotopy type of a graph. Therefore the
classical Leray spectral sequence has only columns0 and1, and its edge
map is onto:

    e:H2(X_S;Q) -> H0(S,R2 f_*Q) -> 0.                  (2)

The NONPROPER version of Hodge compatibility is essential here. We

would NOT justify this application.

Evaluation at s identifies H0 with the invariant subspace of H2(F_s).
It is a mixed-Hodge morphism: restriction H2(X_S)->H2(F_s) factors
through (2), and the edge map is a strict MHS quotient. Therefore (1)
and strictness in (2) prove

    (W2 H2(F_s;Q))^(pi1(S))=0.                         (3)

In (3), invariants are under the ENTIRE global monodromy group, not
under a single local loop and not after an arbitrary finite base cover.
Finite monodromy compact-middle summands can become constant after a
cover; they still have no invariants on the original base if nontrivial.
No H2-vanishing or local semisimplicity is being inferred from (3).

The full complement system B=H3(X minus F_s;Q) has

    0 -> L=Q(-2) -> B -> K=H2(F_s)(-1) -> 0,
    W4 K=(W2 H2(F_s))(-1).                              (4)

The pure weight4 sequence is globally semisimple by the audited Deligne

    (W4 B)^(pi1(S))=L.                                 (5)

In particular every compact-middle summand is globally nontrivial.

## 3. A total-complement check on the unique ambient fixed part

There is also a direct check of the weight used in (5). The total open
complement over the ENTIRE affine parameter line is

    U={(x,s):s!=f(x)} ~= X times C*,  r=s-f(x).

Thus H3(U)=Q(-2), since H2(X)=0. Delete its disjoint smooth parameter
fibres U_a=X minus F_a at the finitely many excluded values. Even when

sequence for these parameter divisors contains

    H3(U) -> H3(U_S) -> direct_sum_a H2(U_a)(-1).        (6)

The last term has weights>=5. Hence W4 H3(U_S) is precisely the image
of the single ambient Q(-2). That image is nonzero by restriction to a
nearby complement and the actual ambient lift. The complement Leray
edge map is onto for the same graph-base reason as (2); its MHS
strictness recovers (5). This calculation uses the actual nonproper
total space, not a proper invariant-cycle theorem with missing hypotheses.

## 4. The canonical geometric boundary quotient

Global semisimplicity of W4 B and (5) give a CANONICAL decomposition

    W4 B=L direct_sum C_f,
    C_f=sum_(g in pi1(S)) image(g-I)|W4 B.               (7)

C_f is the sum of all nontrivial global isotypic components, so it has
no trivial subquotient. The decomposition is geometric Hodge-theoretic:
the fixed part is a sub-VHS and its orthogonal complement for a pure
polarization is the unique nontrivial isotypic complement. Thus C_f is
a pure subvariation, isomorphic to W4 K in (4). Define

    B_boundary = B/C_f.                                 (8)

This is an actual geometric admissible quotient, not a chosen splitting
of nearby boundary homology. It has exact sequence

    0 -> Q(-2) -> B_boundary -> K/W4 K -> 0.             (9)

Its original weights/types are

    weight4: (2,2),
    weight5: (3,2),(2,3),
    weight6: (3,3).

After twist2 these are exactly the level-one types (0,0),(1,0),(0,1),
(1,1). This identifies its Hodge range, not an implemented algebraic
1-motive/curve presentation or an unproved period formula.

At EVERY P1 puncture, including infinity, take unipotent base change.
The pure subvariation C_f has its geometric limit, and its inclusion
into the limit of B preserves limiting F and relative M by canonical

    M_boundary=M_f/I_f.                                (11)

I_f is semisimple with NO trivial subquotients. In particular it cannot

Every differential morphism from I_f to the trivial connection is zero.

    Hom_D(M_boundary,C(s)) ~= Hom_D(M_f,C(s)),           (12)

## 6. The reduction commutes with EVERY polynomial recomposition

Let H be ANY nonconstant polynomial of degree d. On a sufficiently small

    0 -> L tensor ker(pi_*Q -> Q)
      -> pi_* B_f -> B_(H(f)) -> 0.                    (14)

It is geometric and strict for original W. Applying (7) on the primitive

    W4 B_(H(f)) = L direct_sum pi_* C_f.                (15)

Indeed (pi_* C_f) has no global invariants: they are H0(T,C_f)=0,
since deleting extra regular parameter values changes no primitive

    C_(H(f))=pi_* C_f,
    (B_boundary)_(H(f))
       ~= pi_* (B_boundary)_f / (trace-zero ambient L). (16)

Every branch still injects after restriction, by (9) on its residue

    (B_boundary)_f globally semisimple
      iff (B_boundary)_(H(f)) globally semisimple.       (17)

In particular global semisimplicity of the reduced source for ONE fixed

No purity of f or f-a is assumed.

## 7. When boundary genus weight vanishes: an intrinsic dichotomy

    J_boundary B_boundary subset L,

    full safety iff B_boundary globally semisimple.     (19)

This is not an existence theorem for pure composites and not a decision
