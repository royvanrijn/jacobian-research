# The exact moment ideal of the full axial Gaussian cubic slice

Use the original Gaussian Wick functional with E[ZW]=E[Y^2]=1 and
all other basic pairings zero. Put S=ZW and consider

    P=aY^3+bSY+cY^2+dS+eY+f.                         (A1)

All six coefficients are independent. Write mu_n=E[P^n] and let I_N
be the ideal generated by mu_1,...,mu_N over Q[a,b,c,d,e,f]. Then

    I_infinity=I_10, I_9!=I_10,
    m^11 subset I_10, m^10 not subset I_10,           (A2)

where m=(a,b,c,d,e,f). The quotient has length 546 and graded Hilbert
vector, starting in degree zero,

    (1,5,14,29,49,71,90,100,95,70,22).               (A3)

Thus ten is the LEAST generating moment prefix for the ordinary ideal,
and eleven is its exact coefficient-nilpotence index. Neither number
is asserted to be the least set-theoretic moment cutoff. These are
global polynomial-ideal statements, with no localization or completion.

The proof uses retained literal rational polynomials, exact S-polynomial
division and nonzero finite-field minors. It does not infer an infinite
tail from a tested moment prefix. The dimension argument making the
certificate conclusive is given below. Independent replay, formal
verification and external review are not claimed.

## 1. Exact reduction and an all-order moment formula

The first moment is mu_1=c+d+f. Eliminate it exactly by f=-c-d and
make the invertible linear change l=e+3a+b. The remaining phase is

    P=aH_3(Y)+b(S-1)Y+cH_2(Y)+d(S-1)+lY,
    H_3=Y^3-3Y, H_2=Y^2-1.                          (A4)

Let A=Q[a,b,c,d,l] and n=(a,b,c,d,l). Every mu_n after this reduction
is homogeneous of coefficient degree n. Let g_n be its primitive
integer polynomial, obtained by dividing by its positive integer
content. This does not change any ideal over Q. In particular

    g_2=6a^2+b^2+2c^2+d^2+l^2.                     (A5)

Here is an exact formula binding every generator to the original source.
For i+j+k+h+q=n, its coefficient in mu_n is

    n!/(i!j!k!h!q!) D_(j+h)
       *E_Y[H_3(Y)^i H_2(Y)^k Y^(j+q)],            (A6)

where D_r=E[(S-1)^r] is given by D_0=1,D_1=0 and
D_r=(r-1)(D_(r-1)+D_(r-2)). The ordinary Gaussian moments are zero
at odd degree and (2r-1)!! at degree 2r. Independence and the multinomial
theorem prove (A6) at EVERY order. The verifier regenerates g_2,...,g_10
from this formula and separately compares finite fixtures with direct
expansion of the literal two-variable polynomial in S,Y.

The quotient by mu_1 is a graded polynomial-ring isomorphism with A.
The image of m is n. It therefore suffices to prove (A2)-(A3) in A for

    J=(g_2,...,g_10).                                 (A7)

## 2. A certified rational Groebner superset

The compressed
[rational certificate](gaussian_axial_cubic_moment_ideal_certificate.json.gz)
contains 128 explicit homogeneous rational polynomials G. It is an
indispensable finite witness, not a stored CAS success flag. Use graded
reverse lexicographic order in a,b,c,d,l.

The verifier monic-normalizes these polynomials and checks all their
S-pairs. Of 8,128 pairs, 7,731 are explicitly reduced to zero by rational
polynomial division and 397 have coprime leading monomials. For the latter,
writing G_i=m_i+r_i and G_j=m_j+r_j gives the standard representation

    S(G_i,G_j)=r_i G_j-r_j G_i,

whose product leading monomials are strictly smaller than m_i m_j.
Buchberger's criterion therefore proves that the literal G is a Groebner
basis of its own ideal B=(G). No Groebner-generation oracle is called
during verification.

The same exact division checks g_2,...,g_10 reduce to zero, proving
J subset B. Counting monomials outside the leading-monomial ideal of G
gives precisely (A3), followed by zero in degree eleven. The class of
l^10 is a nonzero standard monomial. Consequently

    dim_Q(A/J)_r >= dim_Q(A/B)_r = h_r.              (A8)

This direction alone would not prove G belongs to J. The opposite
dimension bound is supplied independently; no unverified rational lift
or generic specialization is substituted for it.

## 3. Nonzero integer minors give the reverse bound over Q

For each degree r=2,...,11 form the LITERAL homogeneous Macaulay matrix
of J. Its rows are all monomial multiples of g_n, with 2<=n<=min(10,r)
and multiplier degree r-n. Its columns are all degree-r monomials in
five variables. Both orders are explicitly fixed in the
[minor certificate](gaussian_axial_cubic_macaulay_minors.json).

The certificate selects a square minor of size binomial(r+4,4)-h_r
and gives its nonzero determinant modulo the prime 32003. These sizes
are

    1,6,21,55,120,230,400,645,979,1365.               (A9)

The verifier reconstructs every indicated integer entry from (A6),
checks index ranges, uniqueness and prime status, and computes only
those finite determinants. It does not trust a rank or row-reduction
flag. A determinant nonzero modulo a prime is a nonzero integer, so
the corresponding rational matrix has at least that rank. It follows
that

    dim_Q(A/J)_r <= h_r, 2<=r<=11.                  (A10)

Degrees zero and one are immediate because J has no generator below
degree two. Combining (A8) and (A10) proves equality in every degree
through eleven. In degree eleven the selected minor is full column
rank, so J_11=A_11. Homogeneity then gives n^11 subset J, all higher
quotient degrees zero, and equality J=B globally. The nonzero standard
class l^10 proves n^10 is not contained in J. This proves the exact
Hilbert vector, length and nilpotence assertion without the large
expanded rational lift matrices used during generation.

## 4. The infinite tail and least generating prefix

For every n>=11 the ORIGINAL moment mu_n is homogeneous of coefficient
degree n. Thus it belongs to n^11 subset J. The lower moments are the
prefix itself. Therefore

    (mu_2,mu_3,...)=(mu_2,...,mu_10)                  (A11)

in A, and lifting through the exact linear elimination proves the
global equality I_infinity=I_10 in all six original parameters.
There are no intervening moments to check: the prefix ends at ten
and the proved automatic tail starts at eleven.

This is the zero-base instance of the
[mixed-cutoff finite-jet and certified-tail theorem](MIXED_CUTOFF_JETS_AND_INFINITE_TAILS.md).
At the pure base phase zero the original mixed cutoff is C=0, so the
moment r-jet budget is r. The actual coefficient-nilpotence certificate
of index eleven therefore leaves a tail threshold of ten. A generic
cubic bound C=3 is unnecessary at this particular base phase.

The rational certificate also contains a degree-ten linear functional
lambda on A_10, with 496 nonzero rational coefficients. The verifier
checks directly against the ORIGINAL g_n that

    lambda(u g_n)=0 for EVERY monomial u of degree10-n,
                                     n=2,...,9;
    lambda(g_10)=1.                                  (A12)

No omitted monomial multiplier or moment is allowed. Since the ideal
generated by the first nine moments is homogeneous, its degree-ten
part is spanned by exactly those products. Hence g_10 is not in that
ideal. This proves I_9!=I_10, independently of how the separator was
generated. It establishes least ORDINARY ideal prefix ten; it makes
no claim of least set-theoretic prefix, global cubic prefix, or
universal cubic cutoff in other slices.

## 5. Field and coefficient-algebra consequences

Over every characteristic-zero field, the pure locus of (A1) is exactly
the zero phase. Indeed (A2) makes every coefficient nilpotent in the
moment quotient, hence zero after evaluation in a field. Conversely
zero is pure. The ordinary moment scheme itself is nonreduced and has
the exact length in (A3); replacing it by its radical would lose the
nilpotence and least-prefix information.

All ideal identities hold after base change to ANY commutative Q-algebra.
If the first ten moments vanish there, the ideal of the six actual
coefficients has eleventh power zero. In particular P^11=0 as a spatial
polynomial, so every original mixed moment E[Q P^m] vanishes for m>=11.
This is a uniform nilpotent-coefficient conclusion, not a claim that
purity forces the polynomial to be zero over nonreduced rings.

There is also a separate imported finite-image classification on this
slice. For the full original polynomial-time image M_P,

    M_P finite iff a=b=c=d=0,
    M_P=exp(ft+e^2t^2/2)K[t] in those cases.          (A13)

To check the imports precisely, if b!=0 write
P=W(dZ+bZY)+G(Y). The
[arbitrary W-affine fibre theorem](GAUSSIAN_W_AFFINE_ARBITRARY_FIBRE_FINITE_IMAGES.md#criterion)
has nonconstant c(Z)=bZ and excludes finite M because its order is one.
If b=0, P=dS+g(Y) is an independent sum. Finiteness is equivalent to
finiteness of both block images by the
[independent-sum theorem](GAUSSIAN_PRIMITIVE_CLOSURE_UNDER_SUMS.md#2-independent-sums-preserve-and-reflect-finiteness).
The block dS has original vacuum (1-dt)^-1 and image K[t,(1-dt)^-1],
so is finite only for d=0. The one-ordinary-variable result in
[the arbitrary-fibre proof](GAUSSIAN_W_AFFINE_ARBITRARY_FIBRE_FINITE_IMAGES.md#one-ordinary-gaussian-variable-finite-image-means-affine-phase)
excludes deg g>=2 and proves (A13). Its exact absolute comparison import
is the Leading-coefficient obstruction, label `cor:leadingobstruction`,
in [polynomial_stationary_balls.tex](../../../unpublished/gaussian-boundary/polynomial_stationary_balls.tex):
for phase tg(Y)-Y^2/2-psi and scalar volume1/p, the spatial top has
infinity order -1 and the volume has order deg p>=0. It is an absolute
ordinary-Gaussian source, with no Gamma endpoint discarded.

Thus safety on the axial slice is also equivalent to zero phase:
(A13) and the original affine Gaussian formula force f=e=0 for safety.
If an arbitrary strict one-sided angular remainder is added, the
[angular-core theorem](GAUSSIAN_ANGULAR_CORE_FULL_IMAGE_INVARIANCE.md)
preserves the actual vacuum and full embedded M. Purity and safety
are consequently both equivalent to zero axial core in that larger
family. This safety deduction uses the stated imports; the finite
moment-ideal certificate alone is not used as an eventual-tail test
for arbitrary phases.

For comparison the older ternary-cubic purity-to-safety theorem is
`thm:cubic` in the retained
[Gaussian boundary manuscript](../../../unpublished/gaussian-boundary/manuscript.tex).
It is consistent with these conclusions, but is not a dependency of
the ideal certificate or its proof of the zero pure locus. No full
finite-image classification for arbitrary mixed-parity invariant cores
is inferred from this one finite-dimensional slice.

## 6. Replay and assurance

Run

    python verify_gaussian_axial_cubic_moment_ideal.py \
      --output gaussian_axial_cubic_moment_ideal_verification.json

from this directory. Python with python-flint is required. The
[portable verifier](verify_gaussian_axial_cubic_moment_ideal.py) uses
rational polynomial arithmetic, literal finite-field determinants and
the rational separator; its default replay calls no Singular, Groebner,
ideal-membership or row-reduction oracle. Singular 4.3.2 was used only
to generate candidate polynomials and the separator. The compressed
certificate is retained because its literal entries are indispensable.

The [receipt](gaussian_axial_cubic_moment_ideal_verification.json) pins
the proof, verifier, certificates, exact regenerated moments and linked
proof inputs. Corruption controls reject altered basis, dual, minor,
source or moment metadata. The infinite tail is proved by homogeneity
and the certified eleventh ideal power. This is a complete finite
certificate proof for this slice, not an extrapolation of finite
moments, a formal verification or an independently reviewed result.
