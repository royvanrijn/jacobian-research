## 1. The all-order finite-jet lemma

For degree-at-most-three directions Q1,...,Qk,

    D^k mu_n(F0)[Q1,...,Qk]
       =(n)_k h(Q1...Qk F0^(n-k)).                      (2)

The product marker has degree at most 3k. The sharp
[Rodrigues shear cutoff](CONFORMAL_EULER_MIXED_CUBIC_CLASSIFICATION.md)
annihilates it when n-k>9k. This proves the first claim; derivatives
of order greater than n already vanish. Pure nullity handles the
constant Taylor coefficient. Thus mu_n belongs to m^(r+1) whenever
n>10r, which proves (1).

For the harmonic direction w^3 and r>=1, the same sharp identity gives

    D_(w^3)^r mu_(10r)(F0)
       =(-1/2)^(3r) (10r)!/(18r+1)!! !=0.                (3)

The threshold is sharp for individual derivatives. It does not establish
minimality of the finite ideal-generating list in (1).

## 2. A full transverse slice, with no restriction on H1

Use the harmonic basis in increasing degree and charge:

    1;
    w,t,z;
    w^2,wt,t^2-zw,zt,z^2;
    w^3,w^2t,w(t^2-zw/2),t^3-3zwt,
    z(t^2-zw/2),z^2t,z^3.                               (4)

Let J be the 10x16 matrix with entries n h(B_j F0^(n-1)), n=1,...,10.
Exact rational arithmetic gives rank J=10. A pivot set, zero based
in (4), is

    C=(0,1,2,3,4,5,6,9,10,11).

The orbit tangent is generated by F0 and the three rotation derivatives

[Euler note](CONFORMAL_EULER_MIXED_CUBIC_CLASSIFICATION.md).
These have rank four. Together with

    G1=tzw-t^3/3+(17/21)t+(10/3)w^2,
    G2=wz^2-2t^2z-5tw+z,                               (5)

they form a basis of ker J. Later moments add no linear constraints by
(1) with r=1, so the all-moment tangent dimension is six.

Choose the affine slice

    F=F0+aG1+bG2+sum_(j in C) u_j B_j.                  (6)

Its tangent, together with the orbit tangent, spans V. The scalar and
rotation action therefore identifies the completed ambient germ with
the product of its four smooth orbit parameters and this slice. Every
moment pulls back to a unit (the scalar parameter to power n) times
its slice restriction. Thus this slice detects every local branch.

The constant Jacobian J_C is invertible. The first ten equations have
unique formal solutions u_j=u_j(a,b), with no constant or linear part.
Write their substituted phase as

    F(a,b)=F0+G+H_2+H_3+...,
    G=aG1+bG2,

where H_r has PARAMETER degree r and spherical polynomial degree<=3.
These H_r denote corrections, not the spherical harmonic components.
Set R_n(a,b)=mu_n(F(a,b)), so R_1=...=R_10=0 identically.

## 5. Two weighted initial forms isolate the whole local pure locus

Give a,b weights 3,2. Equations (9)--(11) yield

    in_w(R_11)=-64000 P/123934268957499,
    in_w(R_12)=-4096 Q/868407422585195493,

with

    P=ab(1265477a^2-4866582b^3),                         wt(P)=11,
    Q=5242521625a^4-174930621075a^2b^3
                         +112837775358b^6,             wt(Q)=12.  (12)

All omitted terms have strictly higher weight. Indeed parameter degree
at least seven has weight at least fourteen. At degree six, the only
term of weight at most twelve is b^6, already computed in (11).
The complete degree-four and degree-five residuals establish that no
other lower-weight terms occur. No infinite-order assumption enters (12).

These initial forms have no common nonzero zero. On a=0, Q is a nonzero
multiple of b^6; on b=0, it is a nonzero multiple of a^4. Otherwise P=0
requires

    a^2/b^3=4866582/1265477,

and substitution into the quadratic

    5242521625 X^2-174930621075 X+112837775358

is nonzero, as verified over Q. Hence P,Q are a regular sequence with
finite quotient. Their lexicographic Groebner basis has leading monomials

    a^4, a^3b, a^2b^4, ab^7, b^10.                     (13)

There are exactly 22 standard monomials:

    b^j (0<=j<=9),  ab^j (0<=j<=6),
    a^2b^j (0<=j<=3),  a^3.

Their maximum weight is eighteen and maximum ordinary degree nine.

For completeness, the filtered-ring input used here is the following
elementary lemma. If two formal series have positive-weight initial
forms forming a regular sequence, the associated graded quotient by
those series is the quotient by their initial forms. To see this,
cancel any leading relation between the two initial forms by its Koszul
syzygy; regularity says every such relation has that form. Lift the
cancellation to the series and repeat at strictly increasing weight.
Completeness makes this division valid. This also proves the reverse
initial-ideal containment, not just a length upper bound.

Applying the lemma to R_11,R_12 gives

    gr_w C[[a,b]]/(R_11,R_12) = C[a,b]/(P,Q).             (14)

The completed first-twelve transverse algebra therefore has length 22.
All weight>=19 pieces vanish. Its maximal ideal has tenth power zero,
while b^9 remains nonzero, so its nilpotence index is exactly ten.
Its reduced support is the origin.

The Rodrigues orbit consists of all-moment pure points. Since the first

The orbit has dimension four, is irreducible, and is contained in the

## 6. What is and is not known about the full ordinary local scheme

calculation does not claim that they impose no additional nilpotent

UPPER BOUND for the all-moment length.

No moments through ninety were computed or need be computed for this
