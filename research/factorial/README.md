# Factorial moments and rigidity

<!-- Generated from research/MATH_STATUS.json; run make render-navigation. -->

Factorial moment functionals, composition rigidity and Strong Factorial families. Coefficient-uniform windows are proved for all quadratic phases in every dimension. Ordinary two-monomial coprimality in arbitrary dimension first remains at terminal fifteen. The initial homogeneous bivariate trinomial window has a finite global degree reduction and is the immediate completion priority. The arbitrary-dimensional two-monomial residual still contains infinitely many exponent profiles.

**Authored assessment:** Priority: finish the initial homogeneous bivariate trinomial window at orders 1, 2, 3. A computable absolute degree bound reduces every possible failure to a finite exact residual; the bound and full residual are unevaluated, so practical feasibility is unknown. A uniform resultant/remainder sign proof is an alternative finish. Treat the arbitrary-dimensional two-monomial obstruction at exceptional Gaussian limits as a separate research project. Ordinary univariate binomial Strong Factorial is proved at every terminal over every characteristic-zero field. The proof combines the terminal-through-24 theorem, the exact prime, Newton and modular certificate through terminal 3149, and the uniform Padé cutoff at terminal 3150. For arbitrary ordinary multivariate binomials, terminals 2 through 14 and every terminal at or above a computable absolute cutoff are proved. Both-private supports are covered in every degree, and one-private supports are covered when the private total exponent is at least five. For 2<=P<=4 the registered sufficient lower bounds on shared curvature are 73/9, 37/6, 38/9 respectively. Unrestricted coprimality for the remaining multivariate supports and the larger-support conjecture remain open.
