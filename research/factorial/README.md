Scope limits:

- Research allocation: ordinary degree <=5 is complete by the scalar cyclic minor. The next exact degree-six frontier is supports {2,6}, {3,6}, {4,6} at terminal N>=6. Higher-degree ordinary binomials and positive shapes outside the proved domains remain open.
- Unrestricted FC and SFC remain open. Universal special-family theorems do not supply an arbitrary-support reduction.
- Earlier 26/27, no-carry, adjacent-part, residual/parity and all-prime filters retain their proved scopes. They are unnecessary for ordinary degree-five completion; no-carry coverage failure and a surviving local degree are not SFC counterexamples.
- Every prime-power lower-one family X(A+BX^(p^s)) satisfies ordinary SFC at every index, and gap-two lower exponents 2,3 are proved at every positive real shape. The scalar-minor theorem completes ordinary support {2,5}, hence every ordinary degree <=5 binomial. Full binomial SFC and all-real-shape H3 total nonnegativity remain open.
- The gap-two families X^2(A+BX^2) and X^3(A+BX^2) satisfy SFC at every index and every positive real Gamma shape by an exact adjoint elimination and Hurwitz homotopy. Larger lower exponents at gap two remain open.
- Ordinary support {2,5} is proved at every terminal order by projective three-adic balance, a fixed scalar minor with affine unit reduction, and real-field descent. The former N=11 mod27 ray is resolved; the earlier residue theorems remain valid.
- The pencil and coupled-family descriptions retain the scale, Gamma-shape, block and ordinary coefficient-ring normalization restrictions stated in their proofs.
- Higher-gap rank and backward-pivot results are proved criteria. Uniform actual-family rank/nonzero-pivot assertions remain open; finite controls do not discharge them. Priority and independent review are separate obligations.
- New gap-three certificates cover entire infinite matrices for n=2..16 and all beta>=0; initial minors and pivots have separate unbounded-index scopes. Prime-power terminal families and arbitrary-support initial pairs do not close all univariate binomial SFC.
- Effective large-gap and large-lower-exponent bounds reduce each fixed ordinary terminal order to an explicit finite support rectangle; all rectangles as N varies are still unchecked. Fixed-shape auxiliary positive networks do not yet transfer through the signed cyclic Wronskian map.
- All lower exponents and gaps are separated through terminal five for ordinary factorials and through terminal four for every positive Gamma shape. Arithmetic criteria, all-j root-scale monotonicity and the diagonal derivative embedding do not discharge the remaining all-N>=6 rectangles or uniform gap-three H3 total nonnegativity.
- A uniform all-gap identity now normalizes the moment discriminant as an (n-1)-square cyclic Hurwitz determinant. Its nonnegativity is proved for every positive shape, giving the last three gap-three special minors nonnegative at all indices. Strictness is now proved at prime-power gaps for positive rational p-integral shapes; all-real-shape strictness and the remaining intermediate minors are open.
- The all-order formal Gamma-power cumulant fraction and its positive tree coefficients are recorded separately from fixed-shape Hankel nonnegativity. The latter retains an explicit PSZ theorem/application verification input; transferring either structure to consecutive binomial moments at two different shapes remains an open mathematical gap.
- The actual three-adic quintic interpolation has a unique integral common point on m=1+27tau, so uniform all-three-adic noncollision is false. New parity proves neither tau_star nor m_star is a nonnegative ordinary integer. Irrationality and negative-integer exclusion are not asserted; the five-adic analytic-index questions remain open.
- EIT II–IV and original Kontsevich–Zagier are separately inventoried in EIT_KONTSEVICH_ZAGIER_AUDIT.md; compact line/tensor completeness, relative functional comparison, simplex visibility and FC/SFC implications have distinct scopes and open obligations.
- Quintic five-adic scalar Frobenius closure is proved for seven offsets; the coupled joint-rank descent remains open. The canceled analytic index family has two common pairs and a controlled nonzero tail, yielding necessary integer proximity rather than an ordinary SFC counterexample.
- Universal nondivisibility and no-carry prime-power index/factor-degree criteria are proved. Generic and transcendental Gamma-shape results are distinct from ordinary beta1; the positive algebraic specialization countercontrol prevents that inference.
- Residual/parity refinements remain subject to the exact no-carry premise. Universal no-carry pair coverage is false; the independent all-prime degree criterion has no such premise, but uniform degree-set emptiness remains open.
- The Gamma-shape resultant now has exact all-index degree, positive leading coefficient and negative-shape divisors. Its nonnegative positive-axis values reduce the remaining problem to positive zeros of a repeated cofactor part. Universal cofactor positivity or suitable squarefreeness remains open. Beta-Hurwitz stability is refuted by a fixed degree112 positive-coefficient certificate; this resultant route alone does not close an ordinary binomial or all-positive-shape goal; ordinary degree five is completed separately by the scalar minor.
- The separately indexed algebraic-Gamma domain theorem strengthens the fixed-monomial radial family from rational p-integral total shape to positive algebraic total shape with one embedding in O_Cp at prime gaps, or inZp+p^s O_Cp at proper prime-power gaps. Individual positive shapes need not be algebraic. It does not cover every positive algebraic shape or arbitrary integral proper-prime-power parameters.
- Prime-gap SFC covers every positive algebraic shape integral at one place above the gap prime, hence every positive algebraic integer such as sqrt(2). Together with transcendental-shape coprimality, any possible positive prime-gap exception must be algebraic and have negative valuation at every place above that prime; its primitive minimal polynomial is constant and nonzero modulo p. These necessary conditions do not assert failure, and proper-prime-power domains remain restricted.
- Current completion: the scalar-minor theorem proves ordinary support {2,5} at every positive consecutive pair and completes ordinary degree <=5, including the formerly remaining N>=11,N=11 mod27 ray. Earlier 8/9, 26/27, five-adic, residual and Newton theorems retain their exact original scopes. Their stronger joint-descent, analytic-index separation and uniform E(n)-emptiness questions remain optional open statements, unnecessary for ordinary quintic completion.
- The degree-six frontier is an equivalence to three unrestricted families at terminal N>=6. Neither this reduction nor ordinary degree-five completion proves those families or the full all-binomial target.
