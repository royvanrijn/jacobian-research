# All constant shifts of binary quadratics with nonpolynomial residue on a quarter grid

Let E be a characteristic-zero nonarchimedean valued field with v(2)=1 and

                       v(E*) contained in (1/4)Z.          (1)

Take a naturally integral nonconstant binary quadratic

 q=alpha*X^2/2+beta*XY+gamma*Y^2/2+dX+eY,
 alpha,beta,gamma,d,e in O_E,
 R_r=Lambda(q^r)/r!, R0=1.

The constant k may lie in ANY valued extension of E. All coefficient grids in this statement belong to the NONCONSTANT coefficient field, not automatically to the field generated by k.

The [native coefficient proof](QUADRATIC_CONSTANT_NONCRITICAL_DYADIC_WINDOW_TRANSFER.md) proves integrality and the exact formal residue

 Rbar(T)=N(T)/D(T),
 N=1+(dbar+ebar+betabar)T+dbar*ebar*T^2,
 D=(1+alphabar*T+dbar^2*T^2)(1+gammabar*T+ebar^2*T^2).       (2)

**Theorem 1 (quarter-grid all-constant six-window).** If Rbar is NONPOLYNOMIAL, then for every constant k in every valued extension of E, q+k has no six consecutive zero factorial moments at any positive start. No rank-one, Hessian, fixed-correlation or two-pole hypothesis is imposed.

**Corollary 2 (dense actual-support SFC).** If additionally all five nonconstant coefficients are nonzero, q+k satisfies its ACTUAL-support strong factorial window for every constant k. The actual support is five at k=0 and six at k!=0. This statement does not assign sparse phases their smaller actual-support window by restriction.

## 1. Complete proof and valued-extension scope

If a constant is transcendental over E, it cannot be a zero of any monic P_m(Z)=Lambda((q+Z)^m), so every moment is nonzero. Otherwise k is algebraic. For every nonzero a in E(k), a minimal polynomial over E has at least two distinct least-valued terms at a. Therefore some positive integer times v(a) belongs to (1/4)Z. An ordered value group is torsion-free; the restriction to E(k) is consequently rational and real-valued. This reduction makes the real-valued hypotheses in the two new critical proofs applicable even when the originally supplied extension valuation has higher rank. k=0 is treated separately.

If the reduced denominator of Rbar has at least two geometric poles, the installed [two-pole constant-shift theorem](BINARY_QUADRATIC_TWO_POLE_CONSTANT_SHIFTS_STRONG_FACTORIAL_WINDOWS.md) already gives six for every constant. Assume therefore it has exactly one geometric pole.

The [native single-pole classification](BINARY_QUADRATIC_SINGLE_POLE_RESIDUE_UNIT_WINDOWS.md) proves a positive unit-coefficient window w<=2 except for the exact cube

                         Rbar=1/(1+rho*T)^3.               (3)

In the noncube cases, the installed [grid transfer](QUADRATIC_CONSTANT_APPELL_MULTIPLICITIES_FROM_DISCRETE_VALUE_GRIDS.md) with e0=4 gives w+4<=6 for ALL constants. Reduced even-order poles may be named over a residue-field extension; coefficient nonvanishing is unchanged, and no blanket pole-membership assertion is needed.

For the cube, the same classification gives, up to swapping X and Y,

 alphabar=ebar=0, gammabar=dbar=betabar=rho!=0.

The coefficient d is an E-unit. Divide the WHOLE phase by d. This preserves every moment zero, actual support, constant valuation and the coefficient value-grid condition(1). The normalized natural residues become

 alphabar=ebar=0, betabar=gammabar=dbar=1.

All strictly positive valuations of the four E-elements alpha,e,beta-1,gamma-1 are at least1/4. Thus the explicit quarter-gap holds; it has not been inferred merely from the 2-part of a larger grid denominator.

Put delta=v(k). For k=0, the nonpolynomial residue already has a positive unit window <=4. If delta<0 every moment is nonzero; delta=0 gives a two-window; delta>=1 transfers a unit window. At noncritical 0<delta<1 the [noncritical coefficient theorem](QUADRATIC_CONSTANT_NONCRITICAL_DYADIC_WINDOW_TRANSFER.md) transfers the same unit window. At delta=1/2 its elementary four-window applies. These cases all exclude a six-zero block.

At delta=1-1/L,L=2^a>=4, only the cube critical case remains. For L=4, use the [quarter-gap native exclusion](BINARY_QUADRATIC_QUARTER_GAP_NATIVE_SIX_WINDOW.md). Its full written inputs are the [critical residue profile](BINARY_QUADRATIC_QUARTER_CRITICAL_RESIDUE_PROFILE.md), the [lifted native profile](BINARY_QUADRATIC_QUARTER_GRID_LIFTED_NATIVE_PROFILE.md), and the [uniform modulo-four/parity reduction](BINARY_QUADRATIC_NATIVE_MOD_FOUR_AND_QUARTER_PARITY_REDUCTION.md). The native weighted identity makes E6+E7 have valuation exactly one, contradicting the modulo-four necessity for every n=8H+2.

For L=8, the [higher critical cube proof](BINARY_QUADRATIC_QUARTER_GRID_HIGHER_CRITICAL_CUBE_WINDOWS.md), Section4, uses the exact least-cost width12 and root multiplicity six. The minimal initial factor has width two and is a binomial in the square variable; its sixth power has only exponent offsets0,4,8,12. The actual unit coefficient at offset2 is a contradiction. For L>=16, Section3 of the same proof uses the universal first-order native Taylor identity: two required extreme R-coefficients have valuations3/4 and1/4, but their indices agree modulo8 and their difference must have valuation at least1/2. These arguments cover every remaining critical radius and prove Theorem1.

For Corollary2, k=0 has a four-window and actual support five, while k!=0 has the six-window and actual support six. No conclusion about smaller actual supports follows just from these inequalities.

## 2. A genuine necessary frontier beyond the quarter grid

**Corollary 3 (coefficient-field obstruction to a six-zero counterexample).** Suppose q has naturally integral coefficients in a real-valued characteristic-zero coefficient field and its native residue is nonpolynomial. If some constant shift has six consecutive zero moments, that coefficient value group is NOT contained in (1/4)Z. The installed necessary residue frontier also forces k!=0, delta>=3/4 a higher critical radius, and complete cancellation of a single geometric pole by 1+mu*T^L. These are necessary restrictions only, not counterexample existence.

If the coefficient field is a finite extension of Q2 with its MINIMAL ramification index e_E, the combined grid results give

                          4 divides e_E and e_E>=8.       (4)

Indeed the older small-grid theorem excludes 4 not dividing e_E, and the new theorem excludes e_E=4. Equation(4) does NOT say 8 divides e_E: e_E=12 remains outside this conclusion. For an abstract nonminimal grid bound e0 it is wrong to infer 8 divides e0; the invariant statement is the failure of actual quarter-grid containment. A field on a quarter grid can also be presented using a larger nonminimal bound divisible by an odd factor.

For a grid bound e0 with v2(e0)=2, the native single-pole classification and the older multiplicity transfer further show that a remaining one-pole counterexample must be the cube(3). This does not provide the quarter-gap or quarter-step cost constraints when e0=4u with odd u>1.

## 3. Normalization and exact limits

Given a nonconstant binary phase over a coefficient field satisfying(1), divide the WHOLE phase by one of its five natural nonconstant coefficients realizing their minimum valuation. This element belongs to E and preserves zeros and nonzero monomials. The theorem applies when the resulting integral native residue is nonpolynomial. A constant-only phase has no vanishing positive moment and needs no normalization.

The pole, profile, lift, modulo-four, weighted-native and higher-radius arguments concern exact native moments. They do not use an arbitrary holonomic state or a numerical prefix. Their complete written proofs accompany this assembly; the four installed CWB proof modules remain unchanged mathematical inputs.

Polynomial residue series, coefficient groups not contained in the quarter grid, and smaller sparse actual-support critical windows remain outside Theorem1. There is no assertion that arbitrary complex coefficients possess a normalization over the quarter grid. The unrestricted all-start binary-quadratic SFC, SFC(2), SFC(3), and the old anti-coefficient gate are not closed by this theorem. No converse from a necessary exception to an actual zero block is asserted.
