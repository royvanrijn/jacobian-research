# Actual primary scope for the moving-disc localization

The actual published PDF of Martin Lamprecht, *A continuous link between
the disk and half-plane cases of Grace's theorem*, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris,
Ser. I353(2015),11--15, DOI10.1016/j.crma.2014.10.017, was opened and read.
Theorem1.3 on printed13 states the precise degree-n Schur--Szego
composition conclusion used here: if a circular domain contains all roots
of the first polynomial, a composition root is the negative product of
a point of that domain and a root of the second. The domain may be an
open or closed disc; the exterior-disc proviso is irrelevant here.
