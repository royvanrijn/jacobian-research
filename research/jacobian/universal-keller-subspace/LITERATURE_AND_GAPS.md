# Literature and exact added scope

14 November 2026. The canonical proof is [PROOF.md](PROOF.md). This note separates the established algebraic ingredients from this repository extension. No priority or unpublishedness certificate is claimed.

- [Kemper--Mattig, Generic Polynomials with Few Parameters](https://www.math.cit.tum.de/fileadmin/w00ccg/algebra/people/kemper/kem.generic_few.pdf), primary full text: Definition1 explicitly quantifies over infinite extension fields. Theorem11 includes affine groups between special and general linear groups, with cyclic-complement line cases when the vector rank is one. This supplies classical generic-polynomial context. It does not assert a constant-Jacobian self-map of a whole affine space or the finite-base minima established here.
- [Morales--Sanchez, Generic extensions and generic polynomials for multiplicative groups](https://arxiv.org/html/1405.7807), Sections2--3: generic Galois algebras, Hilbert90 and disconnected induced G algebras are explicit. Our additive and multiplicative Hilbert90 uses are classical; the displayed frame and its global polynomial completion are proved in our note rather than inferred from the multiplicative-group theorem.
- [Stacks, Kummer theory, Section59.28](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03PK): a prime-to-characteristic cyclic torsor on affine space is constant up to its base class. This is the published input behind UAFINITE1's one-variable exclusion. Wild translation covers are allowed.
- [Existing canonical line classification and full-field cyclic-affine family](../universal-keller-symmetry/PROOF.md): UA1CANCLASS1 already allowed non-field subspaces and gave their exact field-of-definition criterion. UAAFFSUB1's universal binomial family assumed F_q constants. UASUBDEFORM1 removes that extra assumption wherever a line model actually exists, by applying a composition-preserving weighted deformation to its normalized subspace polynomial.
- [Finite-base point budget](../universal-keller-symmetry/PROOF.md): UAFINCOUNT1 already proves the weighted conjugacy-class bound and translation minima. UASCALARDIM1 evaluates that bound exactly as q+m-1, adds scalar-compatible product constructions over insufficient-constant bases, and obtains exact fixed framed minima when f divides e. It keeps the nondivisible finite-base gap open.
- [Primitive monodromy and stable atomicity](../../foundations/verified/PRIMITIVE_MONODROMY_ATOMICITY.md): the archived statement is in characteristic zero, while its field, open-immersion and unit arguments apply in every characteristic. Section1 of our proof states the characteristic-free degree-one lemma explicitly. Subspace flags, not an unproved converse, give the polynomial decompositions here.

The search supports placing these results beside classical generic-polynomial and Frobenius-module constructions. It does not establish that the precise weighted formulas or consequences have never appeared. The canonical receiver obstruction in characteristic zero is unchanged.

The same weighted construction also admits an explicit finite frame normalization A_(W_i)(z_i)=t*v_i, u=t^m. Its scalar orbits give every boundary branch and ramification index, while x_i=z_i/t identifies exactly the branches omitted by the affine source. This strengthens the concrete boundary analysis without importing characteristic-zero inertia generation into characteristic p.
