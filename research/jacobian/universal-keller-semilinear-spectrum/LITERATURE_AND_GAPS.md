# Arithmetic transfer and the exact remaining boundaries

Reviewed15 November2026. [Proof](PROOF.md) and [source pins](sources.json) separate the classical inputs from the repository deductions. No AI-assistance attribution or publication-priority claim is made.

Jianing Li and Chia-Fu Yu, [The Chevalley-Gras formula over global fields](https://arxiv.org/html/2001.11413v2), Example2.5(1), give the general cyclic ordinary class-group formula, including infinite ramification. Applied to Q(zeta_ell)/Q for prime ell, the real place contributes2 and the unit-norm index is2. The invariant class group is trivial, so sigma-1 is bijective for a Galois generator. This is not an assumption that the whole class group is trivial. The shorter Lemmermeyer note treats prime-degree cyclic extensions; that narrower statement is not substituted for the general formula.

Bang-Zsigmondy's primitive-divisor theorem supplies a prime ell with ord_ell(p)=e outside exactly two relevant exceptional cases. The [author-written Michels proof](sources/michels-zsigmondy-2014.pdf), page1, was visually inspected, with the theorem and both exceptions clearly legible. Original source: Zsigmondy1892, DOI10.1007/BF01692444. The exceptions have direct Gaussian and ninth-cyclotomic constructions; neither Artin's conjecture nor a class-number-one conjecture is used.

The [previous isogeny package](../universal-keller-versal-isogenies/PROOF.md) required commuting maps on a fixed lattice when stating its principal-prime criterion. The new equation [P]=(sigma-1)[I] allows a different ideal lattice and a holonomy automorphism, with a compatible automorphism on the free base. It bypasses that specific obstruction without invalidating it. The degree2048 control is genuinely nonprincipal: the quadratic prime has order3, and relative norm prevents its degree11 extension from becoming principal.

Puncturing the canonical covolume line keeps the volume construction within geometric torus quotients. Field realization images persist because every one-dimensional field line has a nonzero point. A nonzero fixed marked covolume exists only when the tangent determinant character is trivial; the zero-section lift has a different exact boundary. Scheme-family images can shrink under puncturing and never improve by such base change, consistently with the published all-affine obstruction.

The sharp degree spectrum concerns strong atomicity in finite torus-quotient self-geometry and field versality for specified groups or group schemes. It is not full S_N universality in arbitrary rank. Those self-covers retain the symmetric ceiling4 and nonconstant-unit obstruction. The original higher-degree nonfinite affine canonical receiver, simultaneous fixed-locus landing/gluing, smaller-marked-rank ambient atomicity, modular affine cases and A1-rich pseudo-plane mixing remain unresolved. Prescribing every primitive affine group or proving dimension minima is not claimed.
