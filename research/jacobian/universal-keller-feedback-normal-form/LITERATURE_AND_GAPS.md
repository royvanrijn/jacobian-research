[Stacks Section53.12](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0C1B) gives the characteristic-zero Riemann-Hurwitz formula. [Stacks Theorem41.16.1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/025J) and [formal etaleness](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02HF) give the unique nilpotent lift. Consequently G=F composed A near identity is a classical formal-etale fact valid over arbitrary base rings, without any Jacobian conjecture. Our application is the distinction between formal source coordinates and finite-degree polynomial source coordinates for the explicit marked scalar family, with an all-order degree-escape certificate.

We consulted that scope as context. Its full classification is not imported. The stable signal-field normalizer here is proved directly from the one reducible fibre, the generic critical cylinder and whole-source polynomial landing, and applies to Keller endomorphisms without assuming invertibility.
