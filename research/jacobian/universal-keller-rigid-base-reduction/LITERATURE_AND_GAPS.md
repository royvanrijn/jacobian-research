# Literature inputs, scope and remaining gaps

Reviewed 15 November 2026. Primary sources are pinned in [sources.json](sources.json); [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) distinguishes classical ingredients from the new repository deductions. Narrow web searches do not establish novelty or absence of a published result.

1. Jia Jia, Takahiro Shibata, Junyi Xie and De-Qi Zhang, [Endomorphisms of quasi-projective varieties](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.05339v1), v1, 12 April2021, Lemma2.20. Their algebraic torus closure is precisely the classical translation-free finite Galois torus cover used here. Lemmas2.16 and2.19 supply characteristic-zero base change and finite torus-cover facts. The normalizer formula and the particular W centralizer are derived in our proof; the torus closure is not claimed as new.
2. Aravind Asok and Paul Arne Ostvaer, [A1-homotopy theory and contractible varieties: a survey](https://arxiv.org/html/1903.07851v1), v1, Definition4.1.6 and Remark4.1.7. Relative A1-rigidity is established terminology. We apply it to geometric torus quotients and use it to isolate a genuine vertical Keller map in every remaining canonical attempt on the metabelian base.
3. Retained [Smith proofs](../universal-keller-smith/PROOF.md), [fixed-fibre normalization budget](../universal-keller-fixed-fibres/PROOF.md) and [acyclic rigidity/quadratic proofs](../universal-keller-acyclic-boundary/PROOF.md). Whole ell-subgroups were already allowed. The elementary Sylow-stabilizer observation removes the need for the earlier classification-dependent prime-power derangement theorem and extends the even-degree +3 budget to every transitive label group.
4. The [fixed universal quartic construction](../universal-keller-torus-quotient-selfmaps/PROOF.md) remains valid. Its geometric S4 monodromy and primitive atomicity do not give a faithful S4 automorphism action on its source quotient. The exact metabelian automorphism group now excludes that action, including all finite coupled cylinders.

The useful approach found here is classical characteristic-cover and A1-rigidity theory, combined with the repository fixed-fibre budgets. No recent AI-assisted breakthrough is being cited as a verified input to these proofs. No new quartic affine canonical construction has been found.

The precise surviving gate on a rigid metabelian cylinder is an actual canonical polynomial Keller map of the same affine-fibre dimension r. In rank4 it requires r>=5 and generic degree at least7D, where D is the base degree. Finite coefficient promotion cannot help. Different receiver geometries, effective simultaneous fixed-locus landing, modular p-power ranks and the A1-rich pseudo-plane cubic case remain open. Higher generic degree with smaller marked rank still needs a separate atomicity proof.
