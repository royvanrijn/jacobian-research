# Every intermediate factor has a generic surface

Every native whole polynomial Keller map is indecomposable on the target side in the class of factorizations retaining its signal and identities, including all negative Laurent-r sectors. The previous intermediate boundary-pole alternative is closed by restricting to the embedded source curve.

- [General proofs](PROOF.md): embedded-curve cancellation for a plane Keller target, relative affine-plane factor rigidity and the complete necessary surface diagram for arbitrary stabilized factors.
- After arbitrary identity stabilization and intermediate coordinate mixing, a noninvertible target factor must have a NON-PLANE geometric generic retained fibre. That surface is smooth, geometrically integral, an affine complete intersection, parallelizable, carries a whole A1 component, has unit rank at most one and satisfies two exact polynomial saturation identities. The [unit-rigidity extension](../universal-keller-unit-rigidity/PROOF.md) now closes the nonconstant-unit side: a PROPER intermediate has CONSTANT geometric units, and a noninvertible source prefix has an all-irreducible-constant-fibre whole signal component.
- Generic surface self-maps of every degree exist and illustrate why whole-source lifting and volume constraints are separate.
- [Literature](LITERATURE_AND_GAPS.md), [checker](verify.py), [receipt](receipt.json), [sources](sources.json), [integration](integration.json), [focused validation](validation.json), [requirements](requirements.txt).

These are factor obstructions, not full absolute stable atomicity of every native map or a canonical atomic receiver. Independent proof review and novelty assessment remain open.
The unit theorem covers every whole retained-signal Keller map, without native jets. A whole recovered power forces primitive unit index; a cyclic generic deck action would otherwise fix the retained node and contradict etaleness. The [log-Picard extension](../universal-keller-log-picard/PROOF.md) now excludes geometric factoriality for proper native factors: their log Kodaira dimension is negative and their nonzero geometric Picard group has no retained-field Galois invariants. Arithmetic factoriality and an exact volume remain necessary; the higher-power models are not constructed factors.

The [plane-chart extension](../universal-keller-plane-charts/PROOF.md) uses this WHOLE carried curve: if its entire image fits in ANY open A2 chart of the intermediate, plane cancellation and an open-and-closed section invert the entire target. Formal curve neighborhoods still look like the standard plane and do not produce a global chart. Other intermediate surfaces remain open.
