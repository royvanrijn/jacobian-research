# Literature and exact remaining boundaries

Reviewed15 November2026. [Proof](PROOF.md) and [source pins](sources.json) distinguish classical inputs from their applications here. No publication-priority or AI-assistance claim is made.

James Ax, *Injective endomorphisms of varieties and schemes*, Pacific Journal of Mathematics31(1),1969,1-7, [primary paper](sources/ax-1969.pdf), printed page1 theorem and Corollary1. The statement was read and its whole page visually inspected. Combined with Zariski Main, it makes every birational etale self-map of a normal variety an automorphism in every characteristic. It removes the need for an acyclic rigidity argument for SELF-MAP factors. It does not make a birational etale map between different varieties an isomorphism.

The [Stacks Project, Section37.43](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02LQ), Lemmas37.43.2-3, supplies the relative-normalization open immersion and finite completion. Applied to an etale map of degree greater than one, it proves immediately that a nonfinite map cannot be atomic through all normal affine intermediate varieties. Its finite completion can ramify; this is not a factorization statement in a category requiring both factors etale.

The retained [A1-rigidity and torus-closure proof](../universal-keller-rigid-base-reduction/PROOF.md) supplies the intrinsic base and dominant-base finiteness. The new atomic dichotomy uses those results and elementary multiplication of field degrees. Combined with a complete marked fibre it removes the earlier metabelian automorphism restriction. The relative version restricts canonically to a pair of affine-space forms and supplies no coordinate trivialization or automatic identification of the pair.

The dilation f_a=a^-1 f(a v), continued polynomially at a=0, is standard. The new deductions here apply the generic vertical primitive criterion and the intrinsic Gm projection to obtain fixed-degree atomic families and the stable degree-drop invariant. The [retained cubic compiler](../../foundations/verified/UNIVERSAL_RELATIVE_KELLER_MAP.md) supplies one explicit primitive seed. UAALLATOM1 and UAQTILT1 in the [structure proof](../universal-keller-structure/PROOF.md) supply constant-profile three-variable seeds in every degree N>=3. They give the exact nonfinite degree spectrum on the single fourfold Gm times A3, with infinitely many atomic volume-preserving representatives in every allowed degree. The excluded degree2 is the retained quadratic/Galois Keller case. The general theorem needs no new special-case search.

The recent primary paper [Orbits and fields of definition for graded Keller maps](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02863), Sections3-4 and6, studies quotient-cover monodromy for two explicit graded families. Such quotient-cover statements are not silently substituted for a whole-map generic monodromy calculation. Recent graded-Keller and universal-torsor literature was checked as context. None is used to assert a canonical symmetric action, to identify a torus cylinder with affine space, or to turn fieldwise realizations into an all-scheme universal receiver. The scheme-versality obstruction retained in UAVERSLIMIT1 is unchanged.

Remaining: actual higher-degree canonical affine-space maps, effective fixed-locus gluing, simultaneous compatible symmetry, generic monodromy for boundary-fibre candidates, and affine-form/source-target trivializations. The new reductions specify where those requirements live; they do not construct them.
