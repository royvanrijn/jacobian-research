[De Bondt, Quasi-translations and singular Hessians, Proposition1.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1501.05168v1) states the classical quasi-translation equivalence using the actual displacement H and its self-invariance. Its invariant-derivation criterion is useful guidance, but does not apply to an arbitrary noninvertible Keller base with a coefficient-dependent response field. Here an explicit polynomial first integral R_j of the unperturbed response gives a nonzero QUADRATIC determinant defect after self-substitution. The correct response includes B*g.

[Lu, Meromorphic solutions of generalized inviscid Burgers equations and related PDEs, 2020](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.5802/crmath.136/) studies analytic entire and meromorphic solutions in complex affine space. We consulted the primary article page and abstract to compare scope. Its full proof is not imported, and its analytic classifications are not used over arbitrary characteristic-zero fields. The present Laurent degree and reciprocal-Riccati proof is self-contained.

The exact rational family g_lambda=lambda*R_j/(1+c_j*lambda*D_j) gives a concrete next gate. Multiple affine coefficient directions, curved coefficient families and genuine cross-block interaction are not ruled out. Clearing a denominator by localization does not meet the entire affine-source requirement; clearing it in a larger polynomial construction still needs determinant, complete fibre, gluing and full-map atomicity proofs.

The rigidity also covers arbitrary rational controls whenever the resulting whole map extends polynomially. Rational controls depending only on sigma may cancel common direction factors, so the precise conclusion is polynomiality of the EFFECTIVE retained profiles, not unqualified polynomiality of every rational control.
