Meromorphic solutions of generalized inviscid Burgers’ equations and related PDES
Lü, Feng1
1College of Science, China University of Petroleum, Qingdao Shandong, 266580, P.R. China
Comptes Rendus. Mathématique, Tome 358 (2020) no. 11-12, pp. 1169-1178
  • Résumé

The purposes of this paper are twofold. The first one is to describe entire solutions of certain type of PDEs in ℂ n with the modified KdV-Burgers equation and modified Zakharov-Kuznetsov equation as the prototypes. The second one is to characterize entire and meromorphic solutions of generalized inviscid Burgers’ equations in ℂ 2 .
