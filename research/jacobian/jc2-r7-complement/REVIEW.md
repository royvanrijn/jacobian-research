# Author-side audit of the proper comparison and quintic gate

This is not independent external review. Algebraic checks and written
topology have different assurance boundaries.

| Obligation | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|
| The marked infinity type is uniform | The residue coefficient formula uses an arbitrary even series and its first odd relative term21. The order31 coefficient is a unit on both domains; all earlier odd coefficients vanish. The infinity-line contact10 is retained. |
| Infinity does not hide escaping double points | Fourth-root conjugates separate at orders10 or31 with uniform nonzero coefficients. Proper normalization puts every preimage near source infinity. |
| The family used for comparison is actually proper | Work in the full P2 product, with its compact fibre, rather than the affine complement. The homogeneous normalization has no base point and the marked line is a stratum union. |
| Whitney conditions are established, not inferred from equal names of singularities | Nodes continue analytically by transverse collision equations and have local crossing-product coordinates. At infinity the tangent and secant estimates give the Whitney condition directly; the line and ambient-open strata are handled separately. |
| The published theorem applies | All strata project submersively, the stratification is locally finite and Whitney, and the ambient projection is proper. Mather Proposition11.1 is an explicit import; the checker does not prove it. |
| The comparison has the right scope | It identifies marked individual-C complements along each nodal open. It does not fix a chosen projection's braid words, identify the two generic types with each other or transport an unknown joint configuration. |
| The92 bound is an upper bound | There are12 polynomial exceptional cube-root orbits and79 Laurent orbits, with one common orbit; plus at most two nodal types. Additional coincidences may lower the bound. |
| The quintic normalization hypothesis is explicit | An actual extra image is not proved to have degrees(3,5). Under that hypothesis, implicit degree5 and V-degree3 force the thirteen-monomial Newton space. |
| Contact identities are exact throughout both families | The cancellation divides only monic leading coefficients. The residual degree9 coefficient is a unit. The13 restriction-basis pole degrees are distinct, giving the optimal bound and complete closest pencil. |
| The pencil is not silently a Keller construction | A member still needs a polynomial normalization, the required delta4, global image matching and an actual finite cover/source A2. Nine is a sum of intersection multiplicities, not a count of distinct values. |
| Bounded probes supply no theorem | Both nodal probes were terminal at their60-second caps; the logs contain only implicit-equation readiness. They are excluded from the topology and quintic arguments. |
