# Logarithmic volume and the geometric Picard obstruction

15 November 2026. Three written general claims with selected exact controls. No independent whole-proof replay, formal verification, external review or novelty certificate. OP-KDESC remains open. This extends [unit rigidity](../universal-keller-unit-rigidity/PROOF.md): a proper native intermediate must have NEGATIVE logarithmic Kodaira dimension and a nonzero geometric Picard group with NO invariant class over the retained function field.

Work in characteristic zero. Retain identity variables u and the fixed source signal

    t=1+xy, Q=t^2*z+y^2*(1+3t), sigma=t*Q.
    F=(u,sigma,B,C), det DF=delta in k*.

B,C may be arbitrary whole polynomial outputs unless a native witness is explicitly required. In ANY whole polynomial factorization F=E composed G, let

    p=(u,sigma), q=(E_u,E_sigma), K0=k(u,a),
    U=Spec R, R=K0[S,Y,r^(-1)], r=1-SY=1/t,
    U --g--> V --e--> A2_K0                             (0.1)

be the actual generic retained-fibre diagram. UAINTERSURF1 and UAUNITFACT1 give smoothness, geometric integrality, cofinite etale arrows, both polynomial saturation identities, arithmetic factoriality of A=O(V), and constant GEOMETRIC units whenever G is not an automorphism. No nonnegative-r assumption is made. For native F, UARELPLANE1 also says e has degree one exactly when V is geometrically A2.

## 1. UALOGVOL1: a noninvertible source stage has no logarithmic pluriforms

There is an exact dichotomy for EVERY whole F above:

    G is an automorphism iff geometric log kappa(V)=0;
    G is not an automorphism iff geometric log kappa(V)=-infinity. (1.1)

Thus dimensions1 and2 do not occur, and all positive logarithmic plurigenera vanish in the noninvertible branch. The proof uses the distinguished whole Keller volume, not a classification of all affine surfaces.

### The source is a specific log Calabi--Yau pair

Compactify U as P2 with homogeneous coordinates [X:Y:Z], affine S=X/Z, Yaff=Y/Z. Its boundary is

    D=C+L, C:Z^2-XY=0, L:Z=0.

C is a smooth conic meeting L transversely at [1:0:0] and [0:1:0]. Thus D has simple normal crossings and

    K_(P2)+D=-3H+2H+H=0.

The log volume

    eta=dS wedge dY / r                                (1.2)

has divisor -C-L. Consequently every positive plurilogarithmic section space of U is one-dimensional, generated by eta^m.

There is a precise difference from the whole Keller volume. The source chart has

    det D_(x,y,z)(sigma,S,Y)=t=1/r,
    F_eta^*(db wedge dc)=delta*r*dS wedge dY
                        =delta*r^2*eta.                (1.3)

On X=1, with v=Y/X,w=Z/X, the two coefficients are

    eta=dv wedge dw/[w*(w^2-v)],
    r*dS wedge dY=(w^2-v)*dv wedge dw/w^5.              (1.4)

The latter has pole order5 on L, whereas a log form is allowed order1. Abstract triviality of the ordinary canonical bundle does not erase this difference.

### Pullback of logarithmic sections is valid for the actual nonfinite maps

Let theta be a nonzero section of m(K_Vbar+D_V) on a smooth projective SNC completion of geometric V, m>=1. Resolve the graph of g by point blowups over the source boundary. Pullbacks of logarithmic forms stay logarithmic: locally the pullback of a target boundary parameter is a unit times a product of source boundary parameters, so dlog of it has only logarithmic poles.

The section space on the resolved source is still one-dimensional. For a blowup at a boundary point through j=1 or2 boundary branches,

    K_new+D_new=pi^*(K_old+D_old)+(2-j)*Exc.

For j=2 this is equality. For j=1 a putative extra section can have a pole only over the blown-up point; normal Hartogs extension descends it. Iteration therefore leaves the section space generated by eta^m. This proves, without a finiteness assumption on g, that

    g^*theta=c*eta^m, c!=0.                            (1.5)

On the OPEN V the etale map e supplies the nowhere-zero volume omega=e^*(db wedge dc). Hence theta=f*omega^m there, for a regular f on V. Equations (1.3)-(1.5) force

    g^*f=c*delta^(-m)*r^(-2m).                          (1.6)

This is a nonconstant unit on U. The geometric saturation A=R intersection Frac(A) makes f^(-1) regular on V as well; alternatively the cofinite image cannot miss a zero divisor of f. Thus V has a nonconstant geometric unit. UAUNITFACT1 makes the WHOLE G an automorphism.

Therefore a noninvertible G admits no such theta at any positive order, giving log kappa=-infinity. Conversely an invertible G identifies V with U, whose log Kodaira dimension is0. This proves the dichotomy after field extensions and arbitrary identity stabilization/mixing. A finite list of plurigenera checks is not used.

The source endpoint U has a log form because the required multiplier r^(-2m) is a genuine source unit. The target endpoint A2 has negative logarithmic Kodaira dimension. These are consistent endpoints, not proper factors.

## 2. UAGEOPIC1: a proper native factor requires nontrivial geometric divisor classes

We explicitly import Miyanishi's affine-plane characterization in characteristic zero:

    smooth affine + geometric units constant
    + geometric factoriality + log kappa=-infinity
       implies A2.                                    (2.1)

It is the negative-dimension statement recalled in the introductions of [Freudenburg--Kojima--Nagamine (2019)](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.03494v1) and [Pelka--Razny, version2 (2021)](https://arxiv.org/html/2002.10995v2), which cites Miyanishi, Open Algebraic Surfaces, III.2.2.1. This is an imported classification theorem, not independently proved by our controls. The erroneous old two-surface classification in logarithmic dimension0 is NOT used.

For a NATIVE F (or another F with the UARELPLANE1 embedded-curve witness), an actual whole factorization satisfies

    at least one of G,E is an automorphism
       iff Pic(V_geometric)=0.                        (2.2)

For a smooth affine surface, Pic equals the divisor class group, so vanishing is equivalent to geometric factoriality. If G is invertible, V is U, whose localized polynomial ring is geometrically factorial. If E is invertible, V is A2. Conversely suppose geometric Pic vanishes. If G is not invertible, UAUNITFACT1 gives constant geometric units and UALOGVOL1 gives negative log Kodaira dimension. The imported criterion (2.1) gives geometric A2, and UARELPLANE1 makes E invertible.

Thus geometric factoriality is now EXCLUDED for a proper native intermediate. The arithmetic factoriality A over K0 remains true. The remaining proper factor must realize an actual jump of divisor classes after passing to the geometric retained fibre.

This non-plane/Picard conclusion still needs the native curve witness. For arbitrary B,C without that witness, the broader conclusions remain (1.1), constant geometric units and the arithmetic statements; a geometric plane target pair need not be inverted by an unproved JC(2) assertion.

### Every invariant geometric class vanishes

Let Gamma=Gal(K0_bar/K0). For EVERY actual retained-signal intermediate, even in the broader F class,

    Pic(V_geometric)^Gamma=0.                          (2.3)

If G is invertible, geometric Pic is already zero. Otherwise geometric units are constants. There is a specified K0-point: the source section (u,0,0,a) maps under G to a point P in V. In fact it gives a polynomial section of q on the whole retained base.

We give the line-bundle descent argument rather than assuming that all geometric classes descend. A Gamma-invariant geometric line-bundle class has isomorphisms between its conjugates. Choose a nonzero trivialization of its fibre at P and normalize each conjugate isomorphism to preserve that fibre. Two such isomorphisms differ by a global unit; since all geometric units are scalars, equality on that fibre makes them equal. The normalized isomorphisms therefore satisfy the cocycle identity. Continuity is automatic here: the line bundle and the chosen fibre vector are defined over a finite extension, and uniqueness makes the normalized maps equal to its given descent maps on the corresponding open Galois subgroup. The finite-presentation Galois/fpqc descent theorem for line bundles descends the class to V over K0; see [Stacks tag023R](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/023R). But A is a UFD, hence Pic(V)=0. This proves (2.3).

For a PROPER native intermediate we consequently have

    Pic(V_geometric)!=0,
    Pic(V_geometric)^Gamma=0,
    Pic(V)=0.                                         (2.4)

The Galois group here is that of the RETAINED FUNCTION FIELD, not just of the original constants. No torsion-freeness, rank bound or permutation-lattice classification of this group is asserted. Each line-bundle class is defined over a finite extension; a nonzero one cannot be fixed by the full group. In particular a proposed intermediate with all geometric divisor classes already defined over K0 is ruled out.

The standard surfaces of log Kodaira dimension0 classified in 2021 cannot be intermediates in the noninvertible branch, regardless of their factoriality: (1.6) already excludes that whole logarithmic range.

## 3. UADANVOL1: exact volume excludes all ordinary Danielewski surfaces, with an all-degree higher-power boundary

An additional condition comes from e itself. Its volume is ALWAYS exact:

    omega=e^*(db wedge dc)=d(e_b*d e_c).                (3.1)

When V has only constant geometric units, every nowhere-zero volume is a constant multiple of omega. Thus a candidate whose canonical volume has a nonzero de Rham period cannot be such an intermediate, even if its tangent and Picard data otherwise look plausible.

### The entire exponent-one family is excluded

Let P(z) be square-free of degree d>=2 over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, and consider the smooth affine surface

    W_1: u*v=P(z).

Its canonical nowhere-zero form is

    Omega=du wedge dz/u
         =-dv wedge dz/v
         =du wedge dv/P'(z).                           (3.2)

The patches cover W_1 because P is square-free. Its only units are constants: after localizing u the ring is k[u,u^(-1),z], whose units are c*u^ell; valuation on the components u=0,z=alpha_i forces ell=0.

Over C choose two distinct roots alpha,beta and a path z(t) joining them, avoiding other roots. Along its interior take

    u=sqrt(abs(P(z(t))))*exp(i*theta), v=P(z(t))/u.

The circle collapses at each endpoint, giving a compact vanishing2-cycle. On its cylinder, Omega pulls back as i*dtheta wedge dz, so

    integral Omega=2*pi*i*(beta-alpha)!=0.              (3.3)

The endpoint disks have the usual smooth local form because P' is nonzero there; they introduce no boundary. Thus Omega is not exact. A hypothetical algebraic primitive would be analytically exact and have zero integral by Stokes, contradiction. The statement over any characteristic-zero field follows by descent to finitely many coefficients and an embedding into C.

If e:W_1->A2 were etale, its exact nowhere-zero form in (3.1) would be a nonzero constant times Omega, impossible. Therefore NO such etale map exists. This excludes every ordinary smooth Danielewski surface u*v=P(z), in EVERY polynomial degree d>=2, as a generic intermediate. The d=2 affine quadric is included.

This is a volume obstruction, not an application of an unproved affine-plane classification.

### Higher exponents pass this test by an explicit regular primitive

For EVERY integer m>=2 and EVERY square-free P, set

    W_m: u^m*v=P(z),
    Omega_m=du wedge dz/u^m
            =du wedge dv/P'(z).

This is again smooth with constant units and a nowhere-zero canonical volume. Now choose C(z) inverse to P'(z) modulo P(z), and define

    D(z)=(1-C(z)*P'(z))/P(z),
    A'(z)=(D(z)-C'(z))/(m-1), B(z)=A(z)-C(z).

Take ANY polynomial antiderivative A; characteristic zero allows it. Then the GLOBAL REGULAR1-form

    theta=A(z)*u*dv+B(z)*v*du

satisfies

    d theta=[(A-B)*P'+(m*A'-B')*P]*Omega_m
           =Omega_m.                                  (3.4)

Thus all m>=2 pass the exact-volume test, without fitting coefficients or increasing a search cutoff. Passing it does NOT supply an etale map to A2: a regular primitive is not automatically of the special form e_b*d e_c.

Even the obvious target coordinate u cannot be used in a Keller pair on these surfaces when d>=2. If dB wedge du were a nonzero constant multiple of Omega_m, localization at u would give B=-c*z/u^m+f(u). Regularity on u=0,z=alpha_i would require one Laurent coefficient of f to equal c*alpha_i for every root, impossible for distinct roots. No search for a mate of u in larger degrees is needed.

### A sharp arithmetic/geometric Picard model, not a whole factor

Over K=k(a), take P=z^d-a with d>=2, and m>=1. Its coordinate ring is a localization of k[u,v,z] at nonzero polynomials in a=z^d-u^m*v, hence an arithmetic UFD. It has the K-point (1,-a,0).

After algebraic closure, the d components D_i=(u=0,z=alpha_i) generate the divisor class group. Localizing u gives a UFD, and units there are only c*u^ell; the only relation among these components is

    D_1+...+D_d=div(u).
    Pic(W_m_geometric)=Z^d/Z*(1,...,1).                 (3.5)

The source units are constant by the same valuation argument. When k is algebraically closed, the Galois action on the roots of z^d-a is transitive, and the invariant subgroup of (3.5) is zero: over Q a transitive permutation module has only its constant fixed line, removed by the diagonal quotient; the integral quotient is torsion-free because the diagonal relation is primitive. This explicitly realizes the arithmetic/geometric class jump required in (2.4). The generic u-fibre is A1. On a resolved P1-fibred completion the generic fibre has one boundary point, so the log canonical divisor restricts with degree -1. Every positive log pluriform therefore vanishes on the generic fibre, giving log Kodaira dimension -infinity.

For m=1 the nonexact volume excludes the model outright. For m>=2 the regular primitive in (3.4) removes that obstruction. No compatible cofinite etale map U->W_m, etale target pair W_m->A2, whole polynomial factorization, free tangent-frame certificate or canonical marked gluing has been constructed here. These higher-power surfaces are a concrete remaining geometric test family, not proved intermediates or a new canonical receiver.

### The displayed polynomial spread cannot itself be the whole intermediate

There is a separate global obstruction even for the higher-power test models. Their displayed total space is A3 with retained polynomial

    q(u,v,z)=z^d-u^m*v,
    dq=-m*u^(m-1)*v*du-u^m*dv+d*z^(d-1)*dz.           (3.6)

For m>=2,d>=2 its entire line u=z=0 is critical, over a=0. For m=1,d>=2 it still has the critical point u=v=z=0. In contrast, the ACTUAL q=(E_u,E_sigma) is smooth everywhere: it is a coordinate subtuple of the whole Keller map E, whose differential is invertible. Adjoining identities preserves this rank obstruction, as do polynomial coordinate isomorphisms of source and retained base.

Thus the displayed spread is NOT a whole retained-base intermediate, for any exponent. This does not exclude a GENERIC surface isomorphic to W_m over K0: another global model could change the special fibre and would need its own whole smoothness and polynomial realization proof. The exact primitive and geometric Picard computations are necessary-condition models only; they do not certify such a different spread.

## 4. Literature and the remaining construction

The logarithmic viewpoint is informed by recent surface work, including [Hajra, Smooth affine surfaces properly dominated by C* times C*, version1 (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04214v1). Its main classification assumes FINITE surjective domination by the torus. Our source is the hyperbola complement and the relevant maps can be nonfinite, so that theorem is context only. The pullback calculation (1.6) is proved directly in our actual category.

The 2019 factorial-surface paper corrected an older logarithmic-dimension-zero classification; the 2021 Pelka--Razny paper provides the corrected classification. We use only the separately stated negative-dimension affine-plane criterion, not the obsolete finite list. Log dimension zero is excluded here by volume before any such list is used.

The remaining proper native intermediate is geometrically unit-free, has negative logarithmic Kodaira dimension and is geometrically NONFACTORIAL, with arithmetic UFD ring, nonzero geometric Picard group and no invariant class over the retained function field. Its actual embedded field, both saturation identities, whole carried A1 component, cofinite arrows, exact volume and global polynomial realization must still be proved.

This does not construct finite nonlinear native integration, the atomic canonical receiver or improved costs. Complete canonical Spec(A), whole-source landing, effective gluing and absolute stable atomicity remain open. Multi-block settings still require their own two-dimensional generic fibre and curve witness. Imported surface characterization and descent facts, and the new general arguments, require independent proof review; exact controls and literature pins do not establish novelty or formal verification.
