# HC4 trace quotient and uniform cone nilpotence

PF2RULED153. 17 November 2026.

There is a positive, scheme-theoretic step in the proposed Rodrigues transfer.
For the degree-at-most-five graph problem, the actual first-jet trace quotient
has a natural 30-generator cone-deviation ideal J with

    J^4096=0.

This is a uniform ideal identity in the actual quotient, proved below without
passing to its radical or replacing multiplication by an initial algebra.
It survives every further quotient of the degree-five candidate locus.
It is not a termination theorem: no increasing-power containment for the
normal tail, or polynomial reduced body, is supplied. In particular J is
not yet proved to be a tail obstruction ideal.

## 1. The precise coefficient algebra

Use the scaled Meng--Yang family and normal recurrence of
[HC4MYGJ2](../../foundations/HC4_MENG_YANG_GRAPH_OBSTRUCTIONS.md).
Write the source variables as (x,y,p,q), the graph as

    R=T+x S+x^2 a_2+...,

and its four-variable Hessian determinant as D_R. The parent parameters L,N
are units. The desired constant determinant c is also a unit. Let

    K=Q[L,L^(-1),N,N^(-1),M,c,c^(-1)],
    P=K[t_alpha : alpha in N^3, |alpha|<=5],
    T=sum t_alpha y^alpha_1 p^alpha_2 q^alpha_3.

There are 56 trace parameters. The exact plane identity is

    D_R|_(x=0)=F(T)-8 L N^3 S.                    (1)

For a graph of total degree <=5, S has plane degree <=4. Therefore set

    I_plane=( [y^a p^b q^d] F(T) : a+b+d>4 ),
    A_plane=P/I_plane.                           (2)

F(T) has degree at most 10. Equation (2) is a finite, explicit coefficient
ideal (251 possible monomial slots in degrees 5 through 10), not a radical
ideal or a finite prefix of the normal recurrence. The forced first jet
S=(F(T)-c)/(8LN^3) is polynomial of degree <=4 in this quotient. Conversely,
these equations are exactly the condition on T for this first-jet degree
bound. They do not impose the subsequent jet degree bounds.

Every actual degree-five polynomial graph with constant nonzero determinant
factors through (2), as does any more restrictive remaining coefficient
quotient obtained by adding candidate equations. This statement concerns
this specified graph ansatz; it supplies no global normal-form coverage
for hypothetical minimal plane counterexamples.

Write T=sum_(i=0)^5 T_i, with T_i homogeneous in (y,p,q). Define

    J_1=coeff(T_(5,q)),
    E=T_(5,p)-8y T_(4,q),
    J_2=J_1+coeff(E),
    J=J_2+coeff(T_(4,q)).                         (3)

Here coeff means the ideal generated by all polynomial coefficients in the
source variables, in A_plane. Equivalently J is generated by the 20 quintic
trace parameters other than [y^5]T_5 and the 10 quartic trace parameters
containing q. Differentiation multiplies their coefficients by nonzero
integers, which are units. This is the promised natural cone-deviation
ideal, not an ideal declared equal to the Image/Rodrigues algebra.

## 2. Exact plane determinant and its three leading faces

The following formula makes (2) fully reproducible:

    K_y=3(2Lp+M)p+N(21y^2 p+24yq+2T_y),
    K_p=12Lyp+6Lq+3My+7Ny^3+2NT_p,
    K_q=6Lp+3M+12Ny^2+2NT_q,

    H=30Ly^2 p^2+60Lypq+18Lq^2
         +6My^2p+12Myq+6Ny^4p+18Ny^3q-6NyT,

    F(T)=-2LN^2 H+4LN K_y K_q+N^2 K_p^2
          -16N^2 y K_p K_q
          -(16LNp-64N^2 y^2)K_q^2.               (4)

Indeed, the lower 3 by 3 plane Hessian and its determinant are

    G=[8Np 8Ny N; 8Ny 2L 0; N 0 0],
    det(G)=-2LN^2.

The upper-left Hessian entry is H+4NS; the mixed entries are K_y,K_p,K_q.
The block identity det([[h,K^t],[K,G]])=h det(G)-K^t adj(G)K proves
(1) and (4). The checker derives these entries from the actual parent
potential, with a symbolic trace and its derivatives.

Taking total homogeneous faces in the source variables gives

    [degree 10] F =256N^4 y^2 (T_(5,q))^2,       (5)
    [degree  8] F =4N^4 E^2 modulo J_1,          (6)
    [degree  7] F =16LN^3 Q(T_(5,y)-4pQ)
                    modulo J_2, Q=T_(4,q).     (7)

All these identities are valid over every commutative Q-algebra with L,N
units. In particular (5)--(7) do not use that the coefficient ring is a
domain. Cancelling the monomial y^2 in a polynomial identity in (5) is
valid over any coefficient ring, by shifting coefficients.

The old field-valued leading-cone argument set T_(5,q), E and then Q equal
to zero. That would discard nilpotent structure here. Instead we retain
their actual multiplication relations.

## 3. A finite rational evaluation certificate

For a homogeneous quartic P(y,p,q), use the 15 values

    P(1,i,j), i,j>=0, i+j<=4.

The matrix from its 15 coefficients to these values is

    V_((i,j),(a,b))=i^a j^b, a,b>=0, a+b<=4.

In lexicographic ordering of these exponent pairs its determinant is
47,775,744, so V is invertible over Q. Thus the coefficient ideal of P
is exactly the ideal generated by these 15 evaluations, over every
commutative Q-algebra.

If P^2=0, each evaluation has square zero. Every product of 16 of the
15 generators repeats a generator; consequently

    coeff(P)^16=0.                               (8)

This is an identity about actual products, without a reducedness or
geometric-point assumption. Apply it first to P=T_(5,q), using (5),
and then to P=E in A_plane/J_1, using (6). We obtain

    J_1^16=0,   J_2^16 subset J_1.               (9)

## 4. The cubic triangular certificate

Work now in A_plane/J_2. Differentiating E=0 in q, and using T_(5,q)=0,
gives y Q_q=0. Coefficient shifting makes Q independent of q. Thus

    Q=q_0 y^3+q_1 y^2p+q_2 yp^2+q_3 p^3,

    T_5=kappa y^5
        +sum_(i=0)^3 8/(i+1) q_i y^(4-i)p^(i+1).

Put B=T_(5,y)-4pQ. Explicitly,

    B=5kappa y^4+28q_0 y^3p+8q_1 y^2p^2
          +(4/3)q_2 yp^3-2q_3 p^4.              (10)

By (7), the coefficients f_j of Q*B all vanish, where

    f_j=[y^(7-j)p^j](Q*B), 0<=j<=7.

Their actual multiplication gives the following triangular square identities:

    q_3^2 = -f_7/2,
    q_2^2 = (3/4)f_5-(9/2)q_1 q_3,
    q_1^2 = f_3/8-(11/3)q_0 q_2-(5/8)kappa q_3,
    q_0^2 = f_1/28-(5/28)kappa q_1.              (11)

Successively adjoining q_3,q_2,q_1,q_0 doubles the nilpotence exponent
at most: (q_3)^2=0; (q_2,q_3)^2 subset (q_3);
(q_1,q_2,q_3)^2 subset (q_2,q_3); and
(q_0,q_1,q_2,q_3)^2 subset (q_1,q_2,q_3).
Hence (q_0,q_1,q_2,q_3)^16=0 in A_plane/J_2. This also covers kappa=0.
Lifting back and using (9) proves

    J^16 subset J_2,
    J^256 subset J_1,
    J^4096=0.                                    (12)

The exponent is a sufficient uniform bound; minimality is unclaimed.

There are 26 complementary trace parameters. Since every monomial in the
30 generators of J of total degree >=4096 vanishes, A_plane is finite as
a module over the polynomial algebra in the complementary parameters
and L,L^(-1),N,N^(-1),M,c,c^(-1), acting through its natural map. No
injectivity, freeness, flatness or finite total dimension over Q is asserted.
At any fixed rational choice of the complementary parameters and units,
a nonzero fiber is a finite local Artin Q-algebra supported at J=0.
This constructs a genuine finite-algebra setting fiber by fiber; it does
not compute the multiplication table of every such fiber.

## 5. Nonvacuous fiber and a retained transverse control

The retained exact first-jet point at L=1,M=13,N=2 is

    T_star=(51/100)y^5-(47/10)y^3p-(123/20)y^2q,
    c_star=17165601/25.

It has J=0 and degree F(T_star)=4, so the first-jet quotient is nonempty.
Fix its 26 complementary parameters. The coefficient Jacobian of the
>4 plane equations with respect to the 30 bad trace parameters has rank
30 at this point. The checker retains an explicit nonzero 30 by 30 minor.
The equations have no constant term in these bad parameters; their linear
parts therefore imply J=J^2 in this fiber. Together with (12) this gives
J=0. Since evaluation at the point is nonzero, this whole fixed-parameter
fiber is exactly Q, of length one. This is an actual quotient statement,
not just a tangent-space or radical calculation.

This point is NOT a surviving degree-five graph. With
S_star=(F(T_star)-c_star)/64, the already retained transverse obstruction is

    [xy^7]D_(T_star+x S_star)=22032/125.

The universal k=2 normal multiplier is -192. Thus the full formal solution
has [y^7]a_2=459/500, incompatible with the degree-five bound deg(a_2)<=3.
The checker reproduces this control from the actual potential. It is a
known second-jet exclusion used to validate (2), not a newly excluded
architecture, a proof of infinite nontermination, or an HC4 example.

## 6. The exact missing transfer step

Equation (12) supplies a uniform nilpotence certificate for a specified
ideal on the actual first-jet quotient, and therefore on every further
remaining degree-five quotient. It does NOT establish that J is the ideal
of a normal tail. Before a Rodrigues termination argument can use it, one
must still prove, on a precisely specified remaining quotient:

1. the graph modulo J is polynomial with a uniform normal degree D;
2. after that body and shift, every coefficient a_(D+k) belongs to
   J^ceil(k/c_0) for a fixed positive integer c_0.

The first-jet equations alone supply neither statement. If both hold,
(12) gives termination for k>4095c_0. A valid proof may use a finite
module closed under the ENTIRE recurrence, as in
the separate Rodrigues certificate, or a direct
weight argument. Checking a longer coefficient prefix cannot replace it.
