## 2. A terminal congruence independent of characteristic depth

Suppose the D branch has at least two nodes, with last residual gcd
a>=2, and last free-tail order kappa=nu*a. Before its FINAL gcd drop,

The last drop is at

The full contact formula retained in PF50/78 is

    n_D=S_D+2p-sigma+1,
    S_D=sum_(nontrivial sigma conjugates)(2p-j_detected). (5)

All nonfinal groups of contacts contribute multiples of a^2: both
their gcd-drop multiplicities and detected contact orders are
multiples of a. The final group has a-1 conjugates and contact
2p-j_*. Hence, for an integer C,

    S_D=a^2*C+(a-1)(2p-sigma+1-kappa),
    n_D= a^2*Z+(nu+1)*a                                (6)

for an integer Z. This is exact for the congruence even if lower
noncharacteristic terms occur: they do not change the final gcd.

For ANY full complementary source matrix with n_R,q as in PF77,

    delta=n_R*n_D-q^2*p^2
          = n_R*(nu+1)*a mod a^2.                     (7)

If a matching target has delta=c*a, then

    a divides n_R*(nu+1)-c.                            (8)

There is no bound on source support, degree, a or characteristic
depth in this lemma. The divisibility of sigma and preceding indices
by a is required; a single-node source does not automatically have it.
