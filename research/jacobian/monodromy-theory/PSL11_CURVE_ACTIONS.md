# Every PSL2(11) action in the independently stabilized curve class

The surviving degree-eleven curve route is exhaustive over **all**
faithful transitive actions of PSL2(11), rather than just its natural
and exceptional actions. This is a classification of possible inputs
to one field-construction class. It does not decide the action spectrum
of arbitrary polynomial Keller maps.

Throughout, k is algebraically closed of characteristic zero. The
field class is exactly

\[
 k(t,u_1,\ldots,u_j)\subset k(r,u_1,\ldots,u_j),\qquad t=f(r),
 \tag{1}
\]

with the same independent variables on both sides. Arbitrary birational
identifications with prospective affine source and target coordinates
are allowed. More generally, a positive-genus curve source cannot
become rational by adding independent variables, as recalled in
[the curve-descent proof](CURVE_DESCENT_LCM.md). The necessary condition
from KMFI9 is

\[
 \operatorname{lcm}_b c(\sigma_b)=n,\qquad
 c(g)=\gcd\{\text{all cycle lengths of }g\},\quad n=\deg f.
 \tag{2}
\]

In particular, KMFI10 requires \(C(G,G/H)=\operatorname{lcm}_{g\in G}c(g)=n\).
Unramified sheets must be included in every gcd.

## A prime-subgroup test for the stabilizer

**KMFI85.** Let G act transitively on G/H and let p divide |G|.
Write \(p^b\) for the largest p-part of any element order in G.
Unconditionally,

\[
 v_p(C(G,G/H))\le b.
 \tag{3}
\]

If H contains a conjugate of every subgroup of G of order p, then

\[
 v_p(C(G,G/H))\le b-1.
 \tag{4}
\]

Consequently, suppose a Sylow p-subgroup has order \(p^a\), exponent p,
with \(a\ge2\), and all order-p subgroups of G are conjugate. Any action
of index divisible by p fails the necessary curve test \(C=n\).

To prove (3), every cycle length divides the element order. For (4),
take g of order \(p^s m\), with \(p\nmid m\). If s=0 its cycles have
p-prime lengths. Otherwise, its unique order-p subgroup is generated by
\(g^{p^{s-1}m}\). By hypothesis it fixes some coset. The g-cycle
through that coset therefore has length dividing \(p^{s-1}m\).
The minimum p-valuation of a cycle length, which is the p-valuation of
c(g), is at most \(s-1\le b-1\). Take the maximum over g to obtain (4).

For the last assertion b=1. If p does not divide |H|, the index has
valuation a>=2 and contradicts (3). If p divides |H|, Cauchy's theorem
and the single conjugacy class of order-p subgroups give the hypothesis
of (4). Then p does not divide C, whereas it divides the index.

This is a curve-field obstruction. No assertion that \(C=n\) holds
for arbitrary higher-dimensional Keller field pairs is made:
the retained actual natural A8 Keller map already disproves that
assertion (KMFI16). The hypothesis a>=2 also matters: a regular
C_p action has C=p and passes the necessary test.

## Exhausting the stabilizers that survive this first test

Set \(G=\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbf F_{11})/\{I,-I\}\), of order660.
Its element orders and counts are

| Order | 1 | 2 | 3 | 5 | 6 | 11 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Number | 1 | 55 | 110 | 264 | 110 | 120 |

All55 involutions are conjugate. A Sylow2 subgroup S is V4, so the
p=2 corollary of KMFI85 excludes **every even-degree action** from (1).
Every remaining stabilizer H contains a Sylow2 subgroup. Conjugate H
so that S is contained in H.

There are exactly eight overgroups of this fixed S:

| Overgroup | Order | Number containing fixed S | Coset degree |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| V4 | 4 | 1 | 165 |
| A4 | 12 | 1 | 55 |
| D12 (order12) | 12 | 3 | 55 |
| A5, first conjugacy class | 60 | 1 | 11 |
| A5, second conjugacy class | 60 | 1 | 11 |
| G | 660 | 1 | 1 |

The last row gives the trivial, nonfaithful action. The three D12
overgroups are conjugate in G. The two A5 subgroups lie in the distinct
degree-eleven conjugacy classes retained in KMFI82.

This finite list is obtained without an external subgroup-classification
database. Sort all canonical determinant-one matrices modulo sign,
build their660-by660 multiplication table, and start from S with two
involution generators. For every discovered proper overgroup D and
every g outside D, add the subgroup generated by D and g. Repeat until
no new subgroup appears. There are4448 tested extensions and precisely
the eight groups displayed above. The model retains generating sets,
all subgroup elements, and every tested extension as integer matrix
labels.

Completeness is elementary: for any overgroup J of S, if a discovered
D contained in J is smaller than J, choosing g in J-D gives a strictly
larger discovered subgroup still inside J. Iteration reaches J.
Thus the loop exhausts every possible H containing S, not a bounded
family selected by degree or a list of familiar maximal subgroups.

## Genus-zero budgets eliminate the other odd-degree actions

For a branch permutation sigma, write
\(\operatorname{ind}(\sigma)=n-\#\{\text{cycles of }\sigma\}\).
[Riemann-Hurwitz in characteristic zero](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0C1B)
gives, for a rational degree-n curve cover,

\[
 \sum_b\operatorname{ind}(\sigma_b)=2n-2.
 \tag{5}
\]

The exhaustive coset actions give the following indices. They depend
only on the element order in these actions.

| Stabilizer | n | Order2 | Order3 | Order5 | Order6 | Order11 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| V4 | 165 | 78 | 110 | 132 | 136 | 150 |
| A4 | 55 | 26 | 34 | 44 | 44 | 50 |
| D12 | 55 | 24 | 36 | 44 | 44 | 50 |
| Either A5 | 11 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 8 | 10 |

For V4 there is no sum328 from this multiset of allowed positive
indices. Two terms are at most300; four terms are at least312 and
replacing any78 by a larger index exceeds328; five terms are too large.
With three terms, if all are at least110 the sum is at least330;
one78 would require two remaining indices to sum250, which none do;
two78 would require172, and three78 give234.

For A4 there is no sum108. Two terms are at most100. Four terms have
minimum104 and the next replacement adds at least8; five terms are
too large. For three terms, use the sorted choices26,34,44,50:
with26 the other two would sum82, with34 and no26 they would sum74,
and three terms at least44 are too large. No such pairs occur.
These actions admit no rational-source curve covers at all.

For D12, the only necessary order multisets for sum108 are

\[
 (3,3,3),\qquad (2,2,2,3).
 \tag{6}
\]

This is only an index-budget list: neither tuple generation nor cover
existence is asserted from (5). That distinction is immaterial for the
obstruction. Each involution has seven fixed sheets, and each order3
element has one fixed sheet. All branch cycle-gcds in (6) are therefore
one, contradicting (2), which would require55.

All four action types have C=n. Thus the action-level lcm alone
does not eliminate the odd-degree55 and165 candidates. The actual
genus-zero branch budget, followed by the branch-level condition (2),
is essential.

## The exhaustive surviving curve route

**KMFI86.** Any polynomial Keller map whose field pair has form (1)
and whose geometric monodromy group is abstractly PSL2(11) must have
degree11, in one of the two exceptional actions. After independent
Mobius changes on the source and target curves, its curve input is
polynomial and has passport

\[
 \boxed{2^4 1^3\mid3^3 1^2\mid11.}
 \tag{7}
\]

Indeed, every other stabilizer was eliminated above. In either A5
action only elements of order11 have cycle-gcd different from one.
Condition (2) requires an actual order11 branch, contributing10 to
the genus-zero budget20. The remaining index sum10 permits only
another11 branch or one branch each of orders2 and3. A connected
two-branch cover has cyclic monodromy: its branch generators are
mutual inverses. Full PSL2(11) therefore requires (2,3,11).
The eleven-cycle fiber consists of one totally ramified point;
moving that value and point to infinity makes f polynomial.

The conclusion quantifies over every faithful transitive action,
not just the two classical degrees11 and12. It is still a **necessary
reduction**, not a realization theorem or an absolute degree-eleven
obstruction. In particular:

- Passing C=n or (7) is insufficient for an affine completion.
- No uniqueness of Nielsen classes is asserted.
- Arbitrary nonlinear parameter base changes need not preserve (1).
- No statement here reduces every higher-dimensional Keller extension
  to a curve input.
- The absolute natural degree12 exclusion is already supplied separately
  by KMFI67. The exceptional degree11 action and full action spectrum
  remain open.

## Reproduction and assurance

Run

    python3 research/jacobian/monodromy-theory/verify_psl11_curve_spectrum.py

The standard-library checker exhausts all660 matrices, all overgroups
of one Sylow V4, every coset permutation in each surviving subgroup
action, all genus-zero index multisets, and the branch gcd gate.
It checks both exceptional A5 classes and an abstract generating
(2,3,11) tuple, without calling it an affine model. The general
prime-subgroup theorem has the written proof above; its controls do
not replace that proof. The sole new attributed input is the
Riemann-Hurwitz formula, pinned in the source manifest. This is a
hybrid written/exact-computation proof, without formal verification,
independent whole-proof replay or external review.
