# Plane-chart literature and the exact remaining gap

Reviewed15 November2026. [Proof](PROOF.md), [source pins](sources.json). This package does not claim a new Danielewski atlas or a solution of JC(2).

[Dubouloz, Danielewski-Fieseler surfaces, arXiv:math/0401225v3](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0401225v3), introduction and Section2, explicitly gives the multiple-origin torsor interpretation and affine-plane charts with inverse-power translations. Our formulas specialize this classical construction to u^m*v=P(z), proving chart inverses and volume compatibility directly.

[Dubouloz's Basel2009 talk abstract](https://dubouloz.perso.math.cnrs.fr/GdTs/GdTAutos/Prog-Basel-09-11.pdf), page2, is particularly relevant: it presents non-plane affine surfaces containing an open plane as a route to a plane Jacobian counterexample, and explains the insufficiency of naive differential/cohomological obstructions for x^2*z=y^2-1. The exact-volume success in UADANVOL1 does not close this longstanding target-map difficulty. The chart-to-JC(2) reduction is established precedent, not a new discovery here.

[Gwozdziewicz, Injectivity on one line (1993)](https://arxiv.org/html/alg-geom/9305008v1), Theorem1.1, together with its invoked affine-line rectification, supplies the UARELPLANE1 embedded-curve cancellation lemma. [Stacks tag024T](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/024T) supplies the open-and-closed section argument that promotes an invertible chart restriction to an isomorphism of the entire connected surface. We spell out their combination: the actual whole carried native curve cannot lie inside ANY plane chart of a proper intermediate.

[Borisov--Gabber--Vasiu2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05746v1), Section2.2, discusses generalized Danielewski/sphere-like models, affine-plane open embeddings and other morphisms. The reviewed primary HTML was already retained by the HC4 work and is hash-pinned here instead of reconstructed. Positive-characteristic examples and the paper's separate weak-type hypotheses do not yield a characteristic-zero whole factor or solve our simultaneous atlas identities.

The coefficient reduction along the A1 ruling is an elementary all-degree argument. It forces a hypothetical compatible target pair to survive at nondividing ruling degrees at least2 after a terminating sequence of GLOBAL target shears. It does not classify that residual sector.

The carried-curve contact bound is likewise uniform in exponent and all polynomial degrees. The explicit beta-integral family attains the minimal contact but fails geometric injectivity in EVERY exponent. This is a concrete reason not to replace the entire embedded curve by local root contacts or a polynomial parametrization.

Still open: compatible nonautomorphic Keller pairs on the atlas; a carried closed embedded line crossing multiple root components; the actual source arrow; exact saturated fields; another smooth whole retained-base model; canonical complete marks/gluing and stable atomicity. No finite coefficient or formal-order search is proposed.

[Stacks tag025H](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/025H) gives unique section lifting over nilpotent thickenings. Applied along the whole carried line, all infinitesimal and formal plane neighborhoods are standard, yet no global plane chart can contain that line in a proper native factor. This is a classical input and an application of the existing UAETALEGAUGE1 boundary, not a new formal-etale theorem.
