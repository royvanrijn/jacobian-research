# Literature, transfers and exact boundaries

Reviewed15 November2026. Primary HTML versions are frozen in [sources.json](sources.json). The [proof](PROOF.md) distinguishes published results from the repository applications. No source is labelled AI-assisted without evidence from its authors, and no publication-priority certificate is claimed.

- Duncan--Reichstein, [Versality of algebraic group actions and rational points on twisted varieties](https://arxiv.org/html/1109.6093v4), Theorem1.1. Point existence, dense points, unirationality and stable linearizability are different conditions. The free torus and isogeny arguments use the point criterion, proved over finite fields as well.
- First--Florence--Rosengarten, [Algebraic Groups with Torsors That Are Versal for All Affine Varieties](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04458v2), v2 dated15 December2025, Theorem1.1 and Theorems6.3-6.4. Only unipotent groups have one torsor over a quasi-compact scheme specializing to all regular affine test schemes. For perfect fields smooth tests suffice. The quantitative representation/dimension bound transfers directly to rank-N frame torsors. This is a published obstruction, not a new general theorem here. Identity stabilization and canonical-line lifting preserve the exact image on torsors for every test scheme, so they do not repair it.
- Federico Scavia, [A counterexample to a conjecture of Duncan on versal actions](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.25118v1),27 July2026, Theorem1.2. Its degree-two Del Pezzo action has very versal Sylow restrictions while the full action is not versal. It disproves the general dense-open-versality Sylow converse. We do not claim it proves failure of weak point-existence versality. Negative Sylow norm obstructions and the retained fixed-fibre degree budget remain valid.
- Kresch--Tschinkel, [Linearizability notions in equivariant birational geometry](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10965v1),9 June2026, Theorem2.5. In its algebraically closed characteristic-zero setting one generic twist from a genuinely versal action tests the listed properties of all twists. This can replace samples by a legitimate generic gate; it does not turn arbitrary finite-field samples or Sylow successes into a proof.

The induced-character free base, exact isogeny H1 criterion and general Sylow norm obstruction are written out directly, using classical Hilbert90, Kummer exactness, restriction/corestriction and torus-lattice duality. They generalize the [S3 family](../universal-keller-holonomy-families/PROOF.md), whose smaller free base and sharper dimensions remain useful.

The cyclotomic calculation is an exact integer identity: the seventh-root Gaussian period has norm8, quotient F8 and an elementary kernel. No cyclotomic class-number table is assumed. For other fields the principal-prime-ideal requirement is a real additional gate; an existing residue field does not prove a self-isogeny. An irreducible but nonprime algebraic integer distinguishes same-variety stable atomicity from strong atomicity. Nonprincipal ideal quotients cannot be hidden by affine identity stabilization on that fixed cyclotomic architecture.

Positive families include every odd prime-power degree and every even binary exponent, with specified affine group torsors, plus the degree-eight cyclotomic example. Other odd binary exponents require new integral isogenies or different geometry. Constant-group statements retain their root-of-unity hypotheses; genuine group scheme formulations preserve cyclotomic action.

The original whole-affine canonical receiver, effective simultaneous landing/gluing, smaller marked-rank ambient atomicity, modular cases and the A1-rich pseudo-plane mixing gate remain unresolved. The published all-affine theorem excludes a fixed quasi-compact scheme parameter receiver, not a stack receiver retaining inertia or an unbounded/dimension-dependent construction.
