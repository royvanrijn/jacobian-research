# Higher-rank return and recent time-block literature

Reviewed 15 November 2026. [Proof](PROOF.md), [source pins](sources.json). No publication priority is asserted.

The precise common-kernel input remains [Stacks Lemma37.32.1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0G4C), used for active inverse pencils with two independent target parameters. Its field statement applies in relative dimension greater than one. A separating transcendence basis bounds the dimension of derivations, replacing the single-curve constant argument. [Relative algebraic closedness](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0G33) and [flat polynomial saturation](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01V4) give the rational and polynomial common kernels.

[Jedrzejewicz and Zielinski, Section1](https://arxiv.org/html/1601.01508v1) records the proved one-generator closedness theorem. It makes the full auxiliary common kernel polynomial automatically when the return quotient has one coordinate. This is not an assumption of a multigenerator generalized Jacobian conjecture.

[Wang and Yan, Nilpotent Jacobian maps in dimension three and stable tameness in block extensions](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.16580v1), September 2026, presents an explicit nonlinear family in(1.1) with recovery identity(1.2), and claims broader classifications and stable-tameness results. We directly verify and use that displayed time family; its full classification is not an imported theorem here. The common invariant w=y+x^2 supplies its inverse. Inserting this family with invariant parameters is a descended time automorphism and keeps the compiler/auxiliary factors. A nilpotent abstract Schur matrix on an algebraic source leaf is not automatically an ordinary polynomial Jacobian in affine time coordinates.

[Wang and Yan, Quartic Keller Maps and Tameness in Three Variables](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.36495v1), October 2026, concerns TOTAL coordinate degree at most four. The native constant-profile example with generic covering degree four has total degree26. Our marked-preserving degree-four profile has still larger coordinate degree. The paper's degree bound therefore does not exclude these examples. Its full proof is not needed for the present deductions.

The descended time-map class includes any r-dimensional Keller map with invariant polynomial parameters. A theorem asserting every such time map invertible would already contain JC(r). Our factorization does not need that assertion: a noninvertible time map would add a polynomial factor. This is why inserting a hypothetical JC(2) counterexample into this class would not atomicify the full receiver.

The constructive departure is explicit: native profile changes are rank-two returns that need not descend to old outputs. A vanishing polynomial in the RETAINED first output preserves the entire inverse image over a chosen target divisor, hence exact marked fibres with no hidden points. Arbitrary perturbations vanishing only at listed source points do not have this conclusion. All-degree atomic marked auxiliaries follow from the retained profile-monodromy theorem, not a new generic-monodromy claim.

For rank two fixing the cubic's first output, the source leaf is the affine plane minus a hyperbola and has a nonconstant unit. It is not an affine plane, even after identity stabilization. The new relative maps and the constant-Jacobian condition on their source volume form therefore do not settle ordinary JC(2). Canonical deformation families preserve Spec(A) but retain another explicit factorization. A non-descended interaction which proves full marked fibre and absolute atomicity remains unconstructed.

The same retained-signal identity with I(sigma)^ell proves equality over the entire target thickening V(I(a)^ell), for any specified finite ell. Thus arbitrarily high finite-order marked restrictions still leave the generic degree and stable class undetermined. This is a direct corollary of the local identity; no published novelty claim or equality of full formal completions is asserted.
