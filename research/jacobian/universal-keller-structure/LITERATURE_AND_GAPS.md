# Literature transfer and the next structural gate

14 November 2026. The decisive external input this round is older published mathematics.

- [S. D. Cohen, The Galois group of a polynomial with two indeterminate coefficients, Pacific Journal of Mathematics90(1),1980, Theorem1 and Lemma4](https://msp.org/pjm/1980/90-1/pjm-v90-n1-p07-s.pdf). The h(S)+B*S^2-C family with h'(0)!=0 is an immediate case of full symmetric monodromy in every odd characteristic. The linear coefficient supplies the missing residue class modulo p. This is a classical theorem being applied to the Keller compiler, not a novel general monodromy theorem.
- [Cohen's 1981 correction, pages483--485](https://msp.org/pjm/1981/97-2/pjm-v97-n2-p22-s.pdf). Wild ramification invalidated an auxiliary argument used by other parts of the original paper. The correction explicitly preserves Theorem1. The proof here uses a tame double-root inertia element and double transitivity, without assuming that ramification at infinity is tame or that finite inertia generates every positive-characteristic cover.

The direct proof in UAQTILT1 makes the application transparent: the two-root incidence is primitive linear in B, and the critical envelope recovers r^2. This removes the previous characteristic-zero restriction in UAPROFILE1, while avoiding a false transfer of its Riemann--Hurwitz argument into characteristic p.

The exceptional boundary calculation is a separate algebraic consequence of the retained compiler. No result about general Artin--Schreier covers is needed beyond the elementary all-prime irreducibility argument written in the proof. Frobenius perturbations with zero derivative are standard; the claims here concern their complete compiler fibres and generic monodromy.

For canonical construction, UANORMAL1 adds a normal-form and architecture exclusion to UAEQUIV1. Its proof uses finite-group averaging and polynomial units, so it works whenever the group order is invertible, beyond characteristic zero. This does not identify a published general classification of nonlinear equivariant Keller maps, and it makes no claim that such a classification or a new canonical receiver has been obtained.

## Completed gates

1. Arbitrary-profile symmetric monodromy and absolute stable atomicity now hold over every field of characteristic other than two, including the polynomial negative-profile exceptions.
2. Every exceptional pi-zero fibre has an exact two-component description. Boundary rank can be unbounded, but a full-degree boundary fibre necessarily has a rank-two factor.
3. Marked representation-bundle constructions affine linear in moving variables are excluded, regardless of how many trivial coordinates are supplied or how nonlinear the invariant output is.

## Remaining gates

- A receiver forgetting the generator line still needs an effective polynomial realization. A viable linear-action architecture must satisfy all subgroup-fixed restrictions and introduce nonlinear moving terms after the first-jet normalization.
- None of these results lowers a characteristic-zero universal target dimension or proves stable equivalence of different compiler slices.
- UABLEVEL1 now classifies B=constant for the independent promoted atlas; [the later extension](../universal-keller-levels/PROOF.md) also supplies a modular affine-bundle construction. Other affine completions, coefficient relations, negative binary cases and global degree minima remain open.
- Mathematical review is still required. Bounded exact replay is not formalization or independent review of the universal proofs.
