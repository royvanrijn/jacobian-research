- UAQTILT1: classical quadratic-tilt symmetric monodromy in every odd characteristic, with a direct proof.
- UAALLATOM1: every polynomial coefficient-profile section with nonzero top coefficient is absolutely stably atomic; identity parameters are allowed.
- UAMODBOUND1: all exceptional pi-zero fibres, their exact ranks and the impossibility of a full-degree field fibre on that boundary.
- UANORMAL1: a global equivariant normal form; canonical marked lifts must be nonlinear in their moving variables, even after arbitrarily many trivial stabilizations.

These extend the [unified theory](../universal-keller-receiver/THEORY_MAP.md). They do not construct a presentation-free receiver or improve the characteristic-zero dimension bounds. Authority: [MATH_STATUS.json](../../MATH_STATUS.json).

The [all-degree coupling extension](../universal-keller-feedback-rigidity/PROOF.md) allows all retained first outputs in every block profile and constructs every Boolean polynomial factor. It also determines arithmetic restriction-of-scalars factor lengths and classifies all scalar feedback in the trace-filter architecture.
