# Primary inputs and the exact receiver categories

14 November 2026. [Canonical proof](PROOF.md).

[Greither--Pareigis,1987](https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/7117/1/7117.pdf), Theorem3.1 on printed pages247--248, gives the regular-subgroup normalizer criterion for Hopf--Galois structures. The original pages were read and visually inspected. We use its abelian-type criterion as a connection and construct the equivariant torus isogeny explicitly; the source and target character lattices are retained as different unless an isomorphism is proved. Requiring the abelian regular group to lie inside the label group would be an incorrect strengthening.

[Milne, Algebraic Groups,2017](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/iAG2017.pdf), Theorem12.23, gives exact duality with Galois character modules; Lemma12.61 and Definition12.62 identify permutation lattices with induced tori. Theorem12.23 at printed page240 was visually inspected, and the full relevant pages were read. [Stacks Hilbert90,59.24.1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03P7) supplies the field multiplicative-torsor vanishing; Weil restriction and the norm sequence give the cohomological assertions used here. These are classical inputs, not new discoveries.

[Dubouloz--Palka,2018](https://arxiv.org/html/1701.01425), equation(5.12), gives the full automorphism form of S_k. Its torsion-free additive kernel immediately excludes noncyclic finite source groups. Our separate full-degree monodromy argument extends the finite-chart normalizer step of UACANATOM1 to other normal source geometries. Only that step is used; the affine-space Galois obstruction and automatic atomicity conclusions are not imported to tori or pseudo-planes.

The cubic norm-one torus, quartic V4 cover and the equivariant character-lattice construction are derived explicitly in the proof. Their canonical twisting, sharp receiver dimensions, fixed-versus-family obstruction and the unit-lattice stabilization rule are recorded without a publication-priority claim. Classical cubic discriminant and quartic pair-partition resolvent actions are calculated as finite permutation controls.

A torus map is not a polynomial self-map of affine space: its Laurent units, Euler characteristic and finite deck actions differ. In positive characteristic a torus isogeny must have degree prime to that characteristic, complementing rather than replacing the retained Frobenius affine constructions. The normal-affine intermediate category and the same-variety self-map category also differ: the degree-four restriction-of-scalars example is stably atomic only in the latter, while a nonnormal nodal intermediate destroys even prime-degree finite atomicity.

The unresolved original questions require whole affine-space boundary constructions, nonabelian-monodromy receivers in higher natural symmetric rank, or projection-mixing cubic stabilizations of the pseudo-plane. One fixed torus cover cannot replace the canonical cubic or quartic family because its discriminant or resolvent cannot vary. No class-group search or unproved torus self-identification is used.
