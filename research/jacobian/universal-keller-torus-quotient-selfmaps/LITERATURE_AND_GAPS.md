# Primary inputs and the self-map boundary

14 November 2026. [Canonical proof](PROOF.md).

[Yadav, Central quotient versus commutator subgroup of groups, v4](https://arxiv.org/html/1011.2083v4), abstract and introduction, records Schur's finite-centre-index theorem and its history. Our proof includes the elementary transfer argument. Its finite-commutator self-embedding consequence closes finite homogeneous self-covers, using UAHOMOCOST1. We do not extend it to all virtually abelian groups: the norm surface supplies a counterexample.

[Marques--Ward, Cubic Fields: A Primer,2017](https://arxiv.org/html/1703.06219v3), Theorem1.2, its proof and Corollary1.3, were read for characteristic different from3. They supply the classical field normal forms. The transformation r+r^{-1} and the exact critical surface fibre handle pure and disconnected algebras respectively, including finite-field cases.

[Stacks76.7.1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04CR), using its cited Lemma37.9.9, supplies etale differential pullback. The canonical-line lift and tautological exterior-derivative volume are proved intrinsically and locally in section6. This is standard canonical-bundle geometry used as a receiver functor.

The retained [torus inputs](../universal-keller-torus-receivers/LITERATURE_AND_GAPS.md) supply norm-torus cohomology and Hilbert90. Cubic and quartic torsor classes cancel by the coprime3/2 and2/3 exponents, as in the old canonical torus families. A new FREE S3 base packages the varying forms into a fixed quartic SELF-MAP. [Free-quotient and fundamental-group inputs](../universal-keller-homogeneous-receivers/sources.json) remain pinned.

The torus-quotient monodromy proof identifies the intrinsic translation lattice and proves its maximal normal abelian property. It does not assume an arbitrary self-map is affine. This is the usual crystallographic-group mechanism applied to algebraic torus quotients.

Generic dihedral polynomials and Chebyshev isogenies are classical. A polynomial specialization family alone does not supply a finite etale self-map of a fixed smooth affine space, and fieldwise torsor triviality does not choose canonical scheme-level sections. No search establishes unpublished status or priority for the exact constructions recorded here.

The next high-rank finite self-receiver must escape geometric torus quotients. Canonical volume preservation has a general one-line functor; whole affine source geometry and compatible marked descent remain essential. The fixed quartic self-map here is not asserted to have full canonical S4 marked symmetry.
