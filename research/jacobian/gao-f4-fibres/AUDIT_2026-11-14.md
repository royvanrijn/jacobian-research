The following possible gaps were checked before transferring the method:

1. **Missing boundary pieces:** C=x*gamma and gamma|x=0=1 prove that
   x=0 and gamma=0,x!=0 exhaust the source over c=0 and are disjoint.
   The latter gives d0*x^2=12 and a unique stage inverse, so it neither
   loses x-values nor introduces extraneous solutions.
2. **Pointwise versus algebraic inverse:** on the exact 221 stratum,
   gcd(P,P') is its monic quadratic. Its coefficients are regular on
   the fixed-gcd-degree locus by nonzero subresultants. This justifies
   the claimed variety isomorphism, beyond injective parameter counting.
3. **Elliptic completion:** the quartic chart omits sigma=-1/2. That
   exceptional affine point is separately checked smooth, as are all
   three projective points at infinity. Neither excluded line is a
   component. The proof consequently covers the whole smooth cubic.
4. **Finite normalization:** the selected-double-root parameter r is
   integral by monicity of P. Polynomial division recovers residual
   coefficients from r and the coefficient target. The stated A2
   incidence is therefore finite over its image, normal, and birational,
   proving normalization rather than merely a rational parameterization.
5. **Escape at every multiple-root point:** the root incidence of the
   F4 quintic is irreducible. Its equation is linear in Y, and the
   coefficients of Y1,Y2,Y3 are proportional to s(s^2+2), -(s^2+1), s,
   which have no common zero. Its simple-root open is nonempty and
   dense. Thus every multiple-root point is approached by simple roots;
   gamma=P'/9 tends to zero and x=c/gamma diverges when c!=0.
6. **All boundary targets nonproper:** the two quadratic roots selected
   in PROOF.md are simple and have nonzero escape numerators. This works
   for arbitrary fixed last-three target coordinates as c tends to zero,
   including boundary points with three finite preimages.
