# Scope correction for the degree six Stein reduction

14 November 2026. PF2D6E1 is **partial**. Its historical claim that every
normal even row is excluded uses a complete omitted-divisor assertion
that its ramification classification does not prove. This is a proof-gap
audit, not a counterexample to the claimed exclusion.

## The two missing implications

The [historical proof](../../foundations/plane-jc/F2_GEOMETRIC_DEGREE_SIX_STEIN_REDUCTION.md)
Section1 conserves all six local degrees in the terminal passport. Away
from the special value, the completed local factors have rank one. This
proves that the finite map is etale there, and hence locates its possible
ramification. It does not prove that an etale branch belongs to the
distinguished source open. Equation(1.5), stated for every nonproper
component, therefore needs a reconstruction or attachment argument for
the unramified omitted primes. The proved statement at that step concerns
ramified branch germs only.

Similarly Section3.12 states that the even normal row has exactly two
dicritical rows, both (e,f)=(2,1), and substitutes only those into the
complete Orevkov identity. The cubic normal form classifies ramification
rows. It does not exclude further e=1 omitted rows, even over the same
target images. Thus the asserted excess 5−2−2=1 is conditional on that
extra completeness assumption.

The complete form allows 5=2+2+t+excess. For the r=10 nodal pair, the
globally separated ramification has zero exceptional excess and the
[global Euler calculation](GLOBAL_LEDGER.md#5-why-the-old-even-row-excess-budget-needs-a-completeness-audit)
forces t=1. It supplies the missing unit through an unramified omitted
prime. No global realization follows from this numerical repair.

## What remains established

The terminal residue passport, cubic normal forms and discriminant
factorization are retained exact algebra. The conic, line and constant
implicit-difference classifications, saturated loci and nodal collision
certificates are retained. The completed braid computations still exclude
their explicitly checked cusp coefficient strata.

The inference that those strata exhaust every even r=6 or r=8 possibility
also used the excess-one budget. It must be re-established allowing e=1
omitted rows and possible extra ramified images whose terminal locations
were not proved by an omission-complete argument. Neither the restricted
braid witnesses nor their exact enumeration is erased or promoted to a
new whole-family statement. The r=9 polynomial gap exclusion and the r=7
coefficient classification have independent polynomial proofs and remain
valid in their stated scopes.

## The projective common point does not fill the gap automatically

[Borisov's source](../jc2-boundary-attachment/sources/manifest.json)
states its common-point conclusion on the Stein model finite over P2.
The terminal cubic in the historical calculation lies over a target that
has been blown up. Normalizing after that base change changes the Stein
surface. Branches through the old common point can separate. A claim that
all omitted images have one specified terminal residue must track their
strict transforms through those target blowups; the P2 theorem alone
does not identify the new points.

The corrected PF2D6E1 source is this audit. The original computations
remain retained at their original paths. A complete whole-even-row
exclusion requires reconstruction-pole primes, their transported images,
and actual exceptional lengths, or a replacement global argument that
covers all omitted rows.
