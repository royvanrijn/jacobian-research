# Primary inputs and the exact new conclusion

14 November2026. Canonical arguments: [PROOF.md](PROOF.md). No publication-priority certification is claimed.

[Haution, arXiv1507.04582v4](https://arxiv.org/html/1507.04582), Proposition3.2.1, supplies the whole tame prime-group fixed schemes, not just tangent spaces. Theorem3.3.1 and its Lefschetz proof supply nonempty tame cyclic fixed loci and Euler characteristic one, without asserting their connectedness for composite orders. These are different statements and support different parts of the theorem.

[Fein--Kantor--Schacher, Relative Brauer groups II,1981](https://pages.uoregon.edu/kantor/PAPERS/BrauerGroups.pdf), Theorem1 on printed page41, supplies a prime-power derangement in every nontrivial finite transitive action. The original scanned theorem page and preceding page were visually inspected with the PDF skill and bundled PDFium. The general finite-group application imports CFSG; the natural S_N argument instead uses explicit disjoint cycles and has no CFSG dependency.

[Stacks Kummer theory](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03PK) and [Cheng--Ji--Larson--Olander, Theorem of the Base](https://mattlarson2399.github.io/Papers/theorem-of-the-base.pdf), Theorem7.3, support the supplementary characteristic-zero unit and Picard constraints. The main birational rigidity proof is shorter: compact-support localization, Poincare duality and the nonzero top compact-support class of a nonempty variety. [Deligne, Arcata lectures, SectionVI Theorem3.1](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Documents/DeligneArcata.pdf) gives the primary duality input; the official translation is pinned. Rational ell-adic acyclicity suffices for this rigidity lemma. It applies in positive characteristic too and does not import characteristic-zero finite-cover Euler multiplicativity there.

[Dubouloz--Palka,2018](https://arxiv.org/html/1701.01425), TheoremsB/C, Corollary5.2 and Example5.3, provide published nonproper etale maps of Q-homology planes. Our complete image/fibre calculation shows the displayed family is surjective in every degree. It therefore does not provide the missing target required by the canonical fixed-surface gate. Nor may a theorem about self-maps be applied to different fixed surfaces without proving they are isomorphic.

The new degree budget goes beyond the [earlier dimension gate](../universal-keller-smith/PROOF.md): the full generic-degree-N category is impossible in characteristic zero in every dimension. The old [normal-monodromy/automatic-atomicity implication](../universal-keller-affine-lang/PROOF.md) is still correct, but vacuous in that characteristic-zero full-degree category and inapplicable to the remaining M>=N+2 candidates. Identity stabilization preserves generic degree and can never repair this failure. Higher-degree boundary models remain a new geometric requirement rather than a continuation of the same stabilized map.

[Borisov--Gabber--Vasiu, September2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05746), abstract and Sections2/11 inspected for comparison, studies positive-characteristic affine endomorphisms and sphere-like finite completions. Its positive-characteristic omission and degree phenomena are not a characteristic-zero acyclic-surface theorem and are not proof dependencies here. They remain relevant to the separate modular completion questions.

The wider published shear family, Proposition5.5 and Example5.7, supplies actual missing fibres; see the [complete deformation image calculation](../universal-keller-acyclic-boundary/PROOF.md). The surjectivity assertion above applies to the undeformed family only.
