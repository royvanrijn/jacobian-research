# Descent literature and the remaining complete-type gap

Reviewed 15 November 2026. [Proof](PROOF.md), [pinned sources](sources.json).

[Daigle2007, Theorem3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/0705.0334v1) applies to ALL smooth affine characteristic-zero surfaces with trivial ML. Trivial canonical bundle forces u*v=P(z). Combining this published theorem with the actual etale target and the retained UADANVOL1 volume-period proof excludes the nonplane case. The result is a new application to the actual Keller intermediate, not a new surface classification.

[Dubouloz--Kishimoto2014, Section1.3.1 and Proposition4](https://arxiv.org/html/1408.1328v1) proves descent for Galois-invariant rulings. Here invariance of any affine-type ruling is PROVED from uniqueness: two different such rulings would make ML trivial, already excluded. The rational point centers its coordinate, and a weighted Galois trace supplies an explicit invariant coordinate. The actual canonical volume then constructs the descended locally nilpotent Hamiltonian derivation. Ordinary averaging without this uniqueness is disproved by the explicit plane control.

[Dubouloz2015, introduction and Section1](https://arxiv.org/html/1507.05802v1) explicitly recalls the standard equivalence between nontrivial additive actions on normal affine surfaces and affine-type A1-fibrations. Its rigid cubic surfaces carry complete-type rulings. Being smooth affine hypersurfaces, they also have trivial canonical bundle. They show why trivial canonical bundle and geometric ruledness alone do not justify affine-type existence. They are not claimed to satisfy the actual source arrow, exact volume or whole-factor constraints.

[Dubouloz2013, Section2.1 and Remark5](https://arxiv.org/html/1304.4189v1) gives rigid affine surfaces with two inequivalent complete-type rulings. This prevents extending the two-affine-ruling argument to arbitrary rulings. It is a countercontrol for an invalid inference, not a proposed whole intermediate.

The solved subproblem is descent of EVERY geometric AFFINE-type ruling of an actual proper native intermediate. The unresolved problem is descent or exclusion of COMPLETE-type Galois-permuted rulings. The conclusion that all proper-native rulings are complete type is conditional on JC(2). Non-invariance of a complete-type ruling itself is unconditional in the actual arithmetic-UFD branch, since a descended complete base would inject its Picard group into Pic(V)=0.

No general complete-type descent theorem, surviving factor or canonical atomic receiver is asserted. No further orbifold pattern, coefficient cutoff or necessary-condition list is introduced in this extension.
