## Exact extensions

UA1CANCLASS1 replaces a construction for H_q by a classification of every one-dimensional canonical marked transitive realization. The finite affine-group facts and subspace polynomials are classical. The constant-derivative and whole-fibre constraints rule out the larger scalar orbits. The only natural symmetric exceptions are the quadratic case in characteristic two and cubic case in characteristic three.

UAAFFSUB1 treats every scalar complement m dividing q-1 with the same Keller formula. The new conclusion is the fixed framed Keller minimum and an exact atomicity dichotomy: the multiplier must generate all of F_q over F_p, or a written polynomial Keller factorization applies. This strengthens the previous full-affine example without treating every imprimitive map as polynomially decomposable.

UAFIXGEOM1 extends the fixed-space argument to any nonlinear actions whose entire fixed schemes are affine spaces. Published linearization then transfers the ambient bounds, and the actual fixed scheme excludes a genuine nonlinearizable example. Finding a nonlinear action is only one of the requirements for a new canonical receiver.

The retained [KMFI30 signed-alternating proof](../monodromy-theory/SIGNED_ALTERNATING.md) provides actual imprimitive, absolutely stably atomic characteristic-zero maps. Our nonprimitive affine-complement family instead has explicit polynomial factors. Whether a different fixed framed universal map for the nonprimitive groups can remain absolutely atomic is open; no converse from imprimitive monodromy is asserted.

UAFINCOUNT1 uses the classical finite-etale/Galois-set dictionary ([Stacks, Lemma58.2.2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03QR)). Its new application counts source points across all required splitting types, giving B_N=sum_(j<N)p(j), and computes both translation minima over every finite base as ceil(e/f). Over infinite characteristic-p bases both translation minima are one, using a subspace polynomial with constants in the actual base. This supplies numerical compiler-independent bounds in small finite fields, without changing any characteristic-zero claim.

## Boundaries that remain

- Classification is geometric for constant finite groups and affine-line-bundle constructions. It does not classify arbitrary singular curves, nonconstant group schemes or all stack receivers.
- The fixed universal-map theorem includes an actual G-torsor frame on an open finite-etale whole-cover chart. A claim about arbitrary unstructured subgroup fibres would need a separate argument.
- The exact decomposition criterion concerns K_(q,m). It does not turn imprimitive monodromy into an affine-space factorization theorem in general.
- The essential-dimension comparison uses the usual infinite-field versality argument. The Keller construction itself works over all extensions, including finite fields.
- The nonlinear fixed-locus argument needs the whole fixed schemes to be smooth affine spaces. It does not identify a disconnected fixed locus with its tangent representation.
- Tame linearization in three variables is characteristic zero. No higher-dimensional linearization theorem is assumed; nonlinearizable finite actions are published.
- No result changes the remaining unmarked characteristic-zero minima or proves JC(2). All new written proofs await independent mathematical review.
