# Dixmier and Weyl algebras

<!-- Generated from research/MATH_STATUS.json; run make render-navigation. -->

Formal Weyl lifts, scalar mate obstructions, finite centralizers and exact quantum division spectra.

**Authored assessment:** All-order formal lifting, low-order DC1 automorphisms and scoped obstructions remain internally established. Canonical graded scalar Long lifts require spatial degree at least 28 with arbitrary parity; degree 27 is the sharp minimum through h5. The admitted XQ Airy source datum gives a matrix jet through h4 at degree 25, but its entire prescribed-first-coefficient family fails at h5 through degree 26. Three crossing coordinates give a one-order covariant correction test without a uniform bound. Actual centered polynomial Weyl pairs have zero mixed symmetric Weyl coefficient, and their ordinary rational differential intertwiners are scalar. Differential orders ten and eleven force generation; proper pairs have composite orders at least twelve. Global polynomial admission, higher critical faces and arbitrary Weyl-division transport remain open. No bounded full Long lift or DC1/DC2 resolution is obtained.
