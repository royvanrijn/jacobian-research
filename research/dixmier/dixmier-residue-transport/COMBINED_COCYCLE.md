# A strong cocycle for combined native and transported corrections

14 November 2026. **QRT2.** The enlarged correction category has an explicit
16-term strong order-five cocycle with period one. It annihilates both
native and transported corrections, including all compatible lower lifts
and arbitrary finite Laurent poles in X. The same category is stable under
every positional Weyl shear generated by a polynomial H(X,Q) of total
degree at most three. This repairs the precise native-support failure in
[QRT1](PROOF.md), within this enlarged category.

The result concerns the retained degree-five seed and its cubic positional
shear orbit. It does not assert an obstruction for arbitrary classical
symbols, higher-degree Hamiltonians, odd-hbar lifts or unrestricted DC2.
No novelty priority, formal verification or external review is claimed.

## The enlarged category

Use the same S,T, delta and fiber Weyl product as QRT1, at (kappa,tau)=(0,1):

\[
\delta=3X^2\partial_X+(2-6XQ)\partial_Q,\qquad
\Pi=\partial_Z\otimes\delta-\delta\otimes\partial_Z,
\qquad\{S,T\}=1.
\]

For f in Q[X,X^-1,Q,Z], let deg4(f) be the maximum of i+j+4k over its
nonzero monomials X^i Q^j Z^k. Negative i are allowed, but every polynomial
has finite support. Define the four spaces

| Correction | deg4 bound | Z-order bound |
|---|---:|---:|
| s at hbar^2 | 28 | 3 |
| t at hbar^2 | 23 | 2 |
| u at hbar^4 | 22 | 1 |
| v at hbar^4 | 17 | 0 |

Call them E2S,E2T,E4S,E4T. They contain the old native Bernstein boxes:
changing the Z weight from three to four raises a monomial degree by k,
so the old lower bounds 25,21 become at most 28,23 and the old current
bounds 21,17 become at most 22,17. The new spaces are deliberately larger
than the sum of the two polynomial spaces in QRT1; the proof covers the
whole enlarged category rather than a selected branch of that sum.

The exact seed has (deg4,Z-order)=(34,5) for S and (29,4) for T.
The lower-lift equation and current differential are

\[
\{s,T\}+\{S,t\}=-\Pi^3(S,T)/24,\qquad
d_1(u,v)=\{u,T\}+\{S,v\}.
\tag{1}
\]

The order-five defect O5(s,t) is the expression in QRT1. Its Z order is
at most four. The result below covers every pair (s,t) in E2S+E2T satisfying
(1), and every current correction in E4S+E4T.

## Why a finite calculation covers all Laurent poles

The support of Lambda_plus has X exponents between 14 and 20. The seed
has maximum X exponents 11 for S and 10 for T. A current correction's
Poisson image raises its X exponent by at most 12, so an input with negative
X exponent cannot meet the functional's support. It suffices to check
every nonnegative-X current monomial in the table: 466 for u and 171 for v.
All 637 pairings are zero by exact rational arithmetic.

For lower lifts, the quadratic term is Lambda_plus({s,t}). Its monomials
have X exponents i_s+i_t or i_s+i_t+1. The degree bounds give i_s<=28 and
i_t<=23. Therefore this quadratic term sees only i_s>=-10 and i_t>=-15.
The terms linear in the lower lift have the fixed seed as the other factor;
they cannot see lower inputs below -15 either.

Retain all lower monomials with X exponent at least -15, and project the
order-three equation to output X exponent at least -3. Any omitted input
has exponent at most -16, so its image has exponent at most -4; it cannot
alter a retained equation. Every genuine finite-Laurent lower lift thus
maps to this finite projected affine space, and all its period-relevant
coefficients are retained. No assertion that every projected solution
extends to a genuine Laurent lift is needed.

The exact calculation has the following sizes:

| Object | Exact size |
|---|---:|
| Retained s variables | 3004 |
| Retained t variables | 1906 |
| Rank of projected lower equation | 3719 |
| Dimension of projected affine solution space | 1191 |
| Nonzero entries in the ambient bilinear period matrix | 4222 |

Write the projected family as b+sum(z_i k_i). The period is a scalar
quadratic polynomial in the 1191 free coordinates. The constant is exactly
one; all 1191 linear coefficients vanish. If B_ij is the bilinear matrix
on the kernel, exact computation gives B_ij+B_ji=0, including its diagonal.
Only 24 bilinear entries are nonzero before symmetric cancellation. This
certifies all 709836 possible quadratic coefficients, not a sample of
lower-lift points. Equation (2) follows on this larger affine space and
hence on every genuine finite-Laurent lower lift.

## Closure under cubic positional quantum shears

Let H(X,Q) be any polynomial of ordinary total degree at most three, with
arbitrary characteristic-zero coefficients. From QRT1, put

\[
g=\delta H,\qquad A2=\delta^3H/24,\qquad A4=\delta^5H/1920,
\qquad\tau_H:Z\mapsto Z+g.
\]

Their ordinary degrees satisfy deg(g)<=4, deg(A2)<=6, deg(A4)<=8.
Substitution by tau_H and its inverse preserve all four deg4 and Z-order
bounds. The order-two seed shifts A2*S_ZZZ and A2*T_ZZZ have degrees at
most 34-12+6=28 and 29-12+6=23, respectively, and the required Z orders.
The order-four shifts A2*s_ZZZ,A2*t_ZZZ have degrees at most 22,17;
the seed terms A4*S_Z5,A4*T_Z5 also satisfy these bounds. The seed sixth
Z derivatives vanish, so the A2^2 terms vanish here. All these assertions
also hold for the inverse shear.

Consequently the exact quantum shear, including its affine order-two and
order-four shifts, bijects the enlarged lift category with itself over
the changed classical seed. This includes both the transported old category
and the plain native category, as well as their sums and compatible lower
lifts. Finite sums or compositions of these positional cubic potentials
stay within the same class.

Define Lambda_(+,H)=Lambda_plus composed with tau_H inverse. The QRT1
commutator identity gives

\[
O'_5(s',t')+d'_1(q_S,q_T)=\tau_H O_5(s,t).
\]

The affine q terms are now inside the enlarged current spaces. They are
annihilated by the new dual. Thus Lambda_(+,H) is a strong cocycle for
the combined native and transported current spaces and has period one,
independent of all compatible enlarged lower lifts. This is a written
degree and automorphism argument for the whole cubic class; the ten
monomial potential controls in the replay are checks of its ingredients.

For the original nonlinear example H=lambda X^3/3, the native correction
X^12 Q^6 Z pulls back to X^12 Q^6 Z-3 lambda X^16 Q^6. Both terms are
in E4S, so their new period is zero. The exact -1296 lambda failure of
the old transported functional is repaired.
