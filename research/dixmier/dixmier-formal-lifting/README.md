# Formal quantum lifting and remaining Dixmier gaps

A full formal lift of a noninvertible polynomial symplectic map would give
a Weyl counterexample if some representative had one common finite bound
on spatial degree. Formal existence is proved; that bound remains unknown
for Long's map.

QGC1 closes the natural larger-closure carrier with actual admission.
Its quadratic field ramifies inside Long affine source. At ordinary
parameter, the identified classical graph supplies a finite full
valuation-level lattice. With equivariance and the correct torsion-free
degree-two special module, rank specialization and finite character
shifts recover a saturated carrier at every parameter order. Both routes
contradict the etale multiplier criterion. Saturation itself is false
even for genuine constant-cover Weyl carriers; their finite repair is
checked. Nonequivariant ramified data and changed matrix actions need
separate admission. The original rank-one Long graph remains open.

QGD1 excludes direct finite symmetry quotients of the full Long quantum
division image. Branch selectors account for parameter-pole groups moving
the h-valuation; their residue covers are still Galois. Long has an S3
cubic closure and trivial deck group, so its completed automorphism group
is trivial. Its entire generic automorphism group has finite order g,
with g dividing both whole indices and N_l,N_r>=3g. The exclusion
transports under rational source/target division changes and identity
padding. A larger Galois closure with an intermediate subgroup-fixed
source is not excluded and still needs a finite compatible carrier.

QCI1 isolates a residual division extension. For an actual full Long
tuple, adjoining every rational position commutant still leaves
completed degree3. Generic indices factor N=d*M with residual M>=3;
a nontrivial centralizer field has d>=3 and forces whole indices>=9.
Thus ordinary coefficient-field models cannot realize the lift, and
no rational target division automorphism can make all fixed positions
positional, even with parameter poles or identity stabilization.
Mixed source polarizations remain separate. Genuine rational residual
extensions now exist in every degree: F_m=x^(1-m)*p/m,T_m=x^m.
Their bounded powers nevertheless exclude finite diagonal carriers
in every positive generic rank and rational matrix frame, including
identity padding. An algebraic nilpotent matrix with a finite lattice
preserves the higher-rank exception. Finite admission for the correct
Long graph remains open; the delta1 cotangent controls are preserved.

QPD1 extends spectral denominator clearing to rank-one finite projective
Weyl orders. Rational individual mates are exactly torsion of the global
translation classes; torsion is weaker than actual vanishing. Rational
commutants automatically lie in Frac B. Scalar idealizer units and the
field hypotheses make QTB1 tests available in nonfree carriers without
a scalar frame or centralizer finite generation. The charge-one control
has a cusp centralizer, a rational mate and nonzero torsion. The curve
case closes QST1's unused converse; arbitrary moving-coefficient module
localization remains distinct. No actual new Long carrier is supplied.

QTB1 removes finite generation from the birational and Galois-factor
tests. Taylor flatness retains an unramified valuation over every base
divisor in each finite intermediate centralizer field. No nontrivial
finite Galois factor survives; birationality forces polynomiality and
finite completion. An exact new-radical identity from two actual
commutants is already an all-partner obstruction certificate. Point
stabilizers must normally generate monodromy, excluding D4 quartics
but allowing S3 cubics. The scalar unit and actual-field hypotheses
remain essential. No new Long field or radical certificate is supplied.

QET1 links finite generation to boundary geometry. With actual individual
mates, the finitely generated scalar centralizer is etale over the
commuting images, and Weyl units retain every generic base divisor.
A birational field forces polynomiality and finite full completion;
a nontrivial Galois field instead forbids those mates. Nonpolynomial
survivors need non-Galois degree at least three and an unramified sheet
over every generic divisor. The rank-three cubic control makes the
bound sharp there. Its finite-type etale description uses finite generation; QTB1 removes
that requirement for the birational/Galois tests. Actual fields remain
distinct from leading/completed fields and full division indices.
Actual parameter poles of individual mates can also be removed by
finite polynomials, without any finite-generation hypothesis. This
preserves integral curvature exactly, so an actual generic flat tuple
with regular Long positions already descends to a finite integral
tuple with the prescribed classical frame. Rational spatial/operator
denominators do not qualify. No new Long tuple is supplied.

QFC1 gives a positive finite-completion criterion. Actual integral
individual conjugates and module finiteness of the joint centralizer
force that centralizer to be polynomial, even if finiteness is known
only generically. A finite polynomial primitive then kills curvature
in every rank and preserves a flat classical tuple. Independent leading
symbols with a full module-finite Poisson centralizer give a scalar
filtered certificate. Finite algebra generation and completed
polynomiality are insufficient. Universal finiteness in the planar
rescaled cotangent subclass would imply JC2; no Long input is supplied.

QST1 promotes a nonzero Adler period to a rational division-ring
obstruction and computes a nontorsion global spectral class for
G(a)+mu*v in every degree. Rational conjugacy, finite coefficient
covers and fractional projective frames do not repair that pair orbit.
Constant-coefficient G(a) operators instead have critical-value torsion
and rational mates. A torus residue excludes harmonic/Euler functional
classes despite vanishing Adler periods. New rational pair orbits and
genuinely nonscalar matrix constructions remain separate targets.

QSC1 moves the completion test to the actual finite algebra or projective
carrier. Every commuting Long deformation, including a nonpositional first
image, has commutative joint centralizer. The two global spectral classes
test individual conjugates; their remaining relation is one canonical
algebraic curvature class. Neither fiberwise spectral vanishing nor a
completed curvature primitive suffices. Matrix completions with both
positions scalar already trace to scalar completions without increasing
degree. Genuinely matrix-valued positions and nonfree projective frames
remain separate. No actual finite Long carrier is supplied.

QAD1 gives a scalar gate for arbitrary mixed coefficients. If the operator
order and the leading coefficient zero/pole multiplicities are coprime,
the whole low-order commutator image is zero. Thus every nonconstant
squarefree polynomial leading coefficient is excluded, in all degrees
and on every finite coefficient cover. At prime order over K(v), a
survivor needs a scalar times an nth power leading coefficient.
Leading differential exactness and spectral Adler periods give further
tests. Vanishing periods construct only a PDO mate; finite admission is
the constant-linear span of the entire almost-commuting defect family.
The harmonic control passes all periods and has no finite mate. A
universal characteristic-zero period also strengthens QPC1. General
full carriers and arbitrary Fourier completion comparisons remain open.

QPC1 completes the pure polynomial classification: v*H(a)+G(a) has a
finite scalar mate exactly for constant H with affine G and nonconstant S,
or nonconstant linear H with constant G. Every exact-R Long model in this
entire form is excluded, on all finite coefficient covers and in every
partner order. The new constant-H proof isolates one universal noncentral
p-power coefficient. Arbitrary matrix fibers require only a zero derivative
of their characteristic polynomial; the genuine phase matrices control
that boundary. Genuine mixed coefficients, negative-v corrections, other
leading faces, nonpositional first images and general carriers remain open.

QZM1 transports a represented zero-mode cokernel through actual finite
Weyl changes when the module is transported too; new coefficient
localization can kill it. Its leading-linear exclusion supplies one
part of the complete pure polynomial classification in QPC1.

QVL1 closes the proper-power escape when lower coefficients are bounded:
every v-linear Laurent leading H of degree at least two has no finite
mate. Repeated roots normalize to nonlinear ordinary infinity limits;
Euler limits and finite Newton support certificates cover further shapes.
The remaining exact-R scalar class has H degree at most one and inverse
rank at least four. A rational commuting Darboux algebra is excluded in
all orders, while larger noncommuting idealizers remain separate.

QKR1 gives a numerical rank test before higher-rank construction. A cyclic
branch split costs inverse-Fourier rank at least N/2; finite boundary
constituents add cost. Its explicit S=g(a)+2v family really splits; QPC1 now excludes
finite mates for every nonlinear polynomial g in that entire family. QVM1 rejects
primitive v-linear leading polynomials in every momentum degree and every
finite coefficient cover, even with arbitrary lower-growth corrections.
The h-small cubic candidate passes N6/r3 and branch extraction yet fails
that mate test. QVL1 closes proper powers with bounded lower coefficients; other infinity faces remain open.

QFT1 closes rational and logarithmic repairs of the minimal line Fourier
approach, including arbitrary finite pole orders and poles moving with h.
A one-puncture monodromy criterion excludes splitting in every rank at
least three and every finite cover. Rank two can split; formal rank one
can hide higher generic connection rank. An actual higher-rank algebraic
model with the full action and finite global lattice remains a useful target.

QPH1 gives a concrete full phase matrix action and rules out algebraic
branch extraction in its entire nonconstant polynomial g_h form, even
after finite coefficient covers. Its nonsingular polynomial connection
is absolutely irreducible. The second classical branch has an X=0 pole,
so the full rank-two model cannot extend globally as a finite-left carrier.
Formal extraction gives another global Long lift, with S coefficient
degree10n-1 and momentum order n. Different algebraizations remain open;
a linear-g control has a finite scalar alternative despite phase nonsplitting.

QMC1 makes a finite-left full graph carrier an intermediate target even
before its scalar free frame is known. Generic projectivity and two-copy
Stafford freeness produce a finite matrix endomorphism; the classical
degree forces non-surjectivity. This includes nonpositional first images.
No Long carrier is constructed. A finite corner certificate must satisfy
every relation; compressing ordinary generators has an explicit defect.

QFG1 classifies formal fixed-R gauges whose position images have finite
momentum order, allowing unbounded position degrees. They preserve every
finite fiber order, and global admission is a coefficientwise relative
cohomology test. Thus a bounded exact-R target needs its unique comparison
with QPL1 to have unbounded momentum in an original position image.
Explicit global flows fix both R and the exact Euler connection K yet
have this unbounded momentum behavior. Their full frames are unbounded;
a theorem using an actual bounded target remains open.

QPL1 gives an explicit full polynomial-coefficient formal Long lift,
including the fourth image and every Weyl relation in all orders.
A single Painleve-I recurrence keeps the momentum orders at0,2,3,5;
only two exceptional indices require finite global admission checks.
Its S coefficient at h^(2n) has exact degree8n-1, so the family
is spatially unbounded. Its quadratic formal energy has completed
transcendental coefficients, preserving QSO2 and QEI1's algebraic
field boundary. Other bounded formal representatives remain open.

QEM1 supplies a nonzero elliptic cohomology obstruction without a
spatial cutoff. The quantum energy of every rational formal isogeny
lift has commutator order h^2 or h^3 with the natural cubic; an even
lift forces h^3. Exact energy preservation and univariate rational
energy reparametrization are impossible, while unrestricted formal
pairs escape by transverse energy motion. A flat two-parameter Ore
family connects the curve algebra to a localized Weyl central
reduction. The map and full admission must still be constructed.

QIM1 constructs a finite matrix model on every smooth elliptic fiber:
w=y/x gives a monic cubic with unit discriminant, a rank-three free
frame and the entire curve differential action. A six-by-six Weyl-matrix
realization has generator degree at most four. This is a curve operator
algebra, not Long's full Weyl image. A Schur-centralizer and trace argument excludes direct replacement of
its central energy parameter by a nonscalar scalar operator in every
finite matrix size; a compatible noncentral reduction remains open. Across zero energy,
Norm(x)=-4b proves the actual source algebra is intrinsically nonfinite
over the target. The next constructive bridge must handle both issues.

QDI1 separates the completed degree from the finite operator division
extension. Long has completed degree three in every full formal lift,
without fixing R. A bounded rank-two lift has generic left and right
indices between three and D^4. Equality with three requires finite
generation of the actual valuation lattice. It is not automatic: the
retained full rank-three polynomial identity lifts have generic degree
three and completed degree one, with forward bound twelve. This is a
new all-degree constraint and a descent gap, not a bounded realization
or a nonlifting theorem.

QEI1 identifies the exact elliptic three-isogeny and its source quantum
normalizer. In every bounded exact-R lift, even the discriminant energy
must acquire scalar order at least three. Arbitrarily high partner
orders cannot preserve a second-order energy. A formal fixed-energy
flow shows why bounding one energy alone does not bound arbitrary
formal images or establish a bounded full representative.

QHR1 closes full positional and cotangent normalization under every
polynomial canonical source and target change, including after any
identity stabilization. Its invariant counts commuting ambient locally
nilpotent Hamiltonians and is always one below the number required for
such a form. A bounded full quantum carrier remains open; it need not
admit that form. Other high-order nonpositional forms and parameter-pole
changes without invertible classical reductions are not excluded.

QPA1 upgrades the scalar restriction to the whole completed fiber image:
no polynomial source change can recover a canonical pair of order at
most two in the exact-R algebraic coefficient chart. If the first image
merely remains positional, the full image contains no second independent
positional observable. High scalar orders, target changes moving the
position algebra, and nonpositional first images remain open.

QSO2 closes the partner-only order escape in the exact-R bounded class:
all odd scalar partner orders are excluded over algebraic curves, and
integral even-power subtraction also forbids an odd classical partner
under a unit quadratic leading coefficient. Long's actual S,T have
orders two and three. A surviving bounded exact-R lift must increase
S's momentum order; no theorem yet forbids that increase.

For a fixed quantum triple with undeformed R, normalize its fourth
image before testing degree growth: the projection removes exactly
the R-series ambiguity without increasing degree. Other triples and
polarizations remain open. A full scalar-anomaly tuple is a positive
bounded carrier, including every cubic symplectic representative.
Replacing Long's corrected D by D0 is excluded in every degree;
module constructions still need a full right action and compatible
parameter framing. QGR1 exhausts the bounded R-preserving centralizer
group: every change fixes the positions and is a fiber translation.
QRP1's full global class decides admission; vanishing now supplies a
bounded canonical extension with a sharp primitive bound. Arbitrary formal
gauges and canonical changes moving R remain outside this classification.
QNI1 rules out a universal bounded comparison from formal equivalence:
even exact full identity lifts can have bounded images and unbounded
inverses. Both directions of every proposed bounded gauge need evidence.
