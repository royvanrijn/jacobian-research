In the rational canonical chart \(r=R,v=X^{-1},a,d\), the new

\[

\((-\infty,17,25,13)\). This is precisely the new cap-25 direction.

\[

not exclude every finite canonical map sharing the same first
infinitesimal jet. The proof below removes neither source
parameter \(\gamma,\delta\).
