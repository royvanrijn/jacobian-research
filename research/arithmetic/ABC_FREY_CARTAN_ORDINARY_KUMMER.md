# Ordinary Kummer classes in the dual Frey adjoint bound

Use the Frey curve, the two finite Selmer structures, and the
notation of the [dual adjoint comparison](ABC_FREY_DUAL_ADJOINT_SELMER.md).
Thus \(\ell\ge5\), \(m\ge1\), \(B_m=\mathbb Z/\ell^m\),
\(M_m=\operatorname{ad}^0(E[\ell^m])\) and
\(M'_m=\operatorname{Hom}(M_m,\mu_{\ell^m})\).
Write \(P\) for the projective \(\ell^m\)-division field and
\(F=\mathbb Q(E[\ell^m])^{\{\pm I\}}\).

For the nonsplit unit-determinant binary form in the
[subfield descent](ABC_FREY_DUAL_ADJOINT_SUBFIELDS.md), write
\(A\) for the Cartan field of degree \(\ell^{2m-1}(\ell-1)\).
Let \(G_{\ell,m}\) be the image of \(D^0_{\ell,m}\) in
\(A(T,\ell^m)^-\), where \(T\) is the set over \(\ell\).
Define
\[
 G^{\mathrm{ord}}_{\ell,m}
   =G_{\ell,m}\cap A(\varnothing,\ell^m)^-,
 \qquad
 A(\varnothing,\ell^m)
   =\{a\in A^\times/A^{\times\ell^m}:
                       v_w(a)=0\bmod\ell^m\ \text{for all finite }w\}.
\]
The superscript \(\mathrm{ord}\) refers to ordinary ideals,
allowing no primes to be inverted. It concerns valuations of
Kummer classes. A Kummer extension generated by a local unit
can still ramify at the coefficient prime.

There is an index bound
\[
 |D^0_{\ell,m}|\ \le\
     |G^{\mathrm{ord}}_{\ell,m}|\,\ell^{\epsilon_{\ell,m}},
 \qquad
 \epsilon_{\ell,m}=
 \begin{cases}
 1&\ell\mid N\ \text{and}\ t_E\in
                       (\mathbb Q_\ell^\times)^{\ell^m},\\
 0&\text{otherwise},
 \end{cases}
 \tag{OK1}
\]
where \(t_E\) is the local Tate parameter in the multiplicative
case, using the unramified quadratic splitting twist if needed.
For a fixed Frey triple the product of all exceptional factors
in (OK1) has logarithm \(O(\log c/\log(\log c+2))\), uniformly
in all choices of the depths. The original \(q-1\) divisors
in (DA1) remain available. There is also a finite linear test,
using three generators of a projective matrix group, for the
global compatibility of a proposed Cartan class.

## Projective Kummer descent and the compatibility test

The full image gives \(\operatorname{Gal}(P/\mathbb Q)
=\operatorname{PGL}_2(B_m)\). The scalar \(2I\) is in the
image of \(G_P\) and acts as \(4\) on \(\mu_{\ell^m}\).
In particular \((M'_m)^{G_P}=0\). By inflation–restriction,
trace evaluation on \(M_m\) and Kummer theory, there is an
isomorphism
\[
 H^1(\mathbb Q,M'_m)\ \simeq\
 \operatorname{Hom}_{B_m[\operatorname{Gal}(P/\mathbb Q)]}
    (M_m,P^\times/P^{\times\ell^m}).
 \tag{OK2}
\]
On \(G_P\), the action on \(M_m\) is trivial, so the restriction
of a cocycle is evaluated on each constant vector in \(M_m\)
to give a Kummer class. Inflation–restriction applies with
zero end groups because the invariants are zero. The action
on Kummer classes in (OK2) is the ordinary action on field
elements. The twist by \(\mu_{\ell^m}\) is already included
in Kummer theory. Also restriction
\(A^\times/A^{\times\ell^m}\to P^\times/P^{\times\ell^m}\)
is injective, by the same vanishing of \(H^0(P,\mu_{\ell^m})\).
[Full Frey image and finite dual descent, (DA5)](ABC_FREY_DUAL_ADJOINT_SELMER.md).

Fix a basis and represent the nonsplit form by \(S=\operatorname{diag}(1,\eta)\)
with \(-\eta\) a nonsquare unit. Put
\(H=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)\), \(U=e_{12}\), \(V=e_{21}\)
and \(Y=J S=\eta U-V\), with the matrix \(J\) of (BD2).
The equivariant map in (OK2) evaluated at \(Y\) is precisely
the restriction of the Cartan Kummer projection, by
\(\operatorname{tr}(SXJ)=\operatorname{tr}(Y X)\).
In fact \(\operatorname{Ad}(g)Y=(\det g/\mu_S(g))Y\) on the
similitude group. Thus this evaluation is invariant under the
Cartan and transforms by the quadratic orientation sign.
[Orientation descent, (BD6b)](ABC_FREY_DUAL_ADJOINT_SUBFIELDS.md).

Given a proposed \(\alpha\in A(T,\ell^m)^-\), write \(a\)
for its restriction to the projective field, in additive
notation for Kummer classes. Let \(u=1+U\). Put
\[
 b_H=(u^{-1}a-u a)/2,\qquad
 b_U=(u a+u^{-1}a-2a)/2,\qquad
 b_V=\eta b_U-a .
 \tag{OK3}
\]
These are the only possible images of \(H,U,V\).
Indeed \(\operatorname{Ad}(u^{\pm1})Y=Y\mp H+U\).
Choose a generator \(d_0\) of \(B_m^\times\). For each
\(g\) in
\[
          \{\,1+U,\ 1+V,\ \operatorname{diag}(d_0,1)\,\}
\]
and \(X\in\{H,U,V\}\), require
\[
              g b_X=b_{\operatorname{Ad}(g)X},
 \tag{OK4}
\]
where the right side is extended \(B_m\)-linearly from
the three \(b\)'s. These nine linear identities are an exact
test for (OK2). Necessity follows from equivariance.
For sufficiency, the two elementary matrices and their
powers generate \(\operatorname{SL}_2(B_m)\), by row reduction
over the local ring. The diagonal generator supplies the
determinants, hence their images generate the projective
group. The tested map therefore is equivariant, and
(OK2) recovers a unique global class.

In addition to (OK4), membership in \(G_{\ell,m}\) requires
the original local tests over \(\mathbb Q_2\) and
\(\mathbb Q_\ell\), on that recovered class. The valuations
away from \(\ell\) required in \(A(T,\ell^m)\) stay divisible
by \(\ell^m\) on every conjugate in \(P\). Consequently all
three \(b_X\) have this property, and the recovered class is
unramified over \(F\) away from \(\ell\). At a good rational
prime other than \(\ell\) this also gives unramifiedness
over \(\mathbb Q\) locally since the division field is
unramified there. The dual Selmer structure allows every
local class at the odd bad primes other than \(\ell\).
Thus (OK3)–(OK4) together with the two local tests and
the stated valuation condition are sufficient as well as
necessary. In particular the ordinary unit intersection
can be tested by the same equations with unit inputs.

## Kummer valuations from local duality

For a finite nonarchimedean extension \(L/K\) of local fields,
take \(Y_0\in H^0(L,M_m)\) and let \(u_L\) be the unramified
\(B_m\)-valued class with value \(1\) on arithmetic
Frobenius. Here \(K\) has the restricted Galois
representations from the preceding discussion. For
\(\xi_K\in H^1(K,M'_m)\), let \(\alpha_L\) be the Kummer
class obtained by evaluating \(\operatorname{res}_{L/K}\xi_K\)
at \(Y_0\). Tate duality gives, with a fixed sign convention,
\[
 \frac{v_L(\alpha_L)}{\ell^m}\pmod{\mathbb Z}
      =\big\langle \operatorname{cor}_{L/K}(Y_0u_L),\xi_K
                                                            \big\rangle_K .
 \tag{OK5}
\]
The local pairing with \(u_L\) is the valuation divided
by \(\ell^m\): the unramified cyclic algebra of degree
\(\ell^m\) evaluates a uniformizer to \(1/\ell^m\), and
norms include every unit. The rest is the cup-product
projection formula and the invariance of the local
invariant under corestriction. The corestricted class
in (OK5) is unramified. Indeed, Mackey's formula for its
restriction to inertia has terms which restrict
\(Y_0u_L\) to conjugate inertia subgroups of \(L\), and
all those terms vanish.
[Local Tate pairing, Fisher, equation (15) and Proposition 2.2](https://www.dpmms.cam.ac.uk/~taf1000/papers/ctp.pdf).

At a good coefficient prime \(\ell\), every unramified class
in \(H^1(\mathbb Q_\ell,M_m)\) lies in the local
Fontaine–Laffaille condition \(L_{\ell,m}\) of (DA2).
Choose its representative factoring through the unramified
quotient with values in inertia invariants. It becomes zero
over a finite unramified extension, by continuity and the
finite coefficient module. The associated first-order
representation there is the constant lift and has a
finite flat group-scheme model. The descent data extend
uniquely to that model by full faithfulness of generic
fibres in absolute ramification index \(1<\ell-1\).
Finite étale descent gives the finite flat model over
\(\mathbb Z_\ell\), together with the coefficient action.
Its torsion Fontaine–Laffaille weights are \(0,1\) in the
weight convention of (DA2). The determinant is already
fixed by construction. Since the dual local condition is
orthogonal to \(L_{\ell,m}\), (OK5) now forces
\(v_L(\alpha_L)=0\bmod\ell^m\). This applies to every
completion of \(A\) at \(\ell\), with \(Y_0\) the corresponding
conjugate of \(Y\), without an ordinarity assumption.
[Raynaud, Corollary 3.3.6, full faithfulness of generic fibres](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/bsmf.1779.pdf).
[Fontaine–Laffaille, §§9.11–9.12, finite flat groups and their generic representations](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/asens.1437.pdf).

## Multiplicative coefficient primes

At \(\ell\mid N\), the projective local image is triangular
in a Tate basis. Its intersection with a nonsplit Cartan is
trivial in \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(B_m)\), for every conjugate
Cartan. To see this use the description of that Cartan as
multiplication by units of the unramified quadratic algebra
over \(B_m\). A primitive vector is a unit of that algebra.
If a multiplier preserves its line, it acts on it by a scalar
unit of \(B_m\), so the multiplier itself is scalar.
Consequently every completion of the nonsplit Cartan
field \(A\) at \(\ell\) is a completion of \(P\). After fixing
an embedding of \(P\) write \(L/\mathbb Q_\ell\) for this
local projective division field. The valuation of \(\alpha\)
at each prime of \(A\) over \(\ell\) is an evaluation of
the same functional on \(M_m\), with the appropriate
conjugate of \(Y\).

The projective Tate representation cuts out
\(L=\mathbb Q_\ell(\mu_{\ell^m},t_E^{1/\ell^m})\).
In particular its inertia group \(I\) is upper triangular
projectively, with cyclotomic ratio onto \(B_m^\times\).
Choose the lift of a generator of \(B_m^\times\) in \(I\);
since its ratio differs from \(1\) by a unit, it can be
diagonalized by an upper-triangular basis change. This
gives a cyclic complement of order
\(\varphi_m=\ell^{m-1}(\ell-1)\) to the unipotent kernel
\(J_I\) inside \(I\). The adjoint norm of the complement
is zero on both root directions and equals \(\varphi_m\)
on the diagonal. The unipotent kernel is an additive
subgroup of \(B_m\); summing its action on the diagonal
cancels the upper entry because the sum of its elements
is zero. Hence
\[
                \sum_{g\in I}\operatorname{Ad}(g)
                    =\varphi_m |J_I|\,\operatorname{pr}_H
                    \quad\text{on }M_m .
 \tag{OK6}
\]
Here \(\operatorname{pr}_H\) is the projection to the
trace-zero diagonal in the basis of the complement.

To evaluate the corestriction in (OK5), first pass
from \(L\) to its maximal unramified subextension of
\(\mathbb Q_\ell\). The corestriction is unramified and
its Frobenius value is the inertia norm (OK6) of \(Y_0\).
This follows from the coset formula for a totally
ramified extension: take coset representatives in inertia,
and the Frobenius terms in the large field each have
degree one. Corestriction along the remaining unramified
extension is additive. Thus the image of all such
corestrictions is zero if \(J_I\ne1\), and cyclic of
order at most \(\ell\) in every case. Applying (OK5)
shows the same bound for the simultaneous valuations
of a projected class at all primes of \(A\) above \(\ell\).
In particular there is a single possible factor \(\ell\)
per coefficient prime.

The condition \(J_I=1\) is equivalent to
\(t_E\in(\mathbb Q_\ell^\times)^{\ell^m}\).
One direction follows by choosing the Tate root in the
base. For the converse let \(a\) be the maximal
rational \(\ell\)-power depth of \(t_E\), and write
\(t_E=b^{\ell^a}\) with \(b\) not an \(\ell\)-th power in
\(\mathbb Q_\ell\). The depth exists because
\(v_\ell(t_E)>0\). If \(a<m\), the field \(L\)
contains an \(\ell\)-th root of \(b\), since it contains
the necessary roots of unity. The polynomial
\(X^\ell-b\) is irreducible: a root of degree \(j<\ell\)
would give \(b^j\) as an \(\ell\)-th power by taking
norms, hence \(b\) itself. Its degree-\(\ell\) root
field is non-Galois over \(\mathbb Q_\ell\), since
otherwise it would contain \(\mu_\ell\). If \(J_I=1\),
\(L/\mathbb Q_\ell(\mu_{\ell^m})\) is unramified,
so \(L\) lies in the compositum of the cyclotomic
field and an unramified extension of \(\mathbb Q_\ell\).
That compositum is abelian over \(\mathbb Q_\ell\),
a contradiction. This proves the equivalence.
[Tate uniformization and its Kummer representation](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/home/text/others/tate/tate-unif/tate-unif.pdf).

The possible factor \(\ell\) in (OK6) occurs for the local
conditions in the split-coefficient case. If \(t_E\) is
an \(\ell^m\)-th power the local adjoint representation
splits in its three weights. In the twisted diagonal
take the Kummer class of \(\ell\). Its lower root
projection is zero, which satisfies the multiplicative
condition after (DA9). Its diagonal valuation in \(L\)
has order \(\ell\), since the cyclotomic ramification
index is \(\varphi_m\). The conjugates of \(Y\) span
the adjoint dual, so at least one Cartan projection
has a nonzero valuation. This example concerns a
local class; global occurrence is a separate question.

## Consequence for the normalized bound

All other valuations already vanish modulo \(\ell^m\)
by the Cartan Kummer descent. The index of their
coefficient-prime valuation kernel is therefore as in
(OK1). In the exceptional case
\(\ell^m\mid v_\ell(t_E)=v_\ell(\Delta_E)\).
The product of the possible factors \(\ell\), over
all \(\ell\ge5\), divides the product of the positive odd
valuations \(v_q(\Delta_E)\). The divisor estimate of (SA5)
bounds its logarithm uniformly. At the original depth
\(m_\ell=e_\ell+\beta_\ell\) this gives the sufficient
uniform estimate
\[
 (\delta_1)_{\{2,3\}}
 \prod_{\ell\in\mathcal E(E)}
   \frac{|G^{\mathrm{ord}}_{\ell,m_\ell}|}
        {\prod_{q\in Q_\ell}(q-1)_\ell}
              \ll_\varepsilon \operatorname{rad}(abc)^{2+\varepsilon}.
 \tag{OK7}
\]
Equations (OK1) and (DA1) give the signed denominator
bound with the same lower-order height error, then
(SA7) gives full abc. Alternatively at a multiplicative
coefficient prime one can take the maximum of the
original depth and \(1+v_\ell(v_\ell(\Delta_E))\).
The proof of (DA1) holds at these depths, and
\(G^{\mathrm{ord}}_{\ell,m}=G_{\ell,m}\) there.

The ordinary ideal Kummer sequence gives
\[
 0\to \bigl(\mathcal O_A^\times/
                     \mathcal O_A^{\times\ell^m}\bigr)^-
 \to A(\varnothing,\ell^m)^-
 \to \operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_A)[\ell^m]^-\to0 .
\]
Intersecting its first term with \(G^{\mathrm{ord}}_{\ell,m}\)
and taking its image in the last term now gives the two
orders in the exact product for \(|G^{\mathrm{ord}}_{\ell,m}|\).
There is no contribution from inverting primes above
the coefficient prime in either term. The remaining
upper bound must use the compatibility equations and
the original Selmer conditions on the ordinary units
and ideal class torsion. The ambient minus unit rank
calculated after (BD6c) still grows with the field
degree.
[The ordinary ideal and \(S\)-integer Kummer sequences, Cremona–Lingham, §2](https://johncremona.github.io/papers/egros.pdf).
