# Arithmetic Keller fibres

We construct prescribed finite etale fibres of Keller maps and study arithmetic failures in fixed fibres.

**Established internally:** characteristic-zero fibre constructions with a corresponding formal presentation layer. The exact field and formalization scopes are recorded in the sources.

**Unpublished:** [common arithmetic fibres](../../unpublished/common-arithmetic-fibers/README.md), [fixed-map Hasse failures](../../unpublished/fixed-map-hasse-failures/README.md), and [quadratic-gauge nonproperness](../../unpublished/quadratic-gauge-nonproperness/README.md) await the review and publication work in their metadata.

**Open:** broader marked stabilizer, descent and moduli questions. The [non-descent prospectus](prospectuses/tschirnhaus-keller-non-descent/README.md) is a research proposal, not a completed paper.
