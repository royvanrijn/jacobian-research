# Fifth torsion and the Jacobians of quintic norm curves

A primitive fifth-power norm witness gives a rational point
on a genus-two curve \(y^2=x^5+d w^2\). The class groups of
\(\mathbf Q(\sqrt d)\) and \(\mathbf Q(\sqrt{5d})\) control the
dimension of its isogeny Selmer group, up to explicitly located
local terms. Changing \(w\) can also change which global classes
satisfy those conditions, even though the two quadratic fields stay
fixed.

Fix nonzero signed squarefree \(d\ne1,5\) and an integer \(w>0\).
Write \(A=d w^2\), \(C_A:y^2=x^5+A\), and \(J_A=\operatorname{Jac}(C_A)\).
Use \(K_0=\mathbf Q(\zeta_5)\), \(B=K_0(\sqrt d)\) and
\(r_5(B)=\dim_{\mathbf F_5}\operatorname{Cl}(B)[5]\).
For \(v\) a prime of \(K_0\) away from \(2,5\), call it a
place of the set \(T(A)\) if \(B/K_0\) splits at \(v\)
and \(v(A)\not\equiv0\bmod10\). Put \(s(A)=\#T(A)\).

## The local terms and the central class rank

**Proposition QN1.** For the \(1-\zeta_5\) isogeny \(\phi\) over
\(K_0\), one has
\[
 r_5(B)-s(A)-2 \ \le\
      \dim_{\mathbf F_5}\operatorname{Sel}_\phi(J_A/K_0)
       \ \le\ r_5(B)+s(A)+4 .                                    \tag{1}
\]
Also \(\operatorname{rank}J_A(\mathbf Q)
\le r_5(B)+s(A)+4\). At split places \(v\in T(A)\) the local
Kummer image, identified by evaluation at \((0,\sqrt A)\), is
the line generated by the class of \(2\sqrt A\) in
\(K_{0,v}^\times/K_{0,v}^{\times5}\).
At other split places away from \(2,5\) it is the unramified line.
At nonsplit places away from 5 the local cohomology is zero.

Stoll's descent on \(y^2=x^\ell+A\) is the direct antecedent.
The class-group comparison with local image hypotheses has
also been developed for general superelliptic Jacobians by
Wang. His hypothesis on separating polynomial factors can
fail at a split bad place of \(x^2-A\); the term \(s(A)\)
keeps those places in the rank and Selmer comparisons here.
[Stoll, On the arithmetic of the curves and their Jacobians](https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1998.076)
[Wang, Definition 3.1 and Theorem 4.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02173)

To verify the descent used here, let \(\zeta_5\) act by
\((x,y)\mapsto(\zeta_5 x,y)\). The quotient has coordinate
\(y\) and genus zero, so the sum of the five deck actions on
\(J_A\) vanishes. Thus \(\mathbf Z[\zeta_5]\) acts and
\([5]\) is a unit times \(\phi^4\). Since \(\deg[5]=5^4\),
\(\deg\phi=5\). The class \(P_+-\infty\) for
\(P_+=(0,w\sqrt d)\) is nonzero by the genus and generates
\(J_A[\phi]\). The hyperelliptic involution negates this
class, so the module over \(K_0\) is the sign representation
for \(B/K_0\). Restriction to \(B\) in Kummer theory identifies
\(H^1(K_0,J_A[\phi])\) with the anti-invariants of
\(B^\times/B^{\times5}\), or equivalently the classes with
norm a fifth power in \(K_0^\times\). The degree two of
\(B/K_0\) is invertible modulo 5.

At nonsplit \(v\nmid5\), the local Kummer spaces for \(K_{0,v}\)
and the quadratic field over it both have dimension two,
from the valuation and the residue units with \(\mu_5\).
Restriction is injective, hence the extension space is
invariant and its anti-invariants are zero. At a split
place \(v\nmid 2,5\), one has even \(v(A)\).
If \(10\mid v(A)\), rescale \(x,y\) by uniformizer powers
\(2v(A)/10,5v(A)/10\), respectively, to obtain a unit constant
term and good reduction. The finite étale isogeny kernel
over the smooth model gives precisely unramified local
classes.

At a split \(v\in T(A)\), the isogeny on the compact local
group has cokernel of order five. Indeed it is invertible
on a stable small pro-residue-characteristic open subgroup,
since \(\phi^4\) is a unit times \([5]\), and on the
resulting finite quotient the kernel and cokernel have the
same order. The kernel is split of order five. The dual isogeny is
\(1-\zeta_5^{-1}\), a unit times \(\phi\), since the deck
action preserves the principal polarization. The Kummer
evaluation on a point \(P\) is \(y(P)-\sqrt A\) because that
function has divisor \(5(P_+-\infty)\); its leading value
at infinity is 1 in the parameter \(x^2/y\).
[Schaefer, Theorem 2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.08325)
Choose a point with \(x\) of sufficiently large valuation
and \(y\) close to \(\sqrt A\), by Hensel lifting. Then
\((y-\sqrt A)(y+\sqrt A)=x^5\) and
\(y+\sqrt A\) has the class of \(2\sqrt A\), since their
quotient is a principal unit. Consequently the evaluation
generates the claimed local line: \(v(\sqrt A)\) is nonzero
modulo 5. This line and the unramified line intersect only
in zero. For example at \(d=-2,w=11,v\mid11\),
\(x=11^2\), \(y=11\sqrt{11^8-2}\) with the square root
chosen close to \(\sqrt{-2}\) gives valuation 9 for
\(y-\sqrt A\).

For the upper bound in (1), erase the valuation of a Selmer
class modulo 5 at the places where it may be nonzero.
At each split place there is at most one coordinate
because conjugate valuations are negatives. Only \(T(A)\)
and the places over \(2,5\) can contribute. There is one
place of \(K_0\) over each of 2 and 5. The resulting
kernel lies in the anti-invariant global classes with
all valuations divisible by 5. Their image in the class
group has dimension at most \(r_5(B)\), and the
anti-invariant contribution of global units modulo fifth
powers has dimension two. The two unit ranks are 3 and 1,
and \(\mu_5\) belongs to \(K_0\).

For the lower bound use the anti-invariant unramified
Kummer classes of \(B\), of dimension \(r_5(B)\).
Indeed they are the duals of the elementary class quotient
with values in \(\mu_5\), and the invariant part of the class
group vanishes since \(K_0\) has class number one. Away from
the exceptional places these classes already satisfy the
Selmer condition. Requiring zero localization at each split
place in \(T(A)\) and the two places above \(2,5\) imposes
at most one condition each. The unramified local classes
have dimension at most one after restriction to \(B\)
and taking anti-invariants, also at places over 5.
This proves (1).

The vector space \(J_A(K_0)\otimes\mathbf Q\) is a
\(K_0\)-vector space. The action of
\(\operatorname{Gal}(K_0/\mathbf Q)\) is semilinear since it
acts on \(\zeta_5\) through the same automorphisms as on
the deck transformation. Its dimension over \(K_0\) is
therefore \(\operatorname{rank}J_A(\mathbf Q)\), by Galois
descent. The free Mordell–Weil module is torsion-free over
the cyclotomic integer ring, so its quotient by \(\phi\)
has dimension equal to this rank. The Kummer injection
into the Selmer group proves the rank assertion.

## A signed rank moment and the witness-height issue

**Corollary QN2.** Given \(D>0\), assign each signed
squarefree \(d\ne1,5\) an integer \(1\le w_d\le X^D\).
For either sign, the sums of
\(5^{\nu\operatorname{rank}J_{d w_d^2}(\mathbf Q)}\)
over \(1\le |d|\le X\), at \(\nu=3/4,1,5/4\), are
\[
      O_{D,\epsilon}(X^{7/4+\epsilon}),\qquad
      O_{D,\epsilon}(X^{53/26+\epsilon}),\qquad
      O_{D,\epsilon}(X^{5/2+\epsilon}),                         \tag{2}
\]
uniformly in these assignments. The same estimates hold with
the \(\phi\)-Selmer dimension in place of the rational rank.
At \(w=1\), near-linear moments of these Selmer sizes are
equivalent to the subpower weighted class cost in the
first cyclotomic layer of the \((10,2,5)\) descent.

At \(p\nmid10\) dividing \(d\), the quadratic extension is
ramified over every place of \(K_0\) above \(p\), since
\(K_0/\mathbf Q\) is unramified there. Consequently
\(s(d w_d^2)\le4\omega(w_d)\), giving a subpower on
raising 5 to the fixed multiple of the error term in (1).
Use the
[first-layer fifth reflection moment bounds](ABC_FIFTH_REFLECTION_MOVING_KERNELS.md#transfer-of-moments-and-the-support-budget)
for the remaining sum. If all \(w\le W\le X^D\) are
summed, the bound acquires the factor \(W\).
For \(w=1\), (1) gives
\(\#\operatorname{Sel}_\phi(J_d/K_0)\asymp 5^{r_5(B)}\)
since \(s(d)=0\). The equivalence follows by dyadic
summation and the allocation count of the first-layer
descent, or by multiplying the weighted bound by \(X\)
for the reverse direction.

A coherent witness \(u^2-dv^2=4(pq)^5\) gives the point
\((4pq,16u)\) on \(C_{d(16v)^2}\). In the higher odd
torsion count, \(Z=X^{2/13+O(\eta)}\) and
\(V=X^{7/26+O(\eta)}\) at the fifth-power crossover, with
\(|v|\ll V\). The selected pair lower bound on each field
has scale \(Z\), up to subpowers. Even an absolute
constant bound for points in every fixed-\(v\) fiber
leaves the upper cost \(V\) above that scale. Also these
points have logarithmic \(x\)-height at most
\((4/13+O(\eta))\log X\). In Alpöge's normalization,
the coefficient height is \(h(f)=\tfrac15\log|A|\),
so the
[large-point cutoff \(8h(f)\)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.05859)
is at least \((8/5)\log X+O(1)\). A count of
the occupied coefficient fibers or another constraint
coupling them is needed for a feedback bound on the
fifth-torsion moment.

## Prescribing intersections by split primes

**Proposition QN3.** Fix \(d\) as above and write
\(S_0=\operatorname{Sel}_\phi(J_d/K_0)\) in the common
anti-invariant Kummer space over \(B\). Let \(U\subseteq S_0\)
be any subspace stable under the \(C_4\) of
\(\operatorname{Gal}(B/\mathbf Q)\) fixing \(\sqrt d\).
There is a squarefree product \(w\) of at most
\[
              \max_{\chi}\dim_{\mathbf F_5}(S_0/U)_\chi
\]
rational primes, each split completely in \(B\) and congruent
to 1 modulo 25, such that
\[
             S_0\cap\operatorname{Sel}_\phi(J_{d w^2}/K_0)=U .    \tag{3}
\]
The index \(\chi\) runs over the four characters of \(C_4\)
with values in \(\mathbf F_5^\times\). This number of primes is the minimum among products
of primes subject to the stated splitting and congruence
conditions. The primes can be chosen
\(\ll |\operatorname{Disc}(B)|^{694}\), uniformly in the
subspace and \(d\). The empty product suffices for \(U=S_0\).

The space \(S_0\) is stable under the asserted action.
Conjugation by a cyclotomic automorphism sends \(\phi\) to
a unit times \(\phi\), with the same kernel and the same
local Kummer images. Under restriction to \(B\) and the
identification with \(\mu_5\) the natural action can differ
from the action on the Kummer classes only by a nonzero
scalar, which preserves each subspace.

Let \(N/B\) be the extension obtained by adjoining fifth
roots of all the classes in \(S_0\). Kummer theory gives its
Galois group as the full \(\mu_5\)-valued dual of \(S_0\).
The extension is Galois over \(\mathbf Q\) by stability.
The involution of \(B/K_0\) acts by inversion on this Galois
group, so \(N/B\) is disjoint from \(B(\zeta_{25})/B\):
on the latter quintic extension the involution acts trivially
by abelianness over \(\mathbf Q\). Identify \(\mu_5\) with
\(\mathbf F_5\) using \(\zeta_5\). For any specified
functional in \(S_0^\vee\), Chebotarev in the compositum
gives rational primes split in \(B(\zeta_{25})\), with
that Frobenius at a chosen prime of \(B\), avoiding any
finite set. The conjugate primes above the same rational
prime test the conjugate functionals, up to nonzero
scalars from the action on \(\mu_5\).
[Lenstra, Chebotarev density theorem](https://websites.math.leidenuniv.nl/algebra/Lenstra-Chebotarev.pdf)

For \(r=\max_\chi\dim (S_0/U)_\chi\), choose \(r\)
functionals on \(S_0/U\) whose restrictions span the dual
of each character space. This is done by padding bases of
the separate dual spaces with zeros and taking their direct
sums. Each functional and its orbit under \(C_4\) span its
individual character components, by the character
projectors. Choose one distinct split prime for each
functional, away from \(10d\), and multiply them to form
\(w\). At all places over those primes the original Kummer
line is unramified and the new line is transverse by QN1.
Their common condition is precisely zero localization.
The simultaneous kernels of the tests on \(S_0\) are
exactly \(U\) by construction. Conversely, the orbit of a
single functional contributes at most one direction to the
dual of each character space. Thus every such prime product
with intersection \(U\) uses at least \(r\) primes.

The other local conditions agree. Away from \(2,5\) and the
selected primes, the nonsplit cohomology is zero and the
split Kummer images are unramified. At 2 and 5, the prime
congruences make \(w\) a local fifth power: at 2 it is an
odd unit, and at 5 it is in \(1+25\mathbf Z_5\).
Scaling by a fifth root of \(w\) identifies the local
curves and sends the chosen kernel generator
\((0,\sqrt d)-\infty\) to \((0,w\sqrt d)-\infty\).
This proves the equality in (3).

For the quantitative choice, use the conjugacy classes of the
specified functionals in \(\operatorname{Gal}(N(\zeta_{25})/\mathbf Q)\),
with trivial restriction to \(B(\zeta_{25})\). They are distinct:
on any character block of dimension \(r\) the \(r\) functionals
have linearly independent components, while conjugation acts by
a scalar on that block. It follows that one prime per class must
give distinct rational primes.

The extension \(N(\zeta_{25})/B\) is abelian of exponent five and
unramified away from 2 and 5 by QN1 applied to squarefree \(d\).
At primes above these rational primes, a sufficiently deep unit
group consists of fifth powers by Hensel's lemma, with depth
bounded in terms of \([B:\mathbf Q]=8\). The character conductor
norms over \(B\) are therefore bounded absolutely. Apply
[Thorner–Zaman, Theorem 1.1](https://msp.org/ant/2017/11-5/ant-v11-n5-p04-s.pdf)
with the abelian subgroup fixing \(B\), which meets each of the
specified classes. Its prime bound is
\(O(|\operatorname{Disc}(B)|^{694})\), independent of the number
of classes in \(S_0\). Since \(|\operatorname{Disc}(B)|\asymp|d|^4\),
the individual primes are \(O(|d|^{2776})\). The multiplier \(w\)
is the product of up to \(r\) such primes, and these scales are
larger than the coherent prime-path boxes.

## Adding independent global classes in the same squareclass

**Proposition QN4.** Fix \(d\), a stable subspace \(U\subseteq S_0\),
and an integer \(b\ge0\). Put \(r=\max_\chi\dim(S_0/U)_\chi\).
There is a squarefree \(w\) supported on at most \(r+b+1\)
rational primes split completely in \(B\) and congruent to 1
modulo 25 such that
\[
  S_0\cap\operatorname{Sel}_\phi(J_{d w^2}/K_0)=U,\qquad
  \dim\operatorname{Sel}_\phi(J_{d w^2}/K_0)\ge\dim U+b.           \tag{4}
\]
In particular the isogeny Selmer dimension is unbounded over
square multipliers of any one allowed \(d\).
The construction gives \(\log w=O_b((1+r)\log(2|d|))\).

The comparison is with twist results for the same isogeny
over a cyclotomic base. [Yu, Theorem 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.07511)
applies to \(f=x^2-d\) irreducible over \(K_0\), with
\(\operatorname{Gal}(f)=S_2\), and produces all Selmer ranks
among twists parametrized by \(K_0^\times/K_0^{\times5}\).
Here the parameter is a rational square multiplier, with
prescribed overlap with the starting group and controlled
number of prime factors.

For \(b=0\) use QN3. Otherwise let \(q_1,\ldots,q_r\) be the
test primes there. Write \(k=\mathbf Q(\sqrt d)\). Choose
distinct new primes \(p_1,\ldots,p_b\) successively, split
completely in \(N(\zeta_{25})\), where \(N/B\) is the Kummer
extension of \(S_0\). For each \(p_i\) choose a prime of \(k\)
with a generator \(\pi_i\) of norm \(p_i\) such that
\(\pi_i\equiv1\) in a fixed deep modulus over 2 and 5 and
modulo \(q_j\mathcal O_k,p_h\mathcal O_k\) for \(h<i\).
For real \(k\) require the generator to be totally positive.
These conditions follow by splitting \(p_i\) completely
in the narrow ray class field of the corresponding rational
modulus over \(k\). In addition require \(p_i\) to split in the
Kummer extensions over \(B\) of all earlier classes
\[
                          \alpha_h=\pi_h/\bar\pi_h .
\]
The extensions used are Galois over \(\mathbf Q\): the ray
modulus is conjugation invariant and the \(\alpha_h\) are
inverted by conjugation of \(k\). Complete splitting can
therefore be imposed in their compositum. The deep modulus
at 2 and 5 makes all \(\alpha_i\) local fifth powers there.
Choosing the later primes by this rule makes
\(\alpha_i\) trivial locally above \(q_j\) and every \(p_h\)
with \(h\ne i\).

Put \(W_0=\prod_j q_j\prod_i p_i\).
At the prime of \(k\) dividing \(\pi_i\), all of
\(2\sqrt d,\bar\pi_i,W_0/p_i\) are units. Choose an auxiliary
rational prime \(q\) with residue
\[
                q\equiv
       \bigl(2\sqrt d\,\bar\pi_i^2 (W_0/p_i)\bigr)^{-1}
                                    \pmod{\pi_i}                 \tag{5}
\]
for every \(i\). Each residue belongs to the rational
residue field \(\mathbf F_{p_i}\). Also require \(q\) to
split completely in the compositum \(M\) of
\(N(\zeta_{25})\) and the Kummer extensions of all
\(\alpha_i\) over \(B\).

The CRT conditions (5) are independent of the complete
splitting condition. Indeed over \(B(\zeta_{25})\) the
Kummer group of \(M\) is inverted by the involution fixing
\(K_0\) and changing \(\sqrt d\). The involution exists in the
abelian base and fixes \(\zeta_{25}\). The commutator with a
lift of it contains this full quintic Kummer group. Every
abelian subextension of \(M/\mathbf Q\) is therefore inside
\(B(\zeta_{25})\). Its intersection with
\(\mathbf Q(\zeta_{\prod_i p_i})\) is trivial, since the
two fields have disjoint finite ramification supports and
\(\mathbf Q\) has no nontrivial everywhere unramified extension.
Chebotarev now gives the prescribed \(q\).
It is distinct from the \(p_i\) by (5), and distinct from the
\(q_j\) because each \(q_j\) has nonzero Frobenius in \(N/B\).
Take \(w=qW_0\).

For each \(i\), the anti-invariant class \(\alpha_i\) meets the
local Selmer condition at \(p_i\): its quotient by
\(2w\sqrt d\) is
\[
                      \bigl(2\sqrt d\,\bar\pi_i^2 q (W_0/p_i)\bigr)^{-1},
\]
which is a principal unit there and hence a fifth power.
This applies to all places of \(K_0\) above \(p_i\), because
the displayed equality and the prescribed residue come from
\(k\) and \(p_i\) splits completely in \(B\). The inverse
class at the conjugate prime gives the same local line.
At the other selected primes and at 2 and 5 the class
localizes to zero, and at all remaining primes it is
unramified. Thus \(\alpha_i\) is in the new Selmer group.
The \(b\) classes are independent modulo \(S_0\) by their
valuations modulo five at the distinct primes dividing
\(\pi_i\). On \(S_0\), the new primes \(p_i,q\) impose zero
localization automatically since they split in \(N\). The
original test primes still give exactly \(U\), and the
local conditions at 2 and 5 agree as in QN3. This proves (4).

For the stated scale, apply the same Thorner–Zaman least
prime estimate at each step over the fixed degree field
\(B\). The fields imposed over \(B\) are abelian. Their
character conductor norms are bounded by a constant times
a fixed power of the product of the already selected primes.
Indeed the additional ray characters are pulled back
from \(k\) by norm, with fixed depth at 2 and 5 and
tame depth at the other selected primes. The Kummer
extensions are tame there and the earlier \(N/B\) has
absolutely bounded conductor. To exclude prior primes
when imposing complete splitting, include the rational
cyclotomic fields of these prime conductors; they ramify at
those primes and obey the same conductor bound over \(B\).
The last CRT condition also uses only these prime conductors.
The bound on each new prime has logarithm
\(O(\log(2|d|)+\sum\log p)\), summed over the primes already
chosen. QN3 bounds the initial sum by
\(O(r\log(2|d|))\). Iterating a fixed \(b+1\) times proves the
stated logarithmic bound for \(w\).

For a concrete new class take \(d=-2\) and
\(\pi=51+25\sqrt{-2}\), of norm \(p=3851\). The primes
\(p\) and \(q=1845601\) are 1 modulo 25 and split in
\(\mathbf Q(\sqrt{-2})\). At \(p\) use the square root
\(152\) of \(-2\); \(\pi\) vanishes in this orientation
and \(q\equiv972\bmod p\) satisfies (5) for \(W_0=p\).
At \(q\) use the square root \(828598\), giving
\(\pi/\bar\pi\equiv1595836\bmod q\), whose
\((q-1)/5\)-th power is 1. The element \(\pi/\bar\pi\)
is a local fifth power at 2 and 5 since its residues at
the ramified prime over 2 and modulo 25 are 1.
These tests place the class in the Selmer group at
\(A=-2(pq)^2\). Its valuation at the chosen prime over
\(p\) is one, so it lies outside the original Selmer
group at \(A=-2\), which is unramified there.

## Locally admissible classes outside the rational divisor image

**Corollary QN5.** For fixed \(d\) and any \(b\ge1\), one can
choose the multipliers in QN4 so that \(b\) independent new
Selmer classes lie in the nontrivial quadratic character space
of \(C_4\). The image of \(J_{d w^2}(\mathbf Q)\) in the common
Kummer space is in the trivial character space. Consequently
the Selmer quotient by this rational divisor image has
dimension at least \(b\).

To put the new classes in this character space, run the
construction of QN4 with \(\pi_i\) in \(\mathbf Q(\sqrt{5d})\).
A generator of \(C_4\) fixing \(\sqrt d\) conjugates that
quadratic field, so each \(\pi_i/\bar\pi_i\) is negated in the
\(\mathbf F_5\) Kummer space. For (5), at each \(p_i\) use a
prime of \(B\) over the chosen prime of \(\mathbf Q(\sqrt{5d})\)
and evaluate the same congruence there. Its residue field
is still \(\mathbf F_{p_i}\). At the other cyclotomic places
over \(p_i\) the evaluation of \(\alpha_i\) is unchanged or
inverted, while \(\sqrt d\) is fixed by the cyclotomic lifts
chosen in QN3. The line generated by \(2w\sqrt d\) thus
contains these evaluations at every place. The auxiliary
prime argument is unchanged, since the involution over
\(K_0\) still inverts all the new classes.

Conversely a rational divisor class of \(J_A\) has a rational
divisor representative because \(\infty\) is rational.
By moving its support away from the zeros and poles, the
evaluation of \(y-w\sqrt d\) on that divisor represents
its Kummer class. The divisor is defined over \(\mathbf Q\)
and the function over \(\mathbf Q(\sqrt d)\), so this value
is fixed by the \(C_4\) fixing \(\sqrt d\). The nontrivial
quadratic character space meets the trivial space only in
zero. This gives the dimension statement directly.

For an exact nontrivial-character control use \(d=-2\),
\(\pi=101+25\sqrt{-10}\) of norm \(p=16451\), and
\(q=93636601\). Both are primes 1 modulo 25 and split in
\(B\). At \(p\) use the square roots \(654\) of \(-10\)
and \(1042\) of \(-2\), respectively. Then \(\pi\)
vanishes and \(q\equiv13960\bmod p\) satisfies (5)
for \(W_0=p\). At \(q\), the square root \(3621105\)
of \(-10\) gives
\(\pi/\bar\pi\equiv15278590\bmod q\), whose
\((q-1)/5\)-th power is 1. The same unit tests at 2 and 5
apply. Thus \(\pi/\bar\pi\) is a nonzero Selmer class at
\(A=-2(pq)^2\) outside the image of the rational Jacobian.

## A local compatibility test when both quadratic classes use one prime

**Proposition QN6.** Let \(p\nmid10d\) split completely in \(B\)
and admit prime generators of norm \(p\) in each of
\(k_1=\mathbf Q(\sqrt d)\) and \(k_2=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{5d})\).
Fix choices \(\pi_1,\pi_2\) of these generators and put
\(\alpha_i=\pi_i/\bar\pi_i\). There exists a rational integer
multiplier \(w\) such that both classes lie in the local Kummer
image of \(J_{d w^2}\) at every place of \(K_0\) above \(p\)
if and only if
\[
         \frac{\operatorname{Tr}_{k_1/\mathbf Q}(\pi_1)}
              {\operatorname{Tr}_{k_2/\mathbf Q}(\pi_2)}
                       \in(\mathbf F_p^\times)^5 .              \tag{6}
\]
Each class can satisfy the same local conditions individually
for some \(w\), irrespective of (6).

Choose the prime of \(B\) where both \(\pi_i\) vanish,
possible since their quadratic fields are independent.
At this prime \(\alpha_i=p/\bar\pi_i^2\), of valuation one,
and the reductions of \(\bar\pi_i\) are the nonzero traces
in (6). If \(v_p(w)\equiv0\bmod5\), the local image is
unramified and admits neither class. Otherwise write
\(w=p^s b\) with \(s>0\) prime to 5 and \(p\nmid b\).
The local line generated by \(2\sqrt d\,w\) contains
\(\alpha_i\) exactly when
\[
         \alpha_i^s/(2\sqrt d\,w)
                  = (2\sqrt d\,b\,\bar\pi_i^{\,2s})^{-1}
\]
is a fifth power. It is a unit and can be tested in
\(\mathbf F_p^\times\). There is a choice of \(b\bmod p\)
meeting one such condition. It meets both precisely when
the ratio of the traces to the \(2s\)-th power is a fifth
power. Since \(2s\) is invertible modulo 5, this is (6).
The other places give the same test up to inversion of
the classes or compatible embedding choices, as in QN5.

At \(d=-2,p=251\) the generators \(3+11\sqrt{-2}\) and
\(1+5\sqrt{-10}\) have trace ratio 3, and
\(3^{50}\equiv219\bmod251\). The primes separately admit
the local classes, for example with \(w=251\cdot29\) and
\(w=251\cdot10\) respectively at the orientation
\(\sqrt{-2}\equiv91\bmod251\). No multiplier admits both.
For \(p=1601\) the generators \(33+16\sqrt{-2}\) and
