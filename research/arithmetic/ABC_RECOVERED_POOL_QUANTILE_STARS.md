# Quantiles with full powers on a prescribed prime pool

At positive complete multiplicity, full powers on a prescribed shared prime pool can contribute to numerical depth. They combine with affine-star packets on both computed roles and on the query. The marked-divisor price can be calculated from the actual residue classes. The height limit is always taken at fixed \(L\), with \(n=X^L\), before the small-budget limit.

## PQ1. Fixed-star hypotheses and the quantile test

Consider common queries \(V\) of exponent at least \(v>0\), with finitely many fixed distinct original anchors in either or both other roles. Each anchor is a genuine fixed-letter fibre from the original positive primitive triples \(A+B=C\), \(X/2<C\le X\), retaining every original radical cap. The corresponding computed complete values have the form
\[
                  W_i(V)=\epsilon_i V+b_i,\qquad\epsilon_i=\pm1 .
\]
Require their affine zeros to be distinct. There is one query form \(V\), and at least one actual anchor. Suppose \(V=V_0+Gz\) with integer \(z\) in an interval of length \(X^{H+o(1)}\). Fix a full divisor \(D_T\mid G\) on every original role \(T\), allowing \(D_T=1\), such that all \(T/D_T\) in the chosen observations are units modulo \(G\). For \(G=1\) this is automatic. For one RB4 lock use \(G=D_W\), with \(D_W\) the marked computed role's full divisor and all other \(D_T=1\). In a common three-register cell use \(G=D_AD_BD_C\) and the three pairwise coprime full locks. All these assertions are on actual complete integers. Fullness ensures that dividing out the own lock removes its entire prime powers. The two other locks are units by original primitivity.

For a repeated computed role \(W\), let \(r_W\) be its residual radical cap after its own lock, and \(c_W\) the nonatomic mass of that residual profile. Select a compact part \(\mu_W\) of the residual nonatomic profile on the original exact scaled valuation orders. Let \(\mu_V\) be an analogous compact part of the query's residual spectrum or the zero measure. These selections must be realized by the actual full prime powers on each row, with the biggest eligible normalized prime cost arbitrarily small. More precisely, the laws of these selected powers approach the given measures uniformly in the retained boxes on a fixed finite collection of continuity bands. They may equivalently be obtained by the continuity-band and atom-window selection used in CQ4 and FW4. No assertion about a weak atom is made here. The measures are nonatomic, all their supports lie in \([0,B]\) for a fixed \(B\), and all original valuations and heights stay bounded by the given boxes. Write
\[
\begin{split}
 B_W(v)&=\min\{r_W-2v,\ c_W-v\},\
 J_W(x)&=\int_{(x,\infty)} y\,d\mu_W(y),\qquad
 M_W(x)=\mu_W((x,\infty)),\
 \Psi_V(t,x)&=\max_{0\le q\le t}
                   \bigl(F_{\mu_V}(q)+(t-q)x\bigr).
\end{split}
\]
Here \(F_\mu(q)\) is the top first moment of mass at most \(q\). In particular it is constant after the total mass of the measure. A negative \(B_W(v)\) by a fixed margin is already impossible by FL1 or FT1 on the original fixed-anchor fibre.

Suppose there are \(m\ge1\) copies of each repeated computed role \(W\in\mathcal I\), with this \(m\) fixed before the height limit. For every fixed \(0<t<v\), set
\[
 \mathcal K_{m,v}(t)=
 \inf_{\substack{0\le x\le B\
             M_W(x)\le B_W(v)+t/m\quad(W\in\mathcal I)}}
       \left\{\max_{W\in\mathcal I}J_W(x)+\Psi_V(t,x)\right\}.
\]
The set over which the infimum is taken is nonempty, since it includes \(B\). A necessary inequality at the prescribed star exponent is
\[
                      \mathcal K_{m,v}(t)\ \le\ H+Bt/m.        \tag{PQ1}
\]
One can use only the FL1 bound \(r_W-2v\) in \(B_W(v)\) if the chosen spectra have merely small individual costs and part of an eligible spectrum belongs to weak atoms. In that version the finitely many quantile cutoffs are chosen at continuity points and the limiting extremum is taken with the corresponding one-sided values. For the nonatomic statement as written, all tails and first moments in the infimum are continuous.

### Pool bounds and integer reconstruction

The cofactors \(W_i(V)/D_{W_i}\) are integer affine forms in \(z\): fix one actual query as the integer base \(V_0\), and use \(D_{W_i}\mid G\). Their slopes divide \(G\) up to sign. Include in one prescribed prime pool \(P\) all primes of \(G\) and the nonzero pair determinants of the computed affine forms. The support of this pool has polynomial height for the fixed list size, uniformly in the anchors. A prime outside \(P\) cannot divide two distinct computed forms on one query. The query cofactor is coprime at each original query to every computed cofactor by primitivity of the original branch.

On any one computed copy the event that the eligible pool mass exceeds \(r_W-2v+\eta\) is power-smaller than the common-query scale. Add the own supported divisor to the pool and count on this particular original fixed-anchor fibre by FL1, leaving the other two original caps present. Its residual radical cap is \(\le2v-\eta\), and the varying computed letter identifies the original query.

The same conclusion holds when the eligible pool mass exceeds \(c_W-v+\eta\). To verify uniformity, suppose contradictorily that such a class retains exponent above \(v-\eta/2\) along sufficiently late fixed budgets. Stabilize the measures of the removed eligible primes on the compact band, making the inner refinements first. The removed limit \(\lambda\) satisfies \(\lambda\le\mu_W\) and \(\|\lambda\|\ge c_W-v+\eta\), so it is nonatomic. The residual measure is dominated by the initial residual measure minus \(\lambda\), hence its nonatomic mass is at most \(v-\eta\). FT1 applies on that original fixed-anchor fibre outside the prescribed pool, contradicting the retained exponent. This also verifies uniformity for height-dependent pools arising from fixed anchors. One can use a smaller fixed fraction of \(\eta\) for all refinement allowances. Thus after deleting power-smaller subsets each computed copy has at most \(B_W(v)+\eta\) eligible pool mass. No pool deletion is needed on the query form.

Fix the exact \(P\)-supported full parts \(J_i^{\rm rec}\) of all computed cofactors, and \(J_V^{\rm rec}\) of the query cofactor, at uniform height-subpower cost. Their primes are outside \(G\) by the unit assumption. They determine compatible affine congruences on \(z\). The combined step includes the least common multiple of the computed \(J_i^{\rm rec}\), and that least common multiple is coprime to \(J_V^{\rm rec}\), by original branchwise primitivity. Any subsequently selected outside packets are mutually coprime across computed copies, coprime to the query packet, and coprime to \(P\). In all these congruences the relevant slopes are units. In particular the supported part restores at least the **largest** numerical weight discarded on any single computed copy, while all of the query's discarded numerical weight is separately restored.

### Quantile selection

Assume the strict reverse of (PQ1). All choices below have fixed margins expressed in base \(n\) or \(X\), as appropriate. Take a fine finite grid in \([0,B]\). For each query let \(x_-\) be the highest grid level at which some one computed role has eligible **outside** mass at least \(t\) above that order across its \(m\) copies. If none does, set \(x_-=0\), and omit the computed outside payment in the last step. Let \(x_+\) be the next grid level. No order exceeds \(B\), so that upper grid endpoint is never active. If zero is active one still uses its genuine outside supply. On every repeated role, total outside tail above \(x_+\) is less than \(t\). Consequently on some copy of each \(W\) the outside tail above \(x_+\) has mass \(\le t/m\). Its pool carries the complementary eligible tail, proving
\[
 M_W(x_+)\le B_W(v)+t/m+O(\eta),\qquad
 \log_X J_i^{\rm rec}\ge J_W(x_+)-Bt/m-O_B(\eta)
\]
on that copy. The second inequality uses that this compact tail has all scaled orders at most \(B\). The numerical depth of the fixed computed lcm is thus at least \(\max_W J_W(x_+)-Bt/m-O_B(\eta)\).

Choose \(q\) approximating a maximizing split in \(\Psi_V(t,x_-)\). Choose a top query packet of raw cost at most \(q\), up to arbitrarily small rounding, and weight at least \(F_{\mu_V}(q)-O(\eta)\). The fixed query pool part restores its entire lost portion. Record its outside portion, and from the supplied computed outside tail choose packet mass \(t-q\), up to one small-prime rounding, using powers of scaled order at least \(x_-\). When no grid level is active, this computed contribution is zero as required. Thus the combined extra modulus on \(z\) has numerical weight at least
\[
       \max_W J_W(x_+)+\Psi_V(t,x_+)-Bt/m
                  -O_B(\eta)-t|x_+-x_-|.
\]
The \(\Psi\) error uses its \(t\)-Lipschitz dependence on its order argument. The outside paid prime support has total raw at most \(t+o(1)\); allocation of those primes to the finitely many copies, their powers, the fixed pool records, and the finite grid data add only subpower or arbitrarily small normalized cost. A standard compactness argument shows that relaxing the finitely many tail bounds by \(O(\eta)\) changes the infimum by arbitrarily little: use continuity and a limiting minimizing \(x_+\) in the compact interval. Consequently, under a fixed violation of (PQ1), the final congruence leaves at most one \(z\) in the original interval. This bounds the sector by \(n^{t+o(1)}\), a strict contradiction since \(t<v\). Choose \(m\) first, then the grid and all error tolerances, and only then take the small-budget threshold and each inner height limit.

If desired \(H\) can be decreased by a fixed small amount depending on \(v-t\) using the small affine-feature estimate CS1. When the extra modulus has height \(X^{H-\theta}\), its progression index has length \(O(X^\theta)\). Count it on one original fixed-anchor fibre with any fixed normalized allowance \(<v-t\) using CS1. The main statement does not need this refinement.

## PQ2. Arbitrarily many fixed copies

In a full common-query source that allows any fixed number of copies at unchanged limiting exponent \(v\), let \(m\) be sufficiently large in (PQ1) for any prescribed fixed margin. Using compactness in the expanded feasible sets as \(m\to\infty\), then continuity of \(\Psi_V(t,x)\) uniformly in \(x\) as \(t\uparrow v\), gives the necessary profile inequality
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \inf_{\substack{0\le x\le B\M_W(x)\le B_W(v)\ (W\in\mathcal I)}}
       \left\{\max_{W\in\mathcal I}J_W(x)
                      +\max_{0\le q\le v}
                            \bigl(F_{\mu_V}(q)+(v-q)x\bigr)\right\}
              \ \le\ H . \quad}                                 \tag{PQ2}
\]
This reasoning is always in the order “choose a sufficiently large fixed \(m\) for the violation margin, then take sufficiently late fixed budgets.” No choice of \(m\) growing with the inner height is required.

For one computed role and no query packet it reads \(\inf_{M_W(x)\le B_W(v)}(J_W(x)+vx)\le H\). In comparison, the repeated high-degree tail cutoff only supplies \(M_W(H/v)\le r_W-2v\). The support of the omitted tail in the present test is fully paid at height-subpower cost, which accounts for the extra moment \(J_W(x)\). Two computed roles contribute through the maximum of their captured depths and their simultaneous feasibility inequalities, while the query contributes its top quantile independently. Treating the two computed supported parts as necessarily coprime would be unjustified. They need not come from the same original triple. The query does share an original triple with each computed observation, which is precisely the additional arithmetic available.

A fixed-margin violation defines a genuine numerical saving sector distinct from the earlier pointwise tail and two-copy quantiles. For one formal example let \(v=4/25\), \(r_W=1/3\), \(H=1\), and use a wholly nonatomic mass-\(1/3\) distribution with mass \(1/100\) uniform on the interval of radius \(1/100\) centered at \(20\), mass \(1/25\) on the same-radius interval centered at \(27/5\), and mass \(17/60\) on the same-radius interval centered at \(876/425\). The first moment is exactly one. Here \(B_W(v)=1/75\). CQ3's threshold \(H/v=25/4\) sees only the mass \(1/100\), allowed by its bound; its total-mass cutoff is also satisfied. CQ4 is satisfied even before its overlap deduction: concavity gives \(F_\mu(t_1)+F_\mu(t_2)\le2F_\mu(2/25)<0.957\) whenever \(t_1+t_2\le4/25\). But every feasible \(x\) for PQ2 is at least \(5.39\). Up to \(19.99\) the large cluster supplies \(J_W(x)\ge1/5\), so that \(vx+J_W(x)\ge1.0624\); beyond \(19.99\) already \(vx>3\). This example separates the profile inequalities on formal valuation data. One may make \(H\) slightly smaller than one while retaining all inequalities.

## PQ3. Actual dense cells and the price of one marked divisor

On any genuine RT6 cell with edge exponent \(2v\) and three complete dictionary exponents at most \(v\), use any chosen query role and \(m\) anchors in each other orientation. For an original query of degree \(d_V\), each orientation has \(d_V\) distinct anchor neighbors. At any stage at most \(O(m)\) anchors are forbidden by an earlier affine zero: a specified zero fixes at most one complete anchor in that orientation. Neither a cross-orientation independence assumption nor a density in arbitrary residue classes is needed. The ordinary power-mean star count, dividing by all anchor lists, gives \(n^{v-o(1)}\) common queries for any fixed list length. At tolerances \(\tau\) and an edge deficiency \(\chi\) the lower exponent is \(v-M\chi-(2M-1)\tau\) up to inner losses for \(M\) total anchors. All three radical caps remain imposed. With the three full locks use \(G=D_AD_BD_C\). At fixed anchors, the query and every computed form have identical absolute differences, so one may use
\[
 H=\min\bigl(h_V,\ \min_{W\in\mathcal I}h_W\bigr)-
                                \sum_{T=A,B,C}\log_XD_T
\]
with limiting heights and lower bounds on the lock exponents, if this is nonnegative. A negative numerical exponent by a fixed margin permits only one query even before packing. A shorter actual containing interval can also be used.

There is a more precise single-mark transfer which does not require the chosen mark to be asymptotically free. Let the original regular balanced family have row exponent \(d=1/3\), common complete dictionary exponent \(\alpha=d-u\), individual caps \(d\) up to tolerances, and actual full-divisor records on one role \(W\) of catalogue exponent at most \(f\). Fix one record \(D_W\) of fixed positive depth, retaining a chart of exponent \(d-f\). The other two roles are units in matched signed residue classes modulo \(D_W\). Jointly bin these cells by their two complete dictionary sizes and row counts, with a fixed sufficiently fine exponent mesh. A bin of cells has total edge exponent at least \(d-f\) up to the mesh errors. Write \(k\) for its cell-count exponent, \(a,b\) for the typical two part exponents (anchors and queries respectively), and \(e\) for its per-cell row exponent. Then
\[
 e+k\ge d-f,\quad
 a,b\le T(k):=\min\{\alpha-k,(d-k)/2\},
\]
up to those errors. The first upper cap uses disjointness of the complete dictionaries over residues and their original cap \(n^\alpha\). For the second, the \(n^k\) unit-residue list and RB2 bound their union on either side by \(n^{(d+k)/2+o(1)}\); divide by its number of cells to bound the bin size of each part.

Consequently at fixed \(m\) a cell contains an \(m\)-anchor common-query star with exponent at least
\[
 \begin{split}
 m e-m a-(m-1)b
 &\ge m(d-f-k)-(2m-1)T(k)\
 &\ge u-mf .                                           \tag{PQ3}
 \end{split}
\]
For the elementary star estimate the mean query degree grows, since \(e-b\ge u-f\) up to errors and the interesting bound has \(u-mf>0\). Count ordered distinct anchors by power mean and divide by their dictionary count \(n^{ma}\). To verify the last inequality, split at \(k_0=2\alpha-d\). To the left \(T(k)=(d-k)/2\), so the displayed expression is \((d-k)/2-mf\). To the right \(T(k)=\alpha-k\), so it is increasing with slope \(m-1\). Both reach \(u-mf\) at \(k_0\). The errors are \(O_m\) of the mesh and the original outer tolerances, taken after fixing \(m,f\).

Use this actual star in PQ1 with \(v=u-mf\) or any fixed slightly smaller value and \(G=D_W\). Its remaining cap \(r_W\), its marked-away portion of the valuation spectrum, and its depth \(\log_XD_W\) are the actual post-mark values. The numerical width is at most \(\min(h_V,h_W)-\log_XD_W\), up to box errors. In particular the arbitrary marked-divisor price is exactly one \(f\) per repeated observation in the guaranteed local exponent, rather than the larger losses obtained from separate typical-cell estimates. When the price may be arbitrarily small before each fixed \(m\), this recovers the full PQ2 test at \(v=u\). There is no positive star allowance at the occurrence endpoint \(u=0\).

### Marking a spectral slice before choosing the star

The same sequence gives an unconditioned necessary profile inequality at every fixed \(u>0\). In an original stabilized balanced box choose a prescribed nonatomic part of a compact positive-degree band on \(W\), and take its upper slice of mass \(p>0\), with upper-tail threshold a continuity point. Select the actual full powers over this slice on every original row. Its limiting raw mass is \(p\), and its limiting numerical weight is its first moment \(w_{\rm mark}\). Full-support enumeration gives an actual catalogue of cost at most \(p+o(1)\); the chosen divisor is full, so the selected spectrum is removed exactly from its own cofactor. An arbitrary given selection of continuity subbands with the same limits works as well. Fix \(m\ge1\) with \(mp<u\). Applying PQ3 and then limiting from below gives PQ1 with
\[
 v=u-mp,\qquad
 B_W(v)=\min\{d-2u+(2m-1)p,\ c_W^{\rm orig}-u+(m-1)p\},
 \qquad
 H=\min(h_V,h_W)-w_{\rm mark}.
\]
Here the remaining eligible \(\mu_W\) is the original eligible spectrum less the marked slice, and \(c_W^{\rm orig}\) is the original total nonatomic mass on \(W\). This applies for every choice of such a slice with strict margins, and to either original opposite query role. To avoid limiting ambiguity when using the bound, choose \(v\) slightly below \(u-mp\), and choose a smaller lower bound on \(w_{\rm mark}\) before the small-budget threshold. Numerical height and all residual radical masses must be recalculated from the same divisor record.

## PQ4. Joint full records and asymmetric catalogue prices

One may hold full divisors \(D_W,D_U,D_V\) on all three roles at a joint actual catalogue price \(f<u\) without paying for three arbitrary residue registers. Here \(U\) is an anchor, \(V\) a query, and only the register opposite \(W\) is fixed in the cell extraction. Let the fixed divisors remove original radical masses at least \(p_U,p_V\) on the corresponding opposite operands. The matched residue list modulo \(D_W\) is unchanged. Division by the fixed \(D_U,D_V\) permutes each respective unit residue list and gives the sharper bin upper bounds
\[
 a\le T_U(k):=\min\{\alpha-k,(d-p_U-k)/2\},\qquad
 b\le T_V(k):=\min\{\alpha-k,(d-p_V-k)/2\}.
\]
They are from RB2 with the two smaller residual caps, still using the original complete dictionary cap \(n^\alpha\). The total row-per-bin estimate stays \(e+k\ge d-f\). For \(m\ge2\) the function
\[
            m(d-f-k)-mT_U(k)-(m-1)T_V(k)
\]
has minimum over all \(k\) at the first breakpoint \(k=2\alpha-d+\min(p_U,p_V)\). Before both breakpoints the slope is \(-1/2\). Between them the slope is \((m-1)/2\) if \(U\) switches first, and \(m/2-1\) otherwise. After both breakpoints it is \(m-1\). Substitution therefore guarantees a common-query exponent at least
\[
   \boxed{\quad v_{\rm rec}
      = u-mf+\frac{m-1}{2}(p_U+p_V)
                         +\frac{(p_U-p_V)_+}{2}.\quad}        \tag{PQ4}
\]
The usual bin tolerances are \(O_m(\tau)\). The mean query degree is still at least \(n^{u-f-o(1)}\), since \(e-b\ge d-f-k-T_V(k)\ge u-f\) on both branches of \(T_V\). Thus the distinct-star counting is legitimate whenever the resulting exponent is positive.

The actual query is automatically in one class modulo \(G=D_WD_V\), using the fixed matched register modulo \(D_W\) and its own divisibility by \(D_V\). Its cofactor \(V/D_V\) and the computed cofactor \(W/D_W\) are units modulo \(G\). Therefore apply PQ1 with \(v\) slightly below \(v_{\rm rec}\), and use the combined numerical depth of \(D_WD_V\) in \(H\). The anchor lock contributes through the asymmetrical count improvement in PQ4. It does not give an unpurchased extra residue modulo \(D_U\). If one already has an actual three-register cell, the separate mixed-role argument in Section 3 uses all three numerical lock depths.

The common-neighborhood averaging is the elementary power-mean version of dependent random choice. For literature context it is the step preceding the alteration in [Fox–Sudakov, *Dependent Random Choice*, Lemma 2.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.3271). The fixed classical \(K_{a,b}\) counting identity is also written explicitly as equation (3) in [Conlon–Fox–Sudakov, *An Approximate Version of Sidorenko's Conjecture*](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~sudakovb/sidorenko.pdf). The arithmetic estimate uses the support and heights of the actual original rows.


## PQ5. Escaped first moments

In the full statement the actual residual escaped moments \(e_T\), when supplied by the stabilized exact profiles, can be retained. Here \(e_T\) is the difference between the limiting actual residual first moment and the weak-limit first moment. It is not the difference from a numerical upper cap unless that cap is attained. In PQ1 and PQ2 replace
\[
             \max_W J_W(x)+\Psi_V(t,x)
 \quad\hbox{by}\quad
             e_V+\max_W\{e_W+J_W(x)\}+\Psi_V(t,x).
\]
For a claimed fixed violation, first fix \(t<v\), the number of copies, and a count margin. Choose a fixed continuity cutoff \(R>B\) so large that the full tails of scaled order above \(R\) on the finitely many selected actual forms and the query have total raw cost \(O(M/R)\) within the margin \(v-t\). This follows directly by dividing their bounded numerical heights by \(R\). Record these full powers jointly at that cost. The tail weight on every copy of a role is at least \(e_W-o(1)\) in the retained exact boxes. For each computed witness copy, its escaped part is disjoint from its own compact recovered tail, so both powers divide the combined modulus and their numerical weights add on that copy. Parts outside the fixed pool are disjoint across computed copies and from all compact paid factors. The escaped query part is coprime to every computed copy and disjoint from its own compact quantile, so its contribution adds independently. The cutoff stays fixed for each required violation analysis and before taking the corresponding height limit. The same fixed-margin proof now counts at most \(n^{t+O(M/R)+o(1)}\) records. The recorded escaping factors themselves supply the additional congruences.
