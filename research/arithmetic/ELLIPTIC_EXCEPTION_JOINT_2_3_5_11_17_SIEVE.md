# A five-prime elliptic sieve with a smaller Hasse upper coefficient

Adding the complete test at eleven to the joint tests at `2,3,5,17`
gives the exact retained coefficient
`116302241/531619620`, approximately `0.2187696553`. The new loss
occurs in quadratic classes `1` and `5q_0`. A joint-image computation
resolves the possible correlation between reduction at eleven and
the formal coordinate at five.

## 1. The new finite and formal images

Use the model, points and normalization of
[J235171--J235173](ELLIPTIC_EXCEPTION_JOINT_2_3_5_17_SIEVE.md):

\[
 E_0:y^2=x^3-10656x^2+27091730x,\qquad X=x-3552,
 \quad t=-X/y,\quad F(x)=128x-25865,
\]

\[
 P=\left(\frac{373248}{49},\frac{59459616}{343}\right),\quad
 S=\left(\frac{92179201}{6400},\frac{553221611649}{512000}\right),
 \quad T=(0,0),\quad R=P+T.
\]

Write `G=E(Q)`, `q_0=2709173`, and let `E_n(p)` denote the formal
subgroup with depth at least `n` in `t`.

**Lemma J23511171 (the joint five- and eleven-primary image).**
The reduction group at eleven is cyclic of order ten. The reductions
of `R,S` are both `g=(9,5)`, of order five, and the reduction of `T`
has order two. The joint image of the five-primary part of `G` in
the formal group `E_1(5)` and the order-five reduction group at
eleven is their full product. The actual formal image at eleven is
`E_1(11)`.

**Proof.** Direct enumeration and addition give

\[
 \langle g\rangle=\{O,(9,5),(4,9),(4,2),(9,6)\},
 \qquad E(\mathbb F_{11})=\langle g\rangle\oplus\langle\bar T\rangle.
                                                               \tag{1.1}
\]

For the possible five-primary entanglement, project the local group
at five to `E_1(5)`. Since its quotient has order eight, this
projection is `[8]^{-1}[8]` on its five-primary factor. Normalize an
additive coordinate on `E_1(5)` so that its first digit is `t/5`.
Thus its first digit on the projection of a rational point `V` is

\[
 \theta_5(V)=\frac{t([8]V)}{8\cdot5}\pmod5.
\]

Exact rational arithmetic gives

\[
 \theta_5(R)=2,\qquad\theta_5(S)=1.
                                                               \tag{1.2}
\]

In the generator `g`, their order-five reduction coordinates at
eleven are both one. The two rows, modulo five, are therefore

\[
 \begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det=1\pmod5.
                                                               \tag{1.3}
\]

The closed subgroup generated by the two columns is all of
`Z_5 times C_5`: first solve for the two residues modulo five, then
adjust the formal coordinate successively by multiples of five
without changing the `C_5` coordinate. Equivalently this is the
elementary Frattini-quotient criterion for this abelian pro-five
group. Hence the image already supplied by `R,S` fills the target;
no five-saturation or Mordell--Weil basis assertion is required.

At eleven, exact multiplication gives

\[
 v_{11}(t([10]P))=1,\qquad t([10]P)=110\pmod{121}.
                                                               \tag{1.4}
\]

The closed span of this point is `E_1(11)`, by the formal filtration.
The reduction image is already full by (1.1). These facts prove the
lemma. \(\square\)

The local-group inputs are Milne, *Elliptic Curves* (2006), Chapter
II, Theorem 4.1 and Lemma 5.9, printed pp.62--63 and 67:
[primary book](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/ectext6.pdf),
[retained copy](sources/milne-elliptic-curves-2006.pdf).
The first-digit normalization uses that formal addition is additive
modulo the next level; it does not require a numerical elliptic logarithm.

## 2. Class-dependent pole probability and independence

**Lemma J23511172 (the eleven-adic refinement).** Conditional on
any fixed global class and the earlier tests at `2,3,5,17`, the
eleven-adic test has retention factor

\[
 r_{11}=\frac45+\frac15\frac1{133}=\frac{533}{665}
                                                               \tag{2.1}
\]

in classes `1,5q_0`, and factor one in classes `2,10q_0`.

**Proof.** Eleven is a good prime, congruent to two modulo three.
The possible finite zeros of `F` have squareclass `d=51730`, and
`(d/11)=-1`. There are therefore no zero-branch residues. By
[ELV1](ELLIPTIC_EXCEPTION_EXPLICIT_LOCAL_CRITERION.md) the only
nonautomatic disc is the pole disc at `O`. In that disc a point of
formal depth `j` has `v_11(F)=-2j`, so it is soluble precisely when
`3` divides `j`. By the full depth-one image in (1.4), its conditional
soluble mass is

\[
 \sum_{k\ge1}(1-11^{-1})11^{1-3k}
       =\frac1{11^2+11+1}=\frac1{133}.              \tag{2.2}
\]

The local two-isogeny squareclass character on `E(F_11)` has kernel
`<g>`: on finite nonzero `x` it is the Legendre symbol of `x`,
at `T` it is the symbol of `27091730`, and at `O` it is one.
The table (1.1) and its `T` translate verify this directly. The same
character for a rational point is the localization of its global
squareclass. This follows at a unit `x` immediately, at `O` from
`x=t^{-2}(1+O(t))`, and at a point reducing to `T` from
`y^2=x(27091730-10656x+x^2)`; the bracket is a unit of the
squareclass of `27091730`. Consequently the reduction at eleven
belongs to `<g>` exactly in classes `1,5q_0`, since

\[
 (1/11)=(5q_0/11)=1,\qquad
 (2/11)=(10q_0/11)=-1.
\]

For completeness the joint image, rather than separate local
marginals, gives the required conditional probabilities. The old
two-primary data are exactly those of J235172; the new two-primary
coordinate is already determined by the global squareclass just
computed. The old three-primary image is the one of J2171. The
only new finite five-primary coordinate is `<g>` at eleven, and
Lemma J23511171 proves it is independent of the entire formal
five-primary coordinate. The formal seventeen-primary part is
unchanged, and the new formal eleven-primary part is full by (1.4).
Chinese remainder approximation in the integer coefficients of
`R,S,T` realizes the product of these distinct primary images at
every finite level. The old image bounds and the full new factors
also cap the actual rational image, so the calculation is exact.

It follows that in either eligible class the reduction at eleven
is uniform on its five-element group even after all earlier tests;
the pole probability is `1/5`. In the other classes the pole is
impossible. The formal eleven-primary coordinate is independent
of those earlier data and of reduction at eleven. Applying (2.2)
gives (2.1). \(\square\)

## 3. Exact counts and the improved upper bound

**Theorem J23511173 (the five-prime count).** Let `M_{S;a}(B)`
count the admissible full field targets on this elliptic slice with
global class `a`, passing all tests in `S={2,3,5,11,17}`. Then

\[
 M_{S;a}(B)=c_a C_E\log B+O_E(\sqrt{\log B}),
\]

with the following coefficients, normalized relative to the whole slice:

| Class `a` | Quadratic field | `c_a` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1` | `Q(sqrt(-3))` | `6724/8860327` |
| `2` | `Q(sqrt(-6))` | `205/2456` |
| `5q_0` | `Q(sqrt(-40637595))` | `533/55860` |
| `10q_0` | `Q(sqrt(-81275190))` | `1/8` |

In particular

\[
 \boxed{M_S(B)=\frac{116302241}{531619620}C_E\log B
                         +O_E(\sqrt{\log B}),}
                                                               \tag{3.1}
\]

and the all-places Hasse count satisfies

\[
 \boxed{A_E(B)\le\frac{116302241}{531619620}C_E\log B
                         +O_E(\sqrt{\log B}).}     \tag{3.2}
\]

**Proof.** Multiply the class-one and class-`5q_0` coefficients of
J235173 by `533/665`; retain the other two. The exact sum is (3.1).
Apply [ESQ3](ELLIPTIC_EXCEPTION_SHARP_FINITE_LOCAL_ERROR.md) to
this fixed finite set of primes in each class. Negation invariance
and the two generic elliptic preimages of each target are already
included in the whole-slice normalization. Every all-places Hasse
target passes this prefix, giving (3.2). \(\square\)

This is a finite-prefix asymptotic and an all-places upper bound.
The certified quadratic class retains coefficient `1/8`. Hasse
infinitude, the intrinsic all-prime Haar measure and the growing-prime
tail problem remain open; see
[EFA3](ELLIPTIC_EXCEPTION_FINITE_LOCAL_ASYMPTOTICS.md).

## 4. Exact controls

Run from the repository root:

```sh
.venv/bin/python research/arithmetic/verify_elliptic_exception_joint_2_3_5_11_17.py --write-receipt
```

The [checker](verify_elliptic_exception_joint_2_3_5_11_17.py) enumerates
the whole reduction group at eleven, checks the character and branch
tables, computes both five-adic first digits and the eleven-adic
depth witness with exact rational arithmetic, exhausts the first-digit
matrix image, and checks the rational count constants. Its
[receipt](elliptic_exception_joint_2_3_5_11_17_checks.json) pins these
controls and their inputs. The full joint-image and counting arguments
are the written proofs above, using their retained dependencies.
