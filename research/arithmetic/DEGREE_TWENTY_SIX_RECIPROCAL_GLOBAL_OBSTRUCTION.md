# No rational reciprocal paired-critical pencil in degree twenty-six

There is no rational good reciprocal degree-26 pencil in the odd Laurent
ansatz \(f(z)=N(z^2)/z\), after the normalization specified below.
The remaining nonsquare balanced case is excluded by a complete
37-adic argument. Together with the prior sign-class obstructions,
this closes the entire rational reciprocal ansatz in degree 26.

This does not exclude rational nonreciprocal degree-26 pencils.
It does not exclude complex reciprocal models or the general alternating
covers furnished by the Hurwitz construction.

## Setting and theorem

Let \(N\in\mathbb Q[u]\) be monic of degree 13, with
\(N(1)=N'(1)=0\), and write

\[
                2uN'(u)-N(u)=25(u-1)S_N(u).
\]

Assume \(S_N\) is monic squarefree of degree 12, has no root at
0 or 1, and \(N\) is nonzero at its roots. At those roots require
\(N(u)^2/u\) to take six distinct nonzero values, each exactly
twice. Finally require their equal-value pairs to be

\[
                         u\longleftrightarrow\lambda/u
\]

for one nonzero scalar \(\lambda\). These are the normalized good
reciprocal hypotheses used in the
[coupled-ODE theorem](MIXED_RECIPROCAL_COUPLED_ODE.md).
They give thirteen finite branch levels of \(N(z^2)/z\), each with
two simple critical points, including the zero level.

**Theorem.** No such \(N\) exists over \(\mathbb Q\).

The proof below first excludes the whole balanced strong rational
quotient over \(\mathbb Q_{37}\), including its degenerate points.
It then combines the prior obstructions for all the other sign classes.
No integrality, good-reduction or real-critical-point assumption is
imposed on a hypothetical rational model.

## 1. Uniform denominator control descends to the rational quotient

Put \(r=1/\rho\), \(\rho^2=\lambda\), and use the balanced rational
quotient from the
[quotient theorem](BALANCED_RECIPROCAL_RATIONAL_QUOTIENT.md).
Its seven variables are

\[
                 x_0,x_1,x_2,z_0,z_1,z_2,\eta,\qquad \eta=r^2.
\]

Over the quadratic algebra with \(r^2=\eta\), its inverse formulas are

\[
 p_i=(x_i+rz_i)/2,\qquad
 q_i=(-1)^{3+i}(x_i-rz_i)/2,
\]

with \(P=w^3+p_2w^2+p_1w+p_0\) and
\(Q=w^3+q_2w^2+q_1w+q_0\). The strong quotient has thirteen
polynomial equations obtained from

\[
                         P^2\mid X,\qquad Q^2\mid Y,\qquad G=0,
                         \quad G=F(r)/(2r).
\]

Let \(\Gamma\) denote this strong quotient, with the exact primitive
integer equations in
[the authoritative system](degree26_balanced_rational_quotient_system.json).
All degenerate parameters in this closure are retained.

Every \(\mathbb Q_{37}\) point of \(\Gamma\) has integral invariant
coordinates and unit \(\eta\). Indeed, the equation \(G|_{r=0}=25\)
first makes \(\eta\ne0\). Adjoin a square root \(r\) to
\(\mathbb Q_{37}\). The resulting labelled point satisfies the raw
necessary equations \(P\mid X,Q\mid Y,G=0\).
[Uniform integrality](RECIPROCAL_PARAMETER_FINITENESS_AND_INTEGRALITY.md)
applies in every finite extension at \(37>25\), so the coefficients
of \(P,Q\) are integral and \(r\) is a unit. The invariant formulas

\[
 x_i=p_i+(-1)^{3+i}q_i,\qquad
 z_i=(p_i-(-1)^{3+i}q_i)/r
\]

then give integral elements of \(\mathbb Q_{37}\); their residues
lie in \(\mathbb F_{37}\). Thus a 37-adic point would reduce to an
affine point of the retained integer equations with nonzero \(\eta\).

This covers denominators and ramification of the auxiliary quadratic
extension. It does not presume that critical roots or values remain
distinct after reduction.

## 2. All residues of eta are excluded

There are three exhaustive cases.

**Zero residue.** The last primitive integer quotient equation,
specialized at \(\eta=0\), is the constant

\[
                       6692786100\equiv30\pmod{37}.
\]

Thus there is no residue point with \(\eta=0\). This also provides
a direct consistency check on the unit conclusion above.

**Nonzero square residue.** Choose \(r\in\mathbb F_{37}^*\) with
\(r^2=\eta\). The inverse quotient formulas would produce a labelled
point satisfying both square divisibilities and \(G=0\).
The existing
[complete labelled census](DEGREE_TWENTY_SIX_BALANCED_LOCAL_SQUARE_OBSTRUCTION.md)
has no such point in \(\mathbb F_{37}^7\).
Its two complete candidate procedures examine respectively 1925562
and 12275266 monic-\(Q\) candidates, with every rank-deficient branch
retained, and both return the empty solution set.

**Nonsquare residue.** The full nonsquare list is

\[
 2,5,6,8,13,14,15,17,18,19,20,22,23,24,29,31,32,35.
\]

For each value \(e\) in this list, specialize the authoritative thirteen
integer equations at \(\eta=e\) and reduce their coefficients modulo
37. In the polynomial ring

\[
                  \mathbb F_{37}[x_0,x_1,x_2,z_0,z_1,z_2],
\]

the ideal generated by these thirteen polynomials is the unit ideal.
The reduced Groebner basis is \([1]\) in all eighteen cases.
Consequently none has a point even over an algebraic closure of the
residue field.

These are eighteen complete exact eliminations, rather than tests of
selected coordinate values or of the good-reduction locus.
Both exact msolve linear-algebra variants, sparse and sparse/dense,
return the unit basis. Ordinary exact Singular slimgb independently
returns the unit basis in every case.

The three cases prove

\[
                              \Gamma(\mathbb F_{37})=\varnothing,
 \qquad\boxed{\Gamma(\mathbb Q_{37})=\varnothing}.          \tag{1}
\]

The second conclusion uses the integral-coordinate reduction proved in
Section 1. It is an obstruction to every rational point of the strong
closure, not merely to points with good reduction.

The same proof gives \(\Gamma(E)=\varnothing\) for every finite extension
\(E/\mathbb Q_{37}\) whose residue field is \(\mathbb F_{37}\), with
arbitrary ramification: uniform integrality still applies after adjoining
\(r\), and all invariant residues remain in \(\mathbb F_{37}\).
Consequently a number field admitting a balanced good model can have no
prime above 37 of residue degree one. This corollary concerns the balanced
class; it does not enlarge the coefficient-field claims for the other classes.

## 3. The input binding and exact arithmetic

The primary checker specializes the retained integer equations directly.
It emits only coefficients \(0,\ldots,36\), and independently parses
its own positive-monomial input format back to the exact sparse
coefficient dictionaries. Every emitted integer literal is at most 37.

A separate checker reconstructs every specialization directly from the
Euler equation over
\(\mathbb F_{37}[r]/(r^2-e)\), without importing the retained coefficient
generator. It forms \(P,Q,\Sigma,F,X\), computes \(X\bmod P^2\),
and splits its six coefficients into their even and odd \(r\)-parts.
Together with \(G\), these match all thirteen retained equations
up to nonzero base-field row scalars in every one of the eighteen cases.
It then verifies and executes every retained native Singular input.

The [retained independent report](degree_twenty_six_nonsquare_reciprocal_independent_verification.json)
pins those native inputs and their actual unit outputs. The
[primary receipt](degree_twenty_six_nonsquare_reciprocal_verification.json)
records the freshly repeated msolve computations, both exact
linear-algebra variants, the square-case replay and their source pins.

This coefficient bound is material. An exploratory large-prime msolve
run with unreduced coefficients exposed a signed-integer parsing failure;
that run was discarded and contributes no evidence. All retained
modulo-37 inputs are exactly reduced and independently bound as above.
The proof does not use probabilistic linear-algebra options or a
numerical root solver.

The retained evidence consists of complete exact unit-ideal computations
and their independently replayable inputs. No claim of formal proof
checking or of retained explicit Nullstellensatz multipliers is made.

## 4. Completion of the rational reciprocal classification in degree 26

Every rational model has \(\lambda\in\mathbb Q\), because its unique
equal-value pairs are Galois invariant. After choosing \(\rho^2=\lambda\),
the aggregate sign factors have pair counts \(p+q=6\).
Changing the sign of \(\rho\) exchanges them.

If \(\lambda\) is nonsquare, quadratic conjugation exchanges the
sign factors, so \(p=q=3\). Otherwise, up to that exchange, all
possibilities and their exclusions are:

| Raw sign degrees | Arithmetic obstruction |
|---|---|
| \((0,12)\) | [Uniform-sign rational obstruction](PAIRED_CRITICAL_RECIPROCAL_OBSTRUCTION.md), with its projective modulo-11 certificate |
| \((2,10)\) | [No point in the necessary closure over \(\mathbb Q_{61}\)](DEGREE_TWENTY_SIX_MIXED_2_10_GLOBAL_OBSTRUCTION.md) |
| \((4,8)\) | [No point in the strong closure over \(\mathbb Q_{29}\)](DEGREE_TWENTY_SIX_MIXED_4_8_GLOBAL_OBSTRUCTION.md) |
| \((6,6)\) | Equation (1), covering square and nonsquare parameters |

The last row now also excludes every nonsquare balanced model.
These cases exhaust the normalized good reciprocal locus, proving
the theorem over \(\mathbb Q\).

The normalization loses no rational model within this odd Laurent
constant-product ansatz. Before normalization, the thirteen finite
branch levels occur in opposite pairs under \(s\mapsto-s\), so one
level is zero. Its two simple critical points give a unique double
root \(u_0\ne0\) of \(N\). The monic greatest common divisor of
\(N,N'\) is \(u-u_0\), hence \(u_0\in\mathbb Q^*\). Replace

\[
                         N(u)\quad\text{by}\quad
                         u_0^{-13}N(u_0u).
\]

This is a rational coefficient operation giving the required double
zero at 1. Squared critical values are multiplied by \(u_0^{-25}\)
and the reciprocal parameter by \(u_0^{-2}\), preserving all the
good-pair conditions. No choice of a square root of \(u_0\) is needed.

Thus a rational degree-26 construction must depart from this
constant-product reciprocal critical pairing. The unrestricted
nonreciprocal trace chart and the general polynomial-pencil problem
remain open.

This is an arithmetic obstruction in a restricted ansatz. No
nonexistence theorem for all rational degree-26 alternating pencils,
no contradiction to complex Hurwitz existence, no novelty claim,
no external human review and no formal verification is asserted.
