# The degree-eight alternating Hurwitz orbit

This is a complete finite computation on a fixed permutation class. It identifies
the degree-eight algebraic examples with the braid orbit of the original knot
tuple, without choosing numerical root paths or claiming that different functions
are isomorphic.

## Theorem 1. One full-alternating orbit

Let C be the 210 double transpositions on {0,...,7}, and put
P=(0 5 1 2 6 7 4)(3). Let

\[
 \mathcal T=\{(a,b,c,d)\in C^4:abcd=P\}.
\]

Use composition pq=p composed with q. The braid group B4 acts by replacing an
adjacent pair (a,b) with (b,bab); its inverse replaces it with (aba,a).
Exactly 93,296 tuples belong to T. Exactly 47,334 generate A8, and these form
one B4 orbit, containing the literal alternating knot tuple. Consequently
there is one absolute Nielsen orbit of degree-eight covers with four finite
double-transposition branch cycles, full A8, and one distinguished infinity
branch cycle of type (7)(1).

Proof and exact finite reduction. Every double transposition is uniquely a
four-element support and one of its three pairings. This gives all
3 binomial(8,4)=210 class elements, sorted lexicographically in the checker.
For every class-element product m, form the list of ordered pairs (a,b)
with ab=m. All tuples with abcd=P are then exactly the concatenations of
a pair from that list and a pair whose product is m^-1 P. This exhaustive
meet-in-the-middle enumeration gives 93,296 distinct tuples. A separate
direct fourth-factor calculation for all 210^2 tuples with the fixed first
seed entry checks the enumeration by a second calculation.

The two displayed adjacent moves are inverse, preserve product, and preserve
the generated subgroup: replacing a,b by b,bab keeps both new generators in
the old group, and a=b(bab)b recovers the old group. Therefore the generated
group is constant on every braid orbit. Breadth-first traversal with all three
moves and their inverses partitions the finite set completely. For one
representative per component, a second breadth-first traversal of ordinary
permutation products enumerates the generated subgroup. The complete census is:

| Orbit size | Generated subgroup order | Number of orbits |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 47,334 | 20,160 | 1 |
| 15,435 | 2,520 | 2 |
| 10,976 | 2,520 | 1 |
| 1,029 | 1,344 | 2 |
| 1,029 | 168 | 2 |

All generators are even, so a subgroup of order 20,160 is exactly A8.
The literal knot tuple lies in this component. Since there are no other
components of that group order, the stated transitivity follows.

To pass from arbitrary sheet labels to this fixed-product calculation, relabel
any seven-cycle finite product to P. Relabelings preserving P form its
centralizer, the cyclic group generated by P, of order seven. The full twist
in B4 acts on the tuple by simultaneous conjugation by P or P^-1, depending
on convention. Thus this centralizer does not split the braid orbit.
On tuples generating A8 its action is free: a simultaneous centralizer of
all generators centralizes A8, which has trivial centralizer in S8.
Hence the absolute Nielsen class has 47,334/7=6,762 points.

For the centralizer assertion, a permutation commuting with A8 has its image
of 0 fixed by the A7 stabilizer of 0. That stabilizer acts transitively on the
other seven symbols, so its only fixed symbol is 0. Commutation and
transitivity of A8 then force the permutation to fix every symbol.

This computation does not identify the two split seven-cycle conjugacy classes
under inner A8 conjugacy. Absolute sheet relabeling uses S8; the infinity
branch point remains distinguished throughout.

## Corollary 2. The explicit covers lie in the knot tuple's braid orbit

Both the [quadratic-field model](DEGREE_EIGHT_ALTERNATING_PENCIL.md) and the
[rational-coefficient models](RATIONAL_ALTERNATING_PENCIL_REALIZATION.md)
have four finite double-transposition branch values, one infinity value of type
(7)(1), and full geometric A8. After choosing branch loops, arranging the finite
values, and relabeling the sheets, their branch cycles therefore belong to the
same B4 orbit as the original knot tuple.

A [literal original-model continuation certificate](DEGREE_EIGHT_EXPLICIT_BRAID_WORD.md)
also supplies a specific ten-letter braid word after a specified sheet
relabeling. The finite-orbit proof above applies uniformly to all covers with
the stated passport; the continuation certificate binds that word to the
original quadratic-field coefficient model.

The conclusion concerns braid equivalence and the associated Hurwitz component.
The quadratic-field cover's failure to have a rational form is consistent with
rational covers elsewhere in the same component. No equality of branch values
or isomorphism of individual covers is asserted.

## Reproduction and assurance

Run from the repository root:

\[
 \texttt{python3 research/arithmetic/verify\_degree\_eight\_hurwitz\_orbit.py}.
\]

The [checker](verify_degree_eight_hurwitz_orbit.py) uses only integer
permutations and Python's standard library. Its literal seed is read from the
[existing tuple inputs](../meridional-rank/representations/alternating_target_prime_families_inputs.json).
The [receipt](degree_eight_hurwitz_orbit_verification.json) records all component
representatives, exact group orders, deterministic digests of the component
members, software and source hashes, and rejection controls. It was produced
with the additional option
`--write-receipt research/arithmetic/degree_eight_hurwitz_orbit_verification.json`.

The conclusion is a computational theorem because the domain is finite and
exhaustively enumerated by the proved reduction above. It is not a finite
sample supporting an all-degree assertion. No formal verification, independent
external replay, external human review, or priority claim is made.
