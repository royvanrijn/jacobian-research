\maketitle

Three source double-cover groups have exact models with three canonical
Seifert pieces. Their number of pieces alone gives no rank obstruction.
Instead, a specified central filling is nonabelian, contradicting the
meridional requirement in the only three-piece type of the classical
Boileau--Weidmann rank-two list. The finite inputs have independent replay;
the manifold realization, JSJ and classification steps below use explicit
classical imports. This working proof is unformalized and externally
unreviewed. The family audit in
\path{ORIGINAL_SIXTEEN_KNOWN_GRAPH_COVER_FAMILY.tex} places these knots
in the existing BJW18 graph-cover MRC family; exact named priority remains
unestablished.

\begin{theorem}
For $K=13a_{233},13a_{239},13a_{253}$,
\[
 \rank\pi_1(\Sigma_2(K))=3,\qquad \mr(K)=b(K)=4.
\]
Together with the four-piece and final-five companions, these give all
sixteen original-target equalities. The final five are treated in
\path{FINAL_FIVE_DOUBLE_COVERS_SHARP_FOUR.tex}. The full classical
meridional rank conjecture remains open.
\end{theorem}

\section{Exact source presentations and the rank-two condition}

The normalized core presentation on the retained thirteen-crossing PD has
one base arc set to $1$ and crossing relation $o a^{-1}o b^{-1}$.
The retained core-group theorem identifies this group with the double
branched cover group. Independent replay checks all generator eliminations,
inverse free-basis changes and normal-closure-preserving relator replacements.
Each of the three roots has a closed three-generator presentation.
Their abelianizations have Smith factors $(1,1,D)$, where respectively
$D=403,361,337$.

The selected crossing subsets, indexed from zero, are
\[
 I_{233}=\{0,1,2,3,4,5,9\},\quad
 I_{239}=\{0,1,2,3,4,5\},\quad
 I_{253}=\{0,1,2,3,4,5,6\}.
\]
They have four shared physical edge labels, listed below. Independent
overpass arc reconstruction checks each fragment presentation, and the
seventeen fragment arc classes glue by four independent identifications to
the thirteen full source arcs. Normalizing the two fragment base arcs gives
the exact algebraic assembly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:assembly}
 G_i=(P*Q_i*\langle z\rangle)/
 \langle\!\langle p_jzq_j^{-1}:j=1,2,3,4\rangle\!\rangle.
\end{equation}
Indeed the unnormalized inner arc word is its normalized word times $z$;
the two right factors cancel in every core crossing relation. Gluing the
arc classes and undoing the normalizations gives inverse presentations.
A physical tangle sphere or numerical triangulation is not an input to
this algebraic identity.

In all three cases
\[
 P=\langle u,v\mid v^{-1}u^{-2}v^{-1}u\rangle,
\]
and the other fragment is respectively
\begin{align*}
 Q_{233}&=\langle x,y\mid y^{-2}x^{-2}yx^{-2}\rangle,\\
 Q_{239}&=\langle x,y\mid y^{-2}x^3yx^3\rangle,\\
 Q_{253}&=\langle x,y\mid y^{-2}x^{-2}y^{-2}x\rangle.
\end{align*}
All source words, generator maps and exact reductions are retained in
\path{remaining_cover_structure_results.json}; the chosen ports and
models are in \path{three_piece_cover_results.json}.

The needed rank theorem is Boileau--Weidmann, \emph{The structure of
3-manifolds with two-generated fundamental group}, Topology~44 (2005),
283--320, \url{https://doi.org/10.1016/j.top.2004.10.008}.
Theorem~2 of the read author manuscript lists ten necessary JSJ types for
a compact orientable irreducible rank-two manifold with nontrivial JSJ.
Only case~6 has three pieces. It requires two end Seifert pieces over
$A(p)$ or $D(p,q)$ and a central two-bridge link exterior, with the end
fibres glued to the central link meridians. We use this necessity only;
no rank-equals-Heegaard-genus statement or full independent proof of that
classification is asserted. The earlier exceptional irregular-cover type
has two pieces and is irrelevant here.

\section{Explicit Seifert models and injective boundaries}

Put $H=\langle a,t\mid t^2=a^3\rangle$, with infinite central fibre
$h=a^3=t^2$. In $P$, set $t=u^2v$. The relator is equivalent to
$t^2=u^3$, and conversely $v=u^{-2}t$. Thus $P=H_P$, with
\[
 h_P=u^3,\qquad e_P=v h_P^{-1}=ut h_P^{-2}.
\]
Use a Seifert model over $D(2,3)$ with cone generators $u,t$, both with
powers $h_P$. The remaining boundary section may be changed by an integral
fibre twist; $(e_P,h_P)$ is a boundary basis.

Each $Q_i$ is a root amalgam
\begin{equation}\label{eq:root}
 H_Q*_{\langle d\rangle=\langle s^m\rangle}\langle s\rangle
 =\langle a,t,s\mid t^2=a^3,\ s^m=d\rangle.
\end{equation}
The inverse eliminations specifying it are
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lllll}
\toprule
$i$ & $a,s$ & $t$ in old generators & $m$ & $d$\\
\midrule
$233$ & $y,x$ & $x^{-2}y$ & $2$ & $at^{-1}$\\
$239$ & $y,x$ & $x^3y$ & $3$ & $ta^{-1}$\\
$253$ & $x,y$ & $y^2x^2$ & $2$ & $ta^{-2}$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Substitution makes the source relator a cyclic conjugate or inverse of
$t^2a^{-3}$, so these are isomorphisms, not merely quotient maps.
Choose the $H_Q$ peripheral section to be $e_Q=d$ for the first two
rows and $e_Q=ta$ for the third. These are explicit $D(2,3)$ models:
the cone pairs are $(a,t^{-1})$, $(t,a^{-1})$, and $(t,a)$ respectively,
with fibre powers $h,h^{-1}$ in the first two cases and $h,h$ in the
last. In the third row $d=e_Qh^{-1}$.

Let the middle Seifert piece over $A(m)$ have group
\[
 C=\langle k,s\mid[k,s^m]=1\rangle,\qquad f=s^m.
\]
Its boundary bases are $(k,f)$ and $(c,f)$, where
$c=s^\eta k$, and $\eta=1,-1,1$ in the three rows.
This follows from the pair-of-pants times circle construction using
cone generator $s^\eta$, with $(s^\eta)^m=f^\eta$, and the other two
boundary words $k,(s^\eta k)^{-1}$.
Glue the first boundary to $H_Q$ by
\[
 k=h_Q,\qquad f=d.
\]
This is a torus isomorphism: in $(e_Q,h_Q)$ its columns are
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ in the
first two rows and
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&-1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ in the
third. Eliminating $k,f$ gives \eqref{eq:root}; the relation
$[h_Q,d]=1$ is already true in $H_Q$.

Every displayed boundary injects as $\mathbb Z^2$.
The quotient $H/\langle h\rangle=C_3*C_2$ makes each section a
cyclically reduced word of length two, hence of infinite order; $h$
is infinite by the map $a\mapsto2,t\mapsto3$ to $\mathbb Z$.
Similarly $C/\langle f\rangle=\mathbb Z*C_m$ makes both $k$ and
$s^\eta k$ infinite, and $f$ is infinite under $s\mapsto1,k\mapsto0$.
These facts prove independence of the two commuting generators in each
boundary. The amalgam normal-form theorem therefore injects all pieces
and all boundary tori.

\section{Canonical pieces and the meridional filling obstruction}

Glue the three orientable pieces in the chain
\[
 \underset{D(2,3)}{H_P}\;---\;
 \underset{A(m)}{C}\;---\;
 \underset{D(2,3)}{H_Q}.
\]
All boundary components are paired, and orientations can be chosen
consistently along the tree, so this constructs a closed orientable
manifold with exactly the source group. The pieces are irreducible with
incompressible boundary, and gluing preserves irreducibility. Their base
orbifold Euler characteristics are $-1/6$, $-1+1/m$, $-1/6$.
Thus the solid-torus, product and twisted Klein-bundle exceptions to
boundary-fibre uniqueness do not occur. The nonparallel fibre slopes make
the two tori canonical JSJ tori, with exactly three Seifert pieces.
We import these standard Seifert/JSJ facts as stated in Hatcher's
\emph{Notes on Basic 3-Manifold Topology}, Sections~1.2--2.1,
Theorem~1.9, Propositions~1.11--1.13 and Lemmas~1.14--1.15, retaining
the original Jaco--Shalen/Johannson credit. The source cover need not be
identified with this constructed manifold by a numerical recognition
or a homeomorphism theorem: an exact group isomorphism is enough for rank.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:filling}
Filling the $C$ outer boundary along the adjoining $H_P$ fibre does not
give a solid torus in any of the three models.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The filling slope is $c^rf^s$, with
\[
 (m;r,s)=(2;3,-2),\quad(3;-2,-1),\quad(2;-3,2).
\]
The filled group is $C/\langle\!\langle c^rf^s\rangle\!\rangle$.
Quotient further by $f=1$. In the free basis $s,c=s^\eta k$ its
presentation becomes
\[
 \langle s,c\mid s^m=1,\ c^r=1\rangle=C_m*C_{|r|}.
\]
It surjects onto the nonabelian group $S_3$: send $s$ to $(12)$ and
$c$ to $(123)$ when $(m,|r|)=(2,3)$, and interchange these images when
$(m,|r|)=(3,2)$. Thus the filled group has a nonabelian quotient and
cannot be the infinite cyclic group of a solid torus. The finite checker
verifies these permutations and the original filling relation directly.
\end{proof}

A two-component two-bridge link has unknotted components: each component
has at least one bridge, and the two-bridge presentation therefore gives
one bridge on each, making each an unknot. Filling either meridian of
its exterior restores the complement of the other unknot, a solid torus.
In the only three-piece rank-two type, BW05 case~6, the adjoining
$H_P$ fibre must be such a meridian of the central two-bridge link
exterior. Lemma~\ref{lem:filling} contradicts this necessary condition.
The model has an embedded nonabelian $H_P$, so rank one is also impossible.
Its rank is at least three, and the exact three-generator source
presentation gives equality.

\section{Knot conclusion, controls and retained failures}

The even-meridian subgroup argument gives
$\mr(K)\ge1+\rank\pi_1(\Sigma_2(K))=4$.
Each of these three knots has its own independently replayed four-seed,
nine-step Wirtinger-colouring certificate. The retained
Blair--Kjuchukova--Velazquez--Villanueva colouring/bridge theorem gives
$b(K)\le4$. Since $\mr(K)\le b(K)$, both equal four.

Three pieces alone would have been insufficient. For comparison,
$A(2)$ can be the $(2,4)$ torus-link exterior, with genuine meridians
$k$ and $sk$, each having fibre intersection one. Killing either in
$\langle k,s\mid[k,s^2]=1\rangle$ gives $\langle s\rangle$;
the BW05 three-piece type allows rank-two examples with these meridian
attachments. Our obstruction uses the actual attachment slope.
The two preceding four-piece roots are retained positive controls.
Independent replay covers five full core reductions, fourteen fragment
reductions and all balanced four-port cuts in the small selection.
Five altered-port, slope, conjugation, physical-arc and false-transfer
controls reject.

The five proposed transfers all failed, with unequal determinant pairs
$403/389$, $361/359$, $361/337$, $361/311$, $337/311$.
None is used here. Similar PD prefixes cannot identify these covers.
The other five knots need separate lower certificates; their checked
upper-four traces alone do not settle them. All source inputs and failed
searches are retained.
