\title{An all-dimension boundary for binary rank-one meridian bounds}
\author{Uniform reduction and exact finite enumeration}
\date{17 November 2026}
\begin{document}\maketitle

For a knot group representation, the codimension of its common fixed
space divided by the meridian displacement rank gives a meridional
lower bound. On a fixed panel of $293$ named knots, every such bound
from rank-one binary meridian displacements is at most three, in
any finite ambient dimension. The proof reduces the question to
four dimensions and exhausts $7^4=2401$ seed models for each literal
four-meridian diagram. It includes reducible representations and
does not require the image to be a prescribed finite group.

Let $\mathcal P$ denote exactly the $293$ distinct names whose
literal diagrams and census bindings are retained in
\path{rank_one_binary_boundary_inputs.json}. The input also binds
this name set to the frozen panel snapshot. This defines the finite
domain; it is not a claim that all its knots are currently unsolved.

\section{Removing a common fixed space}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:reduction}
Let $\Gamma$ be normally generated by an element $\mu$, and let
$\rho:\Gamma\to\operatorname{GL}(V)$ be a finite-dimensional
representation over any field with $\rk(\rho(\mu)-I)=1$.
Put $F=V^{\rho(\Gamma)}$ and $q=\operatorname{codim}F$.
If $q>1$, the induced representation on $V/F$ has zero common
fixed space and meridian displacement rank one.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Suppose first that $\mu$ acts trivially on $V/F$.
Normal generation makes the whole quotient action trivial.
Every image element then has the form $I+N$ with $N(V)\subset F$
and $N|_F=0$. For two such maps $N,M$ one has $NM=0$, so
\[
                         (I+N)(I+M)=I+N+M.
\]
The image is abelian. Its normal generation by $\rho(\mu)$ makes
it cyclic, generated by $\rho(\mu)$. Hence
$F=\ker(\rho(\mu)-I)$ and $q=1$, a contradiction.
Therefore $\mu$ acts nontrivially on $V/F$; its displacement there
has rank at most one and so has rank exactly one.
The same holds for every conjugate of $\mu$.

If a class $v+F$ is fixed by the quotient action, then
$(\rho(g)-I)v\in F$ for every conjugate $g$ of $\mu$.
The displacement image of each such $g$ is a line whose image in
$V/F$ is nonzero. Its intersection with $F$ is therefore zero.
Consequently $(\rho(g)-I)v=0$ for every such $g$.
These conjugates generate $\Gamma$, so $v\in F$.
The quotient common fixed space is zero.
\end{proof}

The hypothesis $q>1$ is necessary. A single transvection on $\F_2^2$
has a fixed line, and becomes the identity after quotienting that
line. Neither irreducibility nor complete reducibility is used in
the lemma.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:four}
Suppose a knot group is generated by four meridians. If a binary
representation with rank-one meridian displacements has common
fixed-space codimension four, there is a four-dimensional binary
representation with rank-one meridian displacements and zero
common fixed space.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:reduction}. A knot group is normally generated
by a meridian: its Wirtinger generators are conjugate meridians.
\end{proof}

For any four generating meridians, the intersection of their fixed
spaces is the common fixed space, so its codimension is at most
four when their displacements have rank one. Thus codimension four
is the only case that could yield a rank-one lower bound above three.

\section{A complete four-dimensional model}

Every invertible rank-one displacement matrix over $\F_2$ has the
form $R=I+uf$ with nonzero column $u$ and nonzero covector $f$.
If $f(u)=1$, then $R(u)=0$, contradicting invertibility.
Hence $f(u)=0$ and $R^2=I$: every such matrix is a transvection.
There are $15\cdot7=105$ pairs $(u,f)$, since the annihilator of
a nonzero vector has seven nonzero covectors. Over $\F_2$ the pair
is unique, because there is no nontrivial scalar rescaling.

Suppose four such matrices $R_i=I+u_if_i$ generate an image with
zero common fixed space in four dimensions. Their four covectors
have common kernel zero and are therefore a basis of the dual space.
Use them as the coordinate functionals. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:models}
               R_i=I+u_i e_i^{\mathsf T},\qquad
               (u_i)_i=0,\quad u_i\ne0,\qquad 0\le i<4.
\end{equation}
Each column $u_i$ has exactly seven choices. Thus \eqref{eq:models}
gives $7^4=2401$ ordered models containing every four-dimensional
case with zero common fixed space, after change of basis.
No condition on the root span, irreducibility or full image is imposed.

For pairs $R_o=I+uf$ and $R_a=I+vg$, conjugation is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:conj}
 R_oR_aR_o
  =I+\bigl(v+f(v)u\bigr)\bigl(g+g(u)f\bigr).
\end{equation}
The new factors are nonzero and their evaluation is zero, because
$R_o$ is invertible and $R_o^2=I$.
The checker reconstructs all $105^2$ pair-conjugation entries and
independently compares them with literal binary matrix multiplication.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:finite}
For every $K\in\mathcal P$, there is no four-dimensional binary
representation of its knot group with rank-one meridian
displacements and zero common fixed space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each literal thirteen-crossing PD has four distinct seed arcs and a
nine-step propagation trace reaching every arc. At a crossing with
over-arc $o$ and under-arcs $a,b$, involutory images satisfy
$R_b=R_oR_aR_o$ for either signed Wirtinger convention.
Assign the four seeds each model \eqref{eq:models} and propagate
with \eqref{eq:conj}. This determines every meridian matrix.
Check the four unused crossing relations. They, together with the
nine propagation relations, are all thirteen relations.

The exact enumeration gives
\[
\begin{array}{l|r}
\text{literal named diagrams}&293\\
\text{ordered seed models per diagram}&2401\\
\text{ordered seed models checked in total}&703493\\
\text{models satisfying every crossing}&0.
\end{array}
\]
The checker reconstructs every trace and unused crossing set directly
from its PD. Its accepted model family has four independent
covectors by construction; no image-order, transitivity or
irreducibility filter is present. Every hypothetical representation
in the statement would occur in this exhaustive family, proving
the assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:boundary}
For every $K\in\mathcal P$ and every finite-dimensional binary
representation $\rho$ of its knot group with meridian displacement
rank one,
\[
                     \dim V-\dim V^{\rho(\Gamma)}\le3.
\]
Consequently no such rank-one binary representation, in any finite
ambient dimension, certifies $\mr(K)\ge4$ by the fixed-space bound.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The four meridian generators give codimension at most four.
If it were four, Lemma~\ref{lem:four} would produce a representation
excluded by Theorem~\ref{thm:finite}. Thus it is at most three.
The fixed-space lower bound with meridian displacement rank one is
exactly this codimension.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:dual}
For every $K\in\mathcal P$ and every finite-dimensional binary
representation with rank-one meridian displacements, the span of
all meridian displacement images has dimension at most three.
Every irreducible such representation has dimension at most three.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply Corollary~\ref{cor:boundary} to the contragredient
$\rho^\vee(g)=\rho(g^{-1})^{\mathsf T}$. It again has rank-one
meridian displacements. For
$E=\sum_{g\in\Gamma}\operatorname{im}(\rho(g)-I)$ one has
\[
                          (V^*)^{\rho^\vee(\Gamma)}=\operatorname{ann}(E).
\]
Thus $\dim E\le3$. This bounds the span of the meridian displacement
images, which is a subspace of $E$. The latter meridian span is
nonzero and invariant: conjugation carries each meridian displacement
line to another such line. In an irreducible representation it is
therefore all of $V$, proving the dimension assertion.
\end{proof}

\section{Exact inputs, independent control and scope}

Run the standalone standard-library replay:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 verify_rank_one_binary_boundary.py
\end{verbatim}
It checks all $2401$ models for each case, literal sphere diagrams,
one-component conditions, complete four-seed traces and saved
named-census dart maps. It checks a positive seed model with four
independent covectors and rejects corrupted traces and census maps.
Small controls distinguish the codimension-one exception from a
codimension-two quotient that retains its rank-one displacements.

An actual knot provides a further positive control outside $\mathcal P$.
The same $2401$ models on the retained fifteen-crossing diagram of
$T(4,5)$ give exactly one valid model. Its image has order $120$,
every meridian displacement has rank one, and its common fixed space
is zero in dimension four. Every literal crossing is also checked
by matrix multiplication. Thus the same algorithm accepts a genuine
binary rank-one fixed-space bound four on this control knot.

The named records come from \texttt{snappy\_15\_knots}~1.2.1,
read through Spherogram~2.4.1. Their retained dart bijections preserve
edge gluing, cyclic order up to one global reversal and over/under
data up to one global swap. The permitted mirror and orientation
operations preserve the meridian representation question.
The optional \texttt{--census} mode rereads all $293$ actual named
PD and DT records, rereads the torus-knot constructor for the positive
control, and hashes the exact packaged database.
The named census is an explicit trusted import.

The optional \texttt{--full-class-control} mode fixes the first
transvection and checks that $8176$ orbits under its $192$ explicit
inner-centralizer actions cover all $105^3=1157625$ remaining triples.
Its $440$ valid inner-colouring orbits all have seed-covector rank at
most three. Thus some transvection colourings exist; the theorem
excludes those with zero common fixed space in four dimensions.
This control is separate from the $2401$-model finite proof.

The dimension reduction is a written argument, and the finite result
has exact internal checks. Formal verification and external specialist
review are not claimed. The field $\F_2$ and rank-one conditions are
essential to the stated boundary. It does not bound the knots'
meridional rank, unrestricted augmentation rank, other-field methods
or rank-two meridian bounds.
