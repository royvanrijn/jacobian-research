within this programme. Each double-cover group is the group of an explicit
closed irreducible three-piece graph manifold, and a nonabelian central

\]
\end{theorem}

\section{Source identities and three types of root group}

All five source PDs are inspected directly. No mutation or common-cover
transfer is used. Each normalized full core presentation has an exact
three-generator reduction. The independent checker reconstructs physical
overpass arcs, checks crossing words $o a^{-1}o b^{-1}$ with base arc $1$,
and replays each Tietze elimination, inverse free-basis map and
normal-closure relator replacement. The retained core-group theorem
identifies this normalized presentation with the double branched cover
group. Source abelianizations are $C_D$, with $D=389,359,337,311,311$.

For $234$, select crossing indices zero through five and nine.
For $242,243,244$, select zero through five; for $265$, zero through six.
Independent arc reconstruction checks the formal
fragments and the seventeen-to-thirteen arc join over four physical ports.
Exactly as in the preceding source assembly proof, right normalization
by a relative base variable $z$ gives an isomorphism
\begin{equation}\label{eq:assembly}
 G=(P*Q*\langle z\rangle)/
 \langle\!\langle p_jzq_j^{-1}:1\le j\le4\rangle\!\rangle.
\end{equation}
Every core crossing equation is invariant under multiplying all its arc
variables on the right by the same word, and undoing the two normalizations
gives inverse presentations. No literal tangle sphere is needed for this
algebraic identity.

The first fragment is always
\[
 P=\langle u,v\mid v^{-1}u^{-2}v^{-1}u\rangle
   =\langle u,t\mid t^2=u^3\rangle,\qquad t=u^2v.
\]
Set $h=u^3$ and $e=vh^{-1}=ut h^{-2}$. These are a fibre and boundary
section in the explicit Seifert $D(2,3)$ model with cone generators
$u,t$, both having powers $h$. An integral fibre twist gives section $e$.
The other fragment is one of
\begin{align*}
 Q_R(m)&=\langle x,y\mid y^{-m}x^{-1}y^{-m}x^2\rangle,\\
 Q_L&=\langle x,y\mid y^{-2}x^{-2}yx^{-2}\rangle,\\
 Q_S&=\langle x,y\mid y^{-2}x^{-2}y^{-2}x\rangle.
\end{align*}
All three have exact models
\begin{equation}\label{eq:root}
 H*_{\langle d\rangle=\langle s^m\rangle}\langle s\rangle,
 \qquad H=\langle a,t\mid t^2=a^3\rangle,
 \quad h_Q=a^3,
\end{equation}
specified by
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lllll}
\toprule
Type & $a,s$ & $t$ in old generators & $m$ & $d$\\
\midrule
$Q_R(m)$ & $x,y$ & $y^m x$ & $2$ or $3$ & $ta^{-1}$\\
$Q_L$ & $y,x$ & $x^{-2}y$ & $2$ & $at^{-1}$\\
$Q_S$ & $x,y$ & $y^2x^2$ & $2$ & $ta^{-2}$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
In each row, substitution makes the original relator a cyclic conjugate
or inverse of $t^2a^{-3}$. Eliminating the defined $t$ gives the original
presentation back, proving isomorphism in both directions.

Let $C=\langle k,s\mid[k,s^m]=1\rangle$, with fibre $f=s^m$.
This is an orientable Seifert piece over $A(m)$, with boundary bases
$(k,f)$ and $(sk,f)$. The alternate outer basis $(ks,f)$ is valid too:
$ks=k(sk)k^{-1}$ and $k$ commutes with $f$, so it differs only by a
boundary base-path change. Glue $(k,f)$ to $H_Q$ by $k=h_Q^\epsilon$,
$f=d$. Choose $e_Q=d$ for $Q_R,Q_L$, and $e_Q=ta$ for $Q_S$, where
$d=e_Qh_Q^{-1}$. The cone pairs $(t,a^{-1})$, $(a,t^{-1})$ and $(t,a)$
give these explicit $D(2,3)$ sections. The inner torus map, in columns,
is
\[
 N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\ \epsilon&\nu\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 \nu=0\text{ for }Q_R,Q_L,\quad \nu=-1\text{ for }Q_S.
\]
It is unimodular for either sign of $\epsilon$; the commutation relation
$[h_Q,d]=1$ is already true in $H_Q$, so eliminating $k$ gives
\eqref{eq:root} exactly.

All boundary maps inject as $\mathbb Z^2$. Indeed $h_Q$ is infinite
under $a\mapsto2,t\mapsto3$, and the sections project to infinite
cyclically reduced words in $H/\langle h_Q\rangle=C_3*C_2$.
Likewise $f$ is infinite and $k,sk,ks$ have infinite images in
$C/\langle f\rangle=\mathbb Z*C_m$. These facts show independence of
each commuting peripheral pair. Amalgam normal form injects all pieces
and edge groups.

\section{The canonical decomposition and rank obstruction}

Glue the explicit orientable Seifert pieces in the chain
\[
 \underset{D(2,3)}{H_P}\;---\;
 \underset{A(m)}{C}\;---\;
 \underset{D(2,3)}{H_Q}.
\]
All boundary tori are paired, and orientations can be chosen along the
tree. This gives a closed orientable irreducible manifold with exactly
the source group. The pieces are irreducible with incompressible
boundary, and their orbifold Euler characteristics are $-1/6,-1+1/m,-1/6$.
Thus none is a solid torus, a torus product or a twisted Klein-bundle
exception. Their unique boundary fibre slopes are nonparallel at each
gluing. Standard JSJ uniqueness therefore makes these exactly three
canonical pieces. These classical Seifert and JSJ facts are imported
through Hatcher's \emph{Notes on Basic 3-Manifold Topology},
Sections~1.2--2.1, Theorem~1.9, Propositions~1.11--1.13 and
Lemmas~1.14--1.15, retaining original Jaco--Shalen/Johannson credit.
Only a manifold with the exact source group is needed for the rank
argument, not a numerically recognized source-cover homeomorphism.

Theorem~2 of Boileau--Weidmann, \emph{The structure of 3-manifolds with
two-generated fundamental group}, Topology~44 (2005), 283--320,
\url{https://doi.org/10.1016/j.top.2004.10.008}, is the rank input.
Of its ten necessary rank-two JSJ types, only case~6 has three pieces.
It requires a central two-bridge link exterior, with each adjoining
end fibre identified with one of its link meridians. We use this
necessary condition, explicitly imported from the read author manuscript
printed page~3; no rank-equals-genus theorem is assumed.

The actual $H_P$ fibre is $c^rf^q$, with
\[
 (m;r,q)=(2;3,-2),\quad(3;-2,1),\quad(2;-3,2),
\]
the last triple applying to $243,244,265$. Filling $C$ along this slope
and then quotienting by $f=1$ gives
\[
 \langle s,c\mid s^m=1,\ c^r=1\rangle=C_m*C_{|r|}.
\]
This holds for both outer markings: $(s,ks)$ and $(s,sk)$ are free bases
before imposing the relations. It has an $S_3$ quotient, taking $s,c$
to a transposition and a three-cycle when $(m,|r|)=(2,3)$, and reversing
the choices when $(m,|r|)=(3,2)$. All five actual filling relations and
noncommuting images are checked pointwise. The filled manifold thus
cannot be a solid torus, whose group is infinite cyclic.

Each component of a two-component two-bridge link is an unknot: the two
bridges give one on each component. Filling either link meridian
restores the complement of the other unknot, a solid torus. Our actual
filling contradicts the necessary meridional attachment in BW05 case~6.
Since $H_P$ embeds and is nonabelian, rank one is impossible as well.
The exact source upper-three presentation proves cover rank exactly
three in each case.

The retained even-meridian subgroup argument now gives
$\mr(K)\ge1+\rank\pi_1(\Sigma_2(K))=4$.
Each of the five named PDs has its own independently replayed four-seed,
nine-step colouring certificate. The retained
Blair--Kjuchukova--Velazquez--Villanueva colouring/bridge theorem gives
$b(K)\le4$. Hence $\mr(K)=b(K)=4$, proving Theorem~\ref{thm:five}.

\section{Verification scope and next boundary}

The finite receipt checks eight full core reductions, thirty-two fragment
reductions, all balanced four-port cuts and every selected port. Three
previously sharp roots reproduce their sealed models. Six corruptions
reject an altered port, an unadjusted $ks/sk$ order, a missing conjugation,
the wrong $265$ fibre sign, a false physical arc, and a false nonabelian
claim for a genuine meridian slope. The cyclic filling control $k=1$ or
$sk=1$ leaves $\langle s\rangle$. Three pieces alone never suffice.
All literal PDs, plans, group maps, normal forms and finite filling
quotients are retained with input hashes; no failed transfer was replaced
by an unsupported cross-knot identification.

The proof and receipt close the original sixteen-case panel together
with the preceding eleven cases. Their existing-family membership is
established in \path{ORIGINAL_SIXTEEN_KNOWN_GRAPH_COVER_FAMILY.tex}.
Exact named global priority, independent review, full MRC and exhaustive
literature review remain open. Progress outside this established family
needs a separately justified target and a lower-bound mechanism beyond
the excluded affine and finite-module methods.
