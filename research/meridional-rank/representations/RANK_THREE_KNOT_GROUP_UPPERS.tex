The thirty-two knots below have meridional rank four by explicit
finite affine certificates. Their ordinary groups,
however, have generating sets of size three. Every algebraic
reduction in the certificates is independently replayed as a valid
Nielsen or Tietze move. The connection between the initial cell
presentations and the actual diagram exteriors uses the specified
SnapPy combinatorial construction; it is an explicit software
import, not a claim of formal verification.

Put
\[
\mathcal S_8=\{13a_{373},13a_{375},13a_{391},13a_{392},
       13a_{3264},13a_{3266},13a_{3275},13a_{3276}\},
\]
and let $\mathcal T_{24}$ consist of
\[
\begin{split}
\{&13a_{304},13a_{362},13a_{623},13a_{629},
13a_{3232},13a_{3259},13a_{3374},13a_{3375},\\
&13n_{834},13n_{838},13n_{839},13n_{991},13n_{992},13n_{993},
13n_{1367},13n_{1370},\\
&13n_{1371},13n_{1374},13n_{1375},13n_{1376},
13n_{3814},13n_{3840},13n_{3911},13n_{3914}\}.
\end{split}
\]
Put $\mathcal S_{32}=\mathcal S_8\cup\mathcal T_{24}$, and separate
ordinary generation from the meridional comparison.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:upper}
For every $K\in\mathcal S_{32}$,
$\operatorname{rank}\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)\le3$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:rank}
For every $K\in\mathcal S_{32}$, the ordinary group rank satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{rank}\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)\le3
       <4=\operatorname{mr}(K)=b(K).
\]
No assertion that the ordinary rank is exactly three is made.
\end{theorem}

\section{Exact diagrams, triangulations and initial presentations}

The finite inputs are the literal PDs already bound to the named
knots in \path{seven_sheet_cover_inputs.json} and
\path{a6_affine_inputs.json}. For each PD we set
both the Python and SnapPy random seeds to the retained constructor
seed, then call \texttt{Spherogram.Link(PD).exterior()}.
No later randomization is used. The full SnapPea triangulation string
is saved and reloaded as a \texttt{Triangulation}. This fixes the
labelled combinatorial input and its presentation basis; an unlabelled
isomorphism signature alone would not fix that basis.

The unsimplified presentation is extracted with
\texttt{fundamental\_group(False,False,False)}.
SnapPea's source explains its construction: the thickened one-skeleton
of the dual ideal complex supplies a handlebody, while original
triangulation edges supply its two-cell relations. A spanning-tree
and generator selection are combinatorial. There is no Dehn filling
in these inputs. This cell-presentation extraction and the PD exterior
constructor are the trusted exact combinatorial imports of the proof.
Their full implementation has not been independently re-established.

\section{Why every retained algebra move preserves the group}

Use signed integers for free words, with $-i$ representing $x_i^{-1}$.
Free cancellation and cyclic conjugation preserve a relator's normal
closure. For distinct current generators $x_a,x_b$, a Nielsen slide
replaces $x_a$ by one of
\[
 x_ax_b,\quad x_ax_b^{-1},\quad x_bx_a,\quad x_b^{-1}x_a.
\]
In each case its inverse substitution is explicit. For example,
if $y_a=x_ax_b$, then substitute $x_a=y_ax_b^{-1}$ into every
relator and into every recorded original-generator word.
The checker handles all four sign and left/right cases, and verifies
their two free-group inverse compositions. Inversion
$y_a=x_a^{-1}$ is handled in the same way.

The decisive check occurs at every proposed deletion. The checker
requires an actual current relator that freely and cyclically reduces
to $x_a$ or $x_a^{-1}$. It deletes that one relator, substitutes
$x_a=1$ in all the others, and relabels the final generator into
the vacant slot. This is a Tietze elimination justified by its
retained unit relator. A generator is never removed merely because
the external move stream requests its removal. In particular,
a power relation such as $x_a^2=1$ would not justify this operation.

All recorded original-generator expressions are updated by the same
inverse substitutions. At the end every original generator is an
explicit word in three remaining generators. The three final
generators are also recorded as words in the original cell generators.
Thus the original group is generated by those three elements.
The final relators need not equal SnapPy's shorter displayed relators:
the external routine can additionally shorten its relators, while
this proof needs only the group-preserving moves checked above.
This proves Lemma~\ref{lem:upper}.
The separate cover and four-seed proofs supply the meridional and
bridge equalities, and therefore establish Theorem~\ref{thm:rank}. Independently,
the explicit finite-group certificates in
\path{TERNARY_AFFINE_QUOTIENTS.tex} supply the same equalities
without a cover-group rank computation. For $\mathcal T_{24}$,
\path{A6_AFFINE_SHARP_FOUR.tex} gives the independent direct matrix
proofs of $\operatorname{mr}=b=4$.

\section{Replay, source pins and limitations}

The standard-library command
\begin{quote}\ttfamily
python3 verify\_rank\_three\_knot\_groups.py
\end{quote}
checks all retained Tietze reductions and literal input bindings.
It does not reconstruct a three-manifold or infer a name from a
presentation. With \texttt{--snappy}, using the pinned topology
environment, it additionally reconstructs every literal PD exterior,
compares its face pairings, cusp labels and integral peripheral
rows with the saved snapshot, and recomputes its unsimplified cell
presentation. It also checks each PD against the packaged named
HT census by exact planar-map normalization. Floating-point solution
and shape entries in SnapPea strings are ignored in this acceptance;
no numerical isometry, volume or hyperbolicity test is used.

The retained receipt
\path{rank_three_knot_group_verification.json} distinguishes these
two replay modes. Its topology mode was actually run. A missing
set of cell relators is rejected at the first unsupported deletion.
The initial group extraction remains trusted even in topology mode;
the independent part is the complete finite algebra reduction.

The exact primary software source is the PyPI SnapPy~3.3.2 source
distribution, whose SHA256 is
\begin{quote}\ttfamily\small
00252e8b65eeff52a5b9d8f2d01458465f51c7fe16d0cb796fe91b01efaba511.
\end{quote}
The relevant files are retained unchanged as
\path{sources/snappy-3.3.2-fundamental_group.c} and
\path{sources/snappy-3.3.2-fundamental_group.pyx}.
The companion \path{sources/snappy-3.3.2-init.py} pins the
\texttt{\_link\_exterior} wrapper used for the literal PD constructor;
its underlying link-triangulation algorithm remains imported.
The C file's opening comments describe the initial cell presentation
and move encoding. The Cython method
\texttt{generators\_in\_originals} specifies the integer stream's
basis-update conventions. The checker implements those conventions
independently, validates the corresponding inverse substitutions,
and checks the deletion witnesses rather than importing the
software simplifier's correctness.

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:ordinary}
The ordinary-rank inequality
$\operatorname{rank}\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)\le\operatorname{mr}(K)$
cannot prove meridional rank four for these thirty-two knots. In particular,
a known-family theorem whose sufficient hypotheses force ordinary
group rank four cannot apply to them.
\end{corollary}

This does not exclude other meridian-sensitive lower bounds,
nonsymmetric augmentations, or unrelated earlier proofs of their
meridional ranks. Worldwide priority remains a separate question.
The assurance is an internal exact algebra replay with stated
combinatorial software imports, not formal verification or external
specialist review.
