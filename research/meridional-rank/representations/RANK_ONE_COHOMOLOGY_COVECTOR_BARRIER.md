# A universal covector bound also bounds rank-one cohomology certificates

All fields and meridian eigenvalues below are allowed, including
characteristic two and determinant mu=1. Every stated rank-one
displacement is nonzero.

## 1. The general affine-extension lemma

Let G be generated by conjugates m_1,...,m_b of one element m, and let
rho:G->GL(V) be a finite-dimensional representation over any field F.
Assume rank(I-rho(m))=1 and put d=dim V. Define

 Zroot={z in Z1(G,V): z(m) in im(I-rho(m))}.

All its cocycles are root-valued on EVERY meridian. Indeed, for
m_i=gmg^-1 the cocycle identity gives

 z(m_i)=rho(g)z(m)+(I-rho(m_i))z(g),

and both terms lie in im(I-rho(m_i)). This also shows that the chosen
conjugate meridian does not affect the definition of Zroot.

**Theorem CovectorCohomologyBound (universal affine-extension bound).** Suppose every finite-dimensional
representation of G with nonzero rank-one meridian displacement has
covector-span dimension at most c. Then, for the particular rho above,

 dim Zroot<=c,             dim Z1(G,V)<=d+c-1.            (1)

No irreducibility or preserved form is assumed for either representation.

**Proof.** Z1 and Zroot are finite dimensional, since evaluations on the
b generators inject them into V^b. On the space V direct-sum Zroot define

 hatrho(g)(v,z)=(rho(g)v+z(g),z).

Evaluation z->z(g) is linear, and the cocycle identity makes hatrho a
representation. Its inverse is provided by g^-1.
Choose nonzero pairs rho(m_i)=I-u_i f_i. Root-valuedness uniquely writes
z(m_i)=u_i a_i(z), where a_i is a linear functional on Zroot. Thus

 I-hatrho(m_i)=(u_i,0)*(f_i,-a_i).                       (2)

This has nonzero rank one because its restriction to V already does.
Its determinant is det rho(m_i), so invertibility and the common
meridian determinant are preserved, also when that determinant is one.

The a_i span ALL of Zroot*. If they had a nonzero common annihilator,
it would be a cocycle vanishing on every generating meridian and hence
on every word, a contradiction. Projection of the extended covector
span in (2) onto Zroot* is therefore surjective. The assumed universal
covector bound applied to hatrho gives dim Zroot<=c.

Finally Zroot is the kernel of the linear map

 Z1(G,V)->V/im(I-rho(m)),      z->z(m) mod im(I-rho(m)).

The target has dimension d-1. Hence dim Zroot>=dim Z1-(d-1), proving
(1). This argument uses no division by a characteristic-dependent
integer. QED.

The second summand above is canonically Zroot, not its dual; the
extended covectors project to Zroot*. In a chosen cocycle basis both
are represented by ordinary coefficient arrays.

**Lemma UniversalCovectorRootCohomologyEquivalence.** Fix G, its conjugate
meridional generators and a field. Across all finite-dimensional
representations with nonzero rank-one meridian displacement, the
following two uniform bounds are equivalent:

 (i) every covector span has dimension at most c;
 (ii) every Zroot has dimension at most c.

The implication (i) to (ii) is Theorem CovectorCohomologyBound. For the
reverse, in any such representation every coboundary

 b_v(g)=(rho(g)-I)v

belongs to Zroot. The map V->B1(G,V), v->b_v, has kernel V^G. Because
the meridians generate G and I-rho(m_i)=u_i f_i with u_i nonzero,

 V^G=intersection_i ker(f_i),
 dim B1(G,V)=dim V-dim V^G=dim span{f_i}.

Thus the covector-span dimension is at most dim Zroot in each
representation, proving (ii) to (i). This reverse implication uses no
affine extension and includes determinant one and every characteristic.
The equivalence concerns the root-restricted cocycle bound; no converse
from the weaker numerical bound on unrestricted dim Z1 is asserted.

## 2. Consequence for disjoint protected U blocks

The two-sided protected-U theorem supplies the universal covector
hypothesis, including reducible representations.

**Corollary ProtectedRankOneCohomologyBarrier.** Let b>=4k and let a
b-strand braid consist of k disjoint copies of the protected U braid,
followed by a tail fixing the first and fourth free meridians of every
block exactly. Suppose its closure is a knot. Theorem 3 of
[Two-sided protected rank-one defects](TWO_SIDED_PROTECTED_OBSTRUCTIONS.md)
gives c=b-k over every field, for all finite-dimensional representations
with nonzero rank-one meridian displacement. Hence every such coefficient
representation satisfies

 dim Zroot<=b-k,        dim Z1(G,V)-dim V+1<=b-k.         (3)

[Gao--Gu--Li--Wang, arXiv:2611.15673v1](sources/gao-gu-li-wang-2611.15673v1.pdf),
Lemma 6.2, printed pages 38-39, gives the meridional
lower bound

 dim Z1(G,V)-dim V <=(r-1) rank(rho(m)-I).

When this rank is one, its resulting lower certificate is at most b-k
by (3). The restricted-root affine-lift count likewise has dimension
at most b-k. Neither method can exceed the existing complex
augmentation upper bound in this protected architecture.

This is not a claim that cohomology bounds in general are weaker than
augmentation rank. It uses the stronger ALL-REPRESENTATIONS covector
bound. Rank-two and higher coefficient modules are not covered.

## 3. Exact finite controls and remaining finite target

The standard-library checker constructs the full universal affine extension for
one actual eight-strand protected knot and a six-dimensional F11
reflection module. It independently derives the full cocycle kernel,
imposes the root-valued constraint, and checks the augmented matrices
satisfy the complete closed-braid presentation. The dimensions are

 dim V=6, dim Z1=11, dim Zroot=6, covector-span(hatrho)=6.

The original covector span and coboundary map each have rank 5; the
global invariant space has dimension 1. This also checks the reverse
bridge and its possibly strict inclusion B1 in Zroot.

This saturates (3) at b=8,k=2. The checker also has a determinant-one
unipotent control in characteristic two, and rejects an unrestricted
translation outside the original root line because that augmentation
has displacement rank two. These controls supplement the unrestricted
proof; they do not prove it by finite sampling.

The theorem leaves coefficient modules with meridian displacement rank
at least two open. It establishes no new knot, prime realization or
hyperbolic deformation.
