# No MW17 fibration on the pinned determinant948 NS has the required involution

The [complete determinant948 rootless J2 classification](ROOTLESS_J2_COMPLETENESS_TRACK_2026-08-31.md)
has exactly two frame classes. **Neither supports a pointed symplectic
involution.** Thus changing the elliptic fibration on this NS cannot supply
the parent involution for the [order-four correlated-gain mechanism](ORDER_FOUR_LIFT_CORRELATED_GAIN_MECHANISM_2026-09-15.md).
This strengthens the earlier [equation-specific symmetry obstruction](MW17_J_MONODROMY_AND_SYMMETRY_BARRIER_2026-09-15.md).
It does not exclude correlated gain by other mechanisms.

The complete finite calculation is:

| Frame | Full isometry group order | Traces of nonidentity involutions |
|---|---:|---|
| Published R17 |2| -17 |
| Alternate Q80 |4| -17, -3, 3 |

The [checker](scripts/verify_det948_all_frame_symmetry.py) reads the two literal
Grams from the retained complete Niemeier certificate. It recomputes their
full isometry groups with PARI, then closes the returned generators by integer
matrix multiplication in Python. It checks each generator against the Gram,
checks group cardinality against both PARI and the retained census, and tests
every element for square1 and trace. The [result](../artifacts/generated-results/elkies-k3-det948-all-frame-symmetry-v1/result.json)
contains all generators and input/checker hashes. No new frame enumeration
or equation construction is run. Group-order completeness relies on PARI;
there is no independent group-order implementation or formal verification.

For the geometric implication, a pointed automorphism fixes U generated by
fibre and zero-section classes. A nonidentity symplectic involution on a
Picard19 K3 has an eight-dimensional anti-invariant subspace in NS, hence its
rank17 frame action must have trace9-8=1. This is the
[retained cohomological criterion](X1092_ROOTLESS_INVOLUTION_GLUE_GATE_2026-09-15.md).
None of the computed involutions qualifies; the discriminant-action condition
need not be tested after the trace fails.

Every geometric MW17 fibration on this Picard19 NS is rootless by
Shioda–Tate, so its frame belongs to one of these two classes. The argument
does not need a classification of J1 surface-automorphism orbits: a pointed
involution on any representative would induce a trace1 isometry of its frame.
A pointed nonsymplectic base involution also fails, since composition with
fibre inversion would give the excluded symplectic involution.

This rules out the rational-surface8+9 symmetric-parent setup on this NS as
well. In that setup, base change u=t² gives a pointed nonsymplectic involution
on the proposed rank17 K3. Its composition with fibre inversion would again
have the forbidden trace1 action. This conclusion applies to the stated
setup, not to all decompositions of Mordell–Weil groups.

The [correlated-gain objective](CORRELATED_QUADRATIC_GAINS_2026-09-12.md) remains
open. Together with the complete determinant1092 frame gate, this means that
the current symmetry route needs a different NS with a compatible frame.
The determinant1020 witness also fails for its one retained frame, but no
complete-frame assertion is made for that NS.
