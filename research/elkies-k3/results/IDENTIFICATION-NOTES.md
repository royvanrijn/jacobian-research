
## Published local criterion

Elkies' local criterion for L_N / Ness in arXiv:0802.1301 uses N as
the discriminant of the quaternion algebra, not the product of
quaternion discriminant and Eichler level.

For the rank-17 example:

    X(6,79)
    quaternion discriminant D = 6 = 2*3
    level M = 79

Thus the only odd ramified prime tested by this criterion is p=3.

For the recovered rank-17 lattice, an order-3 discriminant class has

    norm = 89/3
    chi_3(89) = -1

while the required character is

    -chi_3(2*6/3) = -chi_3(4) = -1.

Hence the published local criterion is satisfied.

The previous p=79 test was inapplicable because 79 is the level prime,
not a ramified prime of the quaternion algebra.
