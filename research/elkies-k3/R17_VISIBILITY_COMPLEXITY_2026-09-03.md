# Visibility complexity at the published R17 controls (2026-09-03)

## Outcome

Degree alone is not a useful visibility invariant.  A target-fitted moving
genus-one bisection pencil passes through every one of the 38 displayed
exceptional generators at the rank-25--28 controls.  That exact degree-two
statement is a codimension-one incidence construction, not a predictor of the
exceptional direction.

The leakage-aware baseline is different:

| rank lower bound | displayed generators | individually rigid degree-two generators | rational-bisection span dimension | certified predeclared-pencil generators | post-hoc genus-one degree-two generators |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 25 | 8 | 4 | 5 | 0 | 8 |
| 26 | 9 | 0 | 3 | 0 | 9 |
| 27 | 10 | 0 | 2 | 0 | 10 |
| 28 | 11 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 11 |

The third and fourth columns measure different objects.  The familiar
`5,3,2,1` pattern is the dimension of the span generated by split rational
bisections.  It does not say that `5,3,2,1` named basis generators are each
represented by one rational curve.  The exact individual count is `4,0,0,0`.

Consequently the minimum known rational-curve degree is two for only four
ordered generators:

| rank | direction | rigid cover | primitive branch cost `(support,max bits,total bits)` | existing construction cost `(additions,input bits)` |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | `Q1` | `orbit-1cb25` | `(3,41,121)` | `(4,1920)` |
| 25 | `Q3` | `orbit-051a1` | `(3,33,96)` | `(8,3143)` |
| 25 | `Q5` | `orbit-1d5bb` | `(3,35,103)` | `(10,3762)` |
| 25 | `Q6` | `orbit-0d4ca` | `(3,44,128)` | `(9,3773)` |

For the other 34 generators the rational-curve degree remains `UNKNOWN`.
The complete degree-two atlas gives no individual witness, and the existing
degree-three and degree-four equation atlases are not exhaustive.  No
predeclared trace-pencil experiment was logged before these controls were
known, so predeclared trace-pencilled degree is conservatively `UNKNOWN` for
all 38.  The already fitted genus-one witnesses cannot be relabelled
retrospectively as held-out predictions.

## Definition

Fix an atlas `A`, its construction budgets, and its cost order before seeing
the target point `Q` or the control parameter `t`.  For a witness curve `C`
through `Q`, record

```text
vc(C;Q,t) =
  (mechanism class,
   g(C),
   degree(C/P1),
   dim |C|,
   incidence codimension of Q,
   equation cost,
   coefficient-field complexity).
```

Then define `VC_A(Q)` to be the Pareto-minimal set of such tuples.  A scalar
minimum is meaningful only after an order or weights have also been frozen in
advance.  Using a Pareto frontier prevents an arbitrary equation-cost scale
from silently trading against genus or post-selection freedom.

The mechanism class is categorical and is ordered only when a declared
experiment requires it:

1. **Rigid visibility.**  `C` is isolated in its divisor class; on the present
   surface the useful examples are rational curves with `dim |C|=0`.
2. **Predeclared-pencil visibility.**  The trace, pencil, and member-selection
   protocol are fixed before the held-out fibre.  A member chosen by asking it
   to pass through an already known `Q` is not in this class.
3. **Post-hoc visibility.**  A moving family or its member is selected using
   the exceptional point.  This is exact geometric information but is not a
   discovery predictor.

Only the first two classes may enter a predictive score.  The third should be
retained as a diagnostic coordinate, since it describes incidence geometry
and can still expose useful equation constructions.

## Exact post-hoc result

The common trace `tau=-P2-P5` gives a one-dimensional genus-one bisection
system

```text
M(t) = M0(t) + lambda*h(t)^2.
```

For every displayed generator, imposing incidence with the known point gives
one rational linear condition on `lambda`.  Thus these witnesses have

```text
(mechanism, genus, degree, dim |C|, incidence codimension, field degree)
  = (post-hoc, 1, 2, 1, 1, 1).
```

Their primitive quartic branch equations and rational selection parameters
are recorded individually.  Across ranks 25, 26, 27, and 28, the maximum
primitive-coefficient bit ranges are respectively `153--437`, `202--358`,
`220--435`, and `289--525`; the `lambda` projective-height ranges are
`63--141`, `79--121`, `83--142`, and `102--160` bits.  These sizes are useful
equation diagnostics, but they do not repair the target leakage.

## Target-directed experiment

The next arithmetic problem should be posed per missing quotient class rather
than by enumerating an ambient coset space:

```text
Input:  Q in E_t0(QQ)/M_t0 and a declared degree d.
Find:   a geometrically integral rational multisection C/QQ
        with C.F=d and with a rational branch over t0 representing Q.
```

For a rational curve on this K3, adjunction adds the exact constraint
`C^2=-2`.  The solver should combine the target quotient congruence, the
degree condition `C.F=d`, effectiveness/nef-chamber constraints, and the
linear incidence condition at `(t0,Q)` before equation construction.  Only
surviving targeted divisor classes should enter a Riemann--Roch kernel and
exact genus/integrality check.  This is the arithmetic analogue of inverse
ADE: start with the desired exceptional class and reconstruct a witness.

The protocol is:

1. Freeze the target-independent atlas, cost order, degree budget, and failure
   semantics.
2. Run the 38 named directions independently, starting with the 34 whose
   rational degree is `UNKNOWN`.
3. Record the first exact rigid witness, or retain `UNKNOWN`; a bounded miss is
   not a non-existence theorem.
4. In a separate held-out lane, freeze trace pencils and their member-selection
   rule before revealing the fibre, then record predeclared-pencil degree.
5. Compare the resulting complexity distributions with non-controls before
   fitting any scalar rank predictor.

This route explicitly excludes construction of all `18,024,296` norm-20
rational-trisection cosets and all `4^17` quadrisection cosets.

## Conjecture and claim boundary

The working conjecture is:

> The high-rank controls have increasing rigid visibility complexity, even
> though their unrestricted post-hoc all-genus visibility is uniformly degree
> two.

The exact `5,3,2,1` rational-bisection span pattern motivates the conjecture;
it does not prove monotonicity of the full tuple.  The present baseline proves
the four individual rigid minima and all 38 post-hoc incidence witnesses.  It
does not determine the 34 missing rational-curve degrees, certify a
predeclared pencil at any control, identify the full groups `E_t(QQ)`, or give
rank upper bounds.
