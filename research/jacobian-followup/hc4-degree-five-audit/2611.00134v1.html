Abstract

The Hessian conjecture HCn\mathrm{HC}_nHCn​ asserts that if f∈C[x1,…,xn]f\in\mathbb{C}[x_1,\dots,x_n]f∈C[x1​,…,xn​] has det⁡Hess⁡(f)∈C×\det\operatorname{Hess}(f)\in\mathbb{C}^\timesdetHess(f)∈C×, then ∇f\nabla f∇f is a polynomial automorphism. It is known for n≤3n\le 3n≤3, false for n≥5n\ge 5n≥5, and open for n=4n=4n=4, where it implies the plane Jacobian conjecture. Z. Ni proved HC4\mathrm{HC}_4HC4​ for potentials of degree at most four. We prove HC4\mathrm{HC}_4HC4​ for every potential of degree five whose top homogeneous form depends on exactly three linear forms. The proof combines a weighted analysis of the Hessian determinant with exact computer-algebra certificates (Groebner bases and Rabinowitsch tests over Q\mathbb{Q}Q); one branch reduces to plane Jacobian pairs of degrees (4,3)(4,3)(4,3), which are excluded by Magnus' theorem. A self-contained verifier is supplied as an ancillary file. The binary and unary cone tops in degree five remain open.
