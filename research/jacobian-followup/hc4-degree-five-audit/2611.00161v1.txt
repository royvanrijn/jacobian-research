                           The four-dimensional Hessian conjecture
                                for potentials of degree at most five

                                                 David Niedbala Giraudin∗

                                                       6 November 2026




                                                             Abstract
            The Hessian conjecture HCn  asserts that iff∈C[x              1, . . . , xn] has det Hess(f)∈C             ×,  then
       ∇fis a polynomial automorphism.  It is known forn≤3, false forn≥5, and open forn= 4,
       where it implies the plane Jacobian conjecture.  Ni proved HC4 for potentials of degree at most
       four,  and  the  author  treated  degree  five  when  the  top  form  depends  on  exactly  three  linear
       forms.  We prove HC4 for every potential of degree at most five.  The remaining top forms are
       a binary form or a fifth power of a linear form.  Both cases are reduced, by weighted layers of
       the Hessian determinant and exact computer algebra overQ, to four elementary mechanisms:
       a Schur complement in one variable (which reduces to HC3), a partial Legendre transform in
       two variables (which reduces to HC2),  a “dead direction” lemma (which reduces to HC2  and
       the plane Jacobian conjecture in degrees at most (3,4), known by Moh), and explicit triangular
       inverses.   Five  self-contained  verifiers  are  supplied  as  ancillary  files.   The  paper  has  not  been
       refereed.
