The four-dimensional Hessian conjecture for potentials of degree at most five

Abstract

The Hessian conjecture HC_n asserts that if f in C[x_1,...,x_n] has det Hess(f) in C^×, then ∇f is a polynomial automorphism. It is known for n ≤ 3, false for n ≥ 5, and open for n = 4, where it implies the plane Jacobian conjecture. Ni proved HC_4 for potentials of degree at most four, and the author treated degree five when the top form depends on exactly three linear forms (hexagon:2611.00134). We prove HC_4 for every potential of degree at most five. The remaining top forms are a binary form or a fifth power of a linear form. Both cases are reduced, by weighted layers of the Hessian determinant and exact computer algebra over Q, to four elementary mechanisms: a Schur complement in one variable (which reduces to HC_3), a partial Legendre transform in two variables (which reduces to HC_2), a "dead direction" lemma (which reduces to HC_2 and the plane Jacobian conjecture in degrees at most (3,4), known by Moh), and explicit triangular inverses. Five self-contained verifiers are supplied as ancillary files. The paper has not been refereed.
