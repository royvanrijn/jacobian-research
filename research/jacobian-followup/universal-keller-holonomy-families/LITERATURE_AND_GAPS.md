# Literature and exact remaining boundaries

Reviewed15 November2026. [Proof](PROOF.md) and [source pins](sources.json) distinguish written deductions from classical inputs. No novelty or publication-priority certificate is claimed.

The characteristic torus cover is classical: Jia--Shibata--Xie--Zhang, [arXiv2104.05339v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.05339v1), Lemma2.20. It permits an automorphism normalizer description and the complete endomorphism classification once the holonomy module is fixed. We retain the exact primary HTML already reviewed in the rigid-base package.

The abelian regular subgroup condition is the published Greither--Pareigis Hopf--Galois framework, [Theorem3.1](https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/7117/1/7117.pdf), already pinned and visually reviewed in the torus-receiver package. Its group-theoretic classification is not new here. Our canonical quartic argument uses the stronger fact that the characteristic translation lattice is preserved by the compatible source/target actions, so the label group normalizes its regular image. Normalization does not imply containment in the label group. The primary paper [On rho-conjugate Hopf--Galois structures](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-edinburgh-mathematical-society/article/on-conjugate-hopfgalois-structures/4C1C05893D362A40B786DEF80B5CAB67), Section2, likewise states the normalized-regular-subgroup framework; its conjugation results are not needed by our proofs.

The positive family extends the [retained quartic construction](../universal-keller-torus-quotient-selfmaps/PROOF.md). The new all-n step uses the same classical norm identity H1(R^1_R Gm)=K*/Norm(R*) of exponent dividing3. Killing the translation torsor by n makes coprimality with3 sufficient. A Kummer lift of a nonnorm in k(s,t) proves failure of specified torsor versality when3 divides n. Volume normalization and prime-square monodromy are explicit. For n>2 this is universality for the specified affine S3 group action, not for all rank-n-squared algebras.

The general [rigid-base reduction](../universal-keller-rigid-base-reduction/PROOF.md) now extends to projection blocks for every transitive group and a primitive embedding-or-vertical alternative. A commutative rational holonomy commutant makes the kernel of the outer-automorphism map metabelian. Repeated standard modules can defeat this easy automorphism test: Z_3 actually contains S4 in its automorphism group. A separate free order-three translation argument nevertheless excludes all finite canonical quartic maps on every Z_m and finite coupled affine cylinder.

No verified recent AI-assisted theorem was needed for these extensions. The useful external approaches are classical characteristic covers, representation commutants, norm cohomology and Kummer exactness. Narrow searches do not establish absence from the literature.

Still unresolved: original whole-affine canonical descent and effective simultaneous landing/gluing; nonfinite attempts when the primitive group embeds into the base automorphisms; other holonomy and free-translation geometries; modular p-power cases; the A1-rich pseudo-plane cubic mixing case; and strong atomicity for smaller marked rank inside larger generic degree. The dimension2+2m family remains outside affine space by its nonconstant invariant unit z^2.
