The subsequent [weighted binary finite-prefix proof](SQUAREFREE_SINGULAR_FINITE_PREFIXES.md)
supplies the sufficient pure gate cuspidal720. The qualitative
arguments below retain their scopes; earlier absence-of-prefix statements
are historical. No numerical arbitrary-cubic bound follows yet.

## Result

**G3CUSPALL20261114.** Every ternary cubic with irreducible cuspidal
homogeneous leading curve satisfies GMC. If the cusp direction is
nonisotropic, some positive pure Gaussian moment is nonzero, for every
choice of lower terms. The same pure exclusion holds when F2(cusp)!=0,
whether the cusp is isotropic or not. The isotropic F2(cusp)=0 alternative
is GMC-safe by the retained complete W-linear theorem; this note does
not assert its entire pure-null classification separately.

Together with the smooth-leading and [complete nodal-leading theorem](NODAL_FORMAL_FRAME.md),
this leaves only nonhomogeneous **reducible-leading** ternary cubics as
possible GMC failures, including repeated-line degenerations. All
homogeneous ternary cubics were already safe. No explicit global
numerical pure-moment prefix is obtained here. Coefficient schemes and
lower-power GVC remain open.

The subsequent [affine triangle theorem](AFFINE_TRIANGLE_EXCLUSION.md)
also closes all independent three-line leading forms. Remaining possible
cubic GMC failures have a line-smooth-conic, concurrent-line or repeated-line
leading curve.

## 1. The complete affine normal form and rank six

The [cuspidal linear normal form](CUSPIDAL_CUBIC_SHARP_THRESHOLD.md) is
F3=T*u^2+v^3, with its unique cusp on the T-axis. Retain the full affine
Gaussian exponent

    Q=(A*u^2+B*v^2+m*T^2+2*Cuv+2*DuT+2*EvT)/2
        +l_u*u+l_v*v+l_T*T+q0, det Hess Q!=0.

First suppose F2(cusp)!=0. Then grad P is complex linearly coercive.
Indeed a sequence with ||x||->infinity and grad P=o(||x||) must approach
the unique leading singular direction, so u,v=o(T). The u equation
divided by T forces u to approach a finite constant. In the T equation,
the nonzero T^2 coefficient in P then leaves a nonzero constant after
division by T, a contradiction. The
[coercive-gradient theorem](COERCIVE_GRADIENT_MOMENTS.md) excludes pure zeros.
This argument uses neither an ordinary node nor a nonisotropic cusp.

If F2(cusp)=0 and the cusp is isotropic, the
[exact Gaussian W-linear frame criterion](RANK_ONE_WT2_CLASSIFICATION.md)
applies: grad F3(cusp)=0, B(cusp,cusp)=0, F2(cusp)=0. The complete
W-linear theorem supplies GMC, with the retained sufficient888 pure gate
and sharp general mixed coefficient3. It does not require this note's
nonisotropic reduction.

It remains to treat a nonisotropic cusp with F2(cusp)=0, so m!=0.
Complete the uT square by a translation of u, remove the u^2 coefficient
by a constant T translation, and remove v^2 by a v translation. Absorb
the resulting linear and constant terms. Exactly,

    P=T*(u^2+gamma*v+h)+v^3+buv+eu+fv+k.             (1)

All six displayed coefficients are arbitrary. The full Q, including
its means and cross terms, changes under these affine transformations
and is retained. No new independent centered Gaussian frame is assumed.

For S_t=tP-Q and each t!=0, its quadratic coefficient at the cusp is
-m/2!=0. The same gradient argument makes S_t linearly coercive. Its
critical scheme has length6: the three projective quadratic gradient
equations have degree8 in total; their unique point at infinity has
length2. In the chart T=1, writing the homogenizing coordinate w, the
u equation solves u=w*a(v,w), while the T equation is w times a unit
after this substitution, because its constant linear coefficient is -m.
Hence w=u=0 locally and the v equation becomes3t*v^2=0. The point is
isolated with length2. A positive-dimensional affine component would
meet this isolated point at infinity, which is impossible. Thus the
three quadrics form a proper complete intersection and leave length6
in the affine chart. The retained tame comparison gives top twisted
de Rham rank6 over C(t).

## 2. A formal seed-valuation criterion

We use the rational seed-submodule implication proved in
[the nodal formal note](NODAL_FORMAL_FRAME.md), Section1: all positive
pure zeros would supply a nonzero horizontal quotient U->C(t), where
U is the differential submodule generated by the volume seed.

Here is a refinement that also handles integer formal exponents.
Suppose a full formal frame, after a finite ramification r=t^(-1/N),
decomposes the module into rank1 exponential factors. Higher-rank
blocks that are formally trivial after an invertible power-series
gauge are allowed: they are direct sums of trivial rank1 factors.
Assume that for every zero-exponential horizontal functional its value
on the original seed belongs to r*C[[r]]. Then no quotient U->C(t)
with seed value1 is possible.

To prove this, extend to C((r)). The displayed module is semisimple,
so the quotient functional on its submodule U extends to the full
module. A nonzero exponential rank1 factor has no horizontal map to
the trivial module over C((r)): the logarithmic derivative of a Laurent
series cannot equal the derivative of its nonzero negative-power
exponent. On the zero-exponential part, all horizontal functionals are
constant linear combinations of the full zero-exponential frame.
Their seed values lie in r*C[[r]] and cannot equal1. This contradiction
proves the criterion. Ramification can turn a fractional power into
an integer; it cannot turn a strictly positive valuation into zero.

We also use an elementary formal perturbation construction. Let F0
be a fixed polynomial phase with finite top twisted quotient H0 of
dimension d, and let F_r-F0 be divisible by r with polynomial
coefficients. For a basis of dual functionals ell_i on H0, put

    J_i(r)[a]=ell_i(exp(F_r-F0)*a).                 (2)

At each r order the exponential has finitely many polynomial terms.
The product rule conjugates the twisted spatial derivatives for F_r
to those for F0, and differentiation in r inserts partial_r F_r.
Thus (2) gives formal horizontal functionals. Evaluating on a fixed
basis of H0 gives an invertible leading matrix. This block is formally
trivial, irrespective of degenerate critical points of F0. If the
original volume is r^p times the transformed volume, p>0, its scalar
seed values all have positive valuation. For separated one-variable
powers, H0 has dimension product(degree-1): leading-degree twisted
reduction gives the monomial basis in each variable, then tensoring
gives the assertion. No convergence of exp(F_r-F0) is needed.

Finite isolated critical points of P of local type
alpha+s*w+y^d have formal characters exp(alpha*t)*t^(-1-j/d),
1<=j<d, by the explicit recurrences in the nodal note. Their scalar
seed powers are strictly positive after removing exp(alpha*t).
They therefore satisfy the criterion even if formal ramification
removes their noninteger residues. We account for every rank below.

## 3. Smooth parabola: gamma!=0

The critical curve u^2+gamma*v+h=0 is smooth. On it,

    R(u)=v(u)^3+b*u*v(u)+eu+f*v(u)+k,
    v(u)=-(u^2+h)/gamma.

R has degree6 with nonzero leading coefficient, so its critical
derivative has degree5. Smooth conic coordinates and a T shear give
the exact local type alpha+s*w+y^(mu+1), with total finite Milnor rank5.
This includes every degeneracy and repeated critical value.

There is one escape. With u=U/t,v=t*V,T=t^2*K, its limiting critical
equations are

    2KU+bV-DK=0, gamma*K+3V^2=0, gamma*V-mK=0.

Their unique escaping solution is

    V0=-gamma^2/(3m), K0=-gamma^3/(3m^2),
    U0=D/2-bm/(2gamma).

The Jacobian determinant is2*K0*gamma^2!=0, so the branch is a Laurent
series in1/t. Its action has nonzero leading term

    gamma^6/(54*m^3)*t^4.                          (3)

About the full critical branch, fluctuations of u,v,T have scales
t^-3/2,t^-1,1. The limiting Gaussian Hessian has determinant
2*K0*gamma^2!=0; cubic corrections have positive inverse powers.
The volume prefactor is t^-5/2. This gives one nonzero-exponential
formal Gaussian character.

For completeness, take a generic linear form L separating the finite
points and nonzero on the T-axis. Hermite interpolation on the finite
Milnor jets has degree<5. Multiply each finite row by
[(L-L_escape(t))/(L_p-L_escape(t))]^8 and use the degree5 escape row
given by the product of all finite jet factors, normalized at the
escape. Since L_escape has order t^2 and its Gaussian fluctuation is
O(1), the finite-to-escape block is O(t^-8), and the reverse block is
O(t^-10). Finite jet and escape Gaussian leading matrices are invertible.
Thus all six characters form a frame. Its zero-exponential scalar
seed values are finite characters with strictly positive powers.
The criterion excludes all pure zeros.

## 4. Two separated lines: gamma=0,h!=0

The critical conic consists of u=+sqrt(-h) and u=-sqrt(-h).
Each line gives a cubic in v with leading coefficient1, hence finite
Milnor rank2; altogether there are four finite A_mu dimensions, mu<=2.
Their scalar seed powers are again strictly positive.

Put u=U/t,T=(h/m)*t+W,v=V. The phase is exactly

    S_t=(h^2/(2m))*t^2+t*R_e(V)+F0(U,V,W)+O(1/t),
    R_e(V)=V^3+(f-Eh/m)*V+k-l_T*h/m,
    F0=(h/m)*U^2-mW^2/2+(bV+e-Dh/m)*U
          -EVW-l_T*W-BV^2/2-l_v*V-q0.              (4)

The transverse U,W Gaussian has determinant -2h!=0. Its formal
integration leaves an analytic unit in V. The cubic R_e supplies two
power characters in total, including when its two critical points
coalesce. Their prefactors are t^(-1-j/d), d=2 or3; their common
nonzero quadratic action h^2*t^2/(2m) precludes a zero-exponential factor.

A finite Hermite basis has degree<4 in a generic L. Multiply it by
[(T-(h/m)*t)/(T_p-(h/m)*t)]^8. At escape its growth is at most t^3 and
the cutoff contributes t^-8. For the two escape characters use their
degree<2 Hermite polynomials in V, multiplied by [T/((h/m)*t)]^8;
these are O(t^-8) at finite points and tend to their required local
jet basis at escape. The leading six-by-six matrix is block invertible.
The seed criterion proves the claim without an escape Morse assumption.

## 5. The double line: gamma=h=0

Now

    P=T*u^2+v^3+buv+eu+fv+k.                       (5)

The following cases exhaust b,e,f, with the complete Q still retained.

### 5a. f!=0

Let v_+,v_- be the two distinct roots of3v^2+f=0, and put
L_i=b*v_i+e, alpha_i=v_i^3+fv_i+k. Each root contributes rank3.

If L_i!=0, put r=t^-1/3,u=rU,T=K/r,v=v_i+rY. After alpha_i*t,
the leading two scales are

    r^-2*(K*U^2+L_i*U-m*K^2/2)
      +r^-1*(3v_i*Y^2+bUY-(Ev_i+l_T)*K).          (6)

There are three roots U^3=-L_i*m/2, K=U^2/m, and
Y=-bU/(6v_i). Their leading critical Jacobian is nonzero: the U,K
Hessian determinant is -6U^2 and the Y Hessian is6v_i. The mixed
fluctuations are of smaller order. Each gives a Morse formal character
with nonzero action coefficient3*L_i*U/4 times t^2/3 and volume
prefactor t^-7/6. Thus none has zero exponential factor.

If L_i=0, use u=t^-1/2 U,v=v_i+t^-1/2 V,T unchanged. The phase is
alpha_i*t+F0+O(t^-1/2), with volume factor t^-1, and

    F0=T*U^2+3v_i*V^2+bUV-m*T^2/2-lambda_i*T-q_i,
    lambda_i=E*v_i+l_T.                           (7)

Polynomial square completion gives a nonzero quartic in U and two
nondegenerate Gaussians:

    (U^2-lambda_i)^2/(2m)-b^2*U^2/(12v_i)
       +3v_i*V'^2-m*T'^2/2-q_i.

The fixed quotient has rank3, with basis1,U,U^2. Construction (2)
supplies all three formal characters, with scalar seed t^-1 times
power series in t^-1/2. They shrink formally even when alpha_i=0.

These two groups together give a full frame. Polynomial cutoffs
chi_i(v)=[(v-v_j)/(v_i-v_j)]^8, j!=i, separate them. For a center group,
use its three amplitudes1,t^1/2*u,(t^1/2*u)^2 times chi_i. At another
escape their worst growth is t^1/3, while the cutoff is O(t^-8/3).
For an escape group, use degree2 interpolation at its three U roots
in t^1/3*u, also times chi_i. Cross blocks tend to zero. Each diagonal
block is the fixed-phase dual basis or a nonzero Gaussian diagonal.
The full rank is3+3=6. All zero-exponential seed values have positive
valuation, so the criterion applies even when both groups are centers.

### 5b. f=0,e!=0,b!=0

Put r=t^-1/6,u=r^2 U,v=rV,T=K/r^2. The leading phase levels are

    r^-4*F0(U,K)+r^-3*F1(U,V),
    F0=K*U^2+eU-m*K^2/2, F1=V^3+bUV.

There are three U roots U^3=-em/2, with K=U^2/m, and two V roots
3V^2+bU=0 at each. The U,K Hessian is nonzero and6V!=0. Implicit
critical branches exist after this triangular leading system.
Gaussian fluctuation scales in U,K,V are r^2,r^2,r^3/2; their mixed
terms are smaller. A further square-root cover makes all series integral.
All six actions have nonzero coefficient3eU/4 at order t^2/3; the next
V level does not cancel it. The volume factor is t^-13/12. Interpolation
on the six distinct limiting (U,V,K) points proves a full Gaussian frame.
There is no zero-exponential part and no trivial seed quotient.

### 5c. f=b=0,e!=0

Put r=t^-1/3,u=rU,v=rV,T=K/r. The U,K leading phase is r^-2 F0 from
5b, with its three nondegenerate roots. Include -r*l_T*K in its scaled
critical equation and center U,K at the resulting analytic branches.
Their fluctuations have scale r. After removing the positive stationary
action, the limiting phase is a nondegenerate two-variable Gaussian
plus the fixed cubic V^3-E*K_i*V, up to a scalar constant.
It has rank2 for every E, including E=0. All corrections are divisible
by r. The volume factor is r^3=t^-1. Construction (2) gives two formal
functionals per U,K group. Ordinary interpolation in the three distinct
limiting U values, combined with1,V, gives a full frame of rank6.
Every group has nonzero action coefficient3eU/4 at order t^2/3, so no
zero-exponential factor occurs.

### 5d. f=e=0,b!=0

Put r=t^-1/5,u=r^2 U,v=rV,T=K/r. After k*t, the phase is
r^-2*(F0+O(r)), where

    F0=K*U^2+V^3+bUV-m*K^2/2.                     (8)

Its six critical points are the origin and the five points

    V^5=m*b^4/54, U=-3V^2/b, K=9V^4/(m*b^2).

At the origin the Hessian determinant is m*b^2; at every other point
it is -5m*b^2. Their actions are respectively0 and -5V^3/4. The latter
coefficient retains the bUV quadratic term; treating all of F0's
non-Gaussian terms as homogeneous cubic would give an incorrect action.
All six points are Morse, and interpolation in their distinct limiting
coordinates gives a full frame. Every volume prefactor is r^5=t^-1.
At the origin the O(r) phase term is -l_T*K and vanishes at0, so its
critical action after k*t is O(1), not a hidden positive power.
The other five actions are nonzero. If k=0 the origin's scalar seed
value has positive valuation; otherwise it too has nonzero exponential
factor. The criterion excludes all pure zeros.

### 5e. f=e=b=0

Use u=t^-1/2 U,v=t^-1/3 V,T unchanged, with common parameter r=t^-1/6.
After k*t the phase is F0+O(r), with

    F0=T*U^2+V^3-m*T^2/2-l_T*T-q0.

Complete T to obtain (U^2-l_T)^2/(2m)+V^3-m*T'^2/2-q0.
The fixed quotient has rank3*2=6 with basis U^i*V^j,0<=i<=2,0<=j<=1.
The volume factor is t^-5/6=r^5. Construction (2) gives the full rank6
frame with scalar seed exp(k*t)*r^5*C[[r]]. For k!=0 no zero-exponential
factor occurs; for k=0 every scalar seed value has positive valuation.
This finishes all cases of (5), and hence the nonisotropic pure exclusion.
