Finite Gaussian Moments for Quadratics: Classification, Optimal Mixed Cutoffs, and Logarithmic Gap Varieties

Authors/Creators

  • Liu, Zongmin1
  • 1. Stanford University

Description

We prove a finite and effective Gaussian Moments Theorem for complex quadratic polynomials in every covariance rank N. The first 2N+1 vanishing moments force complete moment vanishing, which is classified by A^N=0 and l^T A^j l=0 for 0<=j<N. For every polynomial Q, E(QP^m)=0 when m>floor(N/2) deg Q, and the coefficient floor(N/2) is optimal. The least pure-moment threshold satisfies 2N<=tau_N^(2)<=2N+1 in every rank, and tau_N^(2)=2N+1 for ranks one through seven. The sharp low-rank results are supported by replayable exact finite-algebra and rational-interval certificates. The paper does not claim the unrestricted Gaussian Moments Conjecture or the Jacobian Conjecture.
