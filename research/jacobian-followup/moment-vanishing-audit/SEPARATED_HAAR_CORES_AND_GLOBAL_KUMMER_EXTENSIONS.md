## 1. The separated quotient, without choosing a period

Use the full source and normalized Haar state from the intrinsic theorem:

    H_P = Omega^3(X)_R[t]/(d+t*dP wedge)Omega^2(X)_R[t],
    lambda_P([Q Omega]) = h(Q exp(tP)),
    H_P/tH_P = R[Omega],
    partial_t[Q Omega] = [P Q Omega].                       (1)

Here X is SL2/2O or SL2/2I, R is ANY commutative C-algebra, and P and Q
are original regular functions with coefficients in R. In particular no
reducedness, domain or finite-generation-over-R[t] assumption is made.
Define

    K_P = intersection_(m>=0) t^m H_P.                    (2)

**Theorem.** The actual formal-state kernel is exactly K_P. It is the
largest t-surjective D_R-submodule. In these exceptional sources t is
injective on H_P, so K_P is also the largest t-invertible submodule.
The truncated state maps and completion are

    H_P/t^m H_P = R[t]/t^m,      H_P^ = R[[t]].           (3)

The equalities in (3) are module isomorphisms, with the first induced by
lambda_P. They do not make t^m H_P a D_R-submodule.

Proof. If v lies in (2), its state is divisible by every power of t and
therefore vanishes. Conversely suppose lambda_P(v)=0. Reduction modulo t
writes v=a[Omega] mod tH_P; the constant term of its state is a, hence a=0.
Write v=tw. Since t is injective on R[[t]], lambda_P(w)=0. Repeating proves
v belongs to every t^m H_P. The same argument gives K_P=tK_P. Every
t-surjective submodule maps to zero in formal series, so it is contained
in K_P. Differentiation lowers t-divisibility by at most one; thus K_P
is a D_R-submodule.

For completeness, H_P/tH_P is generated by Omega, and successive lifting
expresses every class mod t^m as a polynomial times Omega. Its state
lambda_P(Omega) has constant term 1 and is a unit mod t^m. If that
polynomial times Omega is in t^m H_P, applying the state makes the
polynomial divisible by t^m. This proves (3) over R, including uniqueness.

Finally the compact deformation retract of X is SU2/Gamma, a closed
orientable three-manifold with finite fundamental group. Its H^1 over C
is zero; Poincare duality gives H^2=0. Algebraic de Rham comparison and
tensoring over C give H^2_dR(X)_R=0. Apply the cohomology exact sequence
to multiplication by t on the full twisted de Rham complex. The term
immediately before the t-kernel on H_P is H^2_dR(X)_R. Consequently
multiplication by t on H_P is injective. This proves the last assertion.

This does NOT justify applying the finite-module Krull intersection
theorem: H_P need not be finite over R[t]. K_P is an intrinsic description,
not a newly implemented procedure to compute the infinite intersection.

## 2. Exact finite quotient descent

Lift P to the cover Y=SL2, and take its full source H_Y. Finite etale
descent of relative differential forms and exactness of Reynolds averaging
give

    H_X = (H_Y)^Gamma,
    t^m(H_Y^Gamma) = (t^m H_Y)^Gamma,
    K_X = (K_Y)^Gamma.                                  (4)

For the middle equality average a preimage under t^m; for the last equality
intersect inside H_Y. These arguments work over every R because |Gamma|
is a unit. The lifted invariant volume generates H_Y/tH_Y=R, and Gamma
acts trivially on this quotient. Every nontrivial Gamma-isotypic summand N
therefore has N/tN=0. Thus N=tN and N is contained in K_Y.

In particular the entire separated quotient H_Y/K_Y is Gamma-trivial,
and (4) identifies it with H_X/K_X. This is an exact assertion about the
actual marked state, not about all source cohomology. Finite group
characters alone cannot remove its remaining obstruction: that
obstruction already lies in the trivial representation.

## 3. A single pure relation and the full remaining implication

Call P pure when h(P^m)=0 for every m>=1. The intrinsic Haar state gives
the exact equivalences

    P is pure  <=> lambda_P(Omega)=1
               <=> [P Omega] belongs to K_P.             (5)

The last equivalence uses differentiation and the constant term 1. For
pure P, put O_R=R[t] and define the actual obstruction

    C_P = H_P/(O_R[Omega]+K_P).                          (6)

Then ALL original fixed markers are eventually zero if and only if

    H_P = O_R[Omega]+K_P  <=> C_P=0.                     (7)

Indeed the image V_P=H_P/K_P is the actual state image, contains O_R
because the unit state is 1, and is contained in R[[t]]. Every marked
series is polynomial precisely when V_P=O_R. The source is spanned
over R[t] by the original amplitudes Q Omega, so no markers are lost.
The exact polynomial-state ideal from the intrinsic theorem becomes

    J_P = Ann_R(C_P).                                   (8)

To check (8), a lies in J_P precisely when a times every original marked
series is polynomial, equivalently aV_P is contained in O_R. This is
exactly annihilation of V_P/O_R. In particular safety requires 1 in J_P;
a nonzero ideal alone is insufficient over coefficient rings.

Consequently, over C, the COMPLETE MZ conjecture on either exceptional
quotient is equivalent to the following implication for EVERY phase:

    [P Omega] in K_P  => H_P = C[t][Omega]+K_P.           (9)

Equation (9) is the remaining all-phase/all-original-marker implication,
not a proof of it. The same criterion is valid over arbitrary R; a field
theorem must not silently be promoted to this stronger ring statement.
Known sufficient semisimplicity and acyclic-fibre criteria remain useful,
but their hypotheses are not established for all pure exceptional phases.

## 4. Global rank-one Ext, including finite monodromy

Here is a complete elementary countercontrol to using local monodromy at
t=0 to settle the remaining extension. It is an ABSTRACT Weyl-module
model; no realization by an exceptional phase or original volume is
claimed.

Let alpha>0 be nonintegral, c in C, D=C[t]<partial>, O=C[t], and

    L = t partial - c t + alpha,
    T_(alpha,c) = D/DL = C[t,t^-1]v,
    partial v = (c-alpha/t)v.                            (10)

The presentation in (10) has no extra punctual term. For example replace
partial by partial-c and use t partial v=-alpha v: normal ordering gives
the Laurent basis t^j v, j in Z, since alpha+j never vanishes for integral
j. This also proves simplicity. More explicitly, from any nonzero finite
Laurent combination, polynomials in t(partial-c), whose eigenvalues
j-alpha are distinct, isolate a monomial; t and partial-c then generate
all monomials. The local horizontal factor of the displayed scalar
connection is e^(ct)t^(-alpha), with monodromy exp(-2 pi i alpha), never 1.
For rational nonintegral alpha this monodromy has finite order.

**Global Ext theorem.**

    Ext^1_D(T_(alpha,c),O) = 0       if c=0,
                            C       if c!=0.            (11)

Proof. The free resolution of D/DL uses right multiplication by L on D;
it is injective because the Weyl algebra is a domain. Applying Hom_D(-,O)
identifies Ext^1 with O/L(O). On the monomial basis,

    L(t^n)=(n+alpha)t^n-c t^(n+1).                     (12)

If c=0 this is an invertible diagonal operator. If c!=0, (12) reduces
every polynomial modulo L(O) to a constant. This constant class is
nonzero: the functional ell(t^n)=(alpha)_n/c^n annihilates L(O) and
ell(1)=1. This proves (11) in every degree, without a truncation bound.

Consider the extension E with generators u,v and relations

    partial u=0,       L v=alpha u.                     (13)

It represents alpha[1] in (11). Its normalized formal state u->1 is unique
and sends v to

    f_(alpha,c)(t) = sum_(m>=0) (ct)^m/(alpha+1)_m,
    t f' = (ct-alpha)f+alpha.                           (14)

For c!=0 this is an entire nonpolynomial function. An exact compact
integral, verified by termwise integration, is

    f_(alpha,c)(t) = alpha integral_0^1
                         (1-x)^(alpha-1) exp(ctx) dx.   (15)

The weight is integrable and has total mass 1, so (15) has exponential
type at most |c|. Its moments are m!/(alpha+1)_m, proving (14).
In standard notation this is M(1,alpha+1,ct); see
[DLMF 13.2.2](https://dlmf.nist.gov/13.2.E2).
[DLMF 13.4.1](https://dlmf.nist.gov/13.4.E1) uses Olver's normalized
bold M, so its formula must be multiplied by Gamma(alpha+1) here.
The integral and Ext calculation above are also proved directly.

Let M=D*1+D*f inside the entire functions. If c!=0, f is not polynomial,
so the induced quotient T->M/O is nonzero, hence an isomorphism by
simplicity. The map E->M is injective on O and on that quotient, hence
is an isomorphism. Thus

    0 -> O -> M -> T_(alpha,c) -> 0                      (16)

is a NONSPLIT extension with t invertible on its quotient, although the
quotient has no eigenvalue 1 in its local monodromy. Differentiating (14)
gives, for j>=1,

    (alpha+j) f^(j) = j c f^(j-1) mod tM.

Together with f=1 mod tM this proves M/tM=C; the constant 1 is not in
tM because all functions in M are entire. Therefore the model has the
same normalized formal fibre as the actual marked image. It still need
not arise from the exceptional geometry.

If c=0, (13) instead splits by v-u, its normalized formal map kills T,
and its actual image is O. Thus the kernel of the normalized formal
state changes from T at c=0 to zero at c!=0, even though the local
monodromy at zero is unchanged. The global exponential parameter and
the lattice at infinity matter.

For a coefficient-ring version of (14), let c belong to any C-algebra R.
Its exact polynomial-state ideal is

    {a in R: a f_(alpha,c) in R[t]}
       = union_(N>=0) Ann_R(c^N).                       (17)

Indeed every coefficient in (14) is c^m times a nonzero complex unit.
Polynomiality is therefore exactly eventual annihilation of these powers.
The ideal contains 1 precisely when c is nilpotent. This retains ordinary
nilpotent information and makes no base-change theorem for general Haar
sources.

## 5. Consequence for the research route

Local monodromy without eigenvalue 1, even finite-order monodromy plus
an entire compact integral, does not exclude a global unit extension.
Nor does quotient descent remove the obstruction by nontrivial finite
characters. The remaining task is (9) for the ACTUAL exceptional source,
or an actual exceptional phase and original multiplier violating it.
We retain every existing geometric restriction and all original markers.
No additional sector calculation, degree sweep, review gate or claim of
full conjecture closure follows from these results.
