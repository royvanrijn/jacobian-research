Sharp finite Gaussian moments for quadratic polynomials

Authors/Creators

  • Liu, Zongmin1
  • 1. Stanford University

Description

We determine the exact finite-moment threshold for centered complex quadratic polynomials under every Gaussian law. In covariance rank N, the first 2N+1 vanishing moments force all positive moments to vanish, and 2N+1 is optimal for every N. Complete vanishing is classified by A^N=0 and l^T A^j l=0 for 0<=j<N. For every polynomial Q, E(QP^m)=0 when m>floor(N/2) deg Q, with the constant floor(N/2) optimal. Sharp examples arise from weighted-projective logarithmic-gap intersections and symmetric rational realizations; the associated proper intersection number is Catalan.
