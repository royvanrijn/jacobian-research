## 1. The exact joint period image is an explicit rank-two algebra

Use the known normalized weighted cotangent map and the two phases S=Y1,T=-Y2 of the [joint-algebra note](JOINT_SAFE_ALGEBRAS_AND_NONCLOSURE.md). The coefficient field K may be Q; extend to Q(i) for ordinary independent Gaussian coordinates. Let O=K[u,v] and D=O<partial_u,partial_v>. For every polynomial source marker Q(x,y,z,p1,p2,p3), set

    M_Q(u,v)=L[Q exp(uS+vT)].

Let M be the actual D-submodule of K[[u,v]] generated by these periods; equivalently it is their O-span. Derivatives correspond to source multiplication by S,T. This is the ACTUAL formal-period image, not an arbitrary scalar annihilating presentation.

Put delta=1-2uv and choose U=delta^(-1/2) with U(0,0)=1. The previously proved inverse slice is

    X=(U-1)/v, Y=v, Z=-v^2*(1+3U)*delta.                   (1)

These are regular formal series; U-1 is divisible by uv. The actual image is exactly

    M=R=O[X] subset K[[u,v]],
    v*delta*X^2+2*delta*X-2u=0.                            (2)

In R one has U=1+vX and delta*U^2=1, so R automatically contains delta^(-1)=U^2. Conversely Z is a polynomial in u,v,X. Position markers and O-coefficients therefore supply all of R. Every momentum-source jet from the completed Gaussian-Lagrange formula is polynomial in u,v and (X,Y,Z): the implicit inverse matrix has determinant1, so its adjugates and every finite jet require no extra denominator. Thus every full source marker maps into R, proving (2) in both directions.

The displayed quadratic relation is irreducible over K(u,v): its discriminant is4delta, which is not a square in that rational function field. Hence the ACTUAL joint generic rank is2. This is not a source-rank upper bound. It does not assert a rank for a single nonlinear time phase H(S,T).

Derivatives in this algebra are explicit:

    partial_u U=v*U^3, partial_v U=u*U^3,
    partial_u X=U^3,
    partial_v X=X^2*(U+2)/2.                               (3)

They show D-stability directly. The source marker x maps to X, and (1+xy)^2 maps to U^2=delta^(-1).

## 2. Two D-generators and the exact boundary defect

Write B=O[delta^(-1)] and C=B+B*U, with U^2=delta^(-1). This is a finite free rank-two algebra over B, with basis1,U. Then

    R=D*X+D*delta^(-1).                                    (4)

Here is a proof that preserves the nonproper boundary. First

    (partial_u-2v^2*partial_v-3v)X=1,

so D*X contains1 and U=1+vX. For odd positive l,

    U^(l+2)=U^l+(2u/l)*partial_u(U^l).

Thus D*X contains B*U. For k>=1,

    delta^(-(k+1))=delta^(-k)+(u/k)*partial_u(delta^(-k)),

so D*delta^(-1) contains B. Hence the right side of (4) contains C.

To see the rest, use the OTHER algebraic branch at v=0. Since X_+=(U-1)/v is regular there, its conjugate is

    X_-=-2/v-X_+.

Expand an element of R on this minus branch and retain only its finite negative powers of v. The coefficients lie in K[u]. This defines a D-linear principal-part map

    pi:R -> H_v=O[v^(-1)]/O,
    pi(X)=-2/v mod O,
    pi(partial_v^(k-1)X)=-2*(-1)^(k-1)*(k-1)!/v^k.          (5)

It is onto. Its kernel is C. Indeed R is contained in C[v^(-1)] and is regular on the plus branch at v=0. If its minus principal part is zero too, write it as (a+bU)/v^k with a,b in B. The two regular branches imply a+bU_+ and a-bU_+ vanish modulo v^k. Since2 and U_+ are units, a,b are divisible by v^k in B, placing the element in C. This proves

    0 -> C -> R -> H_v -> 0.                               (6)

The principal parts in (5) are all supplied by D*X. Subtract them from any element of R to land in C; this proves (4), including all higher source markers. The sequence is NOT split as an O-module or D-module: H_v is locally v-torsion, whereas R, embedded in a field, has no v-torsion. A section would be zero.

All joint pure moments vanish, so the pure seed period is1 and D*1=O. The actual source-marker obstruction N=R/O has generic rank1 and the explicit filtration

    0 -> C/O -> N -> H_v -> 0,
    C/O=(B/O) direct_sum (B*U).                             (7)

The rational boundary term B/O is O[delta^(-1)]/O. The rank-one square-root connection B*U has

    partial_u U=(v/delta)U, partial_v U=(u/delta)U,

or dU/U=-(1/2)dlog(delta); its square-root continuation has sign monodromy. H_v records the branch escaping through v=0. The latter divisor and delta=0 are disjoint. These are actual boundary terms, not removable apparent poles of a nonminimal annihilator.

This closes the joint image/rank/two-generator/boundary question for the explicit pair. It does not decide individual H(S,T) by checking only two scalar moments. Polynomial differential actions on the generators must still be accounted for; a generic-rank calculation alone cannot discard (7). The individually safe phases S,T already show why joint rank2 does not make every nonlinear member unsafe.

## 3. A general factorial-witness recomposition theorem

Let P be pure-null over C and suppose one FIXED polynomial marker Q satisfies

    L[Q P^k]=c^k*(kappa*k)! for every k>=1,                 (8)

where c!=0 and kappa is a positive integer. Let

    h(z)=sum_(j=r)^d a_j z^j, 1<=r<=d, a_r*a_d!=0.

Then h(P) is pure-null and unsafe with the SAME marker Q. In fact its mixed moments are nonzero for every sufficiently large index. Define

    A_m=L[Q h(P)^m]/(a_d^m*c^(dm)*(kappa*d*m)!).

Then

    A_m -> exp(a_(d-1)/(c*d*a_d)) if kappa=1,
    A_m -> 1                           if kappa>=2.        (9)

Take a_0=0, so the degree-one case has limit1. These limits concern finite moment sums, not convergence of an exponential Wick integral.

Proof. Pure-nullness follows because h(0)=0. Let b_j=a_(d-j)/a_d, 1<=j<=d-r, and set absent b_j to0. In expanding h(z)^m, let n_j count the factors of deficit j from the leading degree. Put n=sum n_j and ell=sum j*n_j. The normalized contribution is

    (m)_n/prod(n_j!) * prod(b_j^n_j) * c^(-ell)
      * (kappa*(dm-ell))!/(kappa*dm)!.                     (10)

All indices dm-ell are at least rm. The factorial ratio of one degree decrement is at most k^(-kappa) at index k, since every factor in (kappa*k)!/(kappa*(k-1))! is at least k. Thus the absolute ratio in (10) is at most (rm)^(-kappa*ell). Summing absolute values of every nonleading term is bounded by

    (1+sum_j |b_j|*(|c|^(-1)*(rm)^(-kappa))^j)^m-1.

For kappa>=2 this is O(m^(1-kappa)), proving the second limit.

For kappa=1, use (m)_n<=m^n and dominate each term by

    prod_j (|b_j|/(|c|^j*r^j*m^(j-1)))^n_j / n_j!.

The total of terms involving any j>=2 tends to0, bounded by an exponential series with those coefficients. The remaining terms have only n_1=n. For each fixed n they tend to (b_1/(c*d))^n/n!, and are dominated by (|b_1|/(|c|*r))^n/n!, a summable sequence. Their sum tends to the first exponential in (9). It is nonzero. This proves eventual nonvanishing without positivity of h's coefficients; complex cancellations are controlled rather than excluded by assertion.

The failure conclusion extends to every characteristic-zero coefficient FIELD admitting (8): its finitely many h,c coefficients generate a finitely generated Q-field, which embeds in C, and all moment equalities reflect along that injection. No ordering or absolute value on the original field is assumed.

Two retained applications are immediate.

* Long's ternary quartic P3, with Q=Z, has (8) for kappa=1,c=1. Therefore EVERY nonzero h(P3), h(0)=0, is a pure-null failure with the same fixed LINEAR marker Z. This is an all-degree composition family, not a new smallest endpoint. The retained primary [Long source](sources/long_gaussian_de4082c.tex), Theorem for P3 and its full coefficient proof, gives the input m! identity; existing claim G3X retains its assurance.
* For R0=ST above, the preceding joint proof gives (8) with Q=xy,kappa=2,c=1/2. Hence EVERY nonzero h(R0), h(0)=0, is unsafe with the same fixed QUADRATIC marker xy. This closes the entire neutral one-generator polynomial class. It does not classify general mixed-charge H(S,T).

The argument uses an exact factorial witness, not only unspecified fast growth. It makes no claim that polynomial recomposition preserves every possible GMC failure with an arbitrary moment sequence.

## 4. Arbitrary-ring composition families and their exact normalization ideal

Fix a characteristic-zero field K, a pure-null base P and witness (8), and a maximum recomposition degree d>=1. For any K-algebra A let

    h(z)=c_1 z+...+c_d z^d, I=(c_1,...,c_d) in A.

Then

    h(P) safe over A iff I is nilpotent.                    (11)

If safe, reduce modulo each prime and pass to the fraction field. The field theorem forbids every nonzero recomposition, so every c_j belongs to every prime of A. I is finitely generated; hence it is nilpotent. Conversely I^L=0 implies h(P)^L=0, proving safety directly. All phases in this coefficient family are pure-null before any quotient. No finite-type hypothesis on A, global nilradical bound, holonomic uniformity or symbolic-power containment is required.

In the universal coefficient ring K[c_1,...,c_d], the reduced safe scheme is exactly the origin and the all-ring safe functor is its formal completion, with discrete target algebras. If I^L=0, a sufficient mixed slope is L-1. For the neutral family h(ST), every linear marker has zero moment at every index by weight, so (L-1)/2 is sufficient. These general sufficient bounds are not asserted sharp for all coefficient choices; the earlier monomial scaling cases retain their exact sharpness and individual cutoff ideals.

There is also an exact coefficient-ring Hom computation. Let J_h be the normalization ideal from the [exact Gaussian polynomial-Hom theorem](GAUSSIAN_FORMAL_COMPLETION_AND_POLYNOMIAL_HOM.md):

    J_h={a in A: a*L[Q exp(t*h(P))] is polynomial in t
                    for every source polynomial Q}.

It is the image of polynomial D_t-Hom evaluated on the pure seed at0. Then

    J_h=Gamma_I(A)=union_(N>=0) Ann_A(I^N).                 (12)

Indeed a times all moment tails vanishes exactly when h(P) is safe over A/Ann_A(a). By (11), this is equivalent to I^N being contained in Ann_A(a) for some N. This proves both inclusions, retaining nilpotents and zero divisors. Safety requires1 in J_h, not merely J_h!=0.

In particular over the UNIVERSAL pure coefficient family A=K[c_1,...,c_d], this relative polynomial Hom is zero. At the origin fiber it is one-dimensional, since the phase is0. This is a concrete failure of naive polynomial-Hom base change INSIDE AN ENTIRELY PURE-NULL FAMILY, after the constant coefficient has already been removed. It is not an obstruction to valid fiberwise algorithms or to the known closed safe locus. For A=K[a,b]/(ab), h(z)=az, one has J_h=(b), nonzero although the normalized phase is unsafe. This extends the earlier linear-Gaussian control to a pure-null counterexample family.
