## Exact primary statement and import trust boundary

[Moh's primary author account](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~ttm/jacobian)
states, in its introduction, that Moh and Sathaye computed the three-variable
case with all three polynomial degrees at most3 and obtained an affirmative
result. This is the same primary account already used to import the plane
100 bound in KPAIR1, but the cubic assertion is a separate theorem import.
The whole page is retained as [moh-primary-account.html](moh-primary-account.html),
SHA25629591bbc082ec140b2dd85b9a34c891af021f336a9ac4cb7d84fc81690c01ed3.
Its dated discussion is used only for these explicit mathematical statements,
not as a current account of the general conjecture.

[Vistoli's publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022404998000401)
and [institutional author record](https://ricerca.sns.it/handle/11384/2572)
confirm the subsequent conceptual proof in dimension3 and degree3:
Angelo Vistoli, The Jacobian conjecture in dimension3 and degree3,
Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra142(1999),79-89,
DOI10.1016/S0022-4049(98)00040-1. Both abstract records have been inspected
through the web tool. The full Vistoli article has not been obtained or read;
no theorem number, full-text hash, proof reproduction or formal verification
is asserted. The earlier source gate was missing a precise scope statement.
Moh's directly retained primary statement now supplies it, corroborated by
the published conceptual-proof abstract. The import accepts this established
classical theorem with that explicit external trust boundary. It does not
silently equate an abstract read with a proof audit.

The result is used for complex maps. Any hypothetical characteristic-zero
collision and its coefficients embed into C through a finitely generated
field, so the collision exclusion transfers. The polynomial inverse theorem
itself is imported overC; no additional coefficient-descent proof is claimed.

## Exact consequences

For GLDC1's affine-invariant filtered output flag, maximum entry at most3
means a target basis has all component degrees at most3. A change of target
basis preserves the Keller condition and any collision. Thus every one of
the10 profiles of maximum entry<=3 is impossible. GLDC5 already closes nine
of these because their smallest entry is at most2; the only additional
profile is(3,3,3). The current dense partition is therefore48 excluded and
26 remaining. The complete lists are retained in classical-validation.json.
No nonlinear support bound is assumed in this whole-profile consequence.

The known three-variable degree7 counterexample gives upper bound7, while
the classical theorem gives lower bound4. It does not decide degrees4,5,6.
The newly catalogued Wang-Yan quartic theorem is still unimported pending its
own proof audit. It would now add exactly the three remaining degree4 flags
(4,3,3),(4,4,3),(4,4,4), leaving23 and a three-variable degree interval[5,7].
That conditional statement must not be promoted as a completed quartic audit.

For ordinary cubic counterexamples, dimension1 is affine, dimension2 is
excluded by the existing Moh plane100 theorem, and dimension3 by this
classical import. KCUB3 supplies a verified ordinary cubic in dimension12.
Consequently the available dimension interval is[4,12]. This does not assert
that dimension4 is achievable, exclude intermediate dimensions, or prove
the lower bound strongest among all possible external special-form theorems.
Cubic-linear, cubic-homogeneous, gradient and general cubic forms are distinct
questions; no upper bound in one form is substituted for another.
