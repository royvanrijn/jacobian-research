# A rational natural PSL2(11) cover passing the affine inertia sieve

This audits the existing van Hoeij--Vidunas map F11, rather than claiming
a new Belyi map. The authors' [formula table](https://www.math.fsu.edu/~hoeij/Heun/BelyiMaps),
[classification data](https://www.math.fsu.edu/~hoeij/Heun/Sorted_by_Name),
and [dessin permutations](https://www.math.fsu.edu/~hoeij/Heun/Dessins_and_ModuliFields)
are retained with hashes in [sources/manifest.json](sources/manifest.json).
Their F11 formula-to-dessin identification is an external input. The
checker independently replays its polynomial identities and conjugates
both listed dessin actions to the natural projective matrix action.

## Exact formula and branch placement

Let a satisfy a^4-a^3+a^2-a+1=0 and put K=Q(a)=Q(zeta_10).
The four conjugate choices give exact conjugate formulas. Set

\[
\begin{aligned}
 c_0&=\frac{64}{605}(-21115+22305a-51661a^2+15789a^3),\\
 A&=3025x^2+(157+2276a-2799a^2-4828a^3)x\\
  &\hspace{1cm}-1362-3506a+1049a^2-2632a^3,\\
 B&=x^2-(a^2+1)x+a+a^3,\\
 C&=44x^2+(22a-44a^2-88a^3)x-42-54a+9a^2-16a^3,\\
 E&=2x-2a^3-a,\qquad N=c_0AB^5,\quad D=C^5E .
\end{aligned}
\]

The source formula is phi=N/D, of degree twelve. Its branch fibers at
0,1,infinity have passports 5^2 1^2, 2^6, 5^2 1^2. Replace the target by

\[
 \beta=\frac{\phi}{\phi-1}=\frac{N}{N-D}.
\]

Thus the two finite branch fibers have 5^2 1^2 and the fiber at infinity
has 2^6, exactly the genus-zero possibility in KMFI3. This Mobius
transformation keeps K(phi)=K(beta); no change of cover or group is hidden.

The [coefficient module](model.py) transcribes these formulas. The
[generated exact model](model.json) gives all expanded coefficients in
the power basis 1,a,a^2,a^3, including a monic sextic R and a constant k
such that

\[
 Q=N-D=kR^2,\qquad
 N'Q-NQ'=\lambda B^4C^4R,\qquad
 \beta'=\frac{\lambda B^4C^4}{k^2R^3}.
 \tag{1}
\]

All five boundary polynomials A,B,C,E,R are squarefree and pairwise
coprime. N and D are coprime; deg N=deg Q=12 and deg D=11.
The two finite order-five fibers have their asserted simple branches,
and x=infinity is the second simple point over beta=1. Formula (1)
accounts for all ramification. Its indices are 8+8+6=22, giving genus zero.

## Explicit group identification

For each of the two primary-source dessins, verify.py constructs a
permutation relabeling to matrices in SL2(F_11)/{+/-I}, not just a match
of cycle types or orders. The group generated has 660 elements and acts
naturally on twelve projective points. The relabelings and matrix
generators are stored in [receipt.json](receipt.json).

The separate triple in THEORY.md, of orders (5,5,2), generates the same
natural group. For a fixed order-five element, precisely ten partners of
order five with involutory product generate PSL2(11); ten others generate
A5. The two cases lie in different order-five class pairings. This
control prevents inferring the group from the passport alone.

These calculations identify the monodromy of the *listed dessins*. The
assertion that the displayed rational formula belongs to those dessins
uses van Hoeij--Vidunas's published classification and data. No numerical
analytic-continuation certificate is claimed.

## The arithmetic group needs a constant extension

A source field of definition is not the constant field of the Galois
closure. This example detects the distinction exactly.

Let c be the coefficient-field element stored as discriminant_constant
in model.json. Then

\[
 \operatorname{Disc}_x(N-tQ)=c\,t^8(1-t)^8,
 \tag{2}
\]

and c is **not** a square in K. This is proved by exact factorization
of Z^2-c over K: the factor remains irreducible of degree two.

For completeness, (2) has a resultant proof, rather than being inferred
from finitely many samples. Since Q'=dQ/dx and
Q(N'-tQ')-Q'(N-tQ)=lambda B^4C^4R, evaluating at the twelve roots and
tracking leading coefficients gives, since (-1)^(12*11/2)=1,

\[
 \operatorname{Disc}(N-tQ)
 =\frac{\lambda^{12}\operatorname{Res}(N-tQ,B)^4
  \operatorname{Res}(N-tQ,C)^4\operatorname{Res}(N-tQ,R)}
 {\operatorname{Res}(N-tQ,Q)}.
\]

Even products of the relevant degrees make every exchange of resultant
arguments sign-free. Reducing modulo B, C, R and Q respectively gives

\[
 c=\frac{\lambda^{12}\operatorname{Res}(B,Q)^4
                   \operatorname{Res}(C,N)^4\operatorname{Res}(R,N)}
                  {\operatorname{Res}(Q,N)}.
\]

This is the exact constant used by the checker. Direct discriminants at
t=2,3,-1 are additional regression controls.

The arithmetic group normalizes geometric PSL2(11) in S_12. Its
centralizer is trivial by double transitivity, and
Aut(PSL2(11))=PGL2(11) has order 1320. The latter standard finite-group
input is recorded in the [GAP Character Table Library](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Thomas.Breuer/ctbllib/ctbltoc/data/L2%2811%29.2.html),
which identifies the automorphism group and its natural degree-twelve
action. PGL2(11) contains odd permutations whereas PSL2(11) is even.
Nonsquareness in (2) therefore forces

\[
 G_{\mathrm{geom}}=\operatorname{PSL}_2(11),\qquad
 G_{\mathrm{arith},K}=\operatorname{PGL}_2(11).
\]

The closure's constant extension is exactly K'=K(sqrt(c)).
Over K' the arithmetic and geometric groups both equal PSL2(11).
This proves an explicit quadratic constant-field prescription, without
asserting that the large coefficient c is in a simpler chosen square class.
The group table is an external upper-bound input; the checker constructs
the 1320-element PGL permutation group and verifies its odd outer
generator and normalization as independent controls.

There is also a separate obstruction over the moduli field Q(sqrt(5)).
The authors give the conic
(2+sqrt(5))X^2+sqrt(5)Y^2+Z^2=0 and list its real/5-adic obstruction.
At the real embedding with sqrt(5)>0 it is positive definite, so it has
no projective real point. Genus zero over an algebraic closure therefore
must not be reported as a rational source over that moduli field.
The quartic K used above is the authors' field for their P^1 formula.

## Determinant-one chart and boundary ledger

Let U=A^1_t minus {0,1} and
V=P^1_x minus beta^-1({0,1,infinity}). Over C there are fourteen removed
points, so the unit ranks are two and thirteen. The finite etale map
V -> U has degree twelve. Since x=infinity is removed, x is an affine
coordinate on V, and beta' is a unit there. Consequently

\[
 V\times A^1_z\longrightarrow U\times A^1_Z,\qquad
 (x,z)\longmapsto(\beta(x),z/\beta'(x))
 \tag{3}
\]

has determinant one and exactly the same degree-twelve generic action.
Every fiber over U is complete. This is a smooth-affine chart realization.

Over the displayed K, exact factorization splits A into two linear
factors, leaves B and C irreducible quadratic, E linear, and R irreducible
sextic. Thus V has seven K-prime punctures counting infinity, all on P^1
with a K-rational removed infinity; its unit group modulo K* has rank six.
Use finite-prime generators (A_1,A_2,B,C,E,R). Pullback of the target
generators beta and beta-1 and the rational derivative have columns

\[
 T=\begin{pmatrix}
 1&0\\1&0\\5&0\\0&5\\0&1\\-2&-2
 \end{pmatrix},\qquad
 d=(0,0,4,4,0,-3)^T.
 \tag{4}
\]

The infinity coefficients are determined by degree zero. The Smith
diagonals are (1,1) for T and (1,1,1) for (T,d); thus the target image
is primitive with free rank-four cokernel, and the derivative gives a
primitive nonzero quotient class. These are statements over K.
Factorization and ranks may change over K'; the geometric ranks above
remain valid over every algebraically closed constant field.

In particular, adjoining affine coordinates cannot turn either of the
chart factors in (3) into affine space: their nonconstant units persist.
A different polynomial affine completion must change the boundary, retain
the field extension, and solve the full constant-Jacobian equations.
No such completion is constructed here. The new input removes the
*elliptic-source* obstruction of the modular cover, while the absolute
realization problem for this action remains open.
