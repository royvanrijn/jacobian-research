# The A4 construction objective is closed by the monodromy obstruction

Recorded 15 November 2026. This is a bridge from the older root-incidence
programme to the internal absolute obstruction, not a new proof of it.

## Status and proof dependence

[KMFI66 in the central-cover proof](CENTRAL_LIFT_OBSTRUCTION.md) excludes
geometric generic monodromy A4 for every characteristic-zero polynomial
Keller self-map of ordinary affine space, in every dimension and every
faithful transitive action. The conclusion is internal and subject to that
proof and its cited Brauer/purity inputs. Independent whole-proof review,
formal verification and novelty priority are not claimed.

The natural degree-four action has odd stabilizer C3. Its nonsplit central
double cover SL2(F3) violates KMFI65. The other faithful transitive A4
actions have stabilizers 1 or C2 and degrees twelve or six; their normal
closures are 1 or V4, so KMFI1 excludes them. This exhausts the actions.

## Consequence for the older construction programme

Suppose a new root chart, selector, source/target modification, contraction
module, affine deletion or stabilization preserved the A4 extension and
produced a polynomial Keller map between ordinary affine spaces. Its
geometric generic monodromy would still be A4, contradicting KMFI66.
Thus the entire A4-preserving affine-space Keller construction objective
is closed, including architectures beyond the particular masks already
excluded. Finding a rational boundary component or a new divisor ledger
cannot reopen that objective. Reconsideration requires a substantive
failure of the absolute proof or a change of hypotheses, not a different
chart within those hypotheses.

## What is retained and what remains separate

The local identities, exact divisor allocations, normalized boundary
lattices, conductor obstructions, genus computations, finite sieves and
replay inputs remain valid in their stated scopes. KIG1's determinant-one
map is between smooth affine boundary complements, not ordinary affine
spaces. KIG2's polynomial cone has nonconstant Jacobian. Neither contradicts
the obstruction. Partial KIG records retain their local scope and partial
state: this bridge neither proves a classification of higher selectors nor
promotes bounded searches to universal local theorems.

In particular the full D23 parameter image, higher jet-correction modules
and nonlinear rational-component classifications may remain unresolved as
local geometry. They no longer constitute possible solutions of the closed
A4-preserving affine-space Keller construction objective.

[Wright boundary states](../affine-complements/README.md) labelled A4
require their own analysis. A local boundary image, peripheral group or
intermediate block is not automatically the full geometric generic
monodromy of a polynomial affine-space Keller map. Applying KMFI66 there
requires a separate bridge proving precisely that full-action hypothesis.
This record does not close the remaining Wright A4 boundary trajectories.
Likewise A4 in a specialized fibre, an A4 subgroup of a larger generic
group, positive-characteristic maps, and binary SL2(F3) monodromy are not
excluded by the A4 statement. The full monodromy spectrum and exceptional
degree-eleven PSL2(11) problem remain open.

KIG28 records this construction closure in MATH_STATUS.json. KMFI66 and its
canonical central-cover note remain the authority for the obstruction.
