# Symmetry groups of Keller maps

The [theory and proofs](THEORY.md) distinguish actions, covers, determinant-one charts and polynomial Keller maps on ordinary affine space.

[Rational linear-fiber rigidity](RATIONAL_LINEAR_FIBER_RIGIDITY.md) excludes primitive rational degree-one curvature at polynomial normalized full rank. It closes every perturbation Q*M*p+(M*b)*R with irreducible nonlinear Q having exactly one translation direction b and b-independent R, including a rank3-quadric cubic family. An explicit cubic passes determinant, cyclicity and every basepoint nilpotence test but fails the full graph residues. Other irreducible-quadric directions and the absolute exceptional11 action remain open.

[Complete triangular cubic-chain obstruction](CUBIC_TRIANGULAR_CHAIN_RIGIDITY.md) closes A*(u^3,u^2x,u^2y+ux^2,u^2z+xy^2) for every invertible output matrix and homogeneous multiplier degree at full rank. A general last-coordinate integer multiplicity test and two leading trace arguments force a triangular chart already excluded by rigidity. Other cubic directions, even with the same Jacobian-power base u^2, and the absolute exceptional11 action remain open.

[Nilpotent normal strata and cubic tests](NILPOTENT_NORMAL_STRATA.md) strengthen the normal obstruction: even singular normal derivatives must be nilpotent, and every homogeneous base-point derivative must be nilpotent. The leading multiplier satisfies an exact finite moment test. These conditions exclude a cyclic primitive cubic direction with fourth-power Jacobian in every scalar degree, and restrict an entire triangular cubic family. General cubic directions, nonhomogeneous fields and the absolute exceptional11 action remain open.

[Complete primitive quadratic rank4 obstruction](QUADRATIC_PRIMITIVE_RIGIDITY.md) closes all four direction families for arbitrary output matrices and every homogeneous multiplier degree. A highest-translation trace and an all-order normal-zero-stratum determinant exclude the remaining mixed chain and product shear. Nonprimitive quadratic survivors are exactly the existing scalar-times-linear class; rank4 degree11 is A11. A homogeneous exceptional11 input now needs primitive direction degree at least three. Higher directions, nonhomogeneous inputs and the absolute action spectrum remain open.

[Complete single-square obstruction](SINGLE_SQUARE_QUADRATIC_RIGIDITY.md) closes V=A*(u^2,ux,uy,uz+x^2) for every invertible output matrix and homogeneous multiplier degree at full rank. A fixed-hypersurface lemma excludes both nonlinear ruling patterns; linear factors contradict a differentiated trace. Together with the chained-square result, the remaining quadratic families are the mixed chain and product shear. Their actual fields/groups and the absolute exceptional11 action remain open.

[Complete chained-square obstruction](CHAINED_SQUARE_QUADRATIC_RIGIDITY.md) closes V=A*(u^2,ux,uy+x^2,uz+y^2) for every invertible output matrix and every homogeneous scalar degree at full rank. Its unique projective base point forces scalar cones; two exact trace identities exclude the last linear-power branch. The remaining quadratic direction families are the mixed chain, square shear and product shear. Their actual generic groups and the absolute exceptional11 action remain open.

[Every critical-boundary factor is excluded](MIXED_BOUNDARY_MULTIPLIERS.md) from primitive quadratic rank4 full-rank multipliers, including mixed products. Keeping the logarithmic correction yields a plane-image residue obstruction and closes the remaining chain flags. Every surviving scalar must be nonconstant and coprime to u, with irreducible factors of degree one, two or three. General nonboundary arrangements and their actual monodromy remain open.

[Pure boundary multiplier obstruction](PURE_BOUNDARY_MULTIPLIER.md) excludes every scalar F=c*u^s, for all s>=0, across all four primitive quadratic rank4 families and arbitrary output matrices. A weighted boundary-matrix argument forces the matched chart. Remaining full-rank candidates need a non-u factor of degree one, two or three; the subsequent mixed-boundary theorem also excludes accompanying u powers.

[All unmatched quadratic charts](UNMATCHED_QUADRATIC_CHARTS.md) reduce every determinant-compatible primitive quadratic rank4 direction to four explicit forms with an arbitrary invertible output matrix. A two-sign midpoint proof gives an affine-chart isomorphism, but its target hyperplane need not match the source boundary. Global multiplier divisors satisfy weighted preservation, and every non-u factor has degree at most three. The matrix and low-degree factor solutions, their sweep monodromy and actual affine realizations remain open.

[Complete matched pure-square quadratic rigidity](QUADRATIC_PURE_SQUARE_RIGIDITY.md) closes the whole homogeneous rank4 architecture with V0=u^2, in every scalar degree and every linear parameter. All multiplier divisors lie on straight-line homotopies; ambient automorphy makes the selected ruling lines carry affine actions, and the leading base locus eliminates every remaining factor. Full rank forces primitive degree1 and the old factorized classification; no exceptional11 field occurs here. Unmatched quadratic, higher primitive and nonhomogeneous directions remain open.

[Low-degree multiplier divisors](LOW_DEGREE_MULTIPLIER_DIVISORS.md) exclude every quadratic factor and every non-u linear scalar factor, with exact rank3 and u-boundary controls. The subsequent [full matched quadratic theorem](QUADRATIC_PURE_SQUARE_RIGIDITY.md) closes the higher-factor frontier as well.

[Two quadratic normal forms](TWO_IMAGE_PROJECTIVE_REDUCTION.md) give an exhaustive direction reduction with arbitrary linear mixing and constants, plus coupled-companion and split-scalar obstructions. All their nonaffine matched pure-square members are now excluded by the [full rigidity theorem](QUADRATIC_PURE_SQUARE_RIGIDITY.md).

[Nontriangular quadratic curvature](RANK_ONE_PROJECTIVE_CURVATURE.md) closes every one-dimensional nonlinear-image case, including nonsquare forms and resonant mixing. The subsequent [full rigidity theorem](QUADRATIC_PURE_SQUARE_RIGIDITY.md) also closes both two-image normal forms in the matched pure-square class.

[Coupled triangular projective rigidity](TRIANGULAR_PROJECTIVE_RIGIDITY.md) closes every homogeneous direction with triangular constant-diagonal projective coordinates, allowing curvature in every later coordinate, in every rank and degree. A cyclic eigen-coordinate lemma forces all curvature affine, so every full-rank survivor has primitive degree1 and falls under the existing classification. Repeated and resonant parameters do not escape; nonconstant diagonal and nontriangular directions remain open.

[One curved coordinate](ONE_CURVED_DIRECTION.md) closes arbitrary polynomial curvature in a single last projective coordinate, in every rank and homogeneous degree. Full rank forces that coordinate to be affine, reducing every survivor to the already classified scalar-times-linear family. Mixed/lower terms and resonant parameters do not evade the theorem; genuinely multiple curved outputs and other primitive directions remain open.

[Primitive multiplier criterion](PRIMITIVE_MULTIPLIER_CRITERION.md) determines every homogeneous scalar multiplier from its primitive direction, without a multiplier degree bound: a Jacobian-power gate, unique rational feedback form, nonnegative integer logarithmic residues and a full-rank coframe are necessary and sufficient for each fixed direction. The entire primitive quadratic binary-shear family is excluded in all parameters. A universal Euler identity also rules out fixed cylinder directions for every full-rank normalized branch. Unrestricted primitive directions and exceptional11 remain open.

[Factorized homogeneous fields](FACTORIZED_HOMOGENEOUS_FIELDS.md) close every scalar-times-linear H=g-fp architecture to exceptional11, including non-diagonal matrix attempts and the one-point axis case. The exact degree11 groups are symmetric, alternating or forbidden cyclic. An explicit nonfinite full-rank A11 input now has an actual A6 completion over a degree24 field, with every finite coefficient retained. A remaining homogeneous PSL input needs primitive projective coordinate degree at least2.

[Homogeneous axis obstruction](HOMOGENEOUS_AXIS_OBSTRUCTION.md) computes the entire special axis cover and proves every higher-rank homogeneous-matrix sweep globally nonfinite. Extra axis points lie on the rank-drop locus of X. For exceptional11, an exhaustive A5 quotient table leaves four positive-axis rows; finite behavior near that axis forces matrix degree5. A rational full-rank cyclic control shows why the one-point axis case remains open.

[Differential reconstruction and rank obstruction](SWEEP_DIFFERENTIAL_RECONSTRUCTION.md) now parameterize every normalized polynomial input by exact coefficient equations, without triangular assumptions. A cyclic differential coframe tests full rank. Its kernel-direction derivative forces a nonzero eigenvalue, excluding affine matrices, fixed nilpotent matrix spaces and all quadratic inputs from the full-rank architecture. The remaining rank4 PSL11 field must have genuinely nonlinear matrix variation; no such field is supplied.

[All-rank integrated actions](ALL_RANK_INTEGRATED_ACTIONS.md) classify every integrated/triangular input: symmetric groups in odd rank, alternating groups in even rank, or excluded regular cyclic actions for linear h. Exact arithmetic constants and a coefficient-field degree bound accompany actual affine completions. Every degree11 member is S11,A11 orC11. Line-preserving coordinate equivalences are affine; a nilpotent-Nijenhuis reduction fails an exact countercontrol. The [full-rank degree9 A6 example](INTEGRATED_SWEEP_PENCILS.md) retains every completion coefficient.

[Exceptional-divisor geometry](COMPLETION_EXCEPTIONAL_DIVISOR.md) computes the actual fibers and normalization of the higher-weight completion. In a hypothetical rank4 PSL2(11) completion, C0 is unramified and splits into11 primes; only2 are retained, forcing9 unramified boundary divisors and class rank at least11 for this representative. Exact class-rank growth also gives a binary degree8 control with Cl=Z^7. The required PSL2(11) field is still missing.

[All-rank affine completion](HIGHER_RANK_COMPLETION.md) now turns every full-rank normalized polynomial tautological field with normally generating stabilizer into an actual Keller map on ordinary affine space after one common variable. The finite weighted-jet rectifier has an explicit polynomial inverse. Exceptional11 reduces to a nonfinite rank4 field with birational horizontal-line-ruled ramification surface and one omitted index5 prime; a proved input would automatically receive an A6 completion. The retained expanded A6 control realizes S3, while the required PSL2(11) field remains open.

[Complete normalized-sweep obstruction](SWEEP_BOUNDARY_COMPLETENESS.md) closes the whole remaining nonfinite route to exceptional PSL2(11), with no scalar or primitive-pair degree bound. Compactifying every sweep line shows that missing branches over its ramification surface would force horizontal straight-line rulings, which instead force a finite sweep. Full inertia is therefore exactly one or two three-cycles. A different architecture is still required for the open absolute exceptional11 problem.

[All A_k branch strata](ARTIN_BRANCH_STRATA.md) now have a complete local quotient test:295 pair orbits and660-periodicity cover every k. Ordinary cusps allow S3,A4 or A5 noncyclic images, with different marks in the two exceptional actions. A tacnode already admits full PSL2(11) local monodromy; explicit matrices show why these local restrictions and permitted omissions alone cannot decide the global Keller action.

[Local image and subgroup marks](LOCAL_IMAGE_AND_MARKS.md) now distinguish the two exceptional PSL2(11) actions at S3 branch singularities, although their element cycle types agree. The complete620-subgroup table gives all minimal fixed-sheet obstructions. Pure boundary geometry controls when these marks count actual fibers. Normal-crossing strata and an explicit omitted-curve Keller control keep the image test precise; any exceptional11 realization must also have arithmetic group exactly PSL2(11) and no extra constants.

[Completed cofactor norm-target obstruction](NORM_TARGET_FINITE_FIBERS.md) closes every dominant rational-target descent of the rational marked Klein family to a degree11 PSL2(11) component. The compulsory norm double cover has exact geometric genus3; all finite-fiber and infinity charts are resolved. This target-field obstruction survives birational models and extra variables. Non-dominant specializations, unrelated constructions and the absolute exceptional action remain open.

[Finite normalization and boundary classes](BOUNDARY_CLASS_OBSTRUCTION.md) now give an absolute affine-completion test: omitted prime divisors freely generate the normalization class group, and ramification is its non-torsion canonical vector. Normal global affine complete intersections and normal monogenic integral closures are excluded. Exceptional degree11 PSL2(11) requires class rank at least2, strengthened to3 for independent curve inputs. The existing cubic map has a fully explicit smooth rank-one normalization, showing why smoothness alone remains allowed.

[Every PSL2(11) curve action](PSL11_CURVE_ACTIONS.md) is now covered: only the two exceptional degree-eleven actions with polynomial passport(2,3,11) can survive independent curve stabilization and arbitrary birational affine recharts. A general prime-subgroup cycle-gcd bound eliminates all even degrees; exact Sylow-overgroup exhaustion and genus-zero budgets eliminate the remaining degree55 and165 routes. This does not decide the exceptional affine completion or actions from other field classes.

[Exceptional PSL2(11) residue obstruction](PSL11_RESIDUE_OBSTRUCTION.md) gives the complete seven-row tame residue table for both degree-eleven actions. A nonzero Schur Brauer residue must survive at some actual source prime of odd residue degree: V4 divisors cannot all retain only quadratic tails without a D12 divisor. This is an absolute constraint in every dimension, while cyclic decomposition still passes and the action remains open.

[Linear coefficient pencils and source units](LINEAR_PENCIL_OBSTRUCTION.md) now force every primitive moving root group to be symmetric, including every irreducible degree-eleven affine-linear graph. No-offset pencils have a complete all-degree root classification. Common-factor moving components and nonlinear monic selected components have explicit nonconstant units, so their rational fields do not give affine-space sources. Constant-leading base changes and regular polynomial root-coordinate changes cannot repair that obstruction.

[Polynomial coefficient graphs](COEFFICIENT_GRAPHS.md) now have constructive affine3 Keller models whenever lower coefficients are arbitrary polynomials in b. An exact weight gate forces full symmetric root groups for all q-independent graphs, closing those routes to PSL2(11). The equality boundary has an explicit rational component with a nonconstant unit, so arbitrary polynomial graph restriction does not preserve an affine source. Sparse expanded witnesses and entire Jacobians are retained.

[Marked-root wreath actions](MARKED_WREATH_ACTIONS.md) add actual affine-space maps of degree n(n-2), with exact cyclic wreath or parity-coupled monodromy and cyclotomic arithmetic constants. They are stably atomic despite a full divisor lattice of blocks. Every finite cyclic geometric generic deck group occurs on an atomic map; independent products realize every finite abelian generic deck group.

[Construction closure bridge](A4_CONSTRUCTION_CLOSURE.md) retires every older A4-preserving affine-space Keller continuation, including new charts/selectors/modifications beyond the tested masks. Local calculations remain valid; Wright A4 boundary states need separate analysis. The internal proof awaits independent whole-proof review.

[Absolute central-cover obstruction](CENTRAL_LIFT_OBSTRUCTION.md) excludes every faithful transitive A4 action and natural PSL2(q) for q=3 mod4, including degree-twelve PSL2(11), in every affine dimension. The complete action spectrum through degree4 is now identity, none, S3, S4. The exceptional degree-eleven PSL2(11) case and the full classification remain open.

[Forbidden pair-block fields](HOMOGENEOUS_BLOCK_OBSTRUCTION.md): an infinite homogeneous signed Keller family has exact intermediate curve fields that admit no Keller model, even stably, despite rational source and target and normally generating stabilizers. The degree-twelve block normal closure also gives two excluded PSL2(11) subgroup targets, of genera74038 and74226.

[Branch-centered completion](BRANCH_CENTERED_COMPLETION.md) now realizes the binary SL2(F3) degree-eight action on ordinary affine4 over Q(sqrt(-3)). All611 expanded monomials and the full constant Jacobian are replayed exactly. More generally, every normalized rank-two polynomial tautological sweep admits such a stabilized completion exactly when its sheet stabilizer normally generates the group; finite inputs satisfy this precisely when N>3delta.

[Cubic constant-scalar classification](CUBIC_WAVE_CLASSIFICATION.md) has exactly A7,A8,A10,A12,A21 as field groups. Its curved parabola-pole family has full A21 and an explicit ordinary-affine-four-space Keller completion, beyond affine reduction to the earlier chart. Cubic nonconstant scalars remain open.

[Complete quadratic classification](QUADRATIC_CLASSIFICATION.md) reduces every primitive quadratic wave pair to the completed chart for scalars of every degree. A universal positivity/interlacing proof closes all diagonal weights. Only natural alternating/symmetric or binary degree-eight field actions remain; the only prime degree is5. The [earlier finite certificates](QUADRATIC_SCALAR_OBSTRUCTION.md) are retained as replay evidence.

[Projective pole-direction rigidity](PROJECTIVE_POLE_RIGIDITY.md) now classifies every homogeneous normalization and rejects generic leading directions for scalars of any degree. Every affine primitive pair reduces to the completed chart; every primitive quadratic pair with constant scalar gives only A5, A6 or A8.

[Complete k(w)[z] chart](POLYNOMIAL_MULTIPLIER_CLASSIFICATION.md) now includes polynomial one-parameter multipliers: they are automatically finite. The exact action list adds the binary SL2(F3) degree-eight field to the natural alternating/symmetric and even signed families. Both PSL2(11) actions and natural A4 are excluded through this whole chart. The binary field now has its ordinary-affine4 completion in KMFI60.

[Regular source deck rigidity](GENERAL_DECK_RIGIDITY.md) holds for every characteristic-zero polynomial Keller map in every dimension: only the identity polynomial source endomorphism fixes its target. Generic rational deck groups may still be nontrivial.

[Complete rational chart](RATIONAL_CHART_CLASSIFICATION.md) now settles every z-dependent multiplier in k(w)[z], with any polynomial normalization factor: exact integrability, composite nonhomogeneous degree, all missing affine branches and full natural alternating/symmetric monodromy. Neither PSL2(11) action occurs through this entire chart.

[Tautological boundary constraints](TAUTOLOGICAL_BOUNDARY.md) exclude the natural degree-twelve PSL2(11) action from every tautological four-dimensional Gao lift, including nonfinite input and rank collapse. Its Gauss and local-inertia arguments underlie the complete rational-chart classification above.

[Rational sweeps](RATIONAL_SWEEPS.md) now classify every z-dependent rational multiplier whose normalization factor is independent of z: the full groups are natural alternating or symmetric, including nonfinite inputs and higher poles. A complete polynomial lift supplies an additional infinite family of actual even alternating maps on affine four-space.

[Asymptotic directions](SWEEP_ASYMPTOTICS.md) bound the ramification-surface degree by two and exclude every finite tautological affine-four-space sweep for the exceptional degree-eleven PSL2(11) action. Rational multiplier rigidity and the complete homogeneous rational class are proved in the same note.

[Signed-alternating actions](SIGNED_ALTERNATING.md) 2^(n-1):A_n on 2n signed letters occur on ordinary affine four-space for every even n>=6. Their maps are atomic despite imprimitive monodromy. The degree-twelve member has geometric order11520, arithmetic C2 wr S6 and exact quadratic-character constants; the power-two subfamily is not independently curve-induced.

[All natural alternating actions](ALL_ALTERNATING.md) are realized on ordinary affine three-space for every n>=5. Higher-power radicands give every odd degree; the attributed level-two cyclic cones give every even degree at least six. The A8 map and all A_(2^a), a>=3, are proved to lie outside every independently stabilized curve presentation. [The earlier audit](ALTERNATING_REALIZATIONS.md) retains the quadratic beta family and alternating-shear A12 map. The exceptional degree-eleven PSL2(11) actions remain unresolved; the natural degree-twelve action is excluded by KMFI67.

[Global denominator descent](CURVE_DESCENT_LCM.md) requires the lcm of the branch-fiber index gcds to equal the cover degree. It excludes every natural odd-q PSL2(q) curve input after independent stabilization and arbitrary birational affine recharts. For the surviving exceptional degree-eleven PSL2(11) curve inputs, it forces the polynomial (2,3,11) passport. An explicit cubic Keller map provides a positive curve-induced control.

[Subgroup reduction](SUBGROUP_REDUCTION.md) closes three attempts to obtain PSL2(11) from known A11/S11 maps: the new target curves have positive genera 5678, 7331 and 9343, so no affine target is possible after independent stabilization. The [global two-form proof](SURFACE_FORMS.md) now excludes both A12 subgroup target surfaces as well: they have nonzero regular two-forms and are not stably rational. All five audited subgroup reductions are excluded.

[Subset-orbit forms](ORBIT_FORMS.md) strengthen the surface result: every proper transitive subgroup reduction of either A12 family has a non-stably-rational target. This includes M11 and M12. The same method proves source obstructions for unordered and ordered inverse-root configuration covers of these families; it does not exclude those abstract actions for other Keller maps.

[Fiber incidence](FIBER_INCIDENCE.md) forces connected source/target divisor graphs in independently stabilized polynomial covers. Any exceptional degree-eleven Shabat completion needs two reducible, nonreduced tail fibers with mixed multiplicities modulo three and two, and at least fourteen source prime components across its finite branch fibers. An actual S4 map supplies a positive control.

[Normalized sweeps](SWEEP_MONODROMY.md) give Gao F5 geometric A10, arithmetic S10 and constants Q(sqrt(6)). The [nonlinear extension](SWEEP_FRONTIER.md) proves full symmetric monodromy for all four-dimensional curve-type sweeps, with an actual nonlinear S4 control. It gives exact finite-sweep PSL2(11) dimension gates and excludes primitive finite tautological sweeps with one-parameter polynomial multipliers. The earlier imprimitive candidate is now realized by the signed-alternating family.

The [earlier affine-completion proof](F11_AFFINE_COMPLETION.md) retains the single-fiber obstruction and the locally admissible F11 model that it excludes. [Affine inertia](THEORY.md) also rules out all nontrivial nilpotent groups and even natural dihedral actions.
