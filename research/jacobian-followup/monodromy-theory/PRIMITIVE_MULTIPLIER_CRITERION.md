# Primitive homogeneous directions and the exact multiplier test

The remaining homogeneous field problem is not an unrestricted
search over scalar multipliers of every degree. Full rank forces
a cyclic derivative matrix, which determines the logarithmic
multiplier form exactly. This gives a necessary-and-sufficient
polynomial multiplier criterion for any fixed primitive direction.
It also excludes a whole primitive quadratic shear family.

These are input-construction results. Passing them does not
prove a requested generic permutation action or complete the
Keller action spectrum. Unrestricted primitive directions,
nonhomogeneous inputs and exceptional11 remain open.

## 1. Euler cyclicity and nondegenerate Gauss image

Write X=(f,g), P=Dg-p df, detDS=gamma^r, and assume
generic rankDX=r with r>=2.

**KMFI124.** For every nonzero constant target vector
q=(q0,q_vec), the rational vector q_vec-q0*p is generically
cyclic for the regular nilpotent matrix P:
\[
                  P^{r-1}(q_{\rm vec}-q0p)\ne0.           \tag{1}
\]
In particular p and every nonzero constant source vector
are generically cyclic. This is not a simultaneous pointwise
claim: at a chosen branch center p0, the vector p is zero.

Put v=q_vec-q0*p. Suppose P^(r-1)v0 identically.
For 1<=m<=r-1, KMFI116 gives
\[
 \operatorname{div}(P^m v)
 =-(r-m)dfP^{m-1}v-q0\operatorname{tr}(P^m)
 =-(r-m)dfP^{m-1}v.                                      \tag{2}
\]
If P^m v0, (2) and char0 imply dfP^(m-1)v0.
But P^(m-1)v is in kerP. Full rank says df is nonzero on
that one-dimensional kernel, so P^(m-1)v0.
Descending gives v0, contradicting q nonzero.
This proves (1) without a homogeneity or finiteness hypothesis.

Let w span the left kernel of P. A normal of the irreducible
branch image D=closure(X(A^r)) is (-wp,w).
Equation(1) says it pairs nontrivially with every nonzero
constant target direction. Hence the Gauss image spans the
whole dual projective space. Equivalently, D is invariant
under no nonzero constant translation direction: a directional
derivative of its reduced equation vanishing on D must vanish
identically by degree.

Thus the horizontal line rulings required by KMFI105 cannot
be a cylinder in a fixed direction. This does not exclude
rulings whose directions vary.

## 2. Separate a homogeneous primitive direction

Now take homogeneous P of positive degree and use KMFI115
to center X. Remove the greatest common polynomial factor
of H=g-fp and write
\[
 H=FV,\quad \gcd(V_1,\ldots,V_r)=1,\quad
 \deg F=s\ge0,\quad\deg V=d\ge1,\quad e=s+d.
\]
F may be constant. Let A=DV.

**KMFI125.** Every full-rank normalized input of this form has
\[
 f=-Fh,\quad \deg h=d-1,\quad h\ne0,\qquad
              \det A=\frac d e h^r,                      \tag{3}
\]
and the Krylov matrix
\[
             C=(V,AV,\ldots,A^{r-1}V)                     \tag{4}
\]
is invertible over k(p). In particular detDV must be a nonzero
constant times a perfect r-th polynomial power.

Indeed DH=FA+V dF and A p=dV. Thus
DH=A[F I+(p/d)dF] and
detDH=(e/d)F^r detA.
Since DH=P-fI, this equals(-f)^r.
The UFD property shows that f/F is polynomial, proving (3).

To prove (4), set eta=dF/F and
N=A-hI+V eta=P/F, a regular nilpotent matrix.
If the A-invariant Krylov span W generated by V were proper,
it would also be N-invariant. Every proper invariant subspace
of a single nilpotent Jordan block lies in ImN.
A left kernel row w would therefore kill V.
Then wN0 gives wA=hw, and A p=dV gives hwp0.
Since h nonzero, wp0, contradicting KMFI124.
This proves cyclicity, not merely a generic-matrix assumption.

## 3. A complete scalar multiplier criterion

Fix homogeneous V and a proposed homogeneous polynomial h of degree d-1. If C is singular, reject.
Let chi(T)=det(TI-A), and expand
\[
 1-\frac{(T-h)^r}{\chi(T)}
            =\sum_{j\ge0}\mu_j T^{-j-1}.
\]
Define the unique rational row one-form
\[
               \eta=(\mu_0,\ldots,\mu_{r-1})C^{-1}.        \tag{5}
\]
It satisfies exactly
\[
 \det(TI-[A+V\eta])=(T-h)^r.                               \tag{6}
\]
For a direct verification, the determinant lemma expresses the
characteristic polynomial through eta(TI-A)^-1 V.
The first r coefficients at infinity are precisely the r
linear equations eta C=mu. A polynomial numerator of degree
at most r-1 with these r zero coefficients must be zero.

Factor detC into irreducible source polynomials D_j.
Then a polynomial scalar F exists exactly when
\[
             \eta=\sum_j b_j\,dD_j/D_j,\qquad
                     b_j\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}.              \tag{7}
\]
When it exists, it is unique up to a nonzero constant:
F=constant*product D_j^b_j.
This equality tests the whole form, including closedness and
absence of higher poles or an exact rational remainder.
It must not be replaced by closedness alone.

Necessity follows from eta=dlogF; any zero divisor of F gives
a pole with its positive integer multiplicity. Formula(5)
has no possible pole outside detC.
Conversely (7) reconstructs F exactly, and (6) makes
\[
                  f=-Fh,\quad g=F(V-hp)                  \tag{8}
\]
polynomial with normalized sweep determinant gamma^r.
Full rank is the additional exact rational test
\[
 \det\begin{pmatrix}\rho\\\rho N\\\vdots\\\rho N^{r-1}\end{pmatrix}
 \ne0,\qquad \rho=dh+h\eta,\quad N=A+V\eta-hI.             \tag{9}
\]
Indeed df=-F rho and P=FN, so this differs from KMFI116's
coframe determinant by a nonzero power of F.
Equations(5)--(9) are necessary and sufficient for a polynomial
full-rank normalized input with the fixed primitive V,h.
They do not assert a specified monodromy or automatic normal
generation; KMFI104's field gate is still required for completion.

There is a useful finite parameter reduction without a bound on s.
First factor detA. Unless all its divisor multiplicities are
divisible by r, no multiplier can work.
Otherwise write detA=delta*h0^r, where h0 has degree d-1.
Every possibility has h=lambda*h0 for a nonzero constant lambda.
The entries of eta in(5) are polynomials in lambda of degree<=r,
with a denominator dividing detC, independent of lambda.

Match(7) after multiplying by detC. The logarithmic forms for
the distinct D_j are linearly independent over constants
(their divisor residues distinguish them).
Choose independent coefficient rows to solve uniquely for
candidate residue polynomials b_j(lambda), degree<=r.
The other coefficient rows give univariate compatibility
equations, also of degree<=r. Retain their conditions; solving
a generic inconsistent linear system and discarding isolated
lambda values would be wrong.

After imposing those equations, require all residues to be
nonnegative integers and apply(9). If the compatibility equations
all vanish identically, the integer conditions remain; no finiteness
of the lambda set is asserted.
Euler homogeneity gives automatically
\[
              \deg F=d\lambda^r/\delta-d.                \tag{10}
\]
Thus the unknown multiplier PDE and every possible multiplier
degree have been removed in favor of exact rational forms,
univariate conditions and integer residues.

In rank4, a primitive quadratic V necessarily satisfies
detDV=constant*(linear form)^4, has invertible C, and passes
this complete logarithmic test. A passed fourth-power Jacobian
condition alone is far from sufficient.

## 4. A complete primitive quadratic shear obstruction

**KMFI126.** Let p=(u,v,w,t) and
\[
 V=(u^2,\ auv,\ buw,\ cut+Q(v,w)),                         \tag{11}
\]
where a,b,c are arbitrary constants and Q is any nonzero
homogeneous binary quadratic. No nonzero homogeneous polynomial
F of any degree makes H=FV a normalized full-rank rank4 input.
The conclusion includes repeated-eigenvalue and resonant
parameters, not just a generic open set.
It is an obstruction to this primitive quadratic direction class,
not to every primitive quadratic V or every Keller action.

Here is an all-parameter proof. The matrix A=DV has determinant
2abc*u^4. Full rank first forces abc nonzero and
h=lambda*u with lambda nonzero.
Set
\[
 K=c(c-1)(c-a)(c-b),
\]
\[
 R=(c-2)(c-a)(c-b)Q+
       (c-1)\{a(c-b)vQ_v+b(c-a)wQ_w\}.
\]
Two exact polynomial identities, valid for every parameter, are
\[
 \det C=-ab(a-1)(a-b)(b-1)u^{10}vw(Kut+R),                 \tag{12}
\]
\[
 [\operatorname{adj}(cuI-A)V]_{1,2,3}=0,\qquad
 [\operatorname{adj}(cuI-A)V]_4=u^3(Kut+R).                \tag{13}
\]
They follow by expanding the triangular derivative and its
Krylov columns. Their whole symbolic residuals are checked.
Since C must be invertible, a,b are distinct, neither is1,
and Kut+R is not identically zero.

The characteristic identity(6), evaluated at T=cu, now forces
\[
                  \eta_t=-\frac{u(\lambda-c)^4}{Kut+R}.   \tag{14}
\]
If lambda=c, it gives F_t0. Then f=-cuF and
g=F(V-cu p) are independent of t, contradicting full rank.
If K0, the right side of(14) is constant in t.
A polynomial's logarithmic derivative in t tends to zero
at infinity; hence lambda=c, the same contradiction.
This covers c1,c=a,c=b and every case where K vanishes.
A possible eigenvalue coincidence with2 needs no division.

It remains K nonzero and lambda!=c.
On the chart u1 let S=R/K, q=t+S(v,w).
Equation(14) gives
\[
 \eta_t=j/q,\qquad j=-(\lambda-c)^4/K.
\]
Polynomiality forces j a positive integer and
F(1,v,w,t)=G(v,w)q^j.
Evaluating the same characteristic identity at T=a,b gives,
on this chart,
\[
 \eta_v+\frac{Q_v}{a-c}\eta_t
   =-\frac{(a-\lambda)^4}{av(a-1)(a-b)(a-c)},
\]
\[
 \eta_w+\frac{Q_w}{b-c}\eta_t
   =-\frac{(b-\lambda)^4}{bw(b-1)(b-a)(b-c)}.              \tag{15}
\]
These are rational identities with no denominator a-2 or b-2,
so they include those resonances. They can also be checked
by the triangular adjugate at T=a,b after clearing denominators.

Compare the poles at q0 in(15) with dlog(Gq^j).
They force
\[
                       S_v=Q_v/(c-a),\qquad
                       S_w=Q_w/(c-b).
\]
Equality of mixed derivatives and a!=b imply Q_vw0.
Thus Q=alpha*v^2+gamma*w^2 and
S=alpha*v^2/(c-a)+gamma*w^2/(c-b).

The trace identity makes F a Darboux polynomial for V:
V(F)/F=[4lambda-(2+a+b+c)]u.
In projective coordinates on u1, the vector field is
\[
 (a-1)v\partial_v+(b-1)w\partial_w+
                              ((c-1)t+Q)\partial_t.
\]
Since G has no t dependence, the pole at q0 must vanish.
The coefficient of t fixes the cofactor of q to c-1, yielding
\[
 Q+(a-1)vS_v+(b-1)wS_w-(c-1)S=0.
\]
Its v^2 and w^2 coefficients are
alpha*(a-1)/(c-a) and gamma*(b-1)/(c-b).
Both vanish only when alpha=gamma=0, because a,b are not1.
That contradicts Q nonzero and proves the theorem.

For an explicit replay, choose (a,b,c)=(3,4,5), Q=vw.
The exact multiplier form has logarithmic coefficient matching
condition (lambda-5)^4=0. At its only candidate lambda5,
\[
        \eta=(29/(3u),\ -4/(3v),\ 1/(12w),\ 0).
\]
The fractional and negative residues reject every polynomial F.
The numerical constants are a certificate for that direction;
the preceding identities prove the whole family in every
multiplier degree.

The full-rank condition is essential even after finding an honest
polynomial multiplier. For example, take
a=1+i,b=1-i,c=2,Q=vw,h=2u.
The exact form is eta=(0,1/v,1/w,0), so F=vw is accepted.
It gives a normalized polynomial input with regular nilpotent
matrix at generic points, but X is independent of t and has
rank3. The coframe in(9) vanishes and correctly rejects it.
Thus the theorem does not assert that the shear direction admits
no polynomial normalized multipliers at all.

## 5. Evidence and the remaining field problem

Exact controls verify Euler/Gauss cyclicity through polynomial
coefficient ranks, the feedback characteristic identity, full
logarithmic coefficient matching without losing isolated lambda
values, accepted polynomial norm multipliers, rejected primitive
directions, and the whole symbolic quadratic-shear identities.
The written arguments are unbounded; finite controls are not a
classification search or a formal proof.

The criterion covers every homogeneous primitive V once it is
specified. It does not classify all possible V, compute their
generic groups, or provide an action-level sufficient criterion.
For the remaining rank4 PSL field, a primitive quadratic direction
must have a fourth-power linear Jacobian, cyclic C, nonnegative
integer logarithmic residues and nonzero coframe(9).
The entire shear family(11) is closed even though it passes the
Jacobian-power shape test. Other quadratic directions, primitive
degree>=3 and nonhomogeneous inputs remain open.

No new PSL2(11) map or absolute exceptional-action obstruction
is supplied. Formal verification, independent whole-proof replay
and external review are not claimed. The full faithful-transitive
Keller monodromy spectrum remains the active unresolved objective.
