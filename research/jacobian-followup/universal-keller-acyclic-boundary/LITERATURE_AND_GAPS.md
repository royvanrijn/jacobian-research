# Primary inputs and what the deformations change

14 November 2026. [Canonical proof](PROOF.md).

[Dubouloz--Palka, arXiv1701.01425v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1701.01425), Corollary5.2, equation(5.9), Proposition5.5 and Example5.7 supply the open embedding, finite cyclic cover and shear construction. The undeformed example previously analyzed is surjective; the larger published deformation family is not confined to that property. We compute its complete omission set using the entire finite cover. The published construction is credited; the image formula, prescribed omission sets, resulting simple stable inequivalence invariant, sharp acyclic degree threshold and transfer to canonical degree bounds are deductions recorded here, without a novelty claim.

[Stacks, purity of branch locus, tag0BMB](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BMB) supports the Galois-intermediate lifting argument in every characteristic under its explicit unit/Picard hypotheses. This extends the retained KMFI1 rule from simply connected affine targets to general targets with restricted actual etale covers. [Campbell's1973 paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01349234) is the historical affine Galois case; its publisher record was inspected for attribution, not used as an unread proof dependency. Our argument is written from divisor pullback, valuation conjugacy and purity.

[Haution, Proposition3.2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1507.04582) gives the actual tame prime-group fixed varieties. The [fixed-fibre degree budget](../universal-keller-fixed-fibres/PROOF.md) transfers the sharp mod-2 quadratic obstruction to the even-rank cost. Composite cyclic fixed loci in positive characteristic retain their separate disconnectedness gate; no Smith claim is made for them.

[Stacks Kummer theory](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03PK) and [Cheng--Ji--Larson--Olander, Theorem7.3](https://mattlarson2399.github.io/Papers/theorem-of-the-base.pdf) give the unit and Picard constraints through the compactification argument. Rational ell-adic acyclicity suffices for finiteness in characteristic zero; mod-ell acyclicity additionally excludes ell-primary Picard torsion. The general all-characteristic Galois lifting statement retains its explicit torsion-Picard hypothesis rather than importing the characteristic-zero compactification proof.

The abstract surface examples do not construct canonical Keller maps on affine spaces. Their nontrivial fundamental groups persist under identity stabilization. Embedding the surfaces as the required whole fixed schemes, arranging all subgroup restrictions simultaneously and proving effective gluing remain open. Primitive geometric monodromy supplies stable atomicity here for regular self-maps of the same stabilized acyclic variety; allowing arbitrary intermediate varieties changes that category and the examples already factor through their finite covers.

The cyclic scaling is an actual regular whole-surface symmetry. We use it for effective torsor twisting on the line-bundle hypersurface, obtaining a canonical cyclic marked receiver on forms of S_k. This is a deduction from the published deformation formulas and etale descent, not an assertion that the torsor construction appears in that paper. Its fixed scheme A1 disjoint union Gm makes the precise Smith-hypothesis boundary visible. Fixed field-versality and canonical twisting are proved separately; in prime degree both have stable atomicity in the stated self-map category.
