## What the new arguments close

UAAFFCAN1 uses a two-block stabilizer, not full-group averaging, and applies in wild characteristic. A rank-unit fibre has its own barycentre; if the rank is divisible by p the orbit's affine span plus its subgroup-fixed geometry still costs at least N dimensions for N>=5.

UAINERTIACOST1 strengthens the retained one-fixed-sheet statement to a whole missing-sheet budget. It separates a ramified boundary from omitted unramified branches and proves the rank-one collapse for every natural cyclic-affine generic group with nontrivial complement, not only a displayed polynomial.

UACANATOM1 relates canonical symmetry and atomicity rather than treating them as separate guesses. The full-degree hypothesis yields an invariant finite chart at the marked target. The actual exceptional V4 monodromy in characteristic two prevents an unqualified all-rank claim.

UALANG1 extends the scalar construction to all affine finite-field spaces and a basis-free periodic receiver. Classical Lang and Hilbert90 supply the torsors; constant-Jacobian whole affine maps, the source-action category and actual whole fibres are recorded explicitly.

UA4CHAR2 makes the fixed quartic map globally finite, not just finite on an open chart. Its basis normalization works over all characteristic-two fields, including imperfect fields and F2. The lower bounds do not prove the remaining three-dimensional minimum. The two-dimensional canonical map is a different object and decomposes.

## Exact boundaries

- A marked fibre can have smaller rank than the generic degree. Automatic canonical atomicity requires equality, or an already specified invariant finite chart of that rank.
- The affine bound concerns affine representation bundles. It does not exclude arbitrary nonlinear actions or unmarked lower-dimensional universal maps.
- A loss of sheets is not itself ramification. Strict inequality between actual fibre rank and inertia-fixed labels occurs in the written translation control.
- Periodicity as a property does not choose its gluing isomorphism. Over missing constants the kernel group scheme may be nonconstant.
- The four-coordinate quartic normalization is fieldwise and is not asserted to be a canonical S4-equivariant affine-bundle construction.
- Every canonical affine-plane quartic construction is decomposable under the stated affine-action hypotheses. Arbitrary nonlinear plane actions are outside that classification.
- Individual subgroup fibre algebras are not a global subgroup torsor frame. The finite S4 map in characteristic two demonstrates the difference for A4.
- Characteristic-zero general canonicity and global unmarked dimension minima remain open. Written proofs and exact controls are not independent review or formal verification.
