# Log volume forces a geometric divisor-class obstruction

A noninvertible first stage in any whole retained-signal Keller factorization forces the intermediate generic surface to have negative logarithmic Kodaira dimension. For native maps, geometric factoriality now forces one stage invertible: a PROPER intermediate has nonzero geometric Picard group, while its invariant subgroup over the retained function field is zero.

- [General proofs](PROOF.md): logarithmic volume dichotomy, geometric Picard factor criterion, rational-point descent, and all-degree exact-volume tests.
- Every ordinary smooth Danielewski surface u*v=P(z), degree(P)>=2, is excluded by a nonzero volume period. Higher-power u^m*v=P(z), m>=2, have an explicit regular volume primitive and remain a test family; no required etale diagram or whole polynomial lift is constructed. Its displayed polynomial spread has a critical zero fibre and cannot itself be the whole smooth retained map.

Arithmetic factoriality is distinct from geometric factoriality. The affine-plane characterization is an explicit published input. Independent proof review, novelty, proper intermediate realization, nonlinear integration and canonical absolute stable atomicity remain open.

The [plane-chart follow-up](../universal-keller-plane-charts/PROOF.md) sharpens the higher-power boundary. Any etale target would give JC(2) counterexamples on its classical plane atlas. The whole carried native line must meet at least two root components and satisfy an all-degree contact bound; an explicit family attaining it is noninjective in every exponent. No global plane chart may contain that whole line, even though all its finite/formal neighborhoods are standard.

The [ruling-descent theorem](../universal-keller-ruling-descent/PROOF.md) now separates geometric ruledness from actual retained-field descent. A descended ruling produces a geometric plane atlas and a JC(2) burden; a unique/Galois-invariant ruling does descend. Both proper arrows are nonfinite, and geometric multiple-fibre patterns obey an all-degree orbifold/parity gate.

The [affine-type descent theorem](../universal-keller-affine-descent/PROOF.md) combines Daigle's published classification with the retained volume-period obstruction to identify trivial geometric ML exactly with native target-factor invertibility. Every geometric affine-type ruling of a proper intermediate is therefore unique and explicitly descends. Complete-type Galois-permuted rulings remain the descent issue, conditional on JC(2).
