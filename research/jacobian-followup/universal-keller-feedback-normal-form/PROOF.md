# Two effective controls, affine-r rigidity and formal source coordinates

15 November 2026. Four written general claims with selected exact controls. No independent whole-proof replay, formal verification, external review or novelty certificate. OP-KDESC remains open. The formal-etale lifting principle and Riemann-Hurwitz are classical; their specific feedback consequences are separated below.

Use the normalized cubic K=(sigma,B0,C0), with

    t=1+x*y, q=t^2*z+y^2*(1+3*t), sigma=t*q, X=x*q,
    B0=-y/2-3*x*q/2, C0=x*(5-3*t)-x^3*z.

The native j-th higher-profile response in the last two outputs is

    v_j=(-(j/2)*sigma^2*X^(j-2), -(j-2)*X^j), j>=4.       (0.1)

Signals stay unchanged in Sections1-2. Identity parameters u are retained. Rational proof coordinates are

    S=x/t, r=1/t, X=sigma*S,
    x=S/r, y=(1-r)/S,
    z=sigma*r^3-r*(r+3)*(1-r)^2/S^2.                       (0.2)

UAM1 gives these entire-source function-field identities. They are not a restriction of the source to an inverse chart.

## 1. UANATIVE2GEN1: every native list has two UNIQUE effective controls

Over every commutative coefficient ring with2 invertible, allow finitely many arbitrary POLYNOMIAL source coefficients a_j, including dependence on all blocks and identity parameters. Then

    sum_(j>=4) a_j*v_j = A*v_4+B*v_5,
    A=sum_(j>=4) (5-j)*a_j*X^(j-4),
    B=sum_(j>=5) (j-4)*a_j*X^(j-5).                       (1.1)

This is equality of actual polynomial output maps, with no degree bound. The two controls A,B are unique. Indeed

    v_j=(5-j)*X^(j-4)*v_4+(j-4)*X^(j-5)*v_5, j>=5,
    det(v_4,v_5)=sigma^2*X^7.                            (1.2)

The latter polynomial is a nonzero divisor even over a nonreduced base: x is regular; t=1+xy is regular by its nonzero-divisor leading coefficient y in x; and q is regular as a polynomial in z with regular leading coefficient t^2. Thus sigma^2*X^7=t^2*x^7*q^9 is regular. The native response module is freely generated by v_4,v_5 over the WHOLE source ring, not merely generically.

For a finite coefficient list through index N>=5 its entire kernel is freely generated by

    e_j-(5-j)*X^(j-4)*e_4-(j-4)*X^(j-5)*e_5, 6<=j<=N.    (1.3)

This proves an effective normal form and all coefficient-list redundancies. It works componentwise for any number of mutually dependent blocks. Every Jacobian, fibre, gluing or factorization property is unchanged because the polynomial map is literally unchanged. No claim that arbitrary A,B are Keller is made.

The control degrees remain unbounded. For example the native degree-six profile a_6=1 becomes A=-X^2,B=2X and is the SAME generic-degree-six map. Indices4 and5 in this normal form are not total-coordinate-degree or covering-degree bounds.

Over a field, the basis matrix is invertible away from sigma*X=0. A rational effective-control pole on a divisor coprime to sigma*X cannot disappear in both output components. In particular the scalar formal denominator 1+c_j*lambda*sigma^2*X^(2j-2), with its marked variants, is coprime to sigma*X. A successful polynomial repair must make both effective controls regular there; adding only a second response cannot hide a fixed pole in the first effective control. This does not rule out changing both controls and solving their full determinant equation.

### The full two-control determinant equation

The normal form also gives one fixed-size equation for the remaining one-block problem. At fixed sigma,u put V=sigma^4*S^4, W=sigma^5*S^5 and h0=S+sigma*S^3. For arbitrary rational A(S,r),B(S,r), let

    T=S*h0''-h0'+A*(S*V''-V')+B*(S*W''-W'),
    Omega=V*W'-V'*W.

Then the exact Jacobian equation is

    r*(det DPhi-1)
      =V*A_S+((V/S-V')*r+V*T/S)*A_r
       +W*B_S+((W/S-W')*r+W*T/S)*B_r
       -Omega*(A_S*B_r-A_r*B_S).                         (1.4)

It follows by differentiating the two outputs in (S,r), or the rank-two determinant lemma. This equation includes the control-control term, so freezing either response or dropping that term is not allowed. It is a reformulation, not a solution for all A,B. It removes the need for ever longer native coefficient lists or larger one-block Jacobians.

## 2. UAAFFINER1: all polynomial native feedback affine in r is already a retained profile

Work over a characteristic-zero field. Consider ANY finite polynomial native list a_j(u,x,y,z), or equivalently the unique polynomial A,B in (1.1). Suppose its whole map is Keller and its last two outputs are AFFINE in r over k(u,sigma,S). Equivalently both effective controls are affine in r; the response basis depends only on sigma,S and is invertible in the function field. Then the whole polynomial map is IDENTICAL to a retained-signal profile map with coefficients in k[u,sigma].

This closes every number of coefficient directions in this affine-r sector, not just the earlier one affine coefficient line UASELFCTRL1. It does not classify controls with nonlinear Laurent dependence on r or mutually changing blocks. Parameters u must really be retained for the one-block assertion.

We prove a slightly broader statement: replace the native-list hypothesis by the source-jet conditions

    Bout-B0 in (x^2), Cout-C0 in (x^4),                    (2.1)

for a whole polynomial map with first output sigma, still affine in r. Native polynomial lists always satisfy (2.1). The conclusion is the same retained profile family with indices>=4.

### The Laurent Wronskian

The constant determinant must be one, since (2.1) leaves the full Jacobian unchanged on x=0. Over K0=k(u,sigma), write

    Bout=p(S)*r+b(S), Cout=q(S)*r+c(S).

All four coefficients are Laurent polynomials in S, by (0.2) and uniqueness of coefficients in r. Comparing the source-chart determinant with -r/S gives

    p'*q-p*q'=-1/S, b'*q-p*c'=0.                         (2.2)

A classical Riemann-Hurwitz argument classifies the first equation. Over an algebraic closure of K0, the rational function p/q has no ramification except possibly at0 and infinity: the Wronskian has no zero on Gm, p,q have no common zero there and every finite pole is simple. A degree-d map P1->P1 with ramification supported on at most two source points is a Mobius transform of S^d, since the ramification total2d-2 forces both points totally ramified (degree1 is included separately). The primitive Laurent pairs giving this ratio differ by a Laurent unit. Thus

    p=u*S^m*(a*S^d+b0), q=u*S^m*(c0*S^d+d0).

The Wronskian exponent gives2m+d=0, so d=2n and m=-n. In particular

    p,q belong to span{S^n,S^(-n)}, n>=1.                 (2.3)

The degree1 case cannot satisfy the integral exponent condition. The support statement descends to K0 because p,q already have coefficients there. The only imported curve theorem is the characteristic-zero Riemann-Hurwitz formula, as recorded in Stacks Section53.12.

### Entire-source regularity and the native jets

Set r=1-S*y in (0.2). Then x=S/(1-S*y) and

    z=sigma*(1-S*y)^3-(1-S*y)*(4-S*y)*y^2

are regular formal functions at S=0. Since the original map is polynomial, substituting in p*r+b gives p+b-S*y*p regular at S=0. Comparing y coefficients shows p has pole order at most one at0; likewise q. Thus n=1 in (2.3).

Now use (2.1). For delta p=p-1/(2S), the B condition gives ord_0(delta p)>=1 and delta b=-delta p+O(S^2). The C condition gives ord_0(q-S)>=3 and delta c=O(S^4). Hence

    q=S, p=1/(2S)+kappa*S,
    delta b=-kappa*S+O(S^2), delta c=O(S^4).

The coefficient of S in b'*q-p*c'=0 is -2*kappa, so kappa=0. Therefore p,q are exactly the old cubic coefficients. The output corrections F(S),H(S) are independent of r, polynomial in S, with orders at least2 and4, and

    H'=2*S^2*F'.

Expand F=sum_(n>=2) f_n*S^n. Then

    H=sum_(n>=2) (2*n/(n+2))*f_n*S^(n+2),
    a_(n+2)^eff=-2*f_n/((n+2)*sigma^(n+2)).                (2.4)

These are rational retained-signal profiles. UAPROFILE1 over k(u) forces every effective profile to be polynomial in sigma, since the actual map lands on the whole source. Comparing the C output on y=0,z=sigma descends their parameter coefficients to k[u,sigma], as in UASELFCTRL1. UAALLATOM1 gives the inherited effective generic degree and absolute stable self-atomicity. No new stable class or generic-degree bound is supplied.

The UASELFFORMAL1 source-dependent tangent R_j*v_j has nonzero r derivative in its unique two-control normal form. Consequently no finite polynomial parameter family integrating that tangent can stay affine in r in both effective controls, even if arbitrarily many native coefficient directions are used. The scalar formal solution stays affine in r but is not a polynomial entire-source family. A broader native integration must introduce genuinely nonlinear Laurent-r dependence at some higher order, or change the specified source/first-output architecture. This is not an exclusion of such integrations.

## 3. UASIGFIELD1: the stable Keller normalizer of the signal field is exactly weighted scaling

This section concerns source-coordinate maps F preserving the FIELD k(sigma):

    F^*(k(sigma)) subset k(sigma).                        (3.1)

It is NOT the condition that the first coordinate of a compiler map equals sigma. In particular it is not a classification of all signal-retaining compiler feedback.

Let F:A^(3+m)->A^(3+m) be ANY polynomial Keller self-map over a characteristic-zero field, with extra coordinates w. Under (3.1), necessarily

    F=(eta*x, eta^(-1)*y, eta^(-2)*z, H(P,w)), eta in k*,
    sigma composed F=eta^(-2)*sigma,
    det_w DH in k*.                                     (3.2)

Conversely every such H gives a Keller map of the specified kind. No invertibility of the vertical Keller family H is assumed. For m=0 the maps are exactly the weighted torus. For exact preservation of sigma its base action is only identity or (x,y,z)->(-x,-y,z). For m=1 the vertical family is affine in w and the whole map is an automorphism. For m>=2 the vertical problem contains JC(m); it is not silently solved.

### Proof: one reducible fibre and its distinguished critical cylinder

First, sigma composed F is a rational function h(sigma). The section x=y=0,z=sigma,w=0 proves h is polynomial. The gradient of sigma is unimodular because it is a row of DK with determinant one. Pulling it back and multiplying by the invertible DF shows that h'(sigma)*d sigma is unimodular; h' is therefore a nonzero constant and h=a*sigma+b.

Both t and q are irreducible, and every sigma-c for c!=0 is irreducible. These follow by primitive linear-in-z equations: gcd(t,y)=gcd(t,1+3t)=1, while sigma-c has constant term -c modulo t. Dominance of F makes both t composed F and q composed F nonconstant. Their product a*sigma+b cannot be irreducible, so b=0. Factorization of a*t*q now leaves

    t composed F=gamma*t or gamma*q, gamma in k*.

Assignments of a constant or all of sigma to one factor are impossible by dominance. The irreducibles t,q are comaximal, for example

    1=x^2*q+t*(-3*t^2+5*t-1-t*x^2*z).                    (3.3)

On the generic sigma=s fibre the source is the hyperbola complement

    Spec k(s)[x,y,(1+xy)^(-1)] times A^m,
    z=(s-t*y^2*(1+3*t))/t^3.

The relative critical locus of t there is precisely x=y=0, with t=1. The restriction of F between the generic fibres is etale, so zero relative differential of t composed F is equivalent to the image lying in that critical cylinder. At x=y=0, both candidate pullbacks gamma*t and gamma*q=gamma*s/t have zero relative differential. Their values must therefore equal1. The first gives gamma=1; the second would give gamma*s=1 in k(s), impossible. Thus t composed F=t.

Now F_x*F_y=xy. Polynomial UFD factorization and dominance force F_x=eta*x,F_y=eta^(-1)*y or their swapped version. From sigma composed F=a*sigma,

    F_z=a*z+(a*y^2-F_y^2)*(1+3*t)/t^2.

Polynomial landing of the unswapped version forces a=eta^(-2). The swapped version has a nonzero residue on t=0, since y=-1/x there, and cannot land polynomially. This proves (3.2), in every identity-stabilized dimension. The remaining Jacobian is block triangular, giving exactly the vertical Keller condition.

For several compiler blocks, any polynomial Keller map permuting their individual fields k(sigma_i) is, on those triples, a block permutation followed by independent weighted scalings; any remaining coordinates form a vertical Keller family. Thus coordinate operations respecting those individual signal fields cannot supply hidden block mixing. Preserving only their combined multivariable field is a different, unclassified condition.

An automorphism preserving the whole source divisor sigma=0 normalizes this field, because its principal ideal is preserved up to a constant. If it also preserves one nonzero signal level, its base action is the above order-two group. Fixing a finite marked fibre alone does NOT imply this divisor hypothesis.

## 4. UAETALEGAUGE1: every nilpotent deformation is a source coordinate change, but polynomial degrees can escape

Let R be a commutative ring, I a nilpotent ideal of R, and F:A^n_R->A^n_R polynomial with invertible Jacobian determinant. For ANY polynomial map G congruent to F modulo I, there exists a UNIQUE polynomial source automorphism A congruent to identity modulo I with

    G=F composed A.                                     (4.1)

This is a direct classical consequence of formal etaleness, not a new general lifting theorem. Regard G as a map to the target of F; the identity source map on reduction modulo I lifts uniquely through the etale F. The lift A is an automorphism: A-id raises I-adic order on R[P], so its inverse as an R-linear operator is a finite Neumann sum, and the inverse of a bijective algebra map is an algebra map. If F and G have the same constant Jacobian d in R*, the chain rule gives det DA=1.

For a compatible lambda-adic formal deformation, uniqueness gives compatible A at every finite order and a formal source automorphism in R[P][[lambda]]. This does not imply a finite uniform polynomial degree in P. Over a field, its Newton recursion is explicit:

    A_0=P,
    A_n=A_(n-1)+lambda^n*(DF(P))^(-1)*E_n modulo lambda^(n+1),

where E_n is the lambda^n discrepancy G-F(A_(n-1)). Every inverse matrix is polynomial. No numerical inversion or global inverse of F is required.

If G agrees with F modulo a source ideal J, the lifted A also agrees with identity modulo J: reduce (4.1) modulo J and use formal unramifiedness to compare A with identity, both reducing to identity modulo I. In particular retained first outputs and entire marked thickenings are preserved by these finite/formal source coordinates. Their marked behaviour supplies no new finite-order map class.

### Application to the scalar formal family and its stable polynomial boundary

Apply this to K and the marked or unmarked UASELFFORMAL1 family. All its finite-order polynomial Keller maps are source-coordinate equivalent to K over the corresponding nilpotent parameter base, and the formal family is formally source-coordinate equivalent to K. Thus it is NOT a new formal equivalence class. Its algebraization problem is a polynomial degree problem, not a failure of formal coordinate existence.

The source coordinates necessarily have UNBOUNDED source degree. They preserve sigma exactly. If their compatible formal series had a finite uniform degree in P, it would define a polynomial Keller map over k((lambda)) preserving sigma. UASIGFIELD1 would make its base action identity or the sign involution; reduction at lambda=0 chooses identity, contradicting the nonzero deformation. The same argument holds after adding identity coordinates. This gives an all-order degree-escape proof without computing higher Newton orders.

The same degree-escape argument applies to ANY nontrivial formal deformation of the compiler outputs retaining sigma, not only the scalar example. Purely vertical identity-coordinate changes are excluded from this assertion. Degree escape of the source lift alone does not prove a new polynomial left-right class: a target-coordinate deformation can also have an unbounded source lift. The next tangent test addresses that separate issue for the displayed family.

### Formal triviality allows arbitrary global degree jumps

For every d>=4 and ell>=1, the ACTUAL polynomial family with only native coefficient a_d=lambda*I(sigma)^ell is Keller by UAPROFILE1. UAALLATOM1 gives generic degree d over k(lambda), and degree3 at lambda=0; all nonzero specializations retain effective degree d. UAMARKEDDEF1 makes the restricted morphism over V(I(a)^ell) identical to the cubic for every lambda. Yet every nilpotent reduction and the full formal family are source-coordinate equivalent to K, by (4.1), with the source coordinates fixing that marked thickening and necessarily escaping every source-degree bound. Thus formal marked source equivalence is compatible with arbitrarily large changes in global generic degree and polynomial stable class. It is not an atomicity or global-degree obstruction. The scalar example remains different: no actual polynomial integration in its fixed line is available.

There is also a stable first-order obstruction to explaining this tangent by polynomial left-right coordinate families whose source maps normalize k(sigma), even with arbitrary target coordinate families. Weighted source scalings are output-recoverable for the cubic:

    K(eta*x,eta^(-1)*y,eta^(-2)*z)
       =(eta^(-2)*sigma,eta^(-1)*B0,eta*C0).              (4.2)

Identity-coordinate changes do not affect the compiler outputs to first order. Hence every compiler-output tangent from such source and target operations is a polynomial function of the old outputs and identities. For j=4 the actual tangent R_4*v_4 is NOT output-recoverable: the source points

    v0=(0,0,-1), v1=(-1/2,3,26)

have the same K output(-1,0,0), while their tangent outputs are respectively

    (0,0,0), (0,32/15,32/15).

The marked multiplier I(sigma)^ell is nonzero at this signal and preserves the witness. Thus the tangent escapes these polynomial signal-respecting coordinate orbits, while remaining formally trivial under source coordinates of unbounded degree. Arbitrary source coordinate changes that move the signal field are outside this obstruction. No unrestricted stable inequivalence or new atomic map is claimed.

## 5. Current constructive gate

All finite polynomial native lists reduce to the unique pair A,B. One affine coefficient line is closed by UASELFCTRL1, and all affine-r effective pairs are now closed by UAAFFINER1. The scalar rational denominator cannot be hidden in both outputs away from sigma*X=0. A genuine native polynomial integration of its tangent must change BOTH the scalar-line architecture and the affine-r restriction, or alter the first output/compiler/multi-block setting.

Formal or nilpotent data alone do not certify a whole polynomial equivalence, an atomic construction or a global generic-degree constraint: formal etaleness supplies coordinates at every such order, while actual retained-profile families can still have arbitrary degree jumps. A remaining candidate must establish an entire polynomial formula, the complete canonical marked scheme and effective gluing, and full-map absolute atomicity. Coupling a receiver by source/target coordinate changes alone preserves actual composition factors. Canonical absolute atomicity and improved costs remain open. No bounded coefficient or parameter-order sweep is scheduled in the closed sectors.
