# Remaining constructive directions

The nonzero-target pair family and both of its endpoint routes are now
settled by the [affine frame-limit obstruction](NONZERO_TARGET_LIMITS.md).
No meromorphic completion of that fixed pair family gives a finite map
at zero or infinity.

Constructive possibilities still outside the completed results include:

- finite-parameter support drops with transverse x-components;
- joint variation of a boundary collision pair and the full transverse basis;
- different interior collision families, including degenerations in which
  more parameters vary together;
- nonlinear coordinate changes or a different Keller family.

For a singular limit route, first test conjugation-invariant traces along
the collision axis and along images of points on that axis. A pole closes
the route before an expensive search for frames.

The strict lower-degree-profile question and the broader support minimum
remain distinct. Finite affine changes preserve the foundational map's
degree flag. A finite singular orbit limit could lower it, but the
specified nonzero-target one-parameter endpoint route cannot.
