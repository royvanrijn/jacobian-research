# Primary literature and the receiver boundary

14 November 2026. [Canonical proof](PROOF.md).

[Brion--Szamuely, Prime-to-p etale covers of algebraic groups and homogeneous spaces,2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.2802), Proposition1.1 and Theorem1.2, gives commutativity of the prime-to-p fundamental group for connected groups and connected-stabilizer homogeneous spaces. We read the v4 primary HTML and retain it. Its characteristic-zero case closes every finite connected-isotropy high-rank symmetric route. Section2 of our proof keeps the central extension for disconnected isotropy explicit, rather than applying the connected-stabilizer conclusion there. The prime-to-p condition concerns the Galois-cover order, not merely the original fibre degree.

[Merkurjev, Essential Dimension,2016](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~merkurev/papers/bulletin.pdf), Example2.3(2), Examples2.5--2.6 and Proposition2.7 on printed pages4--5, supplies the classical etale-algebra/torsor dictionary and versal quotient construction. Those pages were read, and page5 was visually inspected. We do not claim to have invented quotient versal torsors. Our fixed SL quotient and its full canonical left-action twist are given explicitly; Hilbert90 and the determinant sequence prove realization over finite fields too, without importing an infinite-field-only density assertion.

[Stacks66.14.2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/07S5) gives finite free-action quotient torsors. Its proof also identifies affine quotients via the invariant ring. Constant finite groups remain etale when their order is divisible by the characteristic; this construction does not use Reynolds averaging. [Stacks58.6.2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BND) supplies the finite-cover/fundamental-group equivalence and functoriality. The retained [Milne torus and Hilbert90 inputs](../universal-keller-torus-receivers/sources.json) include Hilbert90 over fields; the SL vanishing is its elementary determinant-sequence consequence.

The homogeneous central-kernel argument, alternating left-right action, sharp factorial component table and compiler-independent full-degree universal-monodromy implication are proved here as structural applications. No search proves that these formulations are unpublished or establishes priority. The primary Brion--Szamuely HTML's introductory GL(n) example is not used as an exact fundamental-group computation: pi1(GL_m) is nontrivial in characteristic zero. Our exact finite quotient computation uses SL_m and proves its simple connectedness separately.

This changes the next gate. Nonabelian full-degree canonical receivers are available as finite quotient PAIRS in all ranks, including absolutely atomic quartics. There is no reason to search for another finite torus or connected-isotropy construction in high rank. Their finite fundamental groups prevent source/target self-identification after affine identity stabilization. The original whole-affine-space programme still requires nonfinite boundary geometry of generic degree larger than the input rank; universality at the smaller rank does not automatically prove monodromy or atomicity of that larger-degree map.
