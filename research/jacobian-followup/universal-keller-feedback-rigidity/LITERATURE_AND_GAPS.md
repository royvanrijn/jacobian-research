# Literature and coupling boundaries

Reviewed15 November2026. [Proof](PROOF.md) and [source pins](sources.json) separate retained arguments from new deductions. No publication priority is claimed.

UAPROFILE1 and UAALLATOM1 already prove the one-block parameter-response Jacobian identity and all-degree symmetric monodromy. KRP1 already gives the Boolean interval and affine-open uniqueness for separated Keller products. The extension here allows every block coefficient to depend polynomially on ALL retained first outputs. The partial-processing formula constructs every polynomial intermediate model; field imprimitivity alone would not do that.

Primitivity of product-action wreath products is classical; for example [Barbieri and Potocnik, Vertex-primitive digraphs with large fixity](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10231-024-01447-x), Lemma11, recalls the standard criterion. Our arithmetic restriction-of-scalars theorem derives the entire intermediate interval from the explicit point stabilizer, rather than importing a classification theorem. The new application computes base-field stable factor length from the connected components of the input algebra. It explicitly distinguishes arithmetic and absolute atomicity.

The [Stacks Project smooth-morphism section](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01V4), composition and flatness lemmas, supplies the regular descent step for the cubic polynomial first-integral ring. The rational kernel follows directly from the inverse cubic, linear in the output direction. No local-nilpotence theorem is used or asserted.

Schur complements and determinant lemmas are classical. The new scalar-feedback deduction proves its loop response nonzero for every genuinely parameter-dependent polynomial coefficient profile, with no degree bound, and determines every polynomial control preserving Jacobian one. Each such control is recovered from two actual outputs, exposing a reverse-order polynomial factorization. Tests of one quartic would not prove that classification.

A primary-source search found product-action and Jacobian-derivation context, but did not establish novelty. The arbitrary-base trace receiver from the previous package remains constructed and non-atomic. An absolutely atomic canonical replacement still needs a coupling outside these two closed classes; a formal nilpotent feedback matrix is not an actual construction.
