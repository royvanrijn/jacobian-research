# Rank-one feedback, first integrals and geometric line fibres

Reviewed 15 November 2026. [Proof](PROOF.md), [source pins](sources.json). No novelty or independent whole-proof verification is claimed.

The retained UASCALARFEED1 proof treats one scalar control. The new response lemma uses arbitrary, unrelated coefficient directions. This closes ANY number of effective controls into the compiler when the auxiliary return is b(C) times one constant output vector, for the retained quadratic trace C. It does not claim all higher-rank return systems factor.

The [Stacks Project, Lemma 10.47.12](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0G33), gives the classical equivalence between geometric irreducibility and absence of separable algebraic constants. In characteristic zero, combined with the explicit nonzero one-variable derivation, it identifies the rational first-integral field with the relative algebraic closure. The [smooth-morphism section](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01V4), especially composition, flatness, openness and relative dimension, supplies regular descent for the polynomial first-integral ring. These general geometric facts are classical, not new discoveries.

[Jedrzejewicz and Zielinski, Analogs of Jacobian conditions for subrings](https://arxiv.org/html/1601.01508v1), Section 1, states the generalized assertion JC(m,n,k) in terms of rings of constants/algebraic closedness, recalls Nowicki's characterization and the proved one-generator case. Here this is denoted JC_sub to avoid confusion with ordinary JC(2). The new implication says a non-output-recoverable effective control for an auxiliary Keller map with one return direction would violate the particular JC_sub(m-1,m;k) closedness assertion. It does NOT establish that assertion in general, or infer failure of ordinary JC(2) from its failure. Section 2's square-free criterion must not be mistaken for a general algebraic-closedness theorem.

For the retained cubic no conjecture is needed: its inverse equation is linear along EVERY constant target direction, so the quotient's generic curve is rational and every polynomial first integral is output-recoverable. The old special-direction calculation is thus extended to arbitrary directions and arbitrary source-coordinate feedback profiles.

No recent AI-assisted theorem was needed for this extension. The literature connection is a concrete next gate: change the source selector beyond b(C), alter the return geometry beyond rank one, or prove actual failure of output-subring closedness for a proposed new auxiliary block. Neither a disconnected generic fibre nor an extra first integral alone certifies the full canonical fibre or absolute atomicity.

For arbitrary other source selectors the Schur identity yields a finite common-kernel problem for polynomial derivations, with configuration interpolation requirements. Signal-only selectors give actual forward factors even when the effective coefficients are not auxiliary first integrals. An exact nine-variable control verifies this boundary; it prevents extending the fixed b(C) classification to every beta(P). No separating common invariant outside the signals is constructed.
