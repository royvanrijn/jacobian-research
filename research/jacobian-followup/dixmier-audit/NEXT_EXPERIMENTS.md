# Dixmier experiments connected to our recent research

14 November 2026. [QFL1](../dixmier-formal-lifting/PROOF.md) settles
unrestricted formal lifting, and
[QFL2](../dixmier-formal-lifting/BOUNDED_REALIZATION.md) sharpens the
remaining construction gate: a common spatial degree bound for a full
formal lift of a noninvertible polynomial symplectic map would already
give a Weyl counterexample. Its h series need not terminate, and generic
nonsurjectivity follows automatically from the injective classical reduction.

[QVA1](../dixmier-formal-lifting/VALUATION_SYMBOLS_AND_GENERIC_GRAPH_ADMISSION.md)
sharpens the main gate to a finite projective generic rank-one full action
with Gauss-regular images and the intended intrinsic Long shadow. Its saturated
valuation ideal has derivative-stable gcd normalization, hence is principal;
the formal C-frame is automatic. Completed division intertwiners between
regular full graphs have no spatial poles. A nonprincipal initial ideal
excludes such a regular action in every degree; the interior charge-one model
fails, while moving-divisor QPF1 passes this formal test without a constructed
right action. Rational logarithmic full pairs also show finite generic
generation cannot be omitted.

[QPG1](../dixmier-formal-lifting/CENTRAL_PATCHING_AND_QUANTUM_GRAPH_JETS.md)
then patches the generic action to an integral finite carrier. Generic
rank-one freeness gives integral freeness and the scalar bounded lift.
Full formal comparisons reduce to a finite quantum jet, finite exact shears
and an inner unit. These conclusions do not construct the missing generic
Long action, bound a displayed formal series, preserve fixed-R intermediate
constraints or identify arithmetic centers.

[QUR1](../dixmier-formal-lifting/REPOLARIZATION_AND_UMBRELLA_RAMIFICATION.md)
excludes the single monic inverse-isogeny physical normalization at every
quantum parameter order, including arbitrary lower matrix mixing and broken
primitives. An exact ambient polarization makes its completed special module
a rank-two connection on the open Whitney umbrella. A regular-sequence
argument at a moving common zero replaces the unproved persistent-block
hypothesis; no further ramification-order tests are needed for this module.
[QFR1](../dixmier-formal-lifting/FIBRE_RESIDUES_AND_MATRIX_CUSP_OBSTRUCTION.md)

QUR1 does not automatically cover higher-multiplicity normalizations or added
summands. Its exact q-local and z-local full connections have poles. The
ordinary forward Long graph and QRA1 different-Poisson surface actions remain
unaffected. The main target is still a finite normalized corner for the correct
rank-one Long graph, or an intrinsic obstruction to every such corner.

[QRA1](../dixmier-formal-lifting/FINITE_MATRIX_ACTIONS_AND_PRIMITIVE_ADMISSION.md)

uniform degree<=2 companions of every size, and an elliptic energy
connection with all six localized physical surface relations. The
monic x,w model has a different global polynomial A2 action and an

action cannot rationally compress or supply extra commuting matrix
units. Keeping exact Y=XW across b=0 would force X inverse under
the physical Weyl relations, which its special norm -4b forbids.
A changed physical action must release that primitive. Positive
companions show why a quantum correction h*C can matter; a classical
factorization alone is no exclusion. Require the intended rank-one
Long graph and actual finite admission before claiming a counterexample.

[QTC1](../dixmier-formal-lifting/COMPOSITION_DUALITY_AND_BOUNDARY_OF_QUANTUM_GRAPHS.md)

A nilpotent negative-degree matrix can preserve a finite lattice;
bounded powers are incompatible with a Weyl mate, not with every
matrix action. Mixed source fields need separate evidence; arbitrary
formal gauges do not supply it. Delta1 cotangent controls remain allowed.

symbols. The charge-one example now has an exact all-degree graded
idealizer and no scalar Weyl pair with either Bernstein degree at most
two, regardless of the partner degree. Higher-degree pairs and general
rank-two actions remain outside that exclusion. Parameter-compatible
framing and a Long-compatible full right action remain necessary.

[QCL1](../dixmier-formal-lifting/SCALAR_ANOMALY_AND_CUBIC_LIFTS.md)
provides a direct bounded carrier: scalar normalized Moyal anomalies
can be removed by rational symplectic Gram--Schmidt without spatial
degree growth. Every cubic polynomial symplectic map qualifies, in
every Weyl rank. Seek a structural cubic representative retaining a
quadratic layer, or a scalar-anomaly tuple in arbitrary degree. A
symplectic map with one homogeneous nonlinear layer is always
invertible in every degree. Homogeneous cubic Keller counterexamples
also have cotangent degree at least five, so the existing cubic Keller
work does not automatically enter this positive cubic class. No
noninvertible cubic symplectic input or bounded Long lift is supplied.

[QCC1](../dixmier-formal-lifting/CUBIC_CORE_NONCOMPLETION.md)
closes the obvious cubic-core simplification. The pair R=x*(2-3*x*q),
D0=(1+3*x*q)*p/2-3*q^2*z has joint polynomial centralizer
K[U,V,W]/(U*W-V^3) with a Poisson character, so it has no full
polynomial completion in any degree or after adding spectator pairs.
The polynomial fiber shear removing Long's H is canonical exactly
when H belongs to K[R], which Long's correction does not. More
strongly D0 alone has a Poisson character on its entire centralizer,
so it cannot be any component of a full map. A cubic route must
therefore change D genuinely or retain corrected data. A
finite-dimensional polynomial eigenspace also gives a stable quantum
centralizer obstruction; the proof excludes a specified normal-order
family, not every ordering or the corrected Long operator.

[QCR1](../dixmier-formal-lifting/CENTRALIZER_NORMAL_FORM.md)
classifies the remaining conjugate ambiguity in every rank. With
classical R,S,T fixed, all fourth images are D_Long+P(R); every one
has degree at least23 and momentum order five. For an admitted
quantum triple with R undeformed, the full ambiguity is K[R][[h]],
and removing its explicit x^(2k)*q^k pivots never increases degree.
A bounded fourth image exists exactly when this normalized image is
bounded. Do not continue a classical H coefficient search for that
triple. Changing the quantum S,T or polarization remains available;
formal R-fixing is possible but is not a proved bounded normalization.

## Completed transport route and restricted support comparison

The first transport is completed as
[QRT1](../dixmier-residue-transport/PROOF.md), and
[QRT2](../dixmier-residue-transport/COMBINED_COCYCLE.md) now closes the
combined-space gate with a new16-term strong cocycle. Its period is one
on every compatible finite-X-Laurent lower lift in a category stable under
all cubic positional shears. The old -1296 lambda native failure belongs
to the earlier dual. The subsequent
[QRT3 uniform theorem](../dixmier-residue-transport/UNIFORM_POSITIONAL.md)
now covers positional Hamiltonians of arbitrary degree. Mixed-polarization
native categories remain separate; the experiment below is retained as
the motivation rather than a current positional degree-search queue.

The existing quantum work gives strong dual cocycles, while our newer
[two-divisor graph result](../hc4-triangular-graphs/TWO_DIVISOR_EXTENSION.md)
shows how boundary traces can replace a word-length search by a theorem
covering an infinite class. The proposed quantum analogue is to transport
the correction complex, its boundary lattice and its dual functional
together under exact quantized canonical changes.

Start with one generic admitted symbol and its known nonzero cocycle.
Choose one nontrivial elementary canonical shear with a known inverse and
exact Weyl lift. Transport both the images and the allowed corrections;
compute the induced map on the full six-relation complex and verify that
the transported functional has the same nonzero value. Then compare the
transported support with the native support. Any discrepancy is an explicit
missing correction direction, rather than a reason to claim an invariant.

The first useful result would cover every polynomial shear in one declared
direction, proved by a symbolic shear parameter and an all-degree support
argument. Survival for a few numerical parameters or support cutoffs is
only exploratory evidence. Eventually the target is an obstruction for a
declared orbit and correction category, stronger than a single-symbol
rank jump.

Direct reduction modulo the classical boundary ideals is insufficient:
for f=x(1+xy), the Weyl relation gives

\[
[p_x,f]=\hbar(1+2xy),
\]

which vanishes on neither proof divisor. Those ideals are not preserved
by the quantum differentiation. Use shifted valuation lattices, as already
constructed in QRF1, and explicitly test differential stability instead.

## Quadratic scalar operators: the all-partner theorem is complete

[QSO2](../dixmier-formal-lifting/QUADRATIC_OPERATOR_MATE_CLASSIFICATION.md)
completes the proposed all-order extension. It reduces every odd mate
of a quadratic operator to a forced first Painleve hierarchy. A universal
stationary-KdV and adjoint argument proves integer pole indicial roots;
Puiseux unramifiedness, Riemann--Hurwitz and infinity balance exclude
algebraic solutions with every choice of constant lower times. Every
remaining mate is A(P)+Q1 with constant-coefficient A and Q1 of order one.
Even partner orders do occur, so parity cannot be discarded.

The valued consequence, rather than an order-pair census, is the useful
Long application. Keep exact R and the common spatial support assumption.
If S stays quadratic, its leading coefficient is a valuation unit and
Long's T has odd order-three reduction. No finite generic order of T
can satisfy the string relation. A surviving bounded full lift in that
class must increase S itself; no bounded normalization fixes a general
deformed R, and no all-degree theorem yet excludes higher-order S.

The finite checker matches the retained Long polynomials to the scalar
formulas and checks declared GD and Ore identities. The universal theorem
is the written proof, without independent theorem replay or external
review. The arithmetic hypotheses in
[Kanel-Belov--Fedorov](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33919v1)
remain separate. For the hierarchy framework see
[Takasaki](https://arxiv.org/html/nlin/0610073v2) and the
[October 2026 revised correspondence paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.18433v2).

## A longer route is bounded arithmetic complexity

The October graph-lifting criterion suggests computing Berest–Wilson
invariants of actual plane graph-splitting modules as characteristic varies.
An initial implementation should recover the invariant from two independent
ideal presentations and normalize away the common factor of the associated
graded ideal. Automorphism examples provide controls, not evidence for
the difficult cases. Arbitrary rank-one ideals need not be graph modules;
higher-rank graph modules do not acquire this invariant by the same formula.

Our [new cusp-filtration rigidity](../jc2-cusp-filtration-rigidity/README.md)
could help control degrees once a compatible filtration exists. First
prove compatibility: its Poisson bracket has degree zero, so one cannot
assume it supplies a filtration in which commutators strictly lower degree.
Our [finite-target regularization result](../keller-solidity/PROOF.md)
also rules out trying to manufacture global module finiteness merely by
a fixed finite-order differential regularizer on a nonfinite source.
It supplies no noncommutative finiteness theorem automatically.

The concrete arithmetic target is a bound on presentations for a declared
graph-module family, uniform in characteristic under the paper's hypotheses.
Finite prime data can guide that proof but cannot replace it.
