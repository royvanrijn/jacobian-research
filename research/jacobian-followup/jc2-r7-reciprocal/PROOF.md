# The last reciprocal cubic gate and the complete single-ramified-prime exclusion

14 November 2026. PF2R7C3ALL1 excludes the remaining reciprocal cubic
contact architecture for every birational polynomial degree-(6,10),
gap-21 pair. PF2R7ROW1 consequently excludes the entire degree-six,
single ramified affine-prime (e,f)=(2,1), gap-21 stratum, retaining all
unramified omitted primes and using no source finality or Theorem3.8
conclusion. PF2R7RAM2 gives the resulting two-ramified-image frontier
without assuming that the first prime is the entire ramification divisor.

The geometry is a written proof with the named earlier boundary,
positivity and conic-field inputs. The algebra has exact determinant,
scheme-membership, univariate-unit and multivariate-power certificates.
No formalization, independent external review or novelty priority is
claimed. Ordinary JC2 and the other ramification patterns remain open.

This is an additional algebraic route to the scoped exclusion already
registered as [PF2OMIT5](../jc2-omitted-divisors/R7_INFINITY_COMPLETION.md).
The contact certificates do not use that theorem's infinity-cluster census
or limiting-line argument. Section7 recovers its second-image conclusion;
it does not replace the stronger residue and local-boundary information
subsequently established by PF2OMIT6 and PF2OMIT7.

## 1. The cubic base map and the correct conic space

PF2R7G1 proves that the remaining reciprocal configuration has a base
map on E^nu=P1 of degree three. Its value at t=infinity has local degree
two, and the other point in that fibre is one finite parameter h,
of local degree one. It satisfies

    ord_h(p')-ord_h(q')=2.

The remaining image is immersed by PF2R7QEX1, so q'(h)!=0 and p' has
an exact double root. The algebra below needs only p'(h)=p''(h)=0.

Apply a Mobius change of the pencil base that sends this fibre value
to infinity. The base function now has pole divisor 2*(infinity)+(h).
Affine normalization of its leading and constant terms therefore gives

    Z(t)=t^2+v*t+w/(t-h), w!=0.                       (1)

No target transformation is used here. Changing the base does not change
the general conics or their contacts. Every general conic passes through
the same target infinity point [0:1:0]. Its equation has no y^2 term:

    c0(Z)+c1(Z)*x+c2(Z)*y+c3(Z)*x^2+c4(Z)*x*y=0.     (2)

This five-dimensional conic space is crucial; we do not apply the earlier
six-column unrestricted-conic test to the cubic case.

## 2. Universal contact minors

Use the retained normalization

    p=t^6+a*t^4+b*t^3+c*t^2+d*t,
    q=[p^(5/3)+B*p^(4/3)+D*p^(2/3)+E*p^(1/3)]_+,
    rho1=rho3=rho5=rho7=rho9=0.

The parity gap identity forces b!=0 for a birational pair. The condition
rho11!=0 of gap21 is not needed by the following certificates. The
marked double-root equations are exactly

    c=-15*h^4-6*a*h^2-3*b*h,
    d=24*h^5+8*a*h^3+3*b*h^2.                        (3)

Let

    W(t)=(t-h)*(t^2+v*t-Z)+w.

For generic Z it is irreducible and separable over C(Z), since (1) has
degree three and w!=0. Incidence and tangency at the three conjugate
points imply W^2 divides the pullback of (2) in C(Z)[t]. Reduce the
columns 1,p,q,p^2,p*q modulo the monic W^2, in basis1,t,...,t^5.
Pivot the constant column in row zero. The five remaining rows, indexed
by their actual powers1,...,5, form a 5-by-4 matrix M. Every four-row
minor Delta_I of M must vanish identically in Z.

An exact universal coefficient, before (3), is

    [Z^13] Delta_(2,3,4,5)=-60*v^4.

Thus v=0. At v=0 and (3), another coefficient is

    [Z^9] Delta_(1,3,4,5)=40*h*(3*w-b)^4/27.          (4)

We separate h=0 and h!=0; neither case is discarded by dividing by h.

## 3. The centered double-root chart h=0

    3*B-5*a=0, 9*D-2=0, 27*E-4*a=0, a^2=0.        (5)

At complex points, (5) forces a=B=E=0,D=2/9. Consequently the only

    q=t^10+(5/3)*t^7+(7/9)*t^4+(7/81)*t.

For this pair and h=v=0, two coefficients of Delta_(1,2,3,4), at powers

## 4. The nonzero marked root h=1

When h!=0, scale the parameter by h and rescale the two target
coordinates to retain monic degrees (6,10). This puts h=1, retains b!=0,
and preserves the gap-21 equations and fixed-infinity conic space.
Equation (4) now forces w=b/3. Set

    c=-15-6*a-3*b, d=24+8*a+3*b,
    T=a*b-12*a-3*b-36.

The relevant leading contact coefficients factor as

    [Z^10] Delta_(1,2,3,4)=(8/729)*T*S,
    [Z^7]  Delta_(1,3,4,5)=(8/2187)*T*V.             (6)

Here S,V are polynomials affine-linear in B,D,E; they are reconstructed
exactly from these coefficient identities by the checker. We again
separate T=0 and T!=0, preserving every rank-degenerate specialization.

### 4.1 T=0: a univariate determinantal unit

At b=12, T=-72, impossible. Thus T=0 gives

    a=3*(b+12)/(b-12), b!=0,12.

The five gap equations and the next coefficient
[Z^9] Delta_(1,2,3,4) are affine-linear in B,D,E after this substitution.
Clear their denominators, which are only powers of b-12 and nonzero
rational constants, and normalize rational coefficient contents. Their
six rows form an augmented coefficient matrix A(b) with columns B,D,E,1.
A common solution would give a nonzero kernel vector (B,D,E,1), hence
every 4-by-4 minor would vanish. This necessity holds even where the
first three columns have rank below three; no generic inverse is used.

Four selected minors Pi_i(b), frozen in the certificate bundle, are
divisible by

    P(b)=b^12*(b-12)^13.

Their quotient polynomials F_i=Pi_i/P satisfy the literal identity

    sum_(i=1)^4 U_i(b)*F_i(b)=1.                     (7)

Since P is nonzero in this branch, all four Pi_i cannot vanish.
This excludes T=0 without a root search or an unproved rank assumption.

### 4.2 T!=0: a two-variable power certificate

Now (6) gives S=V=0. Use the five gap equations and S,V as seven
affine-linear equations in B,D,E. Their augmented coefficient matrix
A(a,b) again has columns B,D,E,1. Every four-row minor must vanish at a
solution. Normalize by nonzero rational contents and, in one selected
minor, divide by its factor b, which is nonzero in the whole chart.

For a simpler certificate coordinate put r=2*a+b+6, an invertible
affine change of the coefficient variables. Twelve selected normalized

    sum_(i=1)^12 Q_i(r,b)*G_i(r,b)=b^13.             (8)

The checker reconstructs all twelve minors, including the one division
by b and both rational content normalizations, before independently
replaying (8) by sparse Fraction multiplication. If the linear system
had a solution, its minors would vanish, forcing b=0, a contradiction.
The auxiliary inverse variable used in discovery is absent from (8)
and from the default replay. No unresolved denominator stratum remains.

Equations (7)--(8) exhaust h=1; Section3 exhausts h=0. Thus no pair in
the stated polynomial family admits the marked reciprocal cubic contact

## 5. Exactness of the computation

[contact.py](contact.py) uses monic remainders and exact nonnegative
grading to extract only determinant coefficients that can contribute:

    wt(t)=1, wt(Z)=2,
    wt(a,b,c,d,B,D,E,h,v,w)=(2,3,4,5,2,6,8,1,1,3).

The column weights are6,10,12,16. The coefficient of Z^k in row t^r
has weight column_weight-r-2*k. The target determinant coefficient
therefore has a fixed total weight, so an entry coefficient of larger
weight cannot contribute. Filtering precedes every inhomogeneous
specialization, such as h=1 or b=1. All parameter substitutions commute
with coefficient extraction because they do not involve Z.

The checker verifies the grading, compares a selected coefficient with
the unfiltered full matrix, reconstructs all certificate generators and
rank minors, and replays the four endpoint identities separately with
Fraction arithmetic. Mutations fail the respective replays. The genuine
lower-degree control p=-t^2,q=t^4+2*t, Z=t^2+1/t satisfies

    p*q+Z*q+Z*p^2+Z^2*p-1=-(t^3-Z*t+1)^2.

It is an actual three-contact conic family through the fixed infinity
point, outside the excluded target degrees. Thus the method does not
reject cubic contact architectures indiscriminately.

The preliminary unsplit six-variable elimination was capped. It is not

All final certificates are literal identities, not promotions of printed

## 7. A degree-six map containing this image needs a second ramified image

Drop the entire-single-prime assumption, retaining a degree-six Keller
map and a specified ramified prime E of generic (e,f)=(2,1) over a
birational polynomial degree-(6,10), gap-21 image C. PF2R7ROW1 forces
at least one more ramified prime.

That prime cannot have the same image C. The retained gap calculation
gives affine delta12. For an immersed normalization this forces a finite
value with at least two normalization branches. The two nonimmersed
classes also have such values by their explicit node lists. Let t1,t2
be two distinct normalization points of C over one finite target value y.

For each ramified prime over C, the map between affine normalizations is
finite and surjective. There is at least one point over each t_i. The
boundary theorem makes each omitted prime unibranch, so these are two
distinct points of that prime. Distinct omitted primes are disjoint in
the affine normalization. Two ramified primes over C would therefore
give at least four distinct omitted points over y. PF2OMIT2's local
length bound gives length at least two at each one, totaling at least8.
This contradicts the complete finite-flat degree-six fibre length.
Hence a second ramified target image C'!=C is necessary.

The all-prime Euler identity is

    5=sum_D e_D+sum_D E_D,

where E_D>=0 is the exceptional ambient fibre-length excess. Unramified
omitted primes have e1 and E_D0. Since two ramified primes already cost
at least four, there can be exactly two. If e' is the other index,
t the number of unramified omitted primes, and X the sum of the two
ramified excesses, the complete integer possibilities are

| e' | X | t |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 0 | 1 |
| 2 | 1 | 0 |
| 3 | 0 | 0 |

These are necessary ledgers, not realized maps. This argument by itself
does not determine residue degrees, the second image equation, special
lengths or infinity attachment. This is PF2R7RAM2, an alternative derivation
of PF2OMIT5's second **ramified** image gate. The current ledger goes further:
