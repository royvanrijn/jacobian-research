# Ruling descent and topology: primary inputs and limits

Reviewed15 November2026. [Proof](PROOF.md), [source pins](sources.json). No new classification theorem, new fundamental-group formalism or solution of JC(2) is claimed.

[Dubouloz, A1-cylinders over smooth A1-fibered surfaces, 2002.12838v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2002.12838v1), Proposition3 and Definition4, states the two needed standard facts: integral reduced fibres give a Zariski A1-bundle, and degenerate fibre reductions are disjoint affine lines. We import these facts explicitly. We do not use its cancellation theorem or incomplete proofs of more general algebraic-space structure.

[Dubouloz--Kishimoto, Families of affine ruled surfaces: existence of cylinders, 1408.1328v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1408.1328v1), introduction and Section1, emphasizes that an A1 ruling may require a finite field extension. That distinction is essential here: the whole-factor generic ring is a UFD over the retained field, not automatically over an extension. The new application proves that an ACTUALLY descended ruling has integral arithmetic fibres and reduced geometric fibres, and therefore a geometric plane atlas. It does not prove that every geometric ruling descends.

[Cogolludo-Agustin--Elduque, On the topology of fiber-type curves, 2306.07359v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.07359v3), published in2026, Section2.2, Definition2.8 and Remark2.11, supplies the standard surjection to the orbifold group of a connected-generic fibration. For base A1 this is a free product of the cyclic multiplicity groups. Combining it with the actual source pi1 gives the all-degree multiplicity-pattern and parity gates. The paper's curve-complement examples are not asserted to be our intermediate surfaces.

[Hatcher, Algebraic Topology, Chapter1](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATch1.pdf), Theorem1.20 and Proposition1.32, supplies van Kampen and the cover-degree/subgroup-index equality. We explicitly compute pi1 of the hyperbola complement and the dividing-index argument after deleting the nonfinite locus. Finite etale complex maps are proper local biholomorphisms and hence topological covers. The resulting finite-stage obstruction uses Euler characteristic1, and applies to arbitrary products of the actual leaf.

[Stacks tag05K0](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/05K0) supplies Zariski Main factorization through a finite completion; [tag031T](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/031T) supplies normal codimension-two extension. The source-completion boundary cannot consist only of finitely many points in the surface case. This does not contradict cofinite TARGET image.

The sheet-loss identities are standard constructible Euler integration, proved here by stratification and literal fibre-count addition. Pointwise nonnegative sheet loss does NOT force a nonnegative Euler integral. No new positivity theorem is used.

The strongest remaining logical gap is now ruling descent versus the JC(2) burden of a descended ruling. Full global polynomial realization, complete canonical marks, source landing, gluing and stable atomicity remain separate obligations. Product generic fibres and two-dimensional native witnesses are not assumed for an arbitrary changed-signal multi-block receiver.

The SAME Dubouloz--Kishimoto source, Section1.3.1 and Proposition4, supplies a positive descent criterion: a Galois-invariant ruling descends, and uniqueness is sufficient. Thus the unresolved escape requires a nontrivial Galois orbit of inequivalent rulings if JC(2) holds. This special descent theorem is imported, not claimed as a new general result.
