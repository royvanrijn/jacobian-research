## 1. Exact order of a Seifert regular fibre

Let W be an oriented closed Seifert manifold with orientable base
of any genus, normalized rational Euler number epsilon!=0, and
cone orders alpha_1,...,alpha_j. Include an order1 pair if needed
to encode the integer section term. Put Lambda=lcm(alpha_i).
In the abelian presentation of PF70 the cone and global relations
are

    alpha_i*x_i+beta_i*H=0,  sum_i x_i=0,
    epsilon=-sum_i beta_i/alpha_i.

Handle generators are independent. An integer combination producing
only a multiple of H must cancel the x_i coefficients. Hence the
coefficients of the cone relations must be C/alpha_i for a COMMON
integer C divisible by all alpha_i. Conversely C=Lambda gives such
a combination. Its H coefficient is -Lambda*epsilon. Therefore

    order([H])=|Lambda*epsilon|.                            (2)

This is an EXACT order, not just a determinant divisor. In particular
|Lambda*epsilon| is an integer. The usual genus0 determinant is

    |H1(W)|=|epsilon|*product_i alpha_i.                     (3)

At each prime ell, if at most two cone orders are divisible by ell,
the genus0 group is cyclic at ell. If none are divisible, all cone
variables eliminate in favour of H, leaving at most one generator.
If one or two are divisible, a primitive beta relation kills H mod
ell, and the global relation removes one of the remaining cone
generators. Thus if this cone-count condition holds at every prime,
the finite H1(W) is CYCLIC. This is a full-group criterion, not
an inference from its determinant alone.

The explicit Seifert presentation is the audited Cochran--Tanner
model of PF70. For covers we use Neumann--Raymond Theorem1.2,
complete PDF3-4/printed165-166: under an oriented fibre-preserving
map with base degree B and generic fibre degree F,

    epsilon(source)=(B/F)*epsilon(target).                  (4)

This includes branching transverse to the fibres. The statement
and its circle-bundle rationalization proof were rechecked on both
complete pages. No classification or linking-form identification
is imported. Absolute values in (2)-(3) avoid an orientation issue.
For the positive T(e,o) fibration of S3, epsilon=-1/(eo), as in PF66.

## 2. An integral transfer lemma for a filled cable piece

Suppose a connected filled cable piece P is orientably Seifert over
D with two cone orders u,v, where u divides v. Assume it maps to
the target companion solid torus V, whose H1 is generated by L.
Let its ACTUAL boundary basis be (m,l), with pushforwards

    m -> 0, l -> L, h -> v*L,
    h=A*m+v*l in H1(boundary P), gcd(A,u)=1.                 (5)

Here h is its regular fibre. These are explicit peripheral hypotheses,
not consequences of abstract group order or a cyclic-image assumption.

The Seifert presentation gives H1(P)=Z direct-sum Z/u. Its order-v
cone relation makes h divisible by v: gcd(beta,v)=1 provides the
integer Bezout combination. Choose any element pushing to L. The
kernel of pushforward is torsion, since l makes its free map onto.
As v kills that torsion, divisibility and pushforward give h=v*l.
Comparing with (5) gives A*m=0. The class m is torsion of order
dividing u, so coprimality forces m=0. Thus, INTEGRALLY,

    H1(P)=Z*l direct-sum Z/u, m=0, h=v*l.                   (6)

All peripheral coordinates in the torsion summand are zero. On
gluing P to another piece, it therefore has the homological effect
of filling along m, plus a direct summand Z/u. The marked regular
fibre maps to v*l in the retained part, with NO added torsion mark.
No homeomorphism, oriented linking-form splitting or arbitrary
noncyclic homology-torus replacement is asserted.

Degree1 fixed-sheet cable pieces, after meridian filling, are the
target solid torus itself: m=M is zero and l=L generates H1. Thus
they perform the other meridian fillings with no torsion addition.
This retains EVERY fixed sheet in the gluing.

The lemma is valid at arbitrary covering degree when (5) and the
two-cone hypotheses hold. It recovers PF71's specific S4/S4 transfer
with (u,v)=(r/3,r), (m,l)=(M^4,H_companion). It does not apply
to a piece with two remaining boundary tori.

## 3. A4/S3: all representations and all integer lifts

Retain PF63's actual primitive-binomial two-stage hypotheses and
PF60's preferred longitude identity. Put

    c=eo-e-o, k=rc+1, H=a^e=b^o, L=H M^(-eo),
    h=M^k L^r, beta=M^(-c)L^(-1), mu=z beta.

The preferred longitude is h*mu^(-rk). Since rk is even, after
the ramified mu^2 filling its class is h. The meridian-cycle
fillings are primitive sections of the lifted bases when fibre
monodromy is identity; they cap base boundaries without creating
additional exceptional fibres. This applies to both the cable
and companion fillings used in the next two sections.

In A4/S3 the companion is transitive on four sheets: a is a double
transposition, b is a 3-cycle with a fixed sheet. Its central H has
identity monodromy. M is a 3-cycle with one fixed sheet; L=1 in
monodromy because 3|eo. The cable action has a three-sheet S3 orbit
and one fixed sheet. Its root has type (2,1) on that orbit, so r
is even; h=1 also forces 3|k. These cycle types exhaust the sector
by PF63, including exchanging the first exponents.

After all branch meridian-cycle fillings, the three-sheet cable
piece has base D with cone orders r/2,r. The actual companion
boundary basis is m=M^3,l=L, and

    h=(k/3)*m+r*l, gcd(k/3,r/2)=1.

Both gcd and integrality follow from 3|k and gcd(k,r)=1. Apply
Section2: m=0, h=rL, and the added torsion is Z/(r/2), with no
peripheral or marked torsion coordinates. The fixed-sheet cable
piece fills the other companion meridian M.

Hence the full end homology is that of the companion cover after
ALL its meridian-cycle fillings, call it W, plus Z/(r/2). Its
base is S2 with cone orders

    e/2,e/2,o/3,o.

The generic fibre degree is1, since H has identity monodromy; the

    [K_R]=r[H]=0, since r is EVEN.                          (7)

The cone orders from opposite first exponents have disjoint prime
supports, so at most two are divisible by any prime. By Section1,
H1(W) is cyclic; (3) gives its order eo/3. This proves the first
line of (1), for ALL representations and unrestricted actual e,o,r.

For an actual quartic source, PF65 gives zero R mark => cyclic H1.

    gcd(eo/3,r/2)=1, in particular r=2 mod4.                 (8)

## 4. S4/C2: all representations and all integer lifts
