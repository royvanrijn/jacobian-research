# The quartic ruled completion research goal

Status: active. Set 14 November 2026 at the user's request.

Determine the realizability of finite degree-four maps on the forced
compatible F1 completion, beginning with a complete classification or
exclusion of the full pole-order-two layer over the allowed contact-four
boundary family. Prove stronger proximity, differential, ramification and
finiteness constraints that extend to higher pole orders, and use them to
advance the actual ruled-compatibility gate for the surviving quartic
finite-normalization packets.

The full objective includes the finite map and the marked normalization.
An abstract affine-plane complement, a linear coefficient kernel or a
finite list of numerical base-point counts does not establish realizability.
