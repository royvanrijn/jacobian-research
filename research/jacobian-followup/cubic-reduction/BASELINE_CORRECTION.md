# Baseline correction: twelve variables were already verified

The 17-variable and then 13-variable cubic experiments in this directory
were initially described as dimension improvements. That comparison used
the posted 19-variable example and missed two established repository
results:

- BCR2 already verifies a twelve-variable degree-three map.
- BCRDB1 independently verifies de Bondt's twelve-variable, five-cubic
  construction. Its displayed nonlinear count is40.

Both records predate the experiments. They are available in
[the archived twelve-variable proof](../../archive/non-elliptic/verified/TWELVE_VARIABLE_DEGREE_THREE_KELLER_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md)
and [the de Bondt audit](../de-bondt-2610.24889/README.md).
The 13-variable,42-term map is therefore dominated in both dimension and
term count by the existing 12-variable,40-term map. The 17-variable,58-term
map is dominated as well.

Their polynomial identities and validation receipts remain valid. Their
existence theorems KCUB1 and KCUB2 are retained as historical method
experiments, with replacement links in the mathematical ledger. They
supply no improvement to the known dimension bound and are not the
current optimization baseline.

The current contribution is instead the
[36-term integral conjugate of BCRDB1](PROOF12.md), which retains dimension
twelve and reduces the displayed nonlinear count40 to36. It has no
optimality or priority claim.

## Retrieval failure and prevention

A keyword search for “cubic counterexample” did not substitute for reading
the preserved “degree-three” and “economic stabilization” records.
Moreover the central research search currently fails on unrelated missing
metadata sources; that failure must not be treated as evidence that a
result is absent.

Before claiming an improvement, read the direct mathematical-status
entries BCR2, BCRDB1 and BCRDBGRAPH1, their canonical sources, and the
current dimension/form table. Compare identical notions: ordinary degree,
homogeneous cubic form, unipotence, cubic-linearity, tangent normalization
and collision normalization are different constraints.

Reopen the dominated13/17 routes only if a new property escapes the
twelve-variable baseline, or if further work reaches fewer than twelve
variables or improves the current36-term target under comparable
normalization. Repeating their earlier bounded searches cannot improve
the retained size bounds.
