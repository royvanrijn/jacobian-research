# Historical first-pass normal-chart discovery

Not a theorem input. The(2,-1,-1) charts and the mixed(1,-2,-2) charts
were retained correctly, but the first quartic-first(1,-2,-2) list was
not derived from the correct power law. Its correct relation is
Q^2*S^5=constant*U^4, forcing Q=q*L,S=s*L^2,U=t*L^3.
The three nonparallel first-pass quartic charts are not on that leading
locus. Their unit ideals do not establish coverage of the actual class.

The corrected two line charts and all other required charts are rebuilt
under [the v2 directory](../equal-negative-smallweights-v2/), with an
explicit top-coefficient-zero assertion and independent reconstruction.
The original17 models, Singular scripts, logs, certificates and checkpoint
remain as discovery history. No exclusion is inferred from this first pass.
