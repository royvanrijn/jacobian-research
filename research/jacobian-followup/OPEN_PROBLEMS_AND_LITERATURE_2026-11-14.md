# Keller maps JC2 and HC4 research priorities

As of 14 November 2026, the largest surviving problems concern global finite normalization, the affine source, and compatibility of several boundary images. Local classifications, formal solutions and numerical boundary budgets have advanced much further than global realization.

The [mathematical ledger](../MATH_STATUS.json) controls claim status. The [13 November review](RECENT_DEVELOPMENTS_2026-11-13.md) and its 48-record catalogue provide the previous literature baseline. This review incorporates the later local results and a targeted primary-source search through 14 November. New external approaches below are research proposals; they are not imported theorems or new exclusions.

## Largest open problems

### The F2 degree frontier and its complete coefficient system

The conditional larger-coordinate frontier remains 125, with (75,125) the first retained F2 degree pair. Coordinate degrees and geometric degree, the number of generic sheets, are different quantities. The archived [next-degree programme](../archive/non-elliptic/plane-jc/NEXT_DEGREE_FRONTIER.md) records thirteen candidate pairs through maximum coordinate degree 150; this is a regression against the published tables, not a new exhaustive implementation.

The F2 work gives carrier classifications, nonlinear necessary equations, exact boundary packets and logarithmic budgets. The newer [contact calculation](jc2-cusp-contact/README.md) classifies eight highest contact values and reduces them by affine target normalization to five: 507, 520, 585, 715 and 910. It also gives a finite flat degree-seven carrier interface. Under its stated hypotheses the mixed-volume bounds are geometric degree at most 2019, improved to 1347 on contact 507. These replace the loose 9375 bound on those scopes.

What is missing is a complete polynomial Keller pair or a uniform contradiction on its remaining nonlinear source coefficients and global attachments. Early resonant freedoms also occur in commuting, Jacobian-zero controls. A necessary contact lower bound does not give the missing upper bound. The adapted-quintic Euler test has a genuine exceptional monomial E8 endpoint; applying it globally requires the target-transport hypotheses.

The sharp next target is the contact-507 source-band continuation with every exceptional carrier chart retained, followed by the complete Jacobian and attachment equations. A closure here would exclude a named first degree-frontier family, not every degree-125 or larger possibility. Missing historical circuit artifacts must remain recorded as missing.

### The residual r7 cover with several omitted images

The [omitted-divisor programme](jc2-omitted-divisors/README.md) now fixes local lengths, residue degrees, singleton packets and extensive source-fibre constraints. The whole degree-six gap-21 stratum with exactly one ramified omitted prime of index two and residue degree one is excluded under its explicit hypotheses. Earlier reciprocal and quartic-contact continuations inside that stratum are therefore superseded.

The surviving configurations require additional ramification. The first image has two explicit one-parameter coefficient families, with complete affine singularity catalogues. Residual covers have S6 monodromy and primitive degree-six source fields. The [complement comparison](jc2-r7-complement/README.md) gives at most 92 first-image complement types, including the two nodal opens. This bound does not classify the complement of the union with the unknown additional images.

Actual second-image B and optional unramified-image H equations, joint braids, retained residue covers, infinity attachments and a normalized source identified with A2 remain missing. Identity inertia can still accompany an omitted unramified sheet. The [input audit](jc2-omitted-divisors/INPUT_GATE.md) specifies what an actual inverse polynomial or finite normal algebra must certify.

The [Nori bound](jc2-r7-global-cover/README.md), under its quintic and incidence hypotheses, requires infinity contact plus twice finite tangency weight at least 34. The [elliptic contact families](jc2-r7-elliptic-contact/README.md) contain exact contact-34 equality witnesses, so that inequality alone cannot exclude them. The [central-fibre calculation](jc2-r7-central-fibre/README.md) forces an infinity Milnor jump at least 46 and strong genus requirements in its clean strata.

The valuable next theorem is a joint cover obstruction for C union B union H, including marked infinity and omission data. Alternatively, a source-boundary reconstruction could contradict the mandatory Milnor and genus budgets. A generic first-image braid or a sign double cover cannot supply either theorem.

### Finite normalization and recognition of the actual affine source

The archived [quartic Cox programme](../archive/non-elliptic/plane-jc/JC2_GLOBAL_COX_PACKET_ATTACK.md) classified local conductor and gluing data but found that connector number, minimized marked height and contact are unbounded in the abstract decorated packets. A finite search needs new bounds from an actual finite cover.

The [ruled-completion follow-up](affine-complements/README.md) conditionally forces F1 and identifies one marked Wright ring inside C[X,Y]. Every target presentation has pole height at least nine. The first unexcluded reduced pairs are (5,9), (6,9), (7,9) and (8,9). In the pole-(6,9) slice, the noncontact possibilities have reduced to partitions 7+2 and 5+4, with sixteen exact marked leading candidates; contact cases also remain.

These candidates pass necessary leading tests. The newer [complete first cusp-jet calculation](affine-complements/FULL_CUSP_FIRST_JET_LIFTS.md), PF2RULED22, shows that all sixteen also lift through every first-jet equation: each has an affine 116-dimensional solution space with a clean ordinary singleton cusp on a nonempty open locus. This removes no candidates. The uneliminated next coefficient gives at most 53 quadratic Jacobian equations in 116 parameters. The latest refinement separates 46 boundary parameters, 34 independent second-coefficient controls and 36 directions invisible at this order. On clean nonzero-minor charts, the next gate is eight immutable highest coefficients followed by eleven image-membership conditions for a 45-by-34 matrix: at most 19 rational consistency conditions in the 46 boundary parameters. Their independence and consistency are unknown. Higher coefficients must recover all 36 invisible directions; field degree four, finiteness and an actual compatible embedding remain necessary. The compatible ruled completion is not established for every arbitrary normalization.

The auxiliary cubic covers have rational normalization components, so rationality or leading-period exactness supplies no further exclusion by itself. An actual-ring control passes the clean cusp test but fails the Keller identity.

This gives two distinct goals: close the concrete height-nine systems, and prove a global recognition or boundedness theorem covering the original normalization packets. Success on the sixteen leading candidates alone would not settle the latter.

### Arbitrary-degree HC4 and the singleton-zero properness problem

Tang's degree-preserving structural alternative is reproduced locally as HCTANG2, by a written geometric audit supported by exact identities. In its noninvertible alternative a constant linear change gives a potential
f=R(x,y)+z A(x,y)+w B(x,y), with (A,B) a plane Keller map. This establishes HC4 equivalent to JC2 at that reproduced-source tier.

The [quantitative application](tang-hc4-audit/APPLICATIONS.md) proves HC4 through potential degree 101. The [degree-five audit](hc4-degree-five-audit/README.md) independently records reproduction of Giraudin's narrower theorem with 276 symbolic checks and written normalization coverage. General HC4 remains open.

The [standard polynomial graph](hc4-polynomial-graphs/README.md) and [specified triangular dual substitutions](hc4-triangular-graphs/README.md) no longer provide collision routes in any degree. Further mixed Schur constructions must escape those hypotheses and preserve the exact gradient-covector collision condition.

The [relative row orbit](hc4-relative-row-orbit/README.md) isolates another substantial question: prove properness at the singleton zero of its plane Keller output. Ideal equality and a singleton affine fibre do not establish completeness of the finite-normalization fibre. The [pencil controls](hc4-pointed-pencil/README.md) realize arbitrary genus with a fixed nonconstant Jacobian and prevent deducing rationality from row operations alone.

A properness theorem or a criterion forcing polynomial Legendre termination would be meaningful progress. Another graph-degree increment inside an excluded chart would not advance this objective.

### Intrinsic Keller moduli and presentation-independent construction

The broader Keller work realizes finite etale algebras as fibres and constructs substantial families. However, the ledger's OP-KDESC, OP-KMOD, OP-KCOMP and OP-KMON remain open.

The [Tschirnhaus obstruction](../archive/non-elliptic/verified/GENERIC_TSCHIRNHAUS_NON_DESCENT.md) shows that the existing clean quadratic-gauge compiler does not descend through arbitrary primitive presentations for rank at least five. Its boundary varies intrinsically; identity stabilization does not repair that compiler. It leaves open a different invariant compiler, a correspondence or a torsor-valued replacement.

The ambient stable-map stack, its components and generic monodromy, absolute proper-simple action realizations, and global degree or dimension minima remain unclassified. More examples inside a known family do not determine these objects. These are major structural problems, but less immediate routes to JC2 than the explicit F2 and r7 gates.

### Minimal boundary classification of three dimensional Keller maps

The [minimal-boundary programme](../archive/non-elliptic/cancellation/MINIMAL_BOUNDARY_CLASSIFICATION.md),
OP-SUSP, seeks a structural classification beyond the examples already constructed. Conditional chart theorems reduce an eligible marked plane core to a weighted or cancellation suspension. Geometric degree three has a common model, and boundary-minimality removes phantom unramified target divisors in the stated cubic scope.

The unproved step is that every numerically minimal map supplies the required intrinsic witness with all eight predicates. A second route needs boundary geometry to force the local Cohen--Macaulay/S2 or Cartier-different conditions. The surviving non-squarefree symbol and arbitrary dominant source-base-change cases remain open. An abstract minimality score is not the missing geometric witness. This is a large classification problem even if JC2 is eventually resolved.

### Global low degree minima for three dimensional collisions

The [support census](../archive/non-elliptic/extended-geometry/GLOBAL_LOW_DEGREE_SUPPORT_CENSUS.md),
OP-GLDC, investigates all 74 affine-invariant degree flags below (7,6,4), without a support bound. The [complete eight-term follow-up](sparse-eight/PROOF.md), GLDC3, now excludes at most eight nonlinear monomial occurrences in the normalized raw-degree-at-most-seven frame. All 17,482 size-eight residual support orbits are unit ideals over Q in Singular and SymPy, with independently checked exact coverage and coefficient inputs. Every normalized collision in that range therefore needs at least nine terms; attainment at nine remains unknown. The [coordinate-pairing reduction](sparse-eight/COORDINATE_GATES.md), KPAIR1, separately excludes every degree-at-most-ten three-variable collision admitting a constant nonzero Jacobian pairing, by reduction to Moh's plane degree-100 range. The local dense proof excludes four flags with maximum degree at most two. An unstructured enumeration of the 105-digit support universe is not a useful next calculation.

Wang--Yan's external quartic theorem could conditionally exclude every flag with maximum degree at most four. There are 20 such flags, hence 16 additional dense exclusions and 54 remaining flags if its proof passes the required local audit. An affine target change to a degree-adapted output basis preserves the nonzero constant Jacobian, which justifies applying their ordinary-degree theorem to these invariant flags. The [exact application dictionary](literature-2026-11-14/application-gates.json) records the count. The local ledger has not promoted these conditional exclusions.

### A smaller tractable target in the explicit Keller families

F4 and F6 have complete local complex fibre classifications. F7 is surjective, has root multiplicity at most five, and its quadruple-root strata are now complete. Exactly 912 normalized targets there have six-point fibres and 118 have seven-point fibres; the three-point pattern (4,3,2) is excluded.

Only (3,2,2,2) remains for global three-point F7 existence, outside the completed triple-root pole charts. See the [complete quadruple result](gao-f7-quadruple/README.md) and [triple-root result](gao-f7-triple-doubles/README.md). This is a promising bounded algebraic problem, but its closure would not prove JC2. Full F7 strata and F5 classification remain separate.

## Literature that changes the research choices

### Tang and Giraudin already supply substantial HC4 progress

[Tang, 6 November, version 1](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00156v1) gives the HC4 and JC2 equivalence through a structural normal form. Its provenance describes AI-assisted research and review. [Giraudin, 6 November, version 1](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00161v1) treats every degree-at-most-five potential using weighted determinant layers, Schur complements, partial Legendre transforms and exact algebra.

Both already have substantial repository follow-ups. Their current primary pages still expose version 1. Giraudin explicitly labels the manuscript unrefereed; Tang's page says an additional review is planned. The immediate opportunity is scrutiny of the geometric coverage and reuse of its normal form, rather than treating either statement as a new unexamined suggestion.

### Wang and Yan could remove a substantial low degree census block

[Wang and Yan, 27 October, version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.36495v1) state invertibility for arbitrary three-variable Keller maps of ordinary total degree at most four. Theorem1.1 imposes no nilpotence assumption. Its invertibility proof uses leading forms, conic-pencil invariant rings, residue periods and bounded-degree plane reduction; tameness is a separate conclusion.

This is an immediate audit target for OP-GLDC: if accepted, it expands dense exclusions from four degree flags to twenty. It does not exclude geometric degree four, degree-five-through-seven maps or support-seven collisions in those higher degrees. The theorem and selected proof interfaces were inspected; its full proof was not independently reproduced.

### Projective bundle recognition already underlies the quartic route

[Van Dobben de Bruyn, revised 14 September, version 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27341v2) characterizes affine-space complements under projective-bundle and fibre-hyperplane hypotheses. Version2 corrects the main statement and proof. The local affine-complement work already uses this direction. Proving the actual normalization has a compatible completion remains essential; arbitrary surface blowups do not automatically meet the theorem's hypotheses.

### Fonollosa supplies the right normalization model

[Fonollosa, 27 September, version 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33491v1) constructs the smooth finite normalization of marked-root maps and identifies every omitted divisor, including unramified ones.

The transferable mechanism is to compute source-coordinate regularity ideals and their height-one support separately from the ramification divisor. Our omitted-divisor work has already adopted that distinction. A new r7 proposal should supply the full algebra and source open, not merely a discriminant. The source examples are higher dimensional; they do not furnish the missing plane realization.

### Suciu provides an additional method for the joint braid problem

[Suciu, 8 November, version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.11092v1) develops explicit Hurwitz factorizations, meridian-sensitive group comparisons and Alexander or characteristic-variety obstructions to algebraicity. Its degree-twelve twisted example is not isotopic to an algebraic curve. The acknowledgments report AI assistance with exact braid and presentation checks, followed by author verification.

The proposed transfer is to keep component labels and the infinity relation in a C/B/H factorization, then test the required S6 representations and algebraicity restrictions. Its centrality statement requires the appropriate Hurwitz/full-twist setting; the existing singular line at infinity cannot silently be treated as transverse.

This source was not in the 48-record review. It gives methods to audit realization, not an existing exclusion of our configurations.

### Certified braid computation can complement the topology

The current [Sage Zariski–Van Kampen documentation](https://doc.sagemath.org/html/en/reference/curves/sage/schemes/curves/zariski_vankampen.html) supports number fields with a chosen complex embedding and uses SIROCCO. Its arrangement interface retains component labels.

A useful implementation target is a checkable set of braid relations and allowed S6 images, before expensive simplification of the full group. Transport across the parameter strata must still be proved. [LA-MOKA, 31 July](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29333v1) offers a certified discretization approach for line arrangements; applying it to our nonlinear curves would require an extension.

### Affine cancellation suggests a conditional derivation obstruction

The AI-generated [complex cancellation manuscript](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints/An-explicit-failure-of-complex-affine-space-cancellation-September-23-2026) constructs an exact valuation degeneration, lifts additive actions through a principalizing line bundle, and uses polynomial abc and a nonsquare obstruction to restrict locally nilpotent derivations.

This suggests testing derivations compatible with a proposed Wright source inclusion or a decorated normalization. The missing step is an extension or filtration theorem for those actual rings. Additive actions of the source A2 do not automatically extend across its omitted boundary. A normalization is not required to be A2, so proving it nonpolynomial would not exclude a Keller source open.

The [collection's README](https://github.com/openai/math) describes mixed verification stages. This review inspected the construction's introduction and rigidity section, not its full proof or listed Lean development. Its global claim is not imported.

### Logarithmic additivity has restrictive application hypotheses

The AI-generated [reverse logarithmic Kodaira manuscript](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/main/preprints/The-reverse-logarithmic-Kodaira-inequality-and-additivity-September-26-2026) states an upper inequality for smooth projective reduced-SNC pairs with connected fibres and every boundary stratum smooth over the open base.

Its possible use is to organize the image pencil after a compatible compactification and control of bifurcation fibres. Removing those fibres changes the source open and its logarithmic Kodaira dimension. Comparing the altered fibration with the unaltered A2 value would be invalid. The introduction and precise hypotheses were inspected; the proof and formalization were not audited. This is a secondary lead.

### The public JC2 campaign has the identical Wright carrier

The [pinned campaign research map](https://github.com/dcposch/jc2/blob/db01d6034600ce8e85ed706db2d4e8fa4359f4fe/APPROACHES.md) asks about constant-Jacobian pairs in T=C[U,UV,UV^2,UV^3-V]. The source change U=-Y,V=X has Jacobian one and sends these generators to the negatives of Y,XY,X^2Y,X^3Y+X. Thus T is exactly our embedded Wright ring in these coordinates; this is not merely an abstract surface resemblance.

Their [carrier-pencil exclusion](https://github.com/dcposch/jc2/blob/db01d6034600ce8e85ed706db2d4e8fa4359f4fe/xmodel/carrier-pencil-review-integration-swarmHQ-sol56-root-20261016T063000Z.md) rules out constant-direction repairs of one finite-map control in this ring. Their [moment countercontrol](https://github.com/dcposch/jc2/blob/db01d6034600ce8e85ed706db2d4e8fa4359f4fe/xmodel/finite-moment-review-integration-swarmHQ-sol56-root-20261016T010647Z.md) satisfies all specified pure moments but has a nonconstant Jacobian. These directly relevant prior boundaries should be checked before proposing the same repairs. They do not settle arbitrary pairs or the finite quartic slice.

The ring translation is verified in the [application dictionary](literature-2026-11-14/application-gates.json). It does not transport every completion's pole filtration or establish every F10 hypothesis. The pinned reports describe manual AI cross-review; no formal or external human verification is inferred.

### A single target linear functional is a concrete properness target

The campaign's [nonzero-dual criterion](https://github.com/dcposch/jc2/blob/db01d6034600ce8e85ed706db2d4e8fa4359f4fe/xmodel/nonzero-dual-first-integration-swarmHQ-root-20260923T155000Z.md) says that for a complex plane Keller map, A=C[P,Q] inside R=C[x,y], a nonzero A-linear map R to A exists exactly when the map is an automorphism. No normalization or multiplicativity is required.

Its proof passes through a trace multiplier, a nonzero ideal stable under source derivatives, and integrality from Newton identities. This sharpens the output required from a prospective boundary argument. The hard step remains constructing one multiplier valid on every source polynomial. Finite moments, a formal inverse or a field-valued trace do not provide it. The full elementary proof and its scope were inspected; no universal functional was constructed or theorem registered locally.

### Pullback primitivity and projectivity offer conditional boundary tests

The [three-double-fibre criterion](https://github.com/dcposch/jc2/blob/db01d6034600ce8e85ed706db2d4e8fa4359f4fe/xmodel/three-double-fiber-review-swarmHQ-sol56-root-20261020T172346Z.md) preserves rational-pencil primitivity under Keller pullback if there are at most three nonreduced fibre divisors and every component multiplicity is at most two. A candidate must supply the target-primitive pencil and its complete multiplicity data. The existence of our image pencil alone supplies neither.

The [divisorial projectivity report](https://github.com/dcposch/jc2/blob/db01d6034600ce8e85ed706db2d4e8fa4359f4fe/xmodel/divisorial-projectivity-swarmHQ-root-astra-20261025.md) algebraizes a specified compactification and finite iterates under an actual divisorial package. This could organize global attachments, but the package and valuation branch are prerequisites. The report explicitly has no uniform Cartier-index bound; its header retains an unpromoted-public-artifact status. The campaign overview's broader review label does not replace that source scope.

### Legendre membership gives a precise algebraic endpoint

[Adamus and Hajto, 27 October, version 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36736v1) characterize gradient invertibility by membership of g=<X,gradient h>-h in the gradient subring, equivalently termination of a Nambu derivative tower.

This is a useful output certificate for a candidate structural argument. Their paper also notes that doubling supplies no independent tower conditions, and Pascal finiteness is not necessary for all four-variable gradient automorphisms. A method must prove the actual membership or termination criterion it invokes.

### Component geometry and quantization remain conditional alternatives

[Jelonek, revised 25 October, version 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20597v2) proves closedness of bounded-degree Jacobian-one automorphisms. It could organize actual coefficient schemes, but a boundary-image family is not itself such a scheme. One braid sample does not settle its other strata.

[Kanel-Belov and Fedorov, 24 October, version 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33919v1) relate JC2 to a generalized first-Weyl Dixmier assertion. The converse needs characteristic-zero graph-module lifts, or their stated uniform Berest–Wilson boundedness condition. Their uniqueness theorem does not supply existence. This offers a separate conceptual route, with a substantial new prerequisite.

[De Bondt, 17 October, version 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24889v1) gives economic stabilization and smaller higher-dimensional witnesses. It is useful for compression and related vanishing problems, but does not supply plane descent.

[Demin, revised 1 August, version 3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06372v3) exposes reusable F4 tracing for modular and evaluation-based Groebner computations. Our F7 work already used this research. Reusing traces can reduce exploration costs; characteristic-zero identities and complete exceptional-chart coverage remain the proof endpoint.

## Follow-up: a simpler global-functional criterion

The [solidity proof and obstruction](keller-solidity/PROOF.md) give a shorter, characteristic-free argument for the nonzero-functional criterion: for a Noetherian domain base and finite generic field extension, a single nonzero global target-linear functional forces module finiteness. The main theorem is classical Datta--Smith, not a new AI-discovered result. The proof also extends to every finite torsion-free target, and commutator induction rules out every uniformly finite-order differential regularizer when the extension is nonfinite.

For the actual normal Wright inclusion this is an all-degree obstruction to that class of constructions. It does not rule out a Keller pair: deriving a global functional from additional Keller equations would still be a valid contradiction strategy. Finite moments, nonlocal evaluation and torsion-valued maps have different scope. JC2, HC4, r7 global covers and Wright finite realization remain open.

## Recommended next work

1. Pursue the joint r7 covering problem on a declared C/B/H stratum. Use marked braid relations and finite S6 images, and retain all omission flags. The contact-34 controls are useful positive tests; they are not Keller candidates.
2. Continue the contact-507 F2 coefficient gate with the complete lower bands and exceptional carriers. A uniformly proved contradiction is the endpoint.
3. First test the eight immutable high coefficients in the pole-(6,9) next Jacobian equation. Then test the eleven remaining matrix-membership conditions on the 46 boundary parameters. Keep all 36 higher-order invisible directions, contact cases, field degree, finiteness and compatible completion. The 53-quadratic/116-parameter system is the unreduced formulation.
4. Use the remaining F7 (3,2,2,2) system as a smaller exact-algebra target. Preserve nonzero localization factors and produce an identity over characteristic zero or an actual complex solution.
5. Audit the Tang geometric reduction and only then treat arbitrary-degree HC4 approaches as approaches to the same plane problem. Keep the independent singleton-zero properness question explicit.
6. Audit Wang--Yan before launching the dense low-degree census. Its conditional twenty-flag coverage could prevent repeating sixteen complete profile searches.
7. Test source-specific properness arguments against the nonzero-functional criterion, and compare any Wright-ring construction with the campaign's existing pencil and moment controls.
