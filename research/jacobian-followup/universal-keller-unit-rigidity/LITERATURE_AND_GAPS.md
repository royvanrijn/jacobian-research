# Root closure meets a primitive-unit surface obstruction

Reviewed 15 November 2026. [Proof](PROOF.md), [pins](sources.json). Novelty of the surface and compiler deductions is unverified.

[de Bondt--Yan, Irreducibility properties of Keller maps, version2 (2014)](https://arxiv.org/html/1304.0634v2), Corollary2.2, states that Keller pullbacks preserve square-free polynomials. [Jedrzejewicz--Zielinski, Analogs of Jacobian conditions for subrings, version1 (2016)](https://arxiv.org/html/1601.01508v1), Theorems2.4,3.4,3.6, records the square-factorial/root-closed framework. Root closure is therefore a published input, not a new general theorem here. The proof includes a shorter special argument for the polynomial Keller subring used in this application.

The generic curve step uses only [Stacks Riemann-Hurwitz](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0C1B) and [genus-zero curves](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0C6L). A surjective etale image of Gm must again be split Gm: completion has at most two rational missing points, and the one-point case is ruled out by its Laurent derivative and surjectivity. Its monomial deck group extends to the entire source surface and fixes the retained node. Etaleness there forces that group trivial. [Stacks normal-domain intersection, tag031T](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/031T), supplies the final affine Hartogs step. No classification of all affine C*-surfaces or quotient singularities is imported.

The crucial distinction from the previous generic self-map controls is unit index. Such maps have r->r^d and evade primitive-unit rigidity. A WHOLE Keller factor cannot do so: a recovered positive power of the polynomial t recovers t itself, forcing primitive unit index. This closes the entire nonconstant-unit intermediate branch in all degrees.

The remaining whole component has geometrically irreducible constant fibres. The irreducibility reductions discussed in de Bondt--Yan show why this is a meaningful branch, not an inverse theorem. Arithmetic unique factorization of the intermediate generic ring follows from localization of the whole intermediate polynomial ring; geometric factoriality is a separate unproved condition. Unit-free non-plane surfaces with the exact embedded field and whole polynomial realization remain the factorization gap. Full canonical absolute stable atomicity and actual nonlinear integration remain open.
