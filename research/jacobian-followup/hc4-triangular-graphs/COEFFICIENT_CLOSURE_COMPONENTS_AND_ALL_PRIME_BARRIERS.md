# Coefficient closure, components and all-prime barriers for HC4

Claims HC4MYKM0/1/2. [MATH_STATUS.json](../../MATH_STATUS.json) is the
authority. This is a structural all-degree argument,not a growing
coefficient search. It gives a closed two-branch coefficient cover,
a universal inverse away from the cotangent branch on the integer
horizontal closure,and a necessary growing set of bad primes. It uses
the retained HCTANG2 alternative and the classical inverse-degree bound;
neither HC4 nor JC2 is resolved.

## 1. A finite universal inverse certificate: HC4MYKM0

Fix D>=2,and discard constant and linear potential terms,which only
translate the gradient. In four ordered variables z=(x,y,u,v),put

    h=sum_(2<=|alpha|<=D) c_alpha z^alpha,
    A=Hess(h)(0), delta=det A,
    C_D=Z[c_alpha,delta^(-1)].

Let I_D be the ideal generated by all nonconstant coefficients of
det Hess(h). Thus X_D=Spec(C_D/I_D) parameterizes these potentials
with constant unit Hessian determinant delta. The constant term of the
gradient is already zero. Put F=grad(h),m=D-1 and

    B=m^3.

The formal inverse G=sum_(r>=1) G_r exists over C_D,without factorial
division or rational coefficients beyond delta^(-1). Its recursion is

    G_1=A^(-1)Y,
    G_r=-A^(-1)*[F(G_1+...+G_(r-1))]_degree r, r>=2.              (1)

In (1),a new G_r can enter a nonlinear term only at degree greater
than r. Thus each G_r is an integral coefficient polynomial localized
only at delta. Form T_B=sum_(r=1)^B G_r,and let U_D be the finite ideal
of all coefficients of BOTH compositions

    F(T_B(Y))-Y, T_B(F(z))-z.                                  (2)

Their degrees are at most mB. The classical Gabber bound gives inverse
degree at most m^3 for every four-variable automorphism of degree at
most m over a characteristic-zero field. This bound is already a
retained input in
[GVCCLOSEDSAFE20261115](../moment-vanishing-audit/LAPLACIAN_INVERSE_CERTIFICATES_AND_CLOSED_SAFE_LOCI.md).
Consequently,over characteristic-zero fields,

    V(I_D+U_D) = the invertible-gradient locus in X_D.            (3)

Conversely,(2) certifies an actual polynomial inverse over ANY coefficient
ring,including rings with nilpotents;this direction needs no inverse-degree
theorem for such rings. We do not extend the field inverse-degree bound
to arbitrary nonreduced algebras.

Closedness of the bounded-degree Jacobian-one automorphism locus is
already proved by [Jelonek,Lemma2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.20597v1)
and [Xavier,Theorem1.1 and proof](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.22041v1).
Their complete relevant proofs were read. We do not claim that closedness
anew. Here the explicit integral formal recursion supplies a finite
coefficient ideal suited to the Hessian family and reduction modulo primes.
No higher-dimensional counterexample corollary from those papers is imported.

## 2. The projective plane incidence and the horizontal cover: HC4MYKM1

Let Y_D be the closed incidence in X_D times Gr(2,4) consisting of
constant vector planes W on EVERY translate of which h is jointly affine.
Use full polynomial translation identities:

    degree_(s,t) h(z+s*w1+t*w2)<=1.                             (4)

There are finitely many coefficients to set to zero;their vanishing is
basis independent,so these equations glue on Grassmann charts. Ordinary
second derivatives must not replace (4) in positive characteristic.

For clarity,Gr(2,4) over Z is the Pluecker quadric in P^5 given by

    p12*p34-p13*p24+p14*p23=0.

On any chart p_ij invertible,a plane has its standard two pivot columns,
with four free entries;the remaining Pluecker coordinate is determined
by that displayed equation. These chart identifications prove the
projective Grassmann realization used here. By
[Stacks01WC](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01WC),a projective
morphism is proper. This standard properness statement,with its proof,
is the ONE newly imported external proved input. Therefore Y_D projects
to a closed subset P_D of X_D,also over Z. Give P_D its reduced closed
image structure and let J_D in C_D be its radical defining ideal.
In particular I_D is contained in J_D. A geometric field point belongs
to P_D exactly when the potential has a geometric jointly affine plane.

The retained Tang alternative now gives over algebraically closed
characteristic-zero fields

    X_D = V(I_D+U_D) union P_D.                                (5)

This is a cover by TWO CLOSED LOCI. In particular every irreducible
component of X_D over characteristic zero is either entirely composed
of automorphic gradients,or is entirely contained in P_D. A component
containing even one point without such a plane is entirely automorphic.
A component not entirely automorphic is contained in the plane locus,
including its automorphic boundary points. No irreducibility of X_D
or density of automorphisms in every component is proved.

Define the integer horizontal reduced coefficient ideal

    H_D=radical(I_D tensor Q) intersect C_D.                    (6)

This is an integer contraction of the characteristic-zero equations,
in the spirit of Maubach--Rauf's strong Keller equations. It is NOT
defined to be the naive characteristic-p Hessian ideal or a lifting
sufficiency condition. Equations (3)--(5) and the Nullstellensatz imply

    U_D*J_D subset H_D.                                        (7)

Indeed every product vanishes at every characteristic-zero field point
of X_D. Taking the radical in C_D tensor Q and contracting gives (7).
Since H_D is radical,also U_D intersect J_D is contained in H_D:
the square of an element of the intersection belongs to the product.

Two useful consequences go beyond an actual DVR lifting test.

* Over EVERY field of EVERY characteristic,a point satisfying H_D
  has either a polynomial gradient inverse T_B or a geometric jointly
  affine two-plane. If no inverse exists,some generator of U_D is
  nonzero;then (7) forces all generators of J_D to vanish.
* Over ANY coefficient ring R,a map C_D/H_D to R for which the image
  of J_D generates the UNIT IDEAL gives U_D=0 in R by (7). Thus T_B
  is an actual polynomial inverse over R. This includes nonreduced R.
  It is a universal inverse on the open complement of the plane locus
  in the horizontal coefficient closure,with degree at most (D-1)^3.

The ring assertion uses the exact ideal product,not an inference from
all residue-field points. A field point outside P_D is a special case.
The corresponding assertion over the ORDINARY naive coefficient ring
C_D/I_D is not proved:radical containment cannot silently be replaced
by ordinary ideal containment. Nor does (7) select one global branch
over a coefficient ring with zero divisors when J_D is not a unit ideal.

## 3. Uniform degree-wise spreading and a ramification bound: HC4MYKM2

C_D is Noetherian. Choose finite generators u_i of U_D and j_a of J_D.
From (7),for each product there are positive integers n_ia,r_ia such that

    n_ia*(u_i*j_a)^(r_ia) belongs to I_D.

Clear the finitely many rational denominators in its ideal expression.
Taking a common multiple N_D>0 and increasing exponents gives a common
integer R_D>=1 with

    N_D*(u_i*j_a)^R_D belongs to I_D for every i,a.              (8)

No generic Groebner computation,actual N_D value or optimal R_D is claimed.
They exist for every D. In particular,for every prime p NOT dividing N_D,
every degree<=D characteristic-p unit-Hessian potential either has the
explicit inverse T_B or has a geometric jointly affine two-plane.
These are degree-dependent finite bad-prime sets,not a fixed list for
all degrees.

More sharply,let O be a complete mixed (0,p) DVR,pi its uniformizer and
e=val_pi(p). Suppose a residue potential of degree<=D has neither an
invertible gradient nor a geometric jointly affine plane. Choose a
generator u_i nonzero there and a generator j_a nonzero there. In ANY
unit-Hessian lift of degree<=D modulo pi^s,both evaluate to units.
Equation (8) forces

    s<=e*val_p(N_D).                                           (9)

Thus at fixed degree,a SINGLE coefficient certificate bounds every
ramification index linearly,and excludes all sufficiently high finite
precisions. The correction-degree bound D is essential here. This is
not an all-degree finite-precision obstruction and does not rule out
compatible formal lifts whose polynomial degrees increase.

### An elementary control for EVERY prime

For every prime p,over a field of characteristic p let

    h_p=xu+yv+x^(p+1)+u^(p+1).                                 (10)

Its Hessian is exactly Hess(xu+yv),so its determinant is one. Its
gradient is

    (u+x^p,v,x+u^p,y),

and sends both (0,0,0,0) and (1,0,-1,0) to zero. This is an actual
noninjective gradient over the prime field,including p=2.

It has NO geometric jointly affine two-plane. For a line direction w,
the coefficient of t^p in h_p(z+tw),as a polynomial in z,is

    x*w_x^p+u*w_u^p,

except that when p=2 the quadratic term also contributes the CONSTANT
q(w). Its coefficients of x and u still force w_x=w_u=0. All affine
directions therefore lie in span(partial_y,partial_v),and q restricts
there to yv. That whole two-plane is not jointly affine. Hence no
two-plane can satisfy (4),over ANY extension field. For p=2,ordinary
second directional derivatives would again miss the obstruction.

The naive integral representative of (10) has the EXACT determinant

    det Hess(h_p)=1-p^2*(p+1)^2*x^(p-1)*u^(p-1).                (11)

It is a degree-(p+1) constant-unit-Hessian lift modulo p^2,or through
precision2e over any O above. Consequently every N_D satisfying (8)
MUST obey

    val_p(N_D)>=2 whenever p+1<=D;
    product_(primes p<=D-1) p^2 divides N_D.                    (12)

Indeed evaluate (8) on this lift modulo p^2. Some u_i and j_a are
units by its two failures,so N_D itself must vanish modulo p^2.
This lower bound holds for every such certificate,not just a bad choice
of denominators. It proves that NO degree-independent finite set of bad
primes can make the naive positive-characteristic two-branch alternative
hold in every degree. Passing modulo p^2 cannot remove these controls.

By (7),h_p violates H_D for EVERY D>=p+1,even when larger correction
degrees are permitted. Hence it has no characteristic-zero polynomial
unit-Hessian lift in any degree or ramification. It is not a counterexample
to complex HC4. No exact maximal finite precision for (10) is claimed.

## 4. A constructive automorphic component certificate and the identity limit

The nonplane branch is not empty. For arbitrary positive integers r>=1,
m>=2 put L=y+x^r*u and

    k_(r,m)=xv+uL+L^m.                                        (13)

Its Hessian determinant is one over Z. The last gradient output gives
x. Writing the four outputs as (a,b,c,d),its complete inverse is

    x=d,
    L=c-x^r*b,
    u=b-m*L^(m-1),
    y=L-x^r*u,
    v=a-r*x^(r-1)*u*b.                                       (14)

This is a polynomial inverse over Z,and hence over every coefficient
field. Nevertheless (13) has precisely the affine direction span(partial_v).
Its single highest term is x^(rm)*u^m,whose two exponents are at least
two. A translation in x or u has a nonzero coefficient of degree at
least two and cannot be cancelled by lower homogeneous terms. With
both directions zero,the term y^m forces the y direction zero. The v
direction remains affine. Thus (13) supplies actual points outside P_D
at arbitrarily high degrees. Every characteristic-zero irreducible
component containing one is wholly automorphic by (5).

This does NOT prove every component contains such a point. Even a
path to a quadratic potential is insufficient. For any potential h
without affine terms and with quadratic part q0,define

    h_t(z)=t^(-2)*h(tz), h_0=q0.                               (15)

It is polynomial in t,z,has the same source-degree bound and constant
Hessian determinant as h. At every t!=0 its gradient is linearly
equivalent to grad(h). Thus a hypothetical HC4 counterexample would
give a whole curve of counterexamples at t!=0 specializing to the
automorphic quadratic at t=0. Replacing t by t^M makes its contact
with that quadratic arbitrarily high,while preserving the degree bound.
No existence of that hypothetical counterexample is asserted.

Closedness of the automorphism locus does not propagate invertibility
OUTWARD from a special point;it propagates from a dense set to its
closure. A proof that automorphisms are dense in EVERY component,or
that every plane-Keller component is invertible,would be a genuine
remaining global theorem. Connectedness,one automorphic point per
component,or any unproved finite-order rigidity at the quadratic is
not that theorem. The characteristic-p family q+t*x^(p+1)+t*u^(p+1)
already gives an unconditional unit-Hessian curve with the same
automorphic special point and noninjective gradients at t=1.

## 5. Assurance and the remaining global question

The only new proved external import is Stacks01WC projective properness.
Gabber's characteristic-zero inverse-degree bound is retained via the
existing GVC proof/source,not rerun. Tang's classification is retained
with its written-reproduction assurance. Jelonek and Xavier are credited
for their already-published closedness/component results;the finite
integral recursion and horizontal product argument are supplied here.
Maubach--Rauf's integer-contraction viewpoint is context only;no strong
Keller sufficiency or lifting conjecture is imported.

The [checker](verify_coefficient_closure.py),
[receipt](receipt-coefficient-closure.json) and
[context](sources/coefficient-closure-context.json) bind exact inverse
recursion controls,Pluecker identities,all-prime determinant and Hasse
formulas,component examples,rescaling and immutable prior proof bytes.
No old registered checker was replayed. The arbitrary-D ideal containment,
properness import and all-prime proof are written mathematics;the checker
does not compute generic elimination ideals or certify those proofs by
a finite list of examples. Independent replay,formal verification and
external review are not claimed.

Full HC4/JC2 remains open. The new theorem closes the nonplane part of
the horizontal coefficient locus,not the plane-Keller part. It neither
supplies a characteristic-zero counterexample nor proves inverse production
on every plane component. Native critical non-CI recognition and the old
endpoint inversion obligations remain separate and open.
