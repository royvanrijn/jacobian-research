# Nilpotent covers and arbitrary common-prime radical depth

15 November 2026. The abelian intermediate-field obstruction is stronger
than an abelian class-action criterion. It forces Keller base change to
preserve the full group of every finite nilpotent Galois cover of the
target function field. Consequently nilpotent divisor-class actions
close, including arbitrary compositions of quadratic output polynomials
with nonconstant leading coefficients. No special-fibre irreducibility
hypothesis is needed for this new branch.

## 1. Nilpotent target covers retain their full groups (HC4MYIA0)

Let K:A^n->A^n be a complex Keller map, n>=1, and write
F=C(K1,...,Kn), L=C(z1,...,zn). The retained
[HC4MYHW0](ABELIAN_CLASSGROUP_OUTPUT_OBSTRUCTION.md#1-no-abelian-intermediate-extension-hc4myhw0)
proves that L contains no nontrivial finite abelian Galois extension
of F, using square-free pullbacks and cyclic prime-degree valuations.

If M/F is any finite Galois extension with nilpotent group G, then

\[
 M\cap L=F,\quad [ML:L]=[M:F],\quad
             Gal(ML/L)\xrightarrow{\;restriction\;}G
             \text{ is an isomorphism}.                   \tag{1}
\]

There are no monicity, normal-model, ramification-divisor, degree or
fibre hypotheses on this cover. Nilpotent here means the finite group
is a direct product of its Sylow subgroups. We prove the needed group
fact rather than assuming an irreducible action is abelian.

### Every proper subgroup lies below a cyclic prime quotient

First let P be a finite p-group and H<P. The action of H on the
left cosets P/H has orbits of p-power size. Its fixed cosets are
exactly N_P(H)/H. Their number is congruent to [P:H] modulo p,
so is divisible by p. The identity coset is fixed, hence there are
at least p fixed cosets: N_P(H)>H. If H is maximal proper, its
normalizer must therefore be P, making H normal. The quotient
P/H has no proper nontrivial subgroup, so has order p. Any proper
subgroup is contained in a maximal one, hence in a normal subgroup
of index p.

For a finite nilpotent G=product_p P_p, every subgroup J decomposes
as product_p(J intersect P_p). Indeed, for each element its p-primary
component is a suitable integral power of that element, by the Chinese
remainder theorem on its finite order; it stays in J. If J<G, one
of those intersections is proper. Choose a maximal subgroup in that
Sylow factor and keep all other factors. This gives

\[
                      J\subset N\lhd G,\qquad G/N=C_p.    \tag{2}
\]

### Apply the cyclic obstruction to the actual source image

The compositum ML is finite Galois over L. Restrict its group to
M and call the image J<=G. An element of M is fixed by J exactly
when it belongs to L, because the fixed field of Gal(ML/L) in ML
is L. Thus M^J=M intersect L. If J were proper, (2) would give

\[
             F\subset M^N\subset M^J\subset L,
                   Gal(M^N/F)=C_p,
\]

contradicting HC4MYHW0. Hence J=G. Restriction is also injective,
since ML is generated by M and L. This proves all of (1), including
linear disjointness. It does not require that M intersect L be Galois
over F: (2) produces the smaller normal subextension that is needed.

Every irreducible polynomial over F with finite nilpotent splitting
group consequently stays irreducible over L, and its full splitting
group is preserved. Unlike the general normal-monogenic-cover result,
the stronger group statement is justified here by (1). A finite
intermediate field E of L/F whose Galois closure over F is nilpotent
must be F. In particular a hypothetical nonautomorphic Keller map
must have nonnilpotent Galois closure. This does not assert that an
arbitrary solvable closure is impossible.

## 2. Nilpotent divisor-class monodromy suffices (HC4MYIA1)

In the filtered output-base criterion
[HC4MYHW1](ABELIAN_CLASSGROUP_OUTPUT_OBSTRUCTION.md#2-a-class-group-and-geometric-fibre-output-criterion-hc4myhw1),
retain its common free target basis, positive generic domain filtration,
geometrically integral affine height-one fibres, normal geometric
generic ring and torsion-free Weil class group. Replace only the
abelian Galois image on that class group by a **finite nilpotent image**.
Every closed stable hypersurface embedding carrying a plane Keller
pair through those ambient observables still forces the pair invertible,
in every polynomial degree and for every r>=0.

To see this, take the finite Galois field M/F corresponding to the
kernel of the class action. Its group is precisely that nilpotent image.
By (1), the image of the absolute source stabilizer Gal(Omega/L) in
Gal(M/F) is the whole group. The class alpha of the selected source
component is fixed by that source stabilizer, hence by the full target
class action. All d conjugate image components therefore have class
alpha. Their reduced sum is the divisor of the image equation, so
d*alpha=0. Torsion-freeness makes alpha=0, and normality makes every
component principal. The rest of HC4MYHW1 applies verbatim: the
generic component pencil descends, integral affine height-one fibres
give global primitive saturation, and the smooth minimal-label
derivative forces d=1 and the polynomial Keller inverse.

This argument uses full source image in the finite class action;
it does not assert that a stabilizer in a nonabelian group is normal.
There is no new requirement that special graded rings or all closed
target fibres be integral.

## 3. Nonmonic outputs with arbitrary radical depth (HC4MYIA2)

Let P(T,z) in C[T,z] have positive z degree and arbitrary nonzero
leading coefficient a(T). For r>=1, let a closed stable embedding
j:A^(r+2)->A^(r+3) carry a plane Keller pair K through

\[
                            (T,xy-P(T,z)).                \tag{3}
\]

If the generic root group of P(T,z)+V over C(T,V) is nilpotent,
then K is a polynomial automorphism, without assumptions on any
special K1-fibre. All degrees are unrestricted. Indeed, the global
filtration and geometric height-one fibre proof in
[HC4MYHZ2](LEADING_FIBRE_UNIT_GATE.md#3-all-degree-nonmonic-output-equations-and-their-remaining-escape-hc4myhz2)
works for every such P, including leading-coefficient zeros. The
generic class group is Z^m/Z(1,...,1) with its root-permutation
action, a quotient of the nilpotent root group. Apply Section 2.
Alternatively (1) gives full root-group preservation directly, hence
root transitivity and zero invariant classes in this root lattice.

### A uniform family with no bound on composition depth

Fix one prime p. For an arbitrary number k>=1 of stages, choose

\[
 q_i(T,z)=a_i(T)(z-b_i(T))^{p^{e_i}}+c_i(T),
 \quad a_i\ne0,\ e_i>=1,\qquad
 P=q_k\circ q_{k-1}\circ\cdots\circ q_1,                \tag{4}
\]

where composition is only in z and all a_i,b_i,c_i belong to C[T].
Every output (3) for (4) closes, in arbitrary k, exponents, coefficient
degrees and embedding degrees. When p=2, this includes every finite
composition of arbitrary quadratic polynomials in z over C[T]:
completing the square writes each stage in the displayed form over
C(T), and the proof below allows those rational coefficients whenever
the final P is polynomial. No special-fibre hypothesis is added.

Here is a direct proof that the root group is a p-group. Start over
F=C(T,V) at the outer equation q_k(u)=-V. Its full root field is
obtained by a p^e-th root, since F contains every root of unity;
the Galois group is cyclic of p-power order, possibly smaller than
the bound. Suppose a finite Galois p-extension contains all nodes at
one level. For each of those nodes u, adjoin every solution of

\[
              q_i(z)=u,\qquad
              (z-b_i)^{p^{e_i}}=(u-c_i)/a_i.              \tag{5}
\]

Over that level field the automorphism group of the compositum
injects into a product of cyclic p-power groups, one for each node.
The set of all radicands is stable under every F-automorphism of
the level field, so the enlarged field is again Galois over F.
Its group is an extension of p-groups and is therefore a p-group.
Induction reaches every root of P+V. Its splitting field, as a
Galois subextension of this field, also has p-group. This proves the
bound without claiming the whole possible wreath group is attained.
The same construction works with rational functions a_i,b_i,c_i in
C(T), provided a_i!=0 and the final P belongs to C[T,z].

For classical primary context, Barquero-Sanchez--Calvo-Monge give
general cyclic/Kummer and quadratic wreath embeddings in
[Theorem 1.11 and Corollaries 1.13/1.14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.14386v1).
Their broader theorem is not an additional proof import: the restricted
construction needed for (4)-(5) is written above. The
[source record](sources/nilpotent-cover-context.json) preserves this distinction.

### A nonabelian, nonmonic example outside the preceding unconditional gate

Take P=T*(z^2-T)^2. Its generic root group is the dihedral group D8
of order eight, hence nilpotent and nonabelian. Write b^2=-V/T;
then the four roots are +/-a and +/-c with a^2=T+b and c^2=T-b.
The first extension has degree two by the valuation at V. Its field
is C(T,b), since V=-T*b^2. In that rational function field the two
square classes T+b and T-b are independent: the valuations at their
distinct linear prime divisors detect each one separately. Thus
the splitting field has degree 2*4=8. Its action preserves the two
root pairs, embedding it in C2 wr C2 of order eight, so it is exactly
D8. Equivalently a->c,c->-a,b->-b has order four and does not commute
with the sign change of a alone. No full-wreath assertion for every
member of (4) follows from this example.

This P has nonconstant leading coefficient T and a nonzero z^2
coefficient, so is not a translated fourth power. Its nonabelian group
escapes the preceding abelian gate. The new nilpotent gate closes it
even without knowing whether K1=0 is irreducible.

The genuine closed controls j=(s,1,t+P(s,w),w), with an unused v,
still have inverse (T,y-P(T,z),z,v). The independent nondegenerate
quadratic T*v+y*z+x^2 pulls back to

\[
                       1+s*v+t*w+w*P(s,w).
\]

Its actual Hessian determinant is one, with the explicit gradient
inverse retained in HC4MYHY1. These controls construct invertible
gradients, not nonautomorphic Keller maps.

## 4. The exact group-theoretic boundary

Nilpotent cannot be replaced by solvable in this argument. In S3,
the proper order-two subgroup generated by a transposition maps
surjectively onto the abelianization C2. The only normal prime-index
subgroup is A3, which does not contain that order-two subgroup.
Thus the cyclic intermediate-field contradiction cannot exclude this
proper source image. This is a group-theoretic negative control,
not a Keller counterexample or evidence that such a source image occurs.

Nor are arbitrary mixed-prime radical towers nilpotent, even over
C(T,V). The splitting field of z^3-3z+V has group S3: the cubic is
irreducible and its discriminant 27*(4-V^2) is not a square. It is
obtained by first adjoining sqrt(V^2-4) and then a cube root u of
(-V+sqrt(V^2-4))/2; all three roots are zeta*u+1/(zeta*u).
Our common-prime construction keeps every extension degree a power
of the same prime. No claim for general mixed-prime compositions
is made.

Within the full nonmonic family (3), the surviving hypothetical
nonautomorphic branch must therefore have **both nonnilpotent generic
root monodromy and positive localized-unit defect at a leading zero**.
The finite leading-fibre gate of HC4MYHZ1/2 remains independent and
closes any such group when its specified fibres are irreducible.
Other output geometries, torsion class groups, both plane endpoints
and unrestricted HC4/JC2 remain open.

The [checker](verify_nilpotent_covers.py) and
[receipt](receipt-nilpotent-covers.json) supply finite exact controls
for the group boundary and explicit embeddings. The general proofs
are the arguments above, not an extrapolation from those controls.
No formal verification, independent replay, external review or novelty
priority is asserted.
