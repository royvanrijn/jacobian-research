# Global finite-difference lifts and relative flexibility

Canonical claims: HC4MYKC0, HC4MYKC1, HC4MYKC2. The authority is
[MATH_STATUS.json](../../MATH_STATUS.json). These are all-degree statements
about actual polynomial automorphisms and their generated group. They do
not construct the single free action with the quotient required by JV.

## 1. An actual polynomial lift across the apparent denominator (HC4MYKC0)

Work over C. Let a,C be nonzero univariate polynomials with gcd(a,C)=1,
and let D be arbitrary. Neither a degree bound nor squarefreeness is
needed in this section. In R=C[X,P,z,Q] put

    h=a(z)X P+C(z)Q+D(z),
    A(z)a(z)+B(z)C(z)=1,
    H=h-D(z),   K=-X B(z)P+A(z)Q.                    (1)

The Bezout polynomials A,B exist globally. On X!=0 the exact chart is

    R_X=C[X,X^-1,z,h,K],
    P=[A(z)(h-D(z))-C(z)K]/X,
    Q=B(z)(h-D(z))+a(z)K.                           (2)

Both inverse identities follow from Aa+BC=1. In particular, the
Jacobian of (H,K) with respect to (P,Q) is X.

For every polynomial f define the polynomial divided difference

    Delta_t f=[f(z+tX)-f(z)]/X in C[t,X,z].

Indeed the binomial expansion of each monomial has a factor X.
Write z_t=z+tX and f_t=f(z_t). Define

    X_t=X,
    z_t=z+tX,
    P_t=P+(Delta_t A)H-A_t(Delta_t D)-(Delta_t C)K,
    Q_t=Q+X[(Delta_t B)H-B_t(Delta_t D)+(Delta_t a)K]. (3)

Every entry in (3) is a polynomial on the entire source. On (2) this
is exactly translation of z, fixing X,h,K. Therefore (3) preserves
h and K, has the additive group law, and has the actual polynomial
inverse obtained by replacing t by -t. Equality after localization
implies equality in the original polynomial ring. Its full source
Jacobian is one: the chart determinant X is fixed and local translation
has determinant one. Thus (3) is a global h-preserving Ga action.

Its locally nilpotent derivation L_X is

    L_X(X)=0,   L_X(z)=X,
    L_X(P)=A'H-A D'-C'K,
    L_X(Q)=X[B'H-B D'+a'K],
    L_X(h)=L_X(K)=0.                                (4)

Local nilpotence also follows directly from L_X=X partial_z in (2):
each source generator has finite z-degree in that chart. This is an
all-degree proof, with no bounded derivation search. Interchanging
X and P gives a mirror L_P with L_P(P)=0 and L_P(z)=P.

Two other global derivations are

    E_X=C partial_P-aX partial_Q,
    E_P=C partial_X-aP partial_Q.                    (5)

They are locally nilpotent and preserve h. In (2), E_X=-X partial_K,
so E_X commutes with L_X. This does not assert an exact global
description of ker L_X; localization can conceal extra invariants.

For the explicit KB three-point target one may take

    C=(z-1)(z-2)(z-3), a=(3z^2-12z+10)/2,
    A=C', B=(1-a C')/C,
    D=((rho+3)z^2-(3rho+13)z+2rho+12)/2.

The displayed B is polynomial. Formula (3) works for every rho,
including rho=+-1. It moves z on X!=0, so the native divisor a(z)=0
is not preserved by the full h-preserving automorphism group. This
does not classify torus actions or their transported fixed schemes.

## 2. Complete relative invariants and smooth-locus orbits (HC4MYKC1)

Let LND_h(R) denote nonzero locally nilpotent derivations annihilating h.
Define ML_h as the intersection of their kernels, DK_h as the algebra
generated by their kernels, and SAut_h as the group generated by their
actual Ga flows. Relative flexibility means that at every point of a
smooth fibre locus, values of global h-preserving LNDs span its tangent
space. We prove

    ML_h(R)=C[h],   DK_h(R)=R,                       (6)

and SAut_h is transitive on the smooth locus of every value fibre,
including the smooth part of a singular fibre. These conclusions need
only the coprimality hypotheses in Section 1.

First, ker E_X=C[X,z,h] and ker E_P=C[P,z,h]. To justify the first
equality, work over the fraction field of C[X,z], where the translation
kernel is that field[h]. At every height-one prime of C[X,z], one of
C,aX is a unit, since gcd(C,aX)=1. The linear coordinate h can therefore
be completed over that discrete valuation ring, and every coefficient
of an invariant polynomial in h is integral there. Intersection of
these valuation rings is C[X,z]. The second equality is the mirror.

On C[X,z,h], L_X acts as X partial_z. Hence an invariant of E_X and
L_X lies in C[X,h]. The mirror puts a common invariant in C[P,h].
The variables X,P,h are algebraically independent, since C!=0 and h
is linear in Q. Thus C[X,h] intersect C[P,h]=C[h], proving ML_h.
For DK_h, kernels in (5) already contain X,P,z,h, while ker L_X
contains K. The exact identity

    Q=aK+B(h-D)                                     (7)

then supplies the remaining generator. This proves the entire DK_h,
not just an inclusion from a chosen action module.

Here is a finite-flow proof of transitivity. On C(z)!=0, (5) can set
X=1, keeping z fixed. Formula (3) can then move z to any desired value.
At a target with C!=0, (5) first sets P and then X arbitrarily; Q is
uniquely determined by h=s. Consequently the whole C!=0 part of each
fibre is in one orbit.

Every smooth point outside that part can enter it. At C=0, a!=0.
If X!=0 use (3) to move z off the finite root set of C; if P!=0 use
its mirror. If X=P=0, the point is smooth exactly when

    kappa=C'(z)Q+D'(z) !=0.

At this point H=0, A=1/a, K=AQ, and (4) gives

    L_X(P)=-A kappa !=0,
    L_X(X)=L_X(z)=L_X(Q)=0.

In fact along this flow at X=0 and C=0, the change in P is linear
with this nonzero coefficient. It produces P!=0, after which the
mirror moves z off C=0. This handles repeated roots of C as well.

On C!=0,X!=0, the vectors E_X,E_P,L_X are independent in the
three-dimensional fibre tangent space: the first two independently
move P,X, and the third has nonzero z-component. Transport these
three LNDs by the actual finite compositions just constructed. Their
conjugates remain global h-preserving LNDs and span the tangent at
every smooth point. This proves relative flexibility directly.

Each h-s is irreducible: as a linear polynomial in Q it is primitive
over C[X,P,z], because for every root r of C the polynomial
a(r)XP+D(r)-s is nonzero. The constant-C case is immediate. Thus
the smooth locus in the preceding statement is the expected dense
three-dimensional locus; no extra vertical fibre component is ignored.

If C is squarefree, the full critical set consists of exactly one
point over each root r:

    X=P=0, z=r, Q=-D'(r)/C'(r).

Every global h-preserving Ga action fixes every such point, because
it acts on a finite critical set and Ga is connected. Hence the
complete SAut_h orbit decomposition is one smooth-locus orbit in
each value fibre and one singleton for each critical point. Critical
values may coincide. For nonsquarefree C we assert smooth-locus
transitivity, not that every singular point is fixed individually.

There is a useful larger scope. In actual coordinates let
h=d(b)+U f1(b)+V f2(b), with (f1,f2) the radical ideal of any finite
nonempty set of simple points. The first half of KB0, before its
root sign twist, moves these points to (0,i), interpolates
a(i)=Jac(f1,f2)(0,i)/C_N'(i), uses JV2's determinant-one row repair,
and removes d-D_c by a fibre translation. It constructs, with actual
polynomial inverses, precisely the weighted model of Section 1.
This step does not require common squared point orientations. Thus
(6), relative flexibility and the stated orbit decomposition apply
to every such simple cotangent potential. A complete source-function
classification without the common-squared hypothesis is not claimed.

## 3. The same collective geometry in every native frontier (HC4MYKC2)

For h=2xy+B(u,v), B nonconstant, consider the four global LNDs

    D_u(x)=0, D_u(u)=2x, D_u(v)=0, D_u(y)=-B_u,
    D_v(x)=0, D_v(u)=0, D_v(v)=2x, D_v(y)=-B_v,

and the two mirrors interchanging x and y. Their flows are polynomial
by the same elementary divided-difference calculation. The x-fixed
flows move (u,v) arbitrarily when x!=0; the mirrors do so when y!=0.

All x!=0 points in a fibre are in one orbit. From one such point,
move the base to B=s-2x, giving y=1. Keeping y=1, move the base to
B=s-2x_target, giving any prescribed nonzero x_target. Keeping that
x, move the base to the target base point. Such intermediate points
exist because a nonconstant complex polynomial B is surjective:
its restriction to a suitable affine line is nonconstant. This is
an explicit finite composition, not a connectedness assumption.

If x=0,y!=0, a mirror base motion can reach B!=s and hence x!=0.
If x=y=0 at a smooth point, grad B!=0. One of D_u,D_v then changes
y nontrivially while keeping the base fixed, reducing to the previous
case. Thus every smooth point belongs to that one orbit. At an
x!=0 point with B_u!=0, the three fields D_u,D_v and the u mirror
span the fibre tangent; use the v mirror if B_v!=0. Such points
exist. Conjugation gives relative flexibility everywhere. These
arguments apply even on singular value fibres. Each fibre is
irreducible by primitivity of 2xy+B-s as a polynomial in y.

This covers h_D, S_rho and the nondegenerate quadratic endpoint.
For K2, use JS's actual whole-value presentation

    R=C[s,x,y,u,v]/(2xy+F_s(u,v)),
    F_s=(s+uv)^2+v-1,   s=K2.

The four derivations just displayed, with B=F_s and s fixed, are
polynomial global derivations of this ring and hence of the actual
native polynomial source under JS's explicit inverse. For every
s=kappa, F_kappa is nonconstant, so the same argument applies to
every smooth fibre locus, including at kappa=+-1. The coordinate
endpoint has the ordinary translations of its three fibre coordinates.
Together with JS's complete relative ML/DK calculation, all these
models have the invariants (6) and the collective orbit decomposition
of Section 2, with their matching critical-value data. Finiteness
of their critical sets again makes every critical point a singleton.

In particular, S_rho and its explicit KB target R_rho have the same
complete relative ML/DK values, relative flexibility and SAut_h orbit
pattern for every rho, including collisions. K2 and h1 do as well.
These statements construct no polynomial source equivalence. They
do not compare the groups as ind-groups, classify their individual
LNDs, or produce a single action free on every smooth value fibre.
Collective transitivity uses different flows at different stages.
The precise quotient kernel, plinth, missing-line geometry and two
global slices of one candidate action remain the JV/JU gates.
Neither E2 nor E3 is constructed or excluded. The two old inversion
endpoints and unrestricted HC4/JC2 remain open.
