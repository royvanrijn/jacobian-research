## 2. Trace dual, different and discriminant of the residual factor

Write Lambda_C for the trace dual of C over R in its generic product
of separable fields. Finite projectivity and flatness identify the

    Lambda tensor_B R = R times Lambda_C,
    (Lambda/S) tensor_B R = Lambda_C/C.                     (2)

This follows directly by dualizing a local free frame; trace of a block
diagonal multiplication matrix is the sum of its traces. In particular
the original discriminant quotient is NOT killed by removing the
diagonal factor: all its base-changed defect is in the residual factor.

Define the Dedekind different by

    d_S=(S:Lambda)=Ann_S(Lambda/S).

The equality uses 1 in Lambda. After flat base change,

    d_S*A = R times d_C,
    d_S*R = R,                                              (3)

where the last extension uses ONLY mu, the diagonal map A->R.
Annihilators commute here with flat base change: choose finite generators
of Lambda/S and express the annihilator as the kernel of the finite
map S->(Lambda/S)^N sending a to its products with these generators.
Flat tensor preserves that kernel. Product decomposition then proves (3).
It does NOT say d_S*A=A. Similarly the Kahler different base-changes to
R times the residual Kahler different, by the differential presentation
and its Fitting minors. No equality of the Kahler and Dedekind differents
on an arbitrary singular normalization is assumed. They agree in the
smooth monogenic controls below, by a direct derivative computation.

The trace pairing on A is the orthogonal direct sum of the unit rank-one

    Disc(S/B)*R = Disc(C/R).                                (4)

It also follows from the trace definition of the discriminant in

## 4. The residual factor can be seen without normalization calculations

Over R_e introduce the second finite root w and set

    H(w,u)=(f(w)-f(u))/(w-u),
    C_e=R_e[w]/H(w,u).

This is a monic polynomial of degree e in w, H(u,u)=h(u) is a unit,
and the two factors w-u and H are comaximal. The Chinese remainder
decomposition is EXACT:

    R_e[w]/(f(w)-f(u)) = R_e times C_e.                     (6)

Its diagonal idempotent is H(w,u)/h(u) modulo f(w)-f(u).
On C_e, w-u is invertible and

    f'(w)=(w-u)*H_w(w,u).

Thus all residual ramification is measured by H_w, equivalently f'(w).
At the actual retained source point u=1, where h(1)=1 and P=0,

    H(w,1)=w^e.

The residual fiber is the FULL nonreduced length-e algebra C[w]/w^e,
while the diagonal fiber is the reduced point C. The retained source

it. In polynomial bases,

    Disc_w(f(w)-f(u))=h(u)^2*Disc_w H(w,u).                 (7)

The squared factor is a UNIT of R_e; (7) illustrates exactly the ideal
identity (4), not an exception to it. All e>=2 are covered; these are
