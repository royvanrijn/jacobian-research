# Global additive actions and the K2 translation barrier

15 November 2026. Claims **HC4MYJS0/1**. There is an exact global
presentation of the fixed two-point model as a suspension over
its value parameter. It supplies four polynomial additive actions
and computes the complete relative Makar--Limanov and Derksen
invariants for every remaining normalized model.

The outcome is an obstruction to a proposed method: these complete
kernel invariants do not recover any extra source coordinate.
The intrinsic trace plane of JR therefore cannot be recovered from
the whole potential by simply intersecting all invariant kernels.

There is a stronger necessary action condition. A hypothetical
nonautomorphic unit-Hessian source member produces an additive
action free on every noncritical value fibre, with a polynomial
three-variable kernel and a very specific fixed scheme. For K2,
neither of the two full standard translation modules can provide
such an action, in any coefficient degree. This does not classify
all additive actions or close the K2 source orbit.

## 1. Statements and retained inputs

For a domain R and h in R, write

    ML_h(R)=intersection ker delta,
    DK_h(R)=the subalgebra generated by all ker delta,

where delta runs over nonzero locally nilpotent C-derivations
annihilating h. These are relative to the function, not to a
chosen coordinate projection. The first is unchanged if the zero
derivation is included. The second definition is used explicitly
here; no assertion about every variant of a Derksen invariant
is needed.

**JS0.** The JR one-coordinate obstruction also excludes any
source automorphism for which the retained native hyperbolic
coordinate has a nonconstant affine-linear pullback in the actual
source coordinates. A constant affine precomposition changes
that pullback to x, without a nonlinear Hessian change. Hence
every hypothetical unit-Hessian member of h0,h1 has nonlinear
pullbacks of both native hyperbolic coordinates. The same applies
to P,V in the actual representative 2PV+B_rho. For the native
cubic representation this tests P_native composed with Phi and
V_native composed with Phi via JQ's left-composed source map;
affine linearity is tested in the actual final source variables.
This remains a necessary condition, not recovery of a coordinate
from the full potential. The unequal-slope K2 case is not covered
by this affine-visibility exclusion.

Also, if a four-variable h has N>=2 critical points and its full
source orbit contains a unit-Hessian member, there is a nonzero
h-annihilating locally nilpotent derivation delta such that:

- ker delta is a polynomial ring C[b1,b2,h];
- its fixed scheme is N disjoint affine planes;
- h is constant on each fixed plane, with its critical value;
- the action is free on every value fibre outside those values.

The statement assumes the native critical set is finite Morse,
as is necessary under unit Hessian. All remaining fixed models
meet this necessary critical-scheme condition. No quotient
properness or classification of all actions is asserted.

**JS1.** For every nonconstant B in C[z,w] and
h=2xy+B(z,w),

    ML_h(C[x,y,z,w])=C[h],
    DK_h(C[x,y,z,w])=C[x,y,z,w].                         (1)

The same equalities hold for

    K2=w(2xy+z^2-1)+z.                                  (2)

Thus they hold for h0,h1, every representative 2PV+B_rho,
and the corresponding K3 source orbit by actual conjugation of
derivations. The coordinate and nondegenerate quadratic tests
also have the same invariants: use respectively 2xy+z, a literal
polynomial coordinate, and 2xy+z^2+w^2. Thus this barrier applies
to the entire current universal test list. These equalities do
not identify their source orbits or resolve any endpoint.

For K2 there are commuting standard derivations D_u,D_v with
common kernel C[x,K2]. Every nonzero derivation

    a(x,K2)D_u+b(x,K2)D_v, a,b in C[x,K2],              (3)

is locally nilpotent but has a fixed point on every fibre
K2=kappa with kappa!=+-1. The symmetric module over C[y,K2]
has the same property. Every coefficient degree is allowed.
Therefore a JS0 action for K2 must lie outside both whole
translation modules and every conjugate of them by a K2-preserving
polynomial source automorphism. Other, possibly nonlinear, additive actions
remain open; this is not K2 inverse closure or an exclusion of
every action fixing x.

Retain [HCTANG2](../tang-hc4-audit/STRUCTURAL_REVIEW.md),
[HG0](GLOBAL_VALUE_ORBITS.md) and the actual source-map boundary
of [JQ](TWO_NORMAL_MODELS_AND_RELATIVE_RANK_ONE_ALIGNMENT.md).
[JR](ONE_HYPERBOLIC_COORDINATE_AND_INTRINSIC_CYLINDER_PLANE.md)
supplies the one-coordinate obstruction and the intrinsic
exceptional-cylinder plane. The elementary unit and invariant
localization arguments in JR are retained. No flexibility,

## 2. Affine pullbacks and the necessary free generic action (JS0)

Suppose Phi^*x=ell is nonconstant affine-linear. Choose an actual
constant affine automorphism L with ell composed with L=x.
Then Phi composed with L preserves C[x], and the Hessian of
(h composed with Phi) composed with L is the constant congruence
D L^T Hess(h composed with Phi) D L. Thus unit Hessian would
contradict JR1 in every applicable first-normal-jet family.
The same argument applies separately to the other hyperbolic
coordinate in a symmetric suspension. It never conjugates a
nonlinear source map or treats a polynomial coordinate as affine.

For the action assertion, let g=h composed with Phi have unit
Hessian. N>=2 excludes an automorphic gradient. Tang gives,
after a constant linear source change,

    g=D(b)+P F(b)+Q G(b), J(F,G) in C*.

Let Z=V(F,G). The full critical scheme is isomorphic to Z,
as retained in HG0, so Z is N distinct points. The derivation

    delta=G(b) partial_P-F(b) partial_Q                  (4)

is locally nilpotent and fixes g. Its fixed scheme is exactly
Z times A2_(P,Q), reduced because the plane Keller map is etale.
Its invariant linear function is H=PF+QG=g-D(b).

Over the fraction field C(b), the kernel is C(b)[H]. To descend
it, take a height-one prime of C[b]. Since F,G are coprime,
at least one is a unit there. The usual solved linear coordinate
then gives the invariant kernel over that localized coefficient
ring as its polynomial ring in H. Each coefficient of an invariant
in C(b)[H] is therefore regular at every height-one prime.
Unique factorization in C[b] makes it polynomial. Hence

    ker delta=C[b,H]=C[b,g].

All algebra generators are actual global polynomials. On a fixed
plane g=D(b_i), its critical value. At any other value kappa,
no point has b in Z. The row (G,-F) is nonzero at every point,
so the additive translation is free on that whole fibre.
Transporting the derivation by the actual polynomial source
equivalence gives every assertion for h, preserving local
nilpotence, the kernel, fixed scheme and the value fibres.

This action is a necessary global object. A polynomial kernel
alone, a vanishing intersection of kernels, or a free action on
only a dense open subset does not supply all its properties.

## 3. Four explicit actions compute the relative invariants for suspensions

For h=2xy+B(z,w), define

    D_z(x)=0, D_z(z)=2x, D_z(w)=0, D_z(y)=-B_z,
    D_w(x)=0, D_w(z)=0, D_w(w)=2x, D_w(y)=-B_w.

They fix h and commute. They are locally nilpotent: x and
the other base variable are fixed, the moved base variable
has constant derivative, and the y derivative is polynomial
in those variables. Define the symmetric two derivations by
interchanging x and y.

After inverting x the coordinate ring is exactly

    C[x,x^(-1),h,z,w], y=(h-B)/(2x).

There D_z,D_w are 2x partial_z and 2x partial_w, so their
common kernel is C[x,x^(-1),h]. Its intersection with the
original ring is C[x,h]. Indeed if a polynomial in x,h divided
by a minimal positive power of x belonged to the original ring,
its nonzero numerator at x=0 would give a nonzero polynomial
in B(z,w) equal to zero. B is nonconstant, so this is impossible.

The symmetric pair has common kernel C[y,h]. Since x,y,h are
algebraically independent when B is nonconstant,
C[x,h] intersect C[y,h]=C[h]. Every h-annihilating derivation
fixes C[h], and the four displayed ones already leave no more
common invariants. This proves the first equality in (1).

The kernels of these four nonzero derivations contain between
them x,y,z,w. Their generated algebra is the whole coordinate
ring, proving the second equality. No claim of novelty for the
general suspension phenomenon is made; this is the elementary
relative calculation needed for these source functions.

## 4. An exact global suspension presentation for K2

Put q=2xy+z^2 and introduce actual polynomials

    s=K2, u=w, v=1-q.

They satisfy z=s+uv. Therefore the following maps are inverse
isomorphisms of C[s]-algebras:

    C[x,y,z,w]
       = C[s,x,y,u,v]/(2xy+(s+uv)^2+v-1),              (5)

    s=w(q-1)+z, u=w, v=1-q,
    z=s+uv, w=u.

Substitution verifies both composites on every generator;
no variable or value parameter is inverted. This is a presentation
of the entire family of value fibres, not merely a generic-fibre
isomorphism and not an ambient source automorphism of A4 using
four newly independent coordinates. In particular it does not
identify K2 with h1 or preserve native Hessians by itself.

Write P_s(u,v)=(s+uv)^2+v-1. In the presentation (5), set

    D_u(x)=D_u(s)=D_u(v)=0, D_u(u)=2x, D_u(y)=-(P_s)_u,
    D_v(x)=D_v(s)=D_v(u)=0, D_v(v)=2x, D_v(y)=-(P_s)_v.

Both preserve the relation, are locally nilpotent and commute.
The two symmetric actions fix y instead. Under (5) these are
actual polynomial derivations of the original C[x,y,z,w].
In particular

    D_u(w)=2x, D_u(z)=2xv, D_u(y)=-2vz,
    D_v(w)=0, D_v(z)=2xw, D_v(y)=-(2wz+1),
    D_v(v)=2x, D_u(v)=0.

After inverting x, solve y=-P_s/(2x); the common kernel of
D_u,D_v is C[s,x,x^(-1)]. Its intersection with the original
ring is C[s,x], by the same minimal-denominator argument,
since s|x=0=w(z^2-1)+z is nonconstant. The symmetric pair
gives C[s,y], and algebraic independence of s,x,y gives
intersection C[s]. Thus ML_K2=C[K2]. The four kernels contain
s,x,y,u,v between them, which generate the whole original ring
by z=s+uv,w=u. Consequently DK_K2 is also the whole ring.

These are complete equalities, not lower bounds obtained by
testing a few actions. The explicit finite action list proves
the upper bound for the intersection and the lower bound for
the generated algebra; the reverse inclusions are tautological.
In particular the exceptional trace rigidity in JR and the
minimal global invariant here are consistent: they concern
different rings and different sets of derivations.

## 5. Every standard translation module has generic fixed points

Since D_u,D_v commute and annihilate C[x,s], every expression
in (3) is locally nilpotent. The binomial formula proves this
directly for each polynomial: the coefficients remain fixed
and sufficiently many D_u or D_v factors kill it. Nonzero
coefficients give a nonzero derivation, as seen after inverting x.

Fix any kappa!=+-1. On x=0,K2=kappa the actual plane curve
is parametrized by z in A1 minus {-1,1}:

    v=1-z^2, u=(z-kappa)/(1-z^2), y free.               (6)

Put a0=a(0,kappa),b0=b(0,kappa). All the derivation components
except its y component vanish automatically on x=0. The remaining
fixed-point condition, multiplied by the nonzero 1-z^2, is

    2a0 z(1-z^2)^2+b0(z^2-2kappa z+1)=0.              (7)

If both coefficients vanish, every point of (6) is fixed.
If b0=0,a0!=0, z=0 gives an actual fixed affine line. If b0!=0,
the polynomial in (7) has degree five when a0!=0 and degree two
otherwise. Its values at z=1,-1 are respectively
2b0(1-kappa),2b0(1+kappa), both nonzero. Over C it therefore
has a root different from both omitted values, again giving
an actual fixed affine line through all y. No exceptional
endpoint of the chart has been counted as a point.

This proves the assertion for every coefficient degree and
every kappa outside the two true critical values. Interchanging
x,y proves it for the other standard module. Each entire module
therefore fails the necessary free-generic-fibre condition of JS0.
The same is true of every conjugate by an actual K2-preserving
source automorphism: it maps each value fibre to itself and
carries the entire fixed scheme isomorphically. This saturates
the obstruction under all such nonlinear source conjugations,
with no bound on their degree.

The conclusion concerns these full modules, not all h-preserving
locally nilpotent derivations and not all actions fixing x or y.
Combinations from the two different modules need not even be
locally nilpotent. No exhaustion or conjugacy classification
of the possible JS0 action is asserted.

## 6. The remaining global question

All current normalized models have exactly the same extreme
relative kernel invariants: only their value parameter survives
the intersection, while the kernel-generated algebra is everything.
Thus unrestricted coordinate recovery cannot follow merely from
those invariant algebras. Flexibility or a trivial invariant does
not certify a free action with the required global fixed scheme.

For K2 any hypothetical unit-Hessian source member requires a
different action, outside both standard translation modules and
their whole K2-preserving source-conjugacy saturation,
with a polynomial three-generator kernel, two fixed affine planes
at the critical values and no fixed point on any other value fibre.
Whether such an action exists is open. It would still be necessary
to prove the actual unit-Hessian source representation, not merely
an action or matching local jets.

For a concrete limitation, the polynomial

    C2=2xzP+(z^2-1)Q+z

already has the action (z^2-1) partial_P-2xz partial_Q,
kernel C[x,z,C2], two fixed affine planes at values +-1, and
free action on every other value fibre. Nevertheless its native
full Hessian determinant is 16z^4, not a unit. This example is
not asserted inequivalent to K2, nor asserted to lack a unit-Hessian
source member, nor introduced as an additional universal endpoint.
It shows why the action data alone do not certify an actual native
unit Hessian or a source parameterization producing one.

Full K2/h1 equivalence, K2 one-coordinate unit-Hessian routes,
full-source h0,h1 and S_rho, both old endpoints, actual global
embedding recovery and unrestricted HC4/JC2 remain open.
The all-affine reductions are retained and no old outer-degree
search is reopened.
