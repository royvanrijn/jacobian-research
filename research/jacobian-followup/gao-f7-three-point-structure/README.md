# F7 incidence: primitive section and rational-point density

**GAOF7MW1:** the elliptic triple-root incidence surface has trivial geometric
Mordell-Weil torsion and an explicit primitive Q(s)-section of Shioda height
58/3. Every nonzero section has height at least 28/3. Its rational points
are Zariski dense. The full rank is unknown.
