# Quasi-finite descent and polynomial coordinate orbits

Reviewed 15 November 2026. [Proof](PROOF.md), [pins](sources.json). Novelty is unverified.

[Stacks Section29.21](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01TC) gives the finite-fibre/quasi-finite framework, and [Lemma29.36.10](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02V5) shows unramified maps are locally quasi-finite. The divisor proof here needs only dominance and quasi-finiteness of an affine-space polynomial self-map. It extends the retained UAJOINTKER1 etale saturation proof by using divisor image dimension instead of flat going-down. It is an elementary consequence of established dimension facts, not an asserted new general algebraization theorem.

The prior UAAFFINER1 proof supplies the Laurent Wronskian/Riemann-Hurwitz step. Source regularity, the native image line and coordinate irreducibility then force a single global shear. No broad Jung-van der Kulk theorem over a parameter ring and no plane Jacobian conjecture are imported. Generic polynomial plane coordinate changes over k(u,sigma) algebraize to the explicit global shear only within the stated native whole-source class.

[Kanel-Belov, Grigoriev, Elishev and Zhang, Lifting of Polynomial Symplectomorphisms and Deformation Quantization, version1 (2017)](https://arxiv.org/html/1707.06450v1), primary v1 introduction and main approximation statements, discusses tame approximation in formal power-series topology and the separate polynomiality issue for limits. Its full lifting programme is not an input to our theorem and is not treated as a solution of the current global integration or atomicity gate. The versioned primary page is pinned; no priority certificate is asserted.

The constructive lesson is precise: nonlinear-r polynomial Keller solutions and arbitrarily high complete marked contact already occur inside the old target-coordinate orbit. The fixed three-dimensional cubic quotient gives an exact all-degree old-factor test, but non-descent through K does not exclude other factors. The next candidate must leave the classified profile orbit, solve the whole-source determinant and full marked ideal, and prove absolute atomicity separately. Bounded coefficient fitting or further formal approximation cannot replace those certificates.
