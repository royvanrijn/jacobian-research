EQUIVALENCE  OF  THE  FOUR-VARIABLE  HESSIAN  AND
                  PLANAR  JACOBIAN  CONJECTURES


                                         QIYUE TANG


         Abstract.We prove that the four-variable Hessian conjecture and the
         planar Jacobian conjecture are equivalent over the complex numbers.

   Dillen [10] proved the Hessian conjecture in dimension two, and de Bondt
[5] proved it in dimension three.  De Bondt’s proof uses a linear change of
variables and a positive weighting whose leading form has nonzero Hessian
determinant.  In July 2026, Alp¨oge announced a noninjective Keller map in
dimension three.  Its Jacobian determinant is−2 and three distinct points
have the same image [2,17].  Adjoining identity coordinates preserves both
the  determinant  and  the  collision,  so  the  example  refutes  JCn  for  every
n≥3.  Meng and Yang [15, Theorem 6.1] used doubling followed by a one-
variable partial Legendre transform to obtain a degree-fourteen polynomial
in five variables with Hessian determinant 128 and a noninjective gradient.
Adjoining a termx          2n+1  /2 adds an identity gradient coordinate and a Hessian
block  of  determinant  one,  so  this  example  refutes  HCn  for  everyn≥5.
The  one-variable  assertions  follow  from  the  fact  that  a  polynomial  with
nonzero constant derivative is affine.  The two remaining dimension-specific
assertions are therefore JC2 and HC4.
   For HC4, Ni [16, Theorem 1.2] treats quartic potentials by splitting the
highest homogeneous part according to its Hessian rank. Adamus and Hajto
[1,  Theorem  4.17  and  Remark  4.5]  develop  a  Legendre-potential  criterion
for invertibility and distinguish the known implication HC4 ⇒JC     2 from its
converse. The converse follows from the following description of four-variable
potentials.
