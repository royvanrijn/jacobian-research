# Primary sources used at the central-fibre gate

Accessed14 November 2026. The [manifest](sources/manifest.json) pins the
exact PDFs. Search-engine crawl dates are not publication dates.

Gwozdziewicz--Ploski, *Formulae for the singularities at infinity of
plane algebraic curves* (2001), Section4, printed page124, proves the
Suzuki--Gavrilov Euler identity for polynomials C2->C with isolated
critical points. It identifies the difference between a fibre's Euler
jump and its affine Milnor number with the sum of infinity Milnor jumps.
This is the named import in PF2R7CENT1. It turns the full omitted-prime
ledger into exact source infinity requirements, rather than a guess
based only on the image singularities.

Primary PDF: https://gasiull.webs.ull.es/Iagel2001.pdf

Borisov--Gabber--Vasiu, *On endomorphisms of affine spaces and the
Jacobian problem*, author-hosted file BGV160, Section11, was inspected
as a possible source-realization route. Theorem11.2 gives an A1-fibration
over P1 for a normal affine extension of A2 with irreducible complement.
Its part(2) makes the line-bundle conclusion equivalent to multiplicity1
of the boundary fibre. Corollary11.4 and Definition11.6 give the canonical
affine cover by one-boundary-component charts. Corollary11.8 discusses
the etale locus of the finite normalization. These statements are useful
for organizing each omitted prime, but do not provide one common ruled
chart for the whole normalization. Smoothness is not substituted for
the multiplicity1 hypothesis. Its positive-characteristic examples do
not close the characteristic-zero Keller case. This source is context,
not a premise of the central-fibre theorem; no priority date or referee
status is inferred from the filename or search metadata.

Primary PDF: https://www.ihes.fr/~/gabber/BGV160.pdf
