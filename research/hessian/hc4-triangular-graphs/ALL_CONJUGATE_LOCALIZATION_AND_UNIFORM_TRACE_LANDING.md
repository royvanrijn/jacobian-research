## 1. LD0: the all-conjugate algebra is the normal Galois cover of the proper locus

Let K:X=A^2 -> Y=A^2 be complex Keller. Put R=C[x,y], B=C[P,Q],
F=Frac(B), L=Frac(R), and let M/F be the NORMAL CLOSURE of L/F.
Use G=Gal(M/F), with a fixed embedding L subset M. Let S_L and S_M
be the full finite normalizations of B in those fields. Retained KMFI94
identifies X with its actual open in Spec(S_L), and S_L subset R.
Write N_K=V(f) for the TOTAL reduced nonproperness curve of K, as in KP0;
take f=1 for an automorphism. ALL omitted primes are retained in this definition.

Form the domain

    E=B[g(R):g in G] subset M.                            (1)

Then the exact identities are

    E=S_M[1/f],     E^G=B[1/f].                           (2)

In particular E is finite etale over B[1/f], with Galois group G.
This is an all-degree statement. In fact the construction and proof apply
to a dominant affine etale map over a normal characteristic-zero affine
base with finite normalization, whenever its proper locus is the indicated
principal open. It does NOT require the source to be an affine plane.
The source-plane hypothesis is used for the HC4 application and the
retained description of its total nonproperness curve, not to create (2).

Here is a direct proof, using only retained finite quotient, etale diagonal
and Zariski Main foundations.

First, E is an ACTUAL connected component of a finite tensor power of R
over B. For example tensor one copy for every g in G and evaluate that
copy by g:R -> M. Generically the tensor product is a finite separable
product, and the selected quotient is the field generated by all g(L),
namely M. The tensor algebra is etale over normal B, hence normal
(and regular for the present polynomial plane base).
Its finitely many irreducible components are disjoint and OPEN AND CLOSED.
All dominate B: flatness and openness prevent a nonempty component from
missing the generic point. The chosen generic field factor therefore
selects a whole component, and its coordinate algebra is precisely E.
Thus E is a normal finite-type ETALE B-algebra; it need not be B-finite.
Normality gives S_M subset E, because each element of S_M is integral
over B, hence over E, and belongs to Frac(E)=M.

The G-action on Spec(E) is FREE. To see the finite-point assertion as well
as its generic version, compare any two source projections g(R),h(R).
Their equalizer is the inverse image of the diagonal of X over Y, which
is open and closed since K is separated etale. On the connected component
Spec(E), projections which differ generically therefore NEVER agree.
For sigma!=1, some g(L) is moved by sigma because these conjugate fields
generate M. A sigma-fixed point would equate those two distinct source
projections, which is impossible. In characteristic zero the constant
finite group is etale; this proves the scheme-theoretic free-action condition.
The retained free affine quotient theorem now makes E finite etale and
faithfully flat over A=E^G. Descent from E/B makes A/B etale. Also
Frac(A)=M^G=F. Zariski Main over normal Y makes Spec(A) an OPEN IMMERSION
onto a dense open V subset Y.

E is finite over A and normal, so it is the ENTIRE normalization of A
in M. Consequently Spec(E) is the FULL inverse image of V in Spec(S_M),
not a selection of some sheets over V. Let H=Gal(M/L). The normalization
over V in L is Spec(E^H); it is finite over V. The map Spec(E)->X induced
by R subset E agrees over Y with the normalization map to Spec(S_L).
The finite normal map Spec(E)->Spec(E^H) is surjective. Its image is
contained in X_V because that map factors through the actual source.
Therefore ALL of Spec(E^H) is in X_V. Conversely X_V is already an open
of that normalization. Hence X_V equals Spec(E^H), so K is finite on V.

Conversely, on any open W where K is finite, it is finite etale. A tensor
power of that finite etale algebra is finite etale, and so is its chosen
open-and-closed component E_W. Thus A_W is finite over O_W and lies in
Frac(O_W). Normality of W implies A_W=O_W, so W subset V. This argument
can be made on any affine cover of W. We have proved that V is EXACTLY
the maximal open over which K is finite, its proper locus. Since K is
affine quasi-finite, proper over an open is equivalent to finite there.
By the definition of N_K this is Y minus N_K=D(f). Taking the normalization
over D(f) proves (2). This identifies the invariant base rather than
assuming it.

For EVERY subgroup J subset G we also have

    E^J=S_(M^J)[1/f].                                    (3)

Indeed invariants commute with localization at the invariant f, and
S_M^J is the normalization of B in M^J. In particular

    E^H=S_L[1/f]=R[1/f].                                 (4)

The last equality holds because K is finite on D(f). It is essential

## 2. LD1: uniform trace landing is stronger than clearing finite coefficients

There is a simple general algebraic dichotomy. Let B be a Noetherian
domain, L/F a finite separable extension of its fraction field, S a finite
B-order spanning L, and R' any B-subalgebra of L containing S. Set

    Lambda_S={a in L:Tr(a*S) subset B},
    T_(R')={a in L:Tr(a*R') subset B}.                    (5)

Lambda_S is B-finite: identify it with Hom_B(S,B) via the nondegenerate
generic trace pairing. No normality or finite local freeness of S is
needed for this assertion. Then

    T_(R') != 0  <==>  R' is B-finite.                   (6)

If 0!=a is in T_(R'), then for every r in R' and s in S,
Tr(a*r*s) is in B because r*s is in R'. Thus

    a*R' subset Lambda_S.                               (7)

The left side is a B-submodule of a finite B-module. Noetherianity makes
it finite, and multiplication by the nonzero a identifies it with R'.
Conversely, if R' is finite, a finite set of B-module generators has
finitely many rational traces. A nonzero common denominator b in B puts
Tr(b*R') inside B. This proves (6), with UNIFORM quantification over ALL
of R', not a bounded list of elements or derivatives.

For an ACTUAL plane Keller source, apply this with R'=R and S=S_L.
Finite K is finite etale over simply connected complex A2, hence an
automorphism. Consequently under noninvertibility,

    {a in L:Tr_(L/F)(a*R) subset B}=0.                   (8)

Equivalently NO nonzero multiplier carries the ENTIRE source into any
fixed B-finite trace-dual stage. This is a consequence conditional on
noninvertibility, not a contradiction proving that condition impossible.
It explains exactly why the existence of a nonzero denominator for a
FINITE coefficient or finite jet family is insufficient.

For the all-conjugate algebra the conclusion is even more explicit.
Let Lambda_M be the trace dual of S_M over B. Equation(2) gives

    Tr_(M/F)(E)=B[1/f],                                  (9)
    {a:Tr(a*f^-n*S_M) subset B}=f^n*Lambda_M, n>=0.        (10)

For (9), the finite etale trace lands in B[1/f] and any b in B[1/f]
is Tr(b/|G|). Equation(10) follows directly from the definition of the
trace dual. If f is a nonunit, then

    intersection_(n>=0) f^n*Lambda_M=0.                  (11)

For completeness, embed the finite module Lambda_M in a free fractional
B-lattice in M and localize at any prime divisor of f in the polynomial
ring B. Each nonzero vector has a finite valuation and cannot lie in
all arbitrarily deep powers. Thus NO single nonzero multiplier clears
the all-conjugate trace tower. Every FIXED finite stage has a nonzero
denominator, and increasing its depth loses every fixed multiplier.

The field extension M/F and the chord extension k0 subset E_C from LC0
are different. First CHORD derivatives reconstruct actual ordered charts;
they do not bound ORIGINAL-TARGET trace denominators of all source powers.
Adjoining all conjugates and their derivatives already produces the
localization in (2); it does not restore the omitted source data.

## 3. Exact two-dimensional control with a retained unramified sheet

Specialize the standard cubic finite normalization already tested in KU0:

    z=t^3-3*t, h(t)=3*t^2-3, Delta(z)=4-z^2.

Take the ACTUAL etale surjection

    X=Spec C[t,w,1/h] -> Y=A2_(z,w).                     (12)

times A1, NOT the whole affine plane; it is not a Keller counterexample.

value z, t^3-t-z has one removed double critical root and one retained
simple root. For example z=-2 has removed double root1 and retained
simple root-2, where h=9. Thus X still has an UNRAMIFIED source point over that value,
while its map is nonproper there. The total nonproperness divisor is
Delta=0, not the empty set.

    product_i (3*t_i^2-3)=27*z^2-108=-27*Delta,
    1/Delta=-27*product_i (3*t_i^2-3)^-1.                (13)

All factors on the right belong to the ALL-conjugate source algebra,
