# Quartic genus-three chord actions and period equations

This note gives exact canonical functions and action equations for the
GENERIC INTRINSIC QUARTIC branch of an actual complex plane Keller map
whose coordinate conductor has global directional degree six.

The equations retain the ordinary triple-point alternative. They also
exclude every persistently nonnodal quartic branch: a nonzero double
collision sum, the ordinary triple-point class, and the order-three
contact class are closed below. The remaining generic quartic branch
has three ordinary nodes; this note does not exclude it by itself.

The actual polynomial target pair is denoted by (P,Q). Canonical
functions p,q below belong to the y-extended generic coordinate order.
They are NOT asserted to be global Keller target coordinates.

## 1. Rational canonical functions from the filtered order

Put k=C(y), R_k=k[x], A_k=k[P,Q], and assume the generic plane
embedding is in the intrinsic-degree-four genus-three class of the
[audited primary table](sources/neumann-genus-three-source-audit.json).
Its affine-line normalization admits coordinate degrees three and
four. Target affine changes and an affine normalization parameter give

    p=u^3+A*u,
    q=u^4+B*u^2+C*u, A,B,C in k.                         (1)

The coefficients in(1), and the shift u=x-h(y), can be chosen
RATIONAL in y without a uniform family target automorphism.

Here is an exact descent argument. The generic conductor has degree
six and A_k has colength three. Membership of a polynomial of source
degree at most four in A_k is a finite k-linear condition: reduce
modulo the conductor and test membership in its finite-dimensional
subalgebra generated by P,Q. At most six successive degree spans of
products are needed in a six-dimensional algebra.

For a normalized degree-three/four pair, A_k has k[p]-basis
1,q,q^2. The leading degrees 3i+4j, 0<=j<=2, are distinct modulo
three. Thus its degree filtration has semigroup <3,4>, with gaps
1,2,5; degrees at most four have basis 1,p,q. The same dimensions
on a generic parameter open descend by finite linear algebra to k.

Choose the unique monic cubic modulo constants, and depress it by
the rational shift h. Choose the unique monic quartic modulo
the cubic and constants, removing its cubic term. This gives(1).
The coprime degree-three/four index is three, so these functions
generate A_k: the suborder they generate already has the same
colength as A_k.

Pointwise polynomial target equivalence to the actual quartic
embedding identifies these canonical functions. No rational
coefficient is thereby proved regular on every finite y-fibre.

## 2. The exact collision-sum cubic and immersion invariant

Let alpha!=beta be matched normalization parameters. Put

    sigma=alpha+beta, v=alpha*beta, delta=beta-alpha.

Equality of the cubic p gives v=sigma^2+A. Equality of q then gives

    g(sigma)=sigma^3-(B-2A)*sigma-C=0,
    delta^2=-3sigma^2-4A.                                (2)

Conversely(2), with delta!=0, reconstructs a matched unordered pair
as the roots of t^2-sigma*t+(sigma^2+A).

Set

    K=3sigma^2+4A,
    H=15sigma^2+6A-7B,
    U=2A-3B,
    R=4A*U^2+27C^2.

The exact identity

    R=Res_t(3t^2+A,4t^3+2Bt+C)=Res_sigma(g,K)             (3)

shows that R!=0 over k: normalization is immersed, and an invertible
target differential preserves that property. Thus K never vanishes
at a generic collision sum and delta!=0 there.

The monic conductor of(1) is

    kappa_0=u^6+(A+B)u^4+2C*u^3+(A^2-AB+B^2)u^2
                 +C(A+B)u+A(A-B)^2+C^2.                 (4)

It follows either from the monogenic trace-dual ratio or by eliminating
the collision sum. The actual generic conductor is a nonzero k-scalar
times kappa_0(x-h(y)). In particular its root centroid is h.

## 3. A compact exact formula for every chord action

Use coefficientwise polynomial integration, so no integration path
choice is involved. A primitive of p*q' is

    F(t)=4t^7/7+(2B+4A)t^5/5+C*t^4/4
                            +2AB*t^3/3+AC*t^2/2.

The divided differences h_n=(beta^n-alpha^n)/(beta-alpha) satisfy

    h_1=1, h_2=sigma,
    h_n=sigma*h_(n-1)-(sigma^2+A)*h_(n-2).

Substitute these in F(beta)-F(alpha), and reduce by g. The result is

    integral_alpha^beta p*dq
       =delta*K^2*H/420.                                (5)

The canonical action vector cannot vanish at every matched pair,
for ANY immersed quartic parametrization(1). If g has three
distinct roots, simultaneous vanishing would make the nonzero
quadratic H vanish at three points, since K is invertible there.
If g has a repeated root sigma_0, the exact identity

    H+2K=7*g'(sigma)

gives H(sigma_0)=-2K(sigma_0)!=0. That pair alone has nonzero action.
This includes the triple collision-sum root.

Thus every actual fibre embedding in the quartic ambient class has
some nonzero physical matching-endpoint action, by polynomial
target-action scaling. This is a vector statement, not a claim
that every individual chord action is nonzero.

## 4. Common rational scale and the clean three period equations

First assume g is separable. Its three matched pairs include the
ordinary triple-point case; disjointness of all six endpoints is
not being assumed.

Polynomial target automorphisms multiply every matching-endpoint
action by their same constant nonzero Jacobian. Consequently there
is one common scale Lambda, absorbing 1/420, such that

    I_sigma=integral_alpha^beta P*Q_u*du
           =Lambda*delta*K^2*H.                         (6)

It can be defined using any nonzero canonical chord and does not
require a family of target automorphisms.

In fact Lambda belongs to k. The actual chord divided by delta is
a symmetric divided difference of the polynomial primitive of P*Q_u.
It is therefore a polynomial in sigma and v=sigma^2+A, reduced
modulo g, with coefficients in k. The same holds for K^2*H.
Their common scalar identity in k[sigma]/(g) descends Lambda by
comparing any nonzero coefficient of the canonical vector.

The moving-endpoint identity gives

    I_sigma'=-J*delta, J=P_u*Q_y-P_y*Q_u in C*.

Implicit differentiation of g gives
sigma'=((B'-2A')sigma+C')/g'(sigma). The denominator cancels
EXACTLY in the differentiated action:

    d_y(K^2*H)+(delta'/delta)*K^2*H
       =K*[14(6A-5B-3sigma^2)A'
                       +28(3sigma^2-A)B'+105sigma*C'].   (7)

Hence the polynomial

    Lambda'*K^2*H
       +Lambda*K*[14(6A-5B-3sigma^2)A'
                          +28(3sigma^2-A)B'+105sigma*C']
       +J

is divisible by g. An exact inverse used to reduce it is

    K^-1=(3U*sigma^2+9C*sigma+U^2)/R modulo g.

Put V=2B-A. The three coefficient equations are therefore

    12V*Lambda'+42V'*Lambda=-3J*U/R,                    (8)

    45C*Lambda'+105C'*Lambda=-9J*C/R,                   (9)

    4A(6A-7B)*Lambda'
       +[14(6A-5B)A'-28A*B']Lambda=-J*U^2/R.           (10)

They are necessary identities of RATIONAL FUNCTIONS. No assertion
that R is a polynomial unit or Lambda has no finite pole is implicit.

Subtracting U times one third of(8) from(10) gives

    (A-B)*[2W*Lambda'+7W'*Lambda]=0,
    W=4A-3B.                                           (11)

Thus outside the ordinary triple-point alternative A=B, the
nonzero-W subcase has first integral Lambda^2*W^7 in C*.
The cases A=B and W=0 are retained separately.

## 5. Persistent double collision sums are impossible

Suppose g has a double root sigma!=0 over the generic field.
The two corresponding source tangent vectors are parallel:
their determinant is, up to the nonzero gap,

    -K*g'(sigma).

For ANY persistent matched pair of an actual constant-Jacobian map,
parallel tangent vectors must be EQUAL. Indeed, write the second
vector as lambda times the first. Differentiate the two endpoint
identities along y; the discrepancy of their y derivatives is
parallel to the first tangent vector. Taking determinants gives

    J(beta)=lambda*J(alpha),

so the nonzero constant J forces lambda=1.

Equality of the tangent vectors is preserved by the invertible
target differential at their COMMON target point, even when the
target automorphism varies fibrewise. In canonical coordinates,

    p'(beta)-p'(alpha)=3*sigma*delta!=0.

This contradiction excludes a persistent double root sigma!=0.
A depressed cubic with a triple root has that root at zero, giving

    B=2A, C=0.                                         (12)

The latter order-three contact branch is not discarded by the
tangent-vector argument; it is closed below by a fixed-order argument.

## 6. Persistent triple-point and order-three contact families become fixed

There are two additional exact conductor identities from(4):

    B=A:
      kappa_0=(u^3+A*u+C)^2,
      q=u*(p+C);

    B=2A,C=0:
      kappa_0=(u^2+A)^3.                                (13)

The first has three matched preimages of one ordinary triple point.
The second has two smooth branches with intersection multiplicity
three. The generic normalization is immersed in both cases.

Write the actual conductor in original source coordinates as
a_6(y)*G(x,y)^e, where G is monic of degree three or two over k,
and e is respectively two or three. The leading coefficient a_6
is polynomial. It cannot have a finite zero.

To prove this, take a finite conductor branch over a proposed zero
of a_6; one exists because every conductor fibre is nonzero of positive
degree. All roots of G are generic mates of that branch. Their equal
actual P values and monicity of P force ALL these roots to remain
finite after a common Puiseux substitution.

No two of them can merge. Dividing their identical P,Q values by
their nonzero parameter difference and taking the limit would give
P_x=Q_x=0 at the common limit, contrary to the actual Keller map.
Thus G has regular coefficients at this base value. Multiplication
by a_6=0 would make the ENTIRE conductor fibre zero, impossible.

Consequently a_6 is a nonzero constant. The monic rational factor G
of the polynomial kappa/a_6 has polynomial coefficients, by normality
of C[y]. Its midpoint h is polynomial as well.

In the contact case G=u^2+A(y), no two roots may merge, so A(y)
has no zero and is constant. In the triple-point case
G=u^3+A(y)u+C(y), its discriminant has no zero:

    4A^3+27C^2 in C*.

This forces A,C constant. If neither is constant or zero, their
coprimality and the differentiated identity give

    C divides A', A^2 divides C',

which would require deg C<=deg A-1 and
2deg A<=deg C-1. The constant or zero cases give the same
conclusion directly. Thus in both alternatives the entire
centered conductor and its exact (3,4) order are FIXED.

## 7. Fixed orders cannot evade the action contradiction by changing ambient type

The actual functions P,Q on a generic fibre belong to this fixed
finite-colength order T. Its conditions modulo the fixed conductor
are finite linear conditions with constant coefficients, so the
polynomial family belongs to T on EVERY fibre. The exact specialized
defect remains three. Hence A_c=T on every fibre.

The degree filtration of T has gaps1,2,5, so it contains no nonconstant
degree-two element. A special fibre therefore cannot be in the
hyperelliptic genus-three class, which has a quadratic normalization
coordinate. The primary table leaves only quartic or cable embeddings.

For quartic embeddings the nonzero physical action-vector statement
is Section3. For cable embeddings it is the exact
[cable weighted-period theorem](CABLE_GENUS_THREE_WEIGHTED_PERIODS_AND_NONVANISHING.md).
That result concerns the physical cable coordinate action, not
ordinary intrinsic quartic actions; no target covariance is inferred
from an abstract order isomorphism.

Choose a fixed chord on which the generic quartic canonical action
is nonzero. Its actual action is an affine polynomial in y with
nonzero slope -J*(beta-alpha), by the fixed-endpoint identity.
The common scalar Lambda is therefore affine nonconstant. All
matching-pair actions vanish at its complex zero, since their generic
proportionality extends as a polynomial identity.

But at that fibre the embedding is quartic or cable, and each has
a NONZERO physical action vector. This contradiction closes both
fixed alternatives in(13). Together with Section5, every persistently
nonnodal quartic branch is excluded.

## 8. Remaining scope and exact controls

The remaining generic intrinsic-quartic case has g separable and
B!=A: three ordinary nodes with six distinct normalization parameters.
Equations(8)--(11) still apply. Rational canonical coefficient descent
does not supply regularity at every finite parameter, a constant
immersion resultant, or a global family target automorphism.

The checker verifies the exact conductor, collision equations,
compact action formula, derivative cancellation, three equations,
first-integral factor, tangent determinant, both exceptional conductor
powers, and the local degree obstruction used for fixed triple points.
Finite controls do not prove unrestricted coefficient regularity.

The attributed Neumann classification and the retained cable action
lemma are explicit inputs. No full quartic or conductor-degree-six
closure, automatic trace integrality, HC4 or JC2 is claimed here.
Independent replay, formal verification and external human review
are not claimed.
