## 2. An all-degree check against the literal linear classification

For ANY integers p,q>=2 let

    U=x+y^p, V=y+U^q, F=(U,V),

This is a composition of the familiar polynomial shears, with explicit
inverse

    y=V-U^q, x=U-(V-U^q)^p.

Its Jacobian is1 and its derivative at0 is the identity. The real
Hamiltonian vector field (-H_y,H_x) is therefore globally conjugate
by F to the linear rotation (-V,U). Every nonzero orbit is periodic,

It cannot be transformed LINEARLY into the single-shear form above.
Every single-shear Hamiltonian has D_v^3 H=0 for some nonzero constant
direction v (the direction of its quadratic variable). This property
is preserved by nonsingular linear changes, translations, scalar
multiplication of H and addition of a constant.

For our H, its unique top-degree term is y^(2pq)/2. For
v=a partial_x+b partial_y, vanishing of D_v^3 H forces b=0 by that
highest-degree term. But H has degree2q in x with leading coefficient

NO such nonzero direction in ANY p,q. This is a written parameter
