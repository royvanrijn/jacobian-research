# Generic coordinate specialization closes the K2 one-coordinate route

15 November 2026. Claims **HC4MYJW0/1/2**. No polynomial source change
that keeps either native hyperbolic coordinate of K2 affine can produce
unit Hessian. All other coordinates may mix arbitrarily and source
degrees are unrestricted. This closes the K2 one-coordinate question
left open by JR/JT/JV; it does not close its full source orbit.

The new argument controls an actual generic plane coordinate, not only
a displayed locally nilpotent action or a rational quotient type. It
rules out every possible pole along the exceptional source hyperplane
at once, then obstructs its specialization by counting actual affine-line
components across a complete pencil of plane curves.

## 1. Statements

**JW0, specialization and no poles.** Let k have characteristic zero.
If f in k[x,u,v] is a coordinate of k(x)[u,v] and f0=f(0,u,v) is
nonconstant, then over an algebraic closure of k,

    f0=H(K), where K is a plane coordinate.               (1)

In particular every geometric irreducible component of every fibre
of f0 is an actual embedded affine line. This is a necessary condition
for a coordinate degeneration, not a claim that f0 is itself a coordinate.
For example v^2+xu is a generic coordinate specializing to v^2.

Let F in k[x,u,v] have F0 nonzero, and suppose F0 has a geometric
irreducible curve component not isomorphic to A1. In the domain

    R=k[x,u,v,Y]/(xY-F), R_x=k[x,x^-1,u,v],             (2)

every T in R that is a coordinate of k(x)[u,v] belongs already to
k[x,u,v]. Thus actual generic coordinates in this modification cannot
hide arbitrary negative powers of x. No bound on any degree or pole
order is used.

**JW1, the complete K2 exceptional pencil.** Over k=C(s), set

    z=s+uv, F_s=v+(s+uv)^2-1,
    Gamma={F_s=0} subset A2_(u,v).                     (3)

The geometric curve Gamma is A1_z minus {1,-1}. There is no polynomial
plane coordinate K whose restriction to Gamma is alpha*z+beta, for
any alpha!=0,beta in the algebraic closure of k.

In fact the whole pencil F_s=c has exactly one affine-line component
in total: the line v=0 at c=s^2-1. Every other component is a punctured
line with at least one puncture. The two values omitted by alpha*z+beta
would require two distinct coordinate-line fibres disjoint from Gamma,
which cannot both lie in that one available affine-line component.

**JW2, K2 one-coordinate closure.** Let

    h=K2=w(2xy+z^2-1)+z.

For every polynomial automorphism Phi of A4, if Phi^*x or Phi^*y is
a nonconstant affine-linear polynomial, then det Hess(h composed with
Phi) is not a nonzero constant. This includes preservation of C[x]
or C[y] setwise and arbitrary mixing of the other three variables.

The remaining full-source question must change both pullbacks nonlinearly.
The same conclusion holds for value-preserving source conjugates of
these tests: if h composed with psi=h, apply the assertion whenever
(psi inverse composed with Phi)^*x or its y analogue is affine-linear.
This is not a classification of all h-preserving actions fixing x:
an invariant x need not be a base coordinate in an arbitrary quotient.

A related concrete consequence is that K2 is not source-equivalent,
by an x-preserving map, to

    C2=2xzP+(z^2-1)Q+z.                                (4)

It remains unknown here whether a full source equivalence exists that
changes both coordinate algebras. C2 still supplies no new endpoint or
constructed unit-Hessian member.

## 3. Hamiltonian specialization controls every coordinate pole

For a polynomial f in two variables use the Hamiltonian derivation

    D_f=f_u partial_v-f_v partial_u.

If f is a coordinate over k(x), choose a complement g. Its Jacobian
J(f,g) is a nonzero element of k(x), so D_f is a constant multiple
of partial_g in the polynomial ring k(x)[f,g]. It is locally nilpotent.

When f is in k[x,u,v], D_f preserves that ring and fixes x. Every
iterated-derivative identity proved after inverting x holds before
inversion, since the ring injects into its localization. Thus D_f is
locally nilpotent on k[x,u,v], and its reduction modulo x is the plane
LND D_(f0). Nonconstant f0 and characteristic zero make this derivation
nonzero. The imported plane theorem gives ker D_(f0)=k[K] for a
coordinate K, after scalar extension if needed. Since f0 is in its
kernel, (1) follows. Factor H(K)-c over the algebraic closure: each
geometric irreducible factor is K-a and hence has affine-line zero set.

For (2), write T in R_x and choose the least m>=0 with

    f=x^m T in k[x,u,v].

Multiplication by x^m does not change being a generic plane coordinate.
If m>0, minimality makes f not belong to x k[x,u,v], while f lies
in xR. The kernel of

    k[u,v] -> R/(x)=k[u,v,Y]/(F0)

is (F0), so F0 divides the nonzero reduction f0. In particular f0
is nonconstant and has the specified non-A1 geometric component.
This contradicts (1). Therefore m=0. This proves JW0 in all degrees,
including every possible Laurent pole order simultaneously.

This does not justify clearing arbitrary denominators in a rational
quotient generator: T must be an actual element of R and a coordinate
of the generic two-variable polynomial ring. An abstract rational
generator or a merely algebraically independent invariant is insufficient.

## 4. Every component of the exceptional plane pencil

Work over an algebraic closure of C(s), where s!=0 and s^2!=1.
The parameter z=s+uv gives, on F_s=c,

    v=1+c-z^2, u=(z-s)/(1+c-z^2).                      (5)

If 1+c is nonzero and differs from s^2, the two roots of
1+c-z^2 are distinct and neither equals s. Formula (5) and its
reverse z=s+uv identify the whole fibre with A1 minus those two roots.
If 1+c=0, it identifies the whole fibre with A1 minus {0}, since s!=0.
These are integral curves with nonconstant units and cannot be A1.

For 1+c=s^2 the entire factorization is

    F_s-(s^2-1)=v(1+2su+u^2v).                         (6)

The first component is the affine line v=0. The second has u invertible
and v=-(1+2su)/u^2, so it is Gm. They meet at u=-1/(2s),v=0.
This lists all components of every fibre, including both special values.
At c=0, (5) gives precisely Gamma=A1 minus {1,-1}.

Suppose a coordinate K restricts to alpha*z+beta on Gamma. It omits
the two distinct values beta+alpha and beta-alpha there. Each of the
two coordinate fibres is an actual closed A1 in the plane, disjoint
from Gamma. The restriction of F_s to that line is a polynomial unit
on A1 and therefore a nonzero constant. Consequently each coordinate
line is an irreducible component of some F_s=c fibre. The complete
list above supplies only one A1 component in the entire pencil. The
two distinct coordinate fibres cannot coincide with it. This proves JW1.

A further useful formulation is that no polynomial H(K), with K a
coordinate, can restrict to alpha*z+beta either. Degrees of nonconstant
rational functions on P1 multiply under composition. Since alpha*z+beta
has degree one, H must have degree one and K must already have an
affine z restriction, which JW1 forbids.

## 5. Any x-preserving unit member would supply the forbidden coordinate

First suppose Phi preserves C[x]. A constant affine precomposition
normalizes it to Phi^*x=x and preserves unit Hessian. The native K2
has two Morse critical points, at x=y=0,z=+-1,w=-1/(2z), with values
+-1. Thus g=h composed with Phi could not have an invertible gradient.

Its three-relative critical scheme transports from the exact native one

    (h_y,h_z,h_w)=(x,z^2-1,2wz+1).                    (7)

To check equality, h_z=2wz+1 makes w invertible in the quotient;
h_y=2xw then kills x and h_w=2xy+z^2-1 kills z^2-1. The reverse
inclusion follows from these relations. Hence (7) is exactly two
closed reduced affine lines, both at x=0.

Tang supplies a constant jointly affine two-plane W for g. Its projection
to x has rank zero or one. In rank one the retained GR/JR calculation
identifies the entire three-relative critical scheme with one component
fibre G=0 of a plane Keller pair. Its actual closed A1 component makes
that pair invertible by GR0, contradicting the two full critical points.

In rank zero, constant coordinate changes preserving x give

    g=d(x,t)+P F(x,t)+Q G(x,t), J_(x,t)(F,G) in C*.     (8)

Pull these actual coordinates back by Phi inverse. In the native ring
there is therefore a full polynomial coordinate system (x,T,P~,Q~)
with h of form (8) in (x,T). Over C(x), F,G are coprime polynomials
in T. A Bezout completion of their row makes h a coordinate along
with T. In particular T is a coordinate of the actual generic plane
over C(x,h), not merely a rational-field generator.

We next determine the actual trace T0=T modulo x. At x=0 the native
function is B(z,w)=w(z^2-1)+z, with y free. In (8) write
F0=C(t)a(t),G0=C(t)b(t),gcd(a,b)=1. As in JR, the two reduced
critical lines of the trace force C to have exactly two distinct
simple roots t_+,t_-. The Keller Jacobian ensures a common root
cannot have higher multiplicity. An SL2(C[t]) row completion gives

    g0=d0(t)+C(t)R, S free.

Both plane generic fibres are punctured rational lines with at least
two omitted completion points. JR0 identifies their intrinsic plane
algebras. Thus T0 is an actual coordinate of C[z,w]. The line
t=t_+ in this plane is an A1 component of the B=1 fibre, and
t=t_- is one of B=-1, after relabeling. The exact factorizations

    B-1=(z-1)(w(z+1)+1),
    B+1=(z+1)(w(z-1)+1)                               (9)

show each level has just one A1 component; its other component is Gm.
Hence T0-t_+ and T0-t_- are constant multiples of z-1 and z+1.
Subtracting these identities forces their multipliers to agree, so

    T0=alpha*z+beta, alpha in C*, beta in C.             (10)

Now use the uniform native K2 coordinates

    s=h, u=w, v=1-2xy-z^2, z=s+uv,
    2xy+v+(s+uv)^2-1=0.                               (11)

This is the actual presentation R=S[y]/(2xy+F_s),
S=C[x,s,u,v], with R_x=S_x. It is not a four-coordinate automorphism.
The coordinate T from (8) is a native polynomial and a coordinate of
C(x,s)[u,v]. JW0, over k=C(s), excludes every x pole of T, since
F_s=0 is the non-A1 curve Gamma. Therefore T lies in C(s)[x,u,v];
its original expression in S_x also shows it lies in S itself.

Apply JW0's specialization statement once more. The nonconstant
polynomial T(0,s,u,v) is H(K) over the algebraic closure of C(s),
with K a plane coordinate. On Gamma its restriction is alpha*z+beta
by (10). The last formulation of JW1 forbids exactly this. The
contradiction closes the rank-zero case and proves the x-preserving
assertion. Interchanging x,y proves the y-preserving assertion.

If Phi^*x is any nonconstant affine-linear L, a constant affine
precomposition A with L composed with A=x reduces to that assertion.
The actual Hessian of (h composed with Phi) composed with A remains
a unit because A is constant affine. This proves the full affine
pullback form of JW2. No nonlinear Hessian invariance is used.
For the stated value-preserving extension, h composed with Phi equals
h composed with (psi inverse composed with Phi); apply exactly this
affine-pullback test to that left-composed map.

## 6. Boundary, comparison and assurance

The same forbidden-coordinate proof excludes an x-preserving source
equivalence between K2 and C2 in (4): its actual base (x,z) would
supply the coordinate T in Section 5. Its coefficient row (2xz,z^2-1)
is coprime over C(x)[z], and its x=0 trace is the same B. No Keller
condition on that row is required for this comparison. The equality
of the trace and compatible critical slope signs do not provide a
global source equivalence.

This closes a real source class left open by the constant-slope JR
argument. It does not classify all actions fixing x,all generic plane
quotients,or all automorphisms changing both x and y nonlinearly. The
four old tests and the full K2,S_rho source questions remain open.
The auxiliary T_N fibre-cardinality models remain separate from the
original affine graph frontier unless a realization is proved.

The checker controls exact specialization,plane-pencil,relative-ideal,
trace-alignment and generic-coordinate completion identities. All-degree
conclusions come from the written argument and the one explicit plane
classification import,not a pole-order or source-degree search. It is
not independent replay,formal verification or external review. No
unit-Hessian source member,nonstandard necessary action,endpoint solution
or unrestricted HC4/JC2 solution is constructed. The goal remains open.
