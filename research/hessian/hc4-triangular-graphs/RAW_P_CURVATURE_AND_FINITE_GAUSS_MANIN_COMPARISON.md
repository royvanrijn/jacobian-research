## 1. LS0: zero raw p-curvature is automatic before the mate

Let k have characteristic p>0, R=k[x,y], and P be a polynomial with a
polynomial lift E satisfying E(P)=1. Set

    D=P_y partial_x-P_x partial_y,   b=div E,
    M_p=R/D(R),   T[a]=[E(a)+b*a].                 (1)

The LP0 identities [E,D]=-b*D and (E+b)D=DE work in EVERY
characteristic. They make T well defined, with T(P*a)=a+P*T(a).
For the base derivation partial_P, partial_P^[p]=0. We prove

    T^p=0 on M_p.                                (2)

The p-th power E^p is a derivation and annihilates P. Because E(P)=1,
the unimodular row (P_x,P_y) has its polynomial syzygy generated by
(P_y,-P_x). Hence E^p=h*D for some h in R.

On the exterior algebra of differentials, Lie_E is a degree-zero
derivation, so its p-th power is a derivation. On the generators a,da,
it equals Lie_(E^p). Therefore Lie_E^p=Lie_(E^p). On top forms a*dxdy,
Lie_E acts by E+b, and we obtain the EXACT operator identity

    (E+b)^p = E^p+div(E^p)
            = h*D+D(h) = D composed with multiplication by h. (3)

This proves(2), including after localization in the base P. It is the
vanishing of the p-curvature of the RAW characteristic-p quotient.
It uses NO polynomial Keller mate, no bounded degree and no Cartier
or p-curvature conjecture. For an actual Keller pair the normalized
dual lifts E,D even satisfy E^p=D^p=0 on the source ring: both p-th
powers annihilate P and Q, whose gradients form a basis. This stronger
source identity still does NOT identify the finite Gauss-Manin model.

## 3. A geometric comparison can lose injectivity at EVERY odd prime

    J(t)=0,J(x)=1,J(y)=F_x/(2y).                   (6)

For clarity, the column convention is nabla(e_j)=sum_i C_ij e_i.
