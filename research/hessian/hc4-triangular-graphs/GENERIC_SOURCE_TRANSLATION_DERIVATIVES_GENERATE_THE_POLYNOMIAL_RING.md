## 2. Finiteness from a linear slice

    c_v=delta_v^D P=D!*P_D(v) in C*,

The source polynomial s is affine linear. Choose a complementary affine

Put A=J_v intersect C[z]. The elementary slice identity gives

    J_v=A[s].                                               (3)

sum_j(-s)^j delta_v^(i+j)f/(i!*j!), a finite sum in J_v killed by delta_v.
Thus each coefficient lies in A. Conversely (3) is immediate. In particular
A is generated by the finitely many coefficient polynomials of P,Q in s:
their derivatives lie in that coefficient ring, and coefficient extraction
puts them back in J_v.

Since Frac J_v=C(s,z), Frac A=C(z). Hence A contains a nonconstant h(z).
The equation h(T)-h(z), scaled to be monic, makes z integral over A. It
follows that R=A[s,z] is a FINITE J_v-MODULE. No normality of A or J_v

## 3. Only finitely many whole simultaneous-translation collision lines exist

    p(s)=(s,alpha+t*s),

These parameters specify distinct ENTIRE affine lines, not points on a line.
It is enough to show this parameter scheme has dimension zero, since it
then has finitely many closed points.

Suppose a positive-dimensional family exists and choose an irreducible
parameter curve T of distinct lines. Its universal line family has dense
image in an irreducible component C0 of C: a one-dimensional closed curve
has only finitely many irreducible components and hence cannot contain
infinitely many distinct closed affine lines. Write w=q-p:C0->A2.

## 4. Finite, injective and unramified gives the polynomial ring

Exclude the finite collision-line directions of Section3 and the finite

    p(s,t)=(s,s*t), q(s,t)=(s+t,s*t+t^2/2).
