# Boundary-coloring surgery for the \(A_4\) cone

## 1. Outcome

**Status:** exact symbolic experiment.  The two displayed modifications
fail; no universal obstruction to all threefold surgeries is claimed.

The simplest elementary transformation can move the displayed affine
ramification divisor out of the affine chart, but it does not exchange it
with a second ramified prime.

There is no second global height-one prime over the target cubic.  The exact
branch datum is one prime

\[
 (e,f)=(2,2),
\]

so its local degree \(ef=4\) already exhausts the degree-four cover.  The two
cycles of the double transposition appear only after splitting the residue
extension.  They are not two global divisors that can be used simultaneously
as centers of a birational modification over the original target field.

For the residual center \((L,W)\), retain the blowup chart

\[
 q=\frac W L,\qquad W=Lq.
\]

This chart is again \(\mathbb A^3_{U,V,q}\).  It preserves the \(A_4\)
function-field extension and keeps the three target coordinates polynomial.
The old generic \(L\)-valuation has no center in the retained chart, so that
part of the boundary-coloring surgery succeeds.

The replacement divisor is not the missing ramified prime.  The exceptional
divisor \(E=(L=0)\) maps to the target origin, rather than to the generic
point of the target branch cubic.  Moreover the relative Jacobian is

\[
 \det D(U,V,Lq)=L.
\]

It contributes one relative-canonical copy of \(E\) and changes the
determinant ledger.  The transformed polynomial map has

\[
 \det D\Phi'=4q^2K^3L^4,
\qquad
 \mathcal B(\Phi')=q^3K^3L^5.
\]

Thus the proposed elementary transform preserves the field, monodromy,
double-transposition inertia, and polynomial target coordinates, but fails
the determinant-ledger requirement.  It also retains affine critical
divisors, so it is not Keller.

Using the reconstruction denominator instead of \(W\) fares worse.  The
chart \(x=H/L\), defined by \(Lx=H\), is singular over the conjugate
triple-contact cluster.  These are exact failures of the two most direct
surgeries, not a theorem excluding every birational threefold modification.

## 2. The complete branch valuation

On the normalization of \(L=0\), put \(t=U-V\).  The induced parameter on
the normalization of the target cubic is

\[
 s=\frac{P-Q}{R}=\frac{t^2}{2t-3}.
\]

Consequently the residue extension is generated by

\[
 t^2-2st+3s=0.
\]

Its discriminant is \(4s(s-3)\), and the polynomial is irreducible over
\(\mathbb Q(s)\).  Since the pullback of the target cubic has order two
along \(L\),

\[
 e(L/\mathcal B)=2,\qquad f(L/\mathcal B)=2.
\]

Therefore

\[
 e f=4=\deg\Phi.
\]

There is no remaining local degree from which to infer another global prime.
Over a separable closure of the residue field, the degree-two residue
extension splits and inertia acts by two 2-cycles.  Birationally changing the
source model does not turn those two residue embeddings into two distinct
global height-one primes over \(\mathbb Q(s)\).

This corrects the premise of a surgery center involving an “inferred second
valuation”: such a third global center is not defined in the current
function field.

## 3. The residual-center elementary transform

Start from

\[
 \Phi(U,V,W)=(WN_1,WN_2,WH)
\]

with

\[
 \det D\Phi=4W^2K^3L,\qquad
 \mathcal B(\Phi)=W^3K^3L^2.
\]

Blow up the codimension-two center

\[
 C=V(L,W)
\]

and retain the \(L\)-chart

\[
 X'=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb Q[U,V,q],
\qquad
 \pi(U,V,q)=(U,V,Lq).
\]

This is the standard affine modification obtained by deleting the strict
transform of \(L=0\).  At the generic point,

\[
 q=\frac W L,
\]

so

\[
 \mathbb Q(U,V,q)=\mathbb Q(U,V,W).
\]

The target subfield and hence the generic \(A_4\) monodromy are unchanged.
The transformed coordinates are

\[
 \Phi'=(qLN_1,qLN_2,qLH),
\]

which remain polynomial.

The color change can be read directly from \(\pi\).

- The old generic point \(L=0,\ W\ne0\) is absent because \(W=Lq\).
- The new exceptional divisor \(E=(L=0)\) has
  \[
  v_E(L)=v_E(W)=1,\qquad v_E(q)=0.
  \]
- Every coordinate of \(\Phi'\) vanishes on \(E\).  Hence \(E\) is
  contracted to the target origin and does not dominate
  \(\mathcal B=0\).

Thus the new affine-colored divisor is not a replacement ramification prime
over the target cubic.

The chain rule gives the exact obstruction:

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \det D\pi&=L,\\
 \det D\Phi'
 &=\pi^*(\det D\Phi)\det D\pi\\
 &=4q^2K^3L^4.
\end{aligned}
\]

Meanwhile

\[
 \mathcal B(\Phi')=q^3K^3L^5,
\qquad
 \frac{\mathcal B(\Phi')}{\det D\Phi'}
 =\frac{qL}{4}.
\]

The original \(L\)-valuation has crossed the affine boundary, but the
exceptional discrepancy has inserted another \(L\) into the ordinary
Jacobian.  In this explicit model, the stable obstruction is therefore a
relative-canonical contribution rather than a conductor-length change.

## 4. Reconstruction-denominator center

The other direct center uses \(L=H=0\).  On the normalization of \(L\),

\[
 H|_L
 =
 \frac{(2t-3)(t^2-3t+9)^3}
 {t^2(t-3)^2}.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
 \operatorname{Res}_U(L,H)
 =
 19683(2V+3)(V^2+3V+9)^3.
\]

There is one transverse intersection and a conjugate degree-two
triple-contact cluster.  The affine modification chart

\[
 x=\frac H L,\qquad Lx-H=0
\]

has Jacobian ideal with Gröbner basis

\[
 \left(
 x+1-U-\frac23UV,\quad U^2,\quad V^2+3V+9
 \right).
\]

After adjoining the free \(W\)-coordinate, its reduced singular locus is the
pair of conjugate lines

\[
 U=0,\qquad x=-1,\qquad V^2+3V+9=0.
\]

This denominator-centered chart is therefore singular and cannot be an
affine-space source for a Keller map.

## 5. What remains open

The calculation closes only the two elementary charts above.

1. The \((L,W)\) transform proves that changing the color of the old
   valuation is birationally easy, but preserving the ordinary determinant
   ledger is not.
2. The \((L,H)\) transform is already singular at the reconstruction
   contact cluster.
3. There is no second global ramified divisor to exchange with \(L\) while
   retaining the same target field.

A surviving surgery would need a coupled source/target log-crepant
modification that compensates the relative-canonical divisor, remains
quasi-finite on the affine chart, and does not require splitting the residue
field.  No such modification is constructed here.

Run


The checker recomputes the complete \((e,f)=(2,2)\) branch datum, verifies
