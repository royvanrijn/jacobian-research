# Affine-generator Wronskian obstruction for the final `[4]` branch

## Theorem

> **HC4RSD69.** Let
> \[
> A=p(x)y+q(x)z+R(x,w)
> \]
> over characteristic zero and assume \(T=\operatorname{Hess}A\) has generic
> rank three.  If a polynomial Hessian metric \(S=\operatorname{Hess}\psi\)
> has \(\det S\in K^*\) and
> \[
> \det(S+sT)=\det S
> \]
> identically in \(s\), with generic relative Jordan type `[4]`, then
> \[
> \left(\frac{q'}{p'}\right)'=0
> \]
> on every chart where \(p'\ne0\).  Thus the projective kernel line of
> \(T\) is constant.

This strictly contains HC4RSD68.  No degree bound is used.

## Proof

Work over the localization where \(a=p'\ne0\), and put

\[
r=\frac{q'}{p'},\qquad
D=\partial_z-r\partial_y,\qquad
u=y+rz.
\]

The kernel of \(T\) is generated by \(D\).  Generic rank three is equivalent
on this chart to \(R_{ww}\ne0\).

The top pencil coefficient gives

\[
D^2\psi=0,
\]

hence

\[
\psi=zP(x,u,w)+Q(x,u,w).                     \tag{1}
\]

A direct exact expansion of the next coefficient gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
[s^2]\det(S+sT)
={}&3R_{ww}a r' zP_u^2
 -R_{ww}R_{xx}P_u^2-R_{ww}u\,a'P_u^2
 +R_{xw}^2P_u^2\\
&+2R_{ww}a r'P_uQ_u+2R_{ww}aP_uP_x
 -2R_{xw}aP_uP_w+a^2P_w^2.                 \tag{2}
\end{aligned}
\]

If the kernel moves, then \(r'\ne0\).  Since \(aR_{ww}\ne0\), the coefficient
of \(z\) in (2) forces \(P_u=0\), and then (2) reduces to \(a^2P_w^2\).
Thus

\[
P=P(x).                                      \tag{3}
\]

Writing \(H=P\), the final two exact identities are

\[
[s]\det(S+sT)
=-R_{ww}Q_{uu}(r'Q_u+H')^2,                 \tag{4}
\]

and

\[
\det S
=-(Q_{uu}Q_{ww}-Q_{uw}^2)(r'Q_u+H')^2.     \tag{5}
\]

Nonzero \(\det S\) and (4) imply \(Q_{uu}=0\).  Equation (5) becomes

\[
\det S=Q_{uw}^2(r'Q_u+H')^2\in K^*.
\]

Regard this identity in the polynomial ring \(K(x)[u,w]\).  Both factors are
units of that ring.  Hence \(Q_{uw}\in K(x)^*\), while \(Q_{uu}=0\) gives

\[
Q=u(c(x)w+\ell(x))+M(x,w),\qquad c(x)\ne0.
\]

But then

\[
r'Q_u+H'=r'c(x)w+r'\ell(x)+H'(x)
\]

cannot be a unit of \(K(x)[w]\) if \(r'\ne0\).  Contradiction.

Therefore \(r'=0\), so \(q'/p'\) is constant and the top kernel line is fixed.
HC4RSD65 then closes the packet.

## Global meaning

For the general linearly-dependent passive pair

\[
A=yP(x,w)+zQ(x,w)+R(x,w),\qquad J(P,Q)=0,
\]

the polynomial Lüroth/generative-polynomial theorem gives

\[
P=p(h),\qquad Q=q(h)
\]

for one polynomial \(h(x,w)\).  The projective kernel motion is measured by

\[
\Theta(h)=p'(h)q''(h)-q'(h)p''(h).
\]

HC4RSD69 proves \(\Theta=0\) whenever the generator \(h\) is affine-linear.
Thus the only remaining linearly-dependent `[4]` obstruction requires a
**nonlinear generative polynomial** \(h\).  The global target is now to prove
that the HC4 companion Hessian either forces \(h\) to be affine/coordinate or
forces \(\Theta=0\) directly.
