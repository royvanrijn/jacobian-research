# Maximal jointly affine left-right closure at dimension 38

## Scope

The earlier
[dimension-38 gap closure](HVC38_GAP_CLOSURE.md) studied one jointly affine
source block of the twelve-variable map \(K\), and its finite audit combined
only the seventeen kernel relations containing an individually integrable
target direction.  Two gaps remained:

1. the other five maximal jointly affine source blocks;
2. source-only kernel relations and cancellations among target directions
   that do not preserve degree three on their own.

This note closes both gaps for quadratic source shears in complementary
variables and elementary quadratic target shears.  It does **not** prove
that dimension 38 is minimal.

## The six maximal blocks

A source-coordinate set \(J\) is jointly affine when every monomial in every
component of \(K\) has total \(J\)-degree at most one.  Direct enumeration
gives exactly six maximal sets:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&(z_4,z_6,z_9,z_{10},z_{11}),&
&(z_4,z_6,z_9,z_{10},z_{12}),\\
&(z_4,z_7,z_9,z_{10},z_{11}),&
&(z_4,z_7,z_9,z_{10},z_{12}),\\
&(z_5,z_7,z_9,z_{10},z_{12}),&
&(z_3,z_5,z_7,z_9,z_{10},z_{11}).
\end{aligned}
\]

For each \(j\in J\), shear \(z_j\) by every quadratic monomial in the
coordinates outside \(J\).  Add all 792 elementary target directions

\[
Y_i\longmapsto Y_i+\epsilon Y_aY_b,\qquad a,b\ne i.
\]

The first five blocks have 140 source columns and 932 total columns.  The
six-coordinate block has 126 source columns and 918 total columns.

## Exact linearized kernels

The degree-at-least-four and degree-at-least-three coefficient ranks over
the good prime \(p=1000003\) are:

\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|r|r|r}

\end{array}
\]

Sparse echelon reduction records every kernel relation.  Rational
reconstruction followed by direct substitution verifies over
\(\mathbb Q\) that each relation cancels all source terms of degree at least
four.  Since the degree-at-least-three matrices have full column rank, the
displayed kernel dimensions are also the exact dimensions of the resulting
cubic correction spaces.

For the first five blocks, invariant cubic columns give a fixed
\(5\times5\) pivot with determinants

\[
-3,\quad -\frac32,\quad 12,\quad -6,\quad -6,
\]

and a Schur-complement entry equal to the constant \(-2\).  For the last
block, a fixed \(4\times4\) pivot has determinant \(-3\), and a
\(2\times2\) Schur minor has constant determinant \(-2\).  Thus every
linearized degree-preserving family has cubic-output rank at least six.

## Full finite triangular kernel families

Every sparse relation determines a simultaneous triangular source shear.
Its target part is a polynomial quadratic shear of a single output, and all
changed target outputs are disjoint from all target-factor outputs.
Consequently **every** linearized kernel direction, including directions
that do not preserve degree three individually, belongs to one common
finite triangular source-target family.

The one-parameter classification and the full-family degree equations are:

\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|r|r|r|r}
J&\text{source-only}&\text{target integrable}&
\text{target }\lambda^2\text{-obstructed}&
\text{full parameters}&\#\text{ high equations}&
\text{minor packet}\\ \hline
4,6,9,10,11&2&16&3&21&54&1\\
4,6,9,10,12&2&15&1&18&27&1\\
4,7,9,10,11&14&17&5&36&170&32\\
4,7,9,10,12&14&16&4&34&195&32\\
5,7,9,10,12&8&9&3&20&101&1\\
3,5,7,9,10,11&7&9&0&16&0&1
\end{array}
\]

Here the “high equations” are all distinct coefficients of source degree at
least four in the full finite family.  They have parameter degrees two and
three.  In the last block the complete sixteen-parameter family is
automatically cubic.

For blocks one, two, and five, one selected \(6\times6\) cubic minor
suffices.  In the two flexible blocks, the first sparse minor can vanish on
the degree-three locus, so the checker uses pinned packets of the 32
sparsest nonzero base-column minors.  Representative sparse minors are

\[
9(2t_{19}t_{21}+t_{20}-1)
\quad\text{and}\quad
9(t_{17}t_{26}+t_{19}^2-1).
\]

Singular computes, exactly over \(\mathbb Q\), that the ideal generated by
the high equations and the applicable rank-drop minor packet is the unit
ideal for each of the first five blocks.  The last block has the constant
cubic minor \(6\).  Therefore no point of any full finite degree-three
locus has cubic-output rank at most five.

This includes possible cancellations among the directions whose individual
high coefficients have common factor \(\lambda^2\), as well as all
source-only directions omitted from the earlier seventeen-parameter audit.

## Consequence

Within this entire quadratic left-right class, the local \(K_{12}\) route
cannot improve its compressed score from

\[
n+r=12+6=18
\]

to the dimension-36 target \(n+r\le17\).  No new lower-dimensional HVC
counterexample is produced.

The remaining routes require a genuine change of class:

- non-affine or higher-degree source substitutions;
- higher-degree or nonlinear target generators;
- an ordered target construction not represented by the simultaneous
  triangular kernel family;
- a different degree-three Keller collision;
- a non-nested realization or a symmetric HN lift avoiding full cotangent
  doubling.

## Verification

Run

```bash
make verify-hvc38-maximal-block-closure
```

The checker reconstructs \(K\), verifies maximality of the six blocks,
builds all source and target columns, lifts every modular kernel relation
over \(\mathbb Q\), verifies the linear Schur witnesses, constructs every
full finite triangular kernel family, and replays the pinned Singular unit
ideal certificates.  It writes
[`hvc38_maximal_block_closure.json`](../artifacts/generated-results/hvc38_maximal_block_closure.json).

The result is an exact bounded family theorem.  It has no Lean
formalization or external review and makes no global minimality claim.
