# Newfamily replay checklist

Status: preservation and reproducibility checklist for the six-root quartic construction documented in `NEWFAMILY_QUARTIC_ROOTS_AND_CONSTANT_SECTIONS.md`.  This file intentionally separates exact replay targets from exploratory scans.  It does not promote any result into `MATH_STATUS.json` by itself.

## Goal

Recover the work currently living in `/tmp` into deterministic scripts under `elliptic-curves/cas/newfamily/`, then collapse the exploratory scripts into a small number of canonical replays.

The replay suite should establish, in order:

1. the quartic construction and the quartic-locus identity;
2. exact visible-section identities and certified subgroup ranks;
3. the structured `(-a,a,b,c,d,e)` root locus;
4. the constant-`x` resultant factorization and its three `S_4` orbits;
5. the exact rank-dependence identity on the quadratic orbit;
6. the arithmetic obstruction on the degree-6 orbit;
7. the genus/rank/arithmetic analysis of the degree-10 orbit.

## Canonical replay scripts

### 1. `verify_newfamily_construction.py`

Required exact outputs:

- for a monic sextic `f`, construct `P_T(x)=f(x-T)f(x+T)`;
- construct the monic degree-six square part `S(x,T)` matching degrees 12 through 6;
- verify that `R=S^2-P_T` is quartic exactly when

  ```text
  a1^5 - 6*a1^3*a2 + 7*a1^2*a3 + 8*a1*a2^2
  - 8*a1*a4 - 12*a2*a3 + 24*a5 = 0
  ```

- on the translated-root chart `sum r_i=0`, verify the equivalent root relation `2*e5=e2*e3`;
- on the structured chart `(-a,a,b,c,d,e)`, `e=-b-c-d`, verify the asymmetric component

  ```text
  a^2 = b^2 + b*c + b*d + c^2 + c*d + d^2.
  ```

### 2. `verify_rich_seed_rank9.py`

Seed roots:

```text
(-6,-5,1,2,3,5)
```

Required outputs:

- construct the normalized quartic

  ```text
  Y^2 = (T^2+50)x^4 - 90x^3 - (2T^4+589)x^2
        -150(T^2-10)x + T^6 - 50T^4 + 733T^2 - 675;
  ```

- verify all 12 automatic sections `x=r_i +/- T`;
- verify the seven extra affine sections

  ```text
  x =  +(7/5)T + 31/5
  x =  -(7/5)T + 31/5
  x =  +T + 6/5
  x =  -T + 6/5
  x =  3/5
  x =  +(1/5)T - 19/5
  x =  -(1/5)T - 19/5;
  ```

- map all 19 sections to the quartic Jacobian over `Q(T)`;
- verify the exact generic relations found in the exploration;
- certify that the subgroup generated by the 19 displayed sections has rank exactly 9.  This is a statement about the displayed subgroup, not the full Mordell--Weil group.

### 4. `verify_constant_section_quadratic_orbit.py`

On the structured root locus, impose a constant section `x=u` and derive the square conditions

```text
C0 = 0
Delta = C4^2 - 4*C6*C2 = 0.
```

After projectivizing with `b=1`, eliminate `u` and verify the resultant has three quadratic factors, four degree-six factors and three degree-ten factors.  Verify the `S_4` orbit decomposition

```text
[0,1,2]       degree 2
[3,4,5,6]     degree 6
[7,8,9]       degree 10.
```

For the quadratic orbit, parameterize the seed component and verify the exact generic group-law identity

```text
2 P(-a,+) + 2 P(b,-) + 2 P(c,-)
  = P(a,+) + P(d,-) + P(e,-) + 3 Qforced.
```

Equivalently, in the replay's fixed difference indexing,

```text
2 D0 - D2 + 2 D3 + 2 D5 - D7 - D9 - 3 D11 = 0.
```

This proves that the forced constant section does not add Mordell--Weil rank on this orbit.

### 5. `verify_constant_section_degree6_orbit.py`

For a degree-six representative, reduce via symmetric coordinates `S=C+D`, `P=CD` to the genus-one quartic

```text
z^2 = 8*m^4 - 72*m^3 + 249*m^2 - 360*m + 144.
```

Verify its Jacobian

```text
y^2 = x^3 + 52245*x + 7714278
```

has minimal model

```text
y^2 + x*y + y = x^3 - x^2 + 40*x + 155
```

with conductor `126`, rank `0`, and torsion `Z/6Z`.

Enumerate the six rational points on the genus-one quartic and verify that every rational lift is degenerate/non-admissible.

### 6. `verify_constant_section_degree10_orbit.py`

For a degree-ten representative, verify the symmetric quotient cubic

```text
H(S,P) =
  (S^2+36S+36)^2 P^3
  - 9(S+1)(S^2+12S+12)(11S^2+12S+12) P^2
  + 9S^2(S+1)^2(11S^2+8S+8) P
  - S^4(S+1)^3.
```

Required replay milestones:

- the normalized quotient has genus `1`;
- the double cover `W^2=S^2-4P` has branch degree `6` and genus `4`;
- a Riemann--Roch Weierstrass model for the genus-one quotient minimizes to

  ```text
  522i1: y^2 + x*y + y = x^3 - x^2 + x + 7
  ```

  with rank `1`, trivial torsion, generator `(1:2:1)`;
- the central genus-two quotient admits the even model

  ```text
  Y^2 = 20736*z^6 + 340704*z^4 - 428415*z^2 - 19307236;
  ```

- its elliptic factors minimize to

  ```text
  174c1: y^2 + x*y + y = x^3 + x^2 - 5*x - 7,
  rank 0, trivial torsion;

  522j1: y^2 + x*y + y = x^3 - x^2 - 509*x + 4677,
  rank 1, trivial torsion, generator (11:-24:1).
  ```

The exploratory calculation found only degenerate rational lift configurations.  Before promotion into `MATH_STATUS.json`, the canonical replay must close this last rational-point argument explicitly and independently of bounded scanning.
