# Split failures with full residue-prime Galois image

An ordinary-reduction argument at a prime \(p\) of a fixed real-cubic
threefold must distinguish the local Frobenius slopes even when the
global image on \(p\)-torsion is maximal. The following two results
give that configuration on a fixed modular threefold over \(\mathbb Q\)
and simultaneously at arbitrary finite lists on threefolds over number
fields. In each case the real multiplication field is
\(F=\mathbb Q(\zeta_7+\zeta_7^{-1})\).

**Theorem (fixed modular example).** Let \(B/\mathbb Q\) be the threefold attached
to the unique cubic newform orbit of weight two and level \(97\). Then
\(\operatorname{End}^0(B_{\overline{\mathbb Q}})=F\), and exactly one
coefficient pair fails at each of \(p=113,1583,1709\). At all three
primes the full level-\(97\) weight-two cusp space has a separable
\(T_2\) characteristic polynomial modulo \(p\). The image on the Tate
modules at all three primes is the joint product of the groups
\[
 G_p=\{(g_1,g_2,g_3)\in\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb Z_p)^3:
               \det g_1=\det g_2=\det g_3\}.
\]

**Theorem (finite prescription with full images).** Fix any finite list of the
one-failed-pair models from the
[split finite-field construction](CM_NEWTON_SPLIT_POLYNOMIAL_OBSTRUCTION.md)
at level \(3\), with distinct residue primes \(p>11\). There is one
threefold \(B/K\) with \(\operatorname{End}^0(B_{\overline K})=F\)
and the prescribed reductions at all places above those rational
primes. The rational primes split completely in \(K\), and the
joint image of their Tate representations is the product of the full
common-determinant groups \(G_p\). The good reduction outside a fixed
finite rational set in the
[simultaneous-lifts theorem](CM_NEWTON_SIMULTANEOUS_REAL_CUBIC_LIFTS.md)
continues to hold. Here \(B\) and \(K\) may depend on the entire list.

## Exact data for level 97

The [PARI/GP certificate](../scripts/verify_real_cubic_level_97.gp)
computes the exact weight-two newspace and its unique cubic normalized
eigenform. Put \(y=-a_2-1\). Then \(y\) generates \(\mathcal O_F\) and
satisfies \(y^3-y^2-2y+1=0\). The coefficient polynomial has discriminant
\(49\), equal to the field discriminant. The simple quotient of
\(J_0(97)\) associated to the newform has an integral \(\mathcal O_F\)
action generated by \(T_2\). At a prime \(p\ne97\) its relative
Frobenius polynomial is \(X^2-a_pX+p\). These are the weight-two
Eichler–Shimura construction and Frobenius relation.
[Shimura, Theorem 7.14, discussed by Ribet in §2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/alg-geom/9208002)
· [Mazur–Ribet, §1.1 on the Hecke and Frobenius actions](https://math.berkeley.edu/~ribet/Articles/mazur-ribet.pdf).

| \(p\) | \(a_p\) | \(N_F(a_p)\) | \(N_F(a_p^2-4p)\) |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 113 | \(-3y^2-y+8\) | \(-113\) | \(-76433539\) |
| 1583 | \(12y^2-23y-26\) | \(20579\) | \(-168239088703\) |
| 1709 | \(-4y^2+10y+13\) | \(-1709\) | \(-290525486531\) |

The coefficient polynomial has three distinct roots modulo each \(p\).
The trace norm has valuation one and hence exactly one trace is
divisible at the three coefficient places over \(p\). The other factors
are ordinary. The
[one-failed-pair ramification theorem](CM_NEWTON_FROBENIUS_RAMIFICATION_AND_DENOMINATORS.md#inertia-and-the-frobenius-weyl-group)
shows that the reductions at \(113\) and \(1583\) are absolutely simple,
and the powers of their Frobenius eigenvalues retain six distinct
conjugate values. By Honda–Tate the geometric rational endomorphism
algebra at each of these reductions is the corresponding quadratic
field \(F(\sqrt{a_p^2-4p})\). The two quadratic fields differ because
\(1583\) ramifies in its own Frobenius field and is prime to
\(N_F(a_{113}^2-4\cdot113)\). Specialization of geometric endomorphisms
into both fields, preserving \(F\), forces
\(\operatorname{End}^0(B_{\overline{\mathbb Q}})=F\).
[Tate, Theorem 1](https://numdam.org/item/SB_1968-1969__11__95_0/)
· [Yu, Lemma 2.11](https://www.numdam.org/article/AIF_2003__53_7_2105_0.pdf).

On the entire seven-dimensional cusp space of level \(97\) the
characteristic polynomial of \(T_2\) is
\[
 (X^3+4X^2+3X-1)(X^4-3X^3-X^2+6X-1),
\]
with discriminant \(2^{12}7^2\cdot2777\). There is no oldspace in
weight two at this prime level. Thus the three failure primes cannot
give congruences between distinct weight-two systems of cusp
eigenvalues at level \(97\), including distinct embeddings of the
same system. The polynomial's value at the Eisenstein eigenvalue
\(3\) is \(568\), also a unit at the three primes.

## Recognizing the full image

At a split prime \(p>11\) use the three two-dimensional factors of the
representation over \(\mathbb F_p\). The following finite witnesses
suffice for full image, provided the common determinant is surjective.

- In each factor find split and nonsplit semisimple projective elements
  of orders exceeding two, at least one of order exceeding five.
- For each two factors find some group element for which their values
  of the projective invariant \(\operatorname{tr}^2/\det\) differ.

Dickson's classification gives the first implication. A nonsplit
element excludes a Borel. The projective normalizer of either torus
has only involutions outside that torus, so the two elements exclude
both normalizers. The order greater than five excludes the exceptional
groups. The projective image therefore contains
\(\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\), and its determinant squareclass
is surjective, making it all of \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\).
[Serre, §2 on subgroups of \(\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\)](https://wstein.org/sage_summer/bsd_comp/Serre-properties_galoisiennes_des_points_dordre_fini_des_courbes_elliptiques.pdf).

The projective commutator image is a subdirect product of the three
nonabelian simple groups \(\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\).
By Goursat's lemma a proper subdirect product links two factors by an
automorphism. This follows inductively by projecting to all but the
last factor and applying simplicity to the kernel in the last factor.
An automorphism of \(\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\) is conjugation
by an element of \(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\). Indeed it induces
a permutation of the Sylow \(p\)-subgroups, identified with
\(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_p)\). After projective conjugation, this
permutation fixes \(\infty,0,1\). On the translation subgroup fixing
\(\infty\), an automorphism is multiplication by a nonzero scalar on
the additive parameters. Equivariance of the permutation and the fixed
points \(0,1\) force that scalar to be one and the entire permutation
to be the identity. The faithful action then fixes every group element.

The projective commutator is normal in the full projective image.
Thus any conjugacy linking two factors on the commutator links them
on the full image, since the centralizer of
\(\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\) in
\(\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\) is trivial. For example the
centralizer must commute with the two opposite unipotent subgroups,
which have trivial intersection of centralizers. The second witness
excludes every such link. It follows that the projective commutator
image is the full simple product.

The linear commutator lies in \(\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb F_p)^3\)
and maps onto that simple product. Take the preimage in it of a
single simple factor. The commutator of that preimage is trivial
in the other factors, where the preimage is central. Its projection
onto the selected factor surjects onto \(\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\)
by perfectness of the simple group. It must be all of
\(\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\), since otherwise it would have
index two in this perfect group. Doing this for each factor gives
the full linear determinant kernel. Surjectivity of the common
determinant gives the desired linear image modulo \(p\).

For the level-\(97\) form at \(113\) order the embeddings by
\(y\mapsto104,89,34\) modulo \(113\). The projective orders in the

For a finite set of distinct residue primes, there are no links
between their projective commutator factors, since the simple groups
\(\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_p)\) have different orders at different
\(p\). The preceding Goursat and central-kernel arguments therefore
give the full joint residual special linear product. For the
level-\(97\) form the determinants are cyclotomic over \(\mathbb Q\),
so the computed witnesses give the full joint linear residue image.

To lift from residue images to Tate images, work with the closed
commutator subgroup of the joint image at the finite set of primes.
If its mod-prime image is the full product of the special linear
groups, take the preimage of just one factor of that product. Its
projection to that Tate factor is all of
\(\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb Z_p)\) by Serre's lifting lemma. All
other projections are in principal congruence kernels, a product of
prosolvable groups. Consequently the quotient of the selected
projection by its subgroup coming from the pure factor is
prosolvable. The group \(\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb Z_p)\) is perfect
for \(p>3\), so that quotient is trivial. For perfectness, the upper
and lower elementary matrices generate over the local ring, and each
is a commutator with a diagonal matrix whose squared unit differs
from one by a unit. Thus the joint image contains the pure special
linear group in every factor. With the full cyclotomic determinant
image this proves the asserted joint Tate image for the level-\(97\)
form.
[Lombardo, Lemma 2.4 with \(n=1\)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.1703).

## Forcing the witnesses in characteristic zero

For each prescribed prime \(p\), choose three additional and distinct
rational primes \(q\equiv1\pmod{252p}\). They split in \(F(i)\) and
satisfy the level-\(3\) torsion congruence. There are arbitrarily
large choices. At a place over each \(q\), prescribe the split
coefficient Frobenius traces modulo \(p\), which is a fixed modulus
while selecting \(q\). Use the three traces of a split norm-one torus
generator in the first model, the three traces of a nonsplit norm-one
torus generator in the second, and the traces \(0,1,2\) in the third.
The three traces in each of the first two models can all be equal.
Since \(q\equiv1\pmod p\), their relative determinants are one modulo
\(p\). The respective projective orders are \((p-1)/2\) and
\((p+1)/2\), and the third model separates the projective invariants.

These choices are realized by actual models \(B_q/\mathbb F_q\) with
rational level \(3\) and one failed pair at \(q\). The condition on
the three trace residues at \(p\) is one congruence in
\(\mathcal O_F/p\mathcal O_F\), since \(p\) is split in \(F\). Combine
it with \(a_q\equiv2\pmod9\) by the Chinese remainder theorem. In the
finite-field lattice construction this just enlarges the fixed
modulus from \(9\) to \(9p\). Its bounded grid still gives exact
valuation one at the selected coefficient place over \(q\) and
valuation zero at the other two, with archimedean conjugates strictly
inside the Weil cube for all sufficiently large \(q\).
[Trace lattice and Honda–Tate construction](CM_NEWTON_SPLIT_POLYNOMIAL_OBSTRUCTION.md)
· [Prescribing a fixed auxiliary residue](CM_NEWTON_FROBENIUS_RAMIFICATION_AND_DENOMINATORS.md#imposing-a-pole-on-finite-field-models).

Apply the simultaneous-lifts theorem to the original finite list
and all the added witness primes. It realizes the specified reductions
over prime fields and ensures exact geometric endomorphism algebra
\(F\). Every original rational prime \(p\) splits completely in the
resulting field \(K\). Hence \(K\) is linearly disjoint from each
cyclotomic field of conductor supported on the original primes.
Indeed the intersection is unramified away from that set because it
lies in the cyclotomic field and unramified at the primes of the set
because they split in \(K\). It is \(\mathbb Q\) by the discriminant
bound for number fields. This gives the full joint cyclotomic
determinant image at the original primes.

At each selected \(p\) the three auxiliary reductions give the stated
finite witnesses. The joint residual recognition and Tate lifting
arguments with the full determinant image then prove the finite
prescription with full images.

## Verification scope

Run `gp -fq -s 250000000 research/foundations/scripts/verify_real_cubic_level_97.gp`
from the repository root. PARI/GP 2.15.4 reproduced all displayed
coefficient and matrix data. The checker recomputes the exact newspace
and eigenform using PARI's modular-form routines, then checks the
reductions, norms, Hecke discriminant, and projective matrix orders.
An internal cross-check with SageMath 10.9 modular symbols reproduced
the \(T_2\) polynomial and the three specified Fourier coefficients in
the basis defined by \(y=-a_2-1\).
[PARI/GP modular-form routines](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux1.fr/dochtml/ref/Modular_forms.html).

These results give obstructions to using maximum residual or Tate
image at the residue characteristic, or the absence of same-level
congruences, as a local criterion for ordinary reduction. The
finite prescription varies the characteristic-zero variety as the
list grows, and the level-\(97\) calculation establishes the stated
individual primes. The density on a fixed split identity class
remains open.
