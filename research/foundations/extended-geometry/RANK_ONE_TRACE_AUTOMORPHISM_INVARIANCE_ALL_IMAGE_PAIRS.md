# All polynomial automorphisms of the rank-one trace-one variety preserve its normalized contraction trace

The trace-invariance and primitive-power statements below hold for every number n>=2 of original Image pairs. Every proof is algebraic over a characteristic-zero field K and remains valid over arbitrary coefficient-field extensions.

## 1. The affine rank-one projection variety and its filtration

Let S=K[xi_1,...,xi_n,z_1,...,z_n], R=sum_i xi_i z_i. Let C=sum_(D>=0) S_(D,D), the balanced subring, generated by M_ij=xi_i z_j. The defining ideal among M is generated by the 2x2 minors (this identification can also be replaced throughout by the literal subring C). Set A=C/(R-1). Thus its geometric points are rank-one idempotents M with tr M=1; the identity M^2=(tr M)M follows from the rank-one minors.

The natural filtration is the least degree in the matrix entries. Since C is a graded domain and R is homogeneous of degree1, the initial ideal of (R-1) is (R), hence gr A=C/(R). The map C/(R)->S/(R) is injective: if a balanced polynomial equals RH in S, its unbalanced bihomogeneous parts force the corresponding parts of H to vanish, so H itself is balanced. The quadratic R is absolutely irreducible for n>=2 (its rank is 2n>=4). Therefore gr A is absolutely integral. Least degrees add on nonzero products, and A*=K*. These statements hold after every coefficient-field extension.

The variety is geometrically smooth and integral. It is the orbit of diag(1,0,...,0) under SL_n. For smoothness without an orbit theorem, on the open set M_ii !=0 put u=M e_i and v=e_i^T M/M_ii. Then M=u v, v_i=1, and v u=1 determines u_i=1-sum_(j!=i) v_j u_j. Thus this chart is the localization of a polynomial ring in 2n-2 variables at u_i. Such charts cover because tr M=1. Integrality also follows from gr A.

## 2. A global algebraic symplectic volume

Define the regular two-form

    beta=tr(M dM wedge dM).

At any geometric point, a tangent vector U satisfies MU+UM=U, hence MUM=0 and (1-M)U(1-M)=0. In a basis with M=diag(1,0), write U=[0 a; b 0]. A second tangent vector V=[0 c; d 0] has

    beta(U,V)=a d-c b.

This is nondegenerate. Closedness follows from

    d beta=tr(dM wedge dM wedge dM)=0:

relative to the decomposition im M plus ker M, each tangent dM is off diagonal, so a product of three is off diagonal and has trace zero. Since A is geometrically smooth, equality on all geometric tangent triples gives equality of regular differential forms. Alternatively the same identity follows by inserting M+(1-M) between factors.

Consequently

    omega=beta^(n-1)/(n-1)!

is a nowhere-zero global top form and Omega_A^(2n-2)=A omega. Conjugation by SL_n preserves beta and omega by trace invariance.

All tangent vector fields are A-linear combinations of fundamental conjugation fields D_X(M)=[X,M], for constant trace-zero matrices X. Indeed a tangent matrix U obeys the preceding off-diagonal identities and X=UM-MU has trace zero and satisfies [X,M]=U. Expanding X in constant matrix units gives the assertion. Contraction with omega identifies vector fields with forms of degree2n-3. For a fundamental field D_X, Cartan's identity and conjugation-invariance give d(i_(D_X) omega)=0. Thus

    d(f i_(D_X) omega)=(D_X f) omega.

It follows that the coefficient VECTOR SPACE of exact top forms is

    V=sum_(X in sl_n(K)) D_X(A),
    d Omega_A^(2n-3)=V omega.                         (A1)

V is not asserted to be an ideal.

## 3. Explicit coinvariants give the normalized Image trace

Use the balanced polynomial realization. The derivations induced by infinitesimal changes xi -> g xi, z -> (g^-1)^T z are

    L_ij=xi_i partial_(xi_j)-z_j partial_(z_i).

They preserve R and C. Their induced matrix action is conjugation, so they span the same coefficient space V. The diagonal derivation L_ii is available because sum_i L_ii is zero on C, and characteristic zero lets us replace it by a trace-zero diagonal matrix.

For a monomial xi^alpha z^beta with |alpha|=|beta|=D and alpha!=beta, some L_ii has a nonzero eigenvalue alpha_i-beta_i. Its class in A/V therefore vanishes. Put m_alpha=xi^alpha z^alpha. When alpha_i>0 and i!=j, apply L_ij to xi^(alpha-e_i+e_j) z^alpha. This gives

    (alpha_j+1)m_alpha-alpha_i m_(alpha-e_i+e_j) in V.

Moving all the occupation numbers to one coordinate shows that, in each degreeD, every diagonal monomial has class

    [m_alpha]=alpha! q_D

for a single class q_D (one may define q_D=[m_(D,0,...,0)]/D!). Multiplication by R is not an operation on the quotient vector space A/V in general; instead use the following equality of elements of A before passing to classes:

    m_alpha=R m_alpha=sum_i m_(alpha+e_i).

The degreeD+1 monomial relations therefore give

    alpha! q_D=sum_i (alpha+e_i)! q_(D+1)
                =alpha! (D+n)q_(D+1).

Hence q_D=q_0/(n)_D, with q_0=[1]. It follows that dim_K(A/V)<=1.

Define Lambda on C_D by

    Lambda(xi^alpha z^beta)=0 if alpha!=beta,
    Lambda(m_alpha)=alpha!/(n)_D.                    (A2)

This is the normalized original factorial contraction. These formulas are compatible with R=1, by the same literal R identity, so define Lambda on A with Lambda(1)=1. Directly the infinitesimal L_ij identities annihilate Lambda; equivalently one checks the two terms on balanced monomials using factorials. Thus Lambda(V)=0, the dimension is exactly1, and

    V=ker Lambda.                                    (A3)

For H in S_(D,D), the original Image contraction E_n(H), a constant in the z variables for balanced H, obeys

    E_n(H)=(n)_D Lambda(H restricted to R=1).        (A4)

No probability integral, analytic comparison theorem, representation-theoretic complete reducibility, or automorphism-group classification is needed.

## 4. All polynomial automorphisms preserve the trace

Let sigma be any K-algebra automorphism of A. Because the top-form module is free, sigma^*omega=u omega for a unit u. Section1 gives u in K*. Exact forms are preserved, so (A1) implies sigma(V)=V, using sigma^-1 for the reverse inclusion. For every f, f-Lambda(f) lies in V. Since sigma fixes K and1, (A3) gives

    Lambda(sigma f)=Lambda(f).                        (A5)

Therefore all polynomial automorphisms preserve and reflect pure phases, safety and unsafety, with fixed markers transported by sigma or sigma^-1. All mixed moments of a specified pair are exactly preserved. No assertion of pointwise preservation of omega, of ordinary degree, or of an arbitrary automorphism degree formula is made.

## 5. Primitive original lift and full-ring power descent in every number of pairs

Dehomogenization is an isomorphism C_D -> A_<=D: every filtered representative can be homogenized by powers of R; injectivity follows because a homogeneous element of C cannot equal (R-1)Q unless it is zero, comparing the least and greatest nonzero graded components of Q. Let H_D(f) be that balanced lift. Multiplication of least lifts is compatible with degree additivity.

A lift F=H_D(f) is R-primitive in the FULL ring S iff deg_A f=D. If R divides F in S, its quotient is balanced of degreeD-1 by both gradings, so descends to a lower representative. The converse is literal homogenization.

Fix a primitive F, an extension L/K, r>=2 and alpha in L*. Then

    F=alpha H^r in the FULL original S_L
       iff f=alpha g^r in A_L.                       (A6)

For the forward direction, the smallest and largest nonzero xi degrees of H have r-fold smallest/largest degrees in H^r, so bihomogeneity of F forces H to be homogeneous in xi; repeat independently in z. Both degrees equal D/r, so H is balanced and restricts to g. Conversely degree additivity gives D=r deg_A g, so multiplicative least homogenization gives F=alpha H_(deg g)(g)^r with no extra R padding. Thus absolute nonproper-power status is preserved by taking primitive least lifts of automorphism images.

The original Image-kernel identity is also elementary in every n: put D_i=partial_(z_i)-xi_i. Modulo sum_i D_i(S), the equality xi_i H=partial_(z_i)H reduces every xi^alpha z^beta to its original contraction E_n. Conversely E_n(D_i H)=0 because E_n(xi_i H)=partial_(z_i)E_n(H)=E_n(partial_(z_i)H). Thus ker E_n=sum_i D_i(S), so the pure/mixed contraction statements really describe original Image membership and its failure.

For a fixed balanced F, every unbalanced original marker failure has a balanced original marker: select one bihomogeneous component with xi degreeu<=z degreev surviving at infinitely many orders, then one fixed coefficient z^eta, |eta|=v-u, by the finite pigeonhole principle. The identity E_n(xi^eta Q F^m)=partial_z^eta E_n(QF^m) makes the balanced fixed marker survive infinitely often. Thus original balanced safety is equivalent to the sphere/trace safety in A via (A4), without identifying an arbitrary unbalanced marker with an invariant function.

Consequently ANY primitive absolutely nonproper-power balanced n-pair Image failure, if given as input, transports along EVERY polynomial automorphism of the rank-one trace-one variety to another primitive absolutely nonproper-power original Image failure. Its degree is the least ordinary matrix degree after transport; no formula for that degree is inferred. Starting-witness existence, low-degree classification and global unbalanced phases are separate questions.

## 6. Classical context and the boundary of exact controls

The invariant two-form is the classical coadjoint-orbit/Kirillov construction.
For precise context, Bertram Kostant, *Minimal coadjoint orbits and symplectic
induction*, arXiv:math/0312252v1 (12 December 2003), Section3.1,
printed pp24--25, equation(3.2), evaluates the KKS form on fundamental
fields by pairing the orbit point with the reversed Lie bracket:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0312252v1. That source concerns real Lie-group
coadjoint orbits. It is context only: the matrix tangent identities,
nondegeneracy, closedness and all-field exact-form quotient above are proved
directly. No symplectic comparison theorem or automorphism classification
is imported.

The n=2 instance recovers the retained Hopf-quadric calculation: with
M=[[(1+t)/2,x],[y/2,(1-t)/2]], t^2+2xy=1, the form beta is
-dx wedge dt/(2x), a nonzero constant multiple of the Hopf form.
The normalized trace is alpha!/(D+1)!. The general mechanism is that
constant units, a free top form and a one-dimensional exact-form quotient
force automorphism-invariance of the normalized trace.

The standard-library replay verify_rank_one_trace_automorphisms.py checks
literal matrix charts, tangent and differential-form identities, factorial

boundary controls over rational coefficients. These bounded exact controls
