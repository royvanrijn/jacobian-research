# Source review of the adjoint and Pfaffian ordinarity arguments

## Two active adjoint labels

The [adjoint theorem](CM_NEWTON_ADJOINT_PROJECTIVE_TRACE_BOUNDS.md) applies to a polarized abelian variety whose geometric adjoint factors are of type \(A_1\), with at most two Hodge-active labels in each rational simple factor. It gives potential density-one ordinary reduction. [Vasiu, Theorem 1.3.4(a) and Example 1.3.5(iv)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0212066v7) give the Mumford–Tate comparison for this adjoint type since \(1+1=2\) is prime. [Pink, Corollary 7.2](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~pink/ftp/AMT-v3.pdf) already gives a density-one assertion over the connected field under the geometric endomorphism-ring hypothesis \(\operatorname{End}=\mathbb Z\) and its root-type restriction, which allows \(A_1\). Pink's theorem and the repository theorem thus overlap there. The present argument treats higher tensor realizations of the adjoint factor without the geometric endomorphism-ring restriction.

At an absolute degree-one place the linear crystalline operator is over \(\mathbb Q_p\). The active nilpotent root line splits the local projective rank-one factor over its unramified coefficient field. For a lift \(B\) there, a nonzero spectral gap of size at most one is exactly one, since unequal root valuations of a quadratic over an unramified field are integral. In the gap-zero case the trace valuation is at least \(\lceil v_p(\det B)/2\rceil\). Odd residue degree forces odd determinant valuation by the Kottwitz map and gives the extra unit of valuation in \((\operatorname{tr}B)^2/\det B\). The weighted total of failures is at most \(b_i-1\), and each failed local factor costs at least two. Rational conjugacy-class compatibility supplies the descent of the relative invariant into the label field. Finally the central projection comes from the Kottwitz equality, while the adjoint slope comparison determines the remaining cocharacter projection.

The sensitive interfaces for specialist review are the use of the **linear** degree-one crystalline Frobenius with the rational \(G\)-class of [Kisin–Zhou, Theorems 1.2 and 5.3.10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.02165v1), the tensor model and convention in the local Kottwitz comparison, and application of the norm on every rational factor. The three-independent-balanced-factor endpoint remains open. The bound for three active split labels allows one flat label, so the proof cannot exclude it.

## Relative rank four

The [single balanced pair theorem](CM_NEWTON_RANK_FOUR_PFAFFIAN.md) assumes connected projective \(\mathrm{PGL}_4\) on each block. The [linked version](CM_NEWTON_LINKED_RANK_FOUR_PFAFFIAN.md) has distinct restriction-kernel and componentwise conditions and covers additional connected images. [Fité, Theorem 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.10185v3) gives nonzero ordinary density for an abelian fourfold with geometric imaginary quadratic multiplication and signature \((2,2)\), allowing extra endomorphisms. The repository's original-field density-one statement applies on the more restricted monodromy locus. [Cantoral Farfán–Li–Mantovan–Pries–Tang, Assumption 2.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.11174v1) requires a CM type for which exactly one signature multiplicity is one and the others are zero. That hypothesis excludes a \((2,2)\) pair.

The Pfaffian step requires a component in which the half power swaps dual blocks. For its off-diagonal matrices \(B,C\), the alternating pairing yields \(C=-p^{t/2}B^{-\mathsf T}\), so the shifted determinant on one block is \(\det(B^\mathsf T-B)/\det B\). Integrality of the coefficient lattice and the even Hodge multiplicities are used for \(v_p(\det B)\equiv0\bmod2\). A midpoint excess gives a strict lower valuation bound \(>-2\) in each mixed block. Together these force integrality. To get a density-zero exceptional set, the bounded norm must be a nonconstant regular class function on each relevant component. The argument uses the pinned representative and a fixed cocharacter with nonzero weights. In the linked tensor case unequal \(A_1\) weights rely on the distinct-orbit condition for the two supporting factors.

Specialist review is still needed for the integral Dieudonné comparison when the coefficients carry a nontrivial field action, descent and integrality of the relative characteristic polynomials, and exact-power compression across the linked blocks. These checks and the comparison with the stated literature are internal. The findings support separate proof scopes for the adjoint and rank-four cases, and publication priority requires a separate review.
