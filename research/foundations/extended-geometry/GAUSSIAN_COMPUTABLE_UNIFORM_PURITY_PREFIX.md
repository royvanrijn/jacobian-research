# A computable uniform pure-moment prefix for all Gaussian polynomials

Work over characteristic zero. Let Gamma_N be the product standard Gaussian
Wick functional: Gamma_N(X^alpha)=0 if some exponent is odd, and otherwise
Gamma_N(X^(2r))=product_i (2r_i-1)!!. No analytic convergence assertion is
included in its formal exponential generating series.

**Theorem ComputableGaussianPurePrefix.** There is a computable function
B_G(N,D), for all integers N>=1 and D>=0, such that for every characteristic-
zero field K and every polynomial P in K[X_1,...,X_N] of total degree at most D,

    Gamma_N(P^m)=0 for every m>=1
       iff Gamma_N(P^m)=0 for 1<=m<=B_G(N,D).               (U1)

The proof gives a terminating algorithm for B_G, not a numerical formula,
a practical implementation of coefficient stratification, an N=16 prefix,
or equality of ordinary moment ideals. It proves equality of the radicals
of the infinite and finite-prefix coefficient ideals.

Gaussian whole-source construction, pointwise polynomial Hom and actual-jet
selection already exist in the retained research. They are premises here,
not new Gaussian pointwise safety claims. The additional step is their
application at generic points of coefficient varieties, making the
Noetherian finite-prefix argument algorithmic.

## 1. The retained exact source and its actual formal map

For a field k of characteristic zero and P in k[X], take the exact module

    H_P=k[t,X]/sum_i nabla_i k[t,X],
    nabla_i=partial_Xi-X_i+t*P_i,
    D_t[B]=[partial_t B+P B].                              (U2)

The indicated time connection commutes with every nabla_i. The exact source
is an algebraic exponential connection integrated over all X, and is
holonomic over the one-variable Weyl algebra k[t]<D_t>. Its entire module,
with the original amplitude maps, is required. A cyclic unit annihilator
alone is not a substitute for this module in an all-marker safety question.

The retained proof GAUSSIAN_FORMAL_COMPLETION_AND_POLYNOMIAL_HOM.md, Sections
1 and 3, proves over any Q-algebra A that

    Phi_P([B])=Gamma_N(B exp(tP)) in A[[t]],
    Hom_Dt(H_P,A[[t]])=A*Phi_P.                            (U3)

This is formal algebra: modulo t^r, multiplication by the finite exponential
exp(tP) conjugates nabla_i to partial_Xi-X_i, and the unperturbed Gaussian
quotient has the Wick functional as its exact quotient map. Equivalently,
IBP and differentiation at t=0 determine every Taylor coefficient of every
amplitude from its constant functional. These statements do not require
convergence of Gamma_N(exp(tP)).

The source construction and its field-level algorithm are detailed in
WHOLE_GAUSSIAN_SOURCE_HOM_AND_EFFECTIVE_CUTOFFS.md. The implementation is
executed over QQ and simple number fields, with optional timeouts. Its
mathematical exact-field route is broader. The present uniform algorithm
requires effective finitely generated fields over Q and removes timeouts;
it does not assert that the existing producer accepts that interface.

The primary source for the WHOLE integration module is Oaku, *Algorithms
for integrals of holonomic functions over domains defined by polynomial
inequalities* (author PDF Oaku2013.pdf), physical page 14, before Definition
4. The module is generated by ALL spatial monomials X^alpha with |alpha|
up to the maximum integral b-function root and all their module relations;

## 2. A total field-level purity recognizer

Let k=Frac(Q[c]/P_0), where P_0 is a prime ideal with a Groebner presentation.
This includes Q and algebraic number fields. It embeds in C, but no numerical
embedding is used by the algorithm. Exact field arithmetic and zero tests
are obtained from polynomial normal forms modulo P_0 and fraction arithmetic.

Compute the exact whole presentation of H_P with its original unit class.
Because H_P is holonomic over k[t]<D_t>, its cyclic submodule generated by
[1] is holonomic and has a nonzero scalar annihilator

    A=sum_j a_j(t) D_t^j, a_j in k[t], A[1]=0.             (U4)

For example compute syzygies of the relation columns together with the
unit vector and retain a nonzero final entry. If a presentation uses a
spatial monomial basis, the unit is among those monomials: H_P cannot be
zero because its actual formal unit period has constant term 1. More
generally its image can be retained as an explicit module element.

Put F(t)=Phi_P([1])=sum Gamma_N(P^m)t^m/m!. Then A(F)=0. To test F=1 set
h=-A(1). If h=0 use M=A; otherwise use

    M=(h D_t-h') A.                                      (U5)

This is nonzero and annihilates F-1. The origin indicial lemma in the
retained FORMAL_INDICIAL_PURITY_CERTIFICATES.md applies without any
ordinary-point, regular-singular or convergence assumption. Explicitly,
for M=sum b_j(t)D_t^j put

    nu=min_j(ord_0(b_j)-j),
    I_M(s)=sum[ord_0(b_j)-j=nu] lc_0(b_j)*(s)_j.           (U6)

Its largest participating j proves it is nonzero. A nonzero formal
solution of order r has I_M(r)=0. Thus the maximum nonnegative integer
root, together with the known constant term of F-1, bounds a complete
finite identity test. Every required coefficient is an exactly computable
Wick moment divided by a nonzero factorial. On a nonpure input the test
returns a positive nonzero original moment.

The integer-root operation is effective over the stated generic fields,
not an oracle for integer roots over an arbitrary computable presentation.
Clear parameter denominators in I_M(s), reduce its coefficient polynomials
modulo P_0, and expand in the finite set of standard parameter monomials
actually occurring. At least one rational coordinate I_b(s) in Q[s] is
nonzero. Every integer root in k is a root of I_b. Compute these finitely
many rational-polynomial integer candidates, and test each in k. The same
argument supplies integer-root operations in the integration construction
and in the one-variable polynomial-Hom procedure when they are needed.
All other steps are exact Weyl algebra Groebner, syzygy and field operations.
This proves that the generic-point recognizer terminates.

A zero polynomial Hom does not by itself decide this purity question:
pure unsafe phases exist. The unit's formal comparison (U4)--(U6) is the
required separate test. Conversely the existing whole-source Hom criterion
already decides all-marker Gaussian safety, so no new pointwise Gaussian
safety theorem is claimed here.

## 3. The coefficient-space algorithm

Fix N,D. Let R=Q[c_alpha:|alpha|<=D] and P_c=sum c_alpha X^alpha. For m>=1 set

    mu_m(c)=Gamma_N(P_c^m) in R.

All these polynomials are exactly computable. Initialize I=(0), generated
by an empty set of moments. Every later I is proper since c=0 kills every
positive moment. Repeat the following finite operations.

1. Compute the minimal associated prime ideals P_0 of I in the global
   polynomial ring R over Q.
2. For each such P_0 run Section 2 over k=Frac(R/P_0) on the generic phase.
3. If a component is nonpure, select a returned witness mu_r not in P_0,
   replace I by I+(mu_r), and restart.
4. If every generic component is pure, output the maximum moment index
   ever added (or zero if no moment was required).

The primary Singular 4.4.1 manual D.4.27.7, minAssGTZ, specifies exactly the
needed minimal-associated-prime algorithm for a proper ideal, designed for
characteristic zero. Use a global polynomial ordering; no absolute prime
decomposition is required. The effective field operations above work on
these Q-primes even if they split over Qbar.

Every failed pass strictly increases sqrt(I): its witness is outside one
minimal prime of I, whereas sqrt(I) lies in that prime. The Noetherian
ascending-chain condition therefore forces termination. At the stopping
point every mu_m belongs to every minimal prime of I, because it is zero
in every generic fraction field. Hence

    sqrt((mu_1,mu_2,...))=sqrt(I)
      =sqrt((mu_1,...,mu_B)).                              (U7)

The last equality holds because all generators added have index at most B.
A coefficient evaluation in ANY characteristic-zero field has prime kernel.
If it kills the prefix, its kernel contains I and therefore all mu_m by
(U7). This proves (U1), including coefficients outside Qbar. The algorithm
is specified entirely in terms of N,D, so its output is a computable bound
rather than only a non-effective existence statement.

The reduced-ideal criterion has a converse: if the prefix determines all moments over every characteristic-zero field, but a later mu_m is outside its radical, a prime quotient fraction field containing the prefix and excluding mu_m contradicts that field-valued criterion. Thus reduced-ideal equality and the universal field-valued criterion are equivalent at each fixed prefix. No ordinary-ideal equality follows for this particular prefix.

For D=0 one can take B_G(N,0)=1. For D=1 write P=c+sum a_i X_i. The first
moments force c=0 and sum a_i^2=0, and centered Gaussian Wick expansion gives

    Gamma_N(P^(2r))=(2r-1)!!*(sum a_i^2)^r,
    Gamma_N(P^(2r+1))=0.

Thus B_G(N,1)=2 is valid for every N>=1. These elementary cases are not a
claim that a useful B_G(N,D) has been computed for general N,D. In
particular no ternary-cubic prefix 16 follows from the abstract algorithm.

## 4. Transfer to every original Image degree box

The independent shifted-Gaussian bridge proves, for arbitrary H and n>=1,

    E_n(H)(z)=Gamma_(2n)(H(V,z+W)),
    V_i=(X_i-iY_i)/2, W_i=X_i+iY_i.                       (U8)

This is an identity of polynomials in the output parameters z. It follows
on monomials from Gamma(V^alpha W^beta)=delta_alpha,beta alpha!. It does
not assume that H is balanced or bihomogeneous.

For a total-degree-at-most-D input F, the Gaussian polynomial F(V,z+W) has
Gaussian degree at most D and coefficients in the faithful extension
K(i,z). Apply (U1) over that field and then use (U8). Consequently

    B_Image(n,D)=B_G(2n,D)                                (U9)

is a computable universal pure prefix for EVERY Image input of degree at
most D, with arbitrary characteristic-zero coefficients. If i already
belongs to K no quadratic extension is needed; otherwise use the field
K(i), then adjoin independent output parameters z. Polynomial identity
in K[z] is reflected by its injection into this field.

Equation (U9) concerns pure moments only. Universal-marker Image safety
and its theoretical decision follow instead from the separate shifted
bridge and the retained whole Gaussian polynomial-Hom theorem. Computing
an explicit numerical B_Image, exact ordinary coefficient ideals, or a
classification of safe forms remains additional work.
