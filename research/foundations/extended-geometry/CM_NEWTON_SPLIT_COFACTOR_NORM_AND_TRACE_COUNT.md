# A varying quadratic norm and the split trace lattice

Let \(F\) be a totally real cubic field and let the odd prime \(p\) split
completely in \(F\). Write \(a_i=\sigma_i(a)\) for the real conjugates of an
integral relative trace \(a\), with \(|a_i|<2\sqrt p\). Suppose exactly one
coefficient place above \(p\) divides \(a\). Put
\[
t=\operatorname{Tr}_{F/\mathbb Q}(a),\qquad s=\sum_{i<j}a_i a_j,
\qquad b=N_{F/\mathbb Q}(a)/p.
\]

## The cofactor as a short quadratic norm

**Proposition.** Write \(d=-N_{F/\mathbb Q}(a^2-4p)/p=c h^2\) with \(c\)
positive and squarefree and \(h>0\). Then \(p\nmid d\) and
\[
d=V^2-pU^2,\qquad V=2(s+4p),\qquad U=b+4t,
\qquad 0<V<32p,\qquad |U|\sqrt p<V.
\]
In particular \(c\) is a rational norm from \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt p)\). For any
positive squarefree \(c\) prime to \(p\), this rational norm condition is
equivalent to
\[
\left(\frac{p}{\ell}\right)=1\quad\text{for every odd prime }\ell\mid c,
\qquad \left(\frac{c}{p}\right)=1.
\]

The relative discriminant \(D=a^2-4p\) has valuation one at the failed place
and zero at the other places over \(p\). Thus \(d\) is integral and prime to
\(p\). Its squarefree part is also the cofactor defined using the relative
field discriminant of \(F(\sqrt D)/F\), since the latter differs from the
discriminant of the order generated by a root of \(X^2-aX+p\) by an ideal
index squared. See the [discriminant sieve](CM_NEWTON_FROBENIUS_DISCRIMINANT_SIEVE.md).

Expanding the product of the three conjugates gives
\[
\frac{N_{F(\sqrt p)/\mathbb Q(\sqrt p)}(a+2\sqrt p)}{\sqrt p}
=2(s+4p)+(b+4t)\sqrt p.
\]
At the positive embedding of \(\sqrt p\), every factor and the denominator
are positive. At the negative embedding, all three factors and the
denominator are negative. The quotient is therefore totally positive.
Multiplying it by its conjugate gives \(d\), and dividing it by \(h\)
gives the rational norm representation of \(c\). The upper bound on \(V\)
uses \(s<12p\). Also \(0<d<64p^2\) follows from the product of the three
\(4p-a_i^2\). There are \(O(p^{3/2})\) possible pairs \((U,V)\) at each \(p\)
by the displayed bounds.

For the equivalence, the local quadratic norm condition at an odd prime
\(\ell\mid c\) is \((p/\ell)=1\), and at \(p\) it is \((c/p)=1\). At
other odd primes both arguments of the Hilbert symbol are units. The real
symbol is one because \(p>0\). Hilbert reciprocity gives the condition at
2, and Hasse–Minkowski applied to \(V^2-pU^2=cW^2\) gives a rational
representation. Any nonzero solution has \(W\ne0\) since \(p\) is
nonsquare. The actual trace supplies the short integral representation
of \(d\) above.
[Serre, *A Course in Arithmetic*, Chapters III–IV](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4684-9884-4).

The same norm condition holds for the degree of any short prime-to-\(p\)
return covered by the [isogeny return theorem](CM_NEWTON_SPLIT_ISOGENY_RETURNS.md),
because that degree and \(c\) have the same rational squareclass. The
quadratic field here changes with \(p\). The
[fixed-field norm sieve](CM_NEWTON_TRACE_COFACTOR_SIEVE.md) assumes a single
quadratic field for all \(p\).

## Counting candidate traces with a moving auxiliary level

**Proposition.** Fix a rectangular box \(B\subset(-2,2)^3\) of positive
volume. For any coefficient place \(\mathfrak q\mid p\), positive integer
\(M\) prime to \(p\), and \(r\in\mathcal O_F/M\mathcal O_F\), the number of
nonzero \(a\in\mathcal O_F\) with
\[
a\in\mathfrak q,\qquad a\equiv r\pmod{M\mathcal O_F},\qquad
(\sigma_i(a)/\sqrt p)_i\in B
\]
equals
\[
\frac{\operatorname{vol}(B)}{\sqrt{|\operatorname{disc}(F)|}}
\frac{\sqrt p}{M^3}+O_{F,B}\bigl((1+p^{1/6}/M)^2\bigr).
\]
For \(p>64\) each counted trace has valuation one at \(\mathfrak q\) and
valuation zero at the other two places. In particular every auxiliary
residue class occurs uniformly through \(M=o(p^{1/6})\). For
\(M/p^{1/6}\to\infty\), the total number of candidates is eventually
smaller than the \(M^3\) available classes.

As in the [finite-field trace construction](CM_NEWTON_SPLIT_POLYNOMIAL_OBSTRUCTION.md),
choose a basis for \(\mathfrak q\) of Euclidean size \(O_F(p^{1/3})\)
by using a fixed ideal class representative and balancing the logs of
the three conjugates of its scaling factor by a unit. The congruence
selects a translate of \(M\mathfrak q\) by the Chinese remainder theorem.
Its fundamental parallelepipeds have volume
\(M^3p\sqrt{|\operatorname{disc}(F)|}\) and diameter \(O_F(Mp^{1/3})\).
Count their translates intersecting \(\sqrt p B\). The volume error
lies in a boundary region of thickness \(O_F(Mp^{1/3})\). Dividing the box
boundary estimate by the cell volume gives the asserted error, including
the possible removal of zero. For the valuation claim, the inequality
\(0<|N_{F/\mathbb Q}(a)|<8p^{3/2}<p^2\) excludes a second factor of \(p\).
The last assertion uses the count at \(M=1\).

The count applies to the local and archimedean candidate space. The
distribution of the traces selected by one fixed abelian variety within
that space remains open.

## Exact checks

For the [level-97 example](CM_NEWTON_SPLIT_FAILURE_WITH_FULL_RESIDUAL_IMAGE.md)
the three absolute discriminant cofactors \(d\) are squarefree.

| \(p\) | \(U\) | \(V\) | \(d=c\) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 113 | 31 | 886 | \(7\cdot13\cdot7433\) |
| 1583 | \(-151\) | 11932 | \(7\cdot1567\cdot9689\) |
| 1709 | 115 | 13878 | \(7\cdot71\cdot342047\) |

The following PARI/GP calculation replays the norm values and residue tests.

```gp
y = 'y; T = 'T;
f = y^3-y^2-2*y+1;
case_primes = [113, 1583, 1709];
case_traces = [-3*y^2-y+8, 12*y^2-23*y-26, -4*y^2+10*y+13];
{
for(i=1,3,
  p = case_primes[i]; a = Mod(case_traces[i], f); P = charpoly(a, T);
  U = norm(a)/p - 4*polcoef(P, 2);
  V = 2*(polcoef(P, 1) + 4*p);
  d = -norm(a^2-4*p)/p; C = factor(d);
  if(V^2-p*U^2 != d || kronecker(d,p) != 1, error("norm check"));
  for(j=1,matsize(C)[1],
    if(C[j,2] != 1 || kronecker(p,C[j,1]) != 1, error("local check"))
  );
  print([p, U, V, d, C])
);
}
```
