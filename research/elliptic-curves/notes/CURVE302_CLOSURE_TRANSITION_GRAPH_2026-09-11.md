# Curve302 closure transition graph and rank-29 control

This is a deterministic post-processing experiment over the sealed chart replay
and the passed decisive bridge extraction. It launches no point search and uses
no chart-completeness assumption.

It answers two questions:

1. Do the 27 decisive common-core growth stages collapse to a small finite set of
   exact saturated subgroup transitions / bridge-line families?
2. Does the unique rank-29 trajectory stop before one of the successful late
   bridge gates, and does its terminal positive evidence contain any representative
   of those successful bridge-line families?

Historical prefix lattices were independently certified saturated. Therefore a
saturated subgroup is identified exactly by the RREF of its rational span. The
rank-29 comparison projects successful dimension-12/13 late bridges into the
2-dimensional quotient left by the stalled rank-12 prefix.

The rank-29 result remains positive-evidence-only: absence from the terminal
recorded positives is not promoted to exhaustive chart absence.
