## Result

Twenty exactly matched curves in each of three retained score bands completed
860 boxes per arm, for 2,580 boxes total with no timeout, worker failure, or
unresolved point certificate. The extreme-top arm found ten certified
directions in 875.808 discovery worker-seconds; moderate found one in 867.214;
lower found none in 877.662. Its yield was 2.7203 times the equal three-arm
portfolio yield in this sample, and remained ahead in the five
leave-one-family-out comparisons.

The experiment also supplied Curve200's then-new rank-at-least-25 result.
Its current rank-at-least-26 certificate is recorded separately in
[Curve200's later result](CURVE200_PRODUCTIVE_V3_SEARCH_2026-09-09.md).

## Boundary

This supports only a matched finite-pool allocation choice. Nine of the ten
top-band directions came from one curve. It does not prove score optimality,
rank density, exact ranks, record-tail probability, universal novelty, or a
policy for a new population. The frozen diversification criterion failed; no
automatic follow-on sweep is authorized.
