# Class1 ordinary prospective search

The complete class1 experiment, including the original and corrected control
cohorts, frozen runtime, receipts, and commands, is preserved byte-for-byte in
[the archive](../../archive/elliptic-curves/notes/CLASS1_PROSPECTIVE_ORDINARY_SEARCH_2026-09-10.md.txt)
(`sha256: 1826c8ac87c6ffa1beb324283b6813404eecdb7bf5ce3c74247ef3ac89967c4c`).
It is a terminal campaign record and does not authorize a launch, resume,
wider address window, or retry.

The corrected campaign completed 64 ranked fibres and 16 independent controls
in 1,920 calls with no certified gain beyond lower bound 17. This is an
operational no-evidence result, not a rank upper bound or a score-efficacy
theorem. Its [commissioning packet](../../artifacts/generated-results/elliptic-curves/class1_prospective_search_commissioning_v2.json)
binds the fixed v2 selection, source snapshot, score rows, and queue.

The original v1 control rule selected indices congruent to 7 modulo 8. Because
the signed-address enumerator assigns odd indices negative rationals, that
score-independent stride was sign-confounded. The audit stopped further v1
dispatch after one control was allowed to finish. V2 instead used a disjoint
65,536-address window and a hash-selected 8,192-address control stream
(4,153 positive and 4,039 negative); neither its controls nor its selection
used v1 outcomes.

For any future separately authorized comparison, audit the control allocator
against address-derived sign, height, and other structural features before the
first point call. Keep aborted and corrected cohorts separate. The
[control-allocation method](../../knowledge/ALGORITHMS.md#method-ec-control-allocation-audit-balance-structural-address-features-before-dispatch)
holds the reusable rule; the [programme map](../README.md) selects current work.
