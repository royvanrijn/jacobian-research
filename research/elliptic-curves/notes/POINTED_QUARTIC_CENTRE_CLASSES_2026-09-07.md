# What a new point direction changes in pointed quartic searches

There is an exact reason to try centres involving newly discovered directions
before enlarging the parameter population. On an elliptic curve over Q, two
degree-two pointed presentations with centres C and C′ are equivalent by a
rational curve automorphism and a PGL2(Q) coordinate change **if and only if**

\[
C'-C\in2E(\mathbf Q).
\]

Thus a certified new class in E(Q)/2E(Q) supplies degree-two presentations
unavailable from the earlier subgroup. This is an application of classical
elliptic-curve geometry, not a new curve, an extra Selmer direction, or a
guarantee of improved bounded point discovery.

The [portable exact check](../../artifacts/generated-results/elliptic-curves/pointed_quartic_centre_classes_v1/manifest.json)
verifies the raw quartic identity and98 such centre classes in two already
completed own-subgroup follow-ups. It uses exact rational group arithmetic
and independent complete finite-group enumeration. It launches no point
search and does not change the active441-box experiment.

## The raw quartic's involution

Write E:y²=x³+Ax+B and C=(c,d). The slope through −C is

\[
m=\frac{y+d}{x-c}.
\]

Substitute y=m(x−c)−d and B=d²−c³−Ac. Exact polynomial division gives

\[
x^3+Ax+B-(m(x-c)-d)^2
=(x-c)\bigl(x^2+(c-m^2)x+c^2+A+m^2c+2md\bigr).
\]

The discriminant of the quadratic factor is precisely our raw quartic:

\[
v^2=m^4-6cm^2-8dm-3c^2-4A.
\]

The two remaining intersections P and R of the line with E satisfy
P+R=C. The degree-two map therefore has deck involution
\(i_C(P)=C-P\), and its pole divisor is O+C. Denominator clearing, Gauss
changes and the exact PARI reductions used by the mapper change its coordinates;
they do not change this degree-two presentation class.

## Equivalence criterion

The classical ingredients are the group interpretation of degree-zero divisor
classes and the decomposition of curve automorphisms into translation followed
by an automorphism fixing O. See Milne, *Elliptic Curves*, I.4.10, IV.7.13 and
IV.7.18 in the [author's second-edition text](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf).
Over Q, even the exceptional j cases have only ±1 as automorphisms fixing O,
since Q contains no additional fourth or sixth roots of unity.

For \(\phi(P)=\epsilon P+T\), where \(\epsilon=\pm1\), direct conjugation yields

\[
\phi\,i_C\,\phi^{-1}=i_{\epsilon C+2T}.
\]

Equivalent degree-two maps have conjugate deck involutions, so necessarily
C′=εC+2T. Since −C≡C modulo2E(Q), this implies C′−C∈2E(Q).
Conversely, if C′=C+2T, translation by T conjugates the involutions. Their
quotients are both P1 over Q, and the induced isomorphism between the quotient
coordinates is a PGL2(Q) transformation. This proves the criterion.

This classification concerns the **pair consisting of the curve and its
degree-two map**. All these presentations remain isomorphic as unpointed
elliptic curves. Also, inequivalent maps can return overlapping or even
identical finite point sets in particular bounded searches.

## Finite certificates make this operational

Let P1,…,Pr have independent images in a certified product of finite quotients
E(Fp)/2E(Fp), and let G be generated by the first s of these points, s<r.
For any centre C=ΣniPi whose coefficient parity has a nonzero entry after s,
the finite image of C lies outside the image of G. Therefore

\[
C-G\ \cap\ 2E(\mathbf Q)=\varnothing.
\]

The equivalence criterion then excludes every degree-two centre presentation
from G, including all of its unsampled integer combinations. Distinct full
parity words likewise give inequivalent presentations. This argument uses
exact finite independence; an unverified numerical height rank would not
suffice. It does not require knowing the full Mordell–Weil group or proving
global saturation.

Those earlier follow-ups added no further certified direction. They are also
an explicit reminder that new presentation classes do not guarantee search
success. For the active mixed-parent trial, any later updated-subgroup
follow-up must wait for completion and independent certification of the
frozen experiment, then declare its own fixed exposure and inputs.
