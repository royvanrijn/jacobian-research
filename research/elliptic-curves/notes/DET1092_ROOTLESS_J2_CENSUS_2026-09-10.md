## Theorem

The complete pruned Niemeier census has exactly **19 integral-isometry classes**
of rootless rank-17 determinant-1092 complements. The recovered Curve302 frame
is class 6, leaving 18 additional frame types. Class 1 later received a
[rational marked nef U, exact equation and saturated MW17 basis](X1092_CLASS1_RATIONAL_MW17_REALIZATION_2026-09-10.md).

The result classifies the stated J2/frame objects only. It does not classify J1
or surface-automorphism orbits, produce rational realizations or specialization
ranks for the other classes, or predict a high-rank specialization.

## Proof boundary

The enumeration covers every primitive auxiliary embedding through the 16
D5-anchor orbits in the 13 rooted Niemeier types. Exact shared-norm pruning,
rational LDL arithmetic, integral primitivity, saturated kernels and integral
isometry deduplication reduce 10,098 sixth-vector representatives to 208
rootless embeddings and then 19 classes. The certificate retains every Gram,
embedding-to-class isometry and the known-frame isometry. Counts are
representatives in this Weyl cover, not all ambient embedding orbits.

The clean replay reproduces the retained packets with the same enumerator; it
is not an independently written completeness proof. The historical timings and
implementation repairs are evidence for that finite computation, not a general
speed claim.
