# The first seventeen submitted points on record curves 273 and 302

Status: **exact subgroup, quotient, finite-Kummer-code, and component-code
calculation inside the displayed rank-30/rank-31 subgroups; 100-digit
canonical-height computation**.  This does not assert exact Mordell--Weil rank
or saturation in the full Mordell--Weil group.

Let `D` be the subgroup generated by the complete submitted point list, let
`G17=<P1,...,P17>`, and let `C17` be the independently selected primitive
rank-17 candidate from
[`RECORD_CURVES_28_29_273_302_HEIGHT_LATTICES.md`](RECORD_CURVES_28_29_273_302_HEIGHT_LATTICES.md).
The exact coordinate-lattice results are:

| curve | rank `D` | `[Sat_D(G17):G17]` | rank `G17 intersect C17` | rank `D/G17` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 273 | 30 | 1 | 10 | 13 |
| 302 | 31 | 1 | 9 | 14 |

The saturation result is literal: the exact independence certificates identify
the ordered submitted points with a basis of `Z^30` or `Z^31`, and `G17` is
the first-coordinate direct summand.  It says nothing about saturation in
`E(Q)`.  The quotient classes of `P18,...,P30/P31` are the standard basis of
the free quotient `Z^13/Z^14`; in particular every one has infinite order and
there are no relations among these displayed quotient classes.

## Canonical heights and theta profiles

PARI recomputes the two 17-by-17 canonical-height Grams at 100 decimal digits.
The complete matrices and LLL transforms are in the artifact.  Normalize each
rank-17 lattice by the unique positive `lambda` satisfying

```text
det(L) = 948 * lambda^17.
```

| curve | lattice | `lambda` | minimum / `lambda` | lines through `3 lambda` | lines through `4 lambda` | lines through `5 lambda` |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 273 | first 17 | 20.1288372058 | 2.160696 | 57 | 295 | 2,013 |
| 273 | candidate core | 14.7217730919 | 3.202856 | 0 | 474 | 1,564 |
| 302 | first 17 | 23.5561478230 | 2.240500 | 46 | 331 | 1,994 |
| 302 | candidate core | 16.2450072560 | 3.159648 | 0 | 469 | 1,525 |

The first-17 determinant scales exceed the candidate-core scales by factors
`1.36728` and `1.45005`.  More importantly, determinant normalization does not
align the short end of the theta profiles: the first-17 lattices have 57 and
46 unoriented lines below normalized height 3, while both candidate cores have
none.  Their normalized 1,311st nearest heights are closer (`4.75118/4.82059`
and `4.75186/4.80867`), but that bulk statistic does not undo the exact low
intersection ranks or the different shortest-vector profiles.

Canonical heights are real-valued, so the Gram and theta layer is a
high-precision numerical computation, not exact algebraic arithmetic.  The
coordinate, finite-quotient, and component layers are exact.

## Exact local codes

The pinned independence certificates supply faithful products of
`E(F_p)/2E(F_p)`.  Restriction to `G17` has binary dimension 17 on both curves.
After quotienting the full product code by that image, the remaining points
have dimensions 13 and 14 respectively.  Thus every remaining submitted point
contributes an independent exact mod-2 Kummer class modulo `G17`.  The artifact
records a canonical annihilator of the `G17` code and the resulting bit vector
for each point `P18,...`.

The bad-fibre calculation records the complete Neron component map at every
bad prime with nontrivial component group.  Curve 273 uses component moduli

```text
16,12,8,5,5,2,2,4,3,3,2,
```

and curve 302 uses

```text
15,2,3,6,4,5,2,2,3,2,2,2,2,2.
```

For split multiplicative fibres, component orientation is fixed up to the
intrinsic global inversion by declaring the first ambiguous submitted point
positive; every pairwise point sum is then replayed exactly.  The nonsplit
`I4` and additive `IV` places of curve 302 are handled in their component
groups of orders 2 and 3.  In both curves, `G17` already surjects onto the
entire product of bad-fibre component groups.  Consequently all remaining
points have trivial class in the component product modulo `G17`: component
data alone cannot see the 13/14-dimensional quotient, whereas the faithful
good-reduction Kummer code sees all of it.

## Interpretation

This is the third proposed outcome, not either of the first two.  The first
seventeen submitted points do not equal the independently selected structural
candidate, and their intersections have ranks only 10 and 9.  Their
determinant-normalized theta profiles also differ sharply at the shortest end.
The evidence therefore supports point 17 as an ordering or simplicity
boundary in these submissions, not as an exposed generic rank-17 construction
basis.  This conclusion concerns the repository's independently selected
candidate cores; those candidates themselves remain bounded structural
selections and are not identified with a transported geometric `R17`.
