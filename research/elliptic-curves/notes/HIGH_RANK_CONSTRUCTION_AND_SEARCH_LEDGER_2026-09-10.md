# High-rank elliptic-curve construction and search ledger

Status: consolidated historical/strategic ledger through 2026-09-10. This file is an index and interpretation layer, not a replacement for `MATH_STATUS.json` or the individual proof artifacts. When this ledger says **proved/certified**, the cited repository result is the authority. When it says **evidence**, **heuristic**, **reported**, or **open**, no stronger theorem should be inferred.

## Executive summary

The project did **not** originally discover ICARM curve302. Curve302 entered as an external high-rank target from the ICARM catalogue, with 31 displayed independent points. Our contribution has been to verify that rank lower bound, reconstruct increasingly strong parent surfaces around it, prove a full generic-MW17 K3 parent specializing literally to curve302, understand much of the specialization quotient, build new MW17 fibrations on the same K3, and test many candidate mechanisms for why some fibres gain exceptional rank.

The central fact is now:

- curve302 has a certified displayed subgroup of rank at least 31;
- one recovered X1092 elliptic K3 parent has exact generic MW rank 17 and specializes at `t=0` to curve302;
- the specialized generic MW17 is a primitive rank-17 core of the displayed rank-31 subgroup, leaving a displayed quotient `Z^14`;
- X1092 has geometric Picard rank 19, hence generic MW17 is the exact Jacobian-fibration ceiling on this K3;
- a complete rootless-J2 census found **19** determinant-1092 MW17 frame types, of which curve302's frame is class 6 and 18 are nonisometric alternatives;
- class 1 has now been realized over Q as a second exact generic-MW17 elliptic fibration on the same K3;
- changing the frame alone has not yet reproduced curve302-like specialization gains: the first class-1 prospective commissioning run found no gain above 17 in its completed fibres;
- several attractive geometric explanations have been falsified or sharply bounded: smooth quadratic-cover multiplicity, shared smooth branch covers, Euclidean norm-10 bisection incidence, low-degree relation counts, and simple carrier multiplicity do not explain the large jumps;
- the strongest arithmetic constructive result so far is that equation-only principal dependencies can create new strict/Kummer classes before rational points are consulted. At MW16-05, `t=3/17`, two such classes survive modulo the inherited image and were subsequently shown retrospectively to be soluble.

The missing bridge remains prospective and arithmetic:

`parameter/equation condition -> new arithmetic class -> soluble cover -> new rational point -> independent MW direction -> high-rank amplification`.

## 1. How curve302 entered the project

Curve302 is an externally sourced catalogue curve, not a curve generated by the current repository search. The provenance audit pins the ICARM source and verification code; the original unpublished construction route remains unknown. The repository's `ECR31` certificate verifies the displayed 31 points and proves **rank at least 31**, not exact rank 31.

This distinction matters throughout the history. Early work sometimes asked "how was 302 constructed?"; later work separated that into two questions:

1. can we reconstruct *a* structured parent that explains much of its rank-31 point set? — now yes;
2. can we recover the original discoverer's construction? — still unknown and not required for current search.

## 2. What rank knowledge is actually certified

### The fibre curve302

The public/displayed point group `D` has 31 certified independent rational points. Therefore `rank E302(Q) >= 31`.

The repository does **not** currently prove `rank E302(Q)=31`. The displayed group has strong saturation information:

- exact parity-domain saturation at 2;
- finite-quotient saturation at 3, 5 and 7;
- trivial rational torsion;
- no nontrivial rational isogeny to another Q-isomorphism class.

These close several ways in which the displayed group might have hidden small-index defects, but they are not a complete rank upper bound.

### The recovered determinant-1092 parent

`CURVE302_RECOVERED_MW17_PARENT_2026-09-07.md` proves an explicit elliptic K3 over `Q(t)` with:

- generic arithmetic MW rank exactly 17;
- trivial torsion;
- a full saturated MW17 basis;
- height determinant 1092;
- 24 geometric `I1` fibres, hence rootless frame;
- fibre `t=0` literally equal to curve302;
- specialization of the MW17 basis equal to the complete primitive rank-17 core inside the displayed rank-31 group;
- quotient of displayed groups `D / sp(MW17) ~= Z^14`.

A Frobenius computation at 149 plus follow-up geometric work proves geometric Picard rank 19 and geometric MW17. By Shioda-Tate, 17 is the maximum generic MW rank for a Jacobian fibration on this K3.

This is the cleanest current explanation of **where 17 of curve302's 31 displayed directions come from**. It does **not** explain the remaining fourteen as a single mechanism.

### Other parent ranks

The project also constructed or recovered several other parents:

- a completed generic-MW9 K3 specializing to curve302, useful as a baseline but not competitive as a record-search parent;
- determinant-948 rootless MW17 production fibrations, with a complete two-type J2 census and six rational fibration orbits represented by existing charts;
- multiple A1/MW16 parents constructed by the parent foundry; these produced real rank gains in commissioning panels but no evidence of a record-scale tail;
- X1092 class 1, a second exact rational MW17 fibration on the same K3 as curve302, independently replayed with saturated height determinant 1092.

## 3. How the recovered MW17 parent was actually constructed

This history is important because it gives reusable constructive methods, not merely a list of existing frames.

### 3.1 Recover a candidate generic height lattice from specialized points

A mixed-height reconstruction on curve302 found a unique determinant-1092 positive even form after removing one norm-6 outlier from 401 integral-point words; the remaining 400 behaved as norm-4 vectors. Exact enumeration proved minimum 4 and lattice embedding checks connected the form to the determinant-1092 K3 arithmetic type.

This was the key inversion step: use a large specialized point configuration to infer a plausible generic MW lattice.

### 3.2 Build a geometric polarization from the recovered lattice

The successful construction chose a degree-four polarization `D=O+P+Q` with old-fibre degree 3. Fifteen predicted line sections became actual lines in a plane/projective model. Their incidence equations produced a four-dimensional kernel containing three trivial concurrent-line solutions and one nontrivial configuration with all required incidences/nonincidences.

A unique quartic through those lines and the prescribed node was then reconstructed. The pencil `W=tL` produced a residual cubic with a rational origin.

### 3.3 Convert the cubic pencil to Weierstrass form

A tangent-frame/Cremona conversion and a constant normalization gave an explicit elliptic surface whose zero fibre is literally curve302. This is a concrete inverse-construction method:

`specialized point/lattice pattern -> projective polarization -> quartic -> cubic pencil -> Weierstrass family`.

### 3.4 Recover the full generic section basis

Fifteen lines plus the first conics did not automatically give rank 17. The construction initially produced 19 displayed sections but rank only 15. Two further rational conics, chosen using the recovered height form and checked outside the current span, raised the generic rank to 17. A unimodular word matrix then identified a full basis of the recovered core.

### 3.5 Prove exact generic rank and saturation

The proof did not rely on "we found 17 sections, therefore rank 17". It combined:

- exact height Gram determinant 1092;
- K3 fibre geometry;
- Frobenius/Picard upper bounds;
- even-overlattice/discriminant-form saturation arguments.

This construction path is one of the project's strongest reusable tools.

Canonical source: `elliptic-curves/notes/CURVE302_RECOVERED_MW17_PARENT_2026-09-07.md`.

## 4. What we know about why curve302 is high rank

We know substantially more about **structure** than about **cause**.

### Established

- Seventeen directions are generic on the recovered X1092 parent.
- Fourteen displayed directions are independent modulo that generic MW17 core.
- The specialization core is primitive inside the displayed rank-31 group.
- Ordinary finite-quotient machinery can recover many exceptional directions once good seeds/points exist.
- Adaptive search and full-cloud reconciliation matter: returned point clouds can contain independent directions not visible from the nominal search seed alone.
- Large rank jumps are not monotone in simple point-height, half-lattice recovery score, low-degree relation count, native cover count, or smooth-cover multiplicity.

### Strong negative evidence

The project has systematically tested and weakened several tempting explanations:

1. **Euclidean norm-10 rational bisections.** The exact Euclidean construction is real and cheap once the trace is known, but the prospective pilot's certified-MW17 split candidates all closed as inherited integer combinations of the generic sections. Rational split incidence is therefore not a useful rank-gain selector in that tested lane.

2. **One smooth quadratic cover carrying multiple new directions.** The retained carrier atlas normalizes to many arithmetic cover groups, but each observed smooth group contributes quotient rank one. A stronger branch theorem shows same-parent rootless-MW16 smooth covers have disjoint geometric branch images; this whole smooth atlas cannot supply a second direction on one cover.

3. **Simple 2-cover geometry.** Smooth genus-one 2-cover geometry, first-conic rationality and normalized pencil determinant are structural and cannot distinguish rational Kummer classes from Sha. The arithmetic squareclass/solubility obstruction remains.

4. **Low-degree pair/triple relations.** Extensive relation panels found moderate-gain/control phenomena but no monotone signature explaining the largest jumps; notably some low-degree triple relations concentrated on a +4 control rather than the largest jumps.

5. **Carrier multiplicity.** Exceptional fibres and controls both showed singleton multiplicity in the retained squareclass audit. No predictor emerged.

6. **Frame rank alone.** Generic rank matters as baseline, but changing from curve302's MW17 frame to the new class-1 MW17 frame did not immediately create visible extra sections under the first prospective search.

### Positive but incomplete mechanism evidence

The strongest constructive arithmetic result is the MW16-05 `t=3/17` strict-class block:

- two additional strict classes were constructed from equation/generic-only principal dependencies;
- their half ideals provide two independent ordinary ideal-class 2-torsion directions beyond the inherited strict image;
- only after construction froze were known points used to identify rational lifts;
- independent exact checks proved the two associated covers soluble.

This gives a genuine prospective first arrow:

`equation-only arithmetic dependency -> new strict classes`.

What remains unproved is a cheap condition on `t` forcing that event and an oracle-free general solver taking a novel class to a rational point.

## 5. Complete X1092 frame census and what it changed

The complete determinant-1092 Nishiyama/J2 census found 19 integral-isometry classes of rootless rank-17 complements. Curve302 is class 6; 18 classes are nonisometric alternatives. All 208 retained rootless embeddings occurred in the final `2A7+2D5` anchor; the first fifteen anchors had none.

This corrected an important earlier implementation defect: the old census code asserted that every rootless complement must be isometric to curve302's frame, so a genuinely new frame would have aborted. The successor retained all complements before deduplication and exposed 18 alternatives.

The census is a frame/J2 classification, not a classification of surface-automorphism/J1 orbits and not an equation catalogue.

Class 1 was then realized explicitly over Q:

- exact NS transport;
- primitive nef fibre with rational zero;
- rational elliptic equation and birational maps;
- 17 rational sections;
- saturated height lattice determinant 1092;
- exact generic rank 17.

This proves the construction pipeline can turn a new frame class into a usable MW17 parent.

## 6. Constructive methods currently available

The toolbox is broader than "use an existing frame".

### A. Specialized-lattice inversion

Infer candidate generic height lattices from large independent specialized point configurations, parity classes and mixed-height shells. This is how determinant 1092 was recovered around curve302.

Strength: can reverse-engineer hidden structure from a spectacular fibre.
Weakness: retrospective and expensive; not directly a prospective selector.

### B. Polarization / line-and-conic projective reconstruction

Use a lattice-predicted polarization to turn specialized sections into lines/conics, solve incidence kernels, reconstruct a quartic/K3 model, and extract an elliptic pencil.

Strength: produced the recovered curve302 MW17 parent.
Weakness: requires unusually coherent specialized geometry.

### C. Nishiyama / Niemeier frame enumeration

Construct a complementary auxiliary lattice with opposite discriminant form, enumerate primitive embeddings into rooted Niemeier lattices, retain rootless complements, and deduplicate by exact integral isometry.

Strength: complete finite census of generic-rank-ceiling frame types.
Weakness: frame existence is only the first gate; rational marked-U/equation realization may still be difficult and frame type alone has not predicted specialization tail.

### D. Marked-U / NS transport realization

Transport a target frame into a known rational NS marking, find a primitive nef `U` with effective fibre and rational zero, compile the Riemann-Roch pencil, derive a rational elliptic equation, and recover generic sections.

Strength: successfully realized X1092 class 1.
Weakness: currently bespoke enough that realizing all 18 alternatives is not free.

### E. Parent foundry / A1 constructions

Construct new fibrations from lattice moves and marked geometric data, certify equations and generic sections, then run bounded parameter panels.

Strength: produced several genuine new parents and certified +3/+4-style gains.
Weakness: commissioning evidence did not support broad MW16 expansion for record search.

### F. Mestre/square-completion and related point-rich constructions

The repository contains extensive Mestre quartic/square-completion machinery and searches over polynomial/root configurations. These provide many explicit points by construction and were used both for production families and inverse-construction experiments.

Strength: deterministic point-rich curve generation.
Weakness: lower generic baseline or constrained geometry can make rank-30+ ambitions difficult without another amplification mechanism.

### G. Quadratic-cover / bisection constructions

Norm traces, Euclidean bisections, quadratic carriers and 2-cover machinery can produce exact covers and sometimes rational sections after base change.

Strength: exact, algebraic, often cheap incidence tests.
Weakness: tested smooth-cover incidence does not predict independent specialization gain; many split candidates are inherited or Sha-obstructed.

### H. Strict/Kummer principal-dependency construction

Use cubic algebra, norm-square representatives, half ideals, local/global class data and principal dependencies to construct arithmetic classes outside the inherited image.

Strength: first genuinely point-free construction of new classes on a calibration fibre; recent novelty machinery certifies inherited-image dimensions and quotient novelty exactly.
Weakness: ambient quotient construction can hit hard discriminant factorization, and class novelty is not cover solubility or MW independence.

### I. Direct rational-point search / V3 adaptive amplification

Given a certified generic seed subgroup on a specialized fibre, use bounded point-search calls, merge/reconcile complete returned clouds, certify finite-quotient independence, and increase budget aggressively after gains.

Strength: proven ability to amplify when useful directions are visible; exact replay and subgroup certificates are mature.
Weakness: expensive per fibre and poor if the representation hides extra points.

### J. Cheap arithmetic triage

Mestre/Nagao-style point-count scores and local reduction metrics can score very large parameter windows extremely cheaply before expensive point search. The class-1 commissioning run scored 65,536 addresses in only seconds.

Strength: ideal for compute-heavy broad funnels.
Weakness: current class-1 top-score sample has not yet shown rank gains, so score calibration is family-dependent and not a theorem.

## 7. Search history: what has worked operationally

The project repeatedly learned that **breadth plus exact reconciliation** beats deep repetition on a fixed stalled fibre.

Useful search practices now established:

- freeze prospective address windows before seeing outcomes;
- keep score-selected and score-independent control lanes;
- separate generic rank, found points, finite independence and final rank lower bound;
- reconcile every returned point cloud, not just the nominal point;
- adapt budgets upward only after certified gains;
- preserve UNKNOWN for censored or incomplete arithmetic;
- charge cold construction/scoring/admission costs when comparing methods;
- keep historical exceptional coordinates out of prospective selectors.

The high-rank foundry notes also show that seed quality and representation matter. Some known high-rank fibres become easy only after a better subgroup or presentation is supplied. This is one reason negative point-search results are evidence about a **search representation**, not rank upper bounds.

## 10. What curve302 seems to teach us

The accumulated evidence suggests the following hierarchy.

### High confidence

1. A high generic MW baseline is valuable: curve302 starts from an exact MW17 parent.
2. The exceptional part is arithmetic specialization, not merely abstract frame rank.
3. Visibility matters enormously: the same independent direction can be hard or easy depending on parent/presentation/search seed.
4. No tested scalar score or simple smooth-carrier statistic explains rank-jump magnitude.
5. New arithmetic classes can be constructed before points are known, so a real prospective arithmetic selector is possible in principle.

### Plausible, not proved

1. Record fibres may occur where several arithmetic solubility/principalization events coincide, rather than where one geometric cover gains huge multiplicity.
2. Different MW17 fibrations of the same K3 may expose different subsets of those arithmetic events and therefore have very different search visibility despite equal generic rank.
3. Broad search over many representations may outperform heroic depth on one representation.

### Not supported

- "curve302 is high because its MW17 frame lattice is unique" — false; X1092 has 19 rootless MW17 frame types.
- "a split quadratic cover is probably a new direction" — false in the tested Euclidean pilot.
- "more smooth cover multiplicity means larger jump" — not supported.
- "Nagao top score automatically predicts extra visible rank" — not supported on the first class-1 commissioning sample.

## 11. Recommended next compute-heavy campaign

No current experiment should be treated as mandatory. If substantial CPU is available but interactive-agent tokens are scarce, the next job should maximize **data generation per unit of orchestration** and should be resumable with fixed rules.

### Recommended: multi-parent broad commissioning matrix

Do **not** spend the next large budget deepening class 1 alone. Build a dataset across genuinely different parent representations.

Target parents, in order of readiness:

1. recovered X1092 curve302 MW17 parent (class 6);
2. realized X1092 class-1 MW17 parent;
3. the existing determinant-948 R17/MW17 parent;
4. alternate-Q80 / other already-certified X948 MW17 charts;
5. selected strongest A1/MW16 parents only as lower-baseline controls.

For each ready parent, run the exact same frozen parameter/address protocol.

Suggested first wave per parent:

- score `2^18 = 262,144` rational addresses cheaply;
- retain all cheap feature rows, not only one aggregate score;
- expensive-search the top 256 by the existing score;
- expensive-search 64 hash-selected unbiased controls;
- optionally add 64 parameters chosen for feature diversity/extreme disagreement between scores;
- initial point budget 24–32 calls;
- escalate automatically at certified rank >=20, >=23, >=25, >=27 using existing V3 rules;
- reconcile every cloud and certify every gain;
- no LLM/agent decisions inside the run.

This yields roughly 320–384 expensive fibres per parent and a large labelled cheap-feature dataset. With several parents, it directly measures whether representation/fibration choice changes the high-rank tail.

### Features to retain

For every cheap-scored parameter, persist enough information to learn a better selector later without rerunning the window:

- all per-prime `a_p` values used by Nagao/Mestre scoring;
- aggregate score at several prime cutoffs, not just one final value;
- skipped/singular reduction pattern;
- numerator/denominator height and factorization summaries;
- discriminant value bit size, valuation pattern at small primes, and cheap squarefree/gcd summaries;
- root-number/local-sign information if cheap and exact;
- conductor proxies already available without expensive Tate-algorithm work;
- parent/frame identifier;
- inherited-image/local-squareclass features from the new novelty machinery when they do not require full discriminant factorization;
- point-search outcome, number of calls, CPU, number of raw points, reconciled independent gain and final certified lower bound.

The important dataset is not only `score -> rank`; it is `parent + local arithmetic vector -> search visibility/gain`.

### Why this is preferable now

The marginal cost of scoring tens or hundreds of thousands of fibres is tiny compared with one point-search job. We therefore have enough CPU to collect a broad labelled dataset while minimizing token-heavy theorem iteration. If no parent produces gains, the dataset tells us whether the failure is score selection, representation, or universal scarcity. If one parent has a visibly stronger tail, concentrate later depth there.

### Second wave: frozen selector learning without leakage

After wave 1 closes, train a simple model/ranking rule offline using only the retained cheap features and certified outcomes. Logistic regression, boosted trees or even greedy linear combinations are sufficient; interpretability is more valuable than sophistication. Freeze the V2 selector before opening a disjoint address range.

Then run:

`wave-1 data -> frozen V2 selector -> fresh disjoint addresses -> prospective validation`.

Do not repeatedly retune on the same address window.

### Parallel arithmetic lane

If spare cores remain, run incomplete principal-dependency candidate generation on the same parameters. It need not compute a complete ambient class group. Any candidate can be passed through the exact novelty checker; failure to generate a candidate stays UNKNOWN.

The positive calibration requirement should remain: the generator must rediscover at least one of the known `3/17` extra classes without oracle points before its misses are interpreted.

## 12. Immediate decision rule after the wide run

Use compute results, not aesthetics:

- if any parent produces repeated >=20 gains and occasional >=23, deepen that parent and its nearby representations;
- if class 6/curve302 systematically outperforms class 1 under matched address/search rules, study what arithmetic features distinguish the two parents;
- if another X1092 frame outperforms curve302, prioritize realizing neighbouring/tied frame classes;
- if all MW17 parents are flat under ordinary point search, invest in arithmetic class/cover solubility generation rather than larger V3 breadth;
- if cheap features predict gains prospectively, scale the selector over millions of addresses;
- if no cheap feature predicts anything, stop treating Nagao/local scoring as the main selection mechanism and use random breadth plus representation diversity.

## 13. Current scientific target

The record target remains rank 32, but the next useful milestones are more granular:

1. a **new** fibre at certified rank >=23 from a prospective search;
2. a new MW17 parent that demonstrably has a better gain distribution than existing parents;
3. a prospectively constructed novel arithmetic class on an unseen parameter;
4. an oracle-free soluble cover from such a class;
5. a certified independent specialization direction produced by that arithmetic pipeline;
6. only then, repeated amplification toward 27–32.

The project now has enough exact infrastructure that the best next move is not another speculative theorem cascade. It is a wide, frozen, multi-parent experiment that produces a reusable empirical map of where the existing mathematics actually yields rank.
