## Independent Kummer proof and first-class obstruction

**Established description, verified application.** On a monic cubic,
rational Kummer representatives are `X-theta`, and divisor sums multiply
them; see [Cremona et al., section3](https://www.mathe2.uni-bayreuth.de/stoll/papers/Explicit-Descent-III.pdf).
At a good odd prime and a rational cubic root `r`, the restriction class
is tested by the quadratic character of

\[
\operatorname{Norm}_{(\mathbf F_p[t]/g_i)/\mathbf F_p}(X_i(t)-r).
\]

The constructor uses resultants. The independent checker instead uses
explicit multiplication matrices and determinants. Each accepted place
contains **all18 inherited columns**: `F` and the17 old sections.
A denominator or nonunit failure skips the entire place, not a generator.

**Verified arithmetic.** On the generic `z=0` member and the first-seed
member, the frozen64-prime pool gives37 complete places and joint rank12.
This matches the full-image upper bound. Root-free cubics at43 and37,
respectively, exclude rational2-torsion. The first reconstructed class
`eta=[A-B]` raises the joint rank to13. An exact separating row uses
cubic-root characters at109,227,263,271,283,307; its root labels and full
matrix are retained in the
[first-class replay](../../artifacts/generated-results/elliptic-curves/det1092_genus1_picard_image_v1/replay.json).

At each retained place separately the inherited subgroup fills the entire
local Kummer image. Only simultaneous compatibility separates the class.
This is not a global Selmer computation.

**New rational-span deduction.** The finite rank12 injection makes the
index of `H` in its rational-span saturation odd at2. Its saturation has
the same2-Kummer image, so the separating character proves

\[
\boxed{\eta\notin H_{z_*}\otimes\mathbf Q,
\qquad\operatorname{rank}J_{z_*}(\mathbf Q)\ge13.}
\]

The conjugate point has class `rho(F)-eta`, so it is outside the same
rational span. No multiple or group combination of inherited carrier
points can reach either point above302. Every generic section of this
alternative fibration has a class in the full Neron--Severi group and
specializes inside `H_z`; the first point is therefore not any such
generic-section specialization. This excludes an entire generic-section
route in this pencil, not merely a finite list of small multiples.

Yet its original-elliptic image is identically generic section0. The failed
specificity is built into a generic construction, not evidence of an
unknown exceptional old point on a supposedly null control.
