band/family from its fixed 8,192-address signed pool: 10,240 total. Selection
uses the full cached score, good count, denominator and signed numerator,
with only within-prospective-roster rational-isomorphism deduplication.
No public-record target or previous point outcome enters selection.

Fresh scalar traces through 65521 must reproduce the first cached extension
and pass two independent character sums per equation. The first twenty calls
include two per band/family and have a fixed cost gate. Four workers,
20 seconds per call, 80-row checkpoints and a 2,400-second cap apply. All
10,240 scores and the prospective extraction must replay before selecting
six per band/family, sixty total. Wholly disjoint primes 65537 through 131071
validate the frozen finalists and cannot change selection.

The independent [model and retention audit](../cas/audit_higher_mw16_score_coverage.py)

homogeneous reconstruction gives 10,240 distinct rational-isomorphism
classes, with 10,240 distinct j-invariants, all in the declared higher bands.
In every band/family, the worst admitted scalar candidate is strictly ahead
of both signed slices' last retained candidates in the first three ordering
fields. Thus no address discarded at the 4,096-per-slice boundary could have
preceded the selected scalar prefix. This closes retention loss for the
initial score through 32749 in these twenty slices; it does not certify the
global leaders through 65521 or predict ranks. The exact certificate is
[`narrow_higher_mw16_score_coverage_v1.json`](../../artifacts/generated-results/elliptic-curves/narrow_higher_mw16_score_coverage_v1.json).

The point protocol uses only sixteen transported generic sections, 43
