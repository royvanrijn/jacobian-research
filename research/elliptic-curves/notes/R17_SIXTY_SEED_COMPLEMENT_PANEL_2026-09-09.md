## Result

The prospectively frozen 60-fibre panel completed on 9 September 2026. All
generic inputs and final packets verified, and the selected equations are
pairwise nonisomorphic over Q. It found 48 first seeds, 26 on the first call.
The 1,349 seed calls and 4,741 complementary calls were uncensored; saved-cloud
replay added 85 directions beyond the M17 inputs and at most 100 complementary
calls per seeded fibre added 153 more.

The final certified subgroup lower bounds are 12×17, 7×18, 4×19, 5×20, 5×21,
4×22, 6×23, 9×24, 4×25, 3×26, and 1×27. The 103b2 fibre at 877/781 reached
27, with its last gain at call 67. Six other fibres gained at call 75 or later.

## Boundary

This is a descriptive result for the frozen selected panel. It proves no exact
rank, rank-at-least-28 result, causal height effect, conductor improvement,
public novelty, or rank upper bound. Exhausted budgets and late gains do not
authorize extension.

The terminal controller cannot be resumed. A successor needs a new frozen
roster, budget and replay contract; the [current programme](../README.md)
selects whether such work is ever in scope.
