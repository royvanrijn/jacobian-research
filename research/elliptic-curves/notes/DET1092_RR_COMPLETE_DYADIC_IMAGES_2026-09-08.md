## Exact transport to a small local algebra

**Verified application.** Apply the same maximal integral monic homothety
as in the earlier local theorem, obtaining `f`. For this calculation use
the fixed precision `N=512` and replace coefficients by their least
integer residues modulo `2^N`, obtaining `g`. A retained small-prime
irreducibility witness certifies that `Q[x]/g` is a field.

Let `D=v2(disc(f))`. The replay checks `N>2D` and the full coefficient
congruences. Strong Hensel, as proved in the earlier theorem, gives a
Galois-equivariant matching of integral roots `alpha` of `f` and `theta`
of `g` with `v2(alpha-theta)>=N-D`.

**New deduction and verified application: squareclass transport.**
Normalize each evaluation polynomial separately by a rational power of2
to have integral coefficients, and retain its reduction modulo `2^N`.
Do this for every generic `gj` and for the basepoint anchor. If `A` is
the valuation of the norm of a reduced evaluation at `theta`, then every
component evaluation has valuation at most `A`. The checker verifies

\[
N-D>A+2.
\]

Integral-polynomial evaluation is 2-adically Lipschitz. Therefore the
ratio between the matched evaluations is in `1+8 O_L` in each component
field `L`, and is a square by strong Hensel. The rational powers of2
introduced by normalization disappear in the fake scalar quotient.
This proves transport of the actual inherited fake Kummer classes, not
just transport of the branch Galois set. All 17 numerator checks and the
anchor check are retained per case; their norm valuations are at most72.

**Limitation.** This is a local algebra and squareclass correspondence.
The proxy is not a replacement for the original global sextic field,
Jacobian, global unit group, class group, or Selmer group. No global
arithmetic conclusion is transferred from the proxy.

## Construction and independent verification use different arithmetic

**Established implementation documentation.** PARI's
[local-order option](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/dochtml/html-stable/General_number_fields.html#nfbasis)
permits an order maximal only at an explicit set of primes. The constructor
calls `nfinit([g,[2]])`, then reuses the repository's existing finite local
unit/squareclass routine. It does not call a global class-group or global
unit routine or request full discriminant factorization. The routine's
cubic-specific point-dimension convenience property is deliberately not
used for these sextic algebras.

**Verified application.** The independent checker calls none of PARI's
number-field, ideal-group or discrete-log routines. It treats the proposed
basis as untrusted and verifies the following finite certificates.

1. **2-maximal order.** Exact multiplication in `Q[x]/g` proves that the
   proposed rank-six lattice is an order containing `Z2[x]`. Its nilradical
   modulo2 is the kernel of the third iterate of Frobenius. Put
   `I=rad(2O)`. Every candidate multiplier outside `O` has the form `y/2`
   with `y mod 2O` nonzero. The checker tests all64 residue vectors and
   finds no such multiplier satisfying `(y/2)I subset I`.
   The radical multiplier criterion proves 2-maximality; see
   [Stevenhagen, Proposition9.3](https://math.leidenuniv.nl/reports/files/2006-12.pdf).
2. **Separate local valuations.** All idempotents modulo2 are found by
   solving `z^2=z`. Primitive idempotents identify the local factors and
   residue degrees. Small lifted candidates give a uniformizer at one
   factor and units at the others. Exact norm valuations certify this:
   nonzero valuation at that factor and norm valuation equal to its
   residue degree force valuation exactly1 there and zero elsewhere.
3. **Certified unit normalization.** Local arithmetic suggests
   `a=prod(pi_j^v_j)*u`. If `A=v2(Norm(a))`, the checker verifies the
   identity modulo `2^(A+3)` in the exact multiplication table, and checks
   that `u` is a unit. Multiplication by `prod(pi_j^v_j)` has integral
   matrix and determinant valuation `A`; its inverse has denominators
   dividing `2^A` locally. Thus the certificate proves the unit part
   modulo8 independently of the p-adic approximation used to find it.
4. **Complete unit-square test.** Enumerate the4096 elements of `O/4O`,
   keeping units and squaring modulo8. This is complete because adding
   `4O` does not change a square modulo8. A local unit is a square exactly
   when its residue is in this set: `1+8O` consists of squares, and
   2-maximality ensures that the square root of an integral unit is again
   an integral unit. No global unit basis is involved.
5. **Independence modulo rational scalars.** For each nonzero listed
   generator combination and each product of `-1,2,5`, the checker proves
   either an odd local valuation or a nonsquare unit residue modulo8.
   This gives the exact fake lower bound. It equals the previously proved
   upper dimension. Adding `D0` when its fake-kernel class is nonzero
   attains the full true dimension.

complete real images and553 previously certified good-prime pairs, the
