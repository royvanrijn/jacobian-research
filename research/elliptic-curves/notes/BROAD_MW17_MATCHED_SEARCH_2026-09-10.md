# Broad matched MW17 historical handoff

The sixteen-fibre smoke completed, but the prepared 768-fibre campaign was
never dispatched and is stopped. It has no current claim in
[MATH_STATUS.json](../../MATH_STATUS.json), and its controls, score arms, and
frozen address window do not authorize a new campaign.

across generic-rank-17 parents. Any finite null would have been operational
evidence only, never a rank upper bound or a selector theorem. Current work is
selected by the [elliptic-curve programme map](../README.md).

The full protocol, commands, and resource limits are preserved byte-for-byte
in the [elliptic archive](../../archive/elliptic-curves/notes/BROAD_MW17_MATCHED_SEARCH_2026-09-10.md.txt).

[`run_broad_mw17_search.py`](../cas/run_broad_mw17_search.py). Any future
comparison requires a fresh population, frozen protocol, and separate budget.
