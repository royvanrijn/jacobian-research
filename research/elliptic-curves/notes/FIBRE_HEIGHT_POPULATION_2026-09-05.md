## Preserved result

The two fixed selection arms covered 31 distinct curves and 468 completed
charts. Their coordinate changes substantially reduced model coefficient size
and retained worker time, yet both arms found zero new directions. This is a
bounded failure to show a selection advantage, not an equivalence result, a
rank bound, or evidence that smaller coordinates cannot help another family.
