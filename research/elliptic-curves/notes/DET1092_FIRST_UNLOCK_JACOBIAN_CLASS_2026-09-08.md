# The first302 unlock gives a non-generic rational Jacobian class

## Result and scope

**New deduction, independently verified application.** Let `C` be the
previously fixed retrospective RR member `C_(u0,0)` through the first saved
302 unlock, and let `P0=C intersect O` be its inherited rational basepoint.
Let `J=Jac(C)` and normalize restriction from the original K3 surface `X` by

\[
\Phi(L)=[L|_C-(D\cdot L)P_0],\qquad
H=\Phi(\operatorname{Pic}(X)).
\]

For the point `P*` mapping to the independently certified first elliptic gain,
put `xi=[P*-P0]`. Then

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}H=17,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}\langle H,\xi\rangle=18.}
\]

In particular `xi` is outside the **rational span of the full generic-image
subgroup**, not merely outside the subgroup generated by a few inherited
points. This proves `rank J(Q)>=18`, not an upper bound on the whole Jacobian.
It does not raise the elliptic-fibre rank beyond its already certified bound.

**Retrospective boundary.** The member parameter `u0` was obtained from the
known first successful chart coordinate. This is a genuine arithmetic bridge
for that witness, **not an exception-free selector for control RR members**.
No V4 control arithmetic outcome or full Selmer computation is claimed here.

**Subsequent specificity control.** A
[nine-case generic-point panel](DET1092_RR_GENERIC_POINT_SPECIFICITY_2026-09-08.md)
now proves the same Jacobian distinction for points known to lie in elliptic
MW17, including all eight V4 fibres. The present result stays valid, but its
Jacobian-class property alone cannot certify an extra elliptic direction.
