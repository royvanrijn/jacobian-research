# First302 seed: a complete intrinsic low-degree source obstruction

## Theorem and scope

**New deduction, independently verified application.** Fix the existing
norm-eight alternate elliptic fibration, its inherited origin, and its
smooth member `C_(z*)` through the historical first302 seed. Let `G_alt`
be the group generated by translations by arbitrary generic sections of
this alternate fibration and its elliptic inversion.

Neither the first seed nor its carrier conjugate lies on

\[
 \boxed{g(S_v),\quad g\in G_{\rm alt},\quad
               v\in M_{17},\quad \deg(z|_{S_v})\le2.}       \tag{1}
\]

Here `S_v` is an **original generic section curve**, not an arbitrary
rational curve on the surface. Degree zero means vertical. The generic
MW17 lattice is the full integral original section lattice, so no support
or coordinate-size cutoff is implicit in (1).

The complete source set has:

|1|130|Alternate sections specialize inside the inherited Picard image|
|2|1,168|No rational intersection:1,166 modular proofs and2 exact quadratic proofs|

This excludes **1,308 complete intrinsic sources and their entire indicated
translation/inversion orbits**. It is not merely a larger signed-basis
sample. Within this route, an original source capable of reaching either
marked point must have alternate degree at least3. Attainment at degree3
is not asserted.

**Calibration boundary.** The source set is selected entirely from generic

## 2. An exact ellipsoid, independent of the displayed basis

Use one-based section indices in this note. The alternate fibre is

\[
 D=2O+4F+\phi(w),\qquad w=e_{15}-e_{16},\quad\|w\|^2=8.
\]

**Established height/intersection formula, applied here.** For an original
section `S_v=O+(||v||^2/2)F+phi(v)`,

\[
 d(v)=D\cdot S_v=\|v\|^2-\langle w,v\rangle
       =\frac{\|2v-w\|^2-8}{4}.                            \tag{2}
\]

See [Schuett--Shioda, *Elliptic Surfaces*, §§11.4--11.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.0298)
for the underlying Shioda map and height pairing. Thus all desired sources
are exactly

\[
 2v-w\equiv-w\pmod{2M},\qquad\|2v-w\|^2\le16.            \tag{3}
\]

**Verified complete enumeration.** One exact rational LDL recursion finds
the10,130,1168 sources of degrees0,1,2. It visits21,035 nodes, below the
frozen100,000-node and4,096-source caps. It uses a previously saved
unimodular reduction only for efficiency; no numerical bound or CVP oracle
certifies completeness.

The independent checker instead enumerates integer points in the affine
ellipsoid `||v-w/2||^2<=4`, with exact rational coordinate intervals. It
returns the identical full source set, also in21,035 nodes. Every source
word and its degree are retained.

Under a unimodular basis change, the Gram, `v` and `w` transform together,
preserving (2) and (3) bijectively. The set, count and final obstruction
are therefore basis invariant. This is an algebraic proof, not a claim

## 3. Why the modular source certificates lift

For a nonvertical source with short coordinates `X_v(t),Y_v(t)`, the
already established alternate parameter is

\[
 z_v(t)=\frac{h(t)(Y_v(t)+c_y(t))/(X_v(t)-c_x(t))
                    +\mathrm{shift}(t)}{h(t)^2}.           \tag{4}
\]

Its degree over `Q` is the intrinsic value (2), because it is the
restriction of the alternate fibration to that section curve.

**New lifting lemma.** Suppose `z(t) in Q_p(t)` has degree at most `d`,
is integral for the Gauss valuation, and its residue is a rational map
of degree exactly `d`. Then it has good reduction as a degree-`d` map
`P1 -> P1`.

To prove this, choose a coprime degree-at-most-`d` numerator/denominator
pair over `Q_p` and normalize their joint coefficient content to a unit.
Gauss integrality forces the denominator to have a unit coefficient too.
If reduction introduced a common projective zero, or lost the full
homogeneous degree, cancellation would make the reduced map's degree
less than `d`. Exact preservation of degree excludes both possibilities.
Thus the homogeneous pair defines a morphism over `Z_p`.

Consequently, for an integral target `z*`, absence of a projective
`F_p`-preimage excludes a `Q_p`-preimage and hence a rational source point.
In degree2, a nonzero leading coefficient and nonsquare nonzero
discriminant give precisely such a certificate. A degree drop, a rational
root at infinity or a failed Gauss chart is not an exclusion.

**Verified application.** The short elliptic equation has good reduction
over the Gauss residue field `F_p(t)` at each used prime. Reduction of the
generic group word gives its actual reduced section. Whenever (4)'s
ingredients are integral and its denominator is a Gauss unit, the formula
has the required residue. The degree-two check then supplies the lemma's
remaining hypothesis. This does not assume that the entire K3 reduction
has24 irreducible fibres; source-map degree drops are explicitly retained.

The independent checker clears denominators in primitive projective
polynomial triples to reduce the generic points. It uses a manual,
reverse-order group law over `F_p(t)`, not the producer's valuation routine
or Sage elliptic point additions. It rechecks every recorded map, every
failure status and every discriminant certificate.

## 5. Passing from source curves to infinite orbits

**New deduction using the completed incidence and Picard inputs.**
Every element of `G_alt` preserves `z` and is an automorphism on the
minimal elliptic surface. Its inverse takes a rational point on a
translated source, lying on `C_(z*)`, back to a rational source point on
the same carrier.

For degree0 sources, the rational curve is vertical and cannot be a
component of the smooth irreducible genus-one carrier. For degree2
sources, there is no rational source point by the certificates above.
For degree1 sources, the source is an alternate section. Its point lies
in `H_(z*)`; arbitrary generic translations and inversion keep its class
inside that rational span. The first seed and its conjugate are outside
the span. These three cases prove (1).

The result is about the two specified marked points. Adding a generic
point in the **original** fibration generally changes the carrier label;
other representatives of the same original residual direction are not
excluded. Nor are alternating moves between fibrations, original sources
of degree above2, or rational curves which are not original section curves.

**Constructive boundary.** This closes the complete low-degree original
section-source route, not the positive seed problem. The next successful
construction must escape at least one hypothesis of (1). The entire
40,917-orbit original smooth-bisection atlas remains untested; no such
campaign was authorized or launched here. No conclusion about the other
thirteen exceptional directions or amplification is made.
