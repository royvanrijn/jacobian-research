# Early Elkies--K3 experiment notes

These eighteen notes are pre-atlas discovery instructions or superseded branch
audits.  They were unreferenced from the active tree and had no role as a
canonical source, software lock, or status consumer when archived on
2026-09-02.

The tranche covers:

- initial q90/q15/MW4/MW3 neighbour nodes and direct section attacks;
- CM orders, Heegner vectors, Gross lattices, and Atkin--Lehner probes;
- the large modular three-hub reconstruction attempt; and
- the all-IV rank-jump chart rejected by the exact parity obstruction; and
- the draft q8/orbit376 Riemann--Roch branch audit whose listed blockers were
  resolved by the later certified q8-to-q12 route.

The calculations remain useful as provenance, but their “next step” language
belongs to the historical snapshot and must not be read as the current
programme.  See [`MANIFEST.tsv`](MANIFEST.tsv) for original paths and hashes.
