To promote a paper, finish the stated review tasks, freeze and check its PDF/source bundle, and verify its authorized deposit. Its current edition then lives under [results](../results/), with exact deposited files and metadata in `published/`. Later revisions stay at the released paper's top level. The bivariate GVC and GMC revision bundles have been merged into those current homes.

The marker-reduction paper includes **A Specialization Obstruction in Gaussian Marker Generation** as a major section. The one-base fibre paper contains the constant-fibre theory removed from the boundary paper.
