# AI provenance

ChatGPT (OpenAI) assisted with mathematical arguments, literature checks,
exposition, proof review and verification code.

Roy van Rijn directed the research. The AI-assisted work included developing
and checking the quintic exclusion and the trace-norm ordinarity argument,
reconstructing the proper-volume proof, preparing the mathematical
exposition, and implementing and replaying exact verification scripts.
The submission preparation used separate internal AI passes on the
arithmetic literature, the proper-volume argument, the finite arithmetic,
and the rendered manuscript.

The [verification record](verification.json) identifies the edition used
for PDF-only internal review and records the resulting revisions. The
mathematical and computational checks are internal. External specialist
review and the contributor's submission attestations are recorded
separately from these checks.
