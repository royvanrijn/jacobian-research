\begin{abstract}
We fix an explicit polynomial map \(\Phi:\A^3_\Q\to\A^3_\Q\) with
Jacobian determinant one and geometric degree five whose fibers on a
rational target line are the root schemes of
\((X^3-a)(X^2+X+1)\), for \(a\ne0,1\). We classify every Hasse-failing
rational parameter on this line. A nonzero noncube \(a\) is admissible
exactly when \(v_p(a)\) is divisible by three for every prime
\(p\equiv2\pmod3\), and \(a\) is a cube in \(\Q_3\).
In the projective height induced by the actual target embedding, the number
of these targets of height at most \(B\) is asymptotic to
\[
 \frac{11}{288\sqrt3\,\pi^2}
 \prod_{p\equiv2\ (3)}\frac{1+p^{-3}}{1-p^{-3}}
 \frac{B^2}{\log B}.
\]
The proof treats coprimality, the coupled congruence at \(3\), and the
projective content at \(2\) and \(3\), using one-variable
Selberg--Delange estimates. The sufficient integer subfamily has order
\(B/\sqrt{\log B}\). The count is of targets on one line, rather than
distinct fiber algebras or all targets of the map. Finally, the classical
degree obstruction for intersective polynomials shows that geometric
degree five is minimal.
\end{abstract}
