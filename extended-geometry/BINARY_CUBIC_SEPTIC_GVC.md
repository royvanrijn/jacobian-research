# Binary septic GVC for every cubic leading symbol

## 1. Theorem

Work over a characteristic-zero field.  Let
\[
 \Lambda=\Lambda _3+\Lambda _4+\cdots
\]
be a constant-coefficient operator in two variables whose lowest nonzero
positive homogeneous part has order three, and let \(\deg P=7\).

> **Theorem 1.1 (complete cubic-leading septic row).**
> If
> \[
>  \Lambda^m(P^m)=0\qquad(m\geq1),
> \tag{1.1}
> \]
> then, for every fixed polynomial \(Q\),
> \[
>  \Lambda^m(QP^m)=0\qquad(m\gg0).
> \tag{1.2}
> \]

Arbitrary lower pieces of \(P\), arbitrary higher operator jets, and all
three root partitions of the binary cubic are included.  Combined with the
[quadratic-leading septic theorem](BINARY_QUADRATIC_SEPTIC_GVC.md), this gave
the intermediate degree-seven narrowing
\[
 \boxed{
 \text{a binary degree-seven counterexample must have lowest order }
 r\in\{4,5,6\}.}
\tag{1.3}
\]
The later high-order septic theorem closes degree seven, and the
[Hall-envelope theorem](BINARY_GVC_ENVELOPE_CLOSURE.md) proves unrestricted
binary GVC.  This row remains an independent exact regression.

The calculation below is a finite all-scale Newton argument.  Bounded
moments are used only to determine exact face radicals.  Every leaf reaches
an unequal common threshold, where the all-order weight-defect theorem
proves (1.2).

## 2. Hall locus and the two-wing normal form

After scalar extension, factor \(\Lambda _3\) and \(P_7\).  If a marked
root of \(\Lambda _3\) has multiplicity \(e\), Hall failure for the three
derivative copies and seven polynomial factors is
\[
 7-c<e,
 \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad c\geq8-e,
 \tag{2.1}
\]
where \(c\) polynomial factors annihilate the marked direction.  A subset
containing two nonparallel derivative directions sees every polynomial
factor.  Hence the complete leading Hall locus is
\[
 M_e=X^eY^{3-e},
 \qquad
 P_7=y^{8-e}C_{e-1}(x,y),
 \qquad 1\leq e\leq3.
 \tag{2.2}
\]

Shears preserving the marked monomial give the following projective charts.
Terms printed after a plus sign are strict for every weight used below.
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
e&M_e&P_7\\ \hline
3&X^3&x^2y^5+\gamma y^7,\quad xy^6,\quad y^7,\\
2&X^2Y+\alpha X^3&xy^6,\quad y^7,\\
1&XY^2+\alpha X^2Y+\beta X^3&y^7.
\end{array}
\tag{2.3}
\]

Formal division by \(M_e\) removes its multiples.  A normalized operator
monomial of order \(3+h\), with \(h\geq1\), can remain only in the two
wings
\[
 X^aY^{3+h-a},
 \qquad a<e\quad\text{or}\quad a>e+h.
 \tag{2.4}
\]
At order three the marked monomial has \(a=e\), and its homogeneous
cofactor has \(a>e\).  The divided unit and its inverse act locally
finitely on polynomials, so they preserve both (1.1) and (1.2).

The checker exhausts Hall matching and constructs every face directly from
(2.4); no cutoff on the original higher jets is assumed.

## 3. The finite Newton scan

Put \(w_s(x)=w_s(X)=s\) and \(w_s(y)=w_s(Y)=1\), where \(s>1\) is rational.
Suppose the current operator minimum and polynomial maximum contain the
marked monomials
\[
 U=X^aY^b,
 \qquad V=x^iy^j.
 \tag{3.1}
\]
As \(s\) increases, only an operator term with smaller \(X\)-exponent can
cross below \(U\), and only a polynomial term with larger \(x\)-exponent
can cross above \(V\).  Their crossing values are respectively
\[
 s=\frac{b'-b}{a-a'},
 \qquad
 s=\frac{j-j'}{i'-i}.
 \tag{3.2}
\]
Formula (2.4) bounds \(a'<a\), and at a bounded crossing value also bounds
\(b'\).  The polynomial support has degree at most seven.  Thus every scan
interval contains finitely many explicitly enumerable crossings.

At a crossing let \(A_s\) be the complete operator equality face and
\(B_s\) the complete polynomial equality face.  The largest output-weight
part of (1.1) is
\[
 A_s(\partial)^m(B_s^m)=0\qquad(m\geq1).
 \tag{3.3}
\]
Consequently any finite collection of moment equations from (3.3) gives a
necessary face ideal.  All radicals below are exact radicals over
\(\mathbb Q\) of the coefficients from moments \(1\) through \(10\).

If the radical is the face-coordinate origin, every newly tied coefficient
vanishes and the scan advances.  A nontrivial component changes the marked
minimum or maximum and starts one of the child intervals in Section 5.  At
an endpoint \(s=s_*\),
\[
 w_{s_*}(U)=w_{s_*}(V)=W,
 \quad
 w_{s_*}(\operatorname {Supp}\Lambda)\geq W,
 \quad
 w_{s_*}(\operatorname {Supp}P)\leq W.
 \tag{3.4}
\]
The unequal-weight terminal-face theorem then gives a linear coordinate
deficit on the equality faces.  Every strict selection consumes positive
integral weight defect, so a fixed multiplier \(Q\) permits only
\(O_Q(1)\) strict selections.  They cannot repair the linear deficit.
This is the all-order step from a terminal scan to (1.2).

## 4. Initial face radicals

Write \(\mathfrak m\) for the ideal generated by every nonmarked face
coefficient.  The complete crossing ledgers before the first common
threshold are as follows.  A row marked \(\mathfrak m\) has origin radical
at every listed slope except the slopes explicitly treated below.

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|l}
e&(U,V)&s_*&\text{crossings in }(1,s_*)\\ \hline
3&(X^3,x^2y^5)&5&
 \frac54,\frac43,\frac32,\frac53,2,\frac73,\frac52,\frac83,
 3,\frac{10}3,\frac72,\frac{11}3,4,\frac{13}3,\frac92,\frac{14}3\\
3&(X^3,xy^6)&3&
 \frac65,\frac54,\frac43,\frac32,\frac53,2,\frac73,\frac52,\frac83\\
3&(X^3,y^7)&\frac73&
 \frac76,\frac65,\frac54,\frac43,\frac75,\frac32,\frac53,\frac74,2\\
2&(X^2Y,xy^6)&5&
 \frac65,\frac54,\frac43,\frac32,\frac53,2,\frac52,3,\frac72,4,\frac92\\
2&(X^2Y,y^7)&3&
 \frac76,\frac65,\frac54,\frac43,\frac75,\frac32,\frac53,\frac74,2,

\end{array}
\tag{4.1}
\]

There are exactly four exceptional radicals.

### 4.1 Triple root over \(xy^6\)

At slope two the complete face is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 A&=X^3+aX^2Y^2+bXY^4+cY^6,\\
 B&=xy^6+p x^2y^4+q x^3y^2+r x^4.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.2}
\]
Its exact radical is
\[
 \sqrt I=(r,q,c,b,ap).
\tag{4.3}
\]
Thus the central component continues toward \(s_*=3\), while the two axes
pivot to \((X^2Y^2,xy^6)\) or \((X^3,x^2y^4)\).

### 4.2 Triple root over \(y^7\)

At slope \(3/2\),
\[
 A=X^3+aXY^3,
 \qquad
 B=y^7+p x^2y^4+q x^4y,
\tag{4.4}
\]
and
\[
 \sqrt I=(q,ap)=(q,a)\cap(q,p).
\tag{4.5}
\]
The axes pivot to \((XY^3,y^7)\) and \((X^3,x^2y^4)\).

On the central component, slope two has
\[
\begin{aligned}
 A&=X^3+aX^2Y^2+bXY^4+cY^6,\\
 B&=y^7+p xy^5+q x^2y^3+r x^3y,
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.6}
\]
with
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sqrt I
  &=(r,c,bq,aq,bp)\\
  &=(r,c,q,p)\cap(r,c,q,b)\cap(r,c,b,a).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.7}
\]
These are exactly the operator component \(\langle a,b\rangle\), the mixed
component \(\langle a,p\rangle\), and the polynomial component
\(\langle p,q\rangle\).  Choosing the extreme nonzero term after the wall
gives the five marked child pairs
\[
 (X^2Y^2,y^7),\ (XY^4,y^7),\ (X^2Y^2,xy^5),\
 (X^3,xy^5),\ (X^3,x^2y^3).
\tag{4.8}
\]

### 4.3 Double root over \(y^7\)

At slope two,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 A&=X^2Y+aXY^3+bY^5,\\
 B&=y^7+p xy^5+q x^2y^3+r x^3y,
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.9}
\]
and
\[
 \sqrt I=(r,q,b,ap).
\tag{4.10}
\]
Its two axes pivot to \((XY^3,y^7)\) and \((X^2Y,xy^5)\).  There are no
exceptional radicals on the non-pure double-root chart or the simple-root
chart.

The following table gives every child scan.  Every face at every displayed

The shorter double-root operator row is literally the tail of the already
checked \((XY^3,y^7)\) row: its additional \(X^2Y\) cofactor is strict for
every \(s>2\).  The checker verifies equality of the exposed supports, not
just equality of their slope lists.

At each wall with several free coordinates, the monomial primary
decomposition in (4.3), (4.5), (4.7), or (4.10) says which extreme term is
nonzero immediately to the right.  Hence (5.1) exhausts all children and
all their overlaps.  No semigroup-return component remains inside this
degree-seven cubic row.

## 6. Completion

If an initial radical is the origin, its row in (4.1) reaches its displayed
common threshold.  If it is exceptional, its central component continues
the same row and every noncentral component occurs in (5.1).  Thus every
Hall chart reaches a positive unequal weight satisfying (3.4).

The terminal-face theorem and weight-defect argument of Section 3 absorb
all strict homogeneous cofactors, all lower polynomial terms, all higher
normalized operator jets, and the fixed multiplier \(Q\).  This proves
Theorem 1.1.

The important structural point is that degree seven introduces no adelic
packet collision on the cubic-leading row.  The only nonfree behavior is a
squarefree monomial face ideal at slopes \(3/2\) and \(2\); its components
are Newton pivots, and every pivot has an origin-radical corridor to one
common threshold.

## 7. Exact replay

Run

```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/verify_binary_cubic_septic_gvc.py
```

The command requires SymPy and Singular.  It checks the seven-factor Hall
classification, derives the two-wing crossing ledgers from exponent
equalities, verifies all initial and child face radicals exactly over
\(\mathbb Q\), audits the equality of the reused double-root branch
supports, and checks every terminal threshold.  The all-order implication
is the written Hall, local-division, and weight-defect proof above.
