# Repository counterexample scoreboard

## 1. Purpose

The numbers \(19,2,5,40,38\) are certified witness sizes.  The SIC entry is
now an exact endpoint, while the others are not presumed minimal.  This
ledger pairs every repository upper
witness with the strongest lower bound currently justified here and keeps
ambient dimension, rank, nilpotency index, and degree separate.  An upper
endpoint means “smallest currently certified in this repository,” not “best
known in the literature.”  Establishing a literature-wide record would
require a separate and current priority review, which this ledger does not
supply.

The machine-readable version is
[`minimal_counterexample_scoreboard.json`](../artifacts/generated-results/minimal_counterexample_scoreboard.json),
generated by
[`verify_minimal_counterexample_scoreboard.py`](../scripts/verify_minimal_counterexample_scoreboard.py).

## 2. Ambient dimensions

| problem | rigorous lower bound used here | repository upper bound | repository interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| arbitrary noninvertible Keller map | 2 | 3 | \(2\le n_{\rm Kel}\le3\) |
| cubic-homogeneous noninvertible Keller map | 5 | 19 | \(5\le n_{\rm cub}\le19\) |
| Drużkowski noninvertible Keller map | 6 | 451 | \(6\le n_{\rm Dru}\le451\) |
| GMC failure, real Gaussian dimension | 2 | 3 | \(2\le n_{\rm GMC}\le3\) |
| SIC failure, pair dimension | 2 | 2 | \(r_{\rm SIC}=2\) |
| unrestricted constant-coefficient GVC failure | 2 | 5 | \(2\le n_{\rm GVC}\le5\) |
| ordinary-Laplacian generalized VC failure | 2 | 40 | \(2\le n_{\Delta{\rm GVC}}\le40\) |
| homogeneous quartic HN-VC failure | 6 | 38 | \(6\le n_{\rm HN,4}\le38\) |

The reasons are deliberately asymmetric:

- one-variable Keller maps are affine, while the foundational collision is
  three-dimensional;
- cubic-homogeneous Keller maps are known invertible through dimension four;
- every matrix of size at most five has rank or corank below three, so
  Drużkowski's rank-or-corank theorem gives the Drużkowski lower endpoint;
- GMC(1) is proved and Long gives an explicit GMC(3) counterexample;
- the one-pair Image Conjecture and GVC(1) are proved; the full-rank
  bidegree-\((4,4)\) formula fails SIC(2), while the Dvorsky--Long formula
  separately fails unrestricted GVC(5);
- for the ordinary one-variable Laplacian, the hypothesis at \(m=1\) gives
  \(P''=0\); then
  \(\partial^{2m}(QP^m)=0\) once \(m>\deg Q\);
- the symmetric homogeneous nilpotent-Jacobian result through dimension five
  gives the quartic HN lower endpoint.

Thus GMC has the sharpest remaining ambient question:

\[
\boxed{n_{\rm GMC}\in\{2,3\}.}                     \tag{2.1}
\]

The companion [two-real frontier](TWO_REAL_GMC_FRONTIER.md) now proves that a
dimension-two witness, if it exists, must be at least cubic, use at least
three rotational weights, and be nonlinear in both circular coordinates.

## 3. Restricted rank and index

For noninjective cubic-homogeneous Keller maps \(X+H\), and their homogeneous
quartic HN lifts, the current exact intervals are

| invariant | lower | upper | upper witness |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| generic rank \(\operatorname{rank}JH\) | 3 | 17 | 24-variable circuit quotient |
| polynomial nilpotency index of \(JH\) | 3 | 18 | 22-variable circuit quotient |
| generic rank of a cotangent-lift quartic Hessian | 6 | 37 | 44-variable rank-directed HN lift |
| generic rank of an unrestricted quartic HN Hessian | 3 | 37 | same upper witness, broader lower-bound class |

The rank lower bound is the rank-two cubic theorem.  The index lower bound is
elementary: if \((JH)^2=0\), then Euler's identity gives \(JH\,H=0\), so
\(X+H\) is the quasi-translation with inverse \(X-H\).  No full-class
index-three invertibility theorem is claimed; the known power-linear and
symmetric-Jacobian results are narrower.  A uniform inverse-degree-nine
strengthening is impossible:
van den Essen's dimension-five generic-rank-three automorphism has
\((JH)^3=0\) and inverse degree thirteen.

For a cotangent potential \(p(x,y)=y^TH(x)\), the exact block identity

\[
\operatorname{rank}\operatorname{Hess}p
=2\operatorname{rank}JH+\operatorname{rank}(K^TAK)
\]

raises the cotangent-specific lower bound to six.  The lower bound three
belongs to unrestricted quartic HN counterexamples and must not be used as a
cotangent-lift bound.

The upper endpoints are exact invariants of stored witnesses, not sampled
matrix ranks.  Their construction and independent replays are documented in
the [restricted-minima frontier](RESTRICTED_MINIMA_FRONTIER.md).  This exact
certification says nothing by itself about priority or optimality outside the
repository.

## 4. Three exact degree minima within the stated classes

Three degree questions are already closed even though their dimension questions
are not.

### General Keller maps

Wang's theorem proves invertibility in degree at most two.  The stored
cubic-homogeneous collision supplies degree three.  Therefore

\[
\boxed{\min\deg(\text{Keller counterexample})=3.}   \tag{4.1}
\]

### Gaussian moments

The quadratic-Gaussian theorem in
[TWO_REAL_GMC_FRONTIER.md](TWO_REAL_GMC_FRONTIER.md) proves GMC for every
polynomial of degree at most two in every dimension.  Long's explicit
four-real-variable counterexample is cubic.  Therefore

\[
\boxed{\min\deg(\text{GMC counterexample})=3.}      \tag{4.2}
\]

This is independent of the unresolved minimum Gaussian dimension.

### Genuinely ungraded Keller maps

The exact geometric-degree spectrum excludes noninvertible Keller maps of
geometric degree one and two.  The specialization `A=0`, `gamma=1` of the
[universal cubic testbed](../cancellation/UNIVERSAL_CUBIC_UNGRADED_TESTBED.md#8-an-explicit-genuinely-ungraded-cubic)
has geometric degree three and no algebraic-torus-equivariant polynomial
left--right representative.  Therefore

\[
\boxed{\min\operatorname{gdeg}(\text{genuinely ungraded Keller map})=3.}
\tag{4.3}
\]

This supersedes the former quartic upper endpoint.  It is an unstabilized
left--right statement; identity stabilization introduces tautological torus
actions on the added coordinates.

## 6. Frozen search frontier

The broad computational search is closed.  The following observations are
retained to explain the certified frontier, not as an active search queue.

1. Lowering the dimension-19 cubic-homogeneous endpoint requires another
   source-target coordinate cancellation, a cubic-rank reduction, or a
   different source.  The
   21-variable internal witness has exhausted its terminal linear
   compression routes.
2. Lowering rank below 17 or index below 18 requires changing the
   degree-lowering circuit before homogenization.
3. Lowering the collision-derived identity-output witness below SIC(20)
   requires more than another linear restriction: its correction has full
   output span.  The independent full-rank two-pair witness gives the sharp
   SIC pair dimension two, while the Dvorsky--Long witness gives
   unrestricted GVC(5); neither changes the ordinary-Laplacian endpoint 40.
4. Lowering the homogeneous HN dimension from 38 requires a smaller
   homogeneous cubic source or a different symmetric lift.
5. The rank-directed double identity slice has nonhomogeneous HN rank 34,
   cotangent kernel excess zero, and no constant Hessian-kernel direction.
   Further improvement therefore requires more than another constant linear
   quotient of that potential.

No failed beam search changes a lower endpoint.  A new upper endpoint is
promoted only after an exact characteristic-zero generator, collision replay,
and invariant certificate.  Resume this program only for a theorem-directed
classification—preferably cubic-homogeneous dimension five—or a specifically
motivated construction.
