# One-active-direction obstruction for the moving rank-three `[4]` branch

## Status

This note closes an infinite nonhomogeneous family inside the last generic-rank
`[4]` relative-nilpotent stratum.

> **Theorem HC4RSD68 — one-active moving kernel obstruction.**
> Let
> \[
> A(x,y,z,w)=xy+F(x)z+R(x,w)
> \]
> and suppose \(T=\operatorname{Hess}A\) has generic rank three.  Let
> \(S=\operatorname{Hess}\psi\) be polynomial with
> \(\det S\in K^*\), and assume
> \[
> \det(S+sT)=\det S
> \qquad\text{for all }s.
> \]
> If the relative nilpotent has generic Jordan type `[4]`, then
> \(F''=0\).  Equivalently, the top kernel line of \(T\) is constant.
>
> Hence every packet in this family reduces to the fixed-kernel theorem
> `HC4RSD65`, and therefore to the previously closed `HC2/JC2` endpoints.

The point is that the proof uses no degree bound and no coefficient ansatz for
\(\psi\).

## 1. Kernel and adapted orbit coordinate

The Hessian is

\[
T=
\begin{pmatrix}
 zF''+R_{xx}&1&F'&R_{xw}\\
 1&0&0&0\\
 F'&0&0&0\\
 R_{xw}&0&0&R_{ww}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Generic rank three forces \(R_{ww}\ne0\).  Its kernel is generated by

\[
k=(0,-F'(x),1,0),
\qquad
D=k\cdot\nabla=\partial_z-F'(x)\partial_y.
\]

The invariant

\[
u=y+F'(x)z
\]

satisfies \(Du=0\), while \(Dz=1\).

The coefficient of \(s^3\) in \(\det(S+sT)\) is, up to the nonzero rank-three
minor of \(T\), exactly \(k^{\mathsf T}Sk=D^2\psi\).  Hence

\[
D^2\psi=0.
\]

Therefore, without any degree restriction,

\[
\psi=zP(x,u,w)+Q(x,u,w).                                      \tag{1.1}
\]

## 2. The `s^2` coefficient synchronizes the affine coefficient

Substitute (1.1) and use the coordinates \((x,u,z,w)\).  Exact expansion gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
[s^2]\det(S+sT)
={}&3zF''R_{ww}P_u^2
 +2F''R_{ww}P_uQ_u
 -R_{ww}R_{xx}P_u^2+R_{xw}^2P_u^2\\
&-2R_{xw}P_uP_w+2R_{ww}P_uP_x+P_w^2.             \tag{2.1}
\end{aligned}
\]

If the kernel moves, then \(F''\ne0\).  Since also \(R_{ww}\ne0\), the
coefficient of \(z\) in (2.1) forces

\[
P_u=0.
\]

Equation (2.1) then reduces to \(P_w^2=0\).  Thus

\[
P=P(x).                                                   \tag{2.2}
\]

So already at the next pencil coefficient, every allowed companion Hessian is
forced to synchronize with the active variable of the moving kernel.

## 3. The `s` coefficient contradicts unimodularity

Write \(H(x)=P(x)\).  Exact expansion now gives

\[
[s]\det(S+sT)
=-\bigl(F''Q_u+H'\bigr)^2Q_{uu}R_{ww},                \tag{3.1}
\]

while

\[
\det S
=-\bigl(F''Q_u+H'\bigr)^2
 \bigl(Q_{uu}Q_{ww}-Q_{uw}^2\bigr).                  \tag{3.2}
\]

Because \(R_{ww}\ne0\), constancy of the pencil and nonzero \(\det S\) imply
from (3.1)

\[
Q_{uu}=0.                                               \tag{3.3}
\]

Then (3.2) becomes

\[
\det S=\bigl(F''Q_u+H'\bigr)^2Q_{uw}^2\in K^*.
\]

Since the polynomial ring is a domain, both factors are units:

\[
Q_{uw}\in K^*,
\qquad
F''Q_u+H'\in K^*.                                      \tag{3.4}
\]

But (3.3) says

\[
Q=uL(x,w)+M(x,w),
\]

and \(Q_{uw}=L_w\in K^*\) forces

\[
L(x,w)=cw+\ell(x),\qquad c\in K^*.
\]

Hence

\[
F''Q_u+H'=cF''w+F''\ell(x)+H'(x).
\]

This can be a unit only if \(F''=0\), contradicting genuine movement of the
kernel.

Thus the moving case is impossible and HC4RSD68 follows.

## 4. Interpretation

The explicit model

\[
A=xy+\frac{x^2}{2}z+\frac{w^2}{2}
\]

was only the first probe.  The proof above closes the entire family

\[
A=xy+F(x)z+R(x,w)
\]

at once.  The obstruction is therefore not tied to the quadratic example:
it is the incompatibility between

1. a moving passive kernel line \(\partial_z-F'\partial_y\),
2. Hessian-integrability of the companion metric, and
3. polynomial unimodularity of \(S\).

The next global target is the full degenerate-cotangent form

\[
A=yP(x,w)+zQ(x,w)+R(x,w),\qquad J(P,Q)=0.
\]

By the polynomial Lüroth/generative-polynomial theorem, algebraically
dependent \(P,Q\) lie in one polynomial subalgebra \(K[h]\).  Thus the
projective top-kernel direction is in fact a one-parameter function of a
single polynomial \(h(x,w)\).  HC4RSD68 proves the obstruction when that
parameter can be chosen affine-linear.  The remaining problem is to show that
the HC4 companion Hessian forces the generative polynomial \(h\) itself to be
a coordinate/affine-linear, or else derive the same contradiction invariantly
without changing the active coordinates.
