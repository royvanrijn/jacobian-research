# The primitive balanced-degree spectrum in the two-pair Image problem

Over every characteristic-zero field, the primitive, absolutely nonpower
balanced Image counterexamples in two variable pairs occur precisely in
degrees \(4,6,7,8,\ldots\). Every pure balanced phase through degree three
is safe, and every pure radially primitive balanced quintic is safe. One
explicit integer family gives all the failure degrees with the same
marker \(Z=\xi_1z_2\), which detects every positive power.

The paper also proves a density-one ordinary-reduction theorem after
finite extension. It applies to abelian varieties with Rosati-compatible
rank-two multiplication by a CM field and at most two balanced conjugate
embedding pairs. The acting field can sit inside a larger geometric
endomorphism algebra. For the primitive factors of cyclic covers of
orders \(7,9,11,15\), this criterion gives nonzero Frobenius coefficients
that detect nonvanishing polynomial moments in the quintic proof.
