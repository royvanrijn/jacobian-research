*From Explicit Quotient Constructions to a General Classification*

The PDF and `paper.tex` are the exact files downloaded from Zenodo. Their MD5 checksums match the public record; the manifest also records SHA-256. The deposited source is named `paper.tex`; the identical current source is named `main.tex` in the [current repository edition](../).

The paper classifies the reductive homogeneous spaces whose normalized Reynolds integration kernel is a Mathieu–Zhao subspace and constructs fixed-multiplier counterexamples when the property fails.

The [verification record](../verification.json), [source audit](../source_audit.json) and [exact checker](../verify.py) describe the internal review and finite controls supporting this edition. Those supporting files were not deposited on Zenodo. The finite checks do not certify the all-order proofs or classification.
