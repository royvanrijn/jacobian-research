# Sparse Minimality of Gaussian-Moments Counterexamples in Dimension Three

An exact classification excludes counterexamples with both degree at most four and at most four monomials in a Gaussian Witt frame.

## Verification

Exhaustive exact computation of 59,535 supports, followed by all-order arguments for the surviving families. Only degree at most four AND at most four monomials in a Gaussian Witt frame are excluded. This does not rule out denser cubics or higher-degree four-term examples.
