\section{The remaining balanced exception question}\label{sec:balanced}
The complete Eisenstein finite scan also tests
$n/3\le a,b\le2n/3$ in the ordinary family $\AA+\AA$.
For $2\le n<10^8$, $n\equiv2\pmod6$, its only failures are $8$ and $44$.
This suggests the exact classification
\[
 \{n\ge2:\exists a,b\in\AA,\ n=a+b,\ n/3\le a,b\le2n/3\}
       =\{n>0:n\equiv2\pmod6\}\setminus\{8,44\},
\]
where the left side is interpreted target by target.
The finite scan alone does not prove that these are the only failures.
Even an eventual balanced theorem needs an effective onset and a
complete certificate meeting that onset to settle this exact question.
