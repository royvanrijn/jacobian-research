\section{Consequences, verification, provenance, and literature boundary}

Theorem~\ref{thm:main} is a realization theorem, not merely a source of
counterexamples.  Some immediate applications are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every squarefree polynomial of degree at least three is, after one
      translation of its variable, the exact inverse polynomial of a full
      Jacobian-one Keller fiber.
\item A degree-$N$ input has an explicit realization in $\A^3$ of coordinate
      degree at most $6N+2$.
\item Every intersective polynomial of degree at least three transfers to a
      Hasse-failing Keller fiber without stabilization or auxiliary factors.
\item Any real signature, splitting field, or collection of local
      factorization patterns already carried by a finite \'etale algebra is
      carried by the corresponding Keller fiber, compatibly with extension of
      the ground field.
\item Connected fibers require no additional geometric argument: start with
      an irreducible polynomial.
\end{enumerate}

The fourth item is deliberately a transfer statement.  It does not solve an
inverse Galois problem or assert that arbitrary independently prescribed
local algebras globalize; it says that every arithmetic realization which
already exists is preserved exactly by the Keller construction.

The realization is not unique.  Separate boundary calculations in the
accompanying research repository show that the same finite \'etale algebra
can occur in stably inequivalent weighted, cancellation, and quadratic-gauge
Keller maps.  That stable-classification problem is logically independent of
the present theorem and is best treated in a sequel, tentatively titled
\emph{One Fiber, Many Keller Maps}.

\paragraph{Literature boundary.}
A dated search completed on 24 July 2026 found no earlier statement that every
finite \'etale algebra of rank at least three is a full fiber of a polynomial
Keller map, nor the resulting rank classification.  This is necessarily a
qualified priority statement: bibliographic searches can miss unpublished
work, differently phrased results, and sources not indexed electronically.
The appropriate claim is therefore ``to our knowledge'', not an absolute
firstness assertion.  A reproducible search ledger is included with the
paper sources.

The closest results located have different scopes.  Gallagher gives explicit
Keller maps in every geometric degree and complete split rational fibers
\cite{Gallagher}; this realizes $K^N$, rather than an arbitrary finite
\'etale $K$-algebra.  Miranda--Neto proves radicality and maximal-ideal
decomposition properties for fibers already arising from a Keller map
\cite{Miranda}; this is not a prescribed-fiber realization theorem.
Lipton and Markakis study rational images and Hilbert irreducibility
\cite{LiptonMarkakis}, while Shaska studies graded Keller maps and arithmetic
thinness phenomena \cite{Shaska}.  None of these sources states the
finite-\'etale realization theorem proved here.

\paragraph{Exact verification.}
The companion checker
\[
 \texttt{scripts/verify\_finite\_etale\_keller\_fibers.py}
\]
independently constructs the quadratic-gauge map from translated seeds of
degrees three, four, and five.  For each seed it expands the three-variable
Jacobian exactly, checks the determinant-one normalization and coordinate-degree
bounds, recovers the prescribed inverse polynomial, and verifies both directions
of the source reconstruction in the quotient ring using an explicit inverse of
$E'$.  It separately audits the scaling, determinant, target, and fiber of the
integral degree-five map and the inverse equations in the fixed-map infinite
family.

These computations are exact regression certificates, not a proof by testing
finitely many degrees.  The universal map identities are the coefficientwise
calculation in Proposition~\ref{prop:gauge} and
Subsection~\ref{subsec:gauge-identities}; the universal degree estimate is the
term-by-term argument in Corollary~\ref{cor:effective-gauge}.

\paragraph{Lean formalization.}
The repository contains a Lean project at
\[
 \texttt{formal/finite-etale-keller}.
\]
It contains no \texttt{sorry} and introduces no project-specific axioms.  The
formal development proves the universal marked-line identities; the mutually
inverse source/chart reconstruction over arbitrary commutative rings; the
equivalence between roots and the abstract full source-fiber datum; naturality
under every morphism of commutative test algebras; and representation of that
functor by $K[S]/(E)$.

For a squarefree polynomial $P$ of degree at least three, Lean also proves that
there exists an admissible parameter $a$ at which both the linear and cubic
Taylor coefficients are nonzero.  It instantiates the universal datum with
\[
 E(S)=P(a+S),
\]
proves the derivative factorization using the third Hasse derivative, and
composes with the explicit algebra equivalence
\[
 K[S]/(P(a+S))\simeq K[T]/(P(T)).
\]
The final theorem, \texttt{automaticFiberRepresentingEquiv\_natural}, takes
only a squarefree $P$ and the hypothesis $\deg P\geq3$.  For every commutative
test $K$-algebra it gives a natural represented-fiber equivalence directly
from $K[T]/(P)$; the translation parameter and its two nonvanishing witnesses
are chosen internally.  Its axiom report contains only Lean's standard
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.

This formal certificate deliberately has a precise boundary.  The polynomial
identities proved in Proposition~\ref{prop:gauge} identify the abstract datum
with the displayed map's fiber functor $\mathcal F_{\pi,b,c}$, but the complete
all-degree map has not yet been assembled as one multivariate Lean object.
Consequently the universal determinant calculation and the $6N+2$ coordinate-
degree bound remain coefficientwise paper proofs, backed by exact regression
checks.  Monogenicity of finite \'etale algebras and the historical
Campbell--Razar--Wright degree-two theorem also remain mathematical inputs
rather than parts of the Lean certificate.  The explicit optimal quintic map,
its determinant, and its determinant-one output normalization are formalized
separately.  Thus the formalization certifies the new reconstruction and
automatic-translation arguments without mislabelling the classical or
coefficientwise inputs as machine-checked.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
The recent three-dimensional counterexample was publicly attributed to
Alp\"oge and Fable.  Gallagher independently developed a weighted all-degree
viewpoint and an exact public atlas of complete rational fibers
\cite{Gallagher}.  The proof here instead uses the root-engineered
quadratic-gauge construction and the finite-\'etale realization theorem.
Berend and Bilu are credited for the classical intersective quintic.  The
rank-two exclusion is the Galois case proved over $\mathbb C$ by Campbell and
subsequently treated algebraically by Razar and independently Wright
\cite{Campbell,Razar,Wright}; Lemma~\ref{lem:degree-two} includes the descent
argument needed for an arbitrary characteristic-zero ground field.
