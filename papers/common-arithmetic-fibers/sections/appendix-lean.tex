The explicit-example interface is checked separately in
\nolinkurl{PaperExampleCorrespondence.lean}.  A single JSON specification
generates the TeX macros used in Section~\ref{sec:example}, a Lean-readable
map, runnable SymPy and Sage inputs, and an expanded sparse JSON certificate.
The declarations
\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{\nolinkurl{integralMap_eq_generatedPaperMap}},\\
\text{\nolinkurl{generatedPaperInversePolynomial}},\\
\text{\nolinkurl{generatedPaper_integral_to_jacobianOne}}
\end{gathered}
\]
identify the displayed coordinates, inverse polynomial, targets, and both
output scalings with the formal definitions;
\nolinkurl{integralFiberPoint_eval_eq_generatedTarget} additionally checks
the distinguished target built into the literal fiber structure.

The paper-facing declarations are defined in
\nolinkurl{PaperCertificate.lean}.  This module directly imports only the
general gauge construction, its function-field comparison, the page-one
theorem, and the abstract finite-\'etale realization.  It does not import the
arithmetic examples or companion developments.  The exact headline interface is
\[
 \text{\nolinkurl{paperPolynomialPresentation_pageOne}}.
\]
It takes
\texttt{P.Separable}, exactly as Theorem~\ref{thm:polynomial-realization}
does, and converts it to the equivalent internal \texttt{Squarefree}
hypothesis before applying \nolinkurl{automaticRealization_pageOne}.

The page-one certificate exposes geometric degree both through the
convenience definition
\(\texttt{automaticRealizationGeometricDegree}\) and, independently of that
definition, through the direct standard-object equality
\[
 \operatorname{finrank}_{K(\Pi,B)(C)}K(x,y,z)
 =\deg P.
\]
Its Lean type uses \texttt{Module.finrank}, \texttt{RatFunc}, and the fraction
field of the three-variable polynomial ring directly.  The algebra structure
is the pullback by the three displayed target coordinates, and
\nolinkurl{automaticRealizationFunctionFieldComparison} is the explicit
algebra equivalence with the corresponding inverse-root extension.
The certificate also includes the target denormalization, so the fixed
Jacobian-one output normalization does not alter the generated target field.

The formal development already identifies the cubic coefficient of
\(P(a+S)-P(a)\) with the third Hasse derivative.  The headline theorem needs
no generalization: its paper and Lean assumptions are both characteristic
zero.  Removing the blanket \texttt{CharZero} assumptions from the general
gauge, reconstruction, function-field, and supplied-base-change files would
formalize the separate characteristic-\(\ne2\) proposition; that remains
future work.
