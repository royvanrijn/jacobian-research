\section{Consequences, scope, and further directions}

The main theorem is a prescribed-fiber realization result.  Given a finite
\'etale algebra, it builds a Keller map whose full fiber is that algebra.  The
classification by rank is a useful corollary, but it is not the main point:
two finite \'etale algebras of the same rank can have different field
decompositions, Galois actions, signatures, and local behavior, and the
construction preserves all of that data.

Several consequences are immediate.  A squarefree polynomial of degree at
least three becomes an exact inverse polynomial after one translation; an
irreducible polynomial gives a connected fiber; and a degree-\(N\) input is
realized in \(\A^3\) with coordinate degree at most \(6N+2\).  Every map
produced by the construction has geometric and arithmetic generic monodromy
\(S_N\).  Over a Hilbertian field, each such fixed map has infinitely many
connected full fibers with splitting-field group \(S_N\).

Fullness also has an intrinsic geometric interpretation in this family.
Theorem~\ref{thm:exact-nonproperness} identifies the exact reduced
nonproperness locus with the inverse-discriminant zero set, and
Corollary~\ref{cor:intrinsic-fullness} places every distinguished target in
its complement.  The partition formula
\eqref{eq:partition-sheet-loss}, the complete table
\eqref{eq:pi-zero-fiber-table}, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:boundary-arcs} account for the affine and boundary
sheets on every displayed stratum.

This is a transfer statement, not an inverse Galois theorem.  It does not say
that arbitrary local specifications can always be globalized.  It says that
once a finite \'etale algebra with the desired arithmetic properties exists,
the Keller construction preserves it exactly.  Nor does the monodromy theorem
say that one fixed seed realizes every rank-\(N\) finite \'etale algebra: the
prescribed algebra is used to choose the seed.  Its strengthening is that
every resulting cover, including one built for a highly special algebra, has
maximal generic monodromy.

\paragraph{Relation to earlier work.}
To my knowledge, the following prescribed-algebra statement has not appeared
before: every finite \'etale \(K\)-algebra of rank at least three occurs as a
\emph{full scheme fiber} of an explicit Keller map
\(\A^3_K\to\A^3_K\).  The rank set \(\{1,3,4,\ldots\}\) follows from this
theorem together with the known degree-two obstruction.  I searched for
earlier results up to 24 July 2026, but this priority statement is necessarily
qualified: searches can miss unpublished work or a theorem stated in
different language.

The closest constructions have a different scope.  Gallagher gives explicit
Keller maps in every geometric degree together with complete split rational
fibers \cite{Gallagher}; these realize \(K^N\), rather than an arbitrary
finite \'etale \(K\)-algebra.  Miranda--Neto studies radicality and
maximal-ideal decompositions of fibers that already come from Keller maps
\cite{Miranda}.  Lipton and Markakis connect rational images with Hilbert
irreducibility \cite{LiptonMarkakis}, and Shaska studies graded Keller maps
and arithmetic thinness \cite{Shaska}.  The monodromy argument here uses the
classical theorem that a Morse polynomial has symmetric Galois group over the
value-function field \cite[Theorem~4.4.5]{Serre}.

\paragraph{Independent checks and formalization.}
The SymPy checker
\[
 \texttt{scripts/verify\_universal\_quadratic\_gauge.py}
\]
verifies the source chart, the marked-line identities, the Jacobian
cancellation, every generic coefficient contribution, and the degree bound.
The independent Singular checker expands the complete degree-six map over a
rational function field in six coefficients, obtains determinant \(-2\), and
checks the degree profile \((7,38,36)\).  Concrete degree-three, degree-four,
and degree-five regressions reconstruct the polynomial quotient in both
directions.  The additional checker
\[
 \texttt{scripts/verify\_quadratic\_gauge\_nonproperness.py}
\]
independently checks the cubic and quartic discriminant specializations, the
direct \(q=0\) and \(t=0\) charts, discriminant orders and saturated-slice
coefficients through degree ten, and the uniform Newton and
boundary-valuation ledger through degree sixty-four.

The Lean project in
\[
 \texttt{formal/finite-etale-keller}
\]
contains no \texttt{sorry} and introduces no project-specific axioms.  It
constructs the all-degree map, proves its normalized Jacobian and coordinate
degree bound, identifies the literal distinguished fiber naturally over every
commutative test algebra, and proves that its representing quotient has the
expected rank and is finite \'etale.  It also proves irreducibility and degree
of the independent-parameter inverse equation, algebraic independence of the
three map coordinates, and injectivity of the induced function-field
pullback.  It then constructs, over the canonical target presentation
\(K(\Pi,B)(C)\), the algebra equivalence
\[
 K(x,y,z)\simeq
 K(\Pi,B)(C)[S]/(E_{\Pi,B,C})
\]
and transfers the inverse-root finrank to prove the actual geometric degree
\(N\).  A separate theorem checks the inverse target rescaling from the
Jacobian-one map back to the raw gauge.  The public theorem
\[
 \texttt{automaticRealization\_pageOne}
\]
simultaneously records determinant one, geometric degree \(N\), the natural
literal-fiber representation by \(K[T]/(P)\), finite \'etaleness, finiteness,
rank \(N\), and the \(6N+2\) bound.

The Lean axiom reports for these declarations contain only
\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
Monogenicity, symmetric monodromy, Hilbert irreducibility, the
exact nonproperness and boundary-sheet accounting, the
Campbell--Razar--Wright theorem, and the number-theoretic local-solubility
arguments remain ordinary mathematical proofs or inputs.  For the quintic
example, Lean verifies the exact fiber, its rank and finite \'etaleness, the
absence of rational points, and the existence of real and three-adic points;
the other local points remain in the elementary proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:hasse}.

\subsection*{Addendum: arithmetic left for later}

The quadratic gauge has more structure than is needed for the realization
theorem.  The exact nonproperness boundary is proved in
Subsection~\ref{subsec:nonproperness}.  The following arithmetic direction is
recorded so that its exact inverse-family computation is not hidden, but it is
not used as a theorem in this paper.

\paragraph{One map with many Hasse failures.}
There is also a fixed quintic gauge for which exact algebra suggests the
family
\[
 \Spec\Q[X]/\bigl((X^3-\ell)(X^2+X+1)\bigr),
 \qquad \ell\equiv1\pmod{27},
\]
as fibers at pairwise distinct rational targets.  The inverse-polynomial
identity is exact, and the expected local argument combines Hensel lifting,
the residue-class dichotomy used in Theorem~\ref{thm:hasse}, and primes in an
arithmetic progression.  A satisfactory treatment should audit all local
cases and the claimed counting asymptotic as a separate arithmetic theorem.
For the present paper, the single optimal quintic fiber is enough.

Other constructions in the accompanying repository place the same finite
\'etale algebra inside weighted and cancellation-type Keller maps that need
not be stably equivalent.  Their comparison belongs with those deferred
questions, not with the prescribed-fiber theorem proved here.

\paragraph{Provenance and AI assistance.}
The recent three-dimensional counterexample was announced by Alp\"oge, who
credited Akhil Mathew for suggesting the problem and Fable for producing the
example \cite{Alpoge}.  Gallagher independently developed a weighted
all-degree viewpoint and a public atlas of complete rational fibers
\cite{Gallagher}.  The present proof uses a root-engineered quadratic gauge
that contains the announced map as the seed \(S+S^3\).  Berend and Bilu
supplied the classical intersective quintic, while Campbell, Razar, and
Wright supplied the degree-two obstruction
\cite{Campbell,Razar,Wright}.

I used generative AI tools during exploration, symbolic-checker development,
proof review, literature search, Lean formalization, and manuscript editing.
They were useful as fast but fallible collaborators.  I checked the stated
mathematical claims, rewrote the exposition, and take responsibility for the
paper.
