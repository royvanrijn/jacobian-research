\begin{abstract}
Let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero.  We first prove a prescribed
finite-\'etale algebra realization theorem: every finite \'etale $K$-algebra
of rank at least three
occurs as the full fiber of an explicit polynomial map
$\A^3_K\to\A^3_K$ with Jacobian one.  The construction preserves the algebra
itself, not only its rank or its geometric points.  As a corollary, the
result gives an exact fixed-degree classification: for every $N\geq3$, the
full fibers of geometric-degree-$N$ Keller maps in $\A^3_K$ are precisely the
rank-$N$ finite \'etale $K$-schemes.  Rank one is realized by the identity,
whereas rank two is excluded by the known degree-two theorem.  Thus the
attainable nonzero ranks are exactly $1,3,4,5,\ldots$.

The scheme-theoretic engine is a general localized quadratic-gauge fiber
theorem.  For an arbitrary target with inverse polynomial $E$, it identifies
the fiber as
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}\left((K[S]/(E))[1/\overline{E'}]\right).
\]
Its finite-\'etale, or squarefree, corollary says that when $E$ is squarefree
of degree $N$, the localization disappears and the quotient is a full fiber
of rank $N$.  To prescribe an algebra, start with a squarefree polynomial
$P$ of degree $N\geq3$, translate the variable so that
$P'(a)P'''(a)\neq0$, and feed $G(S)=P(a+S)-P(a)$ into the gauge.  The resulting
map has coordinate degree at most $6N+2$; at a distinguished target
$E=P(a+S)$, and the full fiber is naturally isomorphic to
$\Spec K[T]/(P)$.  The cubic seed $G(S)=S+S^3$ gives the recently announced
three-dimensional map coordinate for coordinate.

We compute the nonproperness set of every quadratic-gauge map exactly.  It is
the hypersurface
\[
 S_{F_G}=V\!\left(\operatorname{Disc}_S(E_{\Pi,B,C})\right).
\]
Every repeated-root block records exactly the sheets lost from the affine
fiber.  The slice $\Pi=0$ has a separate Newton-polygon stratification: its
generic fiber has three affine sheets and $N-3$ sheets at infinity.  In degree
three the raw resultant has an extraneous factor $\Pi$, whereas in degree at
least four the plane $\Pi=0$ is a genuine nonproperness component.  Explicit
Laurent arcs and their valuations represent every missing branch.  Since the
distinguished inverse polynomial is the squarefree polynomial $P(a+S)$, the
prescribed fiber lies structurally in the maximal finite-\'etale locus.

For every admissible seed, not merely for a generic one, the splitting field
of the generic inverse polynomial over $K(\Pi,B,C)$ has geometric and
arithmetic Galois group $S_N$.  Thus every prescribed algebra occurs as a
special fiber of a maximally symmetric generic cover.  Over a Hilbertian
field, each constructed map also has infinitely many connected full fibers
whose splitting fields have group $S_N$.

Connectedness, field decomposition, Galois action, real signature, local
points, and scalar extension therefore pass to the Keller fiber unchanged.
Over $\Q$ this gives an explicit rank-five fiber with points over $\R$ and
every $\Q_p$ but no rational point; rank five is minimal, and one fixed Keller
map contains infinitely many such fibers.

The constructive part of the argument is formalized in Lean: the automatic
choice of $a$, the actual three-variable polynomial map, its Jacobian-one
identity, the $6N+2$ degree bound, and the natural equivalence between the
literal map fiber and $K[T]/(P)$.  Independent SymPy and Singular calculations
check the same identities from separate implementations.
\end{abstract}
