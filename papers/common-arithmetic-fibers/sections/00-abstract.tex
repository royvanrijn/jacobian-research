\begin{abstract}
Let \(K\) be a field of characteristic zero.  We prove that every finite
\'etale \(K\)-algebra of rank at least three occurs as the full fiber of an
explicit polynomial Keller map \(\A^3_K\to\A^3_K\) with Jacobian one.  The
construction preserves the algebra itself, not only its rank or its geometric
points.  It gives an exact fixed-degree classification: for \(N\geq3\), the
full fibers of geometric-degree-\(N\) Keller maps in \(\A^3_K\) are precisely
the rank-\(N\) finite \'etale \(K\)-schemes.  Rank one is realized by the
identity, while rank two is excluded by the Galois case of the Jacobian
theorem.  Thus the attainable nonzero ranks are exactly
\(1,3,4,5,\ldots\).

The engine is a localized quadratic-gauge fiber theorem.  For an arbitrary
target with inverse polynomial \(E\), it identifies the fiber as
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}\left((K[S]/(E))[1/\overline{E'}]\right).
\]
When \(E\) is squarefree of degree \(N\), the localization disappears.  To
prescribe an algebra, start with a squarefree polynomial \(P\) of degree
\(N\geq3\), choose \(a\) with \(P'(a)P'''(a)\neq0\), and insert
\(G(S)=P(a+S)-P(a)\) into the gauge.  The resulting map has coordinate degree
at most \(6N+2\); at a distinguished target its inverse polynomial is
\(P(a+S)\), and the literal scheme fiber is naturally isomorphic to
\(\Spec K[T]/(P)\).  A direct function-field comparison proves that the
actual geometric degree is \(N\).  The cubic seed \(G(S)=S+S^3\) recovers the
recently announced three-dimensional map coordinate for coordinate.

For every admissible seed, not merely for a generic one, the splitting field
of the generic inverse polynomial over \(K(\Pi,B,C)\) has geometric and
arithmetic Galois group \(S_N\).  The proof produces a Morse vertical slice
from a birational parametrization of the repeated-root curve.  Thus every
prescribed algebra occurs as a special fiber of a maximally symmetric generic
cover.  Over a Hilbertian field, each constructed map has infinitely many
connected full fibers whose splitting fields have group \(S_N\).
The reduced nonproperness locus is exactly the zero set of the inverse
discriminant.  Direct charts give every fiber on \(\Pi=0\), while a uniform
Newton calculation gives the exact global discriminant order
\(N^2-3N-2\) for \(N\geq4\).

Connectedness, field decomposition, Galois action, real signature, local
points, and scalar extension pass to the Keller fiber unchanged.  Over
\(\Q\), the classical Berend--Bilu quintic gives an explicit rank-five fiber
with points over \(\R\) and every \(\Q_p\), but no rational point; rank five
is minimal.

The polynomial-presentation part is formalized in Lean: the automatic choice
of \(a\), the actual three-variable polynomial map, its Jacobian-one identity,
the \(6N+2\) degree bound, the source-function-field comparison and geometric
degree \(N\), and the natural equivalence between the literal map fiber and
\(K[T]/(P)\), including finite \'etaleness and rank.  Independent SymPy and
Singular calculations check the coordinate identities, Jacobian, degree
profile, concrete quotient reconstruction, and nonproperness boundary from
separate implementations.  The nonproperness theorem itself remains an
ordinary proof rather than a Lean theorem.
\end{abstract}
