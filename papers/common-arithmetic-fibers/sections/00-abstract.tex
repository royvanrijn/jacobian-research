\begin{abstract}
Let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero.  We prove a prescribed-fiber
realization theorem: every finite \'etale $K$-algebra of rank at least three
occurs as the full fiber of an explicit polynomial map
$\A^3_K\to\A^3_K$ with Jacobian one.  The construction preserves the algebra
itself, not only its rank or its geometric points.  As a consequence, the
possible nonzero ranks of full Keller fibers are exactly
$1,3,4,5,\ldots$; rank two is excluded by the known degree-two theorem.

Start with a squarefree polynomial $P$ of degree $N\geq3$, translate the
variable so that $P'(a)P'''(a)\neq0$, and feed
$G(S)=P(a+S)-P(a)$ into a universal quadratic-gauge formula.  The resulting
map has coordinate degree at most $6N+2$.  At one distinguished target its
inverse equation is exactly $P(a+S)=0$, and the full fiber is naturally
isomorphic to $\Spec K[T]/(P)$.  More generally, for an arbitrary target with
inverse polynomial $E$, the fiber is
\[
 \operatorname{Spec}\left((K[S]/(E))[1/\overline{E'}]\right).
\]
The cubic seed $G(S)=S+S^3$ recovers the recently announced
three-dimensional counterexample exactly, coordinate for coordinate.

Connectedness, field decomposition, Galois action, real signature, local
points, and scalar extension therefore pass to the Keller fiber unchanged.
Over $\Q$ this gives an explicit rank-five fiber with points over $\R$ and
every $\Q_p$ but no rational point; rank five is minimal, and one fixed Keller
map contains infinitely many such fibers.

The constructive part of the argument is formalized in Lean: the automatic
choice of $a$, the actual three-variable polynomial map, its Jacobian-one
identity, the $6N+2$ degree bound, and the natural equivalence between the
literal map fiber and $K[T]/(P)$.  Independent SymPy and Singular calculations
check the same identities from separate implementations.
\end{abstract}
