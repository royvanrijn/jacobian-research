\begin{abstract}
Given a characteristic-zero field \(K\), a squarefree polynomial
\(P\in K[T]\) of degree \(N\geq3\), and an admissible translation, we
construct an explicit polynomial map \(F:\A^3_K\to\A^3_K\) with
\(\det DF=1\), geometric degree \(N\), and a distinguished full fiber
naturally isomorphic to \(\Spec K[T]/(P)\).  Consequently every finite
\'etale \(K\)-algebra of rank at least three occurs as a full Keller fiber.
For a supplied polynomial presentation and translation the construction
commutes with scalar extension; the presentation chosen for an abstract
finite \'etale algebra is noncanonical.  The polynomial construction,
root-to-source reconstruction, literal fiber theorem, function-field
comparison, base change, and abstract finite-\'etale corollary are formalized
in Lean.
\end{abstract}
