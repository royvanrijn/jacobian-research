\begin{abstract}
Every prescribed finite \'etale \(K\)-scheme of rank \(N\geq3\) is the
complete full fiber of an explicit polynomial Keller map
\(F:\A^3_K\to\A^3_K\), preserving its entire arithmetic structure.
Here \(K\) has characteristic zero, \(\det DF=1\), and
\(\gdeg(F)=N\).  Given a squarefree presentation
\(A\simeq K[T]/(P)\), the map uses one translation \(P(a+S)\) and a uniform
quadratic-gauge formula of coordinate degree at most \(6N+2\).  The fiber
identification represents
\(\operatorname{Hom}_{K\text{-alg}}(A,-)\), naturally in every commutative
test algebra and compatibly with extension of the ground field.

For every seed produced by the construction, not merely for a generic choice
of coefficients, the generic inverse equation has geometric and arithmetic
Galois group \(S_N\).  Its primitive action implies that every realization
map is absolutely and stably compositionally indecomposable.  Over a
Hilbertian field, each fixed map also has infinitely many connected full
fibers with splitting-field group \(S_N\).  Thus the result prescribes the
complete fiber algebra, rather than only producing maps of a chosen generic
degree.
\end{abstract}
