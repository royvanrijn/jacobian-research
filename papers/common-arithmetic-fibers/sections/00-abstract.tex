\begin{abstract}
Let \(K\) be a field of characteristic zero.  We prove that every finite
\'etale \(K\)-algebra \(A\) of rank \(N\geq3\) occurs as a full Keller fiber
of a polynomial map
\(F:\A^3_K\to\A^3_K\) with Jacobian determinant one and geometric degree
\(N\).  Given a squarefree presentation \(A\simeq K[T]/(P)\) and
\(a\in K\) with \(P'(a)P'''(a)\neq0\), the map is explicit: it uses the
single translation \(P(a+S)\) and a uniform quadratic-gauge formula whose
coordinate degree is at most \(6N+2\).  The fiber identification is natural
in the test algebra and compatible with extension of the ground field.

For every seed produced by the construction, not merely generically, the
generic inverse equation has geometric and arithmetic Galois group \(S_N\).
Consequently, the possible nonzero ranks of full Keller fibers in dimension
three are exactly
\(1,3,4,\ldots\); degree two is excluded by the classical Galois case of the
Jacobian theorem.  Over \(\Q\), the Berend--Bilu quintic gives an explicit
rank-five full Keller fiber with points over \(\R\) and every \(\Q_p\), but
no rational point.  A finite-\(G\)-set lemma and Chebotarev show, over every
number field,
that degree at most four and local solubility at almost all finite places
force a rational point; hence rank five is minimal over \(\Q\).
\end{abstract}
