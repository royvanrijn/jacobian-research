\begin{abstract}
Let \(K\) be a field of characteristic different from \(2\).  Given a
separable polynomial \(P\in K[T]\) of degree \(N\geq3\) and a translation
\(a\in K\) at which the first and third Hasse derivatives of \(P\) are
nonzero, we construct an explicit polynomial map
\(F:\A^3_K\to\A^3_K\) with \(\det DF=1\), geometric degree \(N\), and a
distinguished full fiber naturally isomorphic to \(\Spec K[T]/(P)\).
Consequently, in characteristic zero, every finite \'etale \(K\)-algebra of
rank at least three occurs as a full Keller fiber.  For supplied presentation
data the construction commutes with scalar extension; the presentation and
translation chosen for an abstract finite \'etale algebra are noncanonical.
The characteristic-zero specialization, including automatic translation,
the function-field comparison, base change, and the abstract finite-\'etale
corollary, is formalized in Lean.
\end{abstract}
