\section{Exact verification and finite data}\label{sec:verification}

The proof has three different assurance layers.  The determinant, inverse
factorization, resultants, heights, and finite counts have exact symbolic or
integer checks.  The algebraic fiber argument and the uniform local theorem
also have a Lean formalization.  The Selberg--Delange asymptotic and the
analytic input to degree minimality remain ordinary mathematical proofs.
These layers should not be confused: in particular, the bounded count is
not evidence from which the asymptotic is inferred.

Run
\[
\begin{split}
&\texttt{.venv/bin/python
scripts/verify\_infinite\_hasse\_keller\_fibers.py},\\
&\texttt{.venv/bin/python
scripts/verify\_multiplicative\_hasse\_artifact.py}.
\end{split}
\tag{6.1}
\]

target-line factorization \eqref{eq:target-factorization}, the factor
discriminants and
resultant, the \(3\)-adic substitution, and the first prime and composite
parameters.  The determinant-one identity for \(\Phi\) then follows from
the two displayed linear transformations in
\eqref{eq:linear-normalization}.

The machine-checked chain consists of
\begin{center}
\small
\texttt{FixedHasseFamily.lean}, \texttt{FixedHasseMap.lean},
\texttt{FixedHasseLocal.lean},\\
\texttt{FixedHasseHeight.lean}, and
\texttt{FixedHasseArithmetic.lean}, collected by the isolated publication
entry point \texttt{FixedHassePaperCertificate.lean}.
\end{center}
These modules prove, respectively, the centered factorization and
squarefreeness certificates,
the exact determinant-one normalization and complete literal-fiber
equivalence, the uniform prime-by-prime local theorem, and the primitive
coordinates with exact height \(32a\), followed by multiplicative closure
of the congruence/support conditions and the complete prime-progression
certificate.  In particular, Lean proves that a prime
\(\ell\equiv1\pmod9\) cannot be a rational cube.  The local module derives
oddness from prime support, so its final declaration
\texttt{paperFiberPoint\_hasse\_certificate} states directly that every
admissible noncube parameter gives a literal fiber with no rational point,
a real point, and a point over every \(\Q_p\).  The certificate contains no
\texttt{sorry} and uses only Lean's standard foundational axioms.

The exact displayed-map degree is included as well.  Lean proves the
polynomial identity obtained by applying
\(A^{-1}(x,y,z)=(2x,y,z)\) and \(L^{-1}=L\) to \(\Phi\), recovering
\(F_0\) exactly.  These source and target polynomial automorphisms preserve
function-field degree, so the certified degree-five computation for
\(F_0\) is the geometric degree of the displayed map \(\Phi\), not merely
of an unnormalized auxiliary map.

The table is a convergence and regression check.  The local theorem applies
to every parameter satisfying \eqref{eq:parameter-family}, while the
asymptotics follow from
the Euler products and Selberg--Delange; neither conclusion is inferred
from the bounded enumeration.
