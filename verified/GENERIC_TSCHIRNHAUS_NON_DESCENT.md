# Generic Tschirnhaus non-descent for the quadratic-gauge compiler

This note packages the all-rank consequence of three independent inputs:

1. the universal relative Keller compiler and its normalized presentation
   chart;
2. the all-rank projective Tschirnhaus determinantal theorem; and
3. the exact stable-moduli classification of the quadratic-gauge coefficient
   torus.

The ranks-five-through-seven bridge card is an exact regression for this
theorem, not its proof.

Work over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field `k` and fix
`N>=5`.  All statements below are restricted to the clean locus where the
root polynomial is squarefree and every coefficient used by the
quadratic-gauge stable-moduli theorem is nonzero.

## 1. Presentation groupoid and boundary quotient

Let

\[
 \operatorname{Conf}_N(\mathbb A^1)
 =
 \{(r_1,\ldots,r_N):r_i\ne r_j\text{ for }i\ne j\}.
                                                               \tag{1.1}
\]

An ordered pair `(r,u)` in
`\operatorname{Conf}_N(\mathbb A^1)^2` is a pair of primitive coordinates
on the same split rank-`N` finite-etale algebra.  Interpolation gives a unique
polynomial `q` of degree less than `N` with

\[
 u_i=q(r_i).                                                \tag{1.2}
\]

Thus the product configuration space is the ordered splitting chart of the
primitive-presentation groupoid.  Simultaneous relabeling by `S_N` does not
change either the abstract algebra or any coefficient invariant below.

On the compiler slice `a_3=1`, write the unchanged seed coordinates as

\[
 (u_4,\ldots,u_N)\in\mathbb G_m^{N-3}.
\]

The residual source--target scaling is

\[
 u_j\longmapsto\alpha^{j+1}u_j.                         \tag{1.3}
\]

The quadratic-gauge stable-moduli theorem proves that its saturated invariant
ring is generated by

\[
\boxed{
 \Phi_N=
 \left(
 I_5,J_6,\ldots,J_N
 \right)
 =
 \left(
 \frac{u_5^5}{u_4^6},
 \frac{u_4u_6}{u_5^2},
 \ldots,
 \frac{u_{N-2}u_N}{u_{N-1}^2}
 \right).
}                                                         \tag{1.4}
\]

Consequently the clean coarse stable quotient is

\[
 \mathcal M_N^{\mathrm{quad}}
 \simeq\mathbb G_m^{N-4}.                                \tag{1.5}
\]

The normalized presentation chart `V_N` is isomorphic to the compiler
incidence chart: the `N-3` seed coordinates and the three moving target
coordinates recover all `N` normalized polynomial coefficients.  Projection
to the seed torus is dominant.  The ordered-root-to-coefficient map is finite
and dominant.  Hence the rational map

\[
 \Phi_N:\operatorname{Conf}_N(\mathbb A^1)
 \dashrightarrow\mathcal M_N^{\mathrm{quad}}              \tag{1.6}
\]

is dominant on the clean locus.

It follows that, on the generic flat locus, the equal-boundary relation

\[
 \mathcal E_N
 =
 \{(r,u):\Phi_N(r)=\Phi_N(u)\}                            \tag{1.7}
\]

has codimension `N-4` in the presentation groupoid.  Each individual
coordinate equality in (1.4) is also a proper closed condition.  Therefore
a generic pair changes every applicable coordinate of `Phi_N`.

## 2. Projective locus

The all-rank collision-frame theorem proves that `r` and `u` are related by
one element of `PGL_2` exactly when

\[
 \operatorname{rank}(1,r,u,ru)\le3.                     \tag{2.1}
\]

On the framed primitive open, the `4`-by-`4` minors reduce to `N-3`
independent residuals.  Thus the projective locus

\[
 \mathcal P_N\subset
 \operatorname{Conf}_N(\mathbb A^1)^2                   \tag{2.2}
\]

is smooth of codimension `N-3`.

The codimension comparison is important:

\[
 \operatorname{codim}\mathcal E_N=N-4,\qquad
 \operatorname{codim}\mathcal P_N=N-3.                  \tag{2.3}
\]

In particular, equality of every Fitting-boundary coordinate is not a test
for projectivity.  The generic point of an equal-boundary component is still
nonprojective.  The theorem below must say *generic nonprojective*, not
*every nonprojective*.

## 3. Generic non-descent theorem

> **Generic quadratic-gauge Tschirnhaus non-descent theorem.**
>
> For every `N>=5`, there is a dense open subset of the clean
> primitive-presentation groupoid on which a Tschirnhaus change
> `r -> u=q(r)`:
>
> 1. preserves the abstract rank-`N` finite-etale root algebra;
> 2. is nonprojective;
> 3. changes at least one, and generically every, coordinate of `Phi_N`; and
> 4. therefore does not lift to a stable polynomial left--right equivalence
>    of the two compiled quadratic-gauge Keller maps.

The first assertion is the interpolation isomorphism between the two root
quotients.  The complement of the projective locus is dense by (2.2), and
the complement of the equal-boundary relation is dense by (1.7).  Their
intersection is dense because the presentation groupoid is irreducible.
The last assertion is exactly the stable-normalization and Fitting
functoriality direction of the quadratic-gauge stable-moduli theorem.

There is no hidden fixed-map stabilizer loophole in this conclusion.  The
[stable intruder descent criterion](STABLE_INTRUDER_DESCENT_CRITERION.md)
separates the stable base, boundary-faithfulness, and deck-group gates.  The
corresponding
[all-rank stable marked-stabilizer
theorem](QUADRATIC_GAUGE_STABLE_MODULI.md#42-all-rank-stable-marked-stabilizer-kernel)
shows that the reduced discriminant has the universal exposed intruder
`P^2B^NC`.  Kuroda's stable-invariant theorem then forces every stable
self-equivalence of a fixed clean compiler map to preserve the physical
target ring, and the coordinate-polynomial intruder theorem makes that
restriction the identity.  This holds in every degree and after arbitrary
identity stabilization.

This proves failure of descent of the displayed compiler atlas.  It does not
exclude a different Keller lift whose intrinsic boundary is
Tschirnhaus-invariant.

## 4. One uniform exact witness

Take

\[
 r_i=i,\qquad u_i=i+i^2\qquad(1\le i\le N).             \tag{4.1}
\]

The values `i+i^2` are distinct.  The first-four-root minor of the columns
`1,r,u,ru` is `12`, so the change is nonprojective for every `N>=4`.
Interpolation gives the inverse coordinate and hence an explicit
isomorphism of quotient algebras.

For `N>=6`, the top stable coordinate is

\[
 J_N(P)=\frac{a_{N-2}a_N}{a_{N-1}^2}.                  \tag{4.2}
\]

Power-sum identities give

\[
 J_N(P_r)
 =\frac{(N-1)(3N+2)}{6N(N+1)},                         \tag{4.3}
\]

\[
 J_N(P_u)
 =\frac{(N-1)(5N^2+11N+3)}
        {10N(N+1)(N+2)},                               \tag{4.4}
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{
 J_N(P_u)-J_N(P_r)
 =
 -\frac{(N-1)(7N+11)}
        {30N(N+1)(N+2)}
 \ne0.
}                                                       \tag{4.5}
\]

For `N=5`, the stable coordinate `I_5` has the two exact values

\[
 \frac{75076}{968203125},
 \qquad
 \frac{1296}{50236123},
                                                               \tag{4.6}
\]

which are unequal.  Thus (4.1) is one all-rank arithmetic-neutral witness
for the generic theorem.

## 5. Quantifying the fixed-algebra locus

An unmarked stable Keller map can have many nonisomorphic fibers, so there is
no literal function

\[
 \{\text{stable maps}\}\longrightarrow
 \{\text{finite-etale algebras}\}.                      \tag{5.1}
\]

The correct stack morphism retains the target point:

\[
 \rho:\mathscr I_N\longrightarrow BS_N,\qquad
 (F,y)\longmapsto F^{-1}(y).                            \tag{5.2}
\]

Alternatively, for a fixed algebra `A/K`, one may use the set
`\mathcal R_K(A)` of stable map classes possessing a complete fiber
isomorphic to `Spec(A)`.

Within the clean quadratic-gauge family, the geometric fixed-algebra locus
has exact stable-moduli dimension

\[
\boxed{
 \dim\overline{\mathcal R^{\mathrm{quad,clean}}(A)}
 =N-4
}                                                       \tag{5.3}
\]

after base change to an algebraic closure.  Indeed every geometric
rank-`N` finite-etale algebra is split, and for every clean seed a nonempty
open of the three target coordinates gives a squarefree degree-`N` inverse
polynomial.  Passing to stable ambient map classes gives the full quotient
(1.5).  Over an arbitrary characteristic-zero ground field, the universal
Keller-fiber multiplicity theorem independently proves that
`\mathcal R_K(A)` is infinite.

This is the dimension of the image in unmarked stable-map moduli, not the
dimension of a literal fibre of (5.2).  The
[clean decorated receiver](QUADRATIC_GAUGE_DECORATED_RECEIVER.md) constructs
the quotient span precisely.  The weight-one coordinate `u_5/u_4` supplies
a global slice, so the clean receiver is represented by an explicit scheme
with no coefficient-scaling inertia.  Its marked fibre over a geometric
algebra has dimension `N-1`, its target-forgetting fibres have dimension
three, and its image in unmarked map moduli has dimension `N-4`.

Equation (5.3) is exact only for this clean quadratic-gauge stratum.  No
finite-type global moduli stack of all polynomial degrees and all stable
left--right classes has been constructed, so no dimension of the total
fiber is claimed.

## 6. Consequences for the attack programme

The theorem closes three proposed attack classes for this compiler:

1. direct descent of the quadratic-gauge atlas through `BS_N`;
2. reconstruction of the ambient stable map from the abstract root algebra,
   its Galois action, or collision sheets alone; and
3. use of equality of `Phi_N` as a projectivity criterion.

Three honest continuations remain:

1. extend the
   [clean quadratic-gauge decorated receiver](QUADRATIC_GAUGE_DECORATED_RECEIVER.md)
   beyond this compiler chart;
2. construct a different compiler whose boundary data is
   Tschirnhaus-invariant; or
3. replace a section of (5.2) by a correspondence or torsor-valued lift.

The number `N-4` is not a lower bound for the proposed global invariants
`ktdim` or `kdeg`: it is a vertical modulus count for one compiler stratum.
Global lower bounds require invariants applying to every Keller
realization.
